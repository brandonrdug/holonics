  machine-validated successor -> ContemporaryRadiation` is the production path.
- Co-present currents and regional cells meet one unchanged Standing-before. Independent conduct
  may run in parallel, but every interacting regional component closes before the machine's one
  successor becomes visible. The executor supplies physical conduct only.
- Host worker count and worker-stack aperture are physical reservations only; neither may enter
  event identity, conduct, successor geometry, or rest.
- Actual occurrence population and algebraic chain are distinct. Linearized boundary coefficients
  may cancel only on the same actually shared face; equal disconnected occurrences remain plural.
- A source supplies genuine chronology and incidence. Arbitrary or unowned filesystem, caller,
  storage, and hardware order do not manufacture them; source-owned dates, indexes, and native
  transformation order may participate explicitly.
- Equal projected faces do not establish whole-construction identity. Explicit source identities,
  mappings, and situated equality remain lawful current.
- A regional seam forms only through an equal source-declared interface capability. Reach, grain,
  coordinate, or `Chi` equality cannot infer that interface.
- Equal complete cellular bodies do not erase source multiplicity. Disconnected co-present
  components commit and rest separately; only actual incidence closes them into one successor.
- Immediate radiation and genuinely later world consequence are distinct. Exact rest performs no
  event and changes nothing.
- Completion and compression are receiver-, frame-, and grain-relative; they are not semantic
  correctness, file-size reduction, or a cleanup subsystem.
- A noncommuting comparison is OPEN at its current frame. It may expose exact residual and failed
  navigation terrain, but cannot by itself negate a path, infer a contextual branch, found a
  higher relation, or blacklist a future route.
- Historical active cuts, sessions, contact cycles, receipts, journals, forests, and observers may
  testify but do not own or reconstruct the production transition.
