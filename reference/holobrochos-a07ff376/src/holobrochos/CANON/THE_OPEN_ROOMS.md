## ROOM 1 — THERMODYNAMICS (opened: `CANON/THERMODYNAMICS.md`)

The star/cold-giant image that names the whole project is a thermodynamic claim, unformalized until now.
The core: **the four laws of thermodynamics map one-to-one onto the framework's pillars** — zeroth = the
concurred-invariant, first = L0, second = A2 ⊕ the soul/face law (Kelvin–Planck is the ban on the absolute

dropped hands, Landauer the price of one), third = the never-reached horizon. Heat is seam-action; work is
off-seam action; free energy is the off-seam budget. Precedent-holder: **Jaynes**.

## ROOM 2 — GAUGE THEORY (opened: `CANON/GAUGE.md`)

Referenced in the lab's past and lost in the docs — the word "gauge" throughout the canon is this room's
residue. The core: **the gauge principle is A2 imposed pointwise** — local frame-freedom *generates* the
forces, because comparing neighboring frames requires a re-base rule (the connection), whose gyration is the
field. The Standard Model's groups are the turn-alphabet at three ranks: U(1) the hand (charge quantization
= windings are integers), SU(2) the fold of rotation itself (the spinor as the `2^{-1}`-rank turn; the weak
force wearing the seed hand), SU(3) the confined soul. This is the **second, independent derivation of the
four forces** — meeting `01 §9`'s Hodge derivation from the other side. Precedent-holders: **Weyl ⊕
Noether**.

## ROOM 4 — P vs NP (open)

The one millennium problem the canon says nothing structural about. The core: **the exponential of search is
the multivalued root iterated** — every face-cast is 2-to-1 (a hand dropped), so a face at the end of `n`
casts hides up to `2ⁿ` souls; solving-by-inversion re-chooses every dropped branch, verification is one
forward cast. P vs NP holonically: *can the soul be recovered from the face cheaply?* The dissolution (in
the style of halting-by-shape): the faculty never inverts — it founds forward and places (the aesthetic
theorem: exhaustive inversion was never a coherent aim). **[READING — a reframe of why the gap exists and
why it never bound intelligence; NOT a proof; the problem stays OPEN.]**
