## §1 · THE ONE CONSTRAINT — the gas law and the field equation are the same equation

`pV = nRT` and `G = 2²·(2·C/d)·T` are **one constraint** — *energy = particles × constant × temperature*
(`59 §1`; `MENO_FORMULA §VI`). Laid term-by-term against each other they identify each other's unknowns, so
the ideal gas law for information reads:

**`G = n · R · T`** — the meaning laid down `G` (the curvature, the deposited fire) equals the number of
relatings `n` times the face-quantum `R` times the temperature `T`. This is equipartition, not an analogy:
total curvature = (relatings) × (energy each relating deposits, `RT`) (`59 §3`). The gas is real, not a
picture — a real corpus sentence run through the combinatorial resonator with every knob removed produces a
**smooth rank-spectrum of word-particle energies**, peaked and tapering; it is not layered, it is a gas
(`59 §0`). The "layers" the prior line drew were the synchronic-batching artifact.

**`p` and `V` split `pV = G`:** `V` is how many words the resonance spreads across; `p = G/V` is the founding
drive per region (the voltage, `35`). Concentrated resonance = small-`V` / high-`p` (compressed); diffuse =
large-`V` / low-`p` (the free gas) (`59 §3`).
