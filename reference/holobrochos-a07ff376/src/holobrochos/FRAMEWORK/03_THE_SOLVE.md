## II · THE SWING — PLACE-NOT-SEARCH

held invariant hands the next landing as an **equation**, and the machine **SOLVES it — a derivation, not a

is degree ≤ 2 (**the metric degree, always inside the solvable range**), so the swing never inverts a confined
soul: it solves where the invariant solves and **FOUNDS where it does not** — the same move that resolved the
quintic (`05`). **Never a search, never a nearest-match, never a retry, never a window.**

**The chain's tension resolves the placement.** The recently-landed corners carry a conserved cross-ratio (the
flywheel — the carried gyration, the rotational inertia, the soul); the swing conserves it to place the next
corner (`05`, `MENO.md`). Where no frame holds the conservation, the swing **founds a new axis** (`24 §1`).

**The founder is a thrown spiral** (`52`) — the swing made a physical object:

- **FOLD** — compression along the freefall (`18`): drop every off-direction surface, keep the one you fall
  along. Aerodynamic = aligned with the freefall = least action = the geodesic. The fold commits along the
  dilating arrow (the signed drive, `48`), shedding the reflection-symmetric drag (the diagonal is the mirror,
  `51`).
- **SPIN** — the winding conserved (integers in every frame, why a knot survives a boost). The spin holds the
  nose on the velocity vector; a perfect spiral precesses to follow its own arc. **The carry IS the winding**
  (`13`): running the fold rank by rank, the well stands (the bit) and the winding fires up (the carry) — so
  the scramble that hides the factors is **not information lost; it is the soul the fold conserves** (`52 §3`).
- **FRICTION ⊕ LANDING** — catch-or-slip read per triangle-face; the loop cinches on a catch. The mechanism is
  **recursion about friction where the quantum is the triangle/face — the wheel** (`52 §5`, `22 §5`).

**Place-not-search is the spin placing the nose on the geodesic** (`52 §4`): the football does not *search* for
which way to point — the spin holds it there and the arc-following is automatic. The swing is the spun fold
following the aligned axis on its own, reading the winding it left; the cost is the number of catches (the
overlap density, the face-friction), invisible to any value-register.

> **Founding is cross-ratio conservation, never a recurrence statistic.** The old `recur > μ + √μ`

## III · `χ` — THE CROSS-RATIO, THE CONSERVED SOUL

**`χ` is the cross-ratio — the projective invariant of the inversion (`49 §3`), never the additive sum.** It is

dispersal by proportion, the same ratios at every scale (`MENO.md`). A coherent net holds ONE soul `χ` across
every face.

**`χ` is a ratio of differences** — `A:B :: C:D`, the difference between two ideas holding across a chain
(`MENO_FORMULA §III`). It is fixed by **four corners** (the aperture is three corners with the fourth open;
the swing places the fourth by conserving `χ`).

**`χ` is carried holonic — a pair `(Num, Num)`, NEVER a float** (`MENO.md`, THE UNITS). Collapsing it to a
scalar is the flat additive shadow that re-smuggles the absolute frame.

**The soul is confined; the reader supplies the frame.** Reading a difference (`46 §3`) sorts by the FTC into
three bins — the **exact part** (the transport, recovered by the reader), the **frame** (the constant of
integration, the reader's to supply), and the **soul** (the closed-but-not-exact residue, **confined, the
source's, never uttered**). Nothing is deleted (`∂∂ = 0`): "cancellation" is confinement, never deletion. This
is why a difference reads with no loss of what matters — every value keeps its soul; the soul simply stays
confined while the frame is the reader's (`46 §3`). The interior is gauge; `χ` is what crosses.

> **Disambiguation (hygiene).** The `χ` of the solve is the **cross-ratio** (a holonic pair). It is a distinct

## VI · `P = NP` LOCALLY

accumulative line, so every read is place-not-search and **the recurrence that generates the sequence IS the

family, exactly where the hexis has run — **never globally** (Theorem 2) (`40 §4`).

**Between frames, a solution is never transferred — it is COMMUNICATED.** Verification is the receiving frame's
**coherence-check** of the communicated invariants: *"is your calculus relatively coherent with mine?"* (`40
§4`). The NP certificate was always a communication; the verifier's cheapness is **KINSHIP** — the checking
walk rides the receiver's own standing structure, cheap exactly where the coupling's voltage is high.
Comprehension is the concurrence of invariants — the knot pulled evenly from both ends. Posed relativistically,
the question dissolves into: *can two calculi concur on an invariant at a declared grain in finite time* — an
answer per pair, per frame, per grain.

**The Meno restored to a frame.** `P vs NP` is not our question's parent; it is the **corruption** of an older,
better one. Meno's paradox **IS** finding-vs-checking: *how can you search for what you don't know?* Cook's
formulation restates this from the view from nowhere — an absolute cost detached from every frame, lineage, and
grain — which is why it aporia-locks (`23 §1`). Posed **inside a frame** it has an answer, a measured one:
`π_Β = C/d`, tightening with the lineage toward the terrain's curvature (`23 §2–§3`, corrected per §V above).

**Reading is solving.** The completeness of a read is the Meno asked about the reading itself — the read has
