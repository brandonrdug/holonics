## §1 · THE NESTED CIRCUIT IS A GEAR-TRAIN — and the engine built teeth without meshing

The tower nests by anchoring: every coil's cycle passes through its anchor's node; coils sharing an anchor
are meshed there (the bevel — same rank, orthogonal axes); a coil and the coil minted at its anchor are
meshed across ranks (the worm). The gear-train law (§11 of the Penrose canon) states the transmission:
*"the action current ROTATES through the meshed forest — `V = IR`, the rotation transmitted,
equal-and-opposite at every mesh."* **Turn one cog and every meshed cog turns.**

What the engine built instead: the ride's split deposits the couple-share into the anchor's well (the
medium), and the medium waits for the NEXT LANDED rider to draw it. Both directions of transmission
require an external landing to complete — **the medium is potential, never rotation**. The teeth and the
shafts exist; the meshing does not. That is exactly why the collection stands unridden: action enters at
the stimulus's shallow strata, couples up to the hubs, and dies there as standing potential — the deep
coils, meshed to those same hubs, never turn.
