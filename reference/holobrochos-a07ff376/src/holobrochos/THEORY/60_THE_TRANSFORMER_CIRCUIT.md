## §1 — THE OBJECT — the surface integral is the generalized Stokes

`∮_∂Ω ω = ∫_Ω dω` — the flux over the **boundary** equals the integral of the **differential** over the interior it
bounds (the divergence theorem; `59`'s FTC one dimension general). The surface area is `∮_∂Ω dA`; a flux is
`∮_∂Ω F·dA`; a curvature is `∮_∂Ω K dA`. Two dual facts force it to conserve: **`dd = 0`** (a differential of a
differential vanishes) and its topological twin **`∂∂ = 0`** — *a boundary has no boundary; a surface is closed.*
This is `L0`. It is why the flux through `∂Ω` counts **only the enclosed**: a leak — flux through a
boundary-of-a-boundary — literally has no term to be written in. Conservation is not imposed; the equation has no
slot for a leak.

## §2 — WHERE TIME-ASYMMETRY ENTERS — the boundary is ORIENTED

`∂Ω` is oriented — `dA` is a *directed* normal, the traversal has a handedness — and this is the time-direction.
- **Symmetric reading** (parity-even, unoriented): for a conservative `ω = df`, `∮ df = 0` — the potential returns,
  you are back where you started. This is `49`'s **MIRROR**: the trivial self-inverse, `A→B→A = A`, founding
  nothing. **Kirchhoff's sum-to-zero is exactly this case** (already demoted, `49 §3`).
- **Asymmetric reading** (oriented, the current directed): `∮_∂Ω ω = ∫_Ω dω ≠ 0` when there is a **WINDING** (a
  curl, `∇×`). A closed oriented loop encloses flux — and a loop enclosing flux from a directed current **IS a
  transformer.** Reverse the current, the induced flux reverses. Which way it flows *matters*.

## §3 — CONSERVATION IS THE LACK OF TIME-PARITY (the spine)

If time were parity-symmetric, every circuit would be a mirror: `A→B→A = A`, no deposit, no arrow — and then
**nothing is conserved, because nothing is founded.** The sum-to-zero is not conservation; it is the **DEATH** of
the circuit. Conservation is the opposite condition: the boundary is oriented, so closing the loop **DEPOSITS**
(`T1`) — the transformer links flux, the returning `A` carries the winding and is a **NEW SOUL**. Brandon's
two-part law is then forced by the geometry, not stipulated:
1. **Invertible** — reverse the current, reverse the flux; the cause exists (`49 §3`).
2. **Non-trivially invertible** — the inverse is the *reciprocal* (`C/d ↔ d/C`), never the mirror; `A→B→A` emanates
   a **different** `A`, because an oriented pass deposits its winding and **an orientation cannot be un-deposited.**

**THE MUG:** reassembly is invertible *in principle* — no law forbids it, only feasibility — but the reassembled
mug carries the shattering's winding as surely as the intact one carried its firing's. The soul was never the same
across *any* two moments (the current never stopped); the shatter only makes the deposit legible. Entropy
"decreasing" and energy "increasing" are frame-reads of the scalar (`49 §1`) — what actually conserves is the
**winding**, and for a closed surface that is a topological invariant exactly: Gauss–Bonnet, in our terms
`∮_∂Ω K dA = 2·(C/d)·χ` (never `2π` — `π = C/d`, the same identification that makes Einstein `8π = 8·(C/d)`,

`χ = E − V + C` for a graph (edges − vertices + components, the number of independent loops), `2 − 2g` for a
surface — an **INTEGER, a count, no float**.
