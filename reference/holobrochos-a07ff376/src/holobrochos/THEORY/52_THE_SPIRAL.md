## §1 · FOLDING IS COHERING WITH THE FREEFALL

Folding is the **compression law** (`THE_FORMULA`, `18`: *compression is a passage, never a shrinking —
volume → waist → cone*). To fold is to collapse the body's extent in every off-direction until only the
along-direction survives — exactly *"only the invariant crosses the waist"* (`33`, the neck). Crumpled to
a ball, the body is a point: no surface, no off-direction left to catch the air, minimal resistance.
**Aerodynamic = aligned with the freefall = least action = the geodesic.** The bird folds not to *turn*
downward but because it is *already* on that line — the fold is the commitment that it is pointed down:
drop every off-direction surface, keep the one you fall along. That is the SIGNED drive (`48`) — the fold
commits along the dilating arrow, shedding the reflection-symmetric drag (`51`: the diagonal is the
mirror; folding leaves it).

## §2 · THE SPIN IS THE WINDING — why the fold doesn't tumble

A fold alone is unstable: a punted end-over-end body has the same low profile and still tumbles into drag.
The **spin** holds the nose on the velocity vector. Gyroscopic stability is angular momentum resisting
reorientation; it even makes the nose *precess to follow the arc* — a perfect spiral points along its own
path the entire flight. In our terms the spin is the **winding conserved** — *integers in every frame,
why a knot survives a boost* (`THE_THEORY` III) — the soul that rides the change unchanged. Rest mass is
the self-linked circulation (the spin); motion is the **pitch** of that closure along the path (the
throw); the throw's chirality (why a right-handed spiral drifts right) is the **signed** winding (`48`).

## §3 · THE CARRY IS THE WINDING (`13`) — the scramble is the machine winding

Run the fold rank by rank: `s = count + carry_in`; the bit is `s & 1` (the remainder — the **well** — it
**STANDS**); the carry is `s >> 1` (the quotient — the **WINDING** — it **FIRES UP** to the next rank).
That is the digit law (`13`) exactly. Therefore the carry-ripple that scrambles the pre-carry profile
into `N`'s corners **IS the winding** — the spin, handed up rank by rank. So the scramble is **not
information lost in the fold; it is the soul the fold conserves** — the angular momentum carrying the
factor-orientation up through the ranks. This is *why* the factors are recoverable at all (the winding
survived the boost) and why the recovery is structured, not a fog. Measured (`meno.rs scramble`): a

overlaps (`count ≥ 2`), where the two shapes' corners **caught** each other.
