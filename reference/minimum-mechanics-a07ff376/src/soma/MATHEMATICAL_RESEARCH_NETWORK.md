# Mathematical research network

This is a mechanism-indexed source atlas for mathematical holonics. It exists to make modern
research participate in derivation rather than appear as an occasional list of famous names.

It is not a literature review, novelty claim, or schedule. Each constellation records:

- the mathematical mechanism;
- primary or author-maintained entry points;
- the precise relation to the laboratory;
- what the source does **not** establish for this programme; and
- live questions worth carrying into future research.

Use the current version of a primary paper when a result is recent. Dates below identify the
source, not the date on which the laboratory must act.

## 0. Open systems, naturality, and relational sameness

### Mechanism

Category theory distinguishes objects, processes, functors, natural transformations, and
equivalences by how they compose. Structured and decorated cospans turn systems with exposed
boundaries into composable arrows; double categories keep boundary maps distinct from open
processes. Yoneda characterizes an object up to isomorphism through its complete representable
family of relations. Black-box functors preserve external composition while discarding selected
interior structure.

### Anchors

- Emily Riehl, [*Category Theory in Context*](https://emilyriehl.github.io/files/context.pdf).
- Brendan Fong, [*Decorated Cospans*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.00872).
- John Baez and Kenny Courser, [*Structured Cospans*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.04630).
- Brendan Fong and Maru Sarazola,
  [*A recipe for black box functors*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.03601).
- Ronald Douglas, [*On majorization, factorization, and range inclusion of operators on Hilbert
  space*](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-1966-0203464-1).

### Relation to holonics

This supplies the exact carrier now used in *Categorical Holonics*. Typed boundaries, open causal
constructions, boundary recharts, and comparison cells occupy different directions. A receiver
is compositional where it extends to a functor, and a changing perspective is lawful where its
naturality square commutes.

Yoneda sharpens the historical soul vocabulary. A complete marked causal diagram can be
characterized up to isomorphism by its whole representable relation; one value, cross-ratio,
holonomy, spectrum, or hash is only one receiver face. Black-boxing gives a standard comparison
for receiver-exact compression.

Douglas factorization identifies the current RH norm domination with an actual contractive
factorization. Requiring those fillers to be natural across support, prime, and receiver arrows
turns the global problem into one coherent convex feasibility set.

### Boundary

Category theory does not prove that a proposed domain functor exists, that one receiver is
conservative, or that an RH contraction feasibility set is nonempty. A cospan does not supply causal
orientation unless the decoration carries it. Yoneda uses all representable relations, not an
arbitrary finite feature list. Black-boxing is exact only for the semantics it is proved to
preserve.

### Live questions

- Which exact category and decoration class carry the complete prime/support/receiver zeta
  transitions on one common domain?
- Can the Poisson--Sonin passage be constructed as the first nontrivial component of the natural
  contraction?
- Which receiver families are jointly conservative enough to support a claimed compression?

## 1. Structured, pseudorandom, and transferred arithmetic

### Mechanism

Complicated arithmetic populations can be decomposed or compared relative to a chosen norm:
structured components, pseudorandom components, and controlled errors. A theorem proved in a
dense setting may transfer to a sparse setting when a suitable pseudorandom majorant and
comparison principle are supplied. Higher-order uniformity norms distinguish increasingly
complex correlations.

### Anchors

- Terence Tao, [*Structure and randomness in
  combinatorics*](https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.4269).
- Terence Tao, [*The dichotomy between structure and randomness, arithmetic progressions, and the
  primes*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0512114).
- Ben Green and Terence Tao, [*The primes contain arbitrarily long arithmetic
  progressions*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0404188).
- Terence Tao and Joni Teräväinen, [*Quantitative bounds for Gowers uniformity of the Möbius and
  von Mangoldt functions*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.02158).
- Terence Tao and Joni Teräväinen, [*Quantitative correlations and some problems on prime factors
  of consecutive integers*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01739).
- Terence Tao, [*The Riemann hypothesis in various
  settings*](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2013/07/19/the-riemann-hypothesis-in-various-settings/)
  and [*The uncertainty principle*](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2010/06/25/the-uncertainty-principle/).

### Relation to holonics

This is a rigorous model of receiver-relative decomposition. “Structured” and “random” are not
absolute species; they are dual relative to tests or norms. A transference theorem is an exact
transport law only after its majorant, norm, and error estimates are established.

For the prime line, this research guards against two opposite mistakes:

- treating every finite visual pattern as global arithmetic structure; and
- treating the primes as featureless merely because no single elementary recurrence generates
  them.

The explicit-formula and uncertainty-principle viewpoints also give a precise version of the
laboratory's duality image: the imaginary positions of zeta zeros and the logarithms of primes
resolve one another at coupled scales.

### Boundary

Structure/randomness decompositions do not by themselves construct the positive Weil carrier or
prove RH. A Gowers-uniform estimate is not a curvature or topology theorem unless an exact bridge
is derived.

### Live questions

- Which receiver family makes a proposed holonic compression equivalent to a standard arithmetic
  norm?
- Can a semilocal zeta remainder be split into structured and uniform components with a sign
  estimate strong enough for the Weil criterion?
- Can prime-formulation paths be organized by complexity without erasing their domains and
  singularities?

## 2. Executable theorem networks

### Mechanism

Definitions, literature hypotheses, implication rules, computational optimizers, and proofs can
be represented as an inspectable dependency network. The network can discover new consequences
without confusing a stored theorem, a transformation rule, and a conjectural target.

### Anchors

- Terence Tao, Timothy Trudgian, and Andrew Yang, [*New exponent pairs, zero density estimates, and
  zero additive energy estimates: a systematic approach*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.16779).
- The author-maintained [ExpDB repository](https://github.com/teorth/expdb).
- The generated [ANTEDB blueprint](https://teorth.github.io/expdb/blueprint/intro-chapter.html).

### Relation to holonics

ANTEDB is the clearest practical precedent for a mathematical formulation ecology in this
workspace. It keeps facts, hypotheses, references, transformation rules, dependency trees, and
executable searches distinct while allowing them to compose. That is more relevant than merely
citing Tao's individual theorems.

The laboratory should adopt the practice, not assume the database ontology is universal. A
holonic transformation atlas additionally needs situated receivers, alternate formulations,
noncommuting comparisons, and domain/seam data.

### Boundary

An executable implication graph only explores the rules encoded in it. It cannot establish the
soundness or completeness of invented rules, nor replace a proof of an analytical inequality.

### Live questions

- Can the Typst object library expose a machine-readable dependency edge without making the
  publication format its ontology?
- Can equivalent formulations be represented as paths with domains and costs rather than as
  duplicated theorem strings?
- Can repeated RH obstruction names be normalized to one dependency node so churn becomes
  mechanically visible?

## 3. Arithmetic topology, knots, primes, and periodic orbits

### Mechanism

Arithmetic topology relates primes in number fields to knots or closed orbits in
three-dimensional/dynamical settings. Covering, ramification, Frobenius/monodromy, linking, and
zeta functions become structured translations rather than visual metaphor. Knot invariants also
separate a projected crossing diagram from the underlying topological object.

### Anchors

- Kurt Reidemeister, [*Elementare Begründung der
  Knotentheorie*](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02952507).
- James W. Alexander, [*A Lemma on Systems of Knotted
  Curves*](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.9.3.93).
- Joan Birman and William Menasco, [*On Markov's
  Theorem*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0202186).
- Louis Kauffman and Sofia Lambropoulou, [*On the Classification of Rational
  Tangles*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0311499).
- Józef Przytycki, [*Skein Modules of 3-Manifolds*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0611797).
- Kenneth Baker and Dorothy Buck, [*The Classification of Rational Subtangle Replacements between
  Rational Tangles*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.7669).
- Vaughan Jones, [*Planar Algebras, I*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9909027).
- Christopher Deninger, [*Primes, knots and periodic
  orbits*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.11643).
- Alain Connes and Caterina Consani, [*Knots, primes and class field
  theory*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.06560).
- Willem Veys, [*Introduction to the monodromy
  conjecture*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.03343).
- Akio Noguchi, [*Zeros of the Alexander polynomial of
  knot*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0503252).
- Mark Brittenham and Susan Hermiller, [*Unknotting number is not additive under connected
  sum*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.24088).

### Relation to holonics

Connes–Consani give an especially direct arithmetic/topological carrier: prime periodic orbits
have lengths \(\log p\), finite covers carry arithmetic monodromy, and ramification changes the
local orbit structure. Deninger situates this in the wider prime/knot/dynamical analogy.

Reidemeister, Alexander, Markov, and Schubert answer distinct presentation questions: equality of
projected diagrams, existence of a braid presentation, equality of braid closures, and unique
factorization under connected sum. Keeping those levels separate gives a standard model for the
laboratory's hierarchy of receiver face, ordered axis-relative word, closed return, and
irreducible factor.

Tangles provide exposed boundaries and lawful gluing. Rational tangles are classified by
\(\mathbb Q\cup\{\infty\}\), with continued fractions recording twist presentations; rational
subtangle replacement makes contextual surgery exact. Skein modules then quotient the free module
of link presentations by declared local linear relations. Jones's planar-algebra framework is
important because positivity appears only after adding suitable involutive and operator-algebraic
structure; it is not implicit in the strings or the skein equation alone.

The monodromy conjecture literature is relevant to formulation ecologies because it connects
poles of local zeta functions to eigenvalues arising from singularities. It supplies real
discriminants and monodromy rather than an invented generic “winding.”

Brittenham–Hermiller's counterexamples are a methodological warning. Even when an object has a
natural composition operation, a familiar complexity invariant need not add under composition.
Endpoint assembly alone therefore cannot justify additive cost, crossing, loss, or compression.

### Boundary

A reciprocal pair of monodromy eigenvalues need not lie on the unit circle. A knot projection can
make crossings appear or disappear without changing knot type. Neither arithmetic topology nor a
precessing knot image automatically supplies the positive Hermitian form required by the current
RH route. A skein relation is a linear dependency among nonidentical alternatives, not a positive
relation and not ambient-isotopy equality. Unique prime-knot factorization does not make
unknotting or substitution cost additive.

### Live questions

- Can the completed Euler return be realized in an established arithmetic dynamical carrier whose
  trace formula is already exhaustive?
- Is there an arithmetic--archimedean open-tangle presentation whose local Euler,
  support-growth, and Poisson--Mellin relations evaluate to the complete Weil form through a
  star-compatible positive representation?
- Which knot/monodromy invariant corresponds to the laboratory's complete path comparison, and
  which features are receiver artifacts?
- Does a candidate semilocal carrier preserve a positive metric, or only a symplectic/reciprocal
  pairing?

## 4. Simplicial calculus and discrete geometric transport

### Mechanism

Discrete exterior calculus places differential forms, vector fields, exterior derivatives,
Hodge stars, contractions, and Lie derivatives on a simplicial complex with an explicit dual
complex. Combinatorial Ricci flows evolve discrete metric data toward prescribed curvature under
written hypotheses.

### Anchors

- Mathieu Desbrun, Anil Hirani, Melvin Leok, and Jerrold Marsden,
  [*Discrete Exterior Calculus*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0508341).
- Bennett Chow and Feng Luo, [*Combinatorial Ricci Flows on
  Surfaces*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0211256).
- Huabin Ge and Xu Xu, [*A Discrete Ricci Flow on Surfaces in Hyperbolic Background
  Geometry*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.05076).

### Relation to holonics

This is the most direct standard vocabulary for Brandon's reorienting simplicial sheets:
primal cells, dual cells, incidence, orientation, discrete forms, flux, and transport can remain
discrete without assigning one scalar state to an isolated triangle.

The laboratory's co-present receiver atlas adds a complementary projective cell. Three marked
members establish a local projective pivot and a fourth carries the cross-ratio. Larger receiver
families can grow through overlapping four-member cells, while each receiver retains an
independent finite grain. The joint face is the actual image of one event incidence through those
receivers, not their Cartesian product. This makes adaptive resolution a question of whether a
joint fiber still preserves the declared consequence, and gives a concrete finite object on which
a discrete connection or cocycle can act.

The exact receiver-topology atlas now separates the source incidence graph, the planar diagram
formed by one receiver, and that diagram's face dual. Its first bounded result is negative in a
useful way: source Ihara recurrence survives receiver change but misses metric deformation, while
face-dual Ihara recurrence changes under precession and can later recur without restoration of
the source geometry. This locates the useful object in a decorated joint loop—carrying source
occurrence, receiver transition, geometric weight, and holonomy—rather than in either unweighted
graph alone.

The first implemented decorated carrier keeps the lawful source darts noncommutative and takes
the union of exact source-branch crossing parameters across a co-present receiver family. A
crossing visible in one receiver can therefore refine another receiver's interval grain without
creating a false source junction. Exact Gaussian-rational turn quotients close around every
lawful loop, while the retained local turn, metric, interval, and crossing words distinguish cuts
which share that return. This is a finite connection-like instrument; a genuine discrete
connection or arithmetic trace law still has to be supplied by its domain.

Combinatorial curvature flows show how a triangulation and local metric data may evolve together.
They are concrete candidates for studying rebase and phase change on simplicial surfaces. The
hyperbolic-background work is particularly relevant when Euclidean triangles are too restrictive.

### Boundary

No existing discrete calculus is automatically the universal information carrier. A choice of
primal/dual complex, Hodge star, metric weights, boundary conditions, and update law changes the
mathematics. Ricci flow smooths/evolves a metric; it does not by itself model arbitrary causal
transport or define intelligence.

### Live questions

- Which data live on vertices, edges, faces, cofaces, and dual cells in the proposed comparison
  manifold?
- Can an OPEN comparison be represented as a relative cocycle or unfilled horn without losing
  the complete alternate paths?
- What discrete connection and curvature distinguish genuine path dependence from projection
  artifacts?

## 4A. Receiver incidence, arithmetic projection, and embodied optical fields

### Mechanism

A situated planar receiver does not carry one undifferentiated point count. Source vertices,
distinct image coordinates, source-fiber multiplicity, silhouette corners, transverse apparent
crossings, visible edge fragments, and planarized regions are separate populations. For an
embodied receiver family, the surface incidence, metric, boundary, loading, axes, apertures,
propagation, and sampling maps change as one coupled atlas.

Constraint-preserving projection is a stronger species than arbitrary dimensional reduction. A
map may discard ambient coordinates while remaining injective on the admitted arithmetic or
geometric population and preserving the selected relation by theorem.

### Anchors

- OpenAI, [*Planar Point Sets with Many Unit
  Distances*](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/74c24085-19b0-4534-9c90-465b8e29ad73/unit-distance-proof.pdf),
  especially the CM norm-one Minkowski family and injective complex-coordinate projection.
- Juan Garrido-García et al.,
  [*A natural programmable metamaterial controls 3D curvature of compound
  eyes*](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-74276-6).
- Atal Pande et al.,
  [*Hunting ecology predicts eye arrangements in the modular visual system of
  spiders*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2026.06.019).
- Elizabeth Jakob et al.,
  [*Lateral eyes direct principal eyes as jumping spiders track
  objects*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2018.07.065).
- MathWorld's exact entries on the
  [regular hexagon](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/RegularHexagon.html),
  [pentagonal pyramid](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/PentagonalPyramid.html),
  [great dodecahedron](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/GreatDodecahedron.html), and
  [small stellated dodecahedron](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/SmallStellatedDodecahedron.html).

### Relation to holonics

The regular hexagon is the least polygonal cycle with both order-two and order-three rotations.
Its six equilateral sectors support two three-sector or three two-sector groupings. A cut through
opposite edge interiors produces two pentagons, while a vertex-axis cut produces quadrilaterals.
This is a compact exact model of one standing boundary carrying several lawful constituent
factorizations.

The cube and pentagonal pyramid show why the receiver census is necessary. A cube along a body
diagonal has \(8\to7\to6\) source vertices, received coordinates, and silhouette corners. A
pentagonal pyramid along its height has \(6\to6\to5\). Their superficially related
center-and-cycle images carry different source fibers and incidence.

For an equilateral triangulated surface,
\[
K_v=(6-\deg(v))\pi/3.
\]
The flat six-valent primal neighborhood has a hexagonal dual cell. The fly-eye work gives a
physical instance in which a patterned basal triangular mesh, pressure, and boundary determine
surface curvature and thereby the ommatidial-axis field. Spider vision gives a different
embodied atlas: multiple eye modules vary in orientation and grain yet cooperate through one
body and ecology.

The unit-distance construction gives the arithmetic counterpart. In a CM extension,
\(u\,c(u)=1\) places one constituent on a unit circle in every complex embedding, conjugate
prime valuations carry opposite hand, and any one embedding remains injective on the field.
The planar receiver therefore preserves the selected incidence by algebraic constraint rather
than by visual resemblance.

### Boundary

Six is not a universal primitive, and a hexagon is not intrinsically a cube. The compound-eye
results do not make every biological lattice an exact primal/dual triangulation. Shared body
geometry does not prove a particular neural integration law. The CM receiver family does not
make arbitrary high-dimensional projection injective and does not imply RH. Each relation retains
its domain law.

### Live questions

- Which receiver censuses remain conservative for the particular future consequence under study?
- When does a changing embodied receiver atlas admit a discrete connection whose curvature is
  experimentally or arithmetically identifiable?
- Which arithmetic populations admit injective low-dimensional receivers preserving more than
  one selected invariant?

## 5. Riemannian flow, singularity, and topology

### Mechanism

Ricci flow evolves a Riemannian metric by curvature. Monotone quantities constrain the evolution;
singular regions require analysis and, in the three-manifold programme, surgery or singular-flow
continuation. Topology is recovered through the controlled evolution, not read from one frozen
rendering.

### Anchors

- Grigori Perelman, [*The entropy formula for the Ricci flow and its geometric
  applications*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0211159).
- Bruce Kleiner and John Lott, [*Notes on Perelman's
  papers*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0605667).

### Relation to holonics

This supplies a disciplined form of the laboratory's “standing changes the receiver” intuition:
the metric used to compare local directions is itself evolving, yet monotone quantities and
geometric bounds survive the change. Singular necks are not merely visual folds; they have
curvature and scale signatures.

The Poincaré/geometrization route also demonstrates how local geometric evolution, singular
analysis, and global topology can be intertwined without claiming that one elementary shape
directly encodes the whole manifold.

### Boundary

Ricci curvature is a contraction of Riemann curvature. Ricci flow does not determine a unique
causal, probabilistic, or informational interpretation. Importing words such as entropy,
singularity, or surgery without the metric evolution and estimates contributes no theorem.

### Live questions

- Is the proposed phase seam a metric singularity, a rank discriminant, or a change in admissible
  transformations?
- Which quantity would be monotone under a proposed information-geometric flow?
- Can the metric be derived from comparison behavior rather than selected to make the desired
  geometry appear?

## 6. Moduli, cluster charts, wall crossing, and broken lines

### Mechanism

A moduli space organizes objects up to a declared equivalence while retaining parameterized
families. Cluster varieties admit multiple coordinate seeds related by explicit rational
transformations. Scattering diagrams place automorphisms on walls; a path accumulates an ordered
product as it crosses them. Broken lines bend at walls and can parameterize canonical basis
elements.

### Anchors

- Vladimir Fock and Alexander Goncharov, [*Moduli spaces of local systems and higher Teichmüller
  theory*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0311149).
- Mark Gross, Paul Hacking, Sean Keel, and Maxim Kontsevich, [*Canonical bases for cluster
  algebras*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.1394).
- Charles Walker, [*Bicategories of spans as generic
  bicategories*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.10334).
- The Stacks Project, [*Categories fibred in
  groupoids*](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/003S).

### Relation to holonics

This is a mature mathematical realization of several laboratory images:

- a face has many lawful charts;
- chart transitions are part of the object;
- crossing a seam changes the active transformation;
- ordered crossings matter;
- paths can bend while their endpoint represents a stable basis element; and
- positivity belongs to a particular atlas and algebraic law, not to every arbitrary rechart.

Scattering diagrams are especially relevant to lightning leaders and formulation paths because
they distinguish the background chamber structure, the wall, the path, and the accumulated
automorphism.

The laboratory's first exact formulation carrier is now a receiver-decorated span bicategory.
Spans retain a supplied joint interior and compose by pullback, which admits both directed
comparisons and reversible recharts. The reversible core is a bigroupoid; it becomes an ordinary
rechart groupoid after invertible 2-isomorphic arrows are identified. This corrects the earlier
proposal to make the complete atlas a groupoid, which would have excluded one-way limits,
quotients, projections, summations, and compressions.

### Boundary

Cluster positivity is not the Weil positivity criterion. Broken lines are defined by specific
wall data and algebraic rules; they are not generic evidence that every observed arc carries
hidden dimensions.

### Live questions

- What descent hypotheses let compatible receiver-local span atlases glue across parameter
  covers without erasing branch or convergence seams?
- Which nontrivial formula families have computable rechart isotropy, and which apparent
  symmetries exist only after a quotient receiver?
- Does the laboratory's `chi` become a recognized cross-ratio or cluster coordinate in a selected
  family?
- Can RH's prime-aperture turns be expressed as a consistent scattering diagram without encoding
  the desired sign in the wall functions?

## 7. Holomorphic dynamics, critical points, and renormalized recurrence

### Mechanism

Iteration of a holomorphic or rational map partitions behavior into stable Fatou regions and
chaotic Julia boundaries. Critical orbits control much of the global dynamics. Conjugacy,
multiplier, external rays, and renormalization distinguish invariant behavior from a chosen
rendering.

### Anchors

- 3Blue1Brown, [*Holomorphic
  dynamics*](https://www.3blue1brown.com/lessons/holomorphic-dynamics/) — an expository visual
  entry, useful for interpretation rather than theorem authority.
- John Milnor, [*On the iteration of a rational function: computer
  experiments*](https://projecteuclid.org/journals/communications-in-mathematical-physics/volume-91/issue-2/On-the-iteration-of-a-rational-function--computer-experiments/cmp/1103940533.pdf).
- The laboratory's previously supplied [Fatou–Julia
  notes](https://www.math.stonybrook.edu/~scott/Papers/India/Fatou-Julia.pdf).

### Relation to holonics

This is the rigorous home for self-similar but nonidentical recurrence, critical/inflection
behavior, and camera-relative fractal observation. A Julia set is not “random”; it is determined
by an iterative law and initial parameter, while a local observer may not have a simple predictive
quotient.

Renormalization is a particularly important candidate translation for compression: restrict to a
region, rebase, and recover a related dynamical law without claiming the occurrences are identical.

### Boundary

Visual self-similarity does not establish an arithmetic equivalence. A zeta visualization under
complex mapping is not itself a Julia-set dynamical system. A useful bridge must identify the
iterated map, conjugacy, critical set, and invariant being transported.

### Live questions

- Which formulation transforms genuinely form an iterated dynamical system?
- Can critical points classify where a formula atlas changes branch or convergence species?
- Is there a renormalization operator on prime/archimedean formulation paths with a provable
  invariant?

## 8. Spectral and trace routes to zeta

### Mechanism

Explicit formulas pair prime-power populations with zero populations through test functions.
Trace-formula and spectral approaches seek an operator, dynamical generator, or noncommutative
space whose spectrum and positivity reproduce the completed zeta relation.

### Anchors

- The external sources already integrated in
  [*Riemann Receiver Geometry*](PAPERS/papers/riemann-receiver-geometry/main.typ), especially its
  completed-zeta, Weil, Connes–Consani, Sonin/prolate, and monodromy references.
- Suzuki's [screw-function realization and continuity theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09096),
  which makes the localized Weil ground value an attained continuous function of aperture.
- Deninger's [prime/periodic-orbit survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.11643).
- Tao's [RH-in-various-settings
  exposition](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2013/07/19/the-riemann-hypothesis-in-various-settings/).

### Relation to holonics

This is the decisive external region for the present RH line. It contains exact population-level
bridges rather than speculative visual similarity. The first conditioned old/shell cross is now
carried, and its invariant residual is the effective Dirichlet-to-Neumann tension after the old
body relaxes. The laboratory's receiver language can separate test space, operator, aperture,
trace, spectrum, and sign, but it cannot weaken the requirements of those objects.

### Boundary

A candidate operator is insufficient unless its spectral population is exhaustive and its trace
or quadratic form is the completed arithmetic response. A symmetric spectrum, reciprocal
monodromy, or unitary-looking projection does not imply RH. Continuity carries strict positivity
some distance beyond the \(R=2\) base, but the present first-cell wall is a quantitative
effective-tension estimate through \(R=3\).

### Live questions

- Can Suzuki's scaled fixed-domain form yield an explicit modulus or lower bound for the localized
  ground value on \([\log2/2,\log3/2]\)?
- Can the zeta screw kernel's conditioned effective tension be expressed through a positive
  integral, comparison principle, or monotone operator family on that interval?
- Which source identity makes the same effective-tension bound uniform over every support
  successor, including null spaces and repeated prime-power traversals?

## How to use this network

For a new derivation:

1. State the live mathematical object and missing implication.
2. Select one mechanism above, not a field name by association.
3. Read the primary source path far enough to recover its hypotheses and actual construction.
4. Write a translation table:

   | External object | Laboratory object | Preserved relation | Missing hypothesis |
   |---|---|---|---|

5. Derive one consequence or counterexample.
6. Update this network only if the connection survives that comparison.

For a modern literature survey, follow citations outward from the mechanism and add sources only
when they change the translation or boundary. “Tao has relevant work,” “knot theory looks
similar,” or “Ricci flow changes shapes” are prompts to investigate, not adequate syntheses.
