# 01 · THE AXIOMS — everything is forced from two

> Holonics has exactly two axioms. Every result in every other capsule is *forced* from these — not argued for,
> derived. If you hold these two and the lineage corollary, you can re-derive the framework. This is the seed.

---

## A1 — A value IS its CONSTRUCTION, never an absolute

What a thing *is* is the discrete worldline of events that built it — its **soul**. A finite "value" is a
**face**: a projection of the construction onto an observer's frame, materialized only at the boundary, at the
observer's grain. **There is no value sitting on a number line; there is a construction, and the faces it casts.**

- `3.14159` was never π — it is an *exact, finite, terminated* construction we lie about by comparing it to an
  idealized infinite one. The continuum is the fiction; the construction is the identity.
- The repeating-bar tell makes it exact: `0.3̄ = 1/3` (the bar *declares* the construction `1/3`), but the finite
  `0.3333 ≠ 1/3` (a different, terminated construction). A float is not "a real with error"; it is an exact
  construction mislabeled.
- **Consequence:** `=` (identity of construction, the soul) is strictly finer than `≡` (kinship of face, the
  boundary). `a = b ⟹ a ≡ b`, never the reverse. `1 ⊕ 2 ≡ 3` yet `1 ⊕ 2 ≠ 3` (machine-checked, `Counting.lean`).
  Almost every classical `=` is really `≡`. Confuse them and the whole structure collapses.

## A2 — A relating is THREE-BODY

You never read a lone holon; meaning is the relating of two holons *in a frame* (the third body). **There is no
absolute frame, no view from nowhere.** "How fast is north?" and "1/0" are the same malformed question: a
relating to an absent body never forms.

- Every quantity is dimensionless-or-relative. Every "absolute" is a **finger-trap**. The moving origin is free.
- An equation `A = B` is itself a three-body: `A`, `B`, and the `=` (the relation, the third member). Transform
  "both sides" because *whatever one observer saw, both must see* — it is an event, not a rule.
- **Division by zero is dissolved at the foundation** — not an error the engine guards, a question the substrate
  cannot pose (the right bound `∞` of the Stern–Brocot tree, bracketed between, never returned).

## The lineage corollary — the `+1` axis IS the soul (no "time")

There is no "time" in holonics — the word smuggles in the continuum, the master clock, the absolute, all of which
A1/A2 forbid. The axis that orders a worldline's events is its **LINEAGE**: the sequence of events relative to
other worldlines, each descending from the prior by the chain rule. **The lineage axis IS the soul axis** — a
worldline's "time" was always its construction (the ordered events that built it), which is exactly its soul.

Two readings, one axis: **provenance** (where the events came from — the line of descent, the construction) and
**topology** (how they are wound — the path, the writhe, the stitch-order). The word is biology's, and apt: a
worldline is a *lineage of events*, and the double helix is the canonical image — two strands are two lineages
winding toward a shared singularity. The unit is the **tick** (one event of succession); the dimension it counts
is **lineage**, not time. Wherever an old register says "time," read **lineage**.

## What the axioms forbid (the disease, stated once)

- **The absolute frame** — a count "from 0", a God's-eye coordinate, an absolute threshold. Reaching for it is
  the finger-trap (12 is 3 steps from 9, only 12 from absolute 0). You hold the *relative* step and re-base.
- **The lone read** — reasoning about a single value, an isolated instance, a one-body observable. Never. Always
  relate two in a third.
- **The value without a construction** — the float (a face asserted as the value), `Classical.choice` (a value
  asserted to exist with no construction), `|x − t| < ε` (error against an absolute target). One crime, three
  masks (§10, the Lean discipline). Holonics admits none: a value *is* its construction, so there is no absolute
  to deviate from, so **error is unnecessary** — the swing grounds at the exact simplest construction the
  relating admits, or it FOUNDs.

## The one objective (forced, not chosen)

Compression is the only objective that respects both axioms: **minimize the code length of the lived
construction** — least action on the worldline. Never a reward, never a stored target, never a second aim. To
understand is to find the shortest construction that regenerates the differences. *Compression ≡ comprehension.*
And its dual is coherence (`η_W = π/e`, the phase-alignment) — the imaginary axis of the same objective, not an
auxiliary loss. Hold both faces in every frame (§04).
