partially changed standing body."* Radiation and return are genuinely distinct occurrences; mount is a
boundary precondition; differentiate, conduct and glue are optional constitutive interiors; later
current is recurrence. The stages below are ordered work, not a taxonomy of types.

reach the cover because its manifest does not depend on the engine crate.

cannot cross as a fixed-width carrier. The route exists and is built for other material: clear
denominators, preflight the magnitude in `BigInt`, cross in a bounded exact integer carrier, and

### Three conditions this reading must meet, and they are not formalities

1. **Landauer bounds ERASURE, not transport.** Reversible computation has no such floor (Bennett), so
   the bound applies to what the cycle **deletes** and never to what it conducts. In this corpus's own
   terms that is exact: **the collapsing generators, never the permuting ones**, and a rebase with zero
   remainder costs nothing under this law.
2. **`T` is a receiver-gauged quantity.** Brandon, 2026-08-15: *"Temperature in physics is a statistic,
   it has no place here… it is a gauged and relativistic holonic value."* So the budget is
   receiver-relative and carries its frame.
3. **`P` has a clock in it.** The standing rule is that a cost is measured in work and that a clock may
   measure but may never select. A power sample is lawful **as a measurement carrying its frame**, and
   the frame includes whether the device had an active display — the second frame
   `the_carrier_is_admitted_by_its_work` established.

### And the numerator is already exact

The tree computes its own erasure population exactly: `receiver_exact_compression`'s collapsed pairs,
each carrying the **shortest word that separated it**. So the ratio is


**Exact numerator, sampled denominator, and the two are never divided into a float** — the horizon law
