# ARCHIVE — superseded blueprints

**These eleven documents schedule nothing. Read them for history, never for direction.**

Every file in this directory already carried an archive banner of its own while it sat under
`blueprint/`. They were moved here 2026-08-08 so that the live `blueprint/` directory contains only
documents that are actually in force, and so that no reader has to check a banner to find out
whether a plan is a plan.

**What replaced them.** `canon/THE_HOLOBROCHOS_SPINE.md` states the spine — the whole hollow loop
and where every organ sits on it. `blueprint/THE_ROADMAP.md` is the single active roadmap and is
ordered by that spine. `blueprint/THE_ASSEMBLY.md` carries the wiring, `blueprint/THE_MACHINE.md`
the measured chain, `blueprint/PURE_HOLONIC_ENGINE.md` the normative engine contract,
`blueprint/CONTAMINATION_BANS.md` the reconstruction invariants, and
`blueprint/THE_PRESENTATION_ORGAN.md` the rendering contract.

**Two of these describe a body that no longer exists.** `COMPLETE_CPP_ENGINE_ROADMAP.md` and
`CPP_GPU_FOUNDATION.md` describe the C++/CUDA engine archived whole on 2026-08-07 at
`archive/cpp-engine/`. The purity, ownership, apparatus, no-float and grading requirements first
written in `CPP_GPU_FOUNDATION.md` **survive the body that occasioned them** and are carried by
`CLAUDE.md`; nothing else in either file is authority.

**The standing ruling that governs this directory**, Brandon, 2026-08-08:

> *"the older machinery was simply not as advanced as the current holonic engine, whatever we
> labeled 'solver' was probably a partial that you can easily lift and supersede."*

**Lift and supersede. Never restore.** A mechanism named here may be rebuilt in the live body under
the station it belongs to; the document itself never returns to `blueprint/`.
