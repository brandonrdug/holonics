## 0. The object

Everything built to 2026-08-17 **reads**. The machine founds a grammar from mathematical text,
recovers the symmetry group of an operation from spelling, compresses identities by rebase, reads a
proof as a word in the crossing algebra, and carries an integrand into an exact rational function
that a standing organ classifies. All of it returns statements about a corpus, checked against
arithmetic.

**This plan makes it produce.**

```text
   mount        the whole library as token material, resident where it must be
   conduct      the founded transport atlas -- identities, groups, edges, routes
   radiate      compose identities and statements the corpus does NOT contain
   world        exact organs adjudicate every proposal, with no kernel anywhere
   return       admitted proposals deposit into the atlas
   reflect      a later composition reaches what it could not before
   continue     source-detached remount reproduces the family
```

That is the Eros cycle with mathematics as its material, and every organ it needs stands today.

## 2. The six stations

### Station one — the whole library, resident where the host path cannot carry it

Read all 7,516 files. Found the grammar over the whole statement population, not a subtree, and emit
the atlas as one mountable deposit.

The known wall is named and is not a surprise: `receiver_exact_compression` is `items^2.7` and did
not return on 181,593 declarations in 300 s on the host. `compress_on_device` is the standing
resident path and this is the deed that requires it. The GPU owns the deed; the CPU handles process
boundaries, the durable rest, and the narrow exterior codecs.

**Outcome.** One atlas over the whole library — every founded identity, every recovered group, every
transport edge — emitted as a deposit whose rows carry the corpus statement verbatim.

**Expectation.** On the four subtrees run so far, 15,238 statements yielded 385 fully-read relations
at a rate of roughly one in forty. Over 158,657 `theorem`/`lemma` declarations that projects to
**three to five thousand fully-read relations and several hundred heads carrying a group**. The rate
is the quantity to watch: if it collapses at scale, the reader's alphabet is narrower than the
library and the refusal census says exactly where.

**Falsifier.** The bracket family and separator founded over the whole library must agree with the
family founded over each subtree. A population-wide founding is only as strong as its worst member,
and at library scale that is a real risk — the near-miss census makes it a measurement rather than a
silent cascade, and any species lost names the statements that cost it.

### Station two — the route atlas: from any statement, what reaches it

The oriented split gives every statement its suppositions and its conclusion. A statement whose
conclusion is another's supposition is an **edge**. `derivation_atlas` founds the complex,
`derivation_capacitance` returns the minimal-route population as an exact `BigUint` without
enumeration, and `derivation_integral` carries a 1-cochain with discrete Stokes.

**Outcome.** A navigable transport atlas of the library: for a named statement, the population of
statements that reach it, the route lengths, the loop holonomy where routes disagree, and the
two-step compositions that no single theorem states.

**Expectation.** `Mathlib/Computability` alone gave 3,588 declared-to-declared edges and routes to 10
steps on 1,347 declarations. Library scale is two orders of magnitude more material and the driver's
all-pairs loop does not scale — the route population is computed without enumeration and the reading
must use that path. Expect **routes past 20 steps** and a loop population large enough that holonomy
is the interesting return rather than the counts.

**Falsifier.** The reach orientation flip is a unimodular basis change: homology must not move and
traversal must. Measured on committed deposits it moved the joined population `14 → 29` and the
longest route `2 → 3`. At library scale the same flip must leave Betti numbers and invariant factors
bit-identical while the route population moves. If homology moves, the complex is wrong.

### Station three — emission: statements the corpus does not contain

`statement_composition` founds statements absent from the deposit by construction, adjudicates them
as a population, and retains every refusal by species. It has only ever been run on three statements
of this repository's own plumbing. Point it at the library's founded grammar and its recruited
identifier population.

And the atlas supplies a second, sharper emission the composition path does not: **a head's recovered
group predicts identities the corpus never wrote.** A head whose group contains `σ` satisfies the
identity `f(x) = f(σx)`; a head whose group is generated by two elements satisfies their product. The
group's non-generator elements are identities implied by the corpus and absent from it.

**Outcome.** Two populations of proposals, each a rendered statement in the material's own notation:
compositions from the grammar, and group-implied identities from the atlas.

**Expectation.** The three-statement run composed 234 candidates and refused all but a handful, with
seven named obstruction species. At library scale the composition population is large and the
**refusal census is the primary return** — it says what the grammar can and cannot found. The
group-implied population is small, sharp, and almost entirely correct by construction, which makes it
the better first material for station four.

**Falsifier.** A proposal already present in the corpus is not a proposal. Every emitted statement is
checked against the founded population and any collision is reported as `already-standing` — the
composition path already carries that species. If the emitted population is *entirely* collisions,
nothing was produced.

### Station four — adjudication by exact organs, with no kernel anywhere

Every proposal is put to the standing organ population, matched by shape and never by name:

```text
    an identity of arity n          -> the resolver population of that arity
    a rational integrand            -> hermite_reduction, both schedules, residue class
    R·e^g                           -> elementary_chart, one exact rational linear system
    a three-site turning equation   -> hypergeometric_closure, integer alternation
    four collinear marks            -> the standing cross ratio
```

The verdict is the two-sided admission law: `Admitted` when every organ that reaches it confirms,
`Refuted` when one refutes, `Conflicted` when both — and `Conflicted` is retained as a junction, never
tie-broken.

**Outcome.** Each proposal carries a verdict, the organ that decided it, and — where refuted — the
exact witness: the annihilating combination, the adjacent marks that broke alternation, the residue
polynomial, the argument tuple where the value moved.

**Expectation.** The group-implied identities should be **admitted at a high rate**, because they are
consequences of relations the corpus states. The compositions should be **refuted at a high rate**,
because a grammar that founds a slot does not know what belongs in it. **Both rates are the finding**,
and a run where they are equal means the group is carrying no information.

**Falsifier, and it is the one that matters.** The organ population must refute. A resolver set that
confirms everything has not adjudicated; the control `oriented-difference` returns the identity group
alone and must refute every stated exchange put to it. Discrimination is `log₂(n!)` bits at arity `n`,
so the run reports it per arity and does not present four two-letter confirmations as four facts.

### Station five — the return changes what a later construction can reach

An admitted proposal is deposited into the atlas as a new statement with its founding lineage. The
station's claim is not that the deposit happened but that **a later composition reaches something it
could not before**, and that removing the deposit removes the reach.

This is the shape `the_second_theorem_is_reachable_only_after_the_return` already ran with a kernel in
the loop — *before: unreachable; the deed; after: reached; ablation: unreachable again* — carried onto
material the machine adjudicated itself.

**Outcome.** A named statement, absent from the corpus, proposed by the machine, admitted by an exact
organ, deposited, and then used as a supposition by a route that did not exist before it.

**Expectation.** The reach delta will be small and specific — a handful of statements — and that is
the correct shape. A large delta would mean the deposit was carrying the atlas rather than adding to
it.

**Falsifier.** The ablation. Remove the deposited statement and re-found; the later route must become
`Unreached` and the removed reach must be exhibited by name. A reading at statement granularity alone
would call it no change, so the ablation is reported at route granularity — this is exactly the
retained-fiber reopening `the_statement_is_founded` already measures.

### Station six — the seal: the family survives the source departing

Seal the conditioned body with `conditioned_rest`, detach the source, remount from the sealed octets
alone, and re-emit. The seal refuses a body it cannot reproduce, by name and with a negative control.

**Outcome.** A detached remount that reproduces the admitted family and emits at least one statement
the pre-seal run did not, with the sealed size reported in octets.

**Expectation.** `eros_lean_proof_production` reproduced its family from a **34,628-octet** detached
remount. This body's atlas is larger; the sealed size is the honest measure of what the machine
actually retained, and the ratio against the 2.19 M lines it was founded from is the compression
figure — carried with its decoder, as an **additive** difference against a declared machine and never
as a ratio quoted alone.

**Falsifier.** The seal must refuse a body whose ids cannot round-trip. That refusal is already built
and is the reason it is trusted; a seal that admits everything is not a seal.

## 3. What is refused throughout

- **No kernel and no elaboration.** Lean is text. A `lake env lean` call in any station is the
  correction of 2026-08-16 recurring.
- **No authored partition.** Every returned class traces to a property of the material. The check is
  the one this project states: *which declared input, if varied across two members of one returned
  class, would move them apart?*
- **No count decides.** Frequency is the lived construction; it is reported and never gated.
- **No scalar dashboard.** Every station leads with the decoded artifact — the statement, the
  permutation, the word, the residue polynomial, the route — and counts appear beside the population
  they count.
- **No new driver per station.** The tree carries 207 and the catalog gate holds it true. These are
  sections of one run plus, where the material genuinely differs, one companion.

## 4. What this plan is for

The objective is Brandon's: *a machine that can rigorously perform and analyze computations using
internal machinery that accommodates transport mechanisms between arbitrary charts, the learning is
the intermediary mechanism/law/equation.*

Stations one and two build the terrain. Station three is the current through it. Station four is the
world that returns a genuine difference. Station five is the deposit that changes the terrain, which
is the whole of conditioning — *a pathway a current changes, and a later current that rides the
changed pathway*. Station six is the standing that survives the source.

A machine that founds mathematics from text, proposes what the text does not contain, has its
proposals adjudicated by exact arithmetic it owns, deposits what survives, and reaches further
afterwards **is navigating**. That is the definition this project runs on, and every organ it needs is
already in the tree.
