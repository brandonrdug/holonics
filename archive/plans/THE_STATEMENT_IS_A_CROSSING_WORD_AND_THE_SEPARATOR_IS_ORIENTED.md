## 2. The finding that orders the work: the grammar's founding rules are properties of a three-statement fixture

**`statement_grammar::recover` has never been run on mathematics.** Measured 2026-08-16 by
`grep -rn "statement_grammar" --include='*.rs' crates soma`: the only callers are
`statement_composition` and `crates/holonic-engine/examples/the_statement_is_founded.rs`, and that
driver's population is **three** statements of this repository's own proof plumbing —
`|- (P : Prop) (h : P) : exactCarrier P`, `|- (a b : Nat) : a = b`, `|- (h : P) : exactCarrier P`.

Two of its founding rules do not survive real mathematics, and both were measured today by an
**outside Python instrument** over 387 whole `theorem`/`lemma` statements read from
`Mathlib/Geometry/Euclidean/*.lean`. That instrument is a probe to de-risk this plan, **not a machine
return**, and it is named as one so no figure below is quoted as one.

**Rule that holds.** The bracket pair `( )` balances over the whole population — 386 of 387, the one
failure being the probe's own length cut. So the pair is recoverable exactly as written.

**Rule that fails, and it is an authored charset.** `recover` refuses any statement that is not
ASCII (`StatementGrammarRefusal::StatementIsNotAscii`, excised 2026-08-16 and recoverable only from
git history — the citation this sentence carried is deliberately not repointed, because the
construct is gone). Measured over
18,666 `theorem`-opening lines in `Geometry` + `Algebra`: **14,150 carry a non-ASCII character and
4,516 do not.** The gate refuses **75.8%** of the material, and what it refuses is precisely the
notation — `ℝ`, `ℕ`, `∠`, `≅`, `¬`, `₁`, `ᵥ`, `⟪⟫`.

**Rule that fails, and this one is the result.** The separator is founded as *the character occurring
exactly once at depth zero in every statement*. Over the 387 real statements, **no character
satisfies that**, so the recovery would refuse the population outright. The reason is structural
rather than incidental: `:` occurs once per binder group **and** once for the target, so its count
varies with the number of groups. The measured distribution of `(top-level colons − top-level
groups)` runs from **−8 to +4**, and the value `+1` — the fixture's shape — accounts for only 106 of
385.

**The oriented rule holds instead, and it holds nearly everywhere.** Take the **last** top-level
occurrence of the universal separator. Measured: `:` appears in **385 of 385** statements, and the
oriented split succeeds on **386 of 387**.

```
ANTE  finrank_direction_altitude {n : ℕ} [NeZero n] (s : Simplex ℝ P n) (i : Fin (n + 1))
CONS  finrank ℝ (s.altitude i).direction = 1

ANTE  altitudeFoot_mem_affineSpan_faceOpposite {n : ℕ} [NeZero n] (s : Simplex ℝ P n) (i : Fin (n+1))
CONS  s.altitudeFoot i ∈ affineSpan ℝ (Set.range (s.faceOpposite i).points)

ANTE  inner_vsub_vsub_altitudeFoot_eq_height_sq [NeZero n] {i j : Fin (n + 1)} (h : i ≠ j)
CONS  ⟪s.points i -ᵥ s.points j, s.points i -ᵥ s.altitudeFoot i⟫ = s.height i ^ 2
```

> **The antecedent/consequent axis is not a new axis. It is the separator's ORIENTATION, and the
> unoriented rule is what a symmetric reading returns.** That is the same defect, on token material,
> that `derivation_atlas` carried until 2026-08-14: a reach 1-cell founded with the same sign as a
> recruitment cell, so nothing chained. `ReachOrientation::{IntoDerivation, OutOfDerivation}` is the
> axis; here it is the difference between *a* colon and the *last* colon.

**And the consequent's own top-level punctuation is a founded classification with no Lean noun in
it.** Over the 386 splits: `=` 86 + 26, `∈` 18, `<` 7, `≠` 7, `∉` 7, and 8 bare applications.
Equation, membership, inequality, apartness — read off the token stream, nobody having named them.

## 3. The five stations

Each is a station of the loop; none is an adjacent construction. Every one names its falsifier.

### Station one — the grain is founded over notation, not gated by a charset

Excise `StatementIsNotAscii` and found the token grain from the material, the way the mouth already
founds its grain from octets. `THE_AUTHORED_LEVEL.md` governs: the excision is graded by its
**orbit**, not by a green suite.

**Falsifier.** On ASCII-only material the founded grain must return readings **bit-identical** to
the present ones — otherwise the excision moved something it should not have. On notation it must
return a *typed* reading rather than mush: `∠ a b c` must expose `∠` as a first position with three
arguments, exactly as `exactCarrier P` exposes one. If it cannot, the gate was load-bearing and that
is the finding.

### Station two — the separator is oriented, and the orientation is the antecedent/consequent axis

Replace the exactly-once separator founding with the oriented one, and return **both** readings so
the change is an orbit rather than a repair. The leading region is the antecedent population; the
trailing region is the consequent.

**Falsifier, and it is the one that matters.** A theorem whose consequent is another theorem's
antecedent is an **edge**, and under the unoriented rule no such edge can form because no separator
is recovered at all. Run `derivation_atlas` over the oriented split on a bounded mathlib subtree and
require the joined population and the longest route to **move**, exhibited by name — the same shape
as `14 joined at longest 2` against `29 joined at longest 3`. If the routes do not move, the
orientation is decorative here and must be reported as such.

**The anti-tautology arm.** The classification of a consequent by its top-level punctuation must not
be the preimage of a field the driver authored. It is read off the material, so the check is: vary
the *material* — take a subtree whose mathematics is stated in a different idiom — and require the
signature population to move without the rule moving.

### Station three — an instruction stream is a crossing word

This is Brandon's sentence, and the algebra for it is built. A binder group, a tactic, a call, a
rewrite: each is a **contact with a binary state over a causal order**, which is exactly a subset of
`{1..n}` under symmetric difference. `Bond::species_winding` and `species_sheet` already stamp that
state from the complex's own canonical species population; `multiquadratic` already carries the word.

The deed is to make the word explicit and **compare two of them**. Two proofs of one statement are
two words. Equal in the algebra means one transport — a rebase with zero remainder. Unequal means the
remainder is a **holonomy**, which is the object
`research/records/2026-08-16_THE_CORNER_BREAKS_THE_GROUP_LAW_AND_ITS_REMAINDER_IS_A_HOLONOMY.md`
already carries.

**The intended face decides the grading**, and that is `skein`'s contextual receiver equivalence
verbatim: `L ~_R R'` iff every admitted context returns the same invariants. So *"depending on the
intended faces of the instruction sets"* is a declared receiver family and not a metaphor.

**Falsifier.** Two mathlib proofs of the same statement, one obtained from the other by a move that
is a known no-op for the declared face, must return **equal words**; two proofs that differ
structurally must return a **non-zero** remainder, exhibited. A reading that returns zero remainder
for everything has not gauged anything — the vacuous-gauge defect — so the run must exhibit its own
separating word.

### Station four — the token pattern reaches the figure the machine natively resolves

The joint the centrifuge record named. A consequent whose top-level signature is `=` between two
expressions over a declared ring is an **algebraic identity**, and this body resolves those exactly:
`elementary_chart` decides `∫R e^g` by the consistency of one exact rational linear system;
`hermite_reduction` is the coboundary move; `hypergeometric_closure` decides closure by sorting
integers; `relational-geometry` resolves projective incidence over `BigRational`;
`contact_gluing::Corner` reads a corner as an exact rational cosine with no angle taken.

Mathlib's Euclidean geometry hands over the material in tokens: `side_side_side`,
`side_angle_side`, `angle_side_angle`, `angle_angle_side`, `similar_of_angle_angle` — the congruence
criteria, with `∠ a b c = ∠ a' b' c'` and its primed correspondence legible entirely at token level.

**Falsifier, and it is the whole station.** The machine must resolve a figure it was **not handed** —
constructed from the token pattern alone — and the resolution must be checkable against exact
arithmetic already in this tree. If the driver picks which organ to call, the station has failed and
is a simulator emitting a codec. The organ must be reached **from the founded signature**.

### Station five — admission grades what was founded, never what was declared

Point `FiberStanding` / `FiberAdmission` at station three's move species over real mathematics. A
species that carries in every proof it appears in is `Admitted` — a transport with no recognition
condition owed. One that carries sometimes is `Conflicted`: a junction whose chart is doing work its
name does not carry, which is `H.0362`'s atlas edge with its recognition condition still missing.

**What makes this different from every prior run of the law.** In
`the_kernel_decides_which_moves_are_load_bearing` the motions were this repository's own plumbing and
the kernel was the authority. Here the motions are mathlib's, the evidence is the machine's own
transport, and nothing is elaborated.

**Falsifier.** The population of `Admitted` species must not be the preimage of a declared field.
Trace each admitted species back: if two moves land together because the driver handed them the same
value, the class is authored. The check is the one `CLAUDE.md` states — *which declared input, if
varied across two members of one returned class, would move them apart?*

- **No new `examples/` file per station.** The driver population is 203 and catalogued; these

## 6. What this plan does not claim

The oriented-separator and charset figures are an **outside Python probe**, taken to decide whether
these stations are worth building. They become machine returns only when the organ computes them, and
until then they may not be cited as evidence of a capability. The 387-statement population is one
subtree of one library; the rules that hold there may fail elsewhere, and station two's anti-tautology
arm exists to find out. And nothing in this plan builds an interpreter: the machine founds transport
from material, and a founded transport that only reproduces the codec's own grammar is a restatement,
not a recovery.
