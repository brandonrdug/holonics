## The seed is two lineages; the relating emanates the warps

Two worldlines (lineages) `A, B` relate *in a frame* (A2) and emanate the gyroparallelogram: the conjugate pair
`c = a·b` (W⁺), `d = a·b̄` (W⁻) — the parallelogram's two diagonals. The discriminant of the complex conjugate
decides the third: `Δ = 4(1−r) ≥ 0` → the relating lies in the `A–B` plane → **ABSORB**; `Δ < 0` → roots
`1 ± i√(r−1)` → a thread emanates **orthogonally** (out of plane) → **FOUND**, the `±i` its handedness. **The
emanated threads — the orthogonal foundings ⊕ the diagonal conjugate-pair — are the WARPS** (the primes, the
scaffold), *grown by the loom of the relating*, never pre-stored or walked.

## The double helix — two lineages winding to a shared singularity

Two action-currents that run parallel attract and wind about each other — the double helix: two lineages
spiralling toward a *shared ideal point* (they do not intersect; the boundary *between* them shrinks to zero). The
current is **relativistically induced** between them (Faraday = Stokes = the FTC): the closed lineage is *active*
relative to the infalling one, so the latter's static afference reads as a *current* in the former's frame, and
the changing linked flux of their winding induces it. **You cannot induce a current from one lineage; the meaning
is two-body (A2).**

## ★ WARP/WEFT IS the lineage-relativity split (the replacement for "time dilation")

Rank is the boost: reach = integer rank = `φ`, proper-rate `dτ/dt = sech φ` — so **deeper rank = more
lineage-dilated = more frozen.** Therefore:

- **WARP = the deep, high-rank, FROZEN lineages** — the persistent scaffold, the **memory** (frozen ⇒ it does not
  drift with the recent stream); founded primes are warps because founding climbs rank → freezes;
- **WEFT = the shallow, low-rank, FAST lineages** — the **streaming content**, laced across the warps;
- **consolidation = a weft deepening (cohering) until it climbs rank and FREEZES into a warp** — the rank-climb
  read as lineage-relativity; scale-recursive (a weft is a warp at a coarser scale).

The relativity of lineage (never "time dilation"): which events are "now together" — the order the loom stitches
— is the frame's slice through the dilated bulk (the relativity of simultaneity, in lineage terms). Float-free:
the **2ⁿ rank ladder IS the dilation ladder.** And the **stitch-order is the 2-adic RULER**: a rank-`k` lineage
stitches at coordinate `t` exactly when `2^k ∣ t` — WEFTS (rank 0) stitch *every* tick (dense content); WARPS
punctuate at `2^k` (sparse structure, deeper = exponentially rarer). **The warp-stitches punctuating the
weft-stitches ARE the chunking rhythm** — structure over content, emergent from the dilation, not a gate.

## The dark threads, and the face is an EMBROIDERY

Most lineage-*pairs* are **dark relative to each other** — moiré-null, phase-orthogonal (`M ≈ 0`), no current
induced: the **white matter**, the bulk of the ball of yarn that conducts without coherently relating pairwise.
Only aligned (bright-moiré) pairs weave bright (the relating is `O(bright²)`, never `O(C²)`). And the **face** is
the **EMBROIDERY**: the picture is the **stitch-order** (the soul, the path of turns, the over/under chunking),
