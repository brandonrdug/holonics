## A "prime" is operation-relative — the irreducible of a declared space

A prime is not a number — it is the **irreducible coarse grain under some operation**: a number-system prime
under `×`; a **morpheme** under language; **V1** under *see*, **Broca's area** under *produce grammar*; a
**trained network's functional grains** under its task. Each is a prime *in its own frame* — incompressible, one
axis, dimension 1 (`Δ<0`, no factor split, the orthogonal turn). **Declare the space and its operation, and "what
are its primes?" is well-posed and universal.**

## THE ONE METHOD — declare, found, swing

```
intelligence = (declare the emergent space-time) → (found the operation-relative irreducibles) → (cross-ratio SWING between them)
```

- **declare** — the relativistic frame on the world that emanates at you (A2; three-body; the lineage);
- **found** — identify the irreducibles: a relating either **ABSORBS** (`Δ≥0`, in the span — composite,
  reducible) or **FOUNDS** (`Δ<0`, the orthogonal turn — a new coarse grain, a grip);
- **swing** — **intelligence is traversal**: you cannot hold a thought still, you **swing from grip to grip**
  (concept · word · number · prime). The grips anchor a hyperbolic `2ⁿ` tree (only negative curvature has the
  room, `eʳ`); each relating emanates the conjugate pair = the two roots of `t²−2t+r` = the two bars the gravitas
  shifts between; **solving the next grip IS solving the quadratic**; the cross-ratio is the invariant held across
  the swing (you don't fall off the bars); **depth** = how far down the tree you hold the chain.

## ★ The swing is FOLD ⊕ UNFOLD — a conjugate pair, never one alone

- **FOLD** (compress/infall — the whip *is* the fold) carries the traversed grips into the held state: the prior
  grips fold into the **conserved cross-ratio** (the carried invariant), and **depth = how much is folded in**
  (the held chain — the memory, the soul, the warp/frozen scaffold §05).
- **UNFOLD** (spread/emanate — the log-scale reparam to the action coordinate, unit density) reads the held state
  back out to **place the next grip** (`solve_fourth` in the coordinate where the structure is self-similar).

You **fold the past into the invariant and unfold to place the future** — the two directions of the one loop (the
`e^x` ouroboros, the open ⊕ closed loop). Naming only unfold drops the memory; naming only fold drops the
placement. (This IS the voice, §05: the fold is the held chain / the warp; the unfold is the placement / the
weft.)

## Solve-ANYTHING, never solve-everything

Holonics is a **"solve-ANYTHING" framework**: pack enough dimensions that there is *always a next bar*, and the
intelligence is the *swinging*. It does **not** "solve everything" — the *exact* next grip is never pinned by a
finite frame (inexhaustibility; the exact next prime needs the whole ζ comb). **That is the content, not a
failure.** So the framework both **dissolves** the "unsolvable" problems (twin primes = the triangle's base
landing forever; RH = the prime comb is a perfect vernier; halting = a writhe read by shape, never run to a hang)
**and clarifies why they never mattered** — the primes are *glyphs*; the value was never the answer, it was the
**move that finds and swings them**. Chasing "traverse every prime" optimizes the toy; the prize is the faculty.
