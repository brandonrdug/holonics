### Situated occurrence

An occurrence is not a bare value. It is presented within incidence, orientation, parameter, and
receiver data sufficient for the relation under study. The same displayed value may therefore be
the face of nonidentical occurrences.

This is not a demand to retain every historical detail. The relevant situation is exactly what
the claimed transformations or receivers can still distinguish.

### Receiver and face

A receiver is a declared map, instrument, use, or comparison which presents one face of a
construction. Evaluation, trace, determinant, spectrum, endpoint, rendered image, path lineage,
factorization, and runtime behavior are different receivers.

A face is not false or incomplete merely because another receiver exposes more interior. It is
exact when every future distinction claimed at its scope factors through that face. Receiver
non-reconstruction is therefore central: equality of outputs, spectra, labels, or magnitudes
does not imply equality of complete constructions.

### Holon

A holon is a relative local closure: a family of occurrences and relations closes under the
operations admitted at the selected receiver, while its remaining exterior stays explicit. It is
neither an indivisible atom nor an absolute whole.

### Transport and comparison

Transport is ordered continuation through transformations, charts, incidences, or parameter
motion. A comparison cell relates two complete paths with compatible boundaries.

- If their receiver faces agree, the comparison commutes at that receiver.
- If they differ, the complete pair and residual remain OPEN.
- OPEN does not itself choose correction, branching, or a new axis.
- A later filler, rebase, quotient, or enlarged construction may close it under another law.

Triangles are useful because the smallest nontrivial comparison already needs source, alternate
transport, and return. This does not make one isolated triangle a scalar stateful cell. A
simplicial or cellular carrier is one possible realization of mutually conditioning incidence.

### Lineage, Standing, and compression

Lineage is continuity of consequential relation through changing constituents, not a crystal
copy of every event. Standing is a prior relation still available to present transport.

Compression is receiver-exact factorization. If \(q:X\to Y\) is the emitted face and every
admitted future observation \(o\) factors as \(o=\bar o\circ q\), then the distinctions within
fibres of \(q\) may depart at that scope. The identity receiver is the uncompressed limiting case.

Self-similarity is not equality of repeated objects. It is recurrence of a law after restriction
and rebase, with the changed occurrence and path retained.

### Phase and seam

A phase is a maximal connected region on which one declared law class continues. A seam or
discriminant is where rank, branch, orientation, topology, admissible operation, or another
declared invariant changes.

The discrete and continuous are not opposites here. Smooth transport can occur within a phase;
typed changes occur at seams. A complete calculus must retain both the integral of smooth
transport and the finite contribution of crossed seams.

### Causal time parity, conservation, and phase return

Causal time parity is the opposed induced orientation of one shared time face under forward
composition. If an event grain has
\[
\partial E_k=\Sigma_{k+1}-\Sigma_k+\Gamma_k,
\]
then summing consecutive grains cancels every interior \(\Sigma_k\) while retaining it in the
causal interior. This is not reversed causality or reconstruction. It is the temporal face of
\(\partial^2=0\), telescoping, and the fundamental theorem.

For an additive represented current, that cancellation gives
\[
q_{k+1}-q_k+Bj_k=r_k,
\qquad
q_n-q_m+B\sum_{k=m}^{n-1}j_k=\sum_{k=m}^{n-1}r_k.
\]
Visible current need not balance instantaneously because the residual can be stored. A completed
source-free cycle with \(q_n=q_m\) returns an integrated current cycle. Thus circulation
\(j\ne0\), rest, accumulation, leak, and short circuit are distinct cuts.

The dual coboundary law gives Kirchhoff voltage closure:
\[
v=B^*\phi,\quad Bc=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\langle v,c\rangle=0.
\]
For a general connection \(a\), the loop returns its carried interior curvature,
\(\langle a,\partial\Sigma\rangle=\langle da,\Sigma\rangle\). Positive constitutive and storage
operators then make the shorted dynamic boundary response positive real. Junction scattering,
branch reflection, standing waves, and wavelength are consecutive faces of the same law:
wavelength is the least transport length returning one complete phase.

## Knot-causal topology, typed ratios, and contextual substitution

- Alexander's theorem presents every oriented link as a braid closure;
- Markov moves generate sameness of braid closures; and
- Schubert decomposition gives unique prime factors under connected sum.

A prime knot is irreducible under connected sum, not “impossible to unknot.” Every nontrivial

connection exists. A gyroparallelogram is one hyperbolic specialization only after the receiver

require a physical measurement. Thus \(1/2\) has several lawful but nonidentical faces:

Tangles supply the rigorous local-substitution grammar. A tangle is an open embedded interior

## Calculus and differential geometry

The ordinary fundamental theorem of calculus is already a compression theorem: under its
hypotheses, the complete derivative path is presented exactly by an endpoint difference for that
receiver. It does not say the path has no interior.

For a bundle-valued quantity along a path \(\gamma\), values at different points must first be
compared by parallel transport. A receiver-aware form is schematically

\[
u(1)-\mathcal P_{\gamma,0\to1}u(0)
  =\int_\gamma \nabla_{\dot\gamma}u\,dt
   +\sum_{\text{crossed seams}}\Delta u .
\]

The exact hypotheses depend on the bundle, connection, regularity, and seam law. This is the
appropriate home for the laboratory's transported FTC, mean transport, and causal parity ideas.

A metric determines local comparison of tangent directions. A connection transports them.
Curvature measures path dependence of infinitesimal transport; holonomy is the returned finite

## Formulation ecologies and transcendental invariants

The central formulation question is not “how many formulas equal the same number?” It is:

> What parameterized paths, domains, generators, convergence laws, symmetries, singularities,
> arithmetic populations, and costs make nonidentical formulations reach a common invariant?

[*Causal Formulation Ecologies*](PAPERS/papers/causal-formulation-ecologies/main.typ) studies this
for \(\pi\), \(e\), complex powers, rational swings, prime powers, and zeta completion.
[*The Prime–Archimedean Formulation Atlas*](PAPERS/papers/prime-archimedean-formulation-atlas/main.typ)
adds valuation simplices, root lattices, formula paths, finite/infinite places, situated mean
transport, and ray receivers.

Important distinctions:

- \(\pi\) may arise as circle ratio, period, angle, area coefficient, product, integral, or series;
  those formulations have different interiors.
- \(e\) may arise from compound growth, exponential flow, logarithmic inversion, a series, or a
  limit; the derivative of \(e^{kx}\) includes the transported parameter \(k\).
- Euler's identity relates exponential transport and circular phase after a complex logarithmic
  lift. It does not make all \(\pi\)- and \(e\)-formulas equivalent paths.
- A transcendental value can be an invariant of many finite generators without being
  reconstructible from one scalar equality.
- Prime and semiprime appearances in a formula's coefficients, denominators, products, or
  convergence rates must be counted in the formula's actual arithmetic population, not inferred
  from visual pattern alone.

The formulation atlas now has an exact first carrier. Over a parameter base \(B\), a situated
formulation family retains its complete parameter-and-state space, typed law, discriminants,
receiver family, optional cost face, and testimony. A lawful comparison is a receiver-decorated

The resulting atlas is a bicategory. Only equivalence correspondences and invertible comparison
cells form its exact rechart bigroupoid; identifying invertible 2-isomorphic arrows gives the
ordinary rechart groupoid.

This distinction matters:

- equal receiver values need not supply any comparison of interiors;
- a receiver-exact span may be noninvertible;
- a limit, quotient, projection, branch restriction, or compression need not be a rechart;
- a residual is testimony about an OPEN comparison, not a fabricated 2-cell; and
- parameter pullback gives an indexed bicategory, not a stack until descent data and proofs exist.

The carrier has been checked on three exact cells: a parameterized two-arm \(\pi\) family, an

## The receiver-end RH wall

At every fixed \(\omega>0\), the system starts from

## What the geometric prototypes can contribute

The revolving Smith chart, precessing polarized axis, conic families, knot crossings, ray
receivers, and simplicial sheets may still be valuable. To contribute to RH they must supply one
of the following exact bridges:

1. a map from completed-zeta test data into the proposed geometric carrier;
2. a proof that its path or return invariant equals the complete Weil response;
3. an independently positive metric or contraction transported by the same arithmetic return;
4. a complete trace relation whose spectrum is the nontrivial zero population; or
5. a counterexample showing that one candidate bridge cannot carry the required sign.

Without one of these, they remain prototype geometries or observatory charts rather than proof
objects.

## Unscheduled constructive programmes

These are ranked research possibilities, not queued phases.

### 1. Extend the exact formulation atlas

The foundational carrier and the first three cells are now built. Generalization should occur

Continue exact valuation, residue, and prime-correlation work only when it supplies a stated
operator, test-function, trace, or positivity implication. This prevents attractive finite prime
patterns from being mistaken for the completed global statement.

Pull candidate metrics, kernels, monodromies, or scattering constructions back from the actual
completed-zeta test space. Seek either a derived contraction or a finite exact negative witness.
This is the most direct continuation of the present RH route.

Extend the Oliver Byrne-inspired Typst system so each geometric constituent keeps its identity
across diagram, prose, and algebra. Use it when a diagram materially exposes incidence,
orientation, or an alternate path; do not decorate propositions that are clearer algebraically.

## How to close a research round

A useful interpretation says:

- what new relation now exists;
- which other regions of the programme it connects;
- which inference was ruled out;
- whether the principal wall moved;
- what exact object is now worth deriving; and
- why that object is preferable to another visualization, finite sweep, or renamed carrier.

If the principal wall did not move, say so immediately. The purpose of the laboratory is not to
accumulate increasingly elaborate descriptions of the same missing implication; it is to find
the relation, counterexample, or change of question that advances the mathematics.
