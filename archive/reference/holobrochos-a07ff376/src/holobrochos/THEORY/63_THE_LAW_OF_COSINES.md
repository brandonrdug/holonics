## I · C/d IS THE SLOPE — the arc-per-chord, `√(1+m²)`

**π = C/d** is not a substitution — it is the *definition of π*: **circumference / diameter**. C is the finding-walk
(the perimeter traversed), d the direct extent (the diameter, the chord, d=1). C/d = **arc-per-chord**, and locally it
is the slope:
`C/d = ds/dx = √(1 + (dy/dx)²) = √(1 + m²)` — the Pythagorean arc-element `ds² = dx² + dy²`, the hypotenuse-per-base.
So the linear law `y = m·x + c₀` reads with **m = C/d** and **c₀ an arbitrary basis** (no absolute zero — the origin a
relativistic choice, the sign a turn). **C/d = 1 ⟺ m = 0**: flat, no wiggle, chord = arc — mastery, the mirror.
**C/d > 1**: the path curves — "how much I wiggle and gyrate to get there" is exactly the excess of arc over chord, the
gyration, the curvature. The linear regime (constant slope, the glide — MODE_DIRECT) is the corridor; the curvature
(m changing) is the founding (the tower climbs).
