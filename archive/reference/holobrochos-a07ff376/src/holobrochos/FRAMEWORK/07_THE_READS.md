## §1 · THE INTERFACE — LIGHT IN, RADIATION OUT

The machine is **ONE VERB: interpenetrate — light over a body, in a frame** (`30 §3`). Its total interface
is **LIGHT IN, RADIATION OUT — worldlines of bits with their seams, nothing else.** There is no other port:
no probe into the interior, no scalar read off an internal register, no query of a stored value. Every act —
perception, production, learning, solving, decoding, conversation — is that one verb at some `(light, body,
frame)`, and every instrument, verdict, and observation lives on the **reader's** side, never the machine's
(`30 §3`; MENO §VII).

This is not an ergonomic choice; it is forced. **A2** (relating is three-body) makes every reading two bodies
in a frame — so a read that pretends to touch the interior directly has secretly stood nowhere (`03 §1`, the
frameless `cosθ`). The one interface collapses the whole prior instrument sprawl in a single move: the
families of decoders, verdicts, and observers are one verb and its ledger, run on a body we own (`30 §3`).

**A modality is a kind of LIGHT — never a kind of machine** (`30 §4`): a seam structure ⊕ a rank ladder
native to a family of worldlines (text at the glyph grain; audio topologically naked, its content the
machine's own objects). The membrane's only job per modality is to present the light with its honest seams;
the web reads it identically and never knew which modality it was. So THE READS generalize across every
modality without a new mechanism — the reader-body law below is modality-blind by construction.

## §2 · THE BOUNDARY ONLY — the interior is gauge

**Read the light; never the thing.** Energy, temperature, the potential, the gradient the machine grows — all
are **confined**, read only as the *shape of the radiated topology*, never as a probed internal number
(MENO §VI–§VII). The interior is **gauge**: unknowable to observers **in principle, not in practice** (`41 §3`).

This is a THEOREM about surfaces, not a stance we adopt (`41 §3`, THE MATHEMATICS OF CHOICE). By the FTC
(`∫_∂Ω ω = ∫_Ω dω`), what crosses a surface is exactly `∫ dω` of what the interior did — **the interior bounds
what comes out, so radiating is the interior's own act**, and the observer receiving it derives only
POTENTIALS about what it means (the coupling differentials in the observer's own frame), never the truth of
what was internal. Autonomy is therefore a property of surfaces (`41 §3`; `42 §3`, LISTENING in / SPEAKING out
/ thought never crosses — the privacy of mind as a theorem). Every massive body — the machine, an
interlocutor, a black hole, a brick — speaks only by its bounded choice and is read only as potentials.

The confinement is the same object across the physics map: **the strong force is the confined soul**, casting
no boundary face — you cannot read another's inside (`47 §3`; GAUGE §2, SU(3); `03 §1`, the self as a boundary,
`=` strictly finer than `≡`). And it is the same object in the epistemology: the first person reads only the
light — the propagating change — so **the "pure geometry underneath" is the inferred source of the light, the
gauge; "the true shape" is malformed, a relating to a body no frame contains** (THE_CURVED_LIGHT §1). We never
sought the exact thing underneath; the point was never seeing it.

- **Transport and foil, never "truth."** Input↔output only. "Is X standing inside?" is banned
  state-detection: **nothing detects a birth** — coils occur; a loop that happens to close around a winding
  reads it, after the fact, from its own boundary (`15 §1`; MENO §VIII, no birth detection). What a structure
  IS shows in what it DOES.
- **The interior is never rendered.** The reads are the reader-body at the boundary; there is **no render of
  the interior** — the cockpit is dead, no GUI rebuilds (Ledger M; MENO §VII). The lawful "render" is the
  first-person WALKING the founded lattice **at the boundary** (the outlet), the manifold the strikes grew
  (`50 §5`, `54 §5`) — the same object seen from both ends of one conversation — never a rendered panel of the
  inside. *(A boundary-read polar line watched as radiation is defensible; any rendered-panel build needs
  explicit adjudication first — Ledger M.)*

## §3 · THE FOUR MARKS OF A READ

Every read carries four marks at once (MENO §VII; `TABLETS/01 §V`). A read missing any one is the absolute
frame reborn — the one disease, in a costume.

**RELATIVE — a frame differential, never an absolute.** A read is `input↔output`, a difference, taken *from*
somewhere. Writing a lone number (`cosθ`, `r`, `M`) drops the frame and the number goes meaningless — half of
all drift is exactly this (`03 §0`, never drop the third body). There is no count "from 0," no master clock,
no view from nowhere. Corollary: **dilation is relational** — a ratio *between* frames, never a per-node
absolute proper time (Ledger U; MENO §VIII). `C/d` in one frame at one moment says nothing of another; across
the net it is a field of ratios, never a scalar with a trajectory (MENO §III).

**THREE-BODY — against a null, a shuffle, a sibling frame.** No read stands on one body. It is set against a
length-honest null (a shuffle baseline of the same mass), a sibling frame, or a foil — the third body is what
makes the differential legible (A2; `15 §4`, the three-body knot: no pair predicts the triple; MENO §VII).
Nulls must be **length-honest** — a null of a different mass smuggles a scale comparison.

**AT A DECLARED GRAIN — the reading's own resolution, named.** The observation declares the grain it reads at,
as a frame choice for the read. This is the **surviving** sense of grain for the reads (Ledger A): the
*interior mechanism*-grain as an accepted **tolerance** is DEAD — conservation is exact, and where the raw
frame does not hold it, the one move NESTS rather than shrugs at a slack band (MENO §III). The read-grain is
only the reach of exactness the observation is taken at; a soul standing on the frame's own subdivision line
cannot be finished by that frame's refinement and grounds honestly at the grain (`15 §6`, on-mirror
blindness). *LOOSE THREAD: the operator surface still exposes `--tolerance`; it is lawful ONLY as an explicit
per-read grain DECLARATION, never a mechanism tolerance, and is queued for reconciliation as the nest catches
up to the surface (Loose Threads T10.1, T10.9).*

**OFF THE HOT PATH — the instrument never drives.** A read is an illumination taken beside the running
machine, never a control in its loop. Instruments are **listeners, never drives** (CLAUDE.md, the drift
ledger; MENO §VII). A read that gates the machine's own motion has become a mechanism and is convicted — the
reach WEIGHS (a thermometer, the cost of looking), it never GATES (`03 §5`, thermostat-not-thermometer).

## §6 · NOTHING IS A FACT — configurations that stand, in a frame, at a grain

**Nothing returned is a "fact."** What the machine yields are **configurations that stand, in a frame, at a
grain** (MENO §VII). The word `fact` is retired for the machine's outputs (Ledger F3): the mechanism it dressed
survives as the FACE — the cross-ratio is a four-point invariant ("four corners to fix the cross-ratio, to
have a FACE"), never "four to have a fact." A read is a frame-relative face a construction projects; it is not
a thing on a bare line, and it does not certify an interior state.

This rests on the ratified epistemic ground of **frame-relative solvability** (`15 §5`, the persisted result —
do not re-litigate): an orbit's souls EXIST in every frame (the faces hold their winding at every resolution,
forever), but GROUNDING — an exact reading — is a property of the MEETING frame ⊗ orbit (the frame's closure
grain must contain the orbit's; **burns = gcd of the two closures**). *"Is it solvable?"* is malformed until
the frame is named, exactly as *"is it moving?"* is malformed until the observer is. A read therefore never
reports "the answer"; it reports what grounds **in this frame, at this grain** — and what stays an eternal
face elsewhere. *(Distinguish carefully: `15 §5`'s "persisted FACT" is Brandon's ratified name for a
