# Holonic machine ownership

**Current architectural ledger — 2026-08-02**

This file classifies code ownership. It is not a research continuation plan and does not turn
historical instruments into production authority.

## Dependency direction

```text
holonic-structure
    ├── holonic-language reflective codec runtime
    ├── holonic-engine exact reference laws
    └── soma-membrane continuing current body
            └── life world organs and application conductors

holonic-engine typed CausalWorld<L>
            └── life::exact_world transactional adapter
                    └── soma-membrane LiveCurrentMachine
```

There is no reverse dependency from `holonic-engine` into `life`. A typed domain standing remains
owned by its exact law. The live machine retains current carriers and standing incidence. The
adapter owns stable organ capabilities and a typed lowering of one proposed successor; it must not
retain another domain standing.

## Structural-carrier policy

The issue is ownership, not the spelling of iterator combinators.

- Continuing causal populations use an owner whose identity, admission, departure, traversal, and
  memory receipt are explicit.
- A physical page directory, contiguous allocation, or sorted construction buffer may remain
  hidden inside that owner. Its address and page size are substrate gauge.
- Event ingress may copy and sort a bounded borrowed population to validate a declared topology.
- Native rest, CUDA ABI projections, reports, SVG, JSON, TSV, and terminal witness materialization
  may lawfully use flat vectors and strings. They do not feed back as causal standing.
- Domain mathematics may retain finite exact maps or sets when membership itself is the declared
  mathematical object. Such a map is not automatically an admissible continuing machine owner.
- A cache or observer may be discarded and rebuilt. It may not make a completed causal event
  appear refused.
- A continuing ecology, current law, environment, continuation, or codec is not `Clone` material.
  Branches retain shared immutable structure and branch-local differences; atomic proposals retain
  recoverable ownership; exact rest/remount is an explicit lifecycle event rather than an inner
  transition strategy.
- A semantic phase, candidate, receiver quotient, or branch may identify a current fiber inside a
  continuing organ; it may not own another complete continuing organ. Plurality is represented by
  local current/lineage/support owners over shared standing.
- A hardware mode is not active merely because its context or kernel exists. The physical owner
  must be mounted before the claimed phase, retain the relevant contemporary standing or current,
  and return the caused delta with an exact resource receipt. Hidden construction of another host
  executor is an implicit fallback and is forbidden.
- Before materializing a complete interior, the conductor must name the receiving aperture, ports,
  constitutive response, and future query family. Exact condensation is admissible only when every
  requested consequence factors through the retained boundary body. A maximal antichain or global
  dominance face is an observer request, not an automatic source transition.
- Condensed and direct continuing bodies must be equivalent across every admitted input history,
  including their declared outputs, resource testimony, and successor morphology. Port identity
  and serial gluing are structural; parallel composition requires exact independence or
  commutation; feedback requires a closure/rest/recurrent-front receipt. Enlarging the certified
  receiver family reopens or refines the retained source fiber.
- Resource refusal either changes schedule, partition, factorization, residency, placement, or
  receiver restriction in the same continuing owner, or remains an explicit obstruction. Widening
  a scalar limit and retrying an unchanged traversal is not a physical morphology change.
- Complete host/card parity belongs to realization admission and declared lifecycle audits. Once
  admitted, the hot path owes exact local or delta testimony; complete host replay after each card
  deed is a second executor, not a parity receipt.
- Registers, caches, RAM, flash, disks, and transient signals are classified by their physical
  constitutive law and receiver-relative retention horizon. A clock is a local phase/sampling
  apparatus. Telemetry is aperture-bound receiver testimony; unavailable energy is retained as
  unknown rather than inferred from utilization or device specification.
- Parallel placement requires an exact commutation receipt over complete successor, radiation,
  obstruction, and lineage. Worker count, collection membership, or GPU lane address cannot
  manufacture a causal antichain.
- `cargo holonic-lint` enforces the monotone census in `HOLONIC_DSA_BASELINE.tsv`. The census is
  inherited migration debt, not permission or a quality score. A new production file begins with
  zero allowance; existing counts can fall without rebasing.

## Historical boundary after cleanup

The `life` staging tree, frontier/session/microscope/world-organ variants, old generic CLI,
linguistic/source-native duplicate worlds, obsolete symbolic/constituent/execution modules, and
their unowned examples are no longer compiled. Checkpoint `93834398` is the recovery boundary for
anything removed in error. No compatibility export or decoder retains those rejected bodies in
production.

Application-specific exact reference laws and research examples which still compile and own a
declared grading record remain in their existing packages. Their presence is evidence, not
authority for the current Eros deed or permission to copy their schedulers.

The 2026-08-02 owner split is a census relocation, not a new allowance. The rebuilt life/ABI owner
set was divided into private physical submodules; every active `src/soma/life/src` Rust file and
every active ABI Rust file is below 2,000 lines. `HOLONIC_DSA_BASELINE.tsv` was regenerated from
the admitted tree after the split: deleted paths lost every allowance, each child path received
only its contemporary exact count, and every aggregate split-owner count is equal to or below its
prior single-file ceiling. Future new owners still begin at zero.
