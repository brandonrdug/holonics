# Historical source and completed construction

[definition] This directory preserves completed or superseded plans and frozen predecessor
material. It is evidence, not an alternative source of current construction instructions.

- `plans/`: completed Rust/HNA/RH contracts and their original acceptance boundaries.
- `plans/legacy/`: earlier retired blueprint provenance.
- `cpp-engine/`: the retired C++ body and its evidence.
- `reference/`: pinned predecessor theory/source snapshots.
- `equation-atlas/`: the retired local equation graph.
- `tooling/`: retired indexes, blanket validators and source-size ledgers.
- `operations/`: pre-consolidation operating/document/application contracts.

Current architecture is in [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md). Dated repository records and
subject guides supply research navigation; the retired index/gate suite is in `tooling/`. The
[relocation guide](../docs/REPOSITORY.md) explains old inline source paths. Age alone did not
determine retirement: live dependencies and mathematical foundations remain in their active roots.

## Following an old source address

[definition] Start from the [research routes](../research/records/README.md) for a current
subject/consumer, and use the snapshot's own revision for a historical claim. Pinned predecessor
snapshots preserve their source text and may link outside the imported subtree. Such a relative
address names that original repository's layout; it is not evidence that the corresponding
artifact was imported into this checkout. `git log --all -- <path>` and `git show <revision>:<path>`
recover tracked predecessors; the snapshot's manifest/source record identifies an external
origin. Untracked private `output/`, `runs/` and observation artifacts may survive only locally.
Keep their recorded result and scope, and state when the artifact itself is unavailable.

[definition] September 20 consolidation retains the previous research index and experiment
banner in `operations/`, with their original source revision. Their chronological testimony is
available without prescribing how current research or native executions are to be interpreted.
