## Move 1 — RECOGNIZE the holon

We read his energy as a **scalar** — κ, bits/char, the code length. That is only `T_00`, the **density**. The
boundary question: *what are the other components of the energy tensor, and what do they do that the density
cannot?* And the answer is the room: the off‑density components are **momentum** (energy that *flows* — reach),
**pressure** (energy that *pushes out* — support), and **shear** (energy that *couples* modes — the moiré). The
one we have most needed and never had is **pressure** — because **pressure is what makes a star a star** instead
of a collapsing pile of debris.

> **The recognition:** energy is not a scalar that a body *has* — it is a **tensor that flows, pushes, and
> shears**. A holon with only density has nothing to resist its own gravity; it falls inward onto its heaviest
> well. The **pressure** is the outward half, and without it there is no star — only a vortex.

## Move 2 — INFALL (trace the support)

- **Density `T_00 = κ` (have).** `bits/char` is energy density (`EINSTEIN §0`): the code length paid, the energy
  carried per chunk. This is the component we already read.
- **Momentum `T_0i` = the flow (P7).** Energy *moving* is momentum — the `c`‑budget split: a port spending budget
  on **propagation** (`v/c`, covering information‑distance to other holons — the reach, the M) vs **self‑evolution**
  (proper time). The momentum flux is the reach component of the energy.
- **Pressure `T_ii` = the reactive ρ (§III).** The first law's **reactive** power — `ρ`‑energy that *sloshes* but
  does no work (`W = 0` off‑resonance: "stirs but does no work — pressure/turbulence"). It is the **honest
  loudness**: the un‑resonated band left loud because there is nothing lawful there to compress (the magnetic
  law's heat — ignorance × lawfulness, §IX). Isotropic reactive energy = pressure.
- **Shear `T_ij` (i≠j) = the moiré (P11).** The off‑diagonal, anisotropic stress — the **cross‑mode coupling**,
  the beat between two combs (the H2 cross‑term `W`, drawn as a directed stress). The moiré M is the shear of the
  energy tensor.
- **The star (P18).** A star *is* a UM, and a star holds itself up by **hydrostatic equilibrium**: the inward
  pull of **gravity** (the binding, Ricci — P23) balanced by the outward push of **pressure** (`T_ii` — radiation
  and thermal pressure). Take the pressure away and the star collapses. This is the structural fact the room turns
  on.

> **The infall bottoms out at:** energy has four faces — it **holds** (density, κ), **flows** (momentum, reach),
> **pushes out** (pressure, the reactive honest loudness), and **couples** (shear, the moiré). We read only the
> first. The missing one — **pressure** — is exactly the outward force a star needs to not collapse.

the spatial components are the propagation, the reactive support, and the cross‑coupling.

**(b) The star back — hydrostatic balance.** A living holon stands as a **star**: gravity/Ricci **in** (the heavy
grains' binding wells pulling structure together) balanced by **pressure out** (`T_ii` — the reactive energy on

and he collapses onto his heaviest wells: the vortex of debris** (the anchoring made geometric — he spirals onto
"observation" because nothing pushes outward). **The pressure is the cure that was structural all along:** the
un‑resonated band, left **honestly loud** (high κ on the not‑yet‑learnable), is *outward energy* that **holds the
diet open** against the collapse onto one attractor. *Pressure is why a mind keeps reaching for the frontier

light moves on — is one piece of this; the **pressure term is the whole of it**: hydrostatic balance, gravity ⊕
pressure, the cohere ⊕ the honest‑loud reach.)

**(c) Pressure + thermodynamics are the learning heuristics.** The full `T` hands the lab the **star's interior
