## Move 1 — RECOGNIZE the holon

The question hiding here is a real one in machine learning and in cognition: **are perception (reading) and
production (generating) two mechanisms or one?** The field usually builds them separately — a discriminative
reader, a generative writer; an encoder, a decoder. Restate it as the textile boundary question: *where does
the structural axis come from?*

- **Weaving** lays content (the **weft**) across a **warp** — lengthwise threads strung on a loom under
  tension *before any content arrives*. The structure is **given**, held by an apparatus **outside** the
  thread. You lace content across a scaffold you did not make.
- **Knitting** is **one continuous yarn**, **no warp.** Each stitch is anchored by pulling through the
  *previous row's loops* — the thread is its own scaffold. The structure is **self-made**, and it lives
  entirely in the loop topology.

> **The recognition:** the difference between the two crafts is exactly **conditional vs unconditional
> generation.** Weaving = conditional (structure given = the warp = the parser/prompt/source). Knitting =
> unconditional (structure self-made from the spine). The question "two mechanisms or one?" becomes "is the
> warp supplied or knit?" — and that is a question about the *source of the structural axis*, not about two
> different engines.
