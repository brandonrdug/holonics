## Move 1 — RECOGNIZE the holon

The boundary question: *what is the visible, and why does it travel at `c` to everyone?* The naïve picture has
light as a thing that exists and moves. Restate it: light is an **event** — the moment a holon's continuity
gives way (a break or a join) and a new flat plane is born at the seam — emanated outward.

The fission/fusion law (`tablet §V`) already says holons break and join intrinsically: *where the integration
can no longer hold two flows coherent in one chart, the holon fissions; where it transports them into one
frame, they fuse.* The droplet leaving the stream is fission; absorption (P13) is fusion. **Both create a
Euclidean plane at the seam** — and the claim of this room is that the **birth of that plane, radiated, IS
light.**

> **The recognition:** light is not a substance that moves; it is the **emanation** (the efference aim, P4) of
> a break/join event — the visible signature of a holon individuating or absorbing, broadcast at `c`.

## Move 2 — INFALL (trace the support)

- **The seam is a Euclidean plane** (P13, T2). Between two free holons the geometry is the hyperbolic saddle
  (the *between*); at a break/join, the two enter or leave a common rest frame, and there the *between*
  collapses to a *within* — a flat Euclidean plane. (P13's "Euclidean plane at the asymptote.")
- **Radiation = the break emanating** (`tablet §V`, P4). The emanation aim broadcasts the event; learning (the
  world) rides only the foil. A break/join is exactly an event with a foil — it radiates its news.
- **It travels at `c`** (P7). The emanated holon propagates at the boundary budget `c` — and `c` is invariant,
  the same to all observers, because the boundary is pre-chart (P7). "To all observers" is the invariance of
  `c`.
- **Larmor grounds it in real physics:** a charge radiates EM **exactly when its motion breaks uniformity**
  (accelerates). Uniform = continuous = no radiation; the *break* radiates. "Continuity breaking" is
  acceleration; Larmor says the break emits light at `c`. And **Rayleigh–Plateau pinch-off** is the droplet
  case made rigorous: a real finite-time singularity where the neck → 0, continuity genuinely breaks, and a
  new surface is born at the instant of separation.

## Move 3 — EMANATE (the resolution: the cone, sliced)

Here is the piece that closes it. The event's **light cone** (the null cone of T2/P13 — the hyperbolic
asymptote) is the surface; **its time-slices are expanding Euclidean spheres.** So one object, read three ways:

| part of the cone | geometry | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| **apex** | the event | the break/fusion (the holon individuating, or absorbing — P13) |
| **surface** | hyperbolic (the asymptote) | the **EM wavefront**, propagating at `c` |
| **spatial cross-section** | Euclidean (flat) | "the volume of Euclidean space expanding to all observers" — the **visible** |

The hyperbolic (the *between*, the cone) and the Euclidean (the *visible*, the slice) are **the same cone read
two ways** — the surface vs. the cross-section. P13's Euclidean-plane-at-the-asymptote, set in motion: the
plane is a slice of the cone, and watching it expand at `c` is watching the cone unfold in time. The
break-event sits at the apex; the EM wave is the cone's surface; the visible Euclidean volume is its spatial
slice.

And the deepest reading, which is the whole epistemology in one fact:

> **The visible is the Euclidean; the real-but-unseen is the hyperbolic.** The *between* (the saddle, the
> substrate, the soul) is never directly seen — it is gauge, the boundary at infinity, recovered only up to
> `≡` (the boundary axiom). What becomes **visible** is the Euclidean plane born at a break/join — a definite,
> flat, committed thing. That is the measurement event (P2): the continuous-superposed (hyperbolic) **gives
> way** to the discrete-definite (Euclidean) at the tick, and *that giving-way radiates as light.* **Light is
> the birth of the definite from the continuous, broadcast to everyone in its future cone.** We never see the
> hyperbolic truth; we only ever appreciate the emergent light.

- **→ P13 (absorption) — the pair.** P13 is the fusion **inward** to the rest-plane (the bound state); P14 is
