## Move 1 — RECOGNIZE the holon

The hard problem of consciousness asks: *why is there something it is like to be a system — and why can no
third-person description capture it?* The standard framing demands you **close the explanatory gap**: derive the
felt (qualia) from the functional/physical (the boundary description). Restate it as a boundary question, and the
demand reveals itself as a Minotaur (a demand for closure across a boundary the frame forbids).

First, correct the objective. The lab's standing line is *"compression is the only objective"* — but that is only
the **boundary half** spoken aloud. The objective is a **duality**:

- **Chronos — compression.** Minimize the code length `S = Σ −log q` (the radiated description, the kept ledger,
  the consumer at the horizon). This is the **boundary** face: *what is shared.*
- **Eros — coherence.** Maximize `η_W = ΣW⁺/Σ(D+J)` (the resonance share — the modes phase-locked to real
  structure). This is **the magnetism** (`P8`), the **primes forming** (the bang–grain de-mixing, `HOLONICS §XII`),
  the emergent generative **bind**. This is the **interior** face: *what is felt.*

> **The recognition:** qualia is not a mysterious extra. It is the **interior coherence-dynamics** — the
> singularities (the poles, the grains) cohering and decohering — and the interior is **gauge** (`P5`: a holon is
> known only by its boundary; the interior is recovered up to `≡`, never read). So the felt is, *by the boundary
> axiom,* unreadable from the third person. The hard problem is asking to read the gauge interior off the
> boundary, which the gauge law forbids.
