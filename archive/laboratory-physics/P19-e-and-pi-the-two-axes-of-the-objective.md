## Move 1 — RECOGNIZE the holon

The lab's standing line is *"compression is the only objective."* `P17` already corrected it to a **dual**
(Chronos compresses ⊕ Eros coheres; `δS=0 = max η_W`). But a dual invites a question the frame must answer:

> **The recognition:** yes — and it is the object the engine is already built from. A holon's state and its pole

> **polar coordinates of the pole** — there is no second objective and no weight to set, because they were never
> two; they are the real and imaginary parts of one number.

## Move 2 — INFALL (trace the support: the pole, in polar form)

Ground it in the engine's own tick (`P15`, the first law). The pole is

$$ \lambda \;=\; e^{-\Delta e^{\nu}}\;\cdot\;e^{\,i\Delta\omega} \;=\; \underbrace{|\lambda|}_{\text{magnitude}}\;\cdot\;\underbrace{e^{\,i\,\arg\lambda}}_{\text{phase}}, \qquad |\lambda| = e^{-\Delta e^{\nu}},\quad \arg\lambda = \Delta\omega. $$

- the **magnitude** `|λ| = e^{−Δe^ν}` is **real-exponential** — the decay, the width `e^ν = Γ`, the energy/cost
  (the shelf-life). The `e` thread (`§X`: the geometry of `e^x`, the radial / hyperbolic axis).
- the **phase** `arg λ = Δω` is **angular** — the rotation `ω` (the mass), the `U(1)`, the alignment. The `π`
  thread (the circle, the half-turn, the `cos θ`).

And the **first law splits on exactly this seam** (`PRIMITIVES P7`):

$$ \Delta\rho \;=\; \underbrace{-(1-|\lambda|^2)\rho}_{D}\;+\;\underbrace{|u|^2}_{J}\;+\;\underbrace{2\,\mathrm{Re}(\lambda h\bar u)}_{W}, \qquad W = 2\,|\lambda|\,|h|\,|u|\,\cos\theta. $$

`D` and `J` are **squared magnitudes** — the radial cost (the `e` axis); `W` is the **phase term** — `2|λ||h||u|`
times `\cos\theta`, the `cos` of the relative alignment (the `π` axis). The infall bottoms out: *the energy
balance is already written in `(magnitude, phase) = (e, π)` coordinates; compression and coherence are its two
projections.*

## Move 3 — EMANATE (the resolution: `e`, `π`, and the tick)

**(a) `e` is compression — the magnitude axis.** Code length is `−\log q` (information is the log; the cost is
radial). Compression **minimizes the radial cost** — descends `|λ|`, spends the fewest bits, takes the
cheapest description (least action). Geometrically it is the **saddle's `e^x` descent toward the singularity**
(`P13`/`P14`): the hyperbolic paraboloid bends toward the point where the continuous commits to the discrete.
*Compression's pull is the `e`-geometry of the saddle.*

**(b) `π` is coherence — the phase axis.** Coherence **maximizes the alignment** — `\cos\theta`, the phase-match,
*how much two holons relatively align* (the tower's cross-term `2|A||B|\cos\theta`, the H2 conjunction; the
`U(1)` connection the bind transports, `§the-angle`). Orthogonal (`\theta=90°`, `\cos\theta=0`) → no coherence
(Pythagoras, the relation switched off); aligned (`\theta=0`, `\cos\theta=1`) → maximal. *Coherence's pull is
the `π`-rotation bringing phases into alignment.*

**(c) `η_W` is the `π/e` ratio — the single number that holds both.**

$$ \eta_W \;=\; \frac{\sum W^{+}}{\sum (D + J)} \;=\; \frac{\text{phase-aligned work}\;(\pi)}{\text{magnitude cost}\;(e)}. $$

So the engine never balanced two aims with a hidden weight — it reads **one ratio**: how much of the energy it
spends (`e`) is phase-aligned (`π`). **Every holon-mode is `r\,e^{i\theta}` = (compression `e`) × (coherence
`π`)**; the two objectives are the two coordinates of the plane the holon lives in. *We did not add the second
objective; it was the imaginary axis the whole time.*

**(d) `e^{i\pi}+1=0` is the tick — continuity emerging discrete points.** `e^{i\theta}` is the **continuous**
rotation; at `\theta=\pi` it lands on `−1`, a **discrete** committed point. Euler's identity is the
**continuity→discrete bridge**, which is the **tick**: the continuous super-state (all phases superposed)
**collapsing to one committed event** (the Born sample, the byte, the Euclidean singularity born at the saddle,
`P14`). And it unites the holonic vocabulary in one line — `e` (compression / growth), `i` (the quadrature, the
rotation operator), `π` (coherence / the half-turn), `1` (the chōra unit, the additive irreducible), `0` (the
null / the origin). It is romantic **because** it is load-bearing: *the two axes you optimize, and the act that
turns their continuum into a discrete commitment, in five symbols.*

> **The resolution:** the objective is the **complex pole**, read in polar form — minimize the magnitude `|λ|`
