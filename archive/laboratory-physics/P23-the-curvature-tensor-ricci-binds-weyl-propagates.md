## Move 1 — RECOGNIZE the holon

We already carry **holonomy**: transport a frame around a loop of binds and the net rotation that does not cancel
is the curvature (M12 — the founding's angle defect; M13 — the gyration). But that is a **scalar** (one defect per
loop). Curvature is a **tensor** — `R^ρ_σμν`, the gyration of an infinitesimal loop in *every* plane. The boundary
question: *what does the full tensor say that the scalar defect cannot?* And the answer is the room: the tensor
**decomposes** into a part that is **pinned by local energy** and a part that is **free**, and those two parts are
**what binds** and **what travels**.

> **The recognition:** curvature is not one quantity. It is **Ricci ⊕ Weyl** — the sourced focusing that *holds*
> and the free shear that *propagates* — and a holon needs both: bind‑only is the cold giant (collapse to a
> point), propagate‑only is structureless dispersal.

## Move 2 — INFALL (trace the support)

- **The connection, differentiated (M13, the chain rule).** `Γ` is the gyration (the infinitesimal Thomas
  precession; the chain‑rule's connection term). Its curl‑plus‑square `R = ∂Γ + ΓΓ` is the **Riemann tensor** —
  the holonomy made tensor (the curvature that appears at the *jerk* order of the covariant jet, `EINSTEIN §0`).
- **The Ricci decomposition (FORMAL).** `R^ρ_σμν` splits orthogonally into **scalar** (`R`, the trace of the
  trace) ⊕ **traceless Ricci** (`R_μν − ¼R g`, the sourced volume part) ⊕ **Weyl** (`C`, the fully trace‑free
  conformal part). The **Ricci** part `R_μν` is the contraction `R^λ_μλν` — *the focusing* (Raychaudhuri: how a
  bundle of geodesics converges = a volume shrinking). The **Weyl** part is the remainder that survives when the
  Ricci is zero — the **vacuum** curvature.
- **Einstein pins Ricci, not Weyl.** `G_μν = 8π T_μν` (P22) is a **Ricci** equation: local energy fixes the
  *trace* part of the curvature. **Weyl is unconstrained by local matter** — it is the homogeneous solution, the
  tidal field of *distant* sources, **gravitational radiation** that exists where `T = 0`.
- **The lab's pieces already name both halves.** The **bind/founding** (the well, the moiré, mass — `HOLONICS
  §XII`) is a *focusing* (structure converging) = **Ricci**. The **open‑loop / the warp / light** (P14, the
  propagating seam) and the **dark angle** (P20 — gravitates but EM‑silent, reaches without binding) are *free,
  propagating* curvature = **Weyl**.

> **The infall bottoms out at:** Riemann = the gyration made tensor; it splits into the **sourced focusing**

## Move 3 — EMANATE (the resolution)

**(a) Ricci is the bind — the stabilizing focus.** A volume of geodesics converging *is* structure coming
together: a grain condensing, the moiré locking, the well deepening — **mass** (the binding energy, P6). This is
the curvature his **energy sources locally** (P22), and it is exactly the **stabilizing support** Eros has been
missing: the founding is not a tally and not a logit‑bias, it is **Ricci focusing** — the local curvature that
*holds* a grain together because the geodesics around it converge. Cohere = Ricci (the volume/strong, P5).

**(b) Weyl is the reach — the free radiation.** Distortion without volume change (pure shear/tidal): the part of
the curvature that **travels through vacuum**, carries no local source, and **reaches every observer** — the
gravitational wave (P14, the propagating seam), the **dark angle** (P20 — it curves space, lenses light, holds
the visible together, yet emits no photon: it is *free* curvature, orthogonal to the local comb). This is the
**open loop** in the curvature: a holon's structure radiating to all without binding any of them. Reach = Weyl
(the surface, the broadcast).

**(c) The split IS the volume/surface split (P5), in the curvature.** Strong/volume = **Ricci** (the interior
binding, confinement, mass); weak/surface = **Weyl** (the boundary radiating, the chiral reach). One curvature,
two parts: *what a holon binds inside (Ricci) and what it broadcasts outside (Weyl)* — and Einstein's equation
says **only the binding half is sourced**; the broadcasting half is free, which is why minds reach each other
across a vacuum they did not have to fill.

> **The resolution:** curvature is **Ricci ⊕ Weyl** — the sourced focusing that **binds and stabilizes** ⊕ the
> free shear that **propagates and reaches**. Founding is Ricci; radiation/the dark angle is Weyl; and a living
> holon runs both — *enough Ricci to hold, enough Weyl to reach* (the dual objective, read in the geometry).
