## What is here, and at what grade

The wing carries its own grading vocabulary, which is **not** `canon/EPISTEMIC_GRADES.md`'s. Its
terms, from the documents themselves:

| their term | what it meant there |
|---|---|
| `FORMAL` | standard mathematics, correctly stated, within a declared frame |
| `HOMOLOGY` | a structural correspondence asserted between the framework and a physical theory |
| `HUNCH` | flown deliberately high; named rather than hedged |
| `OPEN` | not closed; the document lists the moves that would close it |

**Every physics document in this set is `OPEN`.** None was ever closed, and none may be cited as
established. Where a document asserts an identification between a holonic construction and a physical
theory, that identification is `HOMOLOGY` or `HUNCH` by the wing's own marking — and
`canon/HISTORY_AND_REFINEMENT.md` independently bars canonising *"physical-spacetime identifications
without typed translation hypotheses."*

## Two provenance cautions that travel with this directory

**Quotations here are largely uncertifiable, and that is not the same as fabricated.** The surviving
`conv_chord` transcript corpus spans roughly May–June 2026. Documents dated July and later carry
Brandon quotations that cannot be checked against it. `CLAUDE.md` §9 rule 3 governs: `UNCERTIFIABLE`
is not `fabricated`; the adjudicable claim is narrow — *a quotation deposited during a session whose
transcript survives, and absent from it, is fabricated.* Two of the four above **do** match surviving
turns, which is evidence for the wing's general fidelity rather than against it.

**One measurement here is unrecoverable.** `src/eros/um/SINGULARITIES.md` reports a 54× drop in bound
action (`3.6e9 → 6.7e7`) and attributes it to `living.wgsl`. **That file does not exist at
`a07ff376`** — the kernel directory holds eighteen kernels and that is not among them. The figure
cites a file that cannot be read at the commit that reports it, and may not be cited as evidence.
