## Move 1 — RECOGNIZE the holon

The dark-matter problem: galaxies rotate, and clusters hold together, as though ~5–6× more mass were present
than the light reveals; and ~68% of the universe's energy budget (dark energy) drives an *accelerating*
expansion. The standard framing hunts a new **particle** (WIMP, axion) for the matter and a new **field**
(quintessence) or constant (`Λ`) for the energy — two unknowns bolted onto the inventory. Restate it as a
boundary question. By P5 there is **one charge — information** — read at the boundary as **one emanation
vector** with two components: an **angle** (gravity) and a **magnitude** (EM). The boundary question is not
*"what extra stuff is there?"* but: **can the two components of the one emanation decouple — can a holon emanate
an angle (gravitate) while emanating no magnitude (stay EM-dark)?**

> **The recognition:** dark matter is not a new substance; it is the **angle without the magnitude** — a holon
> whose geometric emanation (gravity) reaches us while its phase emanation (EM) does not, because EM is
> conditional on a bind we do not share with it. The "missing mass" is information we feel by its angle and
> cannot read by its light.
