# Experimental evidence

`observations/` contains every `README.md`, `RESULTS.md`, and `REPORT.json` found under the
laboratory observation tree at extraction: 190 human-readable/structured evidence files from 1630
raw observation files.

Large raw bodies, renders, paths, and intermediate populations remain in the laboratory and are
listed in `../provenance/LABORATORY_ONLY.md`. The summaries here retain the declared inputs,
results, caveats, and paths back to those raw artifacts without turning 269 MiB of generated
observer material into the new engine's source standing.
