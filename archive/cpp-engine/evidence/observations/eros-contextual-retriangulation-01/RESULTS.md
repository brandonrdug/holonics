## Question and stop

This cell asked:

> Can previously cultivated Soma Standing carry a useful context-dependent portion of inherited
> transformer ecology into later conduct while requiring less source participation, and does
> changed context reopen the relation?

The retained source is the exact OLMo 2 layer-7 ecology from
`eros-pretrained-ecology-cultivation-01`. No transformer was rerun. Six conversation/prose
`drop_then_reverse` currents form one training chart. Three code-comment currents are related
same-operation holdouts; three `reverse_then_drop` currents are changed-operation holdouts.

The pivot law was fixed before Soma conduct:

1. scan the 8,192 source activation columns once in ordinal order;
2. retain a column exactly when it raises rational rank on the six training currents; and
3. stop at rank six, without searching alternate charts or tuning against holdouts.

The stop was one exact law hand-up, lower-row departure, exact rest/remount, twelve later conducts,
one no-Standing control, and exact RIDE/OPEN residuals. No model run, alternate pivot search,
renderer, CUDA passage, larger model, response generation, or engine revision entered.

## The local transformation

Let `G` be the `6 x 8,192` exact BF16-decoded activation chart and let `Y` be the `6 x 5` exact
factorized receiver output on axes

```text
[76,856,954,1326,1476].
```

Deterministic elimination found the first full-rank column chart

```text
P = [0,1,2,3,4,5].
```

For `A=G[:,P]`, exact rational elimination formed the unique local law

```text
A K = Y,                 K in Q^(6 x 5).
```

`K` has 30 nonzero coefficients. Its largest numerator and denominator use 86 and 81 bits,
respectively. Python/Fraction source formation and an independent Rust/BigRational derivation
produce the identical law.

This is an alternate local factorization of the declared six-current chart. It is not six
independent neural experts, a pruning result, or proof that the first six OLMo factors carry a
portable ability.

## What the result does and does not show

The six training equations make `A K=Y` a square interpolation problem. Once `A` is nonsingular,
exact reproduction of those six rows is guaranteed. Training closure by itself is therefore not
evidence of learned generality.

The heldouts are the informative contrast. They show:

- the completed higher law can replace its lower row carriers and still change later conduct;
- a smaller present current can recruit that relation without rescanning 122,685 source
  candidates;
- one related context preserves the coarse receiver geometry; and
- most related contexts, and every changed-operation context, expose the law's local boundary.

The law is dense and its heldout rational predictions have much larger denominators than the BF16
source faces. That is consistent with exact extrapolation outside a small interpolation chart, not
with a stable globally compressed skill.

## Consequence

The experiment replaces the earlier “small selected factor set” picture with a more precise
mechanism: an inherited ecology can supply exact local antecedent/consequent charts; Eros can carry
those charts through completion into a new transformation law; later context activates that law;
and mismatch returns as situated residual rather than failure or global negation.

What remains open is how an OPEN residual should cultivate, divide, or rebase a population of local
charts without turning the source into a full-reservoir scan or fitting one square interpolant per
case. Grounded downstream behavior and actual upstream physical savings also remain separate
questions. This result schedules neither automatically.
