## Question held fixed

Can ordinary exposure to an existing text ecology cause Eros-completed regions to become
higher-grain Standing which participates in the conduct of a nonidentical later utterance?

This was a direct-machine continuation of the earlier raw-octet text result and the historical
Holobrochos cohered-segment observations. It did not reuse the historical segmenter.

The inherited tokenizer supplied categorical source addresses only. One complete corpus line was
one utterance worldline. All tokenizer occurrences in that line were co-present, and the source
declared oriented incidence only between adjacent occurrences inside the line:

```text
one tokenizer-addressed utterance
  -> one regional cell containing its ordered adjacent-token arcs
  -> one direct atomic Eros transition
  -> ended token carriers depart
  -> the completed regional constituent remains as Standing
```

No adjacency crossed a line. No embedding, transformer activation, semantic label, next-token
score, optimizer, reward, or scalar loss entered the machine.

## Why the apparent composition is not yet linguistic

The strongest planned signature was a held-out continuation which imported one higher
constituent carrying several training utterances with different slot material. That signature
appears superficially:

```text
that's your nose
that's your balloon
```

Both candidates imported a grain-928 constituent with ancestry in 1,121 training utterances.
That is not selective frame/slot composition. The same constituent was recruited by every
chronological exact-recurrence actual and sibling, including contrasts such as:

```text
what's that / what's a
oh yeah / oh bear
good job / good for
```

The candidate did not recover a compact “that's your ___” construction. One exact exposed
adjacent-token interface admitted the entire connected regional constituent. Examples such as
`your button / your idea` and `hey violet / hey buddy` show the same boundary more starkly: one
candidate remained a fresh grain-2 OPEN cell while its sibling imported either one earlier line
or the entire 1,121-line field.

The run therefore establishes regional percolation through exact interfaces, not useful
linguistic abstraction. The event law made the complete utterance one cell and allowed every
adjacent pair to be an exterior interface. Recurrent pair overlap progressively joined many
utterances into one component. A later match could then recruit that whole component regardless
of which small part was relevant to the probe.

This is the direct-machine version of the earlier “complexity without language” warning. It is
more informative than the historical result because the causal source of the complexity is now
exact: whole-utterance regional closure plus exact pair-boundary percolation.

## Completion changes availability; it does not preserve a pair ledger

The unseen-pair controls also expose a more subtle and useful fact. All four actual pairs were
absent from training and remained OPEN. Their siblings were source-observed pairs, but only two
of four recruited prior Standing.

An exact inspection of the large chronological constituent carried through an unrelated probe
found:

| Observed sibling pair | Interior instances | Exposed instances | Later recruitable |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `hi big` | 3 | 0 | no |
| `where did` | 2 | 0 | no |
| `is in` | 4 | 1 | yes |
| `yeah yeah` | 1 | 1 | yes |

The first two relations were not forgotten and were not absent. They had become interior to a
higher completion and were no longer available at that later boundary. The latter two still had
one exposed instance and recruited the large constituent.

This agrees with the earlier raw-text observation that recurrence is phase-local rather than a
monotone count. Standing is not an ancestry database. What a later current can contact depends on
the contemporary exterior of the completed geometry.

## Supported conclusion

The experiment answers the fixed question in two parts.

What is established:

1. ordinary tokenized corpus exposure enters the direct machine without a historical segmenter;
2. repeated regional contact emits higher-grain Standing and lets lower constituents depart;
3. that Standing survives source departure and participates in nonidentical later utterance
   currents;
4. chronological and within-utterance-reversed ecologies produce materially different later
   conduct; and
5. completion can retain an observed relation internally while making it unavailable at a later
   exterior.

What is not established:

1. selective phrase, frame, slot, or contextual composition;
2. a useful language distribution or response behavior;
3. automatic founding of a context axis;
4. a semantic interpretation of tokenizer identities; or
5. an efficient large-result observer format.

The missing relation is now concrete. It is not “more corpus” and not a selector over the giant
component. The application law needs a lawful event factorization in which a later local boundary
can recruit the relevant completed region without importing every interior joined through some
other pair. Text already supplies native internal order and later consequences; a future
construction may test whether overlapping ordered regions and actual continuation return create
that factorization. This result does not schedule it.
