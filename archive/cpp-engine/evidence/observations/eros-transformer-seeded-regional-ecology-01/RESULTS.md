## The important negative result

The stored leader is not a portable semantic edge and is not the transformer's contextual law.
When OLMo itself receives the target chronology, it repoints all three selected heads away from the
component-relative occurrences transported above. Heads 2 and 13 choose other occurrences of the
same token address; head 9 chooses a different token address entirely.

Therefore this run does **not** establish transformer-ecology inheritance or the project's
geometric compression. It establishes that a frozen winner capability can stand and recur in the
regional carrier after its tensor source departs. Preserving that finished winner does not preserve
the ecology which selected it.

The corrected next question is not whether more finished arcs should be retained. It is whether
pre-context constituent material plus the inherited transformation law can reform the complete
receiver-relative field when a new context enters. That question is measured separately; this
result schedules nothing.
