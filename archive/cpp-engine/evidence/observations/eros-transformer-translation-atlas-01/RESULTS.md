## Interpretation and boundary

This cell cultivates a translation atlas in the strict local sense: repeated consequence across
two charts becomes a partial map, the map outlives its source passage, and a third chart either
conducts through it or exposes its precise residual. Training disagreement creates no counterfeit
map. Held-out disagreement changes neither the previous map nor the source history; it becomes new
terrain for a possible later relation.

The result does not establish a universal taxonomy, general semantic equivalence, automatic site
discovery, cross-model transport, alternate transformer architecture, useful response generation,
or reduced computation. The source transformer world supplied the site identities and receiver
observations. The bounded construction shows how those lawful inherited faces become a partial
Soma ecology without treating the transformer as a magic black box or copying its complete active
state.
