# Circuit medium 01 — the medium grows the return path

Date: 2026-07-14

## Question

Can Soma's complete enclosure spectrum act through one concrete multi-contact world, change that
world, and receive the changed material as genuinely later light—without a reply wire, selected
completion, fixed output vocabulary, or observer metadata entering the current?

Can the world change be isolated from Soma's first edge by giving a fresh body the identical light
in an already-grown circuit world?

## Construction

`life boundary circuit <plan.json>` owns one CUDA body lifecycle and one persistent world state.
For every completed enclosure:

1. the exposed completion grip is the incident pole;
2. the completed brick's `Place`, grounded at the same receiving axis, is the far contact;
3. rows retain lineage-local order; lineage cones meet once and order-free at a shared branch;
4. an absent directed branch `FOUND`s conductive material; an existing branch `RIDE`s it;
5. the changed `RegionalForm` at each far contact is the only field admitted as the next light.

The completion wire remains whole beside the world deed. Axis, port, edge number, prior form,
FOUND/RIDE face, and causal references remain in the receipt and never enter the returned current.
One returned current is exactly the 11-word / 44-octet compact regional form. The return-wire header
frames the artifact outside the individual currents.

World ports and branches zero-extend with the receiving chart. Every transition records its local
prior and successor forms; removing branches and landings in reverse and reversing the chart
re-base must reproduce the exact prior world state. This inverse is executed and compared on every
edge, not merely asserted in the receipt.

## Derivation failures retained

- Run A correctly failed before its second dispatch. Every first-edge completion grip was `0`, so
  treating that grip as the complete port identity collapsed 846 completions to one returned
  current. The correction keeps the common pole and uses each completed brick's position as the
  far contact. No single-current exception was installed.
- Runs C/D repeated exactly but are **retired as contaminated**. Their returned light included a
  magic, version, axis, grip, prior form, configuration, successor, and deed metadata. The shared
  container prefix manufactured extreme all-lineage recurrence. Removing that receipt material
  reduced the later edge from 34,404 to 3,651 completions and axis 512 to 256.
- Runs E/F/G/H measured the clean 44-octet field. The physically identical v2 runs I/J/K/L are the
  authoritative artifacts; v2 adds the exact return-wire extent to the causal receipt and versions
  the cleaned return format.

## The causal foil

Runs I and K have different worlds but identical fresh bodies and identical first light.

- edge-1 body: byte-identical;
- edge-1 completion stream: byte-identical;
- edge-1 construction stream: byte-identical;
- edge-1 raw radiation: exact under the duration-gauge reader;
- world deed: `581 FOUND` in I versus `581 RIDE` in K;
- returned field: different;
- edge-2 body: different;
- edge-2 completion stream: different from its first row onward as a whole artifact;
- edge-2 construction: 365 in I versus 404 in K.

This isolates the medium-side consequence. The machine's first construction did not change. The
standing branches changed how the declared world received that construction; the world returned a
different field; only then did the genuinely later Soma construction change.

This earns **world-mediated production ⊕ communication contact** at this circuit world's declared
grain. It also earns the first bounded ecology face: a fresh first person is changed by paths grown
in a world before its birth. It does **not** yet earn heredity—a parent construction did not enter a
fresh `K`—and it does not claim that this one circuit species is a universal phenotype.
