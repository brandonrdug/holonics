# Holonics research and construction

Develop the consequence the user requested from the programme's existing constructions.
An intuition is a starting relation to work, not a subject to classify and set aside.
Physics, mathematics and computation have their own productive returns; do not redirect
one into another because a familiar implementation is easier to finish.

## Recover the relevant construction

Resolve the checkout root first. The local repositories are usually
`~/Workspaces/holonics` and the frozen predecessor `~/Workspaces/laboratory`.
Use the active repository's AGENTS, current position and roadmap, retaining context already
read. This skill provides a method, not another authority or schedule.

Read the user's requested relation and the current subject guide. Search both its vocabulary
and its mathematical operations, then follow the actual source imports/callers. A file whose
name resembles the question is an entry point, not the entire evidence aperture. When prior
research is invoked, follow its dated record, direct-message coordinates and predecessor
source before declaring a construction missing. `docs/AGENT_PROTOCOL.md` gives that route.
Preserve the user's later corrections when interpreting historical agent prose.

Load only the route needed:

- [Physics](references/physics.md): forces, atoms, fields, strings, geometry or physical realization.
- [Mathematics](references/mathematics.md): theorem development, source comparisons, topology,
  number theory, spectral/complexity questions and formalization.
- [Computation](references/computation.md): native implementation, generated outputs,
  representation, performance and repeated-execution failures.

## Turn recovery into a consequence

Write the actual chain needed by this request, with available owners on its arrows. Attempt
the composition. If it fails, expose the first incompatible type, missing relation or
counterexample and work that relation. “Needs a map”, “sufficient”, “intelligent”, “physical”
or “not established” alone does not specify a task or complete a requested investigation.

Keep the status of each claim accurate while advancing it. A conditional law is useful when
its hypotheses are supplied. An interpretation should return a derived subclaim, a tested
construction, or an explicit failed equation that narrows it. Never relabel an unproved claim
as proved to satisfy ambition; never use its label as a substitute for attempting the work.

Use existing mathematical/physical owners and bind the consequence to its consuming theorem,
experiment or authorized application. A finite matter Hamiltonian is a physical construction
at its stated approximation; a complete atomic or field theory has further specified content.
A useful solver output does not wait for a conversation demo or a universal intelligence law.

## Keep continuity and verify the return

Preserve the requested consequence, why it matters, the current relation, recovered dependencies,
important failed attempt and next discriminating result in the existing position/record.
Do this before context handoff; do not restart the whole archive or silently drop a requested
branch after handoff. If another branch is deferred, name its concrete unfinished operation.

Verify the changed relation at its actual consumer. Inspect returned source/output and measured
units rather than relying on an agent summary, test count or graph shape. Reconcile the live
clause and consumers when correcting prior prose. Follow `docs/DEVELOPMENT.md` for checks and
the evidence protocol for records; this skill adds no blanket gate, ledger or approval process.
When work is delegated, keep file ownership explicit and run final integration after the
changing owner returns; a build of an intermediate edit is not the completed artifact's check.
