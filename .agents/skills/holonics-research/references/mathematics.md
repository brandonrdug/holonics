# Carry the mathematical question through its actual source

Start with the user-requested relation and existing subject owners in `docs/FORMAL_FRAMEWORK.md`
and `docs/ARCHITECTURE_MAP.md`. Search statements and operations as well as names. A theorem
may survive a file move or historical naming change; an old “open” list may predate its owner.

Read the theorem statement, assumptions and dependencies. Distinguish a structure containing
the desired conclusion as a hypothesis from a derivation that constructs it. When adapting a
general lemma, instantiate the actual source and prove the connecting equation. A second
formula with the same name is not yet a binding to the existing owner.

For cross-domain work, exhibit the source/target map and compute what it preserves. An
invariant can reveal a quotient without classifying its whole source. Try a separating pair
when a receiver seems to determine more than it retains; use the failed pair to refine the
construction, not to dismiss the surrounding subject.

Separate an extremum certificate from the mechanism that approaches it. Recover existing
Swing, action/first-variation and constitutive constructions before proposing enumeration.
Keep an oriented difference before taking a magnitude; one-sided bounds often suffice.
An identity such as `abs(x)=sqrt(x^2)` specifies a mathematical receiver, not a unique
algorithm, physical motion or clock. A retained source/phase lift determines which path
information remains available. Local stationarity alone is not global optimality.

For Millennium/complexity questions, identify the actual endpoint statement and current local
return. Continue a named sign, remainder, realization, representation or source problem.
Do not demand an endpoint solution before using a local theorem, or substitute another HNN
demonstration for the mathematical question. For algorithmic claims keep input encoding,
uniform construction, preprocessing, representation/decoder size and bit complexity visible.
Finite enumeration and cheap lookup alone do not establish a polynomial-time family.

Use `tools/lean_check.sh` on changed owners and relevant consumers. Inspect failures under
progress supervision. Lean remains exterior verification. A passing theorem verifies its
statement; it does not automatically execute in native HNN or calibrate a physical model.
Conversely an exterior exact solver already returns a mathematical inference at its supplied
domain. Preserve useful theorem/application returns and pursue the next authorized consequence.
