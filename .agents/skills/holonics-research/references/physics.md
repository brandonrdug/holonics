Read `docs/MASS_ENERGY_AND_CAUSAL_TRANSPORT.md` and the relevant part of
`docs/FORMAL_FRAMEWORK.md`, then follow source owners from `docs/ARCHITECTURE_MAP.md`.

Search by the physical relation, not only the pictured object. A knot request may require
connection, holonomy, gauge, curvature, action, Hamiltonian and current owners. Inspect the
chain from configuration/field to an observable before claiming it is absent.

Recover the action or constitutive equation and follow its variation/evolution into energy,
charge, force, spectrum or another requested observable. State which coefficients and boundary
conditions were supplied and which consequences were derived. Calibration is required for a
numerical physical prediction, not to forbid derivation with symbolic physical coefficients.

Keep distinctions that change the calculation: base dimension versus internal representation;
topological winding versus finite phase; real-time unitary versus heat flow; frame rebase versus
physical intervention; homogeneous potential versus sourced dynamics. Demonstrate the useful
connection through them instead of listing differences and stopping.

For a new correspondence, build its first source-qualified case and a separator for an
overstrong quotient. For example, a complex link phase must reach the matter Hamiltonian before
claiming a spectral effect; integral compact modes need a spectral receiver before a finite
phase reading can be assessed as a compression. These are worked examples, not obligatory
workloads for every physics question. Follow the requested physical direction through a result.
