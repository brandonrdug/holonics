# One family-supported descent inquiry

For the congruent-number family E_n over Q given by y^2 = x^3 - n^2 x with n > 0,
explain why the two descent slots x and x - n determine square classes supported only
at primes dividing 2n. Return the exact hypotheses and the dependency-minimal argument
from even valuations away from 2n to a finite pair of divisor classes.

Requested receiver faces: a derivation, the exact theorem statement, a Lean-checkable
proof passage or a typed obstruction, the n = 0 boundary separator, and a vector diagram
whose incidence agrees with the returned operation route.
