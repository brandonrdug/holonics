## 2. The Newton–Cotes question, answered

The Newton–Cotes formulas are the **aperture face** of what he means: a finite weighted
census at equal spacing, exact on a declared polynomial band — in the method atlas's terms,
a finite stratum, a table.  **Integration by reflection is what the same census becomes
when the interval closes into a loop**, and the exact law has one classical owner:

- The trapezoidal rule's error is carried entirely by boundary derivative differences (the
  Euler–Maclaurin corrections, whose coefficients are zeta values).  **Reflection deletes
  the boundary** — fold the interval onto a circle (the method of images; classically,
  Clenshaw–Curtis quadrature), and every correction cancels.
- On a closed loop, equal-spacing summation is exact on the winding band and blind exactly
  to the windings the frame divides: `Σ_{j<n} ζⁿ-th-root^{jk} = n·[n ∣ k]` — aliasing.
  **This is the same mechanism as reduction at a prime in the torsion census**: a compact
  frame reading a winding band whole.  The exact law behind both is **Poisson summation**.
- *"Integration through Holonic Interactions in a diffusion kind of way"* has a literal
  classical owner: **the heat kernel on a quotient is the sum of the free kernel over all
  images** — diffusion on a closed or tiled space is the winding-sum of interactions with
  one's own reflections.  On a lattice the image-sum is a **theta function**, whose
  self-duality under the heat flow *is* the reflection; and the sphere-packing bounds
  (Cohn–Elkies; Viazovska in dimensions eight and twenty-four) are Poisson summation on
  lattices.  **Fractal-packing** — the image-sum under a self-similar group instead of a
  lattice, with anomalous diffusion exponents — is the arm still to derive:
