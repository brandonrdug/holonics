## 3. The law

[agent-inferred throughout; every identity checked on rationals by
`region_rulers.py`, rulers 1 to 6 and 9]

### 3.1 The built move is a turned mean

With `w = d/(1 + d)` the built move reads `Q(t) = (1 − w) û Q(t−1) + w N₀ b(t)`, `û = γ̂(q)` a
unit turn: the new charge is a mean of the turned old charge and the site's drive at `N₀`, the
site taking the share `w`. In the built circle `w` is read from the site alone.

### 3.2 The two-way chain, and what its one-way factor carries

Take a strand's sites `s = 1 … T` with complex values `f(s)`, and the quadratic energy

`E(f) = Σ_s c |f(s+1) − û(s+1) f(s)|² + Σ_s j(s) |f(s) − N₀ b(s)|²`,  `c = 1/q₀`.

The first sum is the passage between neighbouring sites (a turn `û` and one series stiffness `c`).
The second is each site's port: a conductance `j(s) ≥ 0`, the site's mass, drawing the strand's
value toward the site's drive. The minimizer is the two-way field of the strand: every site's value
given every site's drive, each site hearing both sides. On the objects it is a tube whose sites are
contacts to their drives, with one constitution (`c`, `j`).

Eliminate the sites behind site `t` (the tube's `Λ_DN`, `tube.dn-elimination`). What remains of
the half strand is one element at site `t`: a stiffness `n(t)` toward a value. So the one-way
transport carries a pair `(η, n)`, an undivided ratio whose value is the charge `Q = η/n`:

- at the site: `η ← û η + j N₀ b`, `n ← n + j`, and the charge after the site is `Q = η/n`;
- to the next site: `(η, n) ← (η, n)/(1 + q₀ n)`, the series rule (`n` in series with `c`).

Ruler 3 checks this against a dense solve on 12 random strands of 14 sites (masses 0 to 5, eight
turns): for every length the half strand's minimizer at its last site is the scan's charge there;
the energy's stiffness in that site's value is the scan's count `n`; and at every site of the whole
strand the two-way minimizer is

`f(t) = (n_L Q_L + n_R Q_R + j N₀ b)/(n_L + n_R + j)`,

the left arrival, the right arrival and the site, each weighed by its count.

### 3.3 The gate form: the friction is what the site brings over what the circle holds

Write the count as a range, `R = 1/(q₀ n)` (the arriving count, in places), and the site's mass
as `m = q₀ j`. Then the pair's move is the built move with

`d(t) = j(t)/n(t) = m(t) R(t)`,  `R(t+1) = 1 + R(t)/(1 + d(t))`.

- The friction at a site is a ratio of two things the built law reads as one: what the site brings
  (`m`, its own) over what the circle holds (`n`, the strand's behind it). `R` is the circle's
  **back range**: how many places of passage the held charge is worth.
- A passage costs one place of range (`R → R + 1`), and a site of mass divides the range it finds
  by `1 + d`.
- Ruler 2: on 1,920 sites of 40 random strands the pair form and the gate form give one charge at
  every site, `n = 1/(q₀ R)` and `d = j/n` at every site, and the move stays the built one:
  `|γ| + κ/N₀ = 1`, `|Q| ≤ N₀ max|b|`.

On the Swing. The back range's move at a site is the Möbius move of
`[[1 + m, 1], [m, 1]] = [[1, 1], [0, 1]] · [[1, 0], [m, 1]]`, determinant one: two shears, the
passage adding a place to the range and the site's mass adding to its inverse (the count). At a
site of no mass it is the shear alone, free fall, `R → R + 1`. At a site of mass the two shears
compose a boost, and its trace gives `(2 + m)² = K + K⁻¹ + 2` at `K = (1 + d)² = 1/|γ|²`, which
is `m = d²/(1 + d)` (ruler 1).

So `contact.friction-range-mass`'s mass is recovered twice over: it is the rest of the count's
balance, and the Swing's trace identity for the back range's boost. On a strand of one mass the
back range rests at `R* = (1 + d)/d`, the group, with `d = m R*` and `d²/(1 + d) = m`; from any
start the gap to the rest falls at every step (ruler 1, from `R = 1` and `R = 1000`). **The built
circle is the derived circle at its rest on a uniform strand.** At the founding `q₀ = 1/20`,
`j = 1`, `m₀ = 1/20`, `R = N₀ = 5`, `d = 1/4`.

### 3.4 On the built chart

The built chart needs no second chart beside it. Read `d̂(t) = d₀ 2^ℓ(t)` as the site's own rest
friction, the friction a strand of such sites rests at, and take

`m(t) = d̂(t)²/(1 + d̂(t))`,  `d(t) = m(t) R(t)`,  `R` at the region's edge the first site's own
rest `(1 + d̂)/d̂`.

Then on a strand whose sites read one `ℓ` the derived circle is the built circle at every site, the
first included, at any friction from one grain up (ruler 9, six uniform strands). The two differ
only where `ℓ` varies along a strand. The log chart, its floor of one grain and its covector stand
as they are.

### 3.5 What a region sets

- **A site of no mass** turns the charge and keeps its modulus (`d = 0`), and the back range grows
  one place a site across a run of them (ruler 4). The ratio `Q = η/n` holds; the pair `(η, n)`
  leaks at every site. So "an open site holds" is true of the ratio, and no store is at rest: the
  circle's count, its quality, falls at every passage.
- **A letter after a gap.** From the founding's rest, a letter after `g` sites of no mass meets a
  back range of `g + 5`, has `d = (g + 5)/20` and takes `(g + 5)/(g + 25)` of the charge: 1/5 at
  no gap, 3/13 at 1, 1/3 at 5, 1/2 at 15, 2/3 at 35, 4/5 at 75 (ruler 4). Sites charted at a low
  friction, `d̂ = 2^(−10)`, give these shares to three digits (ruler 9).
- **A region written thinly**, letters one site in `g` and the sites between of no mass, rests at
  `d²/(1 + d) = g q₀` at its letters (ruler 5):

  | `g` | Friction at a letter | Letters heard | Sites heard | Built circle at the founding: the letters' share of the charge |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1 | 1/4 | 5.00 | 5.0 | 1 |
  | 2 | 0.3702 | 3.70 | 7.4 | 5/9 |
  | 4 | 0.5583 | 2.79 | 11.2 | 125/369, 0.339 |
  | 8 | 0.8633 | 2.16 | 17.3 | 0.240 |
  | 10 | 1 | 2.00 | 20.0 | 5⁹/(5¹⁰ − 4¹⁰), 0.224 |
  | 16 | 1.3798 | 1.72 | 27.6 | 0.206 |

  (decimals are readings of the rest's root, bracketed to `10⁻⁹` on rationals.) The circle counts
  neither letters nor sites: as the writing thins, its reach in sites grows and its reach in
  letters falls, each as the root of the gap while `g q₀` is small (`d ≈ √(g q₀)`).
- **The built circle's bind.** With one friction a site and every site driving, the letters' share
  of the charge in such a region is `w/(1 − (1 − w)^g)`, the last column: at one letter in eight,
  three quarters of the charge is the drive of the sites between. If instead the chart sets the
  sites between to hold (R1), a letter still takes `w = d/(1 + d)` at any gap, 1/5 at the
  founding, so after 75 open sites four fifths of the charge is from letters at least 75 sites
  back. The built circle dilutes or goes stale. The count removes the bind: reach across the
  unwritten is set by the open sites' mass, and sharpness among the written by the letters' mass.

### 3.6 What stands

- Passivity and the bound: `|γ| + κ/N₀ = 1` and `|Q| ≤ N₀ max|b|` at every site (ruler 2).
- The back range is bounded, `1 ≤ R ≤ R_edge + (sites passed)`, and it forgets: the slope of its
  move in `R` is `1/(1 + mR)² = |γ|²` at a site of mass and one at a site of none (ruler 6).
- The founding: with `L = 0` and `ℓ_b = 0` every site reads `d̂ = 1/4`, `R = 5` at every site, and
  the circle is the built founding.

### 3.7 The two arrivals at a place (owed, not part of a first attempt)

The two-way field at a site weighs each arrival by its count (§3.2). The built place reads the two
arriving ratios through located charts (`p = a Q`, returned through `W`), and a chart of the site
cannot know an arrival's count. Reading the pair's first member in place of the ratio,
`p = a · n Q` in the founding's units, would make a thin or stale charge arrive faint, harmonic in
its back range, which is the octave ladder's falloff (`wave.matched-friction-reach`) on one
circle. It is a second law. It is tested alone, after the count, with its own guess.

## 5. What sets a lap's balance between regions

### 5.1 The lever rule

[agent-inferred; standard for a quadratic code]

Let regions `r` share one entry `θ`, each with code `c_r + s_r θ + ½ h_r θ²` near the entry's
place and weight `w_r` in the sum a lap descends. The entry rests at

`θ* = −Σ w_r s_r / Σ w_r h_r = Σ w_r h_r z_r / Σ w_r h_r`,  `z_r = −s_r/h_r` region `r`'s own zero.

Each region is weighed by its weight times its stiffness. Region `r` pays `½ h_r (θ* − z_r)²`, and
with an entry of its own it would gain `s_r²/(2h_r)` from the shared place. A rate sets the spread
about `θ*` and, to first order, not `θ*` (`deposition.rate-temperature-standing-spread`).
Re-weighing the regions moves `θ*` along the lever and costs the first weighing's joint at second
order.

## 6. Charts

[from training; each is a chart of §3 on the objects]

- **A ladder line.** `R(t+1) = 1 + 1/(m + 1/R)` is the input resistance of a ladder with a unit
  series resistance a place and a shunt conductance `m` at a site: the tube's `Λ_DN` read from one
  end. Nothing electrical transfers beyond the series and parallel rules already in the atlas.
- **A recursive estimator.** The pair `(η, n)` is the information form of a running estimate, `d`
  its gain, the passage its loss of count, and the uniform rest its steady gain; the built circle
  is the steady gain used everywhere. What does not transfer: no noise model is assumed here. A
  site's mass is a located port conductance, never a measured variance, and no estimator's
  apparatus enters the machine.
- **Screening.** Where letters stand at density `ρ = 1/g` the region's mass is `μ² ≈ q₀ ρ` and its
  range grows as `1/√ρ`, the screening length of a medium of charges at that density.
- **The Swing.** §3.3: the passage is a shear, the mass is the dual shear, and a site's move is
  their boost.

## 7. For a build

[agent-inferred; the decisions are the circle line's, and its guesses are fixed in its guess file]

- **The forward** carries one more value a circle along the scan, `R` (or the pair), and reads
  `d = m R` where it read `d`. The move, its coupling and its bound are the built ones.
- **The reverse** carries `R`'s covector back along the scan: `∂R'/∂R = 1/(1 + d)²`,
  `∂R'/∂m = −R²/(1 + d)²`, `∂d/∂m = R`, `∂d/∂R = m`. It forgets at the charge's own rate squared.
- **Widths** follow from `1 ≤ R ≤ R_edge + (sites passed)` and the friction's floor of one grain
  (an edge at one grain has `R = 2^16 + 1`).
- **The founding** is the built one (§3.6), so a field founded with the count keeps every face.
- **Entering the line.** By §3.4 the law reads a grown state's chart as it stands, so it can be
  tested as one attempt from the line's end beside the line's own lap, the build with the law off
  reproducing that lap first (CLAUDE.md, "A build enters a line by reproducing it"). A grown
  state's faces change at the law's first read wherever its chart varies along a strand.
- **What the first read does** depends on reading 3. If open and given sites stand at one friction
  the law changes little at first and the lap must find the split. If they stand apart, the first
  read already differs, and its sign in quarter 4 is a test with no lap spent.
- **What would refuse it:** the leak's covector unable to part the kinds of site under the count
  (the chart's read `r̂` does not tell an open site from a given one), or reading 1's fitting.

## 8. Owed to #410

  its stiffness the scan's count; the two-arrival formula of §3.2.
