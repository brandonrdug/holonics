## 3. A cause named before the records were read, and withdrawn

At 15:55Z this thread named the line's repeated reading of the same 1 GB prefix as the likely cause. The argument was
that seen text goes dark, so the covectors that reach a locus shrink. That was a cause fixed before reading what the
records print about its premise, the failure the method rule of November 11 names. Three readings weigh against it:
- **Scale.** A pass is about 10⁹ places. The constitution held 305,145 entries at three lanes, and a fourth lane was
  founded since, about 0.4 million. That is some 2,500 places of passage an entry a pass. A store of that size can hold
  the passage's law, but not its documents (`agent-inferred`, order of magnitude). The data-constrained scaling
  readings agree: repetition costs little where the passage far exceeds what the model can hold (Muennighoff et al.
  2023, `from training`).
- **G187.** The held documents and the prefix's documents are different populations, −0.19 to +0.26 by slice, because
  the store keeps shard order. So a gap between training code and held code cannot measure what was seen.
- **G188 to G190.** On November 14 the line's plateau on its fourth pass was the face's width. One lane founded at zero
  bought more in a thousand sections than five laps did, and its lead grew through the next pass
  ([the plateau record](2026-11-14_THE_LINES_PLATEAU_WAS_THE_FACES_WIDTH_ONE_LANE_FOUNDED_AT_ZERO_BOUGHT_MORE_IN_A_THOUSAND_SECTIONS_THAN_FIVE_LAPS_AND_A_LANES_WIDTH_IS_READ_FROM_ITS_TRAVEL.md)
  §3).

## 4. Three causes, each with the reading that separates it

Each reading is taken on saved lap means or logs, and none needs a lap of training except S2.
- **Repetition: a return as a difference in differences.**
  - The comparison. The same documents return at the same place of each pass, with new cuts and masks drawn from the
    key stream. A section line's code is the comparison read before that section's deposit. So the change of the
    training code at a return, minus the change of the held code over the same laps, cancels G187's population
    offset.
  - What it separates. If the field keeps documents, the training code falls faster than the held code, and more at
    each pass. If it keeps their law, the two fall together.
  - The second reading. G187's fair sampler (documents either side of the prefix's end) is owed beside it.
- **Capacity, priced.** The rule of November 14 §4.1 (`code.capacity-price`, `π = −dC/d ln V`): the face grows by
  partition while a founded lane's price, read against its cost in card time, stands above the price of the same card
  time spent on more sections.
  - Sections now buy about 0.00066 nats a place a lap (the table's 0.0059 over nine laps).
  - The fourth lane priced about 0.025 a nat of cells, against the third lane's 0.040.
- **Temperature.** This is T10 of the
  [heat record](2026-11-15_HEAT_EXPLORES_AND_A_STORE_TEMPERS_A_COMPRESSED_SOURCE_OPENS_INTO_ITS_MEMBERS_AS_ITS_READER_COOLS_SLEEP_CONSOLIDATES_A_FAST_STORE_INTO_A_SLOW_ONE_AND_POWER_FOLLOWS_NEWS.md)
  §8: the standing spread by part and by surprise fifth, which tells whether the deposit's heat follows news. A fixed
  rate holds the line in a basin of that temperature, and the lap mean sheds the spread only from the reading.

The theory's pick is capacity. Its precedent is G190, and color says the later lanes are still opening. Repetition is
read first because its reading costs nothing.

## 6. #404's boxes fixed on the store line

The October machine's acceptance (the alpha plan of October 20, §3 and §4, kept outside the repository) was

below is restated on the store line: its acceptance, the reading that settles it and its guess, all fixed before any
claim.

1. **Online learning.** Reading more of a text lowers the code of what follows.
   - At lap means, held code falls with passage read, in every slice, on documents never met. This is read already
     (§2).
   - Within a document: places 1 to 16 code at least 0.2 nats a place above places past 256, in every slice, on a
     lap's mean (`read-loudness` writes each place's code; the offset in its document is joined by the ruler).
2. **Retention.** Repeated material codes lower each time it returns. This is S1's read, its literal part and its
   difference in differences beside it.
3. **Cold restart on the card.**
   - The test. A fresh process from a mid-lap save trains to the lap's next save, and its `STATE` equals the lap's own
     byte for byte. Where a lap saves only at its end, 48 sections straight are compared with 24, a save, a fresh
     process and 24 more.
   - Guess: equal. The roundtrip line printed at every lap's start (`STORE_STATE_TEXT … roundtrip=equal`) is half of
     it, and the pass line carries the cut's place and the count of key draws.
4. **Answered from the retained state, with its sources.** The old box's "exact" is retired with the precision law's
   wording of November 7.
   - The answer depends on its question. On held prompts (`write-store`'s problems), the reference continuation codes
     at least 0.05 nats a place lower under its own prompt than under a foil prompt from the same slice. The October
     machine read −0.0008 bits.
   - Its sources inside the passage are the field's own: the open rows' shares by source and octave
     (`read-open-rows`).
   - A recitation ruler on the host reads the longest byte span each write shares with the 1 GB prefix, with its
     document and offset. It is a ruler, never machinery, and a table of documents inside the machine would be a cache.
     Guess: the median longest span is 8 to 16 bytes.
5. **Readable text.** The write is shown to Brandon whole, as it came out, with its structure readings beside it.
   His read decides, and no number stands in for it.

A question proper needs passage that asks and answers. The slice owed on November 14 (the plateau record §5: the

## 10. The writer's test, guesses fixed 16:37 UTC

The writer's two receivers (`agent-inferred`, from G182 and the plateau record §1):
- **The sweeps.** They draw each place from the open-stretch receiver, which codes at the whole masks' level, 2.2 to
  2.3 nats a place. That is near a count of three known neighbours a side, so a sweep's draft is text at that reach.
- **The refine.** It redraws each place from the one-place receiver, with both sides known, at about 0.78 nats. It can
  repair a word, but its reach is a few neighbours, so it cannot compose a sentence.

The test is the own-states record's open question: `write-store` at `REFINE` 0, 4 and 16, on the same 20 problems.
- **W1.** The write's one-place code (each place open alone among the written places, its prompt known) falls from 0 to
  4 to 16 passes in every slice. At 0 passes it stands 0.3 to 1.0 nats a place above the reference continuation's
  one-place code, and at 16 passes 0.1 to 0.5 under it, as the draft settles into the field's dense forms.
- **W2.** The share of the write's whitespace-delimited strings of two bytes or more that occur as words in the slice's
  1 GB passage rises by at least 0.10 from 0 to 16 passes, in at least four slices. This is a host ruler.
- **W3.** There is no lock. The commonest byte's share moves by at most 0.03 at 4 passes and rises by at most 0.05 at
  16, in every slice; G182 read it level at 0 to 2 passes.
- **W4.** There is still no sentence at 16 passes. Brandon's eye decides.

The whole-mask code at lap 68's mean is printed beside them. If W2 and W4 both hold, the limit is the sweep's receiver,
