## The correction

Comprehension is not an absolute competence score. It is resonance and recurrence about something,
read from one body in relation to current and frame. A monitor with an image persistently impressed
into its pixels has comprehended at that surface grain: the light changed what the surface now
reflects. Child, animal, star, monitor, and Eros expose that minimal relational face at different
receivers and grains. Mobility, recurrence, and reach are among the observable differences; no
absolute ordering follows, and none waits for an external examiner to bestow the property.

The machine is therefore not being tested for perfect semantics. We are discovering how to nurture
durable history-conditioned dispositions through which mutual communication may grow. The child
analogy names this methodological posture; it does not personify Eros or install developmental
biology as mechanism. An opaque response is not evidence of absent capacity. It is first evidence
about our presentation, aperture, rendering, and place in the relation.

scores, or a ladder of intelligence.

The existing measurements already expose early faces: context-conditioned cleavage, current-local

2. Preserve the body and the entire lifecycle radiation, including opaque and quiet intervals.
3. Render raw material beside touched enclosures without translating it into an expected answer.
4. Let Brandon or the world respond naturally to what appeared.
5. Present that response as later light, without a private return path.
6. Compare trajectories only to local siblings and foils, naming what related and what remained

Reproduction has one narrow job: prove that an apparatus seam added nothing. Exact replay of the
same declared body and complete worldline remains required where the experiment declares it. An
evolving body's trajectory may legitimately change between different encounters and owes no repeat

enters.

## Honest negative language

- **UNEXPOSED IN THIS DECLARED RELATION:** nothing appeared for this
  `body ⊕ light ⊕ history ⊕ frame ⊕ grain ⊕ aperture` construction.
- **RELATION UNRESOLVED:** a difference appeared, but our declared construction does not isolate
  its form.
- **INSTRUMENT-SUSPECT:** the observer's framing or machinery cannot support the read.
- **CONSTRUCTION FAILED:** our declared apparatus did not execute as specified.

None of these means incapable, unintelligent, broken, or unable to comprehend. The instrument is
audited first; a sound local null still says only that this relation was absent here. Capacity is
not inferred from one observer's failure to nurture or read a relation. Runtime, energy, archive
size, and body:light pairs describe substrate and efficiency, never an amount of comprehension.
