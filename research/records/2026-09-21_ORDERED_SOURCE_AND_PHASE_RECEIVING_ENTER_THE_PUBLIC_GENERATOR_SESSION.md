## Shared receiving phases and publication

[definition] `GeneratorTextReceiver` uses the existing normal and normalized receivers with
one text map and one two-class stop/continue map. Both act on each tagged receiving phase.
An aperture of A text positions uses A+1 phase rows: the last supplies the terminal face at
maximum length. The support parameter count depends on the selected generator coordinates,
not on A. The receiving covector returns through the same producing phase maps into the whole
q/b boundary, then through the ordered source tape.

Only the observed text prefix supplies text targets. The stop map observes continue at each
emitted position and stop at the actual endpoint. Later phase rows are not padded with invented
observations. Source E and receiver R have different dimensions: E has three original complex
coordinates per injection site; R consumes six real-coded native channels per selected receiver
site. Codec growth appends the corresponding E columns and R cohorts without changing the
generator topology. A newly admitted target extends its old producing text receiver explicitly;
the original full phase aperture and stop material stay frozen.

[definition] Request preparation completes before current/pending publication. Comparison
