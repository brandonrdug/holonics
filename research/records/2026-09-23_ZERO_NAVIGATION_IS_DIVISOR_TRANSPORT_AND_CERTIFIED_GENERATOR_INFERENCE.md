# Zero navigation is divisor transport and certified generator inference

**Date:** 2026-09-23. **Status:** research proposal with a bounded Lean obstruction/cone return. **Request:** Brandon asks for an exact, deterministic Holonic zero finder whose paths may address irrational, transcendental or hypertranscendental objects, without making a critical strip or real part one-half the solver's primitive. He asks which phase/generator patterns survive intersections and whether local Holonic regions have a relativity-like property. No Typst work belongs to this request.

## The source and target

**Supplied data:** a source generator for f (series, shift recurrence, product, differential/difference law or exact finite expression), an admitted domain D, singularities/poles, exact jet/remainder or other evaluation oracle, admissible region complex K, source symmetries, and a receiving question. A receiving question may ask for one zero, every zero in a compact region with multiplicity, a zero-free region, a source-wide zero-set generator, or a relation among zero populations. These are different tasks.

**Inferred unknown:** an addressed zero fibre f⁻¹(0), possibly a positive-dimensional intersection stratum for several generators, and the generating relation that explains its phase/divisor pattern.

**Returned relation:** source-qualified zero-count receipts, nested rational isolating regions or an exact parametric solution family, multiplicities, phase lifts/winding, group or scale transport, and a future-sufficient unresolved fibre. A root need not have a rational or closed-form coordinate. An exact infinite address with uniqueness and convergence certificate is an exact representation; a finite prefix alone is not the point.

**Consequence:** reuse the relation to find further zeros, transfer it to changed receivers, infer generator keys, or prove a source-specific global zero statement. The Riemann critical line is one possible consequence of the completed-zeta source and its symmetry, never the universal navigation space.

## Finding a divisor and placing one are dual solver questions

The forward problem is f_θ ↦ Div(f_θ). The inverse placement problem asks for the preimage fibre {θ : Div(f_θ)=D*} inside a declared generator family, together with phase, growth, functional-equation and receiver constraints. These fibres are rarely singletons: multiplying by a zero-free analytic factor preserves the divisor but changes values and phase transport, so a zero set alone is not the full Holon. For an unconstrained entire function of one complex variable, a discrete prescribed zero multiset with no finite accumulation is realizable by a convergent Weierstrass product; a simple canonical product already works under an inverse-square summability condition. The Riemann ζ source is vastly more constrained by its Dirichlet, Gamma, functional-equation and growth laws. Placement in that source cannot be inferred from the unconstrained existence theorem. [DLMF §1.10(ix)](https://dlmf.nist.gov/1.10.ix) gives the canonical-product form and its convergence condition.

This is the generator inference target: infer both the allowed divisor pattern and the zero-free factor/phase configuration that realizes the actual source. In an exact solver, the product, logarithmic derivative, normalization and tail travel together. The existing Foster product owners already enforce this for their specified class. A proposed placement is rejected if any of the source's functional, growth, boundary-current or continuation constraints fails, even when its plotted zeros look plausible.

## The zero-search Holon

This is an instance of H=(K,∂ₐ;Π;𝒟;𝓔;G;π), not a new primitive. K consists of oriented source regions and their shared boundary cells. Each boundary port carries the source's phase/current and its dual exact jet/remainder reading. The Dirac join cancels a shared edge with opposite orientation. The constitution is the actual analytic generator, its allowed domain, singularities and residual/capture law. G contains source shifts, factor maps, local Newton/release, reflected/rebased regions and addressed subdivision words. π restricts a region to a finer receiving family or descends equivalent source data. A receiving Holon reads the winding/divisor, a zero-free face, a unique-root capture, or an obstruction. Its state is the currently unresolved region population and current receipts; retention keeps the future-sufficient quotient, not a tape of every split.

For f holomorphic and nonzero on ∂B, let α_f=(2π)⁻¹Im(df/f) on that boundary. The exact integer port reading is N_f(B)=∮_(∂B)α_f, equal to the zero count with multiplicity when f has no poles in B. For a meromorphic source it is zeros minus poles; a zero finder must retain or remove the pole divisor separately. If B is subdivided into B_i and every shared cut avoids source zeros and poles, the port join gives N_f(B)=Σ_i N_f(B_i). This is the solver's conservation law. Phase lifts on edges have integer carry; the internal cut's two hands cancel rather than being reevaluated as independent facts.

The local release is already sourced: where f′≠0, N_(λ,f)(z)=z−λ f(z)/f′(z), and the complete receiving preimage satisfies f′(z)(y−z)+λf(z)=0. A changing source F(u,z) has v=(−F−u̇F_u)/F_z for normalized residual release, with its actual source clock. Multiple roots and critical points route to divisor/winding or local factorization, not division by a zero derivative.

## The local phase net and normalized navigation clock

This is the strongest universal pattern close to Brandon's proposed "speed" analogy. On a regular zero-free patch write L=f′/f=a+ib, u=log|f| and θ=arg f with a local phase lift. Holomorphicity gives the exact covectors

  ∇u=(a,−b),       ∇θ=(b,a),
  ∇u·∇θ=0,         |∇u|²=|∇θ|²=a²+b².

Thus amplitude and phase form an orthogonal equal-scale local net; the source is conformal where f′≠0. The normalized residual direction v=−f/f′=−1/L satisfies L v=−1. Along an admitted differentiable trajectory in that direction, d/dτ log f=−1: log-amplitude drops at one unit per *declared refinement clock* while the lifted phase is fixed. With a real rate κ it is −κ. The moving-source form is F_z v+u̇F_u=−κF. This source-value clock is invariant under suitable joint source/receiver recharting, but the spatial speed |v|=|f/f′| can vary without a universal bound. Continuous residual flow approaches f=0 asymptotically when its hypotheses persist; it does not assert finite-time arrival at the exact zero. Mathematics/AnalyticNavigation.lean and RH/ZeroDynamicsEntire.lean own the local chain rules.

For simultaneous sources F=(f₁,…,f_m) on a complex n-fold, the analogous situated action equation is D_xF·v+u̇∂_uF=−κF. Solving it is an exact preimage-fibre problem. Full row rank m gives solutions; for n>m their kernel is the tangential freedom of a regular local zero manifold, while for m=n invertible Jacobian gives a unique direction. Rank loss or incompatible requested consequence returns a typed obstruction. This is the multi-generator intersection law to bind to the existing exact linear/preimage and normal/factor inference owners. It is more general than guessing a spatial critical strip.

## Structural patterns that really are shared

1. **Factor and product:** away from singularities, dlog(fg)=dlog f+dlog g, so zero-minus-pole divisors and winding receipts add. A zero-free factor changes local phase but contributes no interior divisor when it extends zero-free across the receiving region.
2. **Generator composition:** dlog(f∘g)=g* dlog f where the chain rule is admitted. A zero of f pulls back under g with local branching multiplicity. This is the exact reason a source factorization or recurrence can compress many zero searches.
3. **Region composition:** shared edge currents cancel. A parent with nonzero winding has at least one nonzero child after an admitted partition; a child with winding zero can be removed from the requested zero census. Boundary contact or a pole returns an obstruction, not an invented decision.
4. **Intersections:** for F=(f₁,…,f_m):Mⁿ→ℂᵐ, regular complex rank m gives a local zero manifold of complex dimension n−m. Rank loss produces singular/branched strata and multiplicity. For m=n, isolated zeros can be read by a suitable local degree. This is a conditional holomorphic implicit-function pattern; it is not a claim that every intersection is smooth.
5. **Recharting:** a biholomorphic coordinate change transports source, jet, boundary and phase together. The exact first-arrival family is conjugated by the changing receiver, as Mathematics/AnalyticReceiving.lean already proves. An arbitrary changed chart cannot keep the old Newton coefficients unchanged.
6. **Addresses:** rational subdivision words can converge to irrational, algebraic or transcendental zeros. The address and phase/current certificate are exact. Arithmetic classification is a separate receiver: path complexity or a fractal basin does not prove transcendence or hypertranscendence. Gamma illustrates this distinction: its short shift law coexists with Hölder's hypertranscendence theorem.

These laws apply across a specified analytic source category. They do not assert one universal phase distribution or one critical strip. Source-specific factors, monodromy, arithmetic symmetries, singularities and metric determine the population patterns worth compressing.

## Deterministic intelligent navigation

The solver's exact cycle for a compact receiver is:

1. **Construct** the source current. Infer a useful generator expression and domain from the supplied source; carry complete jets and rigorous remainders. Derive the needed series head from the actual region/grain, as the η atlas already does for Euler–Maclaurin.
2. **Receive** a region. Certify its boundary avoids zero/pole and compute integer winding from oriented phase transport. A zero count is a proof-bearing current, not a score from sampled values.
3. **Route** by the receipt. Zero winding prunes a holomorphic pole-free region. Positive winding routes all counted roots into children. Negative winding routes pole information. Unresolved boundary contact refines the source/region or preserves an explicit obstruction.
4. **Exploit generators.** Before new evaluation, try exact factorization, shift, symmetry, phase carry, scale rebase and previously certified boundary transport. Reuse shared edges and any future-sufficient equivalent region class. A critical-line quotient is applied only if the source proves it preserves the divisor and receiver.
5. **Close locally.** For a simple candidate, certify a self-mapping contraction with the actual f,f′,f″ enclosures and verify winding one. For multiple roots retain multiplicity and factorization; for a positive-dimensional intersection return its local parameterization/stratum, not a fictitious isolated point.
6. **Release** the certificate or a typed obstruction. For infinitely many zeros, return a generator of certified addressed lineages and its continuation law, not an impossible finite list.

This is both search and compression. The solver learns which transformations preserve future zero questions and navigates that quotient. A fixed quadtree or nearest-point search is one application chart, not the object.

## What optimal can mean

There is no source-independent fastest zero solver. The receiving target, source representation and work measure must be declared. A useful exact cost is a partial order over source-jet work, bit growth, new boundary segments, retained certificate size and elapsed device passages. A finite action grammar/horizon can be searched for its Pareto-optimal certified programmes; a receiver may choose one cost chart. A branch-and-bound or min-plus dynamic programme can be exact relative to that grammar, with unresolved alternatives retained. It cannot claim global optimality over all analytic algorithms from one finite search.

For a finite grammar A(S) and horizon h, a precise policy target is the Pareto frontier of

  V_h(S)=ParetoMin_(a∈A(S))
           [cost(a) ⊕ Join_(S′∈CertifiedSuccessors(a,S)) V_(h−1)(S′)],

with V=0 only for a completely certified receiving family. The join accounts for every child needed by the requested zero census; for a single-zero request it may stop at the first certified lineage. A deterministic tie-break uses the exact generator address. This is programme synthesis over certificate transitions, not a floating loss or a claim that a short-looking path is globally best. An online policy can claim this optimum before trying alternatives only if it has certified transition and cost information; otherwise the frontier is an after-the-fact benchmark for the declared grammar.

## The relativity-like part, with its boundary

There are two different candidate laws:

- **Covariance:** a source, its phase current, zero divisor, region and receiver rechart together. This is already the right analogy to a relative observer law. It can hold for holomorphic generators without choosing a propagation speed.
- **Finite propagation:** if each admitted local generator g moves no more than c(g) in a declared metric per clock step, then an ordered word w moves no more than Σ_(g in w)c(g). The new Lean theorem Computation/ZeroNavigation.word_speed_cone proves this exact composition law. It is an operational light cone relative to a chosen contact metric and clock. It is not an identification with physical c.

A universal speed-of-light theorem for *every* generator is false without a locality/metric/clock axiom: one admitted map can jump arbitrarily far in one step, and diffusion-type analytic kernels can have nonzero tails everywhere immediately. Local holomorphic rank also does not imply finite-speed transport. A physical relativistic instance additionally owes Lorentzian metric, characteristic principal symbol, material constitutive law and source units. This qualification makes the proposed analogy testable instead of rhetorical.

Concrete controls: g_M(z)=Mz has arbitrarily large one-step spatial reach as M varies, while f(z)=z² has a rank-deficient zero at the origin with winding two. Neither defeats the phase/divisor laws; both defeat an unrestricted constant-speed or regular-manifold claim. The standing MaxwellEnergyCone identity |S|≤c u is a genuine constitutive example of a speed law. A zero-search analogue would need an equally source-derived positive current/density and its transport identity, not the symbol c attached to a phase portrait.
