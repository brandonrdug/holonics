## 3. The word "lane"

`tree`. The word named six things, and the guide used two of them unqualified.
- A **face lane** is the engine's (`Declaration::lanes`, `lane_chart` in `crates/engine/src/contact.rs`, the card's
  `field_chart`): a place's face of `D` integers is cut into `L` blocks of `W = D/L`, and each block is read as a ratio
  to its own power, a direction with its scale beside it. The line's face is 7 lanes of 32. A lane founded at zero
  beside trained ones leaves every face as it was, because its power is its own; that is why the face widens by lanes
  (`tower.field-growth-by-lanes`).
- A card's **thread**, which the guide's precision law called a lane ("a surface of many slow lanes"), and a host's
  thread, which the rows of November 7 to 15 call a host lane.
- A **bit lane** (`radix.register-presets`), a **PCIe lane**, and a lane of work in the project.

Inside `field_chart` both meet: the variable `lane` is a thread's position in its warp, and `w.lanes` two lines below
is the face's count. A face lane is 32 coordinates and a warp is 32 threads, so one warp reads one face lane, a
coordinate a thread, and its power is one sum across the warp. That match made 32 convenient on the card. It is a
property of the realization and gives no reason for a register of the face to be 32 wide.

The card's "256-bit" is its memory interface width (`vendor`): the data wires between the processor and its memory
chips. It sets the octets a second between the cores and the card's memory and is no width of arithmetic; a core's
register holds 32 bits.

The repair, in this change: the guide says thread for a device's parallel unit and face lane for the engine's, with
the reason. Rows and records written before today keep their wording.

## 4. The card as two capacities

`vendor` for the figures (compute capability 8.9), `agent-inferred` for the join, `tree` for the readings.

**The register file.** A multiprocessor holds `R = 65,536 = 2¹⁶` registers of 32 bits, at most 255 a thread, and at
most `N = 1,536 = 2⁹·3` resident threads in warps of 32. A kernel that holds `r` registers a thread has

```text
n(r) = min(N, 32 ⌊R / (32 r)⌋)      resident threads a multiprocessor,      n(r) · 32 r ≤ 2²¹ bits
```

so `n = 1,536` for `r ≤ 42`, 1,024 at 64, 672 at 96, 512 at 128 and 256 at 255. The card's threads are bought with
bits: a thread that holds a 128-bit sum spends four registers on it. The precision law's clause (terms cut as narrow as
their information allows, to keep the most threads busy) is this inequality. The profiler's reading of November 19
(`receipts/2026-11-18_frontier/reads.txt`): the tiled maps hold 124 to 128 registers a thread and fill 0.302 to 0.306
of the thread positions, against 0.333 from the formula. The bound is on residency and promises no rate: three blocks a
multiprocessor in the maps read 0.996 of the control's time that day, and the vendor's own guide says that more
resident threads do not always do more work.

**The bus.** The octet floor `t_o = O/B` of `tower.field-engine-count`, taken a kernel at a time, reads the carry's
return. `field_pair_scan_pre` moves 70 to 100 octets a cell (two 64-bit covector words read, two written, two 128-bit
parts `dh` written, the pair's material and the kept `Qm` read) over ticks × sites × circles, about 64 million cells a
section at 4 ticks, 31,334 sites and 512 circles: of the order of 5 GB, which at the measured 514 to 572 GB a second
is 9 to 10 ms of its 13.6. The profiler agrees: the three kernels of the return (`field_pair_scan_pre`,
`field_pair_scan_back`, `field_pair_gate_back`) stand at 0.69 to 0.90 of the bus and 0.04 to 0.69 of the arithmetic,
and together they are 43.5 ms of a 455 ms section. Their lever is octets a cell: `dh` is held on 128 bits "until it
meets the chain's sum", a third of the traffic, and the carry gate's narrowing to 32 bits (inside its control's spread
on November 19) moved 8 octets of about 100.

**The tensor cores** follow from the same count (`agent-inferred`). They multiply digits of 8 bits. A word of `ℓ`
such digits against another costs `ℓ²` digit products, so a 32-bit signed word, 4 to 5 digits, costs 16 to 25, which
spends the unit's advantage; the trial of November 19 kept every bit and read 2% slower, on kernels the profiler
shows are not bound by arithmetic. The units open where both operands are declared at one or two digits, which is a
census of the bits the charts' operands hold, by block. It is never a question of the register's kind (the guide's
precision law: an integer and a float are words at their locales).

## 5. A section as a partial order

`agent-inferred` on `tree` owners.

A section is one expression: sums of products over sites, pairs, contacts and ticks, with releases at declared places.
The three laws of arithmetic are three freedoms of its realization, each trading one resource:

| Law | Freedom | Resource | Where it stops |
|---|---|---|---|
| associativity | bracketing: the depth of a sum | time against threads | a section inside a chain |
| commutativity | order and placement | locality, and the device | a join that must see everything |
| distributivity | factoring: a shared factor, a word split into digits, maps stacked on one input | arithmetic units and octets | a factor that is not shared |

The launches of a section form a partial order: launch `a` precedes `b` where `b` reads a buffer `a` writes. Launches
that are pairwise unordered can run as one grid, or on streams side by side. The least number of such groups that
cover the order is the length of its longest chain (Mirsky 1971, the dual of Dilworth's theorem; `from training`), and
the groups by longest-chain rank attain it. So the dispatch term `L t_L` of `holarchy.work-depth-bound` falls to
`D_L t_L` and no further, `D_L` the longest chain of launches, and every level's width says how full the card can be
run while it executes. The line's section is 2,736 launches of 96 kernels in three batches (November 19). Its order
has not been drawn. Until it is, the gain of fusing levels is a guess.

Readings that bound the guess (`tree`): the plain section is 455 ms and the same kernels timed alone, the card
synchronized around each, sum to 478 ms of a 589 ms profiled section; so the host's dispatch already overlaps the
card's work, and what cheaper dispatch can return is at most the card's idle share of a plain section, which the one
reading (87 to 99% busy) puts between 1% and 13%. The profile's 88,801 "allocations" over 60 sections are takes from
the engine's own pool (`Kind::Alloc` in `crates/engine-cuda/src/hnn/profile.rs`; the pool of November 1), 11.5 ms a
section, and its 13,020 copies another 11.5 ms. The ten largest kernels are half of the kernels' time and 86 others
share the rest; every change to one kernel tried from November 15 to 20 moved a section by under 2%.

Four moves keep every bit: a level of launches run as one; maps that read one input stacked; phases ordered by
groups of regions that fit the cache; and the octets a cell of the carry's return cut under a proof of their
magnitudes. Together they are guessed at 10 to 25% of a section. Against that stands the count's own reading: 14 to
18 times the ideal floor in mid-November, at an older declaration. The floor assumes every thread forms products at
the best tile's rate and every octet streams, which divisions, wide words and gathers do not allow in full, and
nobody has shown how much of the factor can be reached. The count is taken again at today's declaration before any
headroom is claimed.

## 7. The physical frame

`agent-inferred`, with the classical results `from training`.

**Stores and ports.** The machine is a graph of stores joined by ports: the register files (2²¹ bits each, 21 MB
over 80 multiprocessors), shared memory (100 KB each), the card's memory (16 GB behind a bus measured at 514 to 572
GB a second), the host's memory (32 GB on two DDR5 channels) and PCIe between them (about 31 GB a second at best, some
twenty times narrower than the card's bus). A computation is a flow through this graph. Across every cut its time is
at least the bits that must cross over the cut's rate, and what must cross is bounded below by the order of the
computation and the size of the store inside the cut (Hong and Kung 1981). The floors `t_a` and `t_o` are the two
innermost cuts.

**A vortex is a relatively complete region.** A working set that stays inside a store while the computation
circulates, with only its boundary flux leaving, is on the objects a region whose boundary bounds an interior with
persistent interior motion. Its figure of merit is arithmetic an octet crossed. The tiled maps are built for it; the
carry's return is its opposite.

**Heat follows every merge, order follows only a section.** Landauer's bound charges a merge, a step that maps
several states to one. Both kinds of release merge: a quotient (a wrap, a word's top dropped) and a section (a
rounding). A quotient is a ring map and commutes with every bracketing; a section does not
(`radix.release-quotient-or-section`). So the releases that owe heat strictly contain the releases that fix an order,
and the point where heat is paid is not the point where a schedule begins to matter. The identification of the two
(made in an outside letter, §11) holds only for sections.

**Keep or recompute.** The return keeps the forward's shares, 16 octets for each of 4 heads and 4 ticks and 24 more,
280 octets a pair of sites (`read_octets` in `crates/engine-cuda/src/hnn/resident.rs`), and that reservation is what
cuts a section into three batches on 16 GB. A step that merges nothing can be run backward and need not be kept
(Bennett 1973). The carry's turn is a Cayley step and keeps its circle; its leak merges. Which of the kept words can
be recomputed from a later state, and what remainders that needs, is §8's question from the other side.

## 8. A series releases at a tolerance only where its tick contracts

`agent-inferred` throughout; the theorems `from training`; the certificate `tree`.

**What is built.** A region's sites take `K = 4` applications of one constitution, `x^(k+1) = x^k + g(x^k)`, and every
reader divides a site's state by its lanes' powers, so a site's state fills as a running mean
(`tower.field-tick-running-mean`): successive changes of its ray stand in the ratio `(k − 1)/(k + 1)`. That falloff is
harmonic. Its sum does not converge geometrically, so no tick is small against the ones before it in the sense a
tolerance needs, and the reading of November 15 is its consequence: a site that moved under a sixteenth of a bit
still carried leverage through its readers, and holding it raised the code. The return composes the four transposed
ticks, each with the operands that produced it, which is the tape of §7.

**The picture of the lens, stated.** A series is released at a tolerance where its terms shrink by a factor. For a
tick `x^(k+1) = F(x^k; s)` with the source `s` held, let `M = ∂F/∂x` and suppose a metric `G ≻ 0` with
`MᵀGM ⪯ ρ²G`, `ρ < 1`, along the path (`parametron.floquet-certificate`; passivity proved, never assumed). Then:

```text
‖x^k − x*‖_G ≤ ρ^k ‖x⁰ − x*‖_G ,        ‖x^k − x*‖_G ≤ ‖x^(k+1) − x^k‖_G / (1 − ρ)
λ* = c + Mᵀλ* = Σ_(j ≥ 0) (Mᵀ)^j c ,     ‖Σ_(j ≥ J)‖_G⁻¹ ≤ ρ^J ‖c‖_G⁻¹ / (1 − ρ) ,     δΘ = (∂F/∂Θ)ᵀ λ*  at x*
```

The first line is Banach's fixed point with its a posteriori bound: the count of ticks is read from the tolerance and
the first residual, and is no longer a declared 4. The second is the covector at the settled state (the implicit
function theorem; Almeida 1987 and Pineda 1987 for recurrent networks, Bai, Kolter and Koltun 2019): the return is
itself a series released at a tolerance, formed from the operands at `x*` alone. On the objects: a store's steady
state under a flux that passes through it (the store law of November 11), read once it has settled to the receiver's
grain, with its remainder bounded and counted.

**What it would give the card.** The kept shares fall from heads × ticks × 16 octets a pair to heads × 16: 280
octets a pair become 88, and a section of about 25 GB becomes about 8, one batch under the board's ceiling. The
forward's ticks follow the tolerance, region by region.

**What it resembles, and what differs.**
- `hnn.held-step-covectors` is its one-mode case, proved: a linear mode with `|λ| < 1` and a held arrival is this
  statement at `M = λ` and `G = 1`, its limit the fixed point and its reverse the geometric sum in closed form. What
  is added here is the whole tick, nonlinear, under a metric that certifies its gain, and the release of both series
  at a tolerance.
- The held tick of November 15 assumed a relaxation in a built tick that is a running mean. Here the contraction is a
  property the tick's form proves, as the carry gate's is (`|γ|² = 1/(1 + d)² < 1` by its form).
- `tower.field-echo`: a symmetric network iterated synchronously settles at fixed points or two-cycles. A contraction
  has one fixed point and no cycle.
- `tower.field-self-hearing`: a release that hears itself is self-consistent however far from the passage. Here the
  source is held and the fixed point is the field's under that source; the release is outside the loop.
- It changes the deposit's bits, so it is a change of law: its tolerance is carried in the learner's state, it runs
  as an arm beside a control, and its guess is fixed before the read.

**The premise, read before any build** (the guide's rule of November 16). Two host rulers on saved states of the
line: the residual `‖x^(k+1) − x^k‖` by tick and region, in the lanes' metric, for `k` past 4; and the largest gain of
the trained tick, by power iteration with the forward and the built return. If the trained tick already contracts,
the construction is a release and a shorter tape. If it does not, the first link that slips is the tick's form, and
the candidates are an averaged step `x ← (1 − a)x + a T(x)` with `T` nonexpansive (Krasnosel'skii 1955, Mann 1953),
which the contact's mean over its partners already is before its maps, with the maps' gains bounded in the metric.

Statements for Lean (#410): the two lines above on a finite-dimensional space with a certified metric, and the bound
on the released remainder of the covector's series. `HNN/HeldStep` holds the scalar case (`held_geometric`,
`held_tendsto`). The metric's bound is asked on a convex set that holds the orbit and `x*`, and the transposed step
contracts in the metric `G⁻¹`, which is the covector's.

## 9. The width

`tree` for the comparison, `agent-inferred` for the two joints.

| | Built on the line | What the lens and the tree's design ask |
|---|---|---|
| Owner | an operator founds a lane and keeps it when its level falls under a control's | the tension at a locus founds the register |
| Size | every lane 32 | each register sized from its need |
| Density | every block reads every lane, about 264,000 entries a lane | each block declares the registers it reads |
| Locality | the same at every place | a region's locale carries its own width |
| Removal | none | a register whose price reads empty is released by partition |

The built growth breaks no law: each lane entered with every read at zero and was read beside a control, which is the
instrument the guide allows (`code.capacity-price`). It is a stopgap, and it is what the card pays for: each lane
costs 16 to 36 ms on every section afterwards, in every block (455, 491.2, 507.5 and 531.8 ms at 128 to 224), for
about 0.002 nats a place by its second lap.

Two joints make the composition buildable. The maps run as tiles of 64 outputs by 64 sites
(`tower.field-site-maps-tile`), so a binding graph of blocks and registers is a list of the tiles that exist, and a
missing edge is a tile never launched: sparse binding needs no new arithmetic on the card. And the face lanes hold no
radix: a lane is a flat block with its scale, and nothing carries from one to the next, while the carry circles
(`Declaration::carry`) hold the engine's only carried digits. A register of the face as a digit with a carry to its
neighbour is the construction the statements of November 10 and 14 point at. The piece not in hand is the ruler that
sizes a register (G114 sized none); the founding law's own reading, the directions of the covector's tension at a
locus that stand above the deposit's standing spread, is the candidate.

## 10. The host

`tree` for the measurements, `vendor` for the figures, `agent-inferred` for the placement.

The processor has 12 cores, 24 threads, 64 MB of third-level cache in two halves and 512-bit vector registers; the
host holds 32 GB. During a lap it runs one and a half threads and 434 MB. At its best it is a few percent of the
card's arithmetic, and the port between them is some twenty times narrower than the card's bus. So
`holarchy.two-surface-placement` stands: no kind in mid-chain moves to the host, and what crosses PCIe crosses at the
section's clock, at its narrowest declared width, never at a launch's. The whole constitution at 32 bits an entry is
7.4 MB, under a millisecond once a section.

The host's ground, by kind: the next section staged in page-locked memory while the card runs (the engine copies from
pageable memory and then waits for the stream, 217 times a section); the laws that act once a section or a lap on
small states (a founding's read, a register's price, the rate's cooling, edits of the binding graph), which is the
graph's side of the machine; and readings from saved states on the idle threads, placed on the core group the lap's
feeding thread does not use (lap 76 ran 7% longer with eighteen host threads reading beside it). The deposit runs on
the host today, serial, between the card's sections; THE_CONSOLIDATION §11 lists it for the card.

One measurement decides a flock that shares the training pass: a region's covector sums add on any schedule, so a
whole region computed on the host joins at the section's end with every bit kept. A map tile and a contact row
written in 512-bit vectors at their proven widths, timed on 12 cores against the card's time for the same region,
gives the share. The host's reference realization is scalar on 128-bit integers and read about 480 times the card's
time a thread, so the measurement comes before any second realization of the kernels.
