## The correction

The full-history application had made the relational receiver nearly the entire language
machine. It could normalize a clause incidence and emit a source-absent voice dual, but a question
still began by selecting stored testimony. Refining which passage won would only have made a
retrieval-and-reinflection machine more polished. Brandon's correction therefore cancels passage
selection as the production frontier.

The new consequence is not a better source match. A prompt now opens independent symbolic
receivers:

```text
received surface
    ├── nested delimiter incidence and unmatched boundary fibers
    ├── expression syntax and exact rational arithmetic
    ├── plural left / right / difference polynomial currents
    ├── root fibers
    ├── homogeneous coordinate-algebra presentation
    └── receiver-local capability / obstruction standing

newly enacted evidence
    -> recurrent articulation morphology
    -> generated linguistic deed
    -> self-emanated return into both text and symbolic standing
```

No corpus passage is eligible to become the mathematical result. The text ecology conditions the
notation spectrum and the reusable language around a returned evidence face. The arithmetic,
polynomial, Dyck, and coordinate-algebra consequences are enacted after the prompt arrives.

## The symbolic body

`ExactDyckReceipt` retains every `()`, `[]`, and `{}` pair with byte coordinates and nesting depth.
Unexpected closings, mismatched species, and unreturned openings remain distinct obstructions;
the parser never silently repairs them.

The algebra receiver parses exact integer/symbol expressions with addition, subtraction,
multiplication, rational division, nonnegative powers, unary orientation, parentheses, and
implicit products such as `3x` or `(x+1)(x-1)`. It normalizes into a sparse multivariate
polynomial over the exact rationals. An equality forms three simultaneous currents: its left
normalization, right normalization, and independently composed difference. Equality closes only
when that difference is structurally zero.

The root organ closes exact linear roots and quadratic formula fibers. A quadratic radical stays
symbolic; no floating approximation enters. Higher-degree or multivariate roots remain named
open fibers until another exact operator is mounted.

An algebraic-geometry prompt transports nonzero equation differences into the existing engine
coordinate-algebra owner. Rational coefficients are cleared exactly; inhomogeneous terms acquire
a fresh horizon generator; every generator and relation carries an event source. The result
reports relation degrees, the exact tangent-carrier dimension at the homogeneous origin, and the
bounded dimension certificate already justified by the engine. One principal relation closes a
hypersurface certificate. Several relations retain the engine's explicit `OPEN` general-ideal
dimension until a Gröbner/Hilbert certificate returns.

## What this establishes—and the exact open seam

This construction establishes:

- newly computed language which is neither a source passage nor a voice transform of one;
- exact arithmetic, polynomial normalization, identity comparison, root fibers, Dyck structure,
  and bounded algebraic-geometry testimony inside one continuing receiver;
- plural computational phases induced by one prompt;
- source/self lineage, exact return, rest/remount, and encountered-obstruction recurrence; and
- a machine-generated account of its operative capabilities and observed unknowns.

It does **not** establish that 50,667 text occurrences have become a general semantic world model.
In the present composition, their complete incidence conditions notation and articulation, while
an arbitrary prose relation such as lightning causation still lacks an executable exterior deed.
The concrete integration seam is therefore not “better retrieval” and not a universal semantic
law. A returned prose relation must be able to found reusable symbolic differentials and typed
world-deed arguments which later prompts can compose with the exact operator ecology. Until that
crossing is built, relational source testimony remains available in its existing owner, but it
must not be mistaken for independently enacted symbolic thought.

The inherited prompt-intent transducer is also bounded. It recognizes arithmetic, identity,
root, algebraic-geometry, delimiter, and introspection morphology; it is not a learned universal
English grammar. Unsupported function symbols, nonconstant rational functions, general theorem
proving, general ideal dimension, and roots above the mounted exact degree remain explicit fibers
rather than fabricated answers.
