## 1. The objects

A **line** is a tube: one learner state carried lap after lap under one law, with a saved state at each lap's end ([the line-entry record](2026-11-11_A_BUILD_ENTERS_A_LINE_BY_REPRODUCING_THE_LINES_OWN_LAP_A_COMMIT_CALLED_A_READING_HAD_CHANGED_THE_TRAINING_COMPARISON_AND_A_ONCE_A_SITE_PART_NEEDS_ITS_OWN_GRID.md) §1). The **trunk** is the one tree that every thread, clone and guide reads. A **port** moves a law's owner from one tree to another. It is a transport between two constitutions, and what it must conserve is fixed at its consumers. A line has two:

- the lap, `T(s)`: the section lines a build prints when it trains from a saved state `s`;
- the reads, `v(s)`: the lines each reading verb `v` prints from `s`, by which the lap is scored.

The port's equations, at those consumers, with the timings aside:

`T_port(s) = T_line(s)`  on every section read,   and   `v_port(s) = v_line(s)`  for every verb a line's read script calls.

A reading leaves the state's bytes the same on and off ([CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md), method). That sorts it from a law. It does not make it unconsumed: its lines are the line's score, and a score read by another tree's verb is a comparison across two trees, which the line-entry rule exists to prevent.

The tree itself is held as retention is: a quotient sufficient for the admitted future, never an archive. The admitted future here is the running lines' laps and reads and the plan's next steps. What no admitted consumer distinguishes leaves the tree, and git history is the archive.

## 3. The ruling

[ruling; theory, 07:42 UTC] By the sizes, what `main` added to the field, the kernels and the trainer from November 6 to 9 is one attempt in the guide's sense (a realization named with its receipt, never "the field"): each failure of the energy field was answered with a further law, and its writes stayed disordered ([the audit record of November 9](2026-11-09_THE_ENERGY_LINE_WRITES_STAY_UNORDERED_THROUGH_259_AND_THE_WORK_STOPS_TO_AUDIT_ITSELF.md)). No lap of a running line was trained under it. So the port is a reset of the field to what trains, sorted by the bits:

- **A law the line trains under** is kept, and wins every conflict in the field, the kernels and the trainer.
- **A law variant no line trained under** is deleted with its switches. It is in history, and a return is a new attempt with its own guess.
- **A reading** is kept and pointed at the field that trains. This clause was first written with "where the re-point is mechanical, else set aside", and that was wrong for a reading a live line is scored by (§5).
- **Records, guides and the atlas** are `main`'s, edited in this change to say what the tree now holds (§7).

The writer state's comparison on its letters off the void (`FIELD_WRITER_LETTERS` on `main`) goes with the variants: the line's every lap compared it on classes, and the one lap trained with the letters was confounded and discarded ([the line-entry record](2026-11-11_A_BUILD_ENTERS_A_LINE_BY_REPRODUCING_THE_LINES_OWN_LAP_A_COMMIT_CALLED_A_READING_HAD_CHANGED_THE_TRAINING_COMPARISON_AND_A_ONCE_A_SITE_PART_NEEDS_ITS_OWN_GRID.md) §2.3). ## 5. The correction: a reading a line is scored by is consumed code

[located about 08:40 UTC, in the table of what the first commit set aside] The first commit set aside, under my clause "else set aside", the release's stages (the settle, the re-draw, the exchange, the charge), the locked visit and the ring and reach censuses. Every lap of both running lines is scored by three of those verbs, the settle, the re-draw and the charge (which also runs the locked refread on the held problems): the lap's read script runs them from the lap's saved state, through a separately staged binary built from `main`'s side. Merged as it stood, the trunk would have held a field whose laps could be trained and not read, and the lines' scores would have kept coming from a tree that no longer existed on `main`.

The cause is mine. I ruled on the readings from the two trees' sizes and never recovered which verbs the lines' read scripts call. The acceptance I had written at 04:53 UTC already asked for it ([the flock record](2026-11-11_A_FLOCK_OF_UNLIKE_MEMBERS_JOINS_A_ROUND_ONLY_AT_ITS_OWN_GRAIN_A_PAIRS_TELESCOPING_PART_IS_TAKEN_ONCE_A_SITE_AND_A_GROUP_DETECTS_THROUGH_ITS_CONTACTS.md) §8.5: `main`'s readers give the same bytes on the line's states), and the ruling of 07:42 dropped it.

The repair [ordered about 08:45 UTC; `claude/port-v14` `e94d44a00`, pushed about 09:56 UTC]: every verb the lines' read scripts call was ported onto the field that trains, with its tests, before the merge, and accepted by §6.5. Three verbs came onto the field that trains (`settle-field`, `redraw-field`, `charge-field`) with the field's owners they call (the settle, the plain and the ring-tilted re-draw, the certified passes, the exchange and re-draw proposals, the root charge and the letters' balance) and six tests; the other verbs the scripts call (the lap itself, the order, the opening, the length, the transmission and the gate's reading) were on the line already. The second commit adds 1,174 lines in four files (the field 442, its tests 175, the trainer 554, the command table 4) and removes one. Its code came from the tree the scan chain's read binary was built from (`claude/exchange-walk` at `0d77939ba`). One function changed on the way: the keyed write read `main`'s release law, whose variants are retired, and it now reads the release's switches as the length reading does; it wrote with those variants off on every lap, so its lines are the same (§6.5).

The rule, added to the line-entry rule in both guides: a build that replaces the binary a line's laps are read with prints, from the lap's saved state, the lines of that lap's own read logs, timings aside; and a reading that a live line's read script calls is consumed code, which a port carries. Its reason is this section. Its scope is the readings of live lines; a reading no live line calls may be set aside, by name, with its code's last commit recorded.

## 9. Owed

The guide bars a run switch for core behaviour since November 8. - **The readings set aside** return one at a time when a question needs them, each pointed at the field that trains and accepted on a saved state. The guide's structure measures name some of them (the letters' balance, the counts of counts); until they return, a ruling that needs one says so and brings it back first.
- **The transmission reading's reference rows.** The read script prints two rows for the text's own count table that were typed into the script and do not agree with each other (levels 3.481, 2.710, 1.898, 1.423, 1.224 nats give decrements 0.771, 0.812, 0.475, 0.199, where the typed decrements read 0.71, 0.73, 0.51, 0.23). - **The lap scripts' reference field.** The lap lines of both chains print an empty field where the lap they are compared with is named. The scripts are corrected after both chains end, since a running chain's script is not edited.
- **The branches.** `claude/port-v14`, `claude/skin-grid`, `claude/scan-clean`, `claude/split-bits` and `claude/v14-face-line` end with this merge, by the census tool on the PC, leaving every worktree that holds a live run.

## 10. The lessons checked

- *A located cause carried unrepaired into a new consumer*: the port is the repair of the located cause of November 11 04:53 (two laws under one name). The correction of §5 is the same failure one level up, caught before the merge: the port would have carried the lines' reads on an unowned binary into the trunk.
- *A design thought in the programming language*: the first ruling sorted code by file sizes and merge hunks. The sort that holds is by consumers: which transports of which saved states must stay equal.
- *A refusal answered with a larger limit*: none. The reads and the reproductions ran under their projected bounds.
- *Seen material graded as unseen*, *bits read as progress*: not touched; the port changes no lap's bits and claims no gain.
