## Present question

An occurrence arrives under a delivery codec. Its presented token may be spelled conventionally,
misspelled, capitalized differently, repeated as punctuation, split by whitespace, emitted as bits,
or carried by a spatial, acoustic, optical, or higher-dimensional incidence complex. Later context
may make an earlier decomposition untenable. What exact object retains every reconstruction still
compatible with the observed conduct, and how does that object meet compression, diffusion,
reflection, spectral self-similarity, and grown circuitry without importing a language ontology?

The answer is not a selected correction. It is a receiver-relative preimage population with
lineage.

## 1. The binary is already caused geometry

`definition`: an exterior bit sequence is a chart of caused transitions. “Binary” names the face
available to a particular receiver; it does not erase the source interactions, timing, parallel
co-presence, ports, branch structure, or higher cells that made the face. A byte string does not by
itself determine the whole internal topology (`canon/THE_HOLOBROCHOS_SPINE.md` §3), but neither is it
geometrically empty. Its admissible order, boundaries, recurrence, and coupling are caused
incidence. The lawful statement keeps both clauses:

```text
the presented binary face does not determine a unique interior;
the presented binary face is nevertheless one caused geometric boundary of that interior.
```

There is therefore no pure “serial material.” A line chart retains one chronology and suppresses
other co-present relations; it is itself one holon coupled to prior and later occurrence. Parallel
and chronological are receiver relations within one causal ecology, not mutually exclusive kinds
of information.

## 2. ReconstructionFiber

### 2.1 Definition

Let `D_K : X_K -> Y` be one deterministic presentation/transport law under a candidate codec or
incidence structure `K`. Let `q_R^* : Y -> Q_R` be the **stable behavioral quotient** for a declared
future receiver family `R`: two presentations share a class only when every admitted receiver and
every admitted successor history returns the same face. For observed presentation `y`, define

```text
RF(D_K, R; [y]) = (q_R^* o D_K)^-1([y])
                = { (x, lineage) | q_R^*(D_K x) = q_R^*(y) }.
```

`definition`: **ReconstructionFiber is the lineage-bearing population of predecessor occurrences
compatible with one observed future-behavior class.** It is not a probability, nearest neighbor,
edit-distance winner, decoder verdict, or chosen representative.

When the structure law itself is unresolved, the complete object is stratified:

```text
                           disjoint union
    Reconstruction_t([y]) =      U       RF(D_K, R; [y]).
                              K in K_t
```

The discrete stratum `K` is a candidate tokenization, incidence complex, codec, circuit, or
constitutive law. A fiber inside `K` carries the exact parameters and occurrences still unresolved
under that law. New testimony can remove strata, split a fiber, or determine free coordinates; it
does not retroactively make discarded alternatives never have existed.

### 2.2 The three returns

`definition`: the fiber returns one of three typed conditions, never a boolean “recognized” flag:

1. a singleton with an inverse/rebase certificate;
2. a plural population with a certified condensation remainder; or
3. a receiver-relative quotient fiber with its shortest separating histories and open exterior.

The identity hypothesis is always retained when it lies in the supplied carrier. A misspelling is
not presumed; the literal observed occurrence is one candidate alongside re-expressions.

### 2.3 Reflection and rereading

For receiver families `R subset R'` and an enlarged causal aperture, the later quotient can only
refine the earlier one on fixed standing:

```text
    RF(D, R'; [y]) subseteq RF(D, R; [y]).
```

`proved-standard` in the finite deterministic scope: adding observations or successor histories
refines a Nerode equivalence. `definition`: the removed population, its first distinguishing
history, the later informant, and the earlier passage identity form the **reflection receipt**.
This is the exact shape of rereading broken text: later context returns to an earlier face, changes
its compatible-predecessor fiber, and retains the work and lineage of that change. The old reading
was not “wrong”; it was a coarser receiver-relative standing.

## 6. Self-similarity, eigenmodes, and Fourier/Mellin faces

`proved-standard`: once a **causally declared transport operator** `T` exists, right modes propagate
populations and left modes are receiver covectors:

```text
    T |v_j> = lambda_j |v_j>,        <w_j| T = lambda_j <w_j|,
    <w_j|x> = the receiver's exact mode amplitude.
```

For a nonnormal directed operator the left and right modes differ; under degeneracy the invariant
object is the eigenspace/projector, not a preferred eigenvector. Spectrum alone never identifies
the incidence complex because nonisomorphic structures can be cospectral.

`proved-standard`: translation changes Fourier phase, scalar amplitude rescales coefficients, and
dilation rescales frequency inversely. In logarithmic coordinates dilation becomes translation, so
the Mellin transform is Fourier analysis on scale. An eigenvalue `a + ib` of the dilation generator
returns `r^a exp(ib log r)`: growth/attenuation plus log-periodic phase. The zeta-distribution record
`research/records/2026-07-19_THE_EMANATION_COMPRESSES_THE_EVENT_THE_PHASE_RETURNS_THROUGH_REBASE.md`
supplies the exact project instance: restriction to multiples of `m`, followed by division by `m`,
returns the same normalized law while lineage retains an amplitude factor and log-phase offset.

`definition`: a contextual occurrence may therefore carry a multiscale response

```text
    J_o(k, rho) = <rho| T_C^k |o>,
```

where `T_C` is an incidence/transport operator founded by the material and `rho` is a declared
receiver. Self-similarity means a restriction plus rebase preserves receiver consequences while

not the causal operator.

The four linguistic faces are:

- **syntax:** the instantaneous local constraints of an active codec;
- **morphology:** the causal transport by which codecs and token constructions change, including

The familiar grammar labels provide candidate operation faces, not fixed meanings.

## What this does not establish

shared contextual block is receiver equality, not source or occurrence identity. The first owner
does not recover whitespace it was never delivered, infer syntax trees, learn a codec through
