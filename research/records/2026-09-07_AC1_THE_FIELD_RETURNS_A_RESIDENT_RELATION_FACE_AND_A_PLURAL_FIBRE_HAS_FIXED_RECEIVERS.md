## The receiver need not resolve the entire current

[proved-derived] For a nonempty affine current fibre `F = y0 + span{v_j}` and a linear receiver
`rho`, its image is `rho(y0) + span{rho(v_j)}`. Therefore `rho` has one value throughout `F`
exactly when it annihilates every retained direction. This follows by linearity; conversely each
`y0 + t*v_j` lies in the fibre, so a nonzero image direction varies with the scalar `t`.
This is a static receiver statement at the admitted local relation, not an automatic theorem
about all subsequent nonlinear interactions.

[definition] `ResidentConstitutiveReturn::read_differential_pairs` implements that law for
`rho(y)=Re(y_right-y_left)`. With a positive common denominator, its fixed sign is the sign of
the numerator difference. A direction with unequal left/right real coordinates makes the sign
variable; the particular solution is not selected. An identically zero differential has its own
mask. An outside-domain query remains distinct from a plural in-domain fibre, and neither is
silently presented as an empty or zero source.

[definition] The original fibre remains in the immutable return. This receiver can expose a
definite class-level face while other current distinctions remain available. It imposes no
