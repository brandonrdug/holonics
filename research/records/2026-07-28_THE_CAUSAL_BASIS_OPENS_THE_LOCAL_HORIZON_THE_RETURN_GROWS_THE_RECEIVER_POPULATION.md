# The causal basis opens the local horizon; the return grows the receiver population

## Exact question

Can a real causal lineage condition a receiver ecology whose members are local
perspectives rather than fixed bins, whose number is not prescribed, whose lower
supports remain present when they return as coarser faces, and whose closure,
subdivision, growth, and mixed branching are exact observable relations rather
than scalar scores?

The bounded application is the native GOES-16 GLM
event → group → flash hierarchy around the beginning of the RELAMPAGO window.
The hierarchy is inherited testimony used to grade the machine. It is not a
claim that GLM groups or flashes are physical lightning channels.

## The receiver is not a bin, point, camera, or scalar radius

Brandon's direct ontology throughout the discussion is that a receiver is a
causal perspective or relay. Its center is not an absolute coordinate origin.
Boundaries arise where transported local distinctions cease to cohere, and the
visible ball or sphere is at most a section of a greater local hypervolume.
Receivers can recur at a coarser grain without erasing their constituents.

The production formalization now distinguishes the following objects.

For a receiver support \(C\), a declared causal coordinate \(t\), and a declared
local coordinate selection \(D\), its causal basis is

\[
B_C=\{x\in C\mid t(x)=\min_{y\in C}t(y)\}.
\]

The basis may be a many-member face. It is not collapsed into a fictitious
center. One gauge point \(\pi_C\in B_C\) is chosen lexicographically in the
declared local chart; testimony identity breaks a tie only when the received
coordinates themselves coincide. The gauge makes computation reproducible but
has no claim to be an ontological center.

Each member supplies the exact signed transport

\[
\Delta_x = D(x)-D(\pi_C).
\]

If \(j\) is the first nonzero coordinate of \(\Delta_x\), the receiver retains

\[
\rho_x = |\Delta_{x,j}|,\qquad
d_x = \Delta_x/\rho_x.
\]

Opposed directions therefore remain opposed. Equal projective directions share
a depth fiber with exact nearest and farthest depths. Exact row reduction over
the rational transports gives the tangent rank. Signed coordinate extents,
zero-transport members, the complete support, the directional fibers, and the
tangent rank form the finite receiver-relative hypervolume used here. No
scalar volume or stored radius replaces that structure.

A finite directional population is reported as `UncertifiedDirectional`.
It may be called a hyperspherical horizon only after an actual link/manifold
certificate. A visually round or high-rank receiver is not such a certificate;
a singular link must remain singular information.

## Growth and subdivision are an overlap diagram

Before a return, the learned relation emits connected proposed supports
\(\widehat\Pi=\{\widehat C_i\}\). A later complete exclusive return supplies
\(\Pi=\{C_j\}\) over the same occurrence population. Construct the bipartite
support-intersection diagram

\[
\widehat C_i\longleftrightarrow C_j
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\widehat C_i\cap C_j\ne\varnothing.
\]

Every connected component has a bidegree \((p,r)\): the number of proposed
receivers and returned receivers in that component.

| Bidegree | Exact receiver morphology |
|---|---|
| \((1,1)\) | exact closure |
| \((1,r>1)\) | subdivision: one open receiver resolves into several returned receivers |
| \((p>1,1)\) | growth: several open receivers close as one returned receiver |
| \((p>1,r>1)\) | mixed branch |

No matching threshold or aggregate accuracy score defines these cases. The
complete proposal and return supports remain in standing, while their exact
overlap populations and component bidegrees are retained as a compact receipt.
The receiver population is therefore caused by the relation and its return; it
has no fixed locus count.

This construction currently grades complete exclusive returns. It does not
pretend that every biological or physical receiver ecology is exclusive.
Overlapping directional covers remain a different return doctrine: absence
from a partial cover cannot lawfully be interpreted as negative testimony.

## Recurrence across observation grain

A returned lower cell can enter later standing as one outer receiver face only
through a complete one-to-one quotient:

\[
q:C_j\longrightarrow u_j.
\]

Production requires:

1. \(C_j\) belongs to an already admitted complete exclusive partition;
2. every lower cell has exactly one supplied outer face and every outer face
   has exactly one lower cell;
3. the outer face's inherited source identity equals the lower cell identity;
4. the target chart and target family are declared;
5. target chronology cannot precede source chronology; and
6. later conditioning or prediction must use the chronology stored by the
   quotient, not a chronology resupplied by the application.

The quotient records the lower cell address, lower and upper families, caused
event, target chronology, and target testimony. It does not replace or delete
the lower support. This is recurrence as a preserved change of grain, not
bitmap caching or naive reuse.

## The real bounded application

`relampago_receiver_ecology -- receiver-dynamics` uses sixty contiguous native
GLM L2 products:

- conditioning: 2018-12-13 22:50:00–23:00:00 UTC, thirty 20-second products;
- held out: 23:00:00–23:10:00 UTC, thirty 20-second products.

The source membrane imports exact packed event coordinates and native
event-parent-group, group-parent-flash, group, and flash identities. Native
floating coordinates on group and flash faces are converted losslessly from
their binary32 values to a common \(2^{-20}\) integer carrier before entering
the exact affine charts. The machine does not receive the held-out parent
partition until after it has emitted its proposal.

| held out | 21,777 | 10,648 | 1,061 |

All 18,965 group and flash outer faces entered through validated grain

| Transition | Proposed | Returned | Exact closures | Subdivisions | Growths | Mixed branches |

The second row is the important ecological result. The higher grain does not
behave like a fixed relabeling of lower bins: some proposed receivers divide,
some join, and five support components contain both operations.

Receiver perspectives also have varying intrinsic rank. Returned event-group
supports range from tangent rank zero through three. Returned flash supports,
computed from group latitude, longitude, time, area, and energy, range from
rank zero through five. In the held-out population, 544 of 1,061 flash
supports attain rank five; none is called a hypersphere because no link
certificate was supplied.

The many-member causal basis is not merely a defensive type distinction. In
the held-out event → group population its mean size is 1.984 and its maximum
is fourteen; in conditioning the maximum is twenty-two. Collapsing the
minimum-time face to the lowest allocated testimony identity would therefore
have installed an arbitrary point as the center of a genuinely extended
causal basis.

## The negative evidence is part of the result

Using every non-stratum received coordinate does not solve group → flash
organization. Its held-out grade contains:

- 92,096 conflicted-lineage witnesses;
- 1,786 returned-apart pairs forced together;
- 1,401 returned-together pairs forced apart; and
- 43 returned-together pairs left open.

Thus the current translation-free, componentwise pair-difference front does
not uniquely determine native flash membership from this conditioning
ecology. This does not show that the native return is random, that a longer
run will fix it, or that deterministic organization is impossible. It
establishes the narrower fact that pairwise differences in the present local
coordinates lose relevant contextual topology at the group → flash boundary.
