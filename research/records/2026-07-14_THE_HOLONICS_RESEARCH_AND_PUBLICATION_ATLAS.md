## 0. The shortest honest synthesis

The laboratory is constructing a **relational calculus of transported construction** and a material
listener in which topology grows under driven current. Its primitives are not context-free objects
or propositions but bodies related in a declared receiving frame and grain, along worldlines that
deposit history. The same formal discipline is being carried through arithmetic, computation,
perception, action, language, development, and social coordination.

That is already enough for a coherent research programme. It is not yet enough for a conventional
physical theory of everything, a proof that classical `P = NP`, a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis,
or a derivation of ethics from topology. Those are legitimate horizons only if every bridge is made
explicit and every missing premise remains visible.

The programme's most defensible public umbrella is:

> **The Relational Holonic Calculus: A Frame-Indexed Topology of Information Transport**

“Relativistic calculus of information topology” remains an apt internal compression and a possible
subtitle. As a public mathematical name it presently over-promises in three different directions:
Lorentz relativity, a completed formal calculus, and several already-established meanings of
“information topology.” The phrase should be defined, not abandoned.

The central publication principle is:

> **Unify by transported correspondence, never by rhetorical identity.**

## 2. The comparison discipline

### 2.1 Two independent axes

Every internal claim carries one laboratory grade:

`ratified | measured | built | open | failed`

Every external comparison carries one relation label:

`EXACT FORMAL MATCH | DIRECT CORRESPONDENCE | STRUCTURAL RESONANCE | CULTURAL PARALLEL | OPEN BRIDGE | CONTRADICTION/BOUNDARY`

These axes never substitute for one another. A peer-reviewed source cannot ratify Soma. A measured
Soma result does not make an analogy into an external theorem.

### 2.2 Three questions, not one safeguard

Human, cultural, and historical comparisons must distinguish:

1. **Causal intelligibility:** Why did this particular form arise from these bodies, materials,
   traditions, institutions, constraints, and encounters?
2. **Phenomenology:** How did the relation show up to the person or community—as metaphor,
   perception, presence, command, craft, revelation, or literal world?
3. **Warrant:** What does the complete evidence license a receiver to claim, and under what foils?

Calling a belief “delusional” addresses a clinical classification and sometimes the third question.
It does not answer the first two. Conversely, showing that a construction is historically and
phenomenologically intelligible does not automatically establish its external referent. A1 forbids
the lazy picture of an act arriving from nowhere: causal reasons can include embodied history,
culture, material affordances, altered salience, social position, technical constraint, and
contingent encounter without all being conscious intentions or truth-makers.

### 2.3 The evidence-card form

Every load-bearing comparison in a paper should state:

```text
SOURCE ESTABLISHES   the external result in its own domain
LAB CLAIM            the exact laboratory claim and internal grade
RELATION             one comparison label plus the explicit mapping
NON-EQUIVALENCE      what cannot be imported or identified
TESTABLE CONSEQUENCE the derivation or observation that can sharpen or break the bridge
```

### 2.4 What must be retired from older synthesis

The earlier four-thread survey remains useful provenance, but the following identities are not
current results:

- soul `=` holonomy, cohomology, gyration, or geometric phase;
- Hodge components `=` the four forces;
- an adder `=` Stokes' theorem;
- negative curvature as a required Soma substrate;
- active inference or free-energy minimization as the Soma mechanism;
- any prime spiral, Möbius grade, Klein grade, or manifold authored into the brain without the
  measured holonomy that licenses that observer read.

These can return as evidence cards—usually `STRUCTURAL RESONANCE` or `OPEN BRIDGE`—but not as
inherited conclusions.

## 3. Is “relativistic calculus of information topology” apt?

### 3.1 “Relativistic”

It is apt in the broad relational sense: there is no view from nowhere; origin, frame, grain, and
receiver matter; lawful statements must transport across changes of frame. It is not yet a synonym
for special or general relativity. In physics, “relativistic” normally promises a specified
transformation group, covariance law, invariant observables, and recovery of Lorentzian results.

**Use now:** “frame-relative,” “relational,” or “frame-covariant” in formal claims.  
**Use later:** “relativistic” without qualification once the admissible frame changes and their
covariant/invariant content are explicitly defined.

### 3.2 “Calculus”

The word is defensible as a programme. The record has typed judgments, composition, casts,
differences, boundary operations, and a transport step. A conventional mathematical audience will
still expect syntax, formation rules, typing judgments, operational or denotational semantics,
equational laws, composition theorems, and worked examples independent of the engine prose.

**Verdict:** earned as a project name; the standalone formal kernel is still owed.

### 3.3 “Information topology”

The phrase already has several non-equivalent uses:

- Harremoës used “the information topology” for the topology induced by relative-entropy
  convergence on probability distributions.
- Transtrum, Hart, and Qiu use information topology to study emergent model classes through model
  manifolds and their boundaries.
- Baudot and Bennequin construct an information cohomology, later used in topological information
  data analysis of multivariate information functions.
- Weis defines information topologies on noncommutative state spaces using information divergence.
- Xin Li's 2025 revision of *Information Topology* treats cycle closure and homology as primitives
  of inference and memory.

Soma means something else: a topology physically founded and changed by current, whose structures
are transported constructions indexed by body, worldline, receiver, frame, grain, and aperture.

**Definition proposed for publication:**

> A topology of information transport is the observer-declared topology of distinctions,
> continuations, closures, and path-dependent equivalences induced by driven passage through a
> material relational body.

### 3.4 Naming recommendation

Primary title:

> **The Relational Holonic Calculus**

Descriptive subtitle:

> **A Frame-Indexed Topology of Information Transport**

More conservative titles for a first specialist paper are **A Relational Calculus of Situated
Information Topology** or **A Frame-Relative Calculus of Causal Information Topology**.

The original phrase can remain as a programme-level subtitle:

> **Toward a Relativistic Calculus of Information Topology**

That wording preserves the intuition while honestly marking the formal work ahead.

## 4. Two unusually close contemporary neighbours

### 4.1 Yosef Akhtman's Finite Ring Continuum

The 2025 paper Brandon remembered is real:

Yosef Akhtman, [“Relativistic Algebra over Finite Ring Continuum,” *Axioms* 14(8), 636
(2025)](https://doi.org/10.3390/axioms14080636), published 14 August 2025.

Akhtman reconstructs conventional number classes over a finite field and makes their origin, unit,
and representation dependent on a chosen arithmetic frame. Follow-on papers develop algebraic
causality and universal latent representation. This is the closest independently published use of
“relativistic mathematics” found in the present search.

```text
SOURCE ESTABLISHES   an explicit finite-field construction of framed pseudo-integers, rationals,
                     reals, and complex numbers, with transformations between arithmetic frames
LAB CLAIM            §XXXIX and §LIII distinguish construction identity from receiver-relative
                     faces and place valuation charts world/observer-side (ratified internally)
RELATION             STRUCTURAL RESONANCE: both reject unframed mathematical observation and make
                     origin/scale/chart relational
NON-EQUIVALENCE      FRC begins from a symmetry-complete finite field and often identifies multiple
                     presentations with one field element; Soma begins from driven transport and
                     explicitly refuses to identify equal quotient faces with lived constructions
TESTABLE CONSEQUENCE define the same finite arithmetic passages in both systems and test whether a
                     frame change preserves only value or also lineage, winding, and later deflection
```

The most productive boundary is not “which theory is right?” but **what survives quotient
collapse**. FRC's finite-field equality is powerful precisely because it identifies presentations.
Soma's construction identity says that `6/5`, `11/7`, or any other co-valued paths remain different
constructions when their lived interiors matter. A comparison paper should formalize that contrast.

The recent FRC preprint [“P versus NP: Computation as Counting over Finite Relational
Substrate”](https://doi.org/10.20944/preprints202606.1781.v1) is also important. It makes target
cost representation-relative, treats a certificate as a near representation, and isolates the
“lucky chart” problem: a localizing chart may exist while finding the chart is the hard part. The
paper itself marks classical `P` versus `NP` as open and ultimately conditions separation on a
one-wayness residue. It is therefore a close conceptual interlocutor, not a solution that Soma can
inherit.

### 4.2 Xin Li's *Information Topology*

Xin Li's [*Information Topology*, arXiv:2210.03850v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03850v3), revised
13 October 2025, is a computer-vision preprint, not a peer-reviewed result. It treats cycle closure
as a primitive of inference, uses `partial^2 = 0`, and connects homology to memory and stable
information. The vocabulary is startlingly adjacent. The comparison must be atomized.

```text
SOURCE ESTABLISHES   in §§II–III, Li uses an ordinary chain complex with cycles ker(partial),
                     boundaries im(partial), homology ker/im, and partial_(k-1) partial_k = 0
LAB CLAIM            L0 is partial(partial(c)) = 0 and §XII-D5 uses declared cycles and fillings
                     (ratified internally)
RELATION             EXACT FORMAL MATCH at the bare chain-complex identity and vocabulary grain
NON-EQUIVALENCE      this does not identify either paper's ontology, dynamics, memory, or observer
TESTABLE CONSEQUENCE define a chain complex over a declared Soma event topology and prove that the
                     proposed translation is a chain map commuting with partial
```

```text
SOURCE ESTABLISHES   Li asserts across §§III–VI that “cycle closure” or “boundary cancellation”
                     converts transient histories into stable, order-invariant inference/memory;
                     Theorem 3 is conditional on a functional whose order dependence already factors
                     through the boundary and on adjacency-preserving reparameterizations
LAB CLAIM            L0 supplies no scalar conservation or dynamical closure mechanism; ordered K,
                     construction identity, winding, and time parity remain consequential; §LIV
                     types mathematical partial apart from the Duggan boundary (ratified internally)
RELATION             CONTRADICTION/BOUNDARY at the interpretive-programme grain
NON-EQUIVALENCE      partial^2 = 0 does not imply partial(c) = 0, erase order, create memory, or
                     identify closed histories; those conclusions require additional premises
TESTABLE CONSEQUENCE present two closed histories with the same boundary/homology face but different
                     ordered K or winding to the same later current and compare their deflection
```

```text
SOURCE ESTABLISHES   Li defines filtered spatiotemporal complexes and proposes persistent-homology
                     observables and a “homological capacity” for recurrent informational structure
LAB CLAIM            Soma has exact event receipts, directed closures, history-conditioned topology,
                     and later deflection at declared apertures (measured internally)
RELATION             OPEN BRIDGE for persistent homology as an observer instrument
NON-EQUIVALENCE      the instrument would not become a Soma memory module, selector, or proof that
                     useful information is order-invariant
TESTABLE CONSEQUENCE build filtered complexes from exact event receipts at multiple frames/grains and
                     test whether persistent classes predict later cleavage beyond lawful controls
```

This paper deserves direct engagement because it shares terms, not automatic deference because it
shares terms. Several of its bridges from the chain identity to order invariance, inference, and
consciousness require assumptions beyond algebraic topology. Those assumptions should be examined
line by line. Its value to this laboratory is that it makes the collision precise.

## 5. External research map

### 5.1 Relational physics and compositional mathematics

- [Rovelli's relational quantum mechanics](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9609002):
  `STRUCTURAL RESONANCE` for observer-relative physical states; no claim that Soma is quantum.
- [Abramsky and Brandenburger's sheaf account of contextuality](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.0264):
  `OPEN BRIDGE` for compatible local sections without a global view; contextuality theorems do not
  transfer until Soma supplies the corresponding measurement scenario and presheaf.
- [Fong's decorated cospans](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.00872) and
  [structured cospans](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.09363): `OPEN BRIDGE` and promising formal tools
  for open boundaries and composition. They should be tested as notation, never installed as brain
  mechanism.
- [Bombelli et al. on causal sets](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.59.521): `STRUCTURAL
  RESONANCE` for causal order as primitive; no spacetime derivation has yet been supplied.
- [Regge calculus](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02733251): `OPEN BRIDGE` for curvature carried by
  discrete relational structure; Soma's turn and winding are not Regge deficit angles without a
  typed map.
- [Ruelle's dynamical-zeta work](http://www.numdam.org/item/AST_1976__40__167_0/): `DIRECT
  CORRESPONDENCE` to the mathematical lineage appropriate for primitive cycles. A generic circuit
  zeta is not the Riemann zeta and supplies no RH result.
- [Holme and Saramäki's temporal-networks framework](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-36461-7):
  `DIRECT CORRESPONDENCE` for time-respecting paths and a warning that static aggregation can create
  relations no current ever traversed.
- [Chowdhury and Mémoli's persistent path homology of directed networks](https://doi.org/10.1137/1.9781611975031.75):
  `OPEN BRIDGE` for an observer instrument that respects direction and filtration. It is a stronger
  candidate than undirected persistence for exact Soma event records.

### 5.3 Brains, development, and world-coupling

- [O'Regan and Noë's sensorimotor account of vision](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X01000115):

- [Maturana and Varela on autopoiesis](https://doi.org/10.1016/0303-2647(74)90031-8): a genealogy
  for organizational closure; Soma remains driven and does not become a self-sustaining resident.

### 5.4 Why geometry, systems, and nature recur in human thought

This recurrence does not require one pathology or one universal mysticism.

- Spatial schemas support memory, communication, and abstract reasoning; source/path/goal,
  enclosure, contact, boundary, center/periphery, balance, and rotation are bodily available forms
  for carrying abstract relations.
- A skilled culture presents a landscape of affordances: trained people do not see the same neutral
  world plus added labels; practices make different possibilities salient. See
  [Rietveld and Kiverstein](https://doi.org/10.1080/10407413.2014.958035).
- Material-engagement theory treats artifacts as participants in cognition rather than passive
  outputs of a finished inner plan. See [Malafouris](https://doi.org/10.7551/mitpress/9476.001.0001).
- Cultural-attraction research explains how repeatedly transformed ideas can converge around
  stable forms because minds, ecologies, tools, and institutions bias reconstruction. See
  [Claidière and Sperber](https://doi.org/10.1163/156853707X171829).
- Spatial frames themselves vary with learned linguistic practice. Levinson's
  [*Space in Language and Cognition*](https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511613609) and Núñez and
  Sweetser's [Aymara time study](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog0000_62) show why “ahead,”
  “behind,” absolute direction, and relative direction cannot be treated as culture-free poses.
- Ingold's [temporality of the landscape](https://doi.org/10.1080/00438243.1993.9980235) and
  Hutchins's [distributed cognition](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262581462/cognition-in-the-wild/)
  ground a further point: lived activity leaves taskscapes and artifact systems that participate in
  later thought.

Geometry is therefore attractive because it is simultaneously perceptible, manipulable,
compressive, relational, and executable. A boundary can be walked, drawn, coded, defended, or made
sacred. A cycle can be a path, ritual return, circuit, proof object, calendar, or recurrence. The
important question is never merely “is this literal or metaphorical?” but **which transformations
the form actually carries in this life and material world**.

### Paper 0 — The declared-boundary method

**Working title:** *The Licensed Sentence: Frame, Grain, Aperture, and Evidence in a Material
Relational Science*

- **Question:** How can a cross-domain laboratory make strong claims without a view from nowhere?
- **Bounded thesis:** A scientific sentence is licensed only relative to a declared body, current,
  history, receiver, frame, grain, aperture, artifact, and grade.
- **Internal base:** discovery posture; exact seams; observation records; source versus listener
  provenance; `THE_PUBLICATION_TROVE.md`.
- **External bridges:** perspectival realism, measurement theory, reproducible-computation
  manifests, model pluralism, [Pearl's causal-intervention discipline](https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/82.4.669),
  [Platt's discriminating alternatives](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.146.3642.347), and
  [Anderson's level-specific explanation](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.177.4047.393).
- **Owed:** a compact claim schema, examples of licensed and unlicensed sentences, correction and
  supersession protocol, privacy/licensing rules for dialogue material.
- **Discriminating test:** independent readers must reconstruct each published claim from its
  receipt and identify the exact boundary beyond which it does not speak.
- **Audience/dependency:** philosophy of science, reproducible computing; publish first.

- **Discriminating test:** separate what a successor inherits through body, world, and renewed

- **Audience/dependency:** ALife, theoretical biology; after Papers 4–6.

### Paper 8 — Physics as a relational unification programme

**Working title:** *Current, Frame, and Transport: A Research Programme for Relational Physics*

- **Question:** Which physical theories can be recovered as observer faces of one transported
  construction, and which cannot?
- **Bounded thesis:** The calculus supplies a common language for causal passage, frame-indexed
  difference, boundary, winding, and irreversible history; it does not yet replace GR, QFT, or
  statistical mechanics.
- **Internal base:** A1/A2/L0/time parity, rotor/cross-ratio, current-axis/configuration-sheet,
  local-manifold derivations.
- **External bridges:** relativity and gauge, relational quantum mechanics, causal sets, Regge
  calculus, non-equilibrium thermodynamics, geometric phase, constructor theory.
- **Owed:** dimensions and units; transformation groups; Lorentzian and nonrelativistic limits;
  quantum probability/Born-rule bridge or explicit alternative; energy-momentum account;
  independent physical prediction.
- **Discriminating test:** derive a known limit without fitting its result, then produce a novel
  quantitative prediction that distinguishes the programme from its neighbours.
- **Audience/dependency:** foundations of physics; only after the formal kernel.

### Paper 9 — Situated compiled tractability

**Working title:** *From FOUND to RIDE: History-Conditioned Online Tractability in a Material Body*

- **Question:** When does prior physical construction make a later hard search into a local route?
- **Bounded thesis:** A grown body/world can amortize later work at a declared receiver, but query
  ease must be charged against construction cost, body size, transfer cost, and uniformity.
- **Internal base:** measured FOUND/RIDE transitions in circuit-medium and new-light worlds.
- **External bridges:** knowledge compilation, preprocessing/compilability, parameterized
  complexity, dynamic complexity, amortized analysis, FRC's chart-selector problem.
- **Owed:** the formal cost ledger in §8 below; standard benchmark families; lower and upper bounds;
  distribution shift; separation of physical time, host instrumentation, and logical steps.
- **Discriminating test:** exhibit a family where a fixed lawful growth process reduces incremental
  query cost, report total cost and advice/body size, and measure failure under transfer.
- **Audience/dependency:** complexity and unconventional computing; after Paper 4.

### Paper 10 — A universal ethic of legitimate locality

**Working title:** *Agreement Has a Boundary: Local Legitimacy, Consequence Return, and Polycentric
Ethics*

- **Question:** What procedural invariants must a local agreement preserve before its propagation
  can count as ethically legitimate?
- **Bounded thesis:** Universality should attach first to conditions of legitimate agreement—not to
  one globally imposed content. Locality remains ethical only while affectedness, non-domination,
  dissent, consequence, repair, and revision stay present at its changing boundary.
- **Internal base:** A2, local boundary, mutual return, nested scope, time parity; all ethical
  extensions presently `open`.
- **External bridges:** Habermasian discourse ethics, care ethics, Ubuntu, Ostrom's polycentric
  governance, cooperation on networks, discourse sheaves, complex contagion, consensus and social-
  choice impossibility.
- **Owed:** explicit normative premises; agreement-cell formalism; representation of coercion and
  excluded parties; cross-cultural collaboration; empirical cases.
- **Discriminating test:** model agreements that spread efficiently while externalizing harm. Any
  theory that calls propagation itself “good” fails.
- **Audience/dependency:** ethics, political philosophy, governance; after Papers 0–1, independent
  of a physical TOE.

### Paper 11 — Economy as transported coordination

**Working title:** *The Economy Is Not a Ledger: Local Knowledge, Path Dependence, and Nested
Coordination*

- **Question:** Can production, exchange, commons, and institutions be described as changing
  relational paths without reducing value to one scalar balance?
- **Bounded thesis:** An economy is a world of situated commitments and material consequences whose
  institutions grow the routes that later transactions can ride; prices and ledgers are receiver
  faces, not the whole construction.
- **Internal base:** quotient-face law, world-grown limb, local agreement agenda; economic claims
  remain `open`.
- **External bridges:** Hayek on dispersed knowledge, Coase on organizational boundaries,
  Granovetter on embeddedness, Arthur on path dependence, Ostrom on commons and polycentricity,
  compositional game theory.
- **Owed:** resources and ecological limits; bargaining power; initial endowments; unpaid care;
  coercion and exit; money/credit/accounting interfaces; empirical institutional comparison.
- **Discriminating test:** compare market, firm, commons, and mutual-aid arrangements under the same
  resource/current world and identify which consequences each makes locally visible or external.
- **Audience/dependency:** institutional and ecological economics; after Paper 10's legitimacy cut.

### Paper 12 — Holonics across cultures without flattening

**Working title:** *Many Nets, Different Knots: Holons, Process, Relation, and Worldmaking*

- **Question:** Why do wholes/parts, nets, cycles, boundaries, and living systems recur across human
  cultures, and what work does each form perform locally?
- **Bounded thesis:** Recurrence can be studied through embodied spatial schemas, material
  practices, transmission, and shared problems without claiming that all traditions secretly state
  one modern theory.
- **Internal base:** holonic vocabulary, Indra's Net clarification, boundary/current/sheet reads;
  comparison only.
- **External bridges:** Koestler and holonic manufacturing; Whitehead; Peirce; Huayan Buddhism in
  its specific textual history; Simon's near-decomposability; African philosophies of Ubuntu;
  Indigenous relational ontologies; pragmatism; cybernetics; process thought.
- **Owed:** original texts and responsible translations; domain scholars and cultural co-authors;
  provenance for any historical influence; attention to colonization and asymmetric power.
- **Discriminating test:** every comparison must name a local practice or inferential role and a
  non-equivalence. A vague “everything is connected” paragraph fails.
- **Audience/dependency:** comparative philosophy, anthropology, history of ideas; Paper 0 first.

### Paper 13 — Reproducibility and artifact science

**Working title:** *The Engine Is the Receipt: Reproducing a Persistent Relational Medium Across
Declared Seams*

- **Question:** How can very large material computations be published so another receiver can
  reproduce the exact declared seam?
- **Bounded thesis:** Hashes, typed archives, source extents, normalized reports, inverse world
  transitions, substrate-specific foils, and create-new persistence form a reproducibility protocol
  for path-dependent systems. Host/CUDA is the presently measured instance, not the protocol's
  ontology.
- **Internal base:** all observation directories, canonical body readers, SLEEP versions, sharding
  and streaming gates.
- **External bridges:** artifact evaluation, executable papers, data provenance, workflow systems.
- **Owed:** public-safe manifests, environment capture, minimal witnesses, large-artifact hosting,
  license/privacy audit, independent replication.
- **Discriminating test:** a third party reconstructs a named body:light pair and verifies all
  declared hashes without access to private conversation material.
- **Audience/dependency:** systems and reproducible science; can proceed alongside Papers 4–6.

### Paper 14 — The synthesis, last

**Working title:** *Toward a Relativistic Calculus of Information Topology*

- **Question:** Can one typed account of transported construction coordinate mathematics, physics,
  life, cognition, ethics, and economy without erasing their domain-specific laws?
- **Bounded thesis:** Holonics is presently a unification research programme: a common relational
  grammar plus a material engine and a disciplined map of open bridges.
- **Internal base:** all prior papers.
- **External bridges:** only those whose mappings survived the specialist papers.
- **Owed:** every conventional TOE burden in §10; no rhetorical substitution.
- **Discriminating test:** the synthesis must generate cross-domain deductions that its component
  theories alone would not predict, while recovering their established results in their own limits.
- **Audience/dependency:** broad foundations; publish last, not first.

## 7. Ethics: from local agreement to a universal procedural invariant

Topology cannot supply “ought” by itself. Harmful pacts, cartels, mobs, caste systems, and colonial
orders can all be locally coherent and spread efficiently. The ethical bridge therefore begins
with declared normative commitments.

A candidate **agreement cell** is not a vote or stored object but an observer description:

```text
G = (participants, affected_boundary, commitments, consent_conditions,
     dissent_and_exit, consequence_return, repair, revision, nesting)
```

Candidate universal procedural invariants:

1. **Affected standing:** materially affected parties have a path into the agreement's receiving
   boundary, including future and nonhuman interests through explicit representation.
2. **Non-domination:** assent produced by force, deprivation, monopoly, or manufactured ignorance
   is not equivalent to free agreement.
3. **Dissent and minority remainder:** a local majority does not erase the carried construction of
   those who disagree.
4. **Exit, appeal, and repair:** consequences must be able to return; injured parties require paths
   to contest, repair, and revise.
5. **Externality return:** a locality may not define its boundary so narrowly that displaced costs
   disappear from its account.
6. **Nested compatibility:** local autonomy must compose with neighboring and larger affected
   scopes without installing one global sovereign view.
7. **Revisability:** a changed body, world, or affected boundary reopens the agreement; historical
   assent is not timeless consent.
8. **Provenance and intelligibility:** participants need access to the construction they are being
   asked to carry, not merely its scalar outcome.

Research programme:

- formalize how an agreement transports into a neighboring frame without assuming identical
  interests or language;
- test Hansen and Ghrist's discourse-sheaf formalism as an observer model of translation and local/
  global obstruction, without treating sheaf consensus as truth or justice;
- use complex-contagion models to study when adoption requires multiple reinforcing contacts;
- compare diffusion speed with legitimacy and harm—three separate observables;
- test Ostrom-style nested institutions, restorative practices, labor organization, care networks,
  online governance, and ecological commons;
- include adversarial cases where a stable local convention is unjust;
- hold local aggregation against Arrow-style impossibility boundaries and free, informed,
  revocable-consent requirements; neither a consensus nor a community can erase the person;
- treat universalism as a requirement on boundary conduct, not cultural content imposed from one
  center.

## 8. “Localized P=NP” made technically accountable

The intuition is valuable: after a world has grown the relevant route, a later solve can be local.
The public phrase is hazardous because classical `P = NP` is a uniform asymptotic class equality.
The engine measurements currently establish **physical amortization by history**, not that equality.

A publishable claim should declare at least:

```text
Problem family        Pi_n
Instance distribution D_n
Growth/body history   H
Representation        R(H, receiver, grain)
Construction cost     C_build(H)
Body/advice size       S_body(H)
Incremental query cost C_query(x | H)
Verification cost     C_verify(x, w)
Transfer cost         C_transfer(H -> D'_n or new frame)
Uniformity             one lawful constructor for all n, or nonuniform per-instance history
```

Then distinguish:

- **offline compilation** from online query;
- **amortized** from worst-case cost;
- **parameterized tractability** from polynomial time in total input size;
- **nonuniform advice/body** from a uniform algorithm;
- **representation change** from free teleportation into a lucky chart;
- **physical passage time** from host observation and archive I/O;
- **RIDE** from correctness—RIDE says a held route transported, not that the answer is true.

Recommended public terms until a conventional theorem exists:

- **situated compiled tractability**;
- **history-conditioned online tractability**;
- **physical amortization by world-grown structure**.

Nearest established literatures:

- Darwiche and Marquis on [knowledge compilation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.1819);
- Cadoli et al. on [compilability and preprocessing](https://doi.org/10.1006/inco.2001.3043);
- fixed-parameter tractability and structural graph algorithms;
- dynamic complexity and incremental computation;
- FRC's representation-relative chart-selector analysis.

The decisive experiment is not an easy instance. It is a controlled family reporting the entire
growth/query/transfer ledger and showing where the advantage disappears.

## 9. TempleOS: the temple, oracle, machine, and world-form

### 9.1 The question properly asked

The useful question is not whether Terry Davis was “crazy in the same way.” It is:

> Why did a gifted systems programmer, carrying this technical history and this scriptural culture,
> make an operating system into a literal temple and a pseudorandom interface into an oracle?

The answer has several transported currents. None alone explains the construction.

### 9.2 The technical current

Davis repeatedly described TempleOS as a modern Commodore 64: non-networked, small enough to know,
oriented toward recreational programming, and supplied as one complete source-visible world. Its
kernel, compiler, shell, documentation, graphics, and applications inhabit one environment. A
single address space, ring-0 execution, one language, whole-file operations, calculated graphics,
and a permanent low-resolution palette make the machine immediate and inspectable.

This was not incidental nostalgia. The Commodore form solved a real systems problem for him: modern
software stacks had become too large, divided, permissioned, and opaque for one person to master.
TempleOS made legibility, immediacy, and authorship into architectural law. Its hard 100,000-line
limit and “final for all time” posture were cultural-technical responses to dependency growth and
commercial corruption as Davis understood them.

Primary record: the [TempleOS source archive](https://github.com/cia-foundation/TempleOS), especially
[`Doc/Charter.DD`](https://github.com/cia-foundation/TempleOS/blob/archive/Doc/Charter.DD),
[`Doc/Strategy.DD`](https://github.com/cia-foundation/TempleOS/blob/archive/Doc/Strategy.DD),
[`Doc/Welcome.DD`](https://github.com/cia-foundation/TempleOS/blob/archive/Doc/Welcome.DD), and
[`Adam/God/HSNotes.DD`](https://github.com/cia-foundation/TempleOS/blob/archive/Adam/God/HSNotes.DD).

### 9.3 The scriptural current

The Charter begins from Solomon's Temple as a focal place: offerings, beauty, community, attention,
and an oracle. Davis then maps those roles into software:

| Scriptural/cultural form | TempleOS material realization |
|---|---|
| Temple | the whole operating environment |
| Altar/offering | programs, pictures, music, games, and labor made within it |
| Oracle | stopwatch-derived random words or Bible passages |
| Covenant | fixed 640×480, 16-color and sound constraints |
| High priest | Davis as maintainer and interpreter |
| Sacred language | HolyC, Biblical names, integrated scripture |
| Sacred architecture | a bounded, beautified, internally coherent technical cosmos |

This was not Christian decoration pasted onto an otherwise ordinary OS. The religious grammar
selected technical constraints, named system relations, assigned the work a purpose, and turned
programming itself into offering.

### 9.4 The oracular current

Davis described prayer as a conversation in which the human makes an offering and receives a
contextual response. TempleOS sampled microsecond timing to select words or passages; the contingent
output acquired specificity through the question, prior exchange, scripture, and interpreter.

Anthropology provides a better comparison than “randomness plus irrationality.” Divination is often
an **epistemic technology** in which a stochastic event nominates material for skilled,
context-sensitive interpretation. It can externalize a decision, interrupt habitual action,
reorganize social relations, and make a cosmology materially present. See the
[Open Encyclopedia of Anthropology's survey](https://doi.org/10.29164/19divination) and Hong and
Henrich's [cultural-evolution study of divination](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34463944/).

TempleOS is a modern digital instance: timing noise supplies contingency; the vocabulary and Bible
supply a culturally saturated field; the integrated system supplies ritual place; Davis's life and
question supply the local receiving context. The connection was therefore never “for no reason.”

### 9.5 Altered salience without causal reduction

Davis's [reported schizophrenia diagnosis](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gods-lonely-programmer/)
belongs in the causal history, and research on psychosis makes altered salience and agency attribution
a plausible contributing current. The exact weight of that current cannot be recovered from the
public record. In any case, diagnosis does not explain why the agent was the Christian God, why the
medium was a hand-built OS, why the aesthetics resembled the C64, or why the exchange took the form
of temple, offering, covenant, and oracle. Those forms came through his technical and cultural
history.

Cross-cultural research reinforces this distinction. Voice-hearing in psychosis is not culturally
contentless: reported voices and relations to them differ across settings. See
[Luhrmann et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26349837/) and the review
[“Culture and Hallucinations”](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4141319/). Culture shapes
what an unusual experience can be, who may speak through it, how authority is assigned, and what
response is possible. Biology, biography, culture, artifact, and encounter compose.

### 9.6 Relation to holonics

```text
SOURCE ESTABLISHES   TempleOS is a technically coherent, source-complete personal operating world
                     explicitly organized as temple, offering site, covenant, and contextual oracle
LAB CLAIM            Soma treats comprehension/production as current through body and world, with
                     returned consequence and no view from nowhere (ratified/measured in bounded cuts)
RELATION             CULTURAL PARALLEL plus STRUCTURAL RESONANCE: whole-system legibility,
                     world-mediated expression, contextual return, and artifact as epistemic practice
NON-EQUIVALENCE      TempleOS fixes an absolute bounded canon and treats pseudorandom selections as
                     divine messages through personal interpretation; Soma grows topology under
                     current and requires declared provenance and causal foils for scientific warrant
TESTABLE CONSEQUENCE compare how different technical/religious cultures build contingent interfaces,
                     what interpretive routes they make salient, and how communal checks alter them
```

The closest resonance is not a shared equation. It is the making of a **technical world in which
matter, symbol, practice, and relation are no longer separable after construction**. The decisive
difference is equally instructive: TempleOS seeks a perfect, final, singularly interpreted core;
Soma's present law is open, developmental, receiver-indexed, and changed by passage.

This case belongs in Paper 12 as “The Temple, the Oracle, and the Machine: Culture as a Technical
World-Form.” It should be written with respect for Davis's engineering and experience, neither
romanticizing suffering nor using diagnosis as an explanation from nowhere.

## 10. What a conventional theory of everything would still owe

“Theory of everything” has at least four meanings:

1. **Ontological grammar:** one vocabulary for relation, construction, change, and observation.
2. **Material architecture:** one generative engine exhibiting that grammar.
3. **Interdisciplinary unification programme:** explicit bridges among physical, living, cognitive,
   social, and mathematical domains.
4. **Physical TOE:** a quantitatively adequate theory unifying the fundamental interactions and
   recovering established physics.

The laboratory has substantial work in senses 1–3. Sense 4 remains open. A physical TOE paper must
at minimum supply:

- primitive mathematical objects and state/transport spaces;
- dimensions, units, observables, and admissible frames;
- transformation and covariance laws;
- a relation to Lorentzian spacetime and general covariance;
- quantum states, composition, probability, measurement, and the Born rule—or a tested replacement;
- thermodynamic/statistical limits and energy accounting;
- recovery of standard successful theories in declared regimes;
- estimates of constants or an account of why they are contingent;
- at least one independent quantitative prediction;
- a feasible experiment that could falsify the bridge;
- clear separation between the physical theory and an observer's metaphorical rendering.

Until then, the honest phrase is **unification research programme**. That is not a demotion. It is a
precise description of a programme with a live engine, an accumulating empirical record, and a
large formal burden.

## 12. The publication harness around the science

Two thin Codex skills now exist outside canon:

- `.agents/skills/carry-the-swing/` traces consequential judgments through the declared receiving
  frame, held construction, meeting, test, deed, changed carry, and grade.
- `.agents/skills/situate-holonics/` produces source-grounded relation maps, evidence cards,
  terminology audits, and paper outlines without ratifying or rewriting Soma.

Two custom agent roles make the existing delegation explicit:

- `.codex/agents/luna.toml` — read-only research, analysis, and documentation;
- `.codex/agents/terra.toml` — bounded implementation only.

The next useful harness is not a new ontology or autonomous judge. It is a generated, reviewable
publication layer:

1. **Claim/evidence registry:** claim → internal grade → declared body/current/history/frame/grain/
   aperture → exact passage/artifact/hash → licensed sentence.
2. **Literature matrix:** claim → primary source → relation label → non-equivalence → discriminating
   test.
3. **Terminology/type linter:** catches `partial`/Duggan boundary, equality/equivalence, generic
   zeta/Riemann zeta, SLEEP/heredity, chart/world, and “localized P=NP” conflations.
4. **Paper assembler:** builds outlines, receipts, and bibliography from the two registries; never
   writes canon automatically.
5. **Replication packager:** emits publication-safe manifests for existing hashes, archive schemas,
   commands, environments, and normalized reports.

No plugin, MCP server, embedding store, or write-blocking hook is warranted yet. A plugin becomes
useful if this bundle is distributed across projects. An MCP source service becomes useful if a
live Zotero/OpenAlex/Crossref or artifact database must be queried repeatedly. Retrieval must return
exact passages and remain observer-side; it may not silently crown relevance. Hooks should follow a
stable workflow and begin as non-blocking checks, because the active main session is legitimately
changing canon.

## Closing orientation

The body of work does not need every neighbour to have anticipated it. It needs to show exactly
where prior thinkers built a related form, why they built it from their own worlds, what that form
can transport into this receiver, and what remains irreducibly different.

That is the scientific and human promise of holonics at this stage: not that one word has finally
made all disciplines identical, but that relations can cross disciplines **without losing their
source, boundary, history, or remainder**.
