## A residual bound for the whole word

[proved-derived] Let M approximate the normal response P with `||P-M|| <= e`. The map
S(p,c)=(c-p,c,p) has norm sqrt(3), so the companion operator error is at most `2e`.
Let Q_0=I and Q_j be the computed point power after j compositions. With

```text
B >= max_(0<=j<=k) ||Q_j||,
eta >= 2 e B + delta,
||T Q_(j-1) - Q_j|| <= eta,
```

where delta bounds the native matrix-product rounding, the exact residual identity gives

```text
T^k = Q_k + sum_(j=1..k) T^(k-j) (T Q_(j-1) - Q_j),
||T^k|| <= B (1+eta)^k,
||T^k-Q_k|| <= B ((1+eta)^k - 1).
```

[definition] The implementation bounds B by integer ceilings of complete row/column absolute
sums. Complex real/imaginary product remainders give `delta <= 2d/S`, where d is the complex
state dimension and S is the declared dyadic scale. Positive scalar powers are rounded outward
with exact integer arithmetic. These are numerical bounds, not an intelligence or stopping rule.

[proved-derived] If the exact initial state lies within radius r_0 of the stored nominal z_0,
and E bounds `||T^k-Q_k||`, the generated joint-current radius may be bounded by

```text
E (||z_0|| + r_0) + B r_0 + actual evaluation-rounding bound.
```

[definition] The native evaluator applies Q_k to the original seed. It does not recursively
replace the originating joint family by independent current centres. This avoids the repeated
wrapping error of using successive marginal balls as if they were the generating state.
The current emitted face and the complete joint `(previous,current)` enclosure remain available.
An arithmetic refusal preserves the prior power cache, word and actual current occurrences.
