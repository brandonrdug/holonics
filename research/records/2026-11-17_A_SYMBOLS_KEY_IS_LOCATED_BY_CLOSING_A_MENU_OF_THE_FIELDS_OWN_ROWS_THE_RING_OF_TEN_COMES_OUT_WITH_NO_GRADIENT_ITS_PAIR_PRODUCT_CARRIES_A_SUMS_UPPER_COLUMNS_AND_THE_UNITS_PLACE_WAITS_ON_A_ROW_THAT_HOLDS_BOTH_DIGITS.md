## 5. Why the tension shows no ring and the closure returns one

over the partner's ten values that ask is the same for every source digit: averaged on its partner, a symbol's tension carries no
order. The order lives in the pair. Descent reaches it only through a product of two parts that both start without it (the lock

act by phases. Under that form little evidence fixes the key (the unicity of a rotor's setting once the machine's form is known):
a menu whose digit pairs follow the law at twice chance returns the wheel. That is the sense of learning as locating keys, read on
the engine: the key that descent has not reached in eighty-six laps is closed from one lap's rows in seconds, by the host.

This does not say descent is the lesser law. The rows the closure reads were formed by descent, and the size of a sum is being
learned by it (§3). It says which part of a composition a slope reaches and which part a closure does.
