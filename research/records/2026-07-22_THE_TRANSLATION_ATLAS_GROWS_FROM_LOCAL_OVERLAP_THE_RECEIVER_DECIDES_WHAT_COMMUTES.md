## The relation

A translation atlas is not one universal coordinate system and not a table claiming that two
representations mean the same thing everywhere. It is a population of partial maps cultivated
where actual local charts overlap.

For source site `s`, context chart `c`, and receiver `r`, let

```text
Phi_r(s,c)
```

be the exact face returned at that receiver. Given two training charts `c_0,c_1`, the local map is
defined only where their returned faces agree:

```text
tau_r(s) = Phi_r(s,c_0)  when Phi_r(s,c_0)=Phi_r(s,c_1),
tau_r(s) = undefined     otherwise.
```

Undefined does not mean false or useless. It says that this overlap has not supplied a transport
law at receiver `r`. Another chart, receiver, event, or later founding may still do so.

When a genuinely later chart `c_2` crosses the same declared site:

```text

tau_r(s) != Phi_r(s,c_2) -> OPEN comparison residual at receiver r,
tau_r(s) undefined       -> no map is invented.
```

The receiver index matters. The same site can commute at one consequence boundary and remain OPEN
at another. Context does not select a static stored edge; its presence supplies a new chart of the
whole local relation.
