## What the categorical explanation adds

equivalence obligations. Identity wiring and shared state must also be mapped; equal tensor
shapes or output dimensions do not supply the equalities.

[definition] Source realization and reduction use distinct directions. For admitted source S,


Observations must factor through the same maps. A bijective complete-state correspondence can
give equivalence; a noninjective correspondence retains a fibre and generally gives a weaker
comparison. A map between architecture families also needs the parameter correspondence, not
only one matching checkpoint. Computation cost does not follow from mathematical equality.

These are displayed algebraic derivations, not new kernel-checked theorems or native experiments.
