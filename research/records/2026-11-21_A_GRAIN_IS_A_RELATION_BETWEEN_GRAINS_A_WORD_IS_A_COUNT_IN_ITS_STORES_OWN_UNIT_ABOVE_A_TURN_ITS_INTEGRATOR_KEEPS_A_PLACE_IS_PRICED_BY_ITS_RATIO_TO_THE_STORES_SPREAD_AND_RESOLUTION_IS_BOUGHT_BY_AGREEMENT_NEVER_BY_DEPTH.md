## 1. The lens

- A tolerance written as a place under a point says only that the machinery still moves that place. Set against magnitudes many orders
  away it says nothing, and it respects neither coarse graining nor the relativity of grains.
- Orders of magnitude are classed by units, and one translates between units. A magnitude far below belongs to a distinct partition.
  It is not a run of leading zeros in one long word. A series' terms distribute along a word's places.
- The axis runs both ways, toward the large as toward the small, and the sign of an exponent has the standing the word "imaginary" has
  for an axis.
- The landmarks of magnitude are number theory's: units told apart as coprime, like classes of spectra; colour seen where units are
  compared and glued; gaps (Galois, de Bruijn, Dirichlet's L-functions); the regularity of the digits of transcendentals.
- No physical law fixes how much goes on at a grain. Clocks are relative, what looks small runs fast and what looks large runs slow, a
  horizon hides a count, and the physics is self-similar across grains that are joined.
- Nothing calls for refinement toward infinity. Every context has a threshold, and biology meets it.
- Precision lives in self-induction with its own error correction, a tension between configurations that cannot hold together. It
  lives at most in the diffusion among limbs, distinct locales whose signals meet by pole and phase, and a body tests the water by
  small motions.
- Reach across grains is an instrument that resonates with both ends, a pantograph. A small thing moves a large one by modulating its
  interior, and communication is that.
- A substrate's constants are lawful where the substrate forces them, and the substrate is not what forces the logical failure.
- It is to hold over the whole codebase and to join the physics: fluids, winding, singularities as regularities, relativity,
  gyrovector spaces and the gyroparallelogram.

## 4. The law

**A grain is a relation between two things the machine holds. It is never a place under a point.** Four clauses, each with its owner
and what it asks of the number layer.

1. **A word's places are counted from a unit, and the unit is a store's state.** The unit of a bottom is the store's own standing
   spread at its reader's clock. The unit of a state is its lane's power. A turn's places are counted from its reader's count. A unit is read
   from the machine, carried in the learner's state, and moved at a named crossing of the machine's own clock. No command hands one in.
   Reason: the point is a gauge. A face is unchanged by a common offset of its exponents and a lane's ratio by a scale of the lane
   (Lean `face_shift`, `lane_ratio_scale`; the scale read of November 11), so a place counted from the point names nothing a reader
   reads. A unit set by hand is a temperature set by hand, which the method's temperature rule bars for the same reason.
2. **A reader takes the count, and the integrator keeps the turn.** Under a change of scale a mode is a count or a turn
   (`hodge.count-or-turn-two-halves`). A word's places are a tower of rings joined by carries, each turning at its own rate under the
   flux that reaches it. A reader's clock picks the ring whose period it matches: the rings above are its count, and the rings below
   turn many times between its reads and reach it only as their carries. For a reader of a lap's mean the ring it matches is the
   store's spread over a lap. So the forward and every reading take a count that ends at the spread, and the deposit keeps the rest
   and pays it out in carries, whose times hold the flux (`receiver.carry-times-hold-the-flux`; Lean `release_conserves`,
   `release_tracks`). The rest is the turn, and nothing but its own integrator reads it.
3. **Units are translated at contacts, and the translation conserves.** A product's unit is the product of its operands' units, so
   places add and two short counts multiply. A sum's terms meet at one unit, and a term whose unit lies far under the sum's belongs to
   the lower partition, summed in its own register and joined through its carry. A difference of two near counts is formed at their
   joint unit before any release, because a contact carries a joint fibre and marginal grains can destroy a cancellation
   (`precision.contact-joint-image`). The gaps are where the information is, and a gap's unit is finer than either operand's.
4. **Resolution past a count's grain is bought by agreement, in time or among parts.** In time: a store that jitters by at least half
   a grain turns its lattice into an unbiased reader, and the mean of `K` reads resolves the grain over the root of `12K` (§6, the
   frozen error). Among parts: coarse channels whose units are coprime resolve their product, and a third channel sees a disagreement
   with no reference outside the three (Lean `two_channels_pin`, `third_channel_detects`; `attribution.syndrome-localization`).
   Neither needs a deep word. Depth is the archive of a clock that nothing reads.

The first-order form of clause 1 is one line: `rent(u) = R·(u/σ)²/12`. At a fixed count of places kept over all entries, the total
rent is least where every entry pays the same rent, so a grain follows each entry's own unit and one grain for all is the least cost
only where every entry is as stiff as every other (Lean `total_rent_ge`, `equal_rent_attains`). That is reverse water-filling
(`floor.water-filling-power`) read on a store.

## 6. The lenses, each as its object and operation

Each line gives the object and operation, the owner, and what follows for the machine as a test. Where no test follows today the line
says so.

- **Units, and a distinct partition.** A word is a unit times a count: the unit a region's scale, paid once for the region, the count
  short. Test: G-R1 and G-R3 (§8). Not taken over: a float's format. A float is one realization of this, a word that carries its
  locale in its exponent, and it fails as storage only by a dropped remainder (`release.float-carried-remainder`).
- **The sign of the exponent.** A power of two is as small at the real place as it is large at the place of two (Lean
  `place_two_cuts`). A word cut at the real place keeps its count and drops its low places. Cut at two it keeps its low places, a
  phase on a ring, and drops its top. The sign says which cut is meant and privileges neither. A release of a top is a quotient and a
  release of a bottom is a section (`radix.release-quotient-or-section`), and the read part and the rest are the two cuts of one word.
- **What looks small runs fast.** A place of unit `u` under a flux `f` turns once in `u/|f|` ticks, and under a walk of step `s` once
  in `(u/s)²`. A reader reads down to the place whose period is its own clock. Test: the spread at a reader's clock is that place,
  which G-R1 reads.
- **The lower layer keeps the upper from hardening.** A count read on a lattice `u` from a store that jitters by `σ` has a frozen
  error, the part no mean removes, of `(u/π) Σ_k ((−1)^k/k) e^(−2π²k²σ²/u²) sin(2πkm/u)`. Its greatest value over the cell is 0.25,
  0.093, 0.0023, 4.8·10^(−6) and 8.5·10^(−10) of a grain at `σ/u` = ⅛, ¼, ½, ¾ and 1 (ruler D, a drawn check beside the closed
  form). A store that jitters by half its lattice's unit or more is read without bias, and a store held still under a coarse lattice
  is frozen to it. With its rest carried, a flux of a thousandth of a grain a tick crosses ten times in ten thousand ticks where a
  plain release crosses never (ruler E). Test: G-R5 (c) and (d).
- **Limbs and a tension that cannot hold.** Distinct locales read one quantity through different units. Where the units are coprime
  two readings pin a value in their product and a third makes an inconsistency visible. On the objects this is loop closure over a
  menu of contacts, and the guide's keys paragraph names it as what learning is. The engine holds it for phase (its coprime rings) and
  for time (E4's carries). For amplitude and for learning it closes one loop: the face against the written symbol trains every part
  (the record of November 17). Which internal loops the engine could close with the parts it has is the lead's question under #1086
  and #1087, and this record fixes no guess on a part it has not read at its owner.
- **The pantograph.** A translation between units is a scaling about an anchor, and scalings compose by multiplying
  (`swing.pantograph`). At a contact the unit of a product is the product of units. Test: none new. It is clause 3's form.
- **Gyration.** Units on one axis compose as collinear boosts: flat, with every route between two units agreeing
  (`lorentz.collinear-flat`). Where a change of unit alternates with a turn, a chain of levels is a word of boosts and turns
  (`wave.junction-boost-delay-turn`), and two routes between one pair of levels differ by a turn, the gyration
  (`frame.gyroparallelogram`, `ym.gyration-curvature`). Test, named and not read at its owner: the defect of the scale square
  (`tower.scale-square`) between the engine's level and its sites. A zero defect is a flat pocket, and a turn is the colour a
  comparison of units shows. Not taken over: velocity space and a limiting speed.
- **De Bruijn.** Heat flow raises a law's entropy at half its Fisher information (`thermal.partition-de-bruijn`). For a store of
  spread `σ` a coarsening that adds a variance `t` adds `t/(2σ²)` nats to its own law, the same ratio as §3's price. The flowed zeros
  (`rh.rodgers-tao-nonneg`) are the same flow on another terrain. Test: none new.
- **Dirichlet, Galois and colour.** A channel of period `q` reads a count through its characters, and a prime's colour on it is its
  Frobenius (`prime.frobenius-colour-chebotarev`, `radix.rotor-galois-orbit`, `colour.colourless-faces`). The faces a move leaves
  uncoloured are the cheap ones. Test: none for the number layer today. It is the chart in which clause 4's channels are chosen.
- **Normality.** Two radixes read independently when neither is a power of a common base, and what both keep has no structure left
  (`radix.normal-readers-independent`). Units in such a ratio never share a place. Test: none today.
- **Singularities as regularities.** Near a place where scales do not separate a quantity is its exponent times a regular series, the
  exponent a count and a turn, and a logarithm appears where two exponents differ by a whole number
  (`lock.riemann-scheme-rigidity`). That is a unit times a count with its carry. Marked `interpretation`.
- **A horizon.** The places under a store's spread lie beyond its reader's horizon. The reader holds them as one number of heat, the
  rent `R`, and the count behind is the integrator's (`gr.horizon-stretch-becomes-quotient`).
