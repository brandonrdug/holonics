## 2. The loop in the objects

- **Receiver.** The receiving ring's bank: four parametrons at the pump's quarter-turn phases
  (Stand, Quarter, Half, ThreeQuarters), one unpumped material, each pumped over one turn by the
  storage crossing the section. Member `m` reads the storage modulated by `i^(mt)`, its line shifted
  by `m` quarter turns. Each member is a receiver in its own frame and clock; the bank is their
  Holarchy, joined without exchange (its joint monodromy is the members' block sum,
  `ReceivingBank::read_turn`).
- **Ratio.** Per candidate, two storages on equal material: the pair storage `z_pair` (the candidate
  and the crossings the closed contact joins to it) and the column alone `z_alone`. Their comparison
  is the ratio of the two motions they pump, `R = Ĝ_(pair←alone)`, read by the joined bank.
- **Swing.** Past the bifurcation a member's in-phase axis boosts; its growth `g_m` is its Floquet
  multiplier's modulus over the turn, `λ_m = log g_m` its exponent (a rate per turn). A member whose
  two storages pump alike does no relative work: it is neutral.
- **Pair contact.** The located key `(δ, f)`, deposited as `(E − B) T = P^δ B`; on member `m`'s line
  the contact turns by `e^(iω_m δ)`, `±1` at `δ = 2` on the quarter-turn members.

## 3. The derivation

**3.1 The bank's full form is a direct sum** [proved-derived; owners `hnn::ring::ReceivingBank::read_turn`
("the bank's joint monodromy is the members' block sum"), `Geometry/Motion.finite_work_form_rechart`].
The bank's joint state is `x = ⊕_m x_m`, its executed turn `T = ⊕_m M_m(z)`, and every metric the
bank certifies is a member's, `G = ⊕_m G_m`. So the full work form over the joint state is

```text
F_r(z) = Tᵀ G₁ T − r G₀ = ⊕_m ( M_m(z)ᵀ G_(1,m) M_m(z) − r G_(0,m) )
```

with no cross term between members. The law's instruction to keep the full form for coupled motion
is met by reading the members one by one: nothing is dropped. **The hypothesis' premise fails**: the
bank's members are not coupled, and the least member is not wrong because it drops cross terms (there
are none).

**3.2 A ratio of joint quadratic forms reads a weight no owner supplies** [proved-derived]. Each
member's chart is its own: `finite_work_form_rechart` transports the form by any initial chart `V₀`,
in particular `V₀ = ⊕_m V_m` with independent blocks, and the member's metric is attained by the Stein
solve and certified by inertia alone (`Floquet::certify` trusts nothing of how `G` was attained), so
`c_m G_m` certifies exactly when `G_m` does, for every `c_m > 0`. The joint squared gain of the
pair's motion against the column's along a joint direction `v = ⊕_m v_m`,

```text
r_v = Σ_m c_m v_mᵀ M_m(z_pair)ᵀ G_m M_m(z_pair) v_m  /  Σ_m c_m v_mᵀ M_m(z_alone)ᵀ G_m M_m(z_alone) v_m
```

moves with the ratios `c_m/c_n` and with how `v` divides between the members: it reads a weight
between members. The readings of the direct sum free of every such weight are functions of each
member's own invariants (its multipliers, so its growth `g_m`). Over repeated turns from a generic
seed the joint quadratic form is dominated by the largest member, so its weight-free limit is the
ratio of the joint growths, `max_m g_m(z_pair) / max_m g_m(z_alone)`; with unit seed energy in each
member's dominant mode it is `Σ_m g_m(z_pair)² / Σ_m g_m(z_alone)²` (the "energy" reading of §5.1).
Both are measured in §5.1: they release the antecedent's copy on order-2.

**3.3 The joined bank's reading is the log determinant** [proved-derived; owners: the loss law
(`ℓ = log R`, its scalar limits including `log det R`; `docs/ELEMENTARY_OBJECTS.md` §9), the block sum
of 3.1]. The ratio of the two motions read by the joined bank is the direct sum `R = ⊕_m R_m`, `R_m`
member `m`'s relative transport from the column's motion to the pair's. Its additive chart on a
direct sum is additive: `log det(⊕_m R_m) = Σ_m log det R_m`. On member `m`'s dominant line `R_m`
reads the ratio of the two growths (a ratio of multipliers' moduli, a similarity invariant: chart-
and scale-free). So

```text
log Γ(j, x) = Σ_m ( λ_m(z_pair) − λ_m(z_alone) ),      Γ(j, x) = ∏_m g_m(z_pair) / g_m(z_alone)
enclosed exactly: [ ∏_m L_pair,m / U_alone,m ,  ∏_m U_pair,m / L_alone,m ]
```

the shift of the bank's total Floquet exponent per turn (the volume growth of its members' dominant
modes). Its properties, each the law's: **neutral means equality** (`Γ = 1` when every member is
neutral, and a neutral member leaves `Γ` unchanged); **joining multiplies** (the gain of two joined
banks is the product of theirs, the additivity of the loss over independent receivers); **no weight**
(each member enters once in its own chart). The least member fails the first: a neutral member caps
it at one, and joining one changes it. The largest member fails it the other way.

**3.4 Why the least member released no alternation section, and why the determinant does**
[proved-derived in the members' first-order chart, the face of lane C's §8.2; the executed law's own
is owed in #62]. In member `m`'s first-order chart (line `ω_m`, `u_m = e^(iω_m δ)`), write `â_m` for
the candidate's own image and `p̂_m` for its partner's (the antecedent's placed image through the
contact), so `ẑ_pair = â_m + p̂_m`, `ẑ_alone = â_m`.

- **The alternation (`f = id`).** The deposit makes every column `E e_x = (1 + P^δ) B e_x`. On a
  member with `u_m = −1` every candidate's `â_m` and `p̂_m` vanish: the member pumps both storages at
  the bare material's growth, **neutral for every class**. The least member is then at most that
  member's ratio, decided by what remains past first order, which is how class 3 (column alone
  `[3/16, 4/16)` on Stand) beat the fit. In `Γ` that member is neutral, and the decision is made on
  the members with `u_m = +1`, where the fit's partner is its own image, `p̂_m = â_m`.
- **The first-order face of the gain.** Where a member's exponent is linear in its pump depth,
  `λ_m(z) ≈ λ⁰_m + κ_m |ẑ_m|`, the member's term is `κ_m (|â_m + p̂_m| − |â_m|) ≤ κ_m |p̂_m|` (the
  triangle inequality), with equality exactly when `â_m` lies along `p̂_m`. The partner is the same for
  every candidate at a station, so each member's term is bounded by the partner's own depth and
  attained by the class aligned with it. **On the alternation the fit attains the bound on every
  member** (aligned where `u_m = +1`, both zero where `u_m = −1`), so it maximizes `Σ_m`.
- **Order-2's copy.** The copy's partner is `p̂_m = u_m â_m`: aligned where `u_m = +1` (the bound) and
  opposite where `u_m = −1`, where its term is `−κ_m |p̂_m|`, the largest loss. The copy pays on the
  half-turned members what it gains on the others; the fit, whose column carries `P^δ B e_a` beside
  its own prior, gains on every member. On one member the copy attains the bound: a reading of the
  largest member alone (3.2) can release it, and §5.1 measures that it does.
