## Statement

[definition] `orbitDirection A ξ = D_A ξ`, the direction of the infinitesimal gauge
transformation generated by the section `ξ`.

[proved-derived] `covariantVariation_orbitDirection`: for a differentiable connection and a `C²`
generator, the covariant variation of the curvature along `D_A ξ` is `[F_ij, ξ]`, by the Ricci
identity. `pairing_bracket_self`: an invariant pairing annihilates `B([F, ξ], F)`, since
`[F, F] = 0`.

[proved-derived] `firstVariation_orbitDirection`: the first variation of the Yang--Mills energy
along every gauge-orbit direction is zero.

## Holonic reading

directions are the covariant derivatives of generators. The energy does not see rebasings, so its
gradient has no component along the orbit: the Yang--Mills direction is orthogonal to the orbit,
and a critical point is a condition on the orbit space. This is the infinitesimal face of the
finite invariance returned by the previous owners, and it names the directions that the descent
must quotient out.
