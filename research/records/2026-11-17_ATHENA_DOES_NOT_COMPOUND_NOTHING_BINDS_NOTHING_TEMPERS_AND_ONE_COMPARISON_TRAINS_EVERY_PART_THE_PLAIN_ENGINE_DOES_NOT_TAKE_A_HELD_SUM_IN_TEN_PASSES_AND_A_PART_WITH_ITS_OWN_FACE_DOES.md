# Athena does not compound: nothing binds, nothing tempers, and one comparison trains every part; the plain engine does not take a held sum in ten passes, and a part with its own face does

November 17, the lead. Refs #404, #405, #73.

Brandon read the first founding attempt's writes at 17:51 UTC and named what they show: messy, no gain over what was already attained, and
the same failure it would have with code, an interpreter's replies or a dictionary. His reading, in my words: Athena does learn structure,
but nothing compounds. Every era's writes hold scrambled almost-words; it never converges on prominent structure; the gradient gives it no
motive or autonomy; its parts do not distribute and organize what they hold so that partials resonate and fragments fuse; it is hot soup
sloshed around and never given the chance to temper and cool. He asked for a step back with the whole arsenal, past his own concerns.

This record is that step back. It holds one controlled run, what the repository already knew, what is at fault, my own part in it, and the
decisions. Receipts: [`receipts/2026-11-17_founding/`](receipts/2026-11-17_founding/).

## 4. What is at fault

1. **The machine that trains is a small masked-reconstruction stack, and its only change is a step on one comparison.** Four heads over
   four ticks on 128 coordinates, 402,805 entries, an Adam-like step (`m / (√v + 1)` at a constant rate) from the covector of the face at
   the stack's end, with a second comparison at its opening. It is the design October 25 measured, grown. It learns by compaction, and it is
   too small to compact seven registers.
2. **Nothing binds.** A row is a soft mixture that never locks. The row a sum's place needs holds 0.29 to 0.39 of its weight on the two
   digits it should hold. A face formed from such rows is a blend of several continuations, and a release that draws several places from
   one read draws them apart. The almost-words are blends. No unit is ever committed as a symbol a higher level reads, so nothing is
   coarse-grained and nothing builds on what was learned.
3. **Nothing tempers.** The deposit has no leak: an entry moved stays moved, nothing is pruned, and a coherent structure has no advantage
   over accumulated noise. The rule that no store is at rest (November 11) is not kept by the constitution's own deposit. The rates never
   fall, so the state stands at one temperature; the records already read lap ends as draws from a standing spread. Retention as a
   quotient is asked of nothing.
4. **Parts have no receipt of their own.** The objects give every receiver its own reading and no global gradient, and #405 names
   learning per contact. In the engine a lock or a product is trained only through everything that follows it, so a law that lives in a
   product of two operands that are both still noise has a zero covector (the saddle of November 16). §2 is this, measured.
5. **A symbol is a free row.** The October transport (a symbol as a located turn on rings) is not in the field; no carry circle holds a
   symbol's phase (the read of this morning).

## 6. Decisions

1. No further lap, and no further part added to this state.
2. **A tempering study on small worlds**, many passes over little: short sums, and a small plain text beside them, so that nothing in it is
   arithmetic's alone. Step one: the engine as it is with a leak and a cooling that are states of the deposit (the deposit stands on the
   host, so the card's kernels are as they are). Step two: every part comparing locally, its own face and receipt, of which the closed
   face is one instance. Each step's guess is fixed first, and its acceptance is held writes read by eye.
3. **Writes are read first** (the guide's method rule of this date): a fixed panel of held prompts is written at every step and shown
   whole, with counts read off the text; no step is called progress on a code.
4. **The engine's guide carries a standing table** of the theory's laws against what the engine has in their place, so that no context
   window designs without it.
5. The machine that follows is decided from what the study writes, not before it.

## 7. Read after the decisions: the law the study builds is October's, and rote is what stepping gives

Read the same evening, after §6 was written. Two things sharpen it.

**The rote read** ([`receipts/2026-11-17_tempering/`](receipts/2026-11-17_tempering/), G-T0). The plain engine took 500 passes over 200
short sums on the card (52 s). On the sums it trained on, the units digit codes 1.56 nats and 11 of 96 prompts are written whole. On held
sums the units digit rose to 2.85 nats, above ten symbols flat, and 4 of 96 are written whole. So a rote regime exists, it is slow, and it
carries nothing to a sum the engine has not met: stepping on the comparison binds a record to its answer and locates no law.

**The learning law is already written, with its acceptance.** §6's "leak and cooling" is the October law said worse. The records of
October 24 and 25 state it on the objects:

- At every block keep the normal law's sufficient statistics over a lap at a held constitution, `H = Σ f̄ f̄ᵀ`, `B = Σ t f̄ᵀ`, `T = Σ t tᵀ`
  (integer sums, the same bits in any order). `B` is the gradient. The tension `S = T − B H⁺ Bᵀ` is what the block cannot produce from
  its present face, and "the step never sees it": the remainder is orthogonal to every face, so a step built from `B` carries none of it
  and it is discarded every section. More passage then only adds discarded remainder.
- A lap founds the face that carries the tension, from a pool of products of the present faces, where its drop `M Q⁺ Mᵀ` pays a
  description-length charge (`½ log₂ N + log₂ |pool|`); each lap's foundings are the next lap's operands, so the reach doubles a lap.
  That charge is the pruning, and it is where a unit is committed.
- The bridge is then refounded exactly, with no section steps, and a lap that founds nothing deposits nothing. That is the cooling: the
  October tower measured a founded ring's held units at 308 of 495, at 247 after one stepped lap of the readouts and at 55 after a full
  stepped pass. A stepped pass erodes what a founding placed.
- A conjunction with no lower part (a parity; the lock and the product together) stays dormant for a pool of pair products and founds only
  when the pool offers it whole, so the pool's navigators matter. The closure of a menu is one: it offered the keyed pair through the row.
- Its acceptance was fixed before its build (the staircase record, §7): held code against a foil lap, every founding predicted before it is
  taken and retested on later laps, the laps stopping on their own, and the writing at each prompt shown whole.

The November field replaced this learner with a stepped one when the line moved to the card; nothing in the law needs the host, since its
statistics are sums of outer products the card already forms for the covector, and the solve at a lap's crossing is a slow variable.

So decision 2 of §6 is restated. The study builds the lap law on the present engine's forward, on small worlds: the engine's charts,
currents and products are the faces; each block's material moves by its bridge's solve and by foundings under the charge, and by no
section step; the closure is a navigator of the pool. Its acceptance is the staircase record's §7, with the writes read first.
