## I · A complete world is not complete contemporaneous light

The corpus, tools, materials, and prior artifacts may all exist without participating in the
current relation. Relativity requires a declared body ⊕ frame; it does not require every available
body to be co-present. A memory beyond the present horizon has not been deleted. A later cue or

The active scale law is therefore:


The world holds availability. The current relation determines participation.

## II · A limb is a returning worldline

An output artifact is not a limb merely because it was emitted. It becomes a limb when it changes
the boundary through which later current passes. A drawn mark may only be terrain. A constructed
pointer, vine, key, compiler input, renderer, or sensor becomes an organ when world consequence
returns through it and changes what the first person can perceive or do.

The boundary is therefore mobile. Something initially called external material may become part of
the effective organ; something damaged or detached may cease to carry the circuit. No ownership
bit changes. The relation changes.

This is also the exact non-extractive reading of the quoted germline: whole body ⊕ shared world
persist, and a later current draws what its own frame reaches. No smaller inherited object, replay,
or universal memory surface is produced.

## III · Growth and damage are local curvature
