- `word_presence_W99_GA2.txt`, `word_presence_W99_GH.txt`: `tools/word_presence.py` on W99's mean (19,351 word starts), with `more`

  `level_links_global_spread.txt` the first read, before the byte controls, with one spread for the whole link.

- `level_tension_bound.txt`: `tools/level_tension_bound.py` on the covector at the level's locus, W101's mean, with its planted law.
- `frontier_splices.txt`: the held registers of W101, the two splices (`splice-level`) and W101r, one place open and at the frontier.
- `word_presence_W101r_GH5.txt`: the word before at the current word's letters 1 to 6 on W101r's mean (the formal thread's G-H8).
- `uncompared_parity.txt`: the card's parity of the place read and not compared, on the field and with the opening compared.
- `GUESS_A3.txt`: the guess of the answered act's arm (G-A3 adopted from the formal thread, G-A4 the lead's).
