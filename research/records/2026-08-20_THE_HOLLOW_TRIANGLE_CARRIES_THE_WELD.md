## The prediction was wrong, and wrong in a useful direction

The previous iteration predicted the doubling chain would be the first test of the descent
criterion and that **the answer would be a negative**. It is not a negative. **It is vacuous.**

The doubling chain is *exact* — `theDoublingChainIsExact` was proved two iterations ago — so its
homology is trivial and there is nothing for a form to descend onto. A form descends to the zero
group for free. The doubling chain cannot test the criterion at all, and I proposed it as the test
without noticing that I had already proved it exact.

> **A test whose outcome is fixed before the material is consulted tests nothing.** That is the
> project's own tautology rule turned on a prediction rather than a receipt, and this is the second
> time in three iterations that the useful move was repairing my own setup rather than answering
> the question as posed.

## What the question actually needed, and the smallest object that supplies it

A chain with **nonzero** homology. The smallest real one is the **hollow triangle** — three
vertices, three edges, no filled face:

```lean
def boundary : Cells →+ Cells := ⟨fun e => (-e.1 + e.2.2, e.1 - e.2.1, e.2.1 - e.2.2), …⟩

theorem theLoopIsTheKernel (e : Cells) : e ∈ boundary.ker ↔ ∃ n : ℤ, e = (n, n, n)
theorem theHollowTriangleDoesNotGlue : ¬ hollowTriangleChain.toPassage.Glues
theorem theHollowTriangleHomologyIsNontrivial :
    ¬ Subsingleton hollowTriangleChain.toPassage.ObstructionGroup
```

Nothing fills the face, so the realized population is trivial while the retained one contains the
loop. **The obstruction group is a copy of the integers, generated by running each edge once.**

## And the weld holds on it

```lean
theorem theRealizedLiesInTheRadical : ∀ r ∈ …realized, ∀ w ∈ …remainder, form r w = 0
theorem theFormDescendsOntoTheHomology : …
theorem theLoopClassPaysThree : hollowTriangleDatum.form (1,1,1) (1,1,1) = 3
theorem theLoopRadicalIsTrivial : ∀ v ∈ hollowTriangleDatum.radical, v = 0
```

> **A chain with a nonzero homology, a form that descends onto it by the criterion, and a strictly
> positive self-pairing there.** The loop class pairs with itself to three — one for each edge it
> crosses.

That is the first object on which `TransportChain`, `CompressedPositivity` and `PayingPairing` all
hold at once, and it is the witness the whole `Millennium/` development had been missing. Every
structure before this was a shape with at most a degenerate instance.

**And the object is the triangle**, which the atlas already carries as the quantum of the
antisymmetric form. The two readings — its oriented span, and its unfilled loop — are recorded as a
correspondence of subject rather than of theorem.

## What this does not do

**One object.** The criterion is general; the instance is not. The weld holds here because the
realized population is *trivial*, which is the easiest possible way to lie in the radical. **A
chain whose realized population is nonzero and still in the radical is not exhibited**, and that is
the case that would show the criterion has content beyond the degenerate one.
