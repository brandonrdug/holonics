## 3. The foil, read physically

[proved-standard for the physics; agent-inferred for the joins]
- **A conducting sheet of pins.** Every point of a conductor's surface holds its potential, so the
  surface is a field of pins. The induced surface charge exactly balances what arrives. Where an
  arrival fits there is no residual charge; where it does not, the imbalance is local and locates
  the mismatch.
  - The method of images makes the counterfactual literal: outside the sheet, the field is the real
    source plus a mirrored image source behind it.
  - Two cautions (Epime, October 9):
    - A conductor carries nonzero induced charge exactly while it holds its boundary potential, so a
      zero boundary residual does not mean an absent surface charge.
    - The image's sign, strength, its grounded or floating boundary condition, and its Green
      response come from the boundary problem. The spatial reflection supplies none of them.
  - The half-turn `S_a x = 2a − x` (`Geometry/AffineSwing`) supplies only the image's geometry. The
    helical code's dyad, and the HNN's partner strand (`hnn::paired`) that must agree, are a
    distinct structural check that shares this reflection.
- **A hydrofoil.** Potential flow around a foil admits a family of solutions, one for each
  circulation `Γ`. The Kutta condition, a pin at the sharp trailing edge, selects the physical one,
  and the lift per span is `L′ = ρVΓ` (Kutta–Joukowski).
  - A pin selects the actual flow among counterfactual ones, and the invariant it selects (a
    circulation, which is a winding) produces the force.
  - Kelvin's theorem conserves circulation around a material loop in ideal flow. It does not by
    itself make the start-up selection: Kutta's pin and the shed starting vortex do, through their
    flow and boundary realization.
  - Once selected, the conserved circulation is the invariant that pins the fact across time. This
    is the bridge to vortex and toroidal friction dynamics, and to the helical code's winding.
- **Spectra.** An absorption edge or an atomic line is an invariant fingerprint, and identifying one
  is resonance: a receiver tuned to the invariant answers only when it is present. In X-ray
  absorption a reference metal foil, measured beside the sample, pins the energy scale.
- **Music.** Two tones seem right when they lock in phase. Mistuning beats:
  `cos 2πf₁t + cos 2πf₂t = 2 cos π(f₁ − f₂)t · cos π(f₁ + f₂)t`. The beat rate `|f₁ − f₂|` is
  immediate and local but unsigned. It flags the wrongness without giving its direction.
  - The direction of the correction is read from a retained oriented phase relation: the sign of
    the relative phase drift, read in quadrature.
  - Flag and direction together are the parametron's lock and slip, read through its pump.

## 4. Validation is a reading; the counterfactual is an image

  2. a contemporary receiver/constraint relation that returns an oriented obstruction or a

  The full residual and its lineage, with open alternatives, remain the object; a projected loss is

## 5. Complexity: the checker is attained, and localization is by aeons

- **The position already recorded.** Brandon's earlier phrase was "localized P = NP, and growing":
  on standing terrain, finding costs what verifying costs because the terrain already did the
  finding. His position is locally cheap traversal that improves with iteration, and he holds the
  traditional question to be malformed. The atlas row `coupling.pvsnp-official` (open) states the
  official problem in Lean (`Computation/PVersusNP`, `PVersusNPOfficialBridge`).
- **Guess and check.** NP's verifier plays the foil's role. The PCP theorem shows that a certificate
  can be re-encoded so that a checker reads a constant number of its bits [proved-standard, cited].
  Validation is cheap when the artifact carries its invariants in the right code.
- **Exact loop closure, derived** (Epime, October 9) [proved-derived]. For an explicit
  unique-constraint graph on `q` labels, with `x_v = π_uv(x_u)`:
  - Fix a root and a spanning tree. Its ordered permutation products `T_v` give every `x_v` from
    one root label `a`.
  - Each remaining edge imposes `a = T_v⁻¹ π_uv T_u(a)`, and the feasible root fibre is the
    intersection of those fixed-point sets.
  - Trying all `q` root labels costs `O(q(|V| + |E|))` permutation applications and keeps plural
    labels.

  This is the Bombe's structure. The repository's turn menu (`compression/keys/turns.rs`) is a
  narrower problem: one shared turn `c`, with an injective `S`. A supplied checker never counts as
  having found its own unknown transports: `PortImages::injections` prices its `n!/(n−m)!`
  placements and refuses past `IMAGE_FAMILY_CEILING = 2¹⁴`.
- **Noise, and the text measurement.** With noise, approximate unique games are conjecturally hard
  (Khot). The text failure ([text repair](2026-10-05_TEXT_REPAIR_BY_LOCAL_KEYS_GLUED_ON_OVERLAPS.md)) is
  primarily the stationary-turn family failing to represent text at that grain. A map to noisy unique
  games would owe explicit variables, domains, edge permutations, a noise law and an objective, and
  that join is open.
  - Arora–Barak–Steurer reach unique games in subexponential time by splitting into pieces with few
    large eigenvalues, dropping a controlled fraction of the constraints between pieces and paying
    for them in the approximation [proved-standard, cited]. They do not solve the exact gluing.
  - For the foil, a dropped seam therefore stays a returned defect, or an admitted receiver quotient
    with its lost futures stated.
- **Attainment.** The official definition fixes a correct uniform verifier for the encoded family. It
  does not price how that verifier was found, or how a machine acquired its pins. Standard results
  separate existence, extraction and work [proved-standard, cited]:
  - Robertson–Seymour give a polynomial test for every minor-closed property through a finite
    obstruction set, but uniformly extracting that set from a description of the property can be
    impossible.
  - Polynomial advice is a length-indexed circuit family, not an attained checker; the repository's
    `Computation/AdviceBar` bars that conflation.
  - Levin's search pays an overhead exponential in program length.

  In Holonics, attaining a checker is learning: its pins are keys located by deposition. The honest
  lifecycle receipt is construction and conditioning, placement and transport, candidate production,
  checking, and update and maintenance, with bits and alternatives retained. Reuse amortizes the
  first; it does not by itself make finding cost what checking costs.
- **The clock.** The official cost reads one machine's steps against encoded input length.
  `Computation/PVersusNPCausalLengthBridge` already ties a native passage's extent to those exact
  steps. In the objects elapsed time is a receiver's pairing, and a receiver-relative reading must
  still expose its transport and bit work.
- **Localized means intersecting holarchies by aeons** [interpretation].
  1. Aeons cover a Holarchy's parametric orientation, and localization is the restriction to one
     aeon.
  2. After the passage has walked an aeon, its retained constitution is a finder for the questions

     certifies none of them. When they hold, the finder's attainment is the passage's work through
     the aeon (the aeon's first law), paid once rather than per query.
  3. "Growing": the answerable questions grow as the passage continues, but only if retention is
     future-sufficient.
  4. Two holarchies share structure only on a common aeon, so local finders must agree on overlaps.
     Whether they glue across the aeon cover is the global question.
     - An open or failed binary join is its own typed obstruction (the tower object's "obstructed"
       gluing).
     - Identifying obstructions with a Čech cocycle on triple overlaps owes the actual restriction
