## 2. ARM ONE FIRED — place-closure and conduct-closure are two objects

**The atom mouth saturates its orbit at 100 places and its conduct partition refines to 128 blocks.**
A presentation can therefore close its orbit while its causal states keep splitting.

> **The causal state is finer than the orbit.** The body's response carries what the landing place
> does not, because it depends on the standing thought and not only on where the arrival fell.

The identification of the two closures — which the posed experiment declared as arm one — is
**withdrawn**. What survives is stronger than the identification would have been: *where* an arrival
lands is a weaker reading than *what the body did with it*, and the machine already computes the
finer one.

> **What the swing consults is the composition depth of the ARRIVAL, not the extent of the terrain.**

A thought cuts where the arrival's aim is orthogonal to the standing thought. A node composed from
twenty differences has more directions available to it than one composed from a single difference —
and that is a property of the arrival, not of the map it lands on. The orbit is not even monotone in
the depth: it peaks in the middle of the ladder, because a coarse presentation has fewer arrivals
with which to reach places. **Orbit size and composition depth were conflated and the arm was aimed
at the wrong one.**

The two depth-one entries are the two *mouths* and are reported as one stratum rather than forced
onto the curve. An earlier form of this reading ordered by grain instead of depth and failed for

## 5. Three instrument defects, each caught by an arm that was written to catch it

Recorded because the arms earned their keep and the corrections are the method:

1. **The input alphabet was every distinct span**, so the shortest-separating-word search branched
   over a thousand symbols and the driver did not return. An input is now the successor's **conduct
   class** — the three declared faces as a three-bit word — which is the coarser and more honest
   question: *what kind of thing came next*, not *which thing*.
2. **Grain 1 is degenerate.** `locate` walks the differences *inside* a span, so a one-byte span has
   none and every one lands at the origin: an orbit of one place. The first run reported it SETTLED
   and the anti-vacuity arm passed on it. A rung whose orbit is a single place is now excluded from
   both regimes by name.
3. **Arm three compared raw completions** while its own declaration read *per arrival*, and a coarse
   rung has an order of magnitude fewer arrivals. It now compares the undivided pair by
   cross-multiplication.

## 6. What is open

**Arm two is weakly specified and its "hold" is not evidence.** It tested only that the collapsed
populations differ between regimes, which is nearly certain, and the direction came back opposite to
the prediction — the settled rung collapses 9,036 pairs against the growing rungs' millions. The
prediction that a settled presentation is *one condensation* is neither confirmed nor refuted here
and needs an arm that can fail.

**And the next construction follows from §4 rather than from a new idea:** raise what the live
arrival composes before it lands, and re-take the completion pair. The curve says what to expect and
the falsifier is already stated — a repair that raises composition depth without raising completions
per arrival refutes this record.
