## 2. What RH contributes

**Truth status:** `proved-standard`.

The Riemann hypothesis states that every nontrivial zero of `zeta(s)` has real part `1/2`.
Riemann's explicit formula transports prime-power data and the zero divisor into one test-function
identity. Weil's criterion turns RH into the required sign of the completed explicit-formula
quadratic form for every test in its full admissible family. In the function-field case, the
corresponding zeta numerator is a characteristic determinant of Frobenius on cohomology, and
purity places the characteristic roots at the required half-weight modulus.

Bombieri's official account makes a second connection that is decisive for this construction. In
the curve-over-finite-field case, the explicit formula is a Lefschetz trace formula, and the
required sign follows from the algebraic index theorem for surfaces. Hodge proved the complex
projective form of the index theorem using harmonic-form theory. Thus an intersection form is not
merely a visual analogy beside RH: in the established geometric analogue it is the sign carrier
behind spectral placement.

Primary boundary: E. Bombieri,
[*Problems of the Millennium: the Riemann Hypothesis*](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/riemann.pdf),
especially Sections IV--V. This establishes the ordinary mathematical statements under their
classical hypotheses. It does not supply a classical-number-field Frobenius, cohomology theory,
trace identity, or index theorem proving RH.

## 3. What Hodge contributes—and what it does not

**Truth status:** `proved-standard`.

For a smooth projective complex variety `X`, Hodge theory decomposes complex cohomology into
`H^(p,q)` pieces; with a Kähler metric, each class has a harmonic representative. A rational
Hodge class of codimension `p` is

```text
H^(2p)(X,Q) intersect H^(p,p)(X)
  = H^(2p)(X,Q) intersect F^p H^(2p)(X,C).
```

An algebraic cycle `Z` of codimension `p` gives such a class `cl(Z)`. The Hodge conjecture asks
whether every rational Hodge class is a rational linear combination of these cycle classes.

Three boundaries are mandatory:

- a harmonic class is not thereby a Hodge class; rationality, even degree, and `(p,p)` type are
  additional conditions;
- the integral version is false, and replacing projective algebraic by merely Kähler is also
  false; and
- in a holomorphic family the flat cohomology local system and the moving Hodge filtration are
  different structures. The filtration varies holomorphically and obeys Griffiths
  transversality; this is not ordinary diffusion between unrelated topologies.

Deligne also emphasizes that cohomology loses cycle information: an algebraic cycle has a finer
lift to an intermediate Jacobian, and no general algorithm is known to decide where a typical
integral class in a family becomes type `(p,p)`.

Primary boundary: P. Deligne,
[*The Hodge Conjecture*](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/hodge.pdf).

## 4. The common spine is a geometry--class--form--spectrum passage

**Truth status:** `interpretation`.


## 8. Why smooth projective toric surfaces are the correct first aperture

**Truth status:** `proved-standard`.

A smooth complete two-dimensional fan gives a smooth complete toric surface. Its torus-invariant
divisors have a lattice presentation

```text
M -> Z^(Sigma(1)) -> Cl(X_Sigma) -> 0,
m |-> sum_rho <m,v_rho> D_rho,
```

and for a smooth surface `Cl=Pic`. The Chow ring is obtained from the ray generators by the
Stanley--Reisner noncone relations and the linear character relations. In cyclic ray order, if

```text
v_(i-1) + v_(i+1) = a_i v_i,
```

then `D_i^2=-a_i`; adjacent invariant divisors meet once and nonadjacent ones do not meet. A star
subdivision of a smooth two-cone by the primitive ray `v_i+v_(i+1)` is the toric blow-up of its
fixed point and produces the exceptional divisor. For smooth complete complex toric varieties,
the cycle-class comparison identifies the Chow classes with the corresponding even cohomology;
off-diagonal Hodge numbers vanish.

These laws make the source finite and exact without mounting the quotient, intersection matrix,
lift, signature, or blow-up consequence as answers. They also give actual algebraic cycles rather
than an abstract span label.
