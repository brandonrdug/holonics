# Receipts: host rulers of November 10 (late evening)

Rulers on the host, never machinery. Every script reads the python-codes passages (and, for the
`field` parts, the held locked-visit dump of the 64-contact line at lap four) under
`${HOLONICS_ROOT}.local/`, which stay private; only aggregate codes are written here. None used the
card. They continue the receipts of the chain record of the same day, whose `yard.py` (the loader
and the Witten-Bell tables) they `exec` and whose `train.npz` and `gi_held.npy` they read.

- `guess_capacity_13_18.txt`: guesses G13 to G18, each with the UTC time it was fixed, before its
  read. G18 and the re-ordered G12 are design's reads and were unread when this was committed.
- `capacity_reads_9.txt`: the reads of this record and the scores.
- `cap_f.py PART`: `split` (four and three letters split between the sides), `prod` (the sides as
  separate branches whose messages multiply at the place, by count of the joint context), `field
  <state>` (a held locked visit multiplied into each ruler, weights chosen on one fold of problems
  and read on the other), `stride` (letters at a stride). Logs `cap_f_split.log.txt`, `cap_f_prod.log.txt`,
  `cap_f_field.log.txt`, `cap_f_stride.log.txt`. `cap_f_field.log.txt` ends in a traceback: the second state
  named on its command line (the plain field at lap 26) was dumped on another sample of held
  places (31,555 records), so only the 64-contact state was read.
- `cap_g.py K [seconds]`: the best product of a left and a right contact, `Q(x|L,R) ∝ a(L,x)
  b(x,R)`, both tables fitted on TRAIN by conditional likelihood (full batch, Adam). Logs
  `cap_g_1.log.txt` (cut to its first 60 lines; the fit had settled) and `cap_g_2.log.txt`.
- `cap_h.py rows|field`: the rows on held 0-31, and the field against the fitted pair reader.
  Logs `cap_h_rows.log.txt`, `cap_h_field.log.txt`.
