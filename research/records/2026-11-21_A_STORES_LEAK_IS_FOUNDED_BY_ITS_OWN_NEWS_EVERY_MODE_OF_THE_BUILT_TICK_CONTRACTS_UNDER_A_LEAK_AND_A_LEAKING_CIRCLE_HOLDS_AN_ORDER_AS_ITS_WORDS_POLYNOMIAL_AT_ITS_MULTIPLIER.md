## 2. The two receipts agree, and against the fill every mode already falls

- the part's place on the chart, `ρ/F`, is multiplied by `(F + ν)/(F + 1)` a tick, so it falls as `F^(−(1 − ν))`;
- successive moves of the chart stand in the ratio `(F + ν)/(F + 2)`, so `1/(1 − ratio) = (F + 2)/(2 − ν)` is a line in the tick count

So two instruments agree to the third digit, and the region's slowest mode is the sites' most boosted one: from tick 5 the pass is that

## 3. The law

At a site, on a face lane, over the ticks `k` of a pass, with `g^k` everything the built tick adds to the lane and `r̂` the lane's chart:

```
built      x^(k+1) = x^k + g^k
the law    x^(k+1) = γ x^k + g^k,      γ = 1/(1 + d),   d ≥ 0 a state of the constitution, one a face lane, founded at d = 0
the pair   (M, x) ← (γ M + 1, γ x + g),   released as y = x/M:      y^(k+1) = y^k + (g^k − y^k)/M^(k+1)
readers    as built: r̂ by lane (the lane's scale is the readers' kernel, so a leak's whole effect is a weight)
news       n^k = g^k − y^k, the arrival less the store's release; on the ray it is the chart's move Δr̂^k = r̂(x^(k+1)) − r̂(x^k)
deposit    c = Σ_(t, k≥1) ⟨Δr̂_t^k, Δr̂_t^(k−1)⟩ / Σ_(t, k≥1) ‖Δr̂_t^k‖ ‖Δr̂_t^(k−1)‖   (a lane's, over a section's sites and a pass's ticks)
           d rises where c > 0 and falls where c < 0, never under zero, at the deposit's rate (a state of the learner)
rest       c = 0: successive moves of the ray are orthogonal on the pass's mean
```

Each line with its reason.

- **The form is the engine's one move.** `d = 0` is `γ = 1`, the built tick's every bit. A leak of the pair and a news write of the
  release are one move (Lean `release_step`), so the `P` the engineering record's §3 left to be named is the store's own release `x/M`,
  and its gain is `1/M′`. At `d = 0` the mass is the tick count and the gain `1/(k + a)`: a mean of a constant. At rest what arrives
  equals what leaves at `M = (1 + d)/d` (Lean `rest_mass`), the reach of `store.finite-quality`: the store's quality `Q`.
- **The comparison is the store's own.** A store's release is its prediction of its next arrival, and the news is the covector of that
  comparison at the store's port. With `a` the next news had the store not moved and `b` this news, the next news after a step of gain
  `K` is `a − Kb`, and a change `h` of the gain changes its power by `−2h⟨a − Kb, b⟩ + h²‖b‖²` (Lean `news_gain_step`). So the inner
  product of successive news is the descent covector of the gain, read where the store is, and the next news is least where the two are
  orthogonal (Lean `news_least`). Successive moves that agree say the store lags what arrives and is too heavy; moves that oppose say it
  overshoots and is too light.
- **It needs the forward alone.** The moves are the chart steps the residual probe already forms; the deposit is a sum of inner products
  a lane. No covector of the face, no tape, no second pass.
- **What the strength states** (from training: Kalman 1960, Muth 1960, Mehra 1970; the reading `agent-inferred`). Where an arrival is a
  level plus a scatter of power `r` and the level drifts by `q` a tick, the mass held falls a tick from `M` to `M/(1 + qM)`: the friction
  is the drift times the mass held, and the rest is at `qM² = 1 + qM` (Lean `rest_mass_drift`). The news of a store at a fixed gain `K`
  has lag-one covariance `P − KS`, `S = P + r`, zero at `K = 1 − r/S`, the share of the news that is not scatter (Lean
  `lag_one_zero_iff`, the algebra alone). The built tick is the end `q = 0`: it reads its arrivals as scattered readings of a constant
  and counts each new one for less. So `d` states how far the level a lane tracks moves within a pass against the scatter of what
  arrives, and the deposit reads that from the pass.
- **Founded at zero it opens by itself.** On the built tick the chart's moves from tick 5 are one mode's, so their cosine is near one and
  the first deposit is positive at every lane (guess B1). The face's comparison had no sign to give here; the store's has one.

Not stated as law: the lane as the leak's grain is the readers' own (one scale a lane is what they do not distinguish, `agent-inferred`);
a friction a mode, an arrival's mass read from its contact's partition sum, and the drift `q` as the deposited word in place of `d` are
other realizations of the same store. Whether a site is given a leak, its integers and its parity are the engineering thread's.

  small push toward a hold, and it waits. Two reads now say the face's comparison does not found a tick's clock.

## 5. What it predicts

At its rest fill `1/δ`, `δ = d/(1 + d)`, a leak multiplies a mode by `1 − δ(1 − ν)` a tick (Lean `leak_multiplier`).

   carries the amendment.

## 6. The polygons and the fork

Both reads scored guesses of [the lattice record](2026-11-20_A_LATTICE_AN_APERTURE_AND_A_DILATION_ARE_ONE_LENS_THE_HEXAGON_IS_THREE_CLASSES_WITHOUT_THEIR_COMMON_MODE_AND_THE_CHARACTERISTIC_POLYGON_LIES_ON_OR_ABOVE_THE_GRAINS_IN_LEAN.md)

**The polygons** (that record's §6). The face's reading has its rates' polygon 26 to 45 bits from its grains' at ranks 32 to 192 and
touching only at the ends. A map splits where the two touch (Katz); touching nowhere, it is one carrier, as read. The same gap in the
tick's block is point 5 above: the bits between the polygons are what a pass pays in its first ticks before its rates govern. And a
segment of one octave of grains (28 to 37 places in the reading's middle) is a set of places that hear an arrival within a factor of two,
so it is the set one friction serves; a face lane of 32 that crosses breaks has its quiet places follow the balance of its loud ones
(`agent-inferred`). If widths are refounded on the polygons' segments, the leak's grain goes with them.

**The fork** (that record's §7). The circles' operand at the line's end read the last closer and little of the order. That is the
figure a leaking store draws. From rest a store `Q ← γQ + b` holds its arrivals as their word's polynomial at its multiplier, the
arrival of age `j` at `γ^j` (Lean `store_eq_sum`). For three closers, two orders that share their last differ by `(γ − γ²)(p − q)` and
two that share their first by `(1 − γ)(p − q)` (Lean `same_last_side`, `same_first_side`): the six orders are the corners of a hexagon
whose sides alternate in the ratio `γ`, the short sides between the orders that share their last closer. At `γ` toward zero it is a
triangle by the last closer; at `γ` toward one it closes to a point, since a sum forgets order. The read's unpumped centroids alternate
so (`fork.txt`): 10, 40 and 58 degrees between orders sharing their last closer against 71, 90 and 90 between the others, their parity
alternating around the plane as a hexagon of adjacent swaps does; at classes a third of a turn apart those sides are multipliers of 0.11,
0.53 and 0.94 by closer (`fork_model.py`; the spread says the closers' drives are unlike, which the model does not hold). Pumped, the
pair on the last closer `}` stays one degree apart and the alternation is lost (88 and 58 degrees inside the other two pairs). The hexagon the guess F2 looked for is the same store at a multiplier that is a turn by a third: there a cyclic
change of the order turns the holding by a third (Lean `third_turn_cycle`), the even orders stand on one triangle and the odd on
another, so parity is a size and the cyclic order a phase (`fork_model.txt`: 1.000 at 0, 120, 240 degrees against 0.240 at 111, 231,
351). So a bank of circles holds an order as its word's polynomial read at its multipliers, the crossings' order where a multiplier
turns by the depth's share at a reach of the depth, and the last closer where it only leaks. The read says L71's circles at this
operand are of the second kind. Prefix states are the other place the order stands, as that record says.

One store, then, along two clocks: along the strand the carry's circles, along the ticks the lane. Both stand at a quality near one on
L71, the first by its deposited friction, the second by the growth of its currents.
