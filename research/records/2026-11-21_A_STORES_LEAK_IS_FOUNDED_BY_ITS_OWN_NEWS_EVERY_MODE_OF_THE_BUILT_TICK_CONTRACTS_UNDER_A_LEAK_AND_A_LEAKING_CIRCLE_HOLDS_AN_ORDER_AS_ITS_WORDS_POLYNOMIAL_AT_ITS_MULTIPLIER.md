## 3. The law

At a site, on a face lane, over the ticks `k` of a pass, with `g^k` everything the built tick adds to the lane and `r̂` the lane's chart:

```
built      x^(k+1) = x^k + g^k
the law    x^(k+1) = γ x^k + g^k,      γ = 1/(1 + d),   d ≥ 0 a state of the constitution, one a face lane, founded at d = 0
the pair   (M, x) ← (γ M + 1, γ x + g),   released as y = x/M:      y^(k+1) = y^k + (g^k − y^k)/M^(k+1)
readers    as built: r̂ by lane (the lane's scale is the readers' kernel, so a leak's whole effect is a weight)
news       n^k = g^k − y^k, the arrival less the store's release; on the ray it is the chart's move Δr̂^k = r̂(x^(k+1)) − r̂(x^k)
deposit    c = Σ_(t, k≥1) ⟨Δr̂_t^k, Δr̂_t^(k−1)⟩ / Σ_(t, k≥1) ‖Δr̂_t^k‖ ‖Δr̂_t^(k−1)‖   (a lane's, over a section's sites and a pass's ticks)
           d rises where c > 0 and falls where c < 0, never under zero, at the deposit's rate (a state of the learner)
rest       c = 0: successive moves of the ray are orthogonal on the pass's mean
```

Each line with its reason.

- **The form is the engine's one move.** `d = 0` is `γ = 1`, the built tick's every bit. A leak of the pair and a news write of the
  release are one move (Lean `release_step`), so the `P` the engineering record's §3 left to be named is the store's own release `x/M`,
  and its gain is `1/M′`. At `d = 0` the mass is the tick count and the gain `1/(k + a)`: a mean of a constant. At rest what arrives
  equals what leaves at `M = (1 + d)/d` (Lean `rest_mass`), the reach of `store.finite-quality`: the store's quality `Q`.
- **The comparison is the store's own.** A store's release is its prediction of its next arrival, and the news is the covector of that
  comparison at the store's port. With `a` the next news had the store not moved and `b` this news, the next news after a step of gain
  `K` is `a − Kb`, and a change `h` of the gain changes its power by `−2h⟨a − Kb, b⟩ + h²‖b‖²` (Lean `news_gain_step`). So the inner
  product of successive news is the descent covector of the gain, read where the store is, and the next news is least where the two are
  orthogonal (Lean `news_least`). Successive moves that agree say the store lags what arrives and is too heavy; moves that oppose say it
  overshoots and is too light.
- **It needs the forward alone.** The moves are the chart steps the residual probe already forms; the deposit is a sum of inner products
  a lane. No covector of the face, no tape, no second pass.
- **What the strength states** (from training: Kalman 1960, Muth 1960, Mehra 1970; the reading `agent-inferred`). Where an arrival is a
  level plus a scatter of power `r` and the level drifts by `q` a tick, the mass held falls a tick from `M` to `M/(1 + qM)`: the friction
  is the drift times the mass held, and the rest is at `qM² = 1 + qM` (Lean `rest_mass_drift`). The news of a store at a fixed gain `K`
  has lag-one covariance `P − KS`, `S = P + r`, zero at `K = 1 − r/S`, the share of the news that is not scatter (Lean
  `lag_one_zero_iff`, the algebra alone). The built tick is the end `q = 0`: it reads its arrivals as scattered readings of a constant
  and counts each new one for less. So `d` states how far the level a lane tracks moves within a pass against the scatter of what
  arrives, and the deposit reads that from the pass.
- **Founded at zero it opens by itself.** On the built tick the chart's moves from tick 5 are one mode's, so their cosine is near one and
  the first deposit is positive at every lane (guess B1). The face's comparison had no sign to give here; the store's has one.

Not stated as law: the lane as the leak's grain is the readers' own (one scale a lane is what they do not distinguish, `agent-inferred`);
a friction a mode, an arrival's mass read from its contact's partition sum, and the drift `q` as the deposited word in place of `d` are
