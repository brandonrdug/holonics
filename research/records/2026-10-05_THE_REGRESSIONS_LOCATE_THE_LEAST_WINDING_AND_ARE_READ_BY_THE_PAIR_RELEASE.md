## 3. Loop 2: a class of windings is one key

**The law** [definition; agent-inferred; the owner `hnn::keys::PairSurvivors::located`, its module
section "A class of windings is one key"]. Contacts compose along the ring's clock: `k` contacts at
`δ₀`, each `y ↦ f₀(y)`, joined end to end, are one contact at `kδ₀` with the map `f₀^k`, and its
holonomy is the sum of theirs:

```text
(δ₀, f₀)^k = (k δ₀, f₀^k),        Σ_(k contacts) c₀ = k c₀  (mod d)
located ⇔ the least survivor δ₀ publishes f₀, and every survivor δ is k δ₀ with published map f₀^k on its ports
```

When the survivors are such a family, its generator is the key, and retention keeps it alone: on the
passage each winding emitted exactly what the generator's composition emits, so it adds no reading
the generator lacks. Any other plural class stays plural: two distances that are not multiples of the
least (`2` and `3`), or a map other than `f₀^k` (`(2, +1)` beside `(4, +1)`). One survivor is its own
only winding, so order-2's location is unchanged: re-read at this build, its log, curve and both
states are byte-identical to lane B's (`keys_order2_log.txt`, timings aside;
`2026-10-05_LOCATED_KEYS_receipts/state_keys_{founded,lossless}.txt`).

**The deposit at the aeon's boundary** [agent-inferred; `research/notebook/hnn_design/hnn_keys_loop.rs`,
module header]. Lane B deposited at the first lock, which on order-2 is the pair held to the end. On
the regressions the first lock is a short-lived family: on the alternation's training passage, a
constant first request locates distance 1 on one class at observation 1, the next request kills it,
and the windings of distance 2 publish the identity on two classes at 16, three at 18 and four at 33.
A deposit at the first lock would leave the classes the key reaches later at their prior. The deposit
is therefore taken where the retention quotient is taken, at the passage's end (the aeon's
boundary), with the pair located there; on order-2 the two are one pair, so its deposit is unchanged.
