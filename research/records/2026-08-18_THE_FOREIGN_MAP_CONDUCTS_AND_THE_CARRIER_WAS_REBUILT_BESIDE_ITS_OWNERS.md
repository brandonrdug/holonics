### Three defects found by measurement, each a dropped dependency

1. **A square is not the product of an enclosure with itself.** Interval multiplication treats its

   negative radicand.
2. **A weighted mean lies inside its population's hull.** Dividing one wide enclosure by another
   drops the correlation between numerator and denominator exactly as a product drops it in a square.

     after contact       widest 2^25   root-mean-square 2^27

   Relative width grows by roughly the square root of the width per stage, so it reaches unity in
   about ten stages and the interval's midpoint stops carrying anything. Two hundred and ninety-four
   chained contractions make that certain. **The repair is `CLAUDE.md`'s own law — an invariant is
   only visible across two frames** — so the carried value is the exact value of the computation at
   the declared grain, what the grain did not carry is a retained population, and the falsifier is
