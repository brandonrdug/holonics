## 0. The recorded failures this work could repeat, and how each is avoided

From the [lessons record](2026-09-29_LESSONS_THE_FAILURES_THAT_REPEATED_AFTER_THEY_WERE_RECORDED.md):
- **1, an authored routine.** Nothing names the order-2 rule, a lag or a class. Every arm moves the
  source port `E` of the declared field by its own comparison's covector; the terrain alone computes
  truth, and only the comparison reads the targets.
- **2, recitation or an index.** The machine reads the request only through its placement on the
  receiving ring; no window, pair table or copier. The confirmation requests are a fresh draw of a
  seed no run has read (§2).
- **3, text as the exception.** One construction for every stream: storage through `E` at residues,
  read by the bank. Nothing here reads a byte or an alphabet's meaning.
- **4, the located cause carried unrepaired.** The located cause is the diagnosis's: the trained
  comparison is not the one the release executes. The repaired arm trains exactly the release's
  comparison along the release's own trajectory; the face arms are its matched controls, kept to
  measure the cause, not carried forward. The second located cause, the request under-weighted where
  generation begins (`ν̂(40)` against placed stations), is named as the expected blocker (§5) and not
  repaired here: this test reads one change.
- **5, an uncertified deposition step.** Every move is certified on its carried successor: the entry
  bound, admission of every crossing, every lock's Floquet certificate, a negative first-order bound
  on the carried move's active branches, and the declared comparison strictly lower by disjoint
  enclosures (`hnn::executed`, "The committed move"). The harness's committed energy bound and entry
  bound refuse adoption.
- **6, seen material graded as unseen.** Transfer is graded only on the confirmation seed, which no
  run reads before this pin is committed.
- **7, bits read as progress.** The comparisons' values are reported beside the releases; the
  acceptance is whole sections on unseen requests.
- **9, a refusal answered with a larger limit.** The moves, the ladder's depth (8 trials a move) and
  the deadlines are pinned; a run that reaches its deadline is reported incomplete and not rerun.
- **11, the programming language.** The contexts are refinements of the release (locked residues),
  the covector the monodromy's eigen-derivative; batches and moves are the realization.

## 3. The acceptance, fixed before any measured run

1. **The exact certificates hold**: every adopted move adopted only with every guard holding (the
   harness prints each move); on every confirmation release every lock's members certified and every
   executed tick closed, and no certificate refused.
2. **Whole sections on the confirmation requests**: the repaired arm (`executed-open`) releases
   strictly more sections equal to their targets than the opening and than each of the three
   controls.
3. **The first request-dependent locks**, reported separately: the first lock at a station whose
   cell two back is a request cell (station 0 or 1) and whether it is right; stations 0 and 1 right.
4. **Everything reported**: releases, holds, incorrect releases, sections reaching the termination,
   stations right by station, and every confirmation section whole (a receipts file beside the
   record; synthetic terrain only).
5. **The regressions**: on the alternation and the line the repaired arm releases no fewer whole
   sections than the opening.
6. **Time and memory**: every run within its deadline and a resident set of 4 GB.

Stage 2 **passes** when 1, 2, 5 and 6 hold. The falsifiers of the diagnosis §5 are read as written:
partition contexts succeeding where the open-section trajectory fails; training improving and
confirmation not; the resource bound reached.

## 5. What is expected, named before the runs [agent-inferred]

- The executed comparison falls on every batch it moves on (Stage 1 and the development reads: each
  adopted move lowers `F` by disjoint enclosures). Whether that transfers to whole sections within 24
  moves is the test.
- **The expected blocker** is the diagnosis's second cause: the section's first two stations read the
  request's last two cells at `ν̂(40)` against the placed stations' `ν̂(v)`; the fitted `E` (Stage 0)
  learned the section's internal order-2 relation (97 of 128 sections internally consistent) and
  missed its seed (17 of 128 whole). If the repaired arm learns the internal relation and not the
  seed, its whole sections stay near a sixteenth of its internally consistent ones.
- The face arms move their own code and are expected to leave the releases near the opening's (the
  learning path's receipt: 0 of 256 stations moved).
