The measurements of §18 (host, debug build, November 1), counts only.

cue-successor.txt: the cue's successor (a permutation of ten words, the separator, a cue and the
word that followed it, the last site unresolved) under §18's laws. Each line: the rate `2^(−rate)`
and the number of still ring sets, then at each checkpoint `sections:held/train/deposited/halvings`,
the held code and the sections' own code before their steps in millibits a site, the steps
deposited so far and the halvings since the last checkpoint. Width 16, two heads of four modes, 16
contacts, two ticks, two ring sets, 16 regions a section, 64 held regions, the fixtures' mixed key
stream. §17's runs of the same task are `../strand-contacts/copy-rates6-9.txt`.

founded-shares.txt: `founded_contact_shares_at_a_question_s_size` before learning, under the former
key stream with the charts at their own scale, then under the mixed stream at unit power: the rows
of 8 regions × 2 still heads of 450 sites over 122 classes that peak at the counterpart, and each
row's share of its weight there on `2^(−20)`.

founded-scores.txt: a removed scratch fixture at 48 and 450 sites, the charts at their own scale,
the former and the mixed key streams: rows peaking at the counterpart of 32, and for four rows the
counterpart's rank, its score in bits and the region's four highest (score@site).

crib-distances.py, crib-distances.txt: the crib terrain's question lengths and the first
calculation's operand distances (from the operand's first byte in the question to the answer site
that writes it), seed 20261101, 2,000 problems; the script repeats `crib_problem`'s draws.
