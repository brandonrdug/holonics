## 2 · The proof (three substitutions)

Norm under dilation, chart `dx`: with `u = x/a`,

    ‖U_a f‖² = ∫ |a^{−β}|² |f(x/a)|² dx = a^{−2Re(β)} · a · ∫ |f(u)|² du = a^{1−2Re(β)} ‖f‖².

Unitary for all `a` ⟺ `1 − 2Re(β) = 0` ⟺ Re(β) = ½. The imaginary part contributes
`|a^{−it}| = 1` — plural characters, norm untouched. ∎

Chart `dx/x`: the measure is itself dilation-invariant (`d(x/a)/(x/a) = dx/x`), so the norm
scales by `a^{−2Re(β)}` alone ⟺ Re(β) = 0. ∎

The intertwiner: `‖Mf‖²_{dx/x} = ∫ |x^{½}f|² dx/x = ∫ |f|² dx = ‖f‖²_{dx}` — unitary; and
`M U_a^{(½)} M^{−1} = U_a^{(0)}` by direct computation — the weight moves whole between the
normalization and the measure. ∎
