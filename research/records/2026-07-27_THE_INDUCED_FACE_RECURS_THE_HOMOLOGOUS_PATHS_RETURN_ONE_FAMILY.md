## 2. Exact recurrent family law

### 2.1 A branch is an intersection of receiver fibers

Let \(B\) be a realised monic degree-\(d\) filler branch over a prime support
\(S\). Its exact coefficient family now has two kinds of local conditions.

For a set \(D\subseteq S\), it retains fixed reductions

\[
f_p\in\mathbb F_p[x],\qquad p\in D.
\]

For the remaining receivers \(V=S\setminus D\), it retains phase strata

\[
U_q(d,\tau_q)=
\left\{
g\in\mathbb F_q[x]:
g\text{ monic},\ \deg g=d,\ \tau_q\in\operatorname{phase}(g)
\right\}.
\]

With \(\rho_p\) denoting coefficientwise reduction, the complete family is

\[
\mathcal P_B=
\bigcap_{p\in D}\rho_p^{-1}(\{f_p\})
\cap
\bigcap_{q\in V}\rho_q^{-1}(U_q(d,\tau_q)).
\]

This is an intersection of inverse images. Since the receivers are distinct
primes, coefficientwise CRT also presents it as the product of the finite
local choices followed by affine lifting modulo

\[
M_S=\prod_{p\in S}p.
\]

Its exact torsor cardinality is therefore

\[
N_B=\prod_{q\in V}\lvert U_q(d,\tau_q)\rvert.
\]

No local polynomial in a variable stratum enters standing. The engine stores
the stratum law and its exact count.

### 2.2 One complete caused face may extend by one receiver

Suppose a later open horn has support

\[
S'=S\cup\{r\}
\]

and the complete face \(S\) carries the feature emitted by \(B\). The new
oriented branch is

\[
\mathcal P_{B,r}
=
\mathcal P_B\cap\rho_r^{-1}(U_r(d,\tau_B)).
\]

Every variable stratum of \(B\) is copied as a carried constraint. The
receiver \(r\) contributes one new stratum. Thus

\[
N_{B,r}=N_B\lvert U_r(d,\tau_B)\rvert.
\]

If any factor is empty, the new branch is an exact obstruction. Overflow or
an inadmissible local search membrane refuses the whole event.

The engine permits this recurrence only when the source face is exactly the
parent filler-space support. A feature visible on a proper subface does not
authorize recurrence, because doing so would forget the parent's conditions
on the omitted receivers.

## 3. A genuinely induced two-generation ecology

The grading ecology uses four receivers:

\[
\{2,3,5,7\}.
\]

It inherits only the three monic quadratics

\[

b(x)=x^2+10x,\qquad
c(x)=x^2+14x.

Write \(R_p\) for the repeated-linear phase over \(\mathbb F_p\), and \(S_p\)

| Probe | Repeated-root pair | Distinct-root pair |
|---|---|---|

| \(b(x)\) | \(\{2,5\}\) | \(\{3,7\}\) |
| \(c(x)\) | \(\{2,7\}\) | \(\{3,5\}\) |

Thus all six pair cells exist. No inherited probe has one phase common to

\[

\{2,5,7\},\quad

Resolving those four supports causes three first-generation branches and one
triangle cell per support. Only after all four caused triangles exist does

- no tetrahedral phase cell exists; and

The second resolution therefore has no inherited face witness from which it

## 4. Exact homologous-path result

For monic quadratics over \(\mathbb F_p\):

\[
\lvert R_p\rvert=p,
\qquad
\lvert S_p\rvert=\binom p2.
\]

The tetrahedral resolution emits 12 causal branches. Each fixed pair can
receive its two complementary strata in either order, so the 12 branches
quotient to six chronology-free families:

| Fixed receiver pair | Variable receivers | Phase | Exact torsors | Causal paths |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| \(\{2,3\}\) | \(\{5,7\}\) | repeated | \(5\cdot7=35\) | 2 |
| \(\{2,5\}\) | \(\{3,7\}\) | repeated | \(3\cdot7=21\) | 2 |
| \(\{2,7\}\) | \(\{3,5\}\) | repeated | \(3\cdot5=15\) | 2 |

| \(\{5,7\}\) | \(\{2,3\}\) | distinct | \(1\cdot3=3\) | 2 |

For example, the repeated-root family fixed on \(\{2,3\}\) can be caused by:

1. resolving \(\{2,3,5\}\), carrying the receiver-5 stratum, then adding
   receiver 7; or
2. resolving \(\{2,3,7\}\), carrying the receiver-7 stratum, then adding

The source faces, missing receivers, and parent branch identities differ.
The chronology-free fixed sections and local strata are identical. Direct
torsor enumeration independently returns the same 35 CRT families for both
paths. The other five pairs satisfy the same exact two-path equality.

This is the bounded result meant here by a homologous path: two caused
alignments preserve the same fixed local material and recombine the same
complementary strata into one family.
