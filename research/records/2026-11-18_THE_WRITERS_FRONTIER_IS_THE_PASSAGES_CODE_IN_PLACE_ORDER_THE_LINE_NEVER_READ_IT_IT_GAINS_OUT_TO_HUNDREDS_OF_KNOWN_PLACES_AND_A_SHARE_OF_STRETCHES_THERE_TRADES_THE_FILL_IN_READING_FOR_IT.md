## 2. What the line trained and what it never compared

Read in the source (`passage::state_of`): half of a pass's stretches are writer states, the context known and the run's first `k`
places resolved in the octave order of their distance, every open place compared and a true end given; half are masks at `τ = u²`
over the stretch or its run. The opening is compared at every place besides. A place with everything before it known and nothing
after it, which is what a writer in place order meets at every place it writes, is compared only as one of a writer state's open
places. The line was scored at one place open with every other place known, a place's news, which no writer meets.

The three readings are different objects (atlas `code.order-agnostic-masked-bound`, `code.chain-one-law-order-averaged`):

- **The frontier's code**, `Σ_t −log p̂(u_t | u_(<t))`, is the passage's code in place order: its description length as it arrives,
  and the surprise that crosses a writer's section.
- **Whole masks**, weighed `m/K`, estimate the code averaged over reading orders. For one joint law the two are equal.
- **One place open** is `−log p̂(u_t | every other place)`. It is no code of the passage: nothing decodes a place from the places
  after it before they are written.

On the held passage at lap 70's control mean they read 2.0574, 2.2089 and 0.7727 nats a place. The field's faces are not one joint
law (the orders differ by 0.15), and the reading the line reported was the third.

## 5. What it says

- **She reads far before the place.** Each octave of known places lowers the frontier's code by 0.05 to 0.16 nats, out to hundreds, on
  a state that was never compared there on purpose, and one lap of comparison there gains most where most is known. I withdraw what I
  wrote on November 17 and 18, that at the frontier she reads like a count of the last four bound symbols: her 1.479 beside that
  count's 1.478 was a coincidence of level at 48 known places on fifty books. What is owed at the frontier is not reach first.
- **The line was never compared where it writes, and comparison there moves both the code and the writes.** One lap on five kinds of
  text: 0.16 nats and nine to eleven points of the panel. Ten passes on one kind: 0.55 nats and 44 points.
- **The readings trade.** At six stretches in eight the fill-in reading rose 0.10 and whole masks 0.08 while the frontier fell 0.16:
  the field does not hold every order at once in the room it has, and a share moves room between them. By G-1118-3's own rule the share
  as built does not enter the line on this lap, and the share is what is read next (G-1118-4). Which reading the product stands on is
  not in doubt: the frontier's is the passage's code and the writer's state, and one place open is a ruler.
- **The count that binds is comparisons a site.** A frontier region compares one place for a whole region's read: about 1.3 ms of card
  for a region of 110 sites (a section's time fits 0.565 ms a region, 0.0046 a site and 17.2 a million pairs). The lap compared about
  700,000 places at the frontier over five kinds of text, where the fifty books met 900,000 a pass for ten passes. At this rate the
  store needs tens of laps to meet what the books met. A read that compares every place of a region at its own frontier in one pass
  (every place hearing only what stands before it, as the frontier's place does) would compare about ninety times the places for the
  same card time by that fit (a projection from the fit at two reads a region, not a reading). It is the realization the method rule asks for before a long run (a rate is judged against the card),
  and it is the next build; it is a change of the forward and its kernels and is not begun.
