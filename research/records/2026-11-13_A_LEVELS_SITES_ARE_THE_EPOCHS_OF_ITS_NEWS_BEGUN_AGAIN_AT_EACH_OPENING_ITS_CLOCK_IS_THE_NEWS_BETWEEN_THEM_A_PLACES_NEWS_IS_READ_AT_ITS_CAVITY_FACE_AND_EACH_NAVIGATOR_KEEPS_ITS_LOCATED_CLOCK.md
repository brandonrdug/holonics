## 1. Why this record exists

The two records of November 12 left a design owed and gave it a first form: a stride read at its
place as the place's code under its face, one clock moving the moments and cutting the level
above, the level's sites the carries of October's clock of news, and an acceptance stated as an
invariance under a change of clock (Brandon, November 12 23:20Z: no clock and no frame's length
is privileged). Three rulers were read before the design was written, each with its guess fixed
first. They changed the design in three places:

- The trained strides are neither a symbol's length nor a symbol's screening of its past: they
  are each clock's own located key (G123). So the moments' clocks are not replaced.
- October's carry places an opening by the whole sum before it, so it privileges an origin: a
  changed start keeps 0.31 of its openings (G124). The cut that keeps its sites begins its sum
  again at each opening.
- A written place has no face, and no form of two symbols reads its news well enough (G124).
  The face it needs exists in the built law: the opening it would have as an open place.

## 2. The one object

A level of the tower is a strand read by receivers, and it has two things that the first form
ran together.

- **Places.** A place is where a receiver's section is arrived at. The elementary objects say an
  epoch is an interval between a receiver's section arrivals, and that epochs count flux through
  the receiver's section. The flux a level receives from the level below is its news. So the
  places of a level are the epochs of the news below it.
- **A clock.** Elapsed time is the pairing `⟨ω_R|γ⟩ = windings + phase`: a difference between two
  places, into which no origin enters. Where the whole windings fall does depend on an origin.

On the octets the places are given (an octet a place) and each navigator's clock is its key (a
rate a class, located by deposition, `tower.field-pace`). On the level above nothing is given:
its places have to be cut and its clock read. The first form cut the places by the clock's whole
windings (October's odometer), which makes the places inherit the clock's origin. The two are
separate readings of one sum `S_t = Σ_(k≤t) c_k` of the places' codes:

```text
clock    τ(b) − τ(a) = (S_b − S_a)/σ                         a difference; no origin enters
places   a place opens when the news since the last opening,   an arrival word; it depends on what
         its own included, passes one section σ               stands before only through the last opening
```

The second line is a joint under a slow drive, as the joints of October 8 state one
(`deposition.threshold-regimes`: the drive loads it, the carry is its strain, and a landing
releases it): the news loads the level's section, the place that breaks it opens, and the load
begins again from that place. Its openings are the arrival word of a ring that locks to the
strand's bright places, and the tree already holds that such a word is not the carry word of
the ring's mean rate (`wave.arrival-word-is-not-the-mean-rate`, a counterexample on a loaded
ring).

## 4. The law

### 4.1 Each navigator keeps its located clock

The paced clocks stay as built: a rate a class, located by deposition, `τ` and `σ` tied by the
one `κ`, a blank at its expected duration. The record of the symbol's length (§8.1) proposed that
the moments' stride become the place's code. No reading supports that, and §3.1 speaks against
it: the rates are frictions of each clock's own, whose carries screen where the news does not
rank them. In a passage's transfer the news at a place and how fast two pasts stop differing
there are two exponents, and a moment's decay is the second. G123's rule also bars reading a
second quantity into the stride (no complex length of its own angle).

### 4.2 A level's sites are the landmarks of the news below it

Restated on November 13 by [the record of the landmarks](2026-11-13_A_LEVELS_SITES_ARE_THE_LANDMARKS_OF_ITS_NEWS_A_PLACE_NO_LARGER_NEWS_STANDS_WITHIN_A_SECTION_OF_THE_CLAIM_THE_CARRIED_CUT_AND_THE_ODOMETER_ARE_ONE_LAW_ANCHORED_NEARER_AND_FARTHER_AND_ONE_PROGRAM_READS_EVERY_SYMBOL_STREAM.md) (G137). The title above this
record keeps the law of its first writing, which is placed at the end of this section.

Let `c_t > 0` be the news of place `t` of a region (§4.4), `S_t` its running sum, the place's
arrival on the clock of news, and `σ` the level's section (§4.6).

```text
u ≻ t          c_u > c_t, or c_u = c_t and u < t
u reaches t    |S_u − S_t| < σ
t is a site  ⇔  no u ≻ t reaches t  ⇔  ρ_t ≥ σ
ρ_t = min( S_t − S_u at the last u < t with c_u ≥ c_t ,  S_u − S_t at the first u > t with c_u > c_t )
L_j = S_(s_(j+1)) − S_(s_j)              the news from site j's arrival to the next site's;  Σ_j L_j = the news between the first site and the last
```

- A site is a landmark of the news at the level's section: a place that no larger news stands
  within a section of. It is the first round of a claim in the order of the news (the
  release's re-draw order on the clock of news), and it is what that claim keeps in every
  stream that agrees within a section of the place (the other record's statement 5).
- **A place that carries a section of news or more is a site.** Two sites stand a section or
  more apart. A place's isolation `ρ_t` is one number, and every level is a cut of it.
- A site leads its epoch: the epoch of site `j` is the site and the places after it up to the
  next site, the moment's own convention (a place's stride is the gap before it).
- One law for every stream. It reads the order of the news and distances on its clock, and
  nothing of the kind of symbol: octets and a ring's arrivals are read by one program.
- The rule is integer sums and comparisons on the code's grain, so any partition of the card
  gives the same bits. A place's isolation is two nearest-greater reads, one either way.
- The sites and the lengths are data to the pass that reads them: a carry has no first-order
  return, and the built clock law already takes the face as an operand of the clock.
- **What it replaces.** This section's first writing cut a level by a restarting carry: a
  place opens when the news since the last opening, its own included, passes the section. That
  is a claim in the order of arrival. It keeps its sites under a changed start (§3.2, §3.3)
  and loses them under a change of the stream between a site and the opening before it. At a
  mean epoch of 6 places, with a symbol replaced at 0.05 of places, it keeps a lift of 0.32
  on octets and 0.30 on arrivals where the landmarks keep 0.85 and 0.87; with the reader's
  most expected symbol inserted after 0.25 of places, 0.12 and 0.10 against 0.56 and 0.81.
  The readings of §3 stand as that cut's.

### 4.3 The level's clock is the news between its sites

Site `j` of the level strides `κ¹_j = L_j/σ` on the paced clock's grain, so
`τ¹_j − τ¹_i = (news from site i's arrival to site j's)/σ`. Two sites stand a section or more
apart, so `κ¹_j ≥ 1`: the level steps at most once a section of news, and a stretch its reader
predicts is one long stride (at 8 bits under the count reader a mean stride is 1.8 to 1.9
sections, about a third of strides are 2 or more, and the longest read is 7.6). This is October's clock of news kept as
a clock: its count law, its carry between sections that divide one another and its nesting
(`hnn.tower-news-clock-carry`) are statements about `⌊S/σ⌋`, and they stand for the level's
elapsed time. It is no longer the law of where the level's sites open. The built packets' level
runs plain clocks (`plain_paces`); the per-site `κ` the clock structure already carries takes
`L_j/σ` in their place, and at equal lengths that is the plain clock. Drive and move stay on one
clock, the level's own: an epoch arrives once and the level steps once (October's repair, the
held step).

### 4.4 A place's news is read at its cavity face

A place's news is the surprise of what stands there under what the others bring to it before
any of them has heard it. The built opening is that face. An open, silent place opens on

```text
x^cav_t = E(∗) + E₋(u_(t−1)) + E₊(u_(t+1)) + C s^cav_t + b_s,      p̃_t = face(x^cav_t)   read through the encoding
```

with `s^cav_t` the place's moment from the other places as they stand (its own terms absent, its
own stride bare, as `Field::silent` has it). For a written place the same expression is its
**cavity opening**: the opening it would have as an open place among the others. So

```text
c_t = −log p̃_t(u_t)            at a written place (its entry compared at its cavity face)
c_t = −Σ_a p̃_t(a) log p̃_t(a)   at an open place (the face's expected code; the blank's law of §16.44 with −log p̃ for the class rates)
```

- It is the house's loss read at a written place: the log of the ratio of what the others'
  currents produce there to what stands there. A written place's surprise is as physical as an
  open one's; only its comparison was never read.
- No echo enters: at the opening no place has heard another. The cavity field of a read
  (`tower.field-echo`) removes the same term after ticks; here it is absent by construction.
- It is first order in the sources: the two strand rows and the moment's sum over every source
  at its skin. Its count analogue is the two-sided form of §3.2, without the moment.
- One law for every place of a region, fixed before the first tick, so the level's sites stand
  through the pass's ticks: a field iterates one constitution on one complex. A release cuts
  again at each stage's opening, and a place that is written there changes its expected code
  for its realized one, which is what the blank's clock law already says of its duration.
- **What was unread, and what the reading gave** (G127, November 13; `p55_cavity.txt` in the
  engine record's receipts; the store's state at the mean of 1,000 states, 30,677 held places
  over five slices, each place open alone with every other place of its stretch written). The
  readout's covector comes from the faces after the last tick, and it does not read an opening.

**The law** (`agent-inferred`; G132's rule (b), fixed before the reading). The opening takes a
lift of its own onto the one tied face, and it is compared at every place of a region:

```text
r̂⁰_t = chart(x^cav_t)                         the built lane chart of the cavity opening (§4.4), D = 64 words
y⁰_t = R⁰ r̂⁰_t + b⁰                            the opening's lift: its own block, D × D entries and D of bias, founded at zero
p̃_t  = face(tied(y⁰_t))   at temperature one   the one tied face: the encoding's rows and bias, which the last tick's face reads through
c_t  = −log p̃_t(u_t)                           at every place t of a region, written or open, u_t the class the passage holds there
δz_t = p̃_t − 1[u_t]                            its covector at unit gain, beside the last tick's comparison at the compared places
```

- *A lift of its own.* A frame has its own transport: the ticks carry a state into the frame
  the lift `R` reads, and the opening stands before them. One lift read at both stages would
  be drawn between two frames; the reading shows the built one at 8.260 on words that hold 2.502.
- *One tied face.* A symbol's key is one word. A second encoding at the opening would give a
  symbol two words and the opening's news a different alphabet from the one the level above
  reads.
- *At every place.* A written place's surprise is as physical as an open one's (§4.4). The
  comparison reaches every place of a region where the last tick's reaches the mask's open
  places, and `c_t` is the level's clock at every place (§4.3).
- *Its return* uses the operands that produced it: the tied face (the encoding's rows and its
  bias), `R⁰` and `b⁰`, the chart and its lanes, then the cavity opening's own operands: the
  unresolved row `E(∗)`, the two neighbours' strand rows, the moment's chart `C` with its bias,
  and through `C` the moment the other places bring (their terms and their clocks). The place's
  own row, own moment term and own stride take nothing: the cavity opening does not hold them.
  At an open place the cavity opening is the built opening, and the covector adds to the one
  the ticks return there.
- *The own stride's remainder* (decided at the build, November 13; `agent-inferred`). In unrounded
  arithmetic the place's own stride takes nothing: the after side reads `ζ^(τ_k − τ_t)`, which
  holds no stride of `t`. The built clock covector reads that side through the absolute clocks
  the prefix sums share, so the stride takes two terms the mathematics cancels, the receiver's
  `−cross(δs_t, s⁺_t)` on the moment released to `2^(−16)` and its sources'
  `Σ_k cross(δE⁻_k, E⁺_k)` on the return released to `2^(−24)`. What reaches it is their
  releases' remainder, declared at those grains and bounded (half a unit a component against
  `|δs_t|₁`, two units against each source's `|E⁺_k|₁`, a unit a released product): the bound
  held on 1,952 receivers, the nearest at 382 of 414 units of `2^(−24)`, and the skin part is
  zero bit for bit (each pair's skin lands on its two ends with one integer). On the fixtures'
  one written place of a class written once the stride's covector read 26 and 18 units on the
  two paced heads of a field with two, and 203 on a field with one. The return keeps the
  operands that produced the forward; a finer grain picked to shrink the remainder would be a
  width set by a reading, and the standing comparison's clock covector carries the same
  remainder on every place's after side, so the line reproduces with it. Zero by construction
  would need the turn's covector formed a pair at a time, landing on both ends with one integer
  as the skins' does, for both comparisons at once; that changes the line's training comparison
  and enters by its own reproduction.
- *Unit gain.* Each comparison's covector is what reached its locus. No weight between the two
  comparisons is declared: a weight would be a literal with no reading behind it.
- *The standing sums.* `s^cav_t` is formed from the region's standing sums (the others' terms
  at their standing phases), the place's own term absent and its own phase at the bare stride.
  In unrounded arithmetic this is the opening of the region with the place silent, the built
  `opening_face` on the region masked at `t`; the two differ by the order of their releases,
  and the difference is printed where the build is checked, never assumed zero.
- *In a release* an open place holds no class: nothing is compared there, and its news is the

### 4.5 An epoch's face is its moment on the news phase

## 9. Acceptance, the device line, and the order of work

**The acceptance is an invariance**, read on the machine's own codes, with the held code beside
it as one reading and no level promised:

1. the share of a held passage's openings kept under a shifted start, and under re-timings the
   trained reader predicts (the doubled indentation on the code slice; one more on prose, named
   in the order), against the odometer's and the built packets' shares on the same pair;
2. a predicted failure: under a re-timing the reader has not met (every octet doubled) the
   added places are bright, the sites double and the share falls; it returns once the reader
   has learned the doubling;
3. the spread of an epoch's news and the screen ratio at the openings;
4. the held code by slice and kind at lap means, and the writes by slice, against the line
   without the level. The founding reads every face of the field it is founded on.

**The order.**

1. *A reading, on the PC, from state C, outside any timing hold* (read November 13, G127;
   §4.4): every held place's code at its cavity opening, by slice; its rank against the last
   tick's code; the restarting cut at 8 bits on those codes; and the count, from source, of what
   a cavity face a site adds to a section. The readout does not read an opening.
2. *A second reading, from the same state* (G132, fixed before its read): what a place's 64
   words hold of its class at the opening and after each of the four ticks, by a fitted face;
   the trained readout's code at each tick; the restarting cut on the code after the ticks; and
   the same fitted faces under the held role's whole masks. It decides the opening's face
   (§4.4) and reads whether the last two ticks add to a place's code.
3. *The law of the opening's face* is derived and recorded from that reading, with its Lean
   obligation named, before any build (done: §4.4a).
4. *The build, in two parts.* First the opening's comparison alone (§4.4a, G139): it is the
   smallest part, it gives the machine's own `c_t` at every place, and every later part reads
   that clock. Then, on its readings and with its own guess and its cost stated against the
   card: the level's sites (§4.2 as restated: a place's isolation is two nearest-greater
   reads), the level's clock, the phase chart, and a field of the level's own founded as the
   built `Scale` is. Each part enters the store's line by reproducing the line's lap with the
   part absent from the state.

**The device line.** The card, under its lock; the reading of item 1 is a host reading from a
saved state and leaves its bytes the same.
