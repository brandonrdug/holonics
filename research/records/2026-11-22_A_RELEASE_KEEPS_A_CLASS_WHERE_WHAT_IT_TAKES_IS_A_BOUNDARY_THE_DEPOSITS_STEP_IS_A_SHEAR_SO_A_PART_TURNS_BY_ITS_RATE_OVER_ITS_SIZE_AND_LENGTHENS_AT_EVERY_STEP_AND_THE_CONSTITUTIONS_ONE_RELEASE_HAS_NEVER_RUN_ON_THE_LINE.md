## 3. The law

### 3.1 A release that differs from the identity by a boundary keeps every class

On a complex with `∂∂ = 0`, let `R = 1 − (∂h + h∂)` for any map `h` one degree up. For a cycle `z` (`∂z = 0`) and a closed reading
`c` (`c∂ = 0`):

```text
Rz = z − ∂(hz),        ⟨c, Rz⟩ = ⟨c, z⟩ − ⟨c∂, hz⟩ = ⟨c, z⟩.
```

What `R` takes from a cycle is a boundary, and no closed reading sees a boundary. `h` is the anisotropy: it chooses what is
taken, and every choice keeps the classes (homotopic chain maps agree on homology; standard). The Hodge friction is `h = νδ`. A
rounding whose remainder is paid to the next place is `h` the carry. A wrap is a ring map and keeps every reading the modulus
admits; what it takes is the lap, and it is lawful where no admitted future reads the lap (October 24 §3).

A scalar leak `R = (1 − ε)` is of that kind only for a store that is read as a ratio to its own power: there what it takes lies
along the scale, which no receiver of the store distinguishes. On a store whose size is read it scales every reading alike and is
lawful only beside the comparison that puts the reading back.

### 3.2 The deposit's step is a shear of a part read as a ratio

Let a part `θ` of `N` entries be read only through `θ/|θ|`. Its covector at a section is orthogonal to it. The deposit moves each
entry by `s = −2^(−r) m/(⌊√v⌋ + 1)`, a count of grains that does not know the part's size.

- **The turn.** The part turns by `|s|/|θ|` a section: its rate over its own size. Two parts at one rate turn unlike by the ratio
  of their sizes. By the test of the precision law's clause on grains the rate is counted from a gauge: scale a lane's writers by
  a factor and their lift by its inverse, and no code changes while every turn does.
- **The lengthening.** `|θ + s|² = |θ|² + 2⟨θ, s⟩ + |s|²`. A step orthogonal to the part is the Swing's shear, free fall, and not
  its turn: it lengthens. With the first moment kept at `β = 2^(−3)` and a white covector of unit power an entry,
  `E|s|² = 2^(−2r) β/(2 − β)`; and since the part has moved along each remembered covector's own descent since that covector's
  section, `2E⟨θ, s⟩ = 2^(−2r) · 2(1 − β)/(2 − β)`. Together `2^(−2r)` of power an entry a section, fourteen fifteenths of it the
  moment's memory, whatever holds the part's direction. Write a part's growth of power an entry a section as `c 2^(−2r)`: at
  `c = 1` the step is alone.
- **No rest.** With nothing along the part its size grows as the root of its age and its turn falls as the inverse. That is the
  body that only coheres, with its rate.

### 3.3 The leak beside the step

`θ′ = (1 − 2^(−K))(θ + s)`.

- A part read as a ratio rests where the leak takes what the steps add, at a root mean square of `2^(−r) √(c 2^(K − 1))`, and there
  it turns by `rms(ρ) 2^(−(K − 1)/2)/√c` a section, `ρ` the step's ratio, whatever its rate. The leak is that part's thermostat, and
  the rate is the unit of its size.
- For that part the leak closes the shear into a turn: at `2^(−K) = c 2^(−2r)/(2 rms²)` it takes along the part what the steps
  add, and the length is kept, the move about a held grip. In bits, `K = 1 + 2 log₂(rms · 2^r) − log₂ c`: the square of the
  part's size counted in its own steps. It is read from the part and is never the rate.
- An entry whose size a comparison reads rests at `2^(K − r)⟨ρ⟩` (`store.leak-relaxation`), and one no comparison reaches at a
  spread of `2^(−r)√(2^K/2)` (`walk.py`, part 1).
- The leak as built is one scalar for every entry, toward zero, released to the nearest grain with its remainder dropped: an entry
  under `2^(K − 1)` grains stands, which is 0.031 at 20 bits on the material's grain, and every entry under one half of the
  letters' rows from 16 bits up (their grain is `2^(−16)`).
