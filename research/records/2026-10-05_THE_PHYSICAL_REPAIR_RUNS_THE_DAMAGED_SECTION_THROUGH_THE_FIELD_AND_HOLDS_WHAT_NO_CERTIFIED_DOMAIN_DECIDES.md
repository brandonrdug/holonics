**Date.** October 5. **Issues.** #73, #148, #63, #62. **Lane.** C of U6 (THE_REBUILD, "U6's order
from October 5", "The task is repair, not continuation"), the physical repair, under the contract
agreed with Codex in the mailbox (six points, adopted exactly; §2). **Grade.** [definition;
agent-inferred] for the pins of §0, fixed and committed before any read; [measured] for every count

**Occasion.** The [repair by reflection](2026-10-05_REPAIR_BY_REFLECTION_THE_LOCATED_PAIR_RESTRICTS_THE_ERASED_CELLS_FROM_BOTH_SIDES.md)
restricts a damaged passage through the located pair read as a relation on classes
(`compression::keys::repair`). It is exact, and it is a routine that knows the key: the field never
moves. The [source entrance](2026-10-05_THE_SOURCE_MOMENT_ENTERS_THE_CONTINUING_LOADED_FIELD_AT_ITS_PORT.md)
built the field's side (`Word::open_exact_received`) and left its repair consumer owed: a caller
must join an actual station clock, retain the interior, account every source imposition, read the
receiving map in its declared frame and clock, and keep the unresolved fibre where no domain or
decoder coverage is certified. This loop builds that consumer in `hnn::prediction` and reads it once
on the repair record's terrain, with the reference repair as the grader only.

The computational object is the helical pair interaction. Of the
[winding guide](../../docs/WINDING_CARRY_AND_PLACEMENT.md)'s six objects this loop touches **the
helix** (station `j` placed at `τ_g + 1 + j` of the source ring's clock, and read at the receiver's
crossing `e_0 + j` of the word's clock), **the pair** (the located contact deposited as the source
port's pair component by lanes B and C, unchanged, which the source storage reads through `E`), and
**faces and placement** (the decoder's face `R · P_R^(τ_R) v_R` at the receiver's grain). The cell
holonomy (the turn menu's loop law), the tube and the tower thread stay attached and unchanged.

## 2. The design, and its equations

**The contract** (Codex's six points, adopted exactly):
1. **Inputs**: the admitted encoded intact cells at declared section placements
   (`DamagedSection`), the contemporary material and lift, the actual `WordOpening` and the
   receiving section's declaration (`ReceivingPhases`). The reference's per-cell families and the
   truth stay outside the forward signature.
2. **The source**: the sparse `SourceMoment` on the declared chart, imposed by one
   `Word::open_exact_received`.
3. **The motion**: the one continuing word through the declared crossings (`Word::forward`) and its
   anchors at each declared epoch; local crossings bound to `opened_at` and the section clock.
4. **The read**: `R` moved into `FieldMaterial`; `ReceivingPhases::read`, `R · P^(lift) · anchor` at
   its grain, returning `ReceivingRead` and its `GrainCell`s; no tree, address, combine or
   population.
5. **The release**: only where the domain projected through the same executed anchor map and
   decoder admits one class; otherwise held with its fibre or unresolved.
6. **The return**: the `SourceOpeningReceipt`, the actual `FieldBalance` and `WordBalance` readings
   and `Word::reception_end`'s carry; the transient passage dropped after its consumer.

**The equations at the consumer** (`hnn::prediction::repair_by_field`):

```text
section     S = {(j, c_j) : j intact} ⊂ [0, L) × ℤ/|A|,   erasures unplaced
source      m_g = Σ_(j intact) Ĝ_g(τ_g + 1 + j)⁻¹ E_g e_(c_j)                 (SourceMoment::continued)
opening     x(0) = Π_int x_prev + s(m),   E_after − E_before = imposed − absorbed (one imposition)
motion      x(k + 1) = Φ_k(x(k)),  k < e_0 + A − 1, then the last junction       (Word::forward)
clock       station j ↔ crossing e_0 + j ↔ refinement tick opened_at + e_0 + j,
            certified by reception_end().ticks = opened_at + e_0 + A − 1
read        f_j = R · P_R^(τ_R) v_R(e_0 + j),  Re f_(j,c) = n + k/L_R + ε,  ε ∈ [0, 1/L_R)
release     width_j = diam{ class(f) : f ∈ R·P·v_R(e_0 + j)[𝒟_j] };  released ⇔ width_j = 0 at tolerance 0
            𝒟_j the certified domain of the coupled drive; uncertified, 𝒟_j = the state space and
```

[definition; agent-inferred] **The clock binding.** The receiver's phase `j` at the word's tick
`e_0 + j` reads cell `p + j` of the passage after the moment's last tick (`hnn::receiving`, "The
receiver reads the passage by epochs"), and the section's station `j` is placed at `τ_g + 1 + j`,
the cell after the lift's tick. So station `j` is read at the local crossing `e_0 + j`. A word
opened on a received carry at tick `T` imposes its source at `T`, so its crossings are the
refinement's ticks `T + e`. The placement alone certifies no clock: the binding is checked against
the carried end's tick, the word's own clock.

[definition; agent-inferred] **Why a point reading releases nothing.** The executed state at a
crossing is one point; its leading class at the grain (`StationRead::leaders`) is a reading of that
point. A release needs every state the coupled drive admits for that station to decode to one
class, and no owner certifies that set: `holon::parametron::PeriodicDomain` certifies a component
under a declared frozen drive, not the word's state-dependent coupled drive (the
[executed-domain record](2026-10-05_THE_QUARTIC_STEP_NEEDS_AN_EXECUTED_DOMAIN_BEFORE_A_LOCK.md):
a closed work account does not bound the executed orbit), and the receiver's rank at rest is a
tangent dimension, not a domain. Uncertified, the domain is the whole state space,
whose image under the decoder lies within the encoding's classes. The unit test
`a_point_reading_with_one_leader_releases_nothing` pins it: a declared nonzero decoder leads one
class at some station, and every erased station is still held.
