## 3. The physics, one propagation

- **A source.** A reed, a lip, a vocal fold, a bowed string and an electric organ each turn a steady flux (a breath, a bow's
  pull, a current) into a cycle: a store and a flow exchanging, held at its amplitude by a nonlinear contact. A free reed's note
  is set by two cuts of its maker, `f = 0.1615 (h/L²) √(E/ρ)`: brass of 0.20 mm sounds 493 Hz at 15.00 mm, and the chord 4:5:6
  above it asks lengths of 13.42 and 12.25 mm (`MEDIUM_AND_GAPS.out`). Each note is a separately located key.
- **A channel.** The wave the source emits winds through a tube whose cross-section `A(x)` varies: `(A p_x)_x = A p_tt/c²`
  (Webster's horn equation). Each change of area is a junction with `Γ = (A₁ − A₂)/(A₁ + A₂)`, the atlas's law with impedance
  `ρc/A`; the tube's resonances are the round trips that close; and the power a section passes is `p²A/(ρc)`, so a channel's
  capacity is its area. A vowel is a pair of cross-sections; a harmonica's hole is one.
- **A medium.** A tube's resonances scale with the speed of sound, `c² = γRT/M`, which is set by what the medium is made of:
  343.2 m/s in air, 267.2 in carbon dioxide (every tube 434 cents lower, between a major third and a fourth), 1,007.5 in helium
  (1,864 cents higher, about a twelfth). Water carries sound at 1,481 m/s, an index of 0.232 against air, and its impedance is
  3,577 times air's, so 0.11% of a voice's power crosses the surface (−29.5 dB). Weakly electric fish signal with their electric
  organ's discharge in the same way, and neighbours shift their frequencies apart to stay distinguishable (the jamming
  avoidance response).
- **The medium is cycles.** The air's `M` and `γ` are states of slow cycles: its oxygen is two billion years of
  photosynthesis, and its carbon dioxide has moved inside a few human generations. What a player reads as a constant is a state
  of a slower build, and the players' flux, summed over that build's span, moves it.

## 4. The law (weight 0.8, `agent-inferred`)

**A composition can stand in a locale only after its causes do. Its cost there is its build: the least number of joins that
make it from the parts the locale already holds, with every made thing kept as a part. That count is a floor on the epochs and
on the action spent before it.**

- **A Holarchy over an Aeon is that build kept.** The pool a build leaves, every made thing with its constituents, is what
  `interconnect` returns; the Aeon is the container of its joins, and it is at least as many epochs long as the depth of what it
  holds. A Holon over an epoch is one join. They are one structure at two sizes because a made thing is a part for the next
  join (`Reaches.made_is_free`, `Reaches.sample`): the guide's "interconnected Holons form a Holon", with its cost.
- **The index is the same relation read from inside.** A slow build is what a fast one reads as its medium: its constitution,
  its constants, its index. A fast build is what the slow one receives as a deposit. The afternoon's mass (where rays circulate)
  is a kept build, and the optical length of a ray through it is that ray's build from the medium's parts: short where the ray
  factors through what the medium holds (0.6).
- **Something to put it together.** With nothing kept, a composition of `n` units over `k` kinds arrives by chance once in
  `kⁿ` tries. With made things kept it costs its depth, and depths add. The ratio between the two is what a lineage is: a
  harmonica is unreachable by the first and a few thousand joins deep by the second.

## 5. The gap boundaries

Proved in `HolonicsResearch/Compression/AssemblyDepth` (`Built glue B l r`: the pool `l` built from the parts `B` with `r`
joins).

1. **The doubling floor** (`Built.size_le`, `addition_floor`, `doubling_built`). Where a join is no larger than the sum of its
   two sizes, `r` joins reach nothing larger than `2ʳ` parts' worth. A composition of `n` units costs at least `⌈log₂ n⌉` joins,
   and the floor is met only by a thing joined to itself each time. Between the floor and the binary method the least count has
   gaps of its own (`MEDIUM_AND_GAPS.out`):

   | units | 7 | 15 | 16 | 23 | 31 | 32 | 60 | 64 | 127 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | the floor `⌈log₂ n⌉` | 3 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 6 | 6 | 7 |
   | the least joins | 4 | 5 | 4 | 6 | 7 | 5 | 7 | 6 | 10 |
   | the binary method | 4 | 6 | 4 | 7 | 8 | 5 | 8 | 6 | 12 |

2. **A prime no join produces** (`Built.prime_dvd_part`, `five_not_from_two_three`). Under multiplication a prime that divides
   a made thing divides a part. Octaves and fifths, `2` and `3`, never make a multiple of `5` at any depth, so the chord 4:5:6
   holds a note no stack of the other two reaches: its reed is supplied as a part or it is absent. A locale's primes are the
   parts it cannot make.
3. **Two coprime periods' gaps** (`frobenius_three_five`, Mathlib's `frobeniusNumber_pair`; Sylvester 1884). Periods `a` and
   `b` leave exactly `(a − 1)(b − 1)/2` sums they never make and make every sum past `ab − a − b`: for 3 and 5 the gaps are
   1, 2, 4 and 7, and for 4 and 5 they are 1, 2, 3, 6, 7 and 11.
4. **The floor of action** (0.7, `agent-inferred`). A join is a selection among what the pool holds, and a selection among `N`
   costs at least `ln N` nats, `k_B T ln 2` a bit to a thermal assembler (`floor.landauer-erasure`). So the least action a
   composition asks of a locale is its code length in that locale's parts: compression, read as causality.

## 6. Generalization is causal correspondence (weight 0.75)

A new composition `X` costs a reader with parts `B` its build `d_B(X)`. Two readers that code the same songs equally can
hold them differently. One holds the songs as parts: each is free, and a new song costs its whole depth. The other holds the
instrument, the reeds and the joins: a new song is a few joins. That is the guide's sentence that equal codes do not mean equal
knowledge, and it is the lessons' recitation counted as generation, stated as a cost. Familiarity with a locale is holding its
parts; beyond the locale stands a prime the reader lacks, and its price is the crossing that supplies it.

## 7. Genres: a pivot and a crossing

Two operations, and the Lean keeps them apart.

- **A pivot.** A made composition `C` taken as a part: `d_B(X) ≤ d_(B+C)(X) + d_B(C)` (`Reaches.sample`), and `C` itself is
  free over the new basis (`Reaches.made_is_free`). It makes nothing newly reachable (`Reaches.same_closure`); it moves what
  is near. Fifteen costs five joins and not the binary six because a made three is reused: `l(15) ≤ l(3) + l(5)`. Sampling
  is this, and a genre is a basis reached by it: a region of the possible made cheap.
- **A crossing.** A part no join of the locale produces arrives from another Holarchy. The harmonica's free reed came to Europe
  in the 1700s with the Chinese sheng, a mouth organ about three thousand years old, and the harmonica of the 1820s pivoted
  from it. A crossing enlarges what is reachable (`Reaches.mono`); a pivot only prices it.

In the words of category theory (Lawvere 1973): bases and compositions with the cost `d` form a category enriched in costs,
whose composition law is the inequality above. A genre is an object of it and a pivot an arrow.

**Budgeted limbs.** An instrument's parts are limbs with a reach: a reed sounds one note, a hole two, a mouth spans a few
holes, and one breath is the budget they share. What a player can sound at once is bounded by the channel's area and that flux.

## 9. What is not claimed

The bounds are theorems on a free join with every made thing kept; a real assembler also pays for holding its pool, and that
upkeep is no part of them. The identification of an Aeon's epochs with a build's joins, the action floor and the optical length
as a build are interpretations at their weights. The physics of §3 is standard. The guesses read her against the law and build
nothing.
