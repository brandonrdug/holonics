## What the elementary laws license

[proved-derived] For symmetric PSD `D`, positive weight and pair slip `J u`,

```text
P(u)=w (J u)*D(J u),       P(u)=0 ⇔ D J u=0.
```

No-slip follows when `ker D ∩ im J={0}`. The existing contact owner already supplies the
PSD null-vector argument; this pass binds it to `pairSlip`. A PSD response `diag(1,0,0)` with
slip `(0,1,0)` reads zero power but has nonzero slip. Consequently `pair_lock` remains a
kinematic test; `(p,q)=(0,0)` is the zero rate, not an address, and the positive `LockAddress`
chart cannot replace stationary/opposite-sign or higher-dimensional kernel cases.

[counterexample] At the common point `(1,0,0)`, rotation about z has velocity `(0,1,0)` and
acceleration `(-1,0,0)`. Translation along y has the same velocity and zero acceleration.
Thus `(p,q)=(1,1)` is an instantaneous lock, but its derivative does not preserve the lock.
Closure of a whole trajectory requires the actual finite action and clocks. Separately,
`RationalPhase.parameter` is a rational **Cayley half-angle coordinate**; it is not a rational
turn rate. A finite `Odometer` level requires a supplied and checked period. Nonclosing
transport retains its exact endpoint/lift and remains usable.

[proved-derived] The medium state and pair rate have different domains. With rate-port map C,
`M_medium=C*J*D J C`; the law `ż=(Ω−M_medium)Gz+Bu` dissipates at `J C Gz`. It does not by itself
assert `C Gz` is the physical configuration velocity. The adapter must exhibit that physical
identification or retain the local linearization scope. The field/current chart and its
geometric action also need a realization equation, not only matching dimensions.

[proved-derived] For `f=(Δ,Q,DQ)` and fixed generators,

```text
(D_θ f)*λ = J*λ_Δ + λ_Q DQ + (D²Q)*λ_DQ.
```

The existing `PairQuadranceJet::pullback` supplies only `λ_Q DQ`. The planned chart, generator,
material and clock variations are additional terms. Normal fitting of W at supplied f does
not infer nonlinear parameters of f. The full pair participation chart is explicitly
`−βQ/2`; its reduction to a bilinear score modulo a row constant requires equal self-energies.

[counterexample] Group inversion is not a generic adjoint. For `A=diag(2,1)` and reflector
`F=[[0,1],[1,0]]`, `A⁻¹FA=[[0,1/2],[2,0]]`, whereas `A*FA=[[0,2],[2,0]]` in the Euclidean
pairing. The permutation/isometric specialization identifies inverse and adjoint. The incident
word keeps its actual derivative adjoint at producing operands.

[proved-derived; formal-checked] A closed edge field has one harmonic representative **per
cohomology class** for its positive weighted complex. This iteration exposes uniqueness using
`HodgeReceiver.harmonic_representative_unique`. Harmonic dimension need not be one. Silence is
relative to the node-divergence and cell-flux receivers; a cycle can distinguish a nonzero

[counterexample; formal-checked] `I₂` and `J=[[1,1],[0,1]]` have identical trace powers and
transfer determinants: `tr(I₂^k)=tr(J^k)=2`, `det(1−T I₂)=det(1−T J)=(1−T)²`. But source
`e₂` followed by receiver `e₁*` reads `0` after I and `1` after J. This pass proves the power

supplied `PairFiniteMotion`s and optional periods. It creates a symmetric homogeneous moment,

differ. An oriented tensor offset `E(a)⊗E(b)*` versus `E(b)⊗E(a)*` retains this separator.
A fixed offset population is still only sufficient for the receivers/actions through which
it actually descends. The test uses the actual one-hot boundary domain, not a scalar surrogate.
