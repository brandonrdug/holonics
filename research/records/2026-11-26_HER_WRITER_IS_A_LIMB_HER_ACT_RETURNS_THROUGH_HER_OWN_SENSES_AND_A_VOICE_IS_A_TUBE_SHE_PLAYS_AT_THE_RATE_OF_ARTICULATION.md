## 2. On the objects

The engine as built keeps the act outside the learning. A lap reads her passage by two streams with every place given; no section holds a
place she wrote. Her writers (`write-next`, `write-forms`) read a saved state from outside it, as a ruler reads one. That is the poking: the
state is stimulated and its endpoint read, and nothing that it does returns to it.

A limb is a receiver of her own (the elementary objects: a receiver is a role of a participating Holon). Her act is an efference: a release
through the limb's ports. The limb's own constitution turns the act into a consequence in the medium. The consequence returns through the
same chart her senses read, and that return is the reafference. The comparison a limb makes possible is between the consequence her
efference copy predicts and the one that returns; what returns that her act does not explain is exafference, the world's answer, and that is
the news she learns from (`receipt.reafference-lag`: a receiver cancels its own release at its own clock's phase, and a lag leaves the chord
as news; `receipt.world-answers-own-act`; `receipt.probe-reads-its-loop`: a receiver's own small act, demodulated against itself, reads the
gain of the loop from its emission to its reception).

Where the limb is a discrete symbol, the efference copy and the return are the same symbol, and the comparison carries nothing. So
reafference asks for a limb with a body of its own: a constitution between the act and its return. The tree has held the first form of
her act inside her passage (`hnn.tower-looking`: the tower's own release re-enters a fork of its passage where it parts from the truth),

## 3. A voice is a tube

Brandon's own account of speaking is the instrument: the volume and cross-sectional areas of the throat and mouth modulate the wave that
propagates from the vibrating source. On the objects that is a chain of junctions (`wave.junction-reflection`: a junction reflects by its
impedance mismatch, and a tube's impedance steps with its area; `wave.junction-boost-delay-turn`: a chain of levels is a word of the swing,
each junction a boost of rapidity half the logarithm of its step and each section's delay a turn). A source of pulses at a pitch drives
the chain, and the chain's few slow controls (the pitch, the loudness, the areas of a handful of sections) move at the rate of
articulation, some hundred a second, while the chain's physics renders the samples.

What follows for her, as tests:
- Her act lives at the frame's clock (the paired slice's, 10 ms), a few places a frame (a pitch level, a loudness level, the areas'
  levels), on a motor chart that is a boundary codec like every other. The samples are the tube's, never drawn one by one by her face, so the
  static of a write drawn a sample at a time (her one-at-a-time writes) cannot arise from it.
- She hears herself through the wave chart: the tube's samples, charted at its 13 levels, re-enter her passage beside her act. Her face
  learns what her act will sound like (a forward model of her limb), and the mismatch between what she predicted and what returned is the
  comparison at her limb.
- Matching a held speaker is vocal learning: the speaker's sound is the world's, her own is the return of her act, and the difference
  between them is what she plays to close.
