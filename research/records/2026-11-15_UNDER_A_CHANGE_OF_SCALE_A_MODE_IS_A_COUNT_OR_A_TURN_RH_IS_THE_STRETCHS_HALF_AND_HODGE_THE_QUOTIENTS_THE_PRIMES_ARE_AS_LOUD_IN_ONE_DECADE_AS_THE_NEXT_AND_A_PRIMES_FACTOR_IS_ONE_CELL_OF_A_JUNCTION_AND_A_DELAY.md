## 3. The statement: a mode is a count or a turn

`[from training]` Scale and turn act together. The nonzero complex numbers are a scale times a
turn, `ℂ^× = ℝ_(>0) × U(1)`, and a Hodge structure is an action of that group on a rational
lattice's complexification: on the part `H^(p,q)` of level `w = p + q` the scale acts by a power
that `w` alone sets, and the turn `e^(iθ)` acts by `e^(i(p − q)θ)`. Over a finite field of `q`
elements the change of scale is Frobenius, and on level `w` each of its modes has modulus
`q^(w/2)` (Deligne 1974): the scale part is the level's and the rest is a turn.

`[tree]` The atlas holds the turn's half of this (`hodge.colourless-classes-weil`: the classes
the turn fixes) and the modulus (`key.scaled-form-places-modes`).

`[agent-inferred]` Put together on the objects, a mode under a change of scale is one of two
kinds.

- **A count.** It scales by a whole power and does not turn. The class of a cycle of codimension
  `k` is of type `(k, k)`, and Frobenius multiplies it by `q^k`: a thing of `d` dimensions over a
  field of `q` elements has about `q^d` points, and a count of points is what such a class is.
- **A turn.** It scales at half its level's rate and otherwise only turns.

The one-variable instance is the primes' own count (`rh.explicit-formula-limit`):

```
ψ(x) = x − Σ_ρ x^ρ/ρ − ln 2π − ½ ln(1 − x^(−2)).
```

One mode at rate 1 with no turn (the count: the pole of ζ, the level every prime shares, read as
one cell); one at rate 0 with no turn (the constant); and the zeros, each a turn of frequency `γ`
on the axis `u = ln x`, at rate ½ if and only if RH holds. A curve over a finite field reads the
same way, as a theorem: `N_n = qⁿ + 1 − Σ α_iⁿ`, two counts and `2g` turns at `q^(n/2)`
(`rh.prime-count-half-digits`).

`[agent-inferred]` The scale record's two operations then carry the two targets.

- **RH is the stretch's half.** On everything that is not a count, the stretch is a turn. No
  turning mode has a rate of its own. In the tree's rows: a zero pair is a null lock on the line
  and a boost off it, and RH says no pair is a boost.
- **Hodge is the quotient's half.** A class is what a quotient leaves of a cycle: homology merges
  every cycle with those it bounds against, and the harmonic representative of the class is what
  friction keeps (`hodge.least-norm`; `from training`: the heat flow of a cycle's current of
  integration tends to the harmonic form of its class). The conjecture says that each rational
  class with no turn that the quotient leaves is the remainder of a cycle that occurred. The same
  statement at Frobenius is Tate's.

What both exclude is a third kind of mode: a turning mode with a rate of its own, or a count that
is the count of nothing. That is the correction's second point on these two charts: no reading
without an antecedent. The lens's reason is one for both. A turning mode with a rate of its own
grows in one direction of scale and dies in the other, a privileged direction that nothing made;
a count with nothing counted is a consequence with no cause. The pairing that the record of November 10 found under both, positive on
what closed routes span, is what forbids the third kind where either statement is a theorem
(curves over finite fields; divisors).

## 4. The primes are as loud in one decade as in the next

`[from training]` Put the primes' fluctuation in the chart where a stretch keeps counts, the half
(`rh.mellin-half-density`), on the axis `u = ln x`:

```
E(u) = e^(−u/2) · (ψ(e^u) − e^u + ln 2π + ½ ln(1 − e^(−2u))) = −Σ_ρ e^((ρ − ½)u)/ρ.
```

- RH holds if and only if the power of `E` over one e-fold, `∫_U^(U+1) E² du`, grows slower than
  every power of the scale. One way: a zero of real part `β > ½` gives power growing as
  `e^((2β − 1)U)`. The other: a bound on that power makes the Mellin transform of `ψ(x) − x`
  converge for every real part above ½, where ζ then has no zero.
- Under RH the power does not grow at all in the mean, and its level is the zeros' own sum
  (Cramér 1922): `(1/U) ∫_0^U E² du → Σ_ρ m_ρ/|ρ|²`, which for simple zeros is
  `Σ_ρ 1/|ρ|² = 2 + γ − ln 4π`, near 0.0462.

  clock (Wintner 1935; Rubinstein and Sarnak 1994): what the primes do across one ratio of scale

## 5. A prime's factor is one cell: a junction and a delay

`[tree]` The scale record put a step of scale between two levels on (forward, backward) waves at
unit power: a junction `Z → bZ` is a boost `B(η)` of rapidity `η = ½ ln b`, which passes
`sech² η = 4b/(b + 1)²`, and a delay `φ` is a turn (`wave.junction-boost-delay-turn`).

`[agent-inferred]` Take one cell, a junction and then a delay, `C = B(η)·diag(e^(iφ), e^(−iφ))`,
and a tower of equal cells. Then

```
tr C = 2 cosh η cos φ = (b + 1) cos φ / √b,        det C = 1.
```

- The cell is one of the Swing's three kinds by its trace. It is a turn, of angle `θ` with
  `cos θ = cosh η cos φ`, when `|cos φ| < sech η = 2√b/(b + 1)`; a shear at equality; and a boost
  beyond. A wave goes down the tower when the cell is a turn, and dies by `e^(−κ)` a level, with
  `cosh κ = cosh η |cos φ|`, when it is a boost. The band's edge is the square root of the
  junction's pass.
- Scaled by the junction's own half, the cell has the characteristic polynomial
  `det(1 − T√b·C) = 1 − aT + bT²` with `a = (b + 1) cos φ = 2√b cos θ`. With `b = p` this is the
  factor a prime contributes to a curve's count (`bsd.hasse-site-rotation`), the cell's angle is
  the prime's angle, and Hasse's bound `|a_p| ≤ 2√p` says the cell is a turn: the bound is the
  tower's pass band.
- With no delay, or a half turn of it, `cos φ = ±1`: the cell stands as far outside the band as a
  cell can, and its roots have moduli 1 and `b`. These are the two counts. So the kinds of §3 are
  the tower's two regimes: a turning mode is a wave the tower carries, and a count is the mode of
  a tower with no delay, the mean, which every level shares. (The band's own edges,
  `|cos φ| = 2√b/(b + 1)`, are the shears, a double root of modulus `√b`.)

`[from training]` The tower is a tree read along its radius. On the tree with `b + 1` branches at
every site and equal lengths between sites, a wave that is the same on every branch meets at each

are the delays. The tree's walk has spectral radius `2√b/(b + 1)` (Kesten 1959), a network of

`[agent-inferred]` So three numbers the tree held in three places are one: the cover's radius

## 7. Hodge: a class is a count only at particular places

`[from training]` The smallest instance. For a torus `E_τ` the classes of the surface
`E_τ × E_τ` that are rational and of the surface's own type `(1, 1)` have rank
`2 + rank End(E_τ)`: three at a `τ` with no relation, and four if and only if
`aτ² + bτ + c = 0` for integers `(a, b, c)`, the places of complex multiplication. At such a
place the fourth class is made by a thing that occurs, the graph of the new endomorphism, and
there the conjecture is Lefschetz's theorem (`hodge.lefschetz-one-one-passage`). The places of
discriminant `D` number `h(D)` up to the modular group, each with `j(τ)` an algebraic number of
degree `h(D)`, and `Σ_(0 < −D ≤ X) h(D) ~ (π/(18 ζ(3)))·X^(3/2)` (Gauss; Lipschitz, Mertens,
Siegel).

`[tree]` In every polarized family the places where a flat rational class has type `(p, p)` form
an algebraic set (Cattani, Deligne and Kaplan 1995, held in the record of September 25). That
