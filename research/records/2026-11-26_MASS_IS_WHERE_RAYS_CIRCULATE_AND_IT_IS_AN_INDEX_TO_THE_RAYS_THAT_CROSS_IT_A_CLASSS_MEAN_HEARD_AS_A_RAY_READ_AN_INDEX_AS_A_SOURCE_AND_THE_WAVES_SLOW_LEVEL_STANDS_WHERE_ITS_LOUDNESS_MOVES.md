## 2. The physics, one propagation

- **An index is a medium's response, summed over what it holds.** A wave crossing a medium is delayed and turned by the medium's
  oscillators. Its index at one frequency is the sum of every resonance's response there, weighted by how much of it the medium
  holds (atlas `em.response-poles-zeros-gap`: a permittivity at one frequency is the whole spectrum read there). The delay and the
  absorption are the two parts of one causal response (`constitution.one-sided-response-is-fixed-by-its-face`).
- **A mass is such a medium.** Light passing a mass of `M` bends and slows as if in a medium of index `1 + 2GM/(rc²)`. On the null
  cone, matter is the term that focuses a congruence of rays (`coupling.raychaudhuri-riccati`: `B′ = −B² − R`), beside the shear,
  whose news delays the clock (`gr.null-clock-shear-delay`).
- **A mass is circulating propagation.** Radiation confined in a box at rest has inertia `E/c²`
  (`mass.confined-radiation`: massive and propagating are not disjoint kinds). A vortex is the case where the confinement is the
  motion's own: a circulation about a core. A wave crossing a lattice of vortices is delayed and deflected by their circulation,
  as by any moving medium.
- **Layers of vortices are each other's medium.** A slow circulation that carries fast ones inside it meets them, averaged over
  its own turn, as an index. The fast ones ride its phase. Cortical rhythms are this chart in a brain: the gamma bursts of a
  phoneme ride the phase of a slower theta rhythm that locks to the syllables of speech
  ([September 27](2026-09-27_THE_LEARNER_MUST_MOVE_A_CHASE_TERRAIN_DATA_AS_PARTICLES_WITH_FLUX_AND_RELEASE_AS_A_THRESHOLD_COMMIT.md):
  auditory cortex phase-locks to the rhythm of speech). The outer layer is the one whose rays cross every layer within it.

## 3. The law (weight 0.75, `agent-inferred`)

**Mass is where rays circulate, and to every ray that crosses it, it is an index.** A locale where many rays converge and
circulate (a grain of dynamics, a vortex, a landmark of a passage) holds their sum. Every other ray that passes it is delayed and
turned by it, by the locale's response at that ray's own frequency, summed over what it holds. A reader hears rays, so a corner's
capacity counts its distinguishable rays (the morning's 0.8 stands). Mass is what the medium does to the rays the reader hears
through. A layer meets the layers within it as such a medium, and the cortex is the layer that meets all of them.

On the engine (`agent-inferred`):

- A ray is a contact's path from a source site to a receiver: the address's hearing, selected by the receiver's lock.
- A grain of dynamics is a site where many receivers' rays land: a landmark, the face where navigator paths converge.
- An index is what prices a ray by what it crosses. Today a ray's falloff counts places: at a turned head, the octave of its
  distance in places, `ψ ⌊log₂ |i − j|⌋` (atlas `contact.friction-of-distance`). That is a uniform medium. At a still head, the
  falloff is the octave of the source's class count, `⌊log₂ c_j⌋`: a crowded class dilutes each of its rays, the one place where a
  mass enters a ray today.
- The lens asks for an optical length: a ray priced by the places it crosses, each weighed by what circulates there. The tree
  holds one such clock already, the news clock, whose stride is a place's code length (`hnn.tower-news-clock`): an index set by
  the news a place carried, which is the shear's part. Mass is the other part, set by the rays that converge at a place.
