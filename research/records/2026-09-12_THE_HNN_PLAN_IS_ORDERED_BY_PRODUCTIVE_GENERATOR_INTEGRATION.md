## Three explicit unifications needed by the implementation

[definition] **Compatibility and prediction.** A fibre of possible laws or currents is not
the empirical weighting of those possibilities. The implementation must compose its actual
source constraints with normal sufficient statistics or a normalized predictive receiver
under the task's admitted objective. Repeated data can change a statistical measurement
without creating a new independent mode. The existing Gibbs/KL, adjoint, normal and
normalized-native owners provide the laws; the current calibration does not bind that full
predictive assembly.

[definition] **Local generators and common ecology.** A homogeneous condition chart is a
legitimate instance, not the ontology of all HNN constituents. The first workshop binds
task-local source/condition/receiver maps; differently shaped terminal receivers already
work through the bilinear product core. New re-entry/composition domains require actual
rectangular maps. A universal alphabet width, padding or a source-name registry cannot
replace those maps. This is a concrete interface/constitution task within existing owners.

[definition] **Generative representation and execution history.** A programme can be
correct while expensive to reevaluate. Fixed actions can compose into resident matrix/factor
blocks; normal formation can close in sufficient statistics; dependent nonlinear material
may need a factored recurrence or joint constraints. Live source-return distinctions remain
through their actual maps. An arbitrary nonlinear programme is not assumed affine, and a
cache over its old sequence is not declared the compressed model.

## Effort and failure control

[project-postulate] The plan chooses the next work by the output it enables, its actual
dependency and the cost of testing it. Before a long run, specify the expected useful return,
the changed owner and a short discriminating case. Inspect the first actual result. If the
same structural failure remains, stop that exposure route and repair its construction rather
than buying more repetitions. A failed composition can direct valuable work; repeated proof
of an unchanged property does not become the product objective.

[established-bounded; source-inspected] The fixed-condition record measured 148,563 ms for
one 199-passage native field after projection reuse, while its complete state and bad output
remained unchanged. That is useful performance evidence at its scope, not a forecast for a
new model. It also shows why broad exposure is a poor first diagnostic when the source law
already proves collapse. The planned first cases use their declared small task domains and
measure new costs before increasing scale.

[definition] Formal research is attached to an exact missing construction: source/condition
closure, stable residual, legal rebase, compiled action or analytic decoder. Hodge, NS and RH
are available sources for those questions. A new attempt at the global Newman threshold is
not the immediate dependency for a caller receiving useful HNN mathematics or text. BSD has
no independent priority under this ruling. Existing work and evidence are preserved.
