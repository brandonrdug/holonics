# The level's inherited mass read at lap 17: an epoch's summed power reads its length, and within one length a louder epoch codes higher

November 15. Refs #404. This is a reading, run on the host from saved outputs while the card trained. It changes no state, and no
build depends on it. Receipts: [`receipts/2026-11-15_level-mass/`](receipts/2026-11-15_level-mass/g200.txt).

## 1. The question and the guess

The change-of-scale record of this day offered three readings for the level, and its third was **inherited mass**: a coarser site
opens at the summed lane power of the places under it, not at one, so an arrival turns it by one over that mass, and its slowness is
inherited (`resolvent.inherited-capacity`) rather than declared. The reading named before any build was whether the code at a
coarser site falls as the summed power under it rises. Its premise, that a loud place is a cheap one, was read on laps 14 to 17: per
place, loudness against code runs negative in code, web, science and books, and positive in wikidata.

**G200**, fixed before the read ([`g200_guess.txt`](receipts/2026-11-15_level-mass/g200_guess.txt)): at lap 17's mean, with each
held stretch cut into the level's epochs (the engine's `level_anchors`, section `log₂ 244` bits),
- (a) an epoch's summed lane power at the last stage runs against its places' mean code (Spearman negative) in at least four of
  the five slices;
- (b) within epochs of one length (2 to 64 places, ranked length by length and pooled), power a place runs against the mean code
  in at least four of the five slices.

A miss of (b) with (a) held says the summed mass reads only the epoch's length.

## 2. The ruler, and a premise corrected in it

The ruler joins `read-cavity`'s table (the `P` rows: one place open, every other written) with `read-loudness`'s by slice, stretch
and place. The guess's text named the news as `c0`, the built opening's code with no tick. That is not the level's news. The level
reads the opening's block (`Constitution::level_news`, from `opening_faces`), which is the table's `cO`. On lap 17 `c0` codes 11.1
to 12.2 bits a place, more than a flat face (7.93), so nearly every place clears the section and the epochs come out 1.6 places
long. `cO` codes 2.1 to 3.5 bits a place, and its epochs run 4.1 to 7.0 places. The reading below uses `cO`. The `c0` reading is kept
in the receipt as the guess's first wording, and it misses both parts too.

## 3. The reading (news `cO`, 17,910 epochs)

| Slice | Epochs | Mean length | (a) summed power vs code | Length vs code | (b) power a place vs code, within one length |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| books | 2,871 | 6.98 | −0.487 | −0.491 | −0.006 |
| code | 3,097 | 6.50 | −0.156 | −0.195 | +0.243 |
| science | 3,090 | 6.50 | −0.436 | −0.452 | +0.058 |
| web | 3,905 | 5.13 | −0.268 | −0.298 | +0.131 |
| wikidata | 4,947 | 4.09 | −0.065 | −0.184 | +0.435 |

(a) holds in all five slices and (b) misses in four (books flat). That is exactly the miss clause: the summed mass reads the epoch's
length (length against code is as negative as summed power against it, or more), and at one length the louder epoch codes higher.
Long epochs are cheap because the level's news, the cut's own clock, makes them long where little is news. Their summed power is
their count of places.

## 4. What follows

- **No build of inherited mass.** A coarser site opened at its places' summed power would carry its count, which the level already
  holds in its clock (the news between its sites). The power a place points the other way within a length.
- **The per-place premise is a mixture.** Loud places are cheap across the region because long, quiet-news epochs hold many places.
  Within an epoch's length, power marks the costly epochs, as it does per place in wikidata. Loudness beside surprise keeps its place
  as a reading of where the field spends (the rule of November 15); read at the level, it is spending, not inertia (`agent-inferred`).
- The change-of-scale record's first two readings (the quotient's kernel, the doubled symbols) stand as offered. This one is read and
  closed.
