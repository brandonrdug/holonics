## What is stored and why

[established-bounded; source-inspected] The actual redundant accumulation was in
`operative/factor_return.rs`: staging copied the previous return vector and pushed another
entry even for a pure current commit. Replacing the vector at publication did not make its
population bounded. The source-only pure-CSR path now omits these already-applied deltas.
Fields with an actual occurrence decoder and legacy unconstrained factor returns keep their
separate contract.

[definition] The continuing pair representation has one fixed template, the current positive
rho vector, the current factor and its error. It has no multiplicative-update ledger and no
list of previous amplitude gradients. Reconstructing from the template prevents repeated
rounding errors from being accumulated as an update history. Current normal statistics and
current q/b remain the continuing model. This fixes the number of pair-state coordinates;
parameter magnitude, admitted vocabulary and outstanding work still have their own costs.

An outstanding comparison still owns its producing source, receiver and temporary reverse
tape. The observed return or explicit release removes that comparison at both body and session
owners. The full nonlinear source tape has not been compressed by this change; its temporary
source-dependent cost remains an economy task. Past completed comparisons do not become a
second memory store under that name.

[definition] Rest stores the fixed template and current amplitudes, with optional fields for
old-wire compatibility. Cold validation checks positive point parameters, exact coefficient
projection, CSR/transpose template agreement including duplicate entries, and the resulting
map-error bound. Live old comparisons deserialize their own producing values. They are not
reinterpreted as contemporary parameters.
