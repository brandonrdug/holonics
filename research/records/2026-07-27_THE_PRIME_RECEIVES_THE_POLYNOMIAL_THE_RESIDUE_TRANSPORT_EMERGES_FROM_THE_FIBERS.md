## 1. The arithmetic source is multiplication, not a prime label

`ArithmeticFiberLaw` is a production `ExactEventLaw`. Its standing begins at
one and accepts only the next integer occurrence. When \(n\) enters, every

\[

when every capable axis has nonzero remainder. Thus primality enters as a
caused failure of prior multiplication to reach the new occurrence, not as an
application-authored Boolean label.

For

\[
n=\prod_{i=0}^{r}p_i^{e_i},
\]

the distinct support

\[

\]

owns one grade-\(r\) incidence cell. Its boundary is the alternating deletion
chain

\[

=
\sum_{i=0}^{r}(-1)^i
[p_0,\ldots,\widehat{p_i},\ldots,p_r].
\]

The engine validates \(\partial^2=0\). The exponents \(e_i\) remain exact
valuation multiplicities on that support. In particular, \(8=2^3\) inhabits
the existing prime-2 vertex with multiplicity three; it does not fabricate a
triangle from three copies of 2.

This separates two geometric roles which a scalar integer normally hides:

- squarefree support changes incidence grade; and
- prime power changes multiplicity inside an extant direction.

## 2. A prime is a receiver of a polynomial fiber

The first native arithmetic receiver family is

\[

- its exact residue in \(\mathbb F_p\);

\[

\quad\longleftrightarrow\quad

### Exact transport to prime powers

For a root \(r\) modulo \(p^k\), write

\[
f(r)=r^2-a,\qquad
b=\frac{f(r)}{p^k}\pmod p.
\]

A lift \(r+t p^k\) must satisfy

\[
