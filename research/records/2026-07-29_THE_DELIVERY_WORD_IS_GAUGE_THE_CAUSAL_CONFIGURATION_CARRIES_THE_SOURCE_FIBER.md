## Exact question

What common receiver-relative mechanism is exposed by the recent Erdős
unit-distance counterexample, the Jacobian-conjecture counterexample, the
failure of unknotting-number additivity, and Kimi K3; and how should that
mechanism change multimodal conditioning so that chronology remains physical
evidence and lineage without making corpus delivery or executor scheduling a
privileged order?

This record deposits the completed review and frames the construction which
would answer that question in production. It does not report that the
construction or the experiments below have run.

## I. The four external receipts

### 1. The Erdős counterexample: a planar membrane can carry a high-degree source

The result in
[Planar Point Sets with Many Unit Distances](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/74c24085-19b0-4534-9c90-465b8e29ad73/unit-distance-proof.pdf)
constructs a fixed `delta > 0` and infinitely many finite planar point sets
whose number of unit-distance pairs is at least `n^(1+delta)`. This disproves
the proposed `n^(1+o(1))` upper behavior.

The construction does not begin as a clever arrangement confined to the
visible plane. It uses:

- an infinite unramified tower of totally real fields with growing degree;
- fixed rational primes which split completely;
- CM extensions formed by adjoining `i`;
- many norm-one elements whose complex embeddings all lie on unit circles;
- a high-dimensional Minkowski lattice restricted through a bounded
  polydisc; and
- an injective projection through one complex coordinate into the visible
  copy of `C`, hence `R^2`.

The [companion remarks](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/74c24085-19b0-4534-9c90-465b8e29ad73/unit-distance-remarks.pdf)
make the methodological lesson unusually explicit: enormous algebraic degree
can be the source of a counterexample rather than dispensable complexity.

The planar unit edge is therefore an exact terminal receipt, but it is not the
complete caused object. Its unexpectedly dense incidence is carried by
simultaneous arithmetic embeddings and norm-one translations in the source
fiber.

For this laboratory, the correct retained object is the factorized generator:
field tower, prime splitting choices, ideal-class quotient, norm constraint,
embeddings, bounded window, and projection. Enumerating the final planar graph
would discard the construction which explains and compresses its incidence.
The theorem is an asymptotic existence result; it does not by itself provide a
cheap finite executor.

### 2. The Jacobian counterexample: local differential truth does not close the horizon

The explicit Alpöge--Fable polynomial map from `C^3` to `C^3` has constant
nonzero Jacobian determinant but is generically three-to-one. The
[Isabelle formalization](https://isa-afp.org/entries/Jacobian_Counterexample.html)
independently verifies the counterexample, while
[Graded Keller maps and the Jacobian Conjecture](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.20210)
analyzes its grading, generic cubic fiber, and global behavior.

Its mixed or hyperbolic weights permit nonproperness. Along the relevant
discriminant, would-be inverse branches can escape through infinity rather
than collide at an ordinary finite critical point. The local Jacobian receipt
therefore remains nonsingular while the global source-to-output relation
retains several sheets and has no polynomial inverse.

This is a direct receiver distinction:

- a differential receiver asking only for the local determinant receives a
  correct constant nonzero answer;
- a source-fiber receiver receives generic multiplicity three; and
- a projective-horizon receiver receives the nonproper escape which prevents
  the local receipts from gluing into one global inverse.

Scale has not merely become a large scalar. Positive and negative weights
rebase the coordinates into different modes while a missing global section
appears at the horizon. A production algebraic receiver must therefore retain
the map, its source fibers, grading, compactification boundary, and transport,
not only its value and derivative at a finite chart.

### 3. Knot additivity: the joined ecology admits paths absent from either summand

Mark Brittenham and Susan Hermiller prove in
[Unknotting number is not additive under connected sum](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.24088)
that there are knots `K_1` and `K_2` with

```text
u(K_1 # K_2) < u(K_1) + u(K_2).
```

For `K = 7_1 = T(2,7)`, `u(K)=3`, while a connected sum with its mirror has a
five-crossing-change unknotting route, strictly improving the separately
composed six-change route. The result is listed as forthcoming by the
[Annals of Mathematics](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/toappear).

This concerns unknotting number, not crossing-number additivity. The
distinction is structurally useful:

- a diagram crossing is a projection-local receipt retaining orientation and
  over/under depth;
- raw crossing count belongs to one diagram;
- crossing number already minimizes over a fiber of diagrams; and
- unknotting number minimizes crossing changes over diagrams, isotopies, and
  intermediate knots.

The connected sum is not merely two independent scalar costs placed beside
one another. Contact changes the transformation space and opens a path that
neither isolated summand exposes. Consequently, a lawful loss cannot in
general be formed by summing local crossing penalties.

One local crossing should retain its source arcs, orientation, depth relation,
receiver chart, and skein alternatives `L+`, `L-`, and `L0`. An unknotting
method is then a transported path through the diagram/isotopy fiber. Its
deterministic cost is receiver-relative to the admitted moves and available
charts.

### 4. Kimi K3: sequence, depth, and width are different transport dimensions

The [Kimi K3 report](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.24653) describes a 2.78
trillion parameter model with approximately 104.2 billion parameters active
per token, a one-million-token context, and a shared native vision/text
backbone. Its relevant separation is architectural:

- KDA layers carry order- and recency-sensitive recurrent delta updates;
- periodic Gated MLA layers provide global content interaction over the
  causal prefix without explicit positional encodings;
- Attention Residuals selectively revisit embeddings and earlier depth
  strata rather than compressing every derivation into one residual state;
- Stable LatentMoE combines full-width shared experts with sparse routed
  participation in a compact latent expert space; and
- multimodal documents and subtasks are interleaved during native
  conditioning.

No positional encoding is not orderlessness. KDA, the causal prefix, and
next-token serialization remain chronological. K3 instead demonstrates that
sequence transport, content association, derivation depth, and contextual
participation need not be collapsed into one homogeneous operation.

The result supports, but does not itself derive, the desired holonic
architecture: retained ecology may continue to grow while contemporary
participation becomes sparse and factorized. It does not establish a
minimal-data theorem for speech recognition and it does not make delivery
permutation invariant.

## II. Common mechanism

The common mechanism exposed by all four receipts is:

> An exact local or projected receipt is not false; it is incomplete. Global
> conduct lives in the compatible source sections, boundary or horizon
> behavior, and transported transformation paths which remain over that
> receipt.

| construction | local receipt | omitted fiber | global conduct |
|---|---|---|---|
| Erdős | planar unit edge | arithmetic embeddings and norm-one translations | superlinear visible incidence |
| Jacobian | constant nonzero determinant | three source sheets and nonproper horizon | local inverse without global inverse |
| knot | crossing in one diagram | diagrams, isotopies, and move paths | nonadditive unknotting route |
| Kimi K3 | current token/layer state | sequence, depth, and expert ecology | sparse selective retrieval and routing |

This refines rather than replaces the existing receiver doctrine. Projection
is a membrane; differential, diagram, pixel, token, and impedance faces are
lawful observations. None may silently become the complete world that caused
it.

## III. Four orders must not be conflated

The generalized training construction must distinguish:

1. **source chronology** -- the partial order of physical or logical cause;
2. **receiver-arrival chronology** -- propagation delay and each receiver's
   local clock;
3. **delivery order** -- file enumeration, modality batching, API arrival, or
   corpus storage order; and
4. **executor order** -- the CPU/GPU schedule used to realize ready work.

Source and receiver-arrival chronology are potential constituents of causal
geometry. Delivery and executor order are provenance and gauge unless the
world membrane explicitly declares that the arrival itself was a physical
receiver event.

The governing sentence is:

> Decouple administration from causation, not time from learning.

Offline delivery of a known earlier event may occur late without pretending
the event happened late. A live organism which actually receives evidence
later has undergone a different physical occurrence and may lawfully learn
differently.

## IV. Configuration-indexed standing

Let an event ecology be

```text
E = (events, causal precedence, conflict, receiver charts, local clocks, transports).
```

A configuration `C` is a downward-closed, conflict-free set of events. Its
standing is

```text
S(C) = Rest(Closure(coproduct over e in C of M(e))).
```

The coproduct juxtaposes inherited material. Only declared interactions make
contact.

Two events are independent only where the contemporary receiver ecology
establishes all of the following:

- neither event causally precedes the other;
- they are not conflicting alternatives;
- they act on disjoint supports, or their shared local interaction square
  commutes exactly; and
- exchanging their enactment preserves source fibers, multiplicities,
  lineage, and obstruction.

The exact diamond grade is

```text
Close_f(Close_e(S))  isomorphic to  Close_e(Close_f(S)).
```

This is receiver-relative independence, not one static global commutativity
table. If the diamond fails, the complete residual or holonomy remains and
the events acquire a dependency in that local ecology. Averaging the two
orders is forbidden.

A delivery permutation may populate the event registry in any order. Events
whose declared predecessors are absent remain pending. The production
machine enacts causally ready antichains, and an executor may vary scheduling
only inside an antichain whose independence is already structural or exactly
graded.

The prime and quintic experiments which reached the same contemporary
geometry under different inherited chronologies are bounded evidence that
such diamonds can close. Their distinct horn histories also show why
contemporary equality must not erase delivery and lineage testimony.

The current exact multimodal construction has already falsified two
representations:

1. independently enumerating every facet combination creates a Cartesian

2. closing all connected reusable facets into one `LiveConstituent` copies

The missing production representation is an exact factorized support family.
It should have the form

```text
F = (
    holistic base face,
```

Its compatible global sections are

```text
Sec(F) = { one germ from each required G_i satisfying every K_a } / Q.
```

`Sec(F)` is a mathematical family, not a vector which production must

realize those relations through exact finite fibers, CRT torsors, polynomial
ideals, incidence sheaves, or other declared algebra. No universal inner
solver is asserted.

## VII. Probability and loss

For a receiver `R`, query `q`, and open compatible prediction fiber
`F_R(q)`:

- possibility means membership in `F_R(q)`;
- deterministic loss is the complete failed gluing, inaccessible
  transformation path, residual, or topology change required by the return;
  and
- probability exists only after the contemporary receiver supplies an exact
  measure `mu_R`, such as recurrence population, capacity, symmetry orbit, or
  torsor measure.

Where such a measure exists,

```text
P_R(A | q) = mu_R(A intersect F_R(q)) / mu_R(F_R(q)).
```

The measure is not universal and the quotient does not replace the fiber.

The knot result supplies the clearest loss correction. Unknotting number is a
deterministic path cost through a diagram fiber, and its nonadditivity proves
that local costs cannot be summed before the joined transformation ecology is
formed. The Jacobian example shows a complementary failure: a
determinant-only receiver reports no obstruction while a fiber/horizon
receiver reports multiplicity and escape. The Erdős construction shows that a
large terminal edge count may emanate from a compact factorized source.

## VIII. Construction grades

### A. Factorized-carrier grades

Before returning to the full corpus, production must pass exact structural
grades:

1. For several small finite families, bounded exhaustive enumeration and the
   factorized carrier return exactly the same compatible global sections,
   multiplicities, transitions, and obstructions.
2. Permuting factor, compatibility, and lineage insertion produces identical
   canonical native words.
3. An unconstrained product has native extent proportional to the sum of its

5. A deliberately noncommuting shared-support pair retains its two order

6. Rest/remount preserves the complete factor family, lineage, symbolic
   population, and obstruction.

Failure of any grade rejects the carrier before a multimodal claim is made.

### B. Administrative-order experiment on the existing GRID world

The same 80 conditioning occurrences and 16 held returns should be delivered
through at least:

- source-file order;
- reverse file delivery;
- modality-batched delivery;
- delayed transcript and delayed acoustic delivery; and
- an adversarial fixed permutation.

Every schedule carries the same declared source chronology and
receiver-local clocks. The final contemporary standing, association topology,
prediction fibers, grades, and exact rest digest must be identical.
Delivery provenance may remain different.

GRID file enumeration does not itself declare a physical chronology between
independent utterances. Within each utterance, word, acoustic, and visual
source chronology and each receiver clock remain physical testimony. Reverse
delivery is therefore not reverse causality: an event whose actual
predecessors are absent remains pending, while incomparable utterances and
other independent material may close through ready antichains.

The counter-grade changes actual receiver chronology rather than file order.
Reversing or displacing the within-utterance acoustic, visual, or transcript
passage must change the appropriate paths, returns, or obstruction. If it
does not, the construction has erased time rather than decoupled
administration.

### C. Late insertion and local work

Withhold one causally earlier occurrence, deliver its later dependent
occurrences, then return the missing occurrence.

The machine must:

- keep blocked dependents out of completed standing;
- admit independent contemporaries;
- release and reform only the returned event's successor cone;
- converge to the same contemporary standing as complete causal delivery;
  and

Touched structural classes, rebuilt factor scopes, and shared untouched tree

Repeat the GRID conditioning run with audio, video, and transcript facets

The required evidence is not immediate speech recognition. It is:

- no Cartesian support enumeration;

If the joined evidence still does not refine the token fiber, that is a

## XI. Falsification conditions

The proposed construction is rejected if any of the following occurs:

- changing only delivery or executor order changes contemporary standing;
- changing physical chronology does not change a path which depends on it;
- a supposedly independent pair lacks an exact commuting diamond;
- the factorized carrier enumerates the Cartesian global-section product in
  production;
- local recurrence copies an entire connected ecology;
- late insertion requires rebuilding unrelated standing;
- an inherited target or published path is reported as machine-induced;
- a finite projection erases source multiplicity or horizon behavior; or
- an observer atlas, application table, or renderer owns behavior absent from
  the live carrier.

These conditions make the research frame testable. A broad or incorrect
multimodal prediction is not by itself a failure of chronology decoupling;
schedule dependence, lost lineage, false gluing, or monolithic support is.
