## 1. The word is a systolic pulse

- [proved-standard] A systolic array computes by rhythmic transport: operands pulse through a
  regular lattice of local cells, each cell multiplies and accumulates where two streams cross and
  passes its operands on, and nothing is fetched from a global store (Kung and Leiserson). Matrix
  multiplication is its canonical case: rows flow one way, columns the other, and each product
  accumulates where they meet.
- [agent-inferred] That is the HNN's word: a tick is a beat, a junction or pair contact is a cell,
  and the product happens where two currents cross (`hnn::propagation`'s junction-local solves, THE
  MACHINE's guard 14, "No global solve"). The hardware law (co-present regions execute together when
  their effects commute; shared immutable input with disjoint staged output) is the systolic
  condition stated on the objects.
- [proved-standard; source-inspected] Tensor cores and matrix units are systolic arrays, and their
  integer modes are exact: integer products accumulate in wider integers. An exact integer is limbs
  and carries, so an exact product of carried integers is a limb-wise integer multiply-accumulate
  followed by the carry cascade between limbs: circle plus carry, the odometer, the tower. Exact
  arithmetic on the card needs no floats. `holonics-cuda` uses no tensor-core path today (searched):
  this is a hardware item for U8, to be derived from the device's capacity, not hacked in.

## 2. Knots, the Möbius return, Cayley graphs, the golden winding

- **Torus knots are locks.** On a torus mesh the operand streams wind. Two streams with rational
  windings `(p, q)` close into a torus knot (the trefoil is `(2, 3)`) and meet at the same cells
  every period: they lock, which is resonance and the place a landmark forms (`HNN/Contact`'s Farey
  lock address, `Geometry/HolonicTorusKnots`).
- **The Möbius return is a half-turn.** A stream that returns with a half-twist comes back with its
  orientation reversed, `e^{iπ}`; in the product it is the pairing of a row with a column, the
  transpose.
- **"God's number" is the schedule's latency.** The number of beats a datum needs to reach any cell
  is the diameter of the Cayley graph of the schedule's moves (the September 28 optimal-policy record:
  the diameter is set by the elementary moves). The quoted labels stay labels.
- **The golden winding never locks.** The golden ratio is the hardest number to approximate by
  rationals (its continued fraction is all ones), so a golden winding spreads its crossings most
  evenly and never closes (atlas `rotation.golden-approximants`, `landmark.golden-mode`; KAM's golden
  tori are the last to break). It is the mixing schedule; rational windings are the locking schedule.
  Compression is the choice of which streams lock (landmarks, retained) and which mix (the
  residual, emanated).

## 3. A table is a navigator's resident face

- A precompute or normalization table is not the defect. The defects are a frozen float table and an
  authored table of a task's answers standing in for learning (the antipattern record).
- A lawful table is the resident face of a navigator at a declared grain: series coefficients held in
  the cells while the argument flows through them (Horner's rule is a systolic pipeline), a
  normalization computed as a systolic reduction over carried exponentials
  (`ratio::exponentiated::CarriedPower`: the largest carry subtracted, the remainders carried), and
  transcendental quantities as their generating navigators (π and `e` are navigators, CLAUDE.md).
  It is part of compression and terrain: the values are generated by active navigation, never stored
  as floats, and the coefficients are derived or located, never authored as answers.

## 4. Embedding dimensions

A dimension is not a hyperparameter to pick. The field's width has several readings: the number of
rings (the torus's dimension), the quadratures a node carries, the tower's levels, the Cayley
generators of the schedule. Which one a layout uses, and its size, follow from the device's capacity
(lanes, the tensor tile's shape) and the data's rank (the Hankel rank of what it must carry), by the
hardware law's "read the device capacity, derive layout and launch".
