Those propositions guide the research. The following is the present engine

\[

## 2. Counting precedes orientation

An oriented coefficient is now represented by two nonnegative populations:

\[
[p,n].
\]

The pairs

\[
(p+k,n+k)
\quad\text{and}\quad
(p,n)
\]

carry the same comparison. Their common population cancels canonically. This
is the group completion of counting:

\[
\mathbb N\times\mathbb N/{\sim}\;\cong\;\mathbb Z.
\]

The engine retains the two populations and derives the signed integer
\(p-n\) only when algebra requires it. Incidence cells do not receive private
numeric state.

This establishes the first part of the relational arithmetic ladder:

\[
\text{counts}
\longrightarrow
\text{oriented offsets}.
\]

Exact rational localization already exists in the engine through `Rat`, and
projective/cross-ratio realizations already exist in their declared receiver
contexts. This construction does not falsely identify every rational,
projective class, and cross-ratio with one carrier. The intended ladder is:

\[
\text{counts}
\to
\text{group completion}
\to
\text{localization by admitted nonzero factors}
\to
\text{projectivization}
\to
\text{ordered cross-ratio receipts}.
\]

Each arrow forgets or quotients particular structure. Therefore the source
population and the derived comparison must remain distinguishable.

## 4. Opposed ends cast a finite relative body

An "infinity" in this ontology is not a maximum scalar. It is an ideal end or
horizon class relative to a declared local body. Let an incoming section
\(E^-\) and outgoing section \(E^+\) have been transported into one region
\(U\). Let \(B_U\) be the contemporary boundary chain being compared. The
engine derives

\[
\Omega_U
=
B_U-E^++E^-.
\]

If

\[
\Omega_U=0,
\]

the boundary is exactly accounted for by the opposed ends in that region. In
one dimension this has the shape of the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus:

\[
\int_a^b df=f(b)-f(a).
\]

In higher chain degree it has the shape of Stokes:

\[
\langle d\omega,W\rangle
=
\langle\omega,\partial W\rangle.
\]

The analogy is structural: an interior law and an opposed boundary
difference are two received faces of one causal body. The implementation does
not claim a universal integration theorem for every engine species.

`compare_opposed_ends` currently requires both end sections to have already
entered the same region and chain grade. It does not fabricate a transport
between unrelated infinities. A nonzero \(\Omega_U\) is retained as an exact
obstruction chain rather than reduced to a loss percentage.

## 7. A receiver is a morphism, not a camera

For each source cell \(c\in S\), a receiver supplies an exact target chain
\(\Phi(c)\). Admission requires

\[
\partial\Phi(c)=\Phi(\partial c)
\]

for every cell in the source section. It also requires:

- exact agreement between the declared source support and the map domain;
- a boundary-closed source support;
- grade preservation for every nonzero image; and
- complete validation of both source and target complexes.

The observation receipt contains both:

\[
\Phi(\sigma)
\]

and the fiber map

\[
d\longmapsto
\{c\in\operatorname{supp}(\sigma):d\in\operatorname{supp}\Phi(c)\}.

Consequently, two distinct higher source faces may cast exactly the same

## 10. What exact cancellation presently means

The implemented cancellations are structural:

- common positive/negative occurrence populations cancel in group
  completion;
- equal and opposite terms cancel in a chain;
- equal monomials combine and cancel in a polynomial;
- the alternating simplex boundary cancels under \(\partial^2\); and
- a receiver must preserve those cancellations because it commutes with
  \(\partial\).

This makes recurrence and compression investigable as algebraic factorization
rather than bitmap reuse. It does not yet prove that every exact geometric
simulation is asymptotically cheaper than floating approximation. Nor does
it provide a general syzygy engine, canonical Gröbner basis, Rees algebra, or
kernel-specific far-field fold.

Those unknowns are now stated at the right level. They are questions about
the ideal, chain, transport, and receiver morphism carried by a contemporary
world—not invitations to substitute another planar fixture.

## 12. Research trajectory made executable

This construction makes the following investigations concrete without
asserting their answers in advance.

### Higher incidence versus lower observation

Construct arbitrary graded source ecologies, declare multiple exact receiver
morphisms, and compare equal terminal quotients through their different
fibers. The distinguishing evidence is the retained source topology, not a
pixel difference.

### Singular centers and directional horizons

Compare chain grade, ideal dimension, tangent dimension, and projective
horizon across smooth and singular homogeneous bodies. The distinguishing
evidence is a dimension or fiber jump certified by exact relations.

### Geometric induction and deduction

A deduction transports a proved relation through a validated morphism. An
inductive proposal founds a candidate higher cell, ideal relation, or
transport and asks whether all exact boundaries, incidences, and receiver
consequences compose. Failed composition remains an obstruction witness.

### Recurrence and computational factoring

Repeated causal material can be sought as common subchains, common
homogeneous factors, transported local algebras, or equal receiver sections
with different fibers. Any claimed reuse must reproduce complete direct
receipts. This defines what evidence can distinguish implicit recurrence from
false cache identity.

### Multidimensional input

An input occurrence can now be interpreted as an ordered algebraic deed or a
composition of elementary chain morphisms rather than a magnitude repeatedly
scaled by cursor distance. The source occurrence, noncommuting order,
boundary, and resulting quotient remain inspectable. This is the mathematical
substrate for a ratio- and traversal-oriented interface; this record does not
claim that the desktop interface has already been replaced.

### Probability and loss as geometry

A conditioned sample space may be represented as a caused region, a query as
a receiver morphism, an event as a subregion or section, and loss as the exact
residual or excluded fiber population relative to that conditioning. A
normalized rational measure is admissible only after the measure law and
denominator population are declared. The engine no longer needs to begin
with an ungrounded percentage.

These are now executable questions because the source can carry the higher
incidence and algebra which the observations compare. The current record
does not schedule a particular experiment, interface, renderer, or backend.
