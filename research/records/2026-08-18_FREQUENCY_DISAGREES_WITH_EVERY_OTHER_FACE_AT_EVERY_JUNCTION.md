# Frequency disagrees with every other face, at every junction

**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Truth status:** `established-bounded`. 180 of 180 plural junctions, measured today.
**Evidence:** `measured`. `soma/life/examples/the_faces_disagree_with_frequency.rs`.
**Provenance:** Brandon, 2026-08-18: *"you're still gating on pure recurrence then? That's
contaminated and malformed, it's not just about recurrence and you know that."* And the same ruling
already twice in the record — 2026-08-08, *"you've been masking frequency as 'recurrence'? That's
dumb. It's just frequency"*; 2026-08-16, *"'frequent' is not the only characteristic in the machine
that dictates whether or not it's relevant."*

---

## 1. The machine carries seven faces per continuation. A generator built this week read one.

`causal_language::ContinuationMember`:

```rust
token · horizon · multiplicity · horizon_profile · sources · gate_would_keep · name
```

with `horizon_profile` documented as *"every horizon this continuation is attested at, with the
multiplicity at each. The complete artifact, so a second receiver can be read off the same population
with no re-run"*, and the junction carrying `RatioFamily` and `SectionModulus` besides.

**The generator's reader was `max_by_key(multiplicity)`.** The horizon, the profile, the sources, the
ratios and the modulus were discarded — on the same day `exact_contact`, `RatioFamily` and
`SectionModulus` were being wired for exactly this reading.

## 2. Measured: the faces never agree

Four readings of one junction, all from the machine's own carriers — greatest multiplicity, greatest
number of distinct attesting depths, deepest licensing context, and greatest multiplicity at the
deepest support:

```text
  plural junctions visited        180
  scale breadth disagrees with Π  180  (100%)
  deepest support disagrees       180  (100%)
  deepest-Π disagrees             170  ( 94%)
```

**One hundred per cent.** There is no junction in this material where frequency stands in for the
others.

```text
  "`"   Π=1178  scale=2 depth=1   AGAINST   "a"   Π=403  scale=3 depth=2
  "`"   Π=1178  scale=4 depth=3   AGAINST   "#"   Π=6    scale=4 depth=3
```

**Frequency picks the backtick, every time.** `Π = 1178` in a markdown corpus, against a continuation
licensed by a context two tokens longer.

## 3. So the degradation had nothing to do with size, bound, or material

The emissions that locked into `` ` ` ` ** Authority :** ` line ; the ** Karhunen `` were **frequency
reading markdown punctuation.** The backtick and the double asterisk are the most frequent tokens in
this corpus, so a frequency reader emits them wherever the context permits, at any bound and at any
corpus size.

**And the previous return's diagnosis is withdrawn.** An n-gram recurrence table was reported —
8.3% of 4-grams recurring at this scale, 16.2% at 79× the material — and the statistics are correct
but they are **not** the explanation of the degradation. That answer replaced a real cause with a
frequency argument, while the error being explained was itself the frequency reading. The corpus-size
finding stands as a statement about n-gram statistics and stands withdrawn as a diagnosis.

## 4. What this does not license

**It does not license a better scalar.** Ranking by scale breadth, or by deepest support, or by any
combination, is another single face and the same defect one seat over — a scoring function is a
governor whatever it scores. The measurement's content is that **the population carries plural faces
and no one of them is the return**; `ContinuationReading` already returns the whole profile, and
`presentation_quotient::divide_junction` already divides it under a *declared family* rather than a
declared scalar.

The four readings above are diagnostics that exhibit the disagreement. They are not four candidate
governors.

## 5. Falsifiers

- A junction where every face agrees would make frequency a lawful stand-in there. Measured: none of
  180.
- If the disagreement vanishes on non-markdown material, the finding is about the corpus's syntax
  rather than about frequency, and it must be re-run on prose without markup to say which.
- A reading that combines faces into one number and reports it as the return is the same defect and
  must be refused on sight.
