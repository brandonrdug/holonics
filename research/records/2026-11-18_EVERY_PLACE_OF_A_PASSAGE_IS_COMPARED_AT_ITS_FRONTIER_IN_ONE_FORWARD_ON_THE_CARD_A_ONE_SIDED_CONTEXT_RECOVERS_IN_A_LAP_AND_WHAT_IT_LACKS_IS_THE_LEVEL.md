## 4. The second stream, its law and its card form

A passage read by two streams (`Region::strand`, atlas `tower.region-strand`): its sites, every one known, are the known stream, and
at each place an open place of a second stream reads what stands before it and is compared with the symbol there at weight one. Its
law, as built:

- its opening is the retarded cavity opening `E(∗) + E₋(u_(t−1)) + C s_t⁻`, with `s_t⁻` the known stream's retarded cavity moment
  of the place, which it also reads at every tick;
- its clock is the clock of the known site before it and one bare stride, at every tick. It is not read again from its own face.
  That costs at most the 0.013 of §3, and it makes the open places' clocks and moments the cavity's own, so the opening's forward and
  return serve the second stream whole;
- it hears the known sites before its place (its near field, then every one sharply), its strand mate is the known site before it,
  and it reads each circle as it stands at its place; no site hears it;
- on the return its moments' covectors are summed over the ticks and returned once through the cavity's return, its clock's covector
  goes to the site before it, a known site's drive takes both of its mates' terms, and a circle's operand takes the open place's term
  beside the known site's;
- where the field compares its opening, the strand compares the retarded one at every place.

The face at a place is the same bits as the open last place of the region that ends there, read as a known stream with that place's
clock bare. One forward of a passage compares every place at its own frontier.

On the card a strand is staged as its known stream's sites and then as many open places, each at the place of the known site of its
index (`site_place`, `region_heard`): the row kernels read the support, the gaps, the crowds and a still head's count by place, the
strand mates and the circles' scan are place-aware, and five small kernels set the open places' clocks and moments and take their
covectors back to their places. The cavity's forward and return were cut out of the opening's comparison as steps of their own.
Card against host (`Constitution::strand_pass`, `strand_returned`), bit for bit, on four fields each read once and grown to read
twice, passages of 2 to 300 places alone and in sections beside regions of the law and known streams: 16 cases, first run.

The pass carries it for the frontier's stretches (`frontier E strand`), a stretch counting its card sites (twice its places) in a
section's bounds. The held read writes the frontier's rows read by two streams (`S` rows): a slice's twenty thousand places take 0.08 s
where a region a place takes fifteen. The one-place writer writes such a state by two streams: the known places and one more site, the
face read at the open place of that last place.

## 11. The level as a passage read by two streams hears it

From §10: the known places must hear the level, a place can only hear epochs that ended before its own began, and the law's block
cannot be the one a one-sided stream reads through. The law, as built on the host (`Constitution::scale_forward_before`,
`scale_return_before`, `strand_level_cut`):

- the news of a place is the code of its symbol at the retarded opening's face, and an open place's is that face's expected code;
- a place is a site where no earlier place within a section of news carries news at least its own (`level_anchors_before`): what
  stands after a place has not arrived, so only the left half of the law's isolation is asked;
- the restriction is the law's, over each epoch's known sites, through the law's own `A`; an open place is restricted by no one;
- the level's field runs on its own clock with each epoch hearing only the epochs before it;
- a known site of an epoch past the first takes the epoch before its own, at its own news phase, through a block of its own, `B⁻`
  and `b_B⁻`, founded at zero after the glue (`Constitution::level_before`, `athena host complete-language open-level`); the open
  place at a place stands in the epoch of the place before it, at the phase its expected news carries it to, and takes the same
  epoch before.

A known site's state stays a function of the symbols up to its own, and an open place's of the symbols before its place. The cut and
the phases are data, as in the law: nothing returns to the news. The block is carried in the learner's state, so a state without it
reads as before and a state with it hears the level in its strands.

Host test (`the_level_a_strand_hears_is_the_epoch_before_and_its_covector_matches_the_codes_difference`): the founding keeps every
face of a strand and of the law, tiles the material and restores from its text; with the block drawn the level moves some face; a
place's face is the same bits in the passage cut after it; the law's own prolongation takes no covector from a strand; and 283

The card's form: a strand batch's level is cut on the host from the news the card reads (each place's code and its expected code),
