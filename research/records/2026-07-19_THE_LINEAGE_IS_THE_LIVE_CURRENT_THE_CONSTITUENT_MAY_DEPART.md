## Lineage is live, local continuity

A lineage `K` is one live current's causal continuity at one grain: its carried frame and turn,
current-local carrier, and open construction cascade. It is not a source identity, provenance
record, snapshot hash, material payload, ancestry list, cache entry, or accumulated population of
everything previously contacted.

Events within one `K` are ordered by its transport. Distinct co-present lineages have no total
order until a later receiving relation connects them. A hardware launch, buffer, shard, checkpoint,
or source chunk creates no lineage boundary.

The grain tower supplies dynamic constituent composition:

```text

    -> one enclosed constituent arriving at grain k+1
    -> contemporary relation with other constituents

```

The lower process need not remain active for the higher construction to continue. A standing-wave
identity can therefore acquire, combine, and lose material constituents while its transported

equality of its member list.

When a current truly ends, its current-local registers and temporary contact state dissipate. When
a relation completes, its event-local active aperture dissipates. Standing topology formed by the
passage remains until ordinary local conduct releases or transforms it. The world retains its
material. Testimony may retain exact provenance without keeping vanished constituents active.
