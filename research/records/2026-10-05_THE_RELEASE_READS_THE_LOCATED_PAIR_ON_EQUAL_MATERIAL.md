- **Swing.** Past the bifurcation the pump's period map boosts the in-phase axis (a growth above one
  is the lock). A candidate that fits the contact doubles its antecedent's image at every node, so
  the boost deepens at every member. The antecedent's copy carries its own line `δ` ticks on; on

## 3. The design, and its equations

**3.1 The closed pair contacts** (`hnn::prediction::closed_pairs`; the slip is
`hnn::executed::pair_slip`, the deposit's own; the declared port is
`hnn::constitution::declared_source_port`, which `Constitution::initial` now reads):

```text
C = { δ ∈ [1, d) : ∃ y, x  P^δ B e_y − (E − B) e_x = 0,  B e_y ≠ 0 }
B the field's declared source port E₀ (the sign sequence times ½), E the material's
```

Every opening closes none; lane B's deposited states close exactly `{2}`; a deposit at `δ = 3`
closes `{3}` (the owner's test). The closure is exact: a later deposit that moves a located column
reopens its contact.

**3.2 The pair storage** (`BankPlacement::pair_storage`). The candidate `x` at station `j` and every
placed crossing a closed contact joins to it, residues `c_p ≡ c_j ± δ` (a request phase, or a locked
station of the same release), as one population at the contact's own weight (Brandon's correction,
relayed October 5: the nearest lock decides because one scalar modulus contracts every datum by
`ρ^age`, while the located key's mode is a turn, `|μ| = 1`). The contact's channel is the turn the
deposit carried, `U = P^δ` in `(E − B) T = P^δ B` (`hnn::executed::pair_deposit`): a rotation of the
ring, stored and carried without loss. So the partner is read through it without the tube's `ρ^δ`,
and the neighbour, which no closed contact joins, is not read at all: it reaches the candidate only
through the contracting tube, whose modes carry no located key. No weight is chosen: `ν̂(N)` is the
population chart of the passage's one population at modulus one, the same chart every reading at
`ρ = 1` uses. The per-mode modulus is derived from the existing owners (the deposit's `U`, the ring's
rotation, the navigator's transport modulus) and needs no new law:

```text
z_pair(j, x)  = ν̂(N) ( P^(λ−c_j) E e_x + Σ_p P^(λ−c_p) E M[p] ),   N = 1 + Σ_p n(p)
z_alone(j, x) = ν̂(N) P^(λ−c_j) E e_x
x = f(a):  z_pair = ν̂(N) ( P^(λ−c_j) B e_x + 2 P^(λ−c_a) B e_a + P^(λ−c_a) (E − B) e_a )
```

The fit carries its antecedent's prior image a second time (the owner's test holds it exactly). A
station no closed contact joins to a placed crossing is not read; it waits for its antecedent (or
its consequence) to lock.

**3.3 The gain on equal material** (`prediction::gain`). At each member `m` of the bank the ratio of
the pair storage's growth to the column's alone, enclosed exactly, and the least over the members.
Equal material is read mode by mode: each member is one pump phase, reading the storage on its own
spectral line, and no scalar weight is set between members or between the two storages:

```text
Γ(j, x) = min_m [ L_pair,m / U_alone,m ,  U_pair,m / L_alone,m ]
```

A growth is a multiplier over the turn, so the gain is a ratio, and a class's own column enters both
of its terms. **Why the least member** [agent-inferred, from the development reading and the
distance's phase]: with the joint growth (the largest member) the antecedent's copy won at station
2 onward on the development seed (20 of 64 stations right, 0 of 8 whole; receipt
`development/pair_dev_joint_sections.txt`). The members read the storage on the quarter-turn lines,
where on the flat ring the distance's transport is `P^δ = e^(iω_m δ) = ±1` at `δ = 2`. The copy
`x = a` places `E e_a` and itself `δ` ticks apart: on the members where `P^δ = +1` it adds to itself,
on the others it cancels. The fit adds `B e_a` to itself on every line. Read along the target's
trajectory on the first development request (`pair-members`, receipt
`development/pair_members_dev.txt`), the copy's pair storage reads far above its column on the
Quarter and ThreeQuarters members and at or below it on Stand or Half at every station, so its least
ratio falls below one, while the fit's least ratio stays above every other class's (§5.3). The lock iteration is otherwise unchanged: the flip and the gap read `Γ`, the bank
locks on the pair storage's own joint growth (`lower > 1`), and each lock is certified there on
every member.

**3.4 One joint refinement.** Each refinement reads every open station joined to a placed crossing,
locks together the stations whose gap is not certified below the largest, and places them. In the
first refinement only stations 0 and 1 are joined (to the request's `τ − 1` and `τ`); a lock at
station `j` joins `j ± 2` in the next. Nothing is drawn and re-ingested, no whole output is
selected, nothing is compared with a target. A material that closes no contact is read by the
span's law, unchanged; the executed comparison (`hnn::executed`), which reads the span's joint
growths, refuses a material that closes one (typed), since it does not compare what the release
then executes.

## 6. What is not done, and the measured causes

- **The declared pair ports' own candidate term.** A field that declares offsets carries bilinear
  pair ports `E^(δ)` (`FieldMaterial::pair_port`); `BankPlacement` still places only the request's
  term through them (`z_pairs`), never a candidate's. The order-2 field declares none
  (`pair_port(0, δ)` is `None` for every `δ`), so the located key's contact is the source port's
  pair component, read by `closed_pairs`. Placing a candidate's own bilinear term is owed where a
  field declares offsets.
- **`ExecutionPort::release` is not folded** into this owner (the spine's S3 row): it still reads the
  receiving storage through `ReceivingPhases::read`.
- **No text release** (lesson 3's acceptance, one release on text and one non-text terrain): no key
  is located on text.
- **The alternation and the line** were not read: their keys stay alias classes (lane B), never one
  contact, and `closed_pairs` would close every alias distance whose deposit holds.
- **Distances the members cannot see.** On the flat ring, at `δ ≡ 0 (mod 4)` the transport is
  `P^δ = +1` on all four quarter-turn members, so the copy gains at every member as the fit does;
  the declared bank cannot resolve such a contact. A bank with members at other orders of `60`, or a
  ring with a half-turn holonomy (lane A's), would move those lines.

## 7. What lane C needs from lane A's Lock

The gain reads one reading per member of the bank on a given turn. From A's
`Lock { sheet, amplitude enclosure, certificate } | Unresolved`: the lock read **per member** (each
pump phase) on a storage's turn, with its amplitude enclosure, so the gain becomes each member's

`Unresolved` member leaves the candidate's gain unresolved there. The lock that decides the station

## 8. Owed in #62

1. **The pair storage's doubling**: with `(E − B) e_(f(y)) = P^δ B e_y`, the fit's pair storage holds
   `2 P^(λ−c_a) B e_a`, exactly (the owner's test states it).
2. **The members' phase on the contact**: in the square-law chart of a member's line `ω` (the bank
   reads a monodromy, not one line; this is its first-order face), the copy's pair term is
   `(1 + e^(iωδ)) Ê_a(ω)`, which vanishes where `e^(iωδ) = −1`, while the fit's carries `2 e^(iωδ) B̂_a(ω)`
   on every line; on the flat ring, at `δ ≡ 0 (mod 4)`, the quarter-turn members cannot separate
   them.
3. **The closed contacts are the deposit's distances**: after `pair_deposit` at `δ`, `closed_pairs`
   contains `δ`, and contains no other distance unless some prior column is a rotation of another.
