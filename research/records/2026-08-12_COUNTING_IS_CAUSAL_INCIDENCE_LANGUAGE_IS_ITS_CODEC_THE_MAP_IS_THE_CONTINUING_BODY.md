## Present question

What changes when arithmetic is no longer treated as a symbolic skill to be discovered from a
corpus, and language is no longer treated as the condition for intelligence?

The answer is a change of causal order. A thing exists in an ecology before it names the ecology.
It cannot avoid being interacted with. If interactions leave residue in its continuity, the
residue changes what later interaction can do. Discrete event population, recurrence, composition,
boundary, rate, and accumulated change therefore precede numerals, arithmetic notation, formal
languages, and reports about them. Mathematics is not supplied by a codec. A codec exposes one
chart of mathematical and physical dynamics which already occur.

This correction also changes the next useful experiment. The material should not be seven
hand-authored arithmetic laws wearing several surfaces. A Python corpus can instead expose
different causal constructions—iteration, recursion, rebinding, nesting, call and return—which may
produce equal exterior values while carrying different internal transport. Python is chosen only
as a comparatively simple material chart. No Python interpreter, AST, grammar table, keyword
taxonomy, or authored operational semantics may supply the machine's internal law.

## 1. Being precedes report

Brandon's correction is direct:

> *"As a thing that exists, you simply cannot avoid being interacted with, and if in your
> continuity the interactions have residue, then they determine future behavior, where residue is
> discretely founded."*

This gives the order:

```text
situated being
  -> unavoidable interaction
  -> a difference crosses a local boundary
  -> residue changes continuing standing
  -> later current meets changed standing
  -> a receiver may report one face of the passage.
```

The report is last. An intelligence is not founded by acquiring words for the earlier relations.
Language may amplify, stabilize, externalize, and recombine navigation, but it does not create
causal multiplicity, memory, comparison, anticipation, or changed conduct. Evolution produced
intelligent navigation before human language and without a symbolic mathematics curriculum.

This does not equate every interaction with consciousness. It removes language, introspective
report, and notation as necessary thresholds. The project's operational questions remain
receiver-indexed: which transports continue, which residues stand, which distinctions propagate,
which phase transitions occur, and which later currents ride the altered terrain.

## 2. Counting is the discrete face of incidence

Let `E_U` be the finite population of caused events crossing a declared receiver aperture `U`.
The receiver's count is

```text
N_U = |E_U|.
```

The event population is primary; `N_U` is its scalar shadow. A living body need not choose to
count, carry a numeral, or execute a counting procedure for multiplicity to affect it. Two
crossings and three crossings are different caused populations before any observer calls them two
or three.

Ordinary finite mathematics records structural consequences which do not depend on notation:

```text
disjoint composition:       |A disjoint-union B| = |A| + |B|
independent joint axes:     |A product B|        = |A| |B|
oriented returned change:   Delta N              = N_after - N_before
repeated composition:       T^n                  = T composed n times
finite variation:           Delta f(x)            = f(x + step) - f(x)
accumulated local change:   sum over a caused path or cell complex.
```

`proved-standard`: these are ordinary finite cardinality and finite-difference identities in the
displayed scope. `project-postulate`: their occurrence in a body is not a learned arithmetic
faculty. They are receiver faces of how events compose.

Frequency is a count of crossings or windings relative to a temporal aperture. Wavelength requires
a spatial or phase chart in which recurrence is placed. Their comparison requires a receiver and a
scale; neither the scalar nor its unit floats free of that relation. Where affine offset and scale
are chart gauge, an ordered cross-ratio can survive them:

```text
[x1,x2;x3,x4]
  = ((x3-x1)(x4-x2)) / ((x3-x2)(x4-x1)).
```

The invariant relation is not created by the coordinates used to present it. A physical momentum
claim requires the appropriate constitutive physical law and calibrated apparatus in addition to
the recurrence count; the project does not promote an analogy about taps into a new quantum law.

## 3. Calculus is continuing causal change

Calculus here is not first a notation of limits. It is the organization of how a contemporary
state differs from its predecessor and how that returned difference changes its successors.

For continuing standing `|B_t>` and a local current `|j_t>`, write

```text
|B_(t+1)> = T_(Lambda_t)(|B_t>, |j_t>),
|delta_t> = |B_(t+1)> - transported(|B_t>).
```

The difference is situated: it carries the cut crossed, hand, lineage, obstruction, and the
receiver under which subtraction is meaningful. Diffusion distributes such differences through
caused incidence under a constitutive law. Integration is the accumulated transported residue,
not an exterior faculty that decides to perform an integral. Differentiation is the distinction a
receiver can make between neighboring passages, not necessarily a written derivative.

Arithmetic, geometry, topology, and algorithms are thus different faces of causal composition:

- arithmetic reads multiplicity and composition;
- geometry reads placement, adjacency, orientation, and scale;
- topology reads what continues through recharting and deformation;
- an algorithm is an enacted state-transition geometry;
- calculus reads how these constructions change and accumulate.

A numeral, equation, proof language, or program is a codec through which those relations may be
presented. None is their ontological source.


source-detached generative transport, behavioral quotient, shortest interventions, mixed
interaction phase, exact ablation, and rebase. Its limitation belongs to the declaration: four

The correction is methodological:

- an archetypal example poses a family; it does not define the entire experiment;
- a declared fixture may test apparatus, but cannot stand for emergent ecology;
- the qualitative return must be designed into what the apparatus can measure before execution;
- do not report a deliberately bounded absence as a machine deficiency;
