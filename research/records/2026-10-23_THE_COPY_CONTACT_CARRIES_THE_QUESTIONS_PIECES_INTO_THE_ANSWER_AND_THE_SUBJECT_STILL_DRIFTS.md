## The failure this step works on

The gated tower reading the founded units continues the first held-out question ("John volunteers
at a shelter twice a month for 3 hours at a time.  How many hours does he volunteer per year?") in
sentences with their grammar, but no sample answers about John: each opens a new subject within its
first line, and the arithmetic is wrong. The tower's modes fade, and nothing in it holds what the
question just said: over eight key streams its 16 samples write `shelter`, `volunte`,
`3 hours at a time`, `a month` and `How many hours does he` zero times each. This step works on
the first of the two failures: reuse of what the question said.

## The copy contact

**Law** (atlas `hnn.unit-copy-contact`; the consumer is
`examples/solenoidal_alpha/complete_language/unit_copy.rs`). A unit's index is written in
`K = ⌈log₄ A⌉` base-four cells, high first (`hnn::tower::cell_phases`: a byte takes four, the 1,318
units take six). The tower's cell face at phase `k` is the mass of its class face under the prefix
already drawn, split by the next cell (`TowerFace::cell_masses`, now for any alphabet; a cell past
the alphabet holds no mass and is never released). At each cell the keyed join with no modes
(`hnn::keyed_modes`) reads the tower's cell face `q` together with the passage tree
(`hnn::passage`) over the cells already taken:

```text
p̂(d) ∝ q_d 2^(α_e f_d + α_b [d = ĉ]),      f_d = log₂ p_loc(d) − log₂ q_d
```

`f` is the passage's news over the tower at its evidence depth `e`, and `ĉ` is the node lock: the
class that followed the deepest recurred context's last visit, in its bin `b` (the octave of the
run of correct locks and the quarter of the locating depth). The weights open at zero, where the
joined face is the tower's, and take the Newton steps of `hnn.passage-weight-newton` and
`hnn.passage-node-lock` every 1,024 cells; the cell's code is convex in the weights, so the step
descends. The tower carries what its modes hold; the passage carries what this passage itself
said, so the continuation can take its pieces from the prompt.

The reading is prequential: each cell's face is read before its class arrives, then the weights
receive the comparison and the passage counts the class. A sample forks the frozen tower and the
frozen weights. The passage opens empty at the prompt and receives it; each released cell is
drawn exactly from the joined face, enters the passage's context and is not counted (a release
read back as evidence is reafference, and the passage then repeats what it released).

Nothing here is text-specific: the cell chart reads any alphabet of classes, and the passage reads
cells of whatever the units encode. Depth 24 cells (four units) and the horizon `2^12` cells are
the byte route's declared values; depth 48 was measured against 24 and is lower on no window
(below).

## The recorded failures this design could repeat

- **An authored routine standing in for learning** (a copier). The lock is the passage tree's
  located equality, found by loop closure over the machine's own passage, and it enters only
  through a learned weight per bin; at zero weights the face is the tower's. It computes no answer
  and holds no table of contexts beyond the passage's decayed counts.
- **Recitation counted as generation.** The copied runs are measured (below): the longest run
  found verbatim in the training text is 28 bytes, and the longest taken from the prompt 47. The
  prompt's pieces are what this step is for and are reported as reuse, not as composition.
- **Seen material graded as unseen.** The tower is trained on the training partition only. The
  join's weights are learned during the prequential read of the held-out problems, which include
  the prompt's problem; they are 138 weights on evidence depths and lock bins, and the passage
  opens empty at the prompt, so no text of that problem reaches a sample.
- **Bits read as progress.** The code below is a reading beside the samples. The samples are the
  result, and they are shown whole.
- **A located cause carried unrepaired.** The arithmetic stays wrong; this step does not touch it,
  and says so.

## Measured

All 747 held-out problems (397,669 bytes, 151,684 units), the units' gated tower after its third
pass (state `2bf016e3…`), bits a byte of the held-out text:

| reading | all | questions | answer prose | digits | markup | lock right |

| joined, depth 24 | 1.587 | 1.804 | 1.264 | 2.339 | 1.400 | 66.3% of cells |

The joined code is 13.6% below the tower's: 26.7% in the answer prose, where an answer restates
its question's names and quantities, and 6.3% in the questions. At depth 48 the read reached its
deadline at 98,305 units; across its twelve completed windows it reads above depth 24 at every one
(1.663 against 1.643 in the first, 1.539 against 1.534 in the twelfth), so depth 24 stands and 48
is not rerun. The depth-24 read took 125.6 s (about 0.8 ms a unit) at 322 MB.

The weights learned on the held-out read put the evidence on the two shallowest depths (0.977 and
0.882, then 0.04 to 0.26 deeper) and the lock on its long runs: a lock that has been right for 16
or more cells in a row weighs 1.05 to 1.54 at the deepest located contexts, and a lock read just
after a miss weighs between −0.17 and 0.04.

**Why the subject still drifts** (agent-inferred, from the law, not measured). `John` is the
prompt's first unit, and the passage receives it under an empty context; the answer opens after
`?\n`, a context the passage has never visited before. No recurred context at the answer's opening
names the subject, so the lock cannot carry it, and the tower, whose modes do not hold the name
forty units back, opens a name of its own. This is the next step's subject.

## Not claimed

The join is the keyed join of the node-lock record with the units' tower face in place of the
route's, read in the cell chart; the copy is a classical match model's (a PPM-style deepest
context with its successor), weighted by learned exponents. A lower code is a reading beside the
samples, not their success. Nothing here grades a generation against an answer, and the arithmetic
is unchanged.
