## Formal law

[proved-derived; formal-checked] `Computation/HolonicResidentRestriction.lean` defines compact
rows, zero insertion, contraction and a packed state comprising immutable base plus full overlay.
`decodeState_packedSuccessor` proves that decoding commutes with a concrete additive deposit;
`decodeState_runPacked` extends this to arbitrary finite ordered deposit words. A nonzero deposit
on an omitted base row survives decoding; a receiver reduction that silently keeps that row zero
is refuted. The definitions do not require a blanket noncomputable section.

[definition] The theorem concerns exact finite algebra and the stated additive generator. It
does not prove the Rust/CUDA program, derive every nonlinear numerical adjoint, establish arbitrary
foreign fidelity, or supply dependency-driven incremental execution.
