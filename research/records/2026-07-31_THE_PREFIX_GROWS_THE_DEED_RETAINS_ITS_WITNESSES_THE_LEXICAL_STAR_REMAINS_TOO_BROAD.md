# The prefix grows; the deed retains its witnesses; the lexical star remains too broad

Date: 2026-07-31  
Status: completed production construction, real-lineage experiment, and exact obstruction

## Question

The information-theory standard in
`2026-07-31_THE_RETURN_CHANGES_THE_CONTINUATION_FIBER_THE_INTERMEDIATE_BODY_IS_THE_LOCAL_INFORMATION_LAW.md`
required a serious first experiment:

> When successively larger portions of the real Codex dialogue are admitted, does the current
> research intelligence recruit work through the reached local causal star, preserve the actual
> generated deed and its witnesses, and expose any remaining dependence on corpus extent?

The competing claims were not “generation works” and “generation does not work.” Generation was
already in standing. The alternatives were

1. the admitted corpus can grow while active consequence formation remains receiver-local; or
2. hidden global dependence changes the selected deed or causes work to follow total corpus
   extent without an inspectable path.

The result separates three currents which would be obscured by one latency number:

- construction of indexed dialogue standing;
- recruitment of candidate dialogue sections into leader membranes; and
- downstream relational thought and emitted consequence.

## The obstruction exposed by the same run

The stronger claim that recruitment is already scale-independent is false.

The five leaders perform the same 34 feature-address lookups, but the postings behind those
addresses grow with the corpus. At the full prefix they cause 8,748 candidate visits over 2,701
unique dialogue sections. The exact sparse incidence atlas avoids scanning all 11,795 sections,
but its current lexical star still reaches

\[
\frac{2701}{11795}\approx 22.9\%
\]

of the complete local dialogue surface for a question whose final witness contains no dialogue.
The decimal is only this declared observer quotient; the exact receipt retains `2701/11795`.

The finite dialogue aperture then emits 40 return visits representing 24 unique dialogue passage
identities. Because each fresh `LaboratoryResearchEcology` currently commits every returned
section immediately, those 24 passages condition persistent morphology even though none belongs
to the selected minimal witness family.

The precise issue is therefore not an all-corpus clause scan and not failure of generation. It is

> broad union-based lexical recruitment followed by commitment-before-witness.

The current dialogue lookup forms approximately

\[
S(R)=\bigcup_{f\in R} I(f),

\]

sorts the resulting sections by local match count and chronology, and returns a finite aperture.
Common features make \(S(R)\) wide. The aperture bounds material admitted per leader, but it does
not factor the growing postings and it treats candidate contact as sufficient cause for continuing
conditioning.

## Derived production correction

This experiment opens one concrete construction, not a new universal-law question.

### Factor the receptive star

The feature atlas should retain exact intersections and shared posting bodies for discriminating
feature combinations rather than repeatedly materializing a broad union. One target form is

\[
I(R)=
\bigcup_{K\in\operatorname{MinCover}(R)}
\bigcap_{f\in K} I(f).

\]

where `MinCover` is founded by the receiver's clause and entity morphology, not by an externally
assigned inverse-frequency score. A common high-incidence face may remain a factored base section
until paired with a discriminating face. Complete single-feature alternatives remain lineage;
they are not declared false merely because they are not made resident.

### Separate provisional contact from continuing cultivation

A source section which crosses a leader membrane should first inhabit that question's provisional
current. It becomes continuing conditioned standing when it

- belongs to a minimal closed witness family;
- supports a still-open front which survives rest; or
- returns an obstruction which changes the morphology.

Sections which did none of these remain inherited and addressable in `ExactResearchWorld`; they
are not deleted, but mere lexical contact no longer trains the continuing relation body. The
generated answer itself remains a caused return and may condition later conduct.

This is not a compensatory relevance gate. It is the missing transaction boundary between world
reference and training already required by the information standard.

## Nonclaims

- This does not establish a corpus-independent query complexity bound.
- Candidate visits are logical receipt counts, not bytes, cache misses, joules, or GPU work.
- The elapsed measurements are one machine realization, not hardware-independent laws.
- The invariant answer is one real executable deed, not general language breadth.
- Excluding dialogue from this witness family does not make the dialogue globally irrelevant; it
  is irrelevant to this returned deed under this receiver.
- The proposed receptive-star factorization must preserve complete alternatives and be graded
  against the current full result before it may replace production behavior.
