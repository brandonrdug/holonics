# Bounded Lorentzian ratios, coefficient gauges and Hodge bounds

[established-bounded; source-inspected] The supplied paper is Bathija, Rohatgi and Soskin,
[Bounded ratios for Lorentzian polynomials](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.05341v2), v2,
September 8, 2026. The relevance review inspected the definitions, Theorems A–C, affine-quotient
lemmas 2.9–2.12, and the stated optimization/normalization. It is not a full independent proof
audit. V2 extends the sharp ternary result beyond the cubic case described in v1.

[proved-standard; source-inspected] For positive coefficients of a strictly Lorentzian
homogeneous polynomial, the paper characterizes bounded coefficient ratios by balanced
exponents and their nonnegative pairing with every M-convex valuation. The dual construction
quotients affine functions. For three variables and degree n≥3, triangular ratios generate
the bounded-ratio cone and Theorem B determines sharp constants. One cubic example in raw
coefficient convention is `P_300 P_111 ≤ (4/3) P_210 P_201`.

[proved-standard; source-inspected] Theorem C compares **bounded-ratio cones** for all
Lorentzian forms and products of nonnegative linear forms. They coincide exactly when n=2,
k=2, or k=3 with n≤5. This is not an equality of polynomial families, a statement that every
Lorentzian polynomial factors, or an automatic equality of every extremal constant on the
two families. Factorial coefficient normalizations affect the sharp constants.

## The direct Holonics bridge

[proved-derived] Write `R_γ(P)=∏_α P_α^(γ_α)`, where every multi-index has total degree n>0.
The balance `Σ_α γ_α α=0` implies `Σ_α γ_α=0`. Under positive coefficient/variable rebase

`P'_α=exp(b+a·α) P_α`,

`ln R_γ(P')=ln R_γ(P)+b Σγ_α+a·Σγ_αα=ln R_γ(P)`.

Thus the ratio is an affine-gauge-invariant log contrast. If `p_α=P_α/Z` and
`ell_α=-log₂ p_α`, then `Σγ_α ell_α=-log₂R_γ(P)` and a ratio bound becomes a code-cost
contrast bound. The balanced ratio can stay fixed while the full normalized distribution
changes: its gauge is receiver-relative, not equality of all predictive conduct.
