# Scaled integer temporaries preserve the normal fit

The all-real empirical material still stores exact rational H, B, target energy, applied M
and the realization error against the ORIGINAL H/B. Only the temporary bounded dyadic
proposal changes representation. For S=2^b, encode x as trunc0(S*x); multiplication is
trunc0(nx*ny/S), division trunc0(nx*S/ny), and integer sums are exact. BigInt avoids a new
finite machine-width assumption. The original nested rounding, LDL/solve order, strict
positive diagonal and full realification majorant 2m/S remain unchanged. An all-real check
includes the old applied imaginary entries. Complex material retains the existing generic
rational-grid path. Decoding the final real coefficients does not change the exact residual,
normal-fit realization enclosure, existing range checks or staged publication owner.

This is execution reuse under the existing selected response law, not a different fitting
clock, optimizer, physical update or whole-section score veto. It does not delay observations
or claim that normal-fit realization is a universal loss-decrease condition.

Five alternating matched fits on the CURRENT 25-dimensional production geometry took
23,857,521–24,012,571 ns with rational-grid temporaries and 5,944,553–5,978,907 ns with integer
temporaries. Every full material and cold byte matched. Each fit replaces 8,725 rational
grid calls with 725 encodes, 7,600 integer multiplies, 400 integer divisions and 100 decoded
coefficients. The unchanged true-residual calculation still takes about 5.6 ms. This is not
a comparison against the older 50-dimensional block calculation, a sustained throughput
measurement, learned compression or a language result.

The existing shared 40-class command, including its declared cuts and cold checks, measured
2,356,538,072 -> 1,606,373,141 ns on host and 2,479,261,140 -> 1,742,718,488 ns with CUDA
forward/return/aggregate. All ten complete saved checkpoint files match their prior bytes;
non-timing epoch payloads also match. The shared card control proves actual 40 forwards,
40 retained returns and 4 aggregates; a separate actual late-card fault preserves rollback.
The comparison is sequential same-work CODE evidence, not a sustained larger-data estimate
or a claim that CUDA is faster than host. Across 38 fits the CUDA factor+solve window falls
from 561,749,721 to 13,810,995 ns; the exact residual remains 201,103,893 ns and the unchanged
comparison batch remains 527,539,667 ns. Those remaining costs must stay visible.

Focused controls cover signed truncation, 512-bit intermediates, precision changes, the
complex fallback, positive-diagonal and late residual-range refusal, exact H/B/C/M/error,
cold state and staged rollback. An initial negative range fixture did not actually exceed
its bound; its failed receipt is retained and a genuinely late-refusing operand replaces it.

Transient measurements distinguish source preparation, native execution and actual material
observation. Context sealing/contraction nests in source preparation. Accumulation and total
fit nest in actual observation; grid/factor/solve/true-residual nest in total fit. These are
clock readings outside native/material codecs, not additive quantities across nesting levels.
Native execution includes shared preparation and transport, not only a GPU kernel.
