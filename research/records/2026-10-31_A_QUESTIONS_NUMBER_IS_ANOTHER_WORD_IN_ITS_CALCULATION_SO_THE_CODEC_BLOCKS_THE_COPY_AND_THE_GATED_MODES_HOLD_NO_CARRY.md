# A question's number is another word in its calculation, so the codec blocks the copy, and the gated modes hold no carry

October 31. Refs #404 #406 #405. Status: [measured] for the counts and the readings cited with
their receipts, [derived] for what follows from the declared law and the code, [agent-inferred] for
each choice. Claude (opus), design lead.

Computational object: the helical pair interaction. The review touches faces and placement (the
codec's words, where a number sits and whether two places meet in one face), the helix (a digit's
turn and the carry that is its winding), the pair (the contacts and participation's pairing) and the
tube (the section the covector crosses). Cell holonomy and the tower thread stay as the working
declaration has them.

## 1. Why this review

From October 27 to 31 the race changed what ran (the heading, the credits, the pair contact, the
split, participation, the rate) and read each change by bits a byte, then by the GSM8K receipt
(record `2026-10-30_THE_COARSE_LEVEL_HEADS…`, §43 to §45). Two readings never moved under any of
them: the answer's first calculation takes the question's numbers in some 7 to 9 of 120 releases
(the references 92 of 125), and a calculation the learner never heard is written exactly in 2 to 5
of 139, as the n-grams write it. Brandon asked for the design to be reviewed so the work goes at the
blockers instead of around them, and for the open items to be dealt with rather than listed. This
record asks why the declared law cannot carry either reading, from the theory and the code, and
fixes the plan and its acceptance before anything runs.

## 3. The first blocker: the question's numbers do not reach the first calculation

**What the step needs.** Writing `48/2` after a question that says 48 is a copy: a place in the
answer reads the place in the question where 48 stands and writes what stands there. A copy is a
located equality: the answer's open face meets the question's face where the two agree.

**[measured] The codec writes one number as two words.** The founding joins a pair of units
wherever joining codes the passage shorter, and digits stand beside marks and spaces often enough
to be joined to them: 113 of the 1,062 merges hold a digit, among them `<<4`, `2=`, `8 `, `24>>`,
`12>>12` and `120>>12`. Natalia's 48 reads `4|8 ` in her question and `<<4|8` in her calculation.
Over the first 1,500 training problems, of the 2,287 first-calculation operands that the question
writes as numerals, 552 (24.1%) are the same unit word in the question and in the calculation;
1,735 are not. Read in the passage's bytes, all 2,287 are the same word (receipts `RUN.md`).

[derived] So at the codec's grain there is no equality to locate for three numbers in four: the
learner can reach the calculation only by learning, digit by digit and context by context, which
unit of one family stands for which unit of another, and a literal copy of the question's unit
writes the question's context (its trailing space) into the calculation. This holds whatever reads
the passage: the modes, participation, the heading or a larger body.

**[measured] The history of this cause.** On October 26 a foil of the founding with every
digit-bearing merge removed (record `2026-10-26_THE_DIGIT_PIECES_ARE_NOT_THE_CAUSE…`) took the first
calculation from the question in 49 of 618 releases against the committed units' 25 of 637, on
plain two-level towers. That test's decision was fixed on another reading (heard calculations
written back at the equals), so this reading was set aside and the committed units stayed. On
October 31 (§33) the race moved from bytes to these units because they led in bits a byte early in a
run, the one reading the receipt was built to stop relying on. [agent-inferred] The foil itself is
not the repair: a founding rule that names digits is text-specific. The radix-move record
(`2026-10-29_A_VALUE_IS_A_RADIX_MOVE…`, §1) already states the lawful form: the founded units are
the static first case of the packet law, and the news clock founds packets online from the field's
own prediction, which the working declaration runs at levels 4 and 5. Read in bytes, every number
is the same word everywhere, and the clocked levels found its packets.

**[derived] The copy route exists and has had nothing to find.** Participation
(`hnn::tower::participation`) probes a section's earlier sites with
`z_ij = β Re⟨conj(U_i) Ȟ x_i, conj(U_j) H x_j⟩ − κ(L̄_i − L̄_j)` and returns their current
`conj(U_j) Ψ x_j`, where `x_j` is the level's input at site `j`. At level 0 that input is the
arriving unit's encoding alone, so a level-0 head can find an earlier equal unit but returns that
same unit, not the one after it. From level 1 up a site's input carries the lower levels' read of
the units before it, so a head can find the site whose predecessor equals the present unit and
return what stood there: the copy. That route was joined from level 0 up on October 31 (§44) and
moved only recalled calculations, on the welded codec. [agent-inferred] On bytes an arrival level's
section of 128 bytes does not reach back from a first calculation to its question (a question is a
median 208 bytes), while a clocked level's 128 ticks span several hundred bytes (§38; the
radix-move record's consumer), so the route that reaches is participation at the clocked levels,
whose sites are the packets the news clock founds.

## 4. The second blocker: unheard arithmetic stays at the n-grams' level

**[measured] The same codec writes results as words of a vocabulary.** A result is welded to the
mark that closes it and often to its repetition (`24>>`, `12>>12`, `120>>12`). On October 27, 97
to 100% of every tower's wrong values were values heard after some `=` in training
(record `2026-10-27_THE_REVIEW_FINDS_THE_READ_CUTS…`, §1): the learner draws a result word it has
heard, which is what word statistics do.

**[derived] The working modes hold a number and make no carry.** A gated mode steps
`h_t = λ_t h_(t−1) + γ_t B x_t` with `λ_t = |λ|^(c r_t) u`: the arriving symbol opens and closes it,
and it turns at its founded rate whatever arrives. As a register (the radix-move record, §1) a
passive mode reads a numeral in its own radix `|λ| < 1`, the latest digit heaviest, which can hold a
number's digits at a declared grain. A sum's digits are a different reading of the same digits:
each place's turn on the ring of ten plus the carry from the place below, a step that couples two
places across the ring's wrap. Nothing in a gated mode wraps, and the only steps that are not linear
are the contacts between levels, one pass of them a unit written. The turned mode (keyed turn) and
the pumped mode (the lock) were built on October 23 for exactly this, and the working declaration
cannot hold either: the passage credit refuses a turned mode (`tower.rs`, "the passage credit does
not carry a turned mode"), participation refuses a pumped one, and growth refuses both.

**[measured] What the tower did on a terrain of digits.** On October 23
(`2026-10-23_ON_A_TERRAIN_OF_INTERACTING_DIGITS…`) the gated tower read in bytes on 22,551 generated
lines of two-digit sums, differences and products wrote 153 of 241 unheard sums whole after twelve
passes; where the units carried and the tens were wrong, the tens were the tens without the carry
in 20 of 30. Products came out at the right length and leading digit and at chance on their cycles
(residues mod 9 and 11). The turned and pumped towers did not do better there: the turned mode put a
sum's units on a circle where neighbours read alike, and the pump's lock snapped parity and not the
ring of five. The carry, the winding that couples one place's circle to the next (the helix: circle
plus carry), was the part none of them held.

**[measured] GSM8K alone holds little arithmetic for its size.** Its training split writes 23,716
calculations, 10,772 distinct, 8,084 of them written once; of the 18,664 binary whole-number ones,
7,212 are products and 3,302 quotients (receipts `RUN.md`). On October 23 the byte tower coded the
digits of GSM8K's unheard results at 3.54 bits (a blind guess among ten is 3.32); with 24,000
generated calculations added it coded them at 3.21. [agent-inferred] So on GSM8K alone the products
and quotients that make up most unheard calculations cannot be located by this tower, and the sums
can be read only once the codec writes digits as digits.

## 6. The plan, with its acceptance fixed before any run

[agent-inferred] **The codec in the code.** Bytes are the unit route under a founding of no merges
(`founded-units-v1 0`): `Units::parse` with no merges is the identity, so the byte runs use the
same route, binary and declaration as the units runs, and differ only in the founding file. The
clocked participation is the participating pair route with `TOWER_PARTICIPATION_FROM=4`.

**The copy partition** (`tools/gsm8k/copy_roles.py`, built with this record): each held-out byte
carries 0 for the question, 1 for answer bytes that are not digits, 2 for the digits of an answer
numeral the question writes, and 3 for the digits of one it does not. The trainer prints them under
its region names, so in these runs `N` reads "answer digits the question writes" and `M` "answer
digits it does not". In the units runs a unit's region is its first byte's, so `N` and `M` are read
across codecs with that caveat; the first-calculation reading is the comparison that is equal on
both.

**The runs**, on the RTX 4080 SUPER under the card's lock, from C′'s founding keys at 160 streams,
the declaration of §45 at `2^(−8)`, 40 laps each, the receipt at laps 10, 20, 30 and 40 (the written
rules at four release draws, the coupling read, the copy partition, bits by region, one writing
whole):
- **R0**, the committed units (the §45 learner rerun from its founding, with the copy partition);
- **R1**, the bytes;
- **R2**, the bytes with participation at the clocked levels (`TOWER_PARTICIPATION_FROM=4`, its
  section 128 of its own ticks).

Each run's projection is the largest measured lap time over a one-lap development read times 40,
its deadline the projection's upper end; a run whose laps pass the projection's per-lap bound stops
and is reported incomplete. Device memory stays under 12 GB; a development read that needs more
halves the streams.

**Acceptance, fixed now.**
1. R0's training code equals the §45 learner's lap for lap (the same declaration, keys and binary).
2. **The codec.** At lap 40, R1's first calculation from the question's numbers, as the four draws'
   mean, is at least twice R0's and at least 16 of its first calculations.
3. **The copy route.** At lap 40, R2's first-calculation reading is at least twice R1's and at least
   16; R2 codes the answer digits the question writes (`N`) at least 1 bit a byte below those it does
   not (`M`), and below R1's `N`; and R2's numerals-chart question contact (own question less the
   next problem's) is above R1's.
4. **Unheard arithmetic on the codec.** At lap 40, R1 writes at least twice R0's mean of never-met
   calculations right at the equals, and at least 9 of 139, read by operation; for never-met sums
   that carry, how often the written tens are the tens without the carry is reported.

**What follows each outcome, fixed now.**
- 2 passes: bytes are the working codec. 2 fails: the codec is not the cause alone, and R2 still
  reads the route; a copy partition that moves within one section and not across one makes the
  lower level's push into the credit the next build.
- 77 (the step read on the next section) is built while R0 to R2 run; once it passes, the passing
  declaration runs on the step it certifies in place of the `2^(−8)` dial.
- 4 moves the sums and the written tens drop the carry: the next loop is the carry. The turned
  mode's moments through its keyed turn are derived so the passage credit carries it, and the read of
  its winding (the place circle's whole turns) is derived before it is built; that loop fixes its own
  acceptance.
- The products and quotients: after the GSM8K-alone readings above, a terrain generated by exact
  arithmetic (`tools/arithmetic_terrain.py`; a terrain may generate its truth, and only its text
  reaches the machine) joins beside GSM8K and is read by the same receipt.

## 7. The recorded failures this plan could repeat

- **Bits read as progress.** Every acceptance is a written-rule, copy or coupling reading; bits a
  byte are reported beside them and decide nothing.
- **Text on its codec's grain as the exception.** The repair removes a text-shaped founding instead of
  adding one: bytes are the passage's own grain for text, image, acoustic or motor input, and the
  clocked levels found packets by the field's own news. No rule names a digit.
- **An authored routine standing in for learning.** The copy partition, the written rules and the
  coupling read are exterior; nothing they read enters the machine or selects a release. The
  arithmetic terrain is material, never a receiver.
- **A located cause carried unrepaired into a new consumer.** The October 26 foil located the codec's
  effect on the first calculation and the race carried the welded units on; this plan repairs the
  codec before any further part joins.
- **A refusal answered with a larger limit.** Deadlines come from development reads and are not
  raised; a run past its bound is reported incomplete.
- **A design thought in the programming language.** The causes are stated as faces that do not meet,
  a register that does not wrap and a winding that is not read, and the repairs in the same terms.
