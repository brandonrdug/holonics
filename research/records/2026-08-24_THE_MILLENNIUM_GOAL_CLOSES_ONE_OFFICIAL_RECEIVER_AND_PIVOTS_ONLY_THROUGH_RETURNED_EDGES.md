## Holonic realization passage

[definition] The admissible passage is source-specific even when its middle laws are shared:

```text
situated source occurrence
  -> oriented returned difference / Swing
  -> addressed transport word and complete occurrence population
  -> source constitutive law
  -> boundary, annihilator, quotient, or retained remainder
  -> chart transport and exact reconstruction fibre
  -> scale descent, compactness, or gluing
  -> official receiver.
```

[definition] The official receiver is the terminal test. A local face closes an edge only when the
later receiver is constant on every retained fibre of that face. An equal-local/unequal-official
pair is not failed progress: it is a `ReceiverInsufficiency` witness and one of the six admitted
returns, because it proves exactly which distinction the current passage erased.

## Pivot rule

[project-postulate] A pivot is justified only by a returned dependency edge. It may occur when a
new source theorem is reusable by another official passage, when an insufficiency witness refutes
the present receiver, or when an assumption audit proves that another source owns the same strictly
smaller missing law. Similar notation, visual analogy, a generated catalog row, or a restatement of
the target under a new structure is not a pivot receipt.

[project-postulate] Conditional theorems remain useful only when they remove or factor a named
hypothesis. A structure whose sole field is the desired conclusion does not construct a carrier or
constitutive law and does not count as a deed.
