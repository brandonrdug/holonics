
## Pivot rule

[project-postulate] A pivot is justified only by a returned dependency edge. It may occur when a
new source theorem is reusable by another official passage, when an insufficiency witness refutes
the present receiver, or when an assumption audit proves that another source owns the same strictly
smaller missing law. Similar notation, visual analogy, a generated catalog row, or a restatement of
the target under a new structure is not a pivot receipt.

[project-postulate] Conditional theorems remain useful only when they remove or factor a named
hypothesis. A structure whose sole field is the desired conclusion does not construct a carrier or
constitutive law and does not count as a deed.
