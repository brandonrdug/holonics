## 0. Verdict

**Every taxon of the directive's panel is enacted as a matched sibling of one fixed base deed and
compared under one declared receiver family, on the card, and every one of the twelve falsifiers
passes.** The base deed is Station C's tower on *"The capital of France is"* (5 tokens, the declared
`MidpointQuotient` chart, grain 2^-48, 14 series terms); a replayed base returns all **128** faces of
the receiver family bit-identically. Sixteen siblings each insert exactly their intervention
occurrences at one declared site — typed as the caller's by their testimony (a quotient or an
intervention-only transport), never as source law — and the vision projection's sibling withdraws a
span of the soft tokens. Each taxon returns its caused change, the first face in chronology that
separates (the shortest separating history) with its positions, the control it predicted unmoved
measured unmoved, and its remaining ambiguity.

Three findings order the rest:

- **The KV reuse is separated from the K/V weights.** Withdrawing presented+carried family 0 at the
  stored sliding layer 22 first moves layer 22's contact (5,120 of 10,240 entries, widest gap 4.77)
  and every later layer; withdrawing the same family from the **shared standings at layer 24 only**
  leaves all 72 faces of layers 0–23 bit-identical and first moves layer 24's contact — the same
  5,120 of 10,240 at the same widest gap 4.77, because the withdrawn standing *is* layer 22's K/V at
  that family. The weights and the reuse are two sites with one consequence; the atlas names both.
- **The normalization's gauge is exhibited by domination, not by a threshold.** Multiplying the
  standing by 2 *before* the input rebase is quotiented away to the epsilon-and-rounding residue: it
  separates 84 of 128 faces, but at widest gap **1.3e-11** anywhere, while the same multiplication
  *after* the rebase separates at **2.3e-7** (×1.8e4 wider) and dominates the gauge at every
  separated face; the order face is unmoved under both. The exact receiver at 2^-48 still sees the
  residue: **tolerance is the receiver's aperture** (`CLAUDE.md` §0m), and this receiver's aperture is
  finer than the gauge's residue. A control declared as *"nothing separated"* was refuted by the
  first run and re-declared as what it is.
- **Position 0 is an unchanged unrelated control for every contact-side intervention**, and the data
  said so before the control was declared: under the identity chronology, the reversed positions,
  the receiver-head permutation, the ratio's temperature and the carried-head permutation, layer 1's
  return at position 0 is bit-identical to the base (2,560 of 2,560) while positions 1–4 separate.
  The causal contact at position 0 has one key, so its ratio is 1 whatever the receiver and the two
  grouped heads carry the same single V row; a relative angle of 0 is a rotation by the same angle.
  The controls were then declared with that mechanism and held on the rerun.
