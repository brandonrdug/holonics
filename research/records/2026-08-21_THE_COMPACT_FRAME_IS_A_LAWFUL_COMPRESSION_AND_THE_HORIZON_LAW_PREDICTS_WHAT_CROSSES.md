## 2. The horizon law predicts the classification

sharpest content of this record.

The horizon law: **across a frame boundary only a ratio or a winding survives; magnitudes do
not cross.** Reduction at a prime is literally a frame crossing, and the law predicts, before
any computation, exactly what the census found:

| carrier | species | crossing behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| torsion order | **winding** | crosses whole — injectivity on torsion, faithful in every good frame |
| height | **magnitude** | does not cross — no single compact frame sees the rank |

heights themselves in the leading coefficient's regulator. The frames delete magnitude one at
a time; the conjecture says the family's ratios repay it. Nothing here claims movement on the
conjecture; the reading gives it the corpus's type.
