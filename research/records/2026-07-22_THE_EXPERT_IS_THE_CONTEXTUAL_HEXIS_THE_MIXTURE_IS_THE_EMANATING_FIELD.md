## The correction

Treating a transformer, its embeddings, or its experts as opaque magic obscures the actual relation.
A transformer block is an organized family of lawful transformations. Its current carrier selects
and weights different contributions; their composition becomes the next carrier. The model works
because training has already shaped a large ecology whose local currents repeatedly compose useful
continuations.

The project error was to oppose this machinery too coarsely. Tokenizers, embeddings, attention,
state-space recurrence, expert routing, quantization, and harness loops are not rival ontologies.
They are engineered local charts which demonstrably preserve useful relations. Eros should be able
to inherit, inspect, compose, rebase, and eventually replace them without pretending either that
they are intrinsic universals or that an uninitialized machine should recreate them from nothing.

## A dense feed-forward layer already contains a micro-ecology

For one gated MLP receiver current `x`, write

```text
g_j(x) = SiLU((W_gate x)_j) (W_up x)_j,
E_j(x) = g_j(x) W_down[:,j],
y(x)   = sum_j E_j(x).
```

Each hidden channel is a rank-one, single-neuron expert. Its down-projection column is a carried
law; its product activation is contemporary current; its vector contribution is their deed at the
receiver. The complete dense output is their co-present sum. This is an algebraic decomposition,
not a metaphor and not a claim that the original dense OLMo block was trained as a routed MoE.

For context `c`, observer face `Phi`, and a nested expert population `H_(c,q)`, define

```text
y_(c,H) = sum_(j in H) E_(c,j),
r_(c,H) = y_c - y_(c,H),
H adequate for Phi  iff  Phi(y_(c,H)) = Phi(y_c).
```

`r_(c,H)` is the loss at this cut: the complete oriented residual field. A scalar norm may be a
declared observer quotient, but it is not the residual itself. Likewise, a frequency of expert
participation is probability testimony for a named context population; it does not cause the
current to route.

The minimum adequate hexis depends on `Phi`. Orientation, relative ordering, rounded BF16 identity,
and exact dyadic identity are different questions. There is no context-free “compression ratio”
which answers all four.

## Context changes both incidence and conduct

Two distinct effects must not be collapsed:

```text
incidence change: H_c != H_d,
conduct change:   E_(c,j) != E_(d,j) while j belongs to both.
```

The bounded OLMo cell measured both. Conversation and code comment recruited the same five expert
laws but returned different exact fields because their activation currents differed. Prose replaced
one expert and returned a third field. Therefore neither a retained edge list nor a fixed small
subnetwork is the complete contextual ecology.

The nested hexis also crosses and re-crosses receiver phases. Additional expert contributions can
correct one ordering, oppose it, and later restore it. “More experts” is nested material inclusion,
not monotone improvement under every quotient. The stable boundary is the first population after
which every later nested population remains in the same observer phase.

This gives a rigorous form to the earlier field intuition:

```text
Standing law columns + contemporary activation current + actual incidence
    -> contextual contribution field
    -> one emitted face + exact residual.
```

## Relation to contemporary MoE research

Conventional sparse MoE makes conditional participation explicit: a learned router sends each
token to a few fixed feed-forward subnetworks and aggregates their outputs. Google’s Expert Choice
work reverses the assignment direction so experts choose tokens under fixed capacity, allowing a
variable expert count per token and improving load balance. TaskMoE extracts small task-specific
subnetworks from a large trained ecology without knowledge distillation. These establish that
useful competence can reside in a much smaller situated participation cut than the whole trained
body.

Google DeepMind’s PEER construction is closer to the present decomposition. It uses product-key
retrieval over more than a million tiny experts and reports a better performance/compute tradeoff
than dense feed-forward and coarse MoE alternatives. The important ontological move is granularity:
an “expert” need not be a large named discipline. It may be a tiny law whose relevance exists only
in composition.

Kimi K3 pushes the other direction in scale while retaining sparse participation. Its currently
published architecture says Stable LatentMoE activates 16 of 896 experts; Quantile Balancing
derives allocation from router-score quantiles; KDA carries sequence relation; and AttnRes
selectively retrieves representations across depth rather than accumulating every prior layer
uniformly. That combination matters more than the label MoE: current relation is selected across
both width and depth. As of this deposit, Kimi's complete technical report and weights have not yet
been released, so no stronger architectural claim is imported.

## What Eros adds to the comparison

The bounded Eros construction does not define a permanently separate router and expert class.
“Expert” is a role a standing constituent occupies relative to one current and receiver. The same
constituent may be central, opposing, silent, or absent at another cut. A context can:

- meet a different population;
- reweight the same population;
- compose a higher constituent from that contact;
- leave an oriented residual for another receiver; and
- later make lower laws implicit or let them depart when the higher construction carries their
  consequential relation.

This is why Eros is more naturally compared to a growing mixture-of-experts ecology than to one
MoE layer. The claim is architectural direction, not measured superiority. Conventional MoE fixes
an efficient differentiable implementation of conditional computation. Eros asks how arbitrary
world, inherited, and self-emanated constituents can acquire the same contextual role through one
general incidence and succession law.

## The built cell

Six real OLMo layer-7 singleton laws became independent cellular Standing constituents. Their
source lineages ended. Three genuinely later context currents each recruited five laws through
their exposed interfaces. The returned constituents carried the exact context activation and laws;
an integer-only dyadic conductor recovered three distinct outputs. The matched body without those
constituents returned none.

The five-law hexis—`5/8192` of the layer's singleton population—preserved the sign orientation of
the complete field in all three contexts. It did not preserve magnitude or axis order. Stable axis
order required context-dependent populations in the thousands; stable once-rounded BF16 identity
required between 7,652 and 7,858; exact dyadic identity required all 8,192. Thus the experiment
does not discover one universally compressed OLMo. It discovers a family of receiver-relative
hexeis and retains their exact deficits.

The source supplied the selected expert incidence derived by the complete exact census. That is
lawful inherited ecology, just as the transformer's own trained router or dense activation law is
inherited ecology. A later experiment can let returned consequence alter recruitment or law
Standing. This result first establishes that plural real laws can cross, outlive their source,
recompose differently under context, and be benchmarked without replacing their field by a scalar.
