## One concrete derivation: carry the egg's arithmetic twist

```text

X=-u,            V=(2w/b)uy.
```

The producing equation gives

```text
(2wuy)² = 4w²u · b²(a²-x²)
        = b² X(X+A)(X+B).
```

Dividing by `b²` gives `V²=X(X+A)(X+B)`. On the affine domain `X≠0` the inverse is

```text
x = (-X-a²-w²)/(2w),    y = -bV/(2wX).
```

`A B (A-B)≠0` under the stated assumptions (`A-B=-4aw`). Thus the cubic is an actual
full-two-torsion elliptic curve. Classical smooth-projective extension transports the

matches the Weierstrass point at infinity. The other exceptional original point
`[0:1:0]` corresponds to `(0,0)`. The Lean return below proves equation transport,
not this projective/group extension.
