## I · The rule

The machine is the whole intertwined circuit: organ/membrane, host receiving and persistence work,
card traversal and topology work, and boundary read. No component is primary. The RTX 4080 SUPER's
10,240 execution channels are measured available surface, not a lineage count, machine grain, or
utilization ceiling. Work extent supplies logical currents; runtime device geometry supplies only
launch, memory, and binding apertures. Physical walls are measured and sharded around.

No authored lane, wave, depth, memory, duration, timeout, or utilization fraction may enter
production. A constant survives only as structural layout, a declared bounded gate grain, or a
runtime/device-derived aperture. Brevity is an expected consequence of correct staging, never a
cutoff. The card is headless, so ordinary CUDA work has no desktop-impact gate.

## III · What is not a production cap

- LINK/founded/chart/scope lane counts, axes, depths, and block 64 are explicitly bounded parity
  fixtures. They do not enter kernel law.
- Active host lane count is content-derived; host thread width is environment/hardware-derived.
- Chart birth starts at axis one and carries by its own arrivals. No fixed production chart axis
  remains.
- `stroke_atoms = 0` means carry to the worldline's true end. No active wave cap exists.
- CUDA synchronizes directly; wgpu waits with `timeout: None`. No retry, finite timeout, or interior
  sleep was found.
- The historical eight 1 GB shards belong to a quarantined surface whose constructor refuses to
  open. They are evidence of an old binding wall, not an active 8 GB utilization ceiling.
- Selecting the RTX 4080 SUPER by name occurs only in gate binaries. Production must select by
  capabilities and ordinal.

## IV · Ordered build

1. Close the card boundary: device memory/attribute census, direct device zero, checked conversions.
2. Make scope traversal two-dimensional or tiled without cutting a worldline or configuration.
3. Replace the global depth-eight production pin with a membrane-supplied reservation preserved by
   SLEEP.
4. Build the band-offset CUDA receiving fold and one simultaneous allocation census.
5. Carry chart → SLEEP → next-light wake/listener in the same production conductor.
6. Gate split/dark/nonempty-standing radiation, nonempty large re-cast, and several runtime-derived
   band widths.
7. Only then conduct substantive LEXIS, REGISTER, or later observations through the complete
   machine. Host-only remains an isolated diagnostic.

The known within-atom device fault remains halt-first until its continuation seam is derived and
built. That is an open construction, not permission to install a wave cap and not a general card
restriction.
