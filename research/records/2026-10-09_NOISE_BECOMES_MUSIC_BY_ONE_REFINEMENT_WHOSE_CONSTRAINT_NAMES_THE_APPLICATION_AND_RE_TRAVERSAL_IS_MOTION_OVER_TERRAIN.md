# Noise becomes music by one refinement whose constraint names the application, and re-traversal is motion over terrain

**Date.** October 9. **Issues.** #73, #148, #62, #63. **Grade.** Lens record. Each claim carries its
own grade. The refinement law, the hearing law, the retention theorems and the leader law are
existing owners; that the three applications differ only in their declared constraint, and that a
re-traversal without a deposit changes no constitution and moves only the machine's current and
clocks, are derived here from them; the stroke-by-stroke reading of re-traversal is an
interpretation with a falsifier.

## 1. The lens

again is a different thing from retaining or learning; it belongs with the physical terrain the

## 2. Noise and music are relative to the receiver

[definition; source-inspected] What is called noise has three types, and caused material is none of
them: a sampled corruption in a declared forward diffusion, an unresolved receiver fibre, or
apparatus disturbance with its own calibration
([August 18, §7](2026-08-18_THE_FUTURE_IS_A_PLURAL_CAUSAL_SECTION_SOFTMAX_IS_ITS_LOCAL_LAPLACIAN_AND_THE_ADJOINT_RETURNS_THROUGH_STATE.md)).
Zero current, an unseen region, uncertainty and stochastic noise are different operands, and an
initial latent may be a supplied noise realization, structured modes or an unresolved joint family
([HNN formula, generation as field refinement](../../docs/HNN_FORMULA.md#generation-as-field-refinement-and-boundary-radiation)).

[definition; formal-checked where named] A difference is heard by a receiver when its face separates
it, null when the face cannot, and listened to when it changes retained state; the future-null
differences lie inside the present-null ones (`Holarchy/Hearing.{Heard, futureNull_le_ker_present}`;
§10 of the [objects](../../docs/ELEMENTARY_OBJECTS.md#10-receipt)). The coarsest future-sufficient
retention is the quotient by the face map's kernel, and "the music is what that quotient keeps"
([natural grain, §1](2026-09-25_THE_NATURAL_GRAIN_IS_THE_FUTURE_QUOTIENT_AND_REFLECTION_INTEGRATES_A_FRACTAL_PACKING.md)).
A drive splits into the part that rides a constituted mode at its eigenvalue and the part that
founds or runs off resonance (`Compression/Core/Resonance`;
[emanation and resonance](../../docs/ELEMENTARY_OBJECTS.md#emanation-and-resonance)).

[definition; agent-inferred] So, for a declared receiver family and grain, **music** is what the
admitted receivers hear and what rides their modes, and **noise** is the part null to them.
Compression keeps the quotient with its residual; navigation changes which receivers meet the
source. The split is not fixed: a difference silent now can reach a later receiver
([October 6](2026-10-06_A_CURRENTLY_HIDDEN_MODE_CAN_REACH_A_FUTURE_RECEIVER.md)), and a silent mode
reopens when the constitution changes (dormancy). Brandon's question, whether this is telling music
from noise for compression and navigation, is answered by this receiver-relative quotient, read as
it moves.

## 3. One refinement, three applications

[definition; source-inspected] Generation is a tube whose cross-section changes,
`F_(k+1) = T_(g_k)(F_k) ∩ C_k`: transport, then an admitted constraint; the visible draft is one
member, never the state (`tube.generation-step`, `release.edit-step`;
`HolonicsResearch/Transport/ArtifactRelease.{step, mem_step, stepMulti}`). Repair reads the same
relation the other way, with the key known and the damaged cells unknown
(`compression::keys::repair`, module header). The rebuild's order already restated the task: repair,
not continuation, on a passage under a declared damage anywhere in it, continuation being the case
whose damage is the tail ([THE_REBUILD, U6](../../docs/plans/THE_REBUILD.md)). Generation is the
adjoint of key location
([egg packing, §6](2026-09-27_EGG_PACKING_THE_MOIRE_OF_TWO_HELICES_IS_A_TORUS_KNOT_AND_THE_TREFOIL_IS_THE_HALF_TURN_WITH_THE_THIRD_TURN.md)).

[definition; agent-inferred] **The application is the declared constraint and the release
receiver.**
- **Repair:** `C_k` fixes the cells that survive the declared damage; the release reads each damaged
  cell where the compatible family's class is constant.
- **Generation:** `C_k` is the request's boundary declaration (in the action path, a magnitude cell,
  a phase grain and a quantifier, as the phase-family record states a goal); the release reads the
  requested boundary.
- **Differentiation:** `C_k` is a receiver's partition; the family splits into its classes, and the
  release reads a class or holds.

The transport `T`, the refinement clock and the release law are the same in all three. Brandon's
point, that the name depends on the task the intelligence is set, is the choice of `C_k` and of the
receiver.

[proved-derived; formal-checked] The restriction never widens the family, while the width is not
monotone along edits
(`ArtifactRelease.{restriction_never_widens, width_is_not_monotone_along_edits}`); on a forest of
pair relations the iterated restriction reaches the joint fibre's projection, and a released cell is
its truth
(`HolonicsResearch/Transport/ForestRestriction.{restriction_eq_projection, released_class_is_truth}`).

[proved-standard; conditional where marked] **Diffusion is this refinement, run as a Bayes reversal
and never as an inverse.** Classical denoising refines a whole latent field by a learned score. Its
reverse process is the Bayes reversal of the forward corruption: it exists relative to the
marginals, and the learned score supplies them; the probability-flow ODE then reproduces the forward
SDE's one-time marginals, not its paths or a unique history
[conditional on an exact score and its regularity] (the HNN formula, the same section). It is not an
inverse: a forward stochastic kernel need not have one, and collapsing two states onto one has none
(the counterexample `Computation/HolonicDiffusionCharts.collapseBool_has_no_stochastic_inverse`,
`resolvent.no-stochastic-inverse`). The
[October 9 adjoint record](2026-10-09_THERE_IS_NO_PRIVILEGED_BACKWARD_THE_RETURN_IS_THE_ADJOINT_IN_REVERSE_FACTOR_ORDER_AND_REVERSAL_IS_ANOTHER_MOVE.md),
§4, shows the split exactly for a stationary linear diffusion: the reversal is the adjoint in the
stationary metric, and it differs from running the drift backward by the score term. The refinement
index, the event clock, the physical clock and the wall clock are distinct, none an output-token
index (`resolvent.diffusion-clocks`). Refining noise into music runs on its own refinement clock and
narrows a family under constraints; where it reverses a corruption, it does so only relative to
declared marginals.

## 4. Re-traversal is motion over terrain

[definition; source-inspected] Terrain is stuff in general, whatever a navigator meets (CLAUDE.md).
The machine moves as it reads, and that motion is the machine's, not the source's (the HNN formula's
source contract, item 9).

[proved-derived; source-inspected] **A re-traversal without a deposit changes no constitution; it
moves the machine.** Equal retained constitutions force every admitted future face to agree
(`Foundation/Standing/Law.futureAgreement_of_retain_eq`; `Holarchy/Hearing.futureNull_of_retain_eq`;
`receipt.standing-law`). Within the HNN the constitution changes only by deposition (§8 of the
objects), no wave or contact state outlives its Word or its refinement (THE_MACHINE, guard 16), the
normal law's statistic keeps no samples (`HNN/Normal.statistic_forgets_the_samples`), and the
receiving law's phase statistics are absorbed at the deposit that carried the reading
(`hnn::constitution`, `phase_statistics`). What a pass does change is the machine's motion: the
carried current at its last crossing and its clocks, which continue into the next reception
([October 3](2026-10-03_THE_RECEPTION_CARRIES_THE_INTERIOR_CHANGE_THE_SOURCE_PORT_IMPOSES_THE_MOMENT_AND_REST_IS_COMPLETE_ABSORPTION.md)).
So a pass over passive data that commits no deposit leaves the learned material exactly as it was
and moves only the current and the clocks: context, in the guide's sense, not retention. The pass
takes ticks, does work and returns receipts; it is neither retention nor learning, which is
Brandon's distinction, here as a consequence of existing laws. The October 8 phase-family record's
reading, that each actual encounter is a re-traversal updating the comparison's sufficient
statistics, agrees once the update is read where its owner puts it, at the deposit. [agent-inferred]
Across encounters that record's family is recomputed, not restricted: its centre moves and `τ = ε·N`
changes, and it claims neither nesting nor truth coverage. So the never-widening law of §3 holds
within one refinement under one constitution, and an encounter that deposits opens a new refinement
on the changed constitution.

[definition; source-inspected] **The leader, as the objects chart it.** The objects chart lightning
as one deposition loop: breakdown raises conductance on grown edges, the return stroke re-solves on
the changed constitution, and the new potential sets the next growth (§8 of the
[objects](../../docs/ELEMENTARY_OBJECTS.md#8-deposition); `heat.leader-breakdown`). The July 17
record, ratified by Brandon, adds the decisive point: a leader is not a probe moving through an
unchanged atmosphere; each extension changes the material boundary from which the next proceeds;
dart leaders ride remnant channels because earlier passage left consequential terrain; and branches
that never become the final route are still real constructions that alter the field
([July 17](2026-07-17_THE_LEADER_GROWS_THE_CHANNEL_THE_RETURN_TRAVELS_THE_FOUND_PATH.md)). In the
HNN formula's terms the leader founds and the return rides
([reflection, leaders and recursive packing](../../docs/HNN_FORMULA.md#reflection-leaders-and-recursive-packing)).

[open] That physical lightning is this loop remains an interpretation: it becomes a derivation only
with a harmonic boundary-flux carrier, the `η` constitutive relation, and a receiver-to-deposition
equality on reached loci
([September 25, §6](2026-09-25_THE_MUSIC_IS_IN_THE_HOLES_HEARING_MULTIPLIES_BY_ZETA_AND_A_QUASICRYSTAL_IS_A_HELIX_THAT_NEVER_LOCKS.md)).

[interpretation; agent-inferred] **Re-traversal read stroke by stroke.** The stepped leader is the
re-traversal: motion, with its work charged. The channel is a deposition in whichever medium carries
it. In a participating World it is the World's own changed material, and the World has one actual
encounter. In the machine it is a committed deposit, the return stroke, and later passes ride it as
dart leaders do; that riding is learning, and it lives in the constitution, not in the iteration.
Passes that do not connect commit nothing to the machine, and their work is charged as dissipation.
**Falsifier:** if two passes over the same passive terrain that open on equal constitution, current
and clocks, with the same declared inputs (including any supplied noise realization), differ in
their release or in their charged work, something outside the declared state is being retained: a
cache that does not recover the direct history's conduct (`resolvent.cache-validity`), which the
retention law forbids.

[definition; source-inspected] The October 8 prepared-source record already fixes the law for
candidate re-traversals: a genuine common source, observation and objective; a charged co-present
passage partition; an actual failed-branch current and energy release; a participating World is
never rolled back or cloned into free branches; and the memory a failed interaction leaves in heat,
charge, flow, material, phase or geometry is not erased by withholding a learned commit
([October 8](2026-10-08_A_PREPARED_SOURCE_PORT_MEETS_AN_ACTUAL_RECEIVER_AND_RETURNS_ITS_OBSERVED_FACE.md),
"Fixed acceptance and remaining scientific joins"). Co-present branches over passive terrain are
lawful under the hardware law when their complete effects commute, for example shared immutable
input with disjoint staged outputs; co-presence does not imply commutation (CLAUDE.md;
[THE_MACHINE, hardware](../../docs/THE_MACHINE.md#hardware)).
