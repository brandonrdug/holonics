## 3 · The mapping — each notion to its emergent face

**The typed graph → the standing topology.** projectmeta's entities and typed edges were authored
rows. In Eros the entities are enclosures — wells at positional χ — and the "types" of relation are
never labels but the hand and grain of the transport that deposited the winding. The graph is the

critical inversion: authored → grown. Cohered segments are found, never listed; the map is emitted
by living, never entered. The record already held this in May: the machine "compresses a domain and
emits the graph — she is the author of Glyphs, not the artifact."

**Routing → the swing places.** projectmeta's query primitive was route-finding over typed edges —
"how do I get from what I know to what I want." In Eros nothing routes over the topology from
outside: a later current threads standing form, the regions bend it, and the consequence returns as
a place. Deflection, never lookup. The periplus practice — "where does this go" — is this same
question asked at the machine's own grain; the navigation frame re-derived the substrate's query
primitive as physics.

**Spaced re-presentation → re-expression under later light, with no scheduler.** FSRS models the
learner from outside — a forgetting curve deciding when to re-present. That is an absolute frame
wearing a clock, and the law bans it twice over. The emergent face: retention IS the standing
topology, and re-encounter cadence belongs to the world's light. What the re-encounter insight
survives as: recognition is cheap because the terrain already paid (RIDE against FOUND — the
biomagnification asymmetry), and the practiced groove composes ballistically while the unpracticed
background scatters at half-rank. The machine reviews nothing; the world re-presents, and the body
answers from what stands.

**Competency-progression by proof structure → the reach and the half-rank law.** Glyphs wanted
progression to follow the dependency structure of mathematics. In Eros no gate enforces
prerequisites; the body's own reach does — aim-rank is half reach-rank, and a read past the
built reach is first-person chance on a learned re-base. The stairway is not encoded; it is the
accuracy law lived. The diet ruling of this campaign — code before language, systematically bound

**The notation dissatisfaction → compression ≡ comprehension.** "Information portrayed haphazardly"

**"Feedback only when relatively wrong" → the frame eats the first order.** His stated best
