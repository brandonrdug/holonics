## The coefficient expression and its source

[definition] A source-map return stores its actual source occurrence, admitted contact prefix,
complex overlap coefficients and per-overlap error. The existing two-factor return and complete
covector bound remain. The native update is

```text
D_next = D_before + Q_F(P k† + V ell† + D_source H).
```

The quantizer acts once after the complete sum. A sum of separately projected pieces would
generally be a different numerical update. The source map is the one that produced the named
emission, before any subsequent response to that emission.

[proved-derived] A producer for emission s includes births through s and journal returns with
`at_cut <= s`; a response applied after emission s has `at_cut=s+1` and is excluded. A retained
source-map return at receiving cut r depends on a source s with `s+1<r`. Its source-map dependency
is therefore earlier than its own update. The decoder can evaluate the resulting coefficient
expression in chronological order without assuming any update is invertible.
