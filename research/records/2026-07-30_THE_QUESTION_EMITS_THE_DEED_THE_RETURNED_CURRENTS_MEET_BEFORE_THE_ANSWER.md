## Resource falsifications and corrections

The production migration was guided by actual failed runs rather than by an
assumed optimization story.

Earlier variants had already exposed:

- approximately 11.4 GB for a word-bag-conditioned complete body;
- approximately 4.7 GB for one flat exact relation body;
- approximately 6.3 GB when the question was copied into the observation
  organ; and
- approximately 20 GB when every incomparable passage pair conditioned a
  full recurrent product.

This construction exposed further bounded failures:

- one selected full pair ecology reached approximately 7.1 GB;
- materializing every admissible seam return reached approximately 11.2 GB;
- launching all returned section organs concurrently reached approximately
  11.5 GB;
- eager recurrent factorization of every returned episode made the later
  question reach approximately 15.1 GB; and
- indexing every episode by the complete prior question woke unrelated organs
  on the follow-up.

Each run was stopped before acceptance. These are approximate observed
resident-memory faces, not portable benchmark constants.

The accepted correction:

- keeps candidate answers as sparse `MorphologicalGeneratedCurrent` values;
- materializes one complete returned Swing body only after selection;
- keeps every admissible seam as lightweight exact testimony and materializes
  one receiver witness;
- executes independent local organs in deterministic two-worker antichains,
  ending each antichain before the next begins;
- routes by inclusion-maximal ordered receiver faces before realizing a local
  recurrent factorization;
- retains returned passages as exact structural organs and realizes their
  larger morphology only when reached; and
- indexes later organs by their actually returned or emitted surfaces, not by
  a copy of every surface in the question which caused them.

The final real instrument conditions its inherited body in 209 ms and
completes both live questions in the same run without the prior expansion.
Elapsed time is an observer receipt only and is not part of agent Standing.
