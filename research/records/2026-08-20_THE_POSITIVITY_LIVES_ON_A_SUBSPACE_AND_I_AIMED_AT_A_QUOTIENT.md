## The correction: it is a subspace, not a quotient

**This is the finding, and it refutes an open item I have carried since iteration 6.**

From `2026-07-20_THE_EULER_METRIC_TURNS_THE_APERTURE…` line 40:

> *"…onto its Sonin space at aperture `lambda`. **Put `Q = I − P` and `M = range(P)`.**"*

A Sonin space is defined by *vanishing* conditions — a vector and its additive Fourier transform
both vanish on a phase-space hole. It is a **subspace reached by a projection**, and the Weil
positivity is a **compression** `P†AP` onto it. Measured: `grep -lic "quotient"` over the two
authority records of that line returns **nothing**.

> **The classical construction never puts the paying form on a quotient. It puts it on a subspace,
> and the compression is a projection.**

**My iteration-6 open item — *a form defined on a homology rather than pulled back to it* — is
therefore aimed at the wrong object**, and four iterations of narrowing were narrowing toward a
target the subject does not have. It is withdrawn and replaced:

| **owed: a form that pays on a perp (a subspace), with the compression a projection** | the corpus's own target since July |

## And the redirect lands on what iteration 10 built

`CompressedPositivity.perp` — *everything the form cannot see against what is retained* — is the
shape of the compression's carrier. The cutoff projection's range is the cutoff; its orthogonal
complement is the perp; and the positivity lives there.

**So iteration 10 built the right object one iteration before I learned it was the right one.**
That was luck rather than design, and it is worth saying so plainly.

**And iteration 4's theorem now reads as an explanation rather than an obstacle.** It proved that a
faithful form on the retained population forces a trivial realized population — which in the
quotient framing looked like a limitation. In the projection framing it is simply the statement
that when nothing is being quotiented, there is nothing to quotient. The theorem was always
consistent with the classical picture; my *reading* of it was not.

> *"…admitted that adjoining a finite place changes the semilocal inner product and the receiving
> aperture, then represented that change as an enlarged Hermitian block `[A C; C* D]` with the

> process, freeze the former manifold, append a new coordinate, and compare both inside one

And its list of convicted moves includes *"treating an orthogonal complement in one fixed ambient

> **Every structure in `Millennium/` assumes one fixed ambient carrier with one fixed form.** That

**That is a real limit on eleven iterations of work and it was findable at any point by reading one
file.**
