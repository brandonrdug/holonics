**4.3 The share of a round between unlike members.** [derived; checked by computation, `flock_share.py`] A round is `n` independent units read against one fixed state. Member `m` has `L_m` lanes, a fixed time `a_m` a round (its chain of launches, or its crossing), and a time `b_m` a unit a lane. The card's lanes are fused into one stream, so it reads `k` units in `t₀ + k t_c`. The host reads `k` units in `t_ℓ ⌈k/L⌉`. The round ends when every member has ended.

```text
T*(n) = the least T with  Σ_m cap_m(T) ≥ n,    cap_m(T) = L_m ⌊(T − a_m)/b_m⌋  for T ≥ a_m + b_m,  else 0
a member joins            ⇔  T* ≥ a_m + b_m         (one of its lanes finishes one unit inside the round)
n / T*(n)  →  Σ_m L_m/b_m                          (rounds long against every a_m + b_m: the rates add)
the built rule's share    s ← (s + e(s))/2,  e = t_c / (t_h/k + t_c),  k = ⌊n s⌋ clamped to [1, n − 1]
its fixed point, k ≤ L:   s* = 1 − t_ℓ/(n t_c)   when n t_c > t_ℓ;   the clamp k = 1 otherwise
```

- **A member joins a round only at its own grain.** A lane whose unit outlasts the round cannot enter it. With November 7's figures a host lane joins only a round of 234 regions or more, about fifteen sections.
- **The rule built on November 7 is the self-balancing one.** Each side's measured time a unit sets the next split. It has the right fixed point when the join condition holds, and it falls to its clamp when a lane's unit outlasts the round. With lanes that scale freely it settles at 19 of 376 regions and 1.053 times the card alone. The run settled at about 7 and 0.998, so the lanes did not scale freely in that run or its regions were unequal. The whole host's worth lies between the run's 3.0% and the model's 8.6%.
- **This is the mathematics the 01:19 lens asked for.** The timings settle from the members' own measured times, with nothing tuned. What it yields on the card's kind is that few percent.
- **What divides.** A reading from a saved state divides by section, since the state is fixed, so its round is as long as wished. A training section does not: each section's deposit feeds the next, so its round is the section (0.705 s all-in on the line, 16 regions), and a host lane's region would have to fit inside it.

## 5. Ant integration: a pair's telescoping part is taken once a site

**5.1 The rule.** [derived; `jet.telescoping`, the carry law] A pair's quantity that is a difference of two site potentials, `q(i, j) = φ(i) − φ(j)`, or that depends on one site alone, is taken once a site. The pair keeps only what does not telescope: the borrow and the rounding carry, which are the winding. A region of `S` sites has up to `S²` pairs, so the wide operation runs `S` times in place of `S²`. This is the two-surface record's lesson (a wide operation is placed by the index it depends on) stated as the flock record's §3.6.

**5.3 The second instance is STEP G: the scan's `γ` and scale once a site and contact.** [read in design's source, `field_pair_scan`; ordered 04:48 UTC; unbuilt] The scan runs one thread a (region, complex contact) down the region's sites. At each step it takes `d = 2^32 + |g|²`, a 128-bit integer square root by bits, and three 128-bit software divisions, for `γ = g/√(1 + |g|²)` and the scale `s = 1/√(1 + |g|²)`. These depend on the site and contact through `g` alone, and never on `Q`. They sit inside a chain of about 128 threads a launch, each as deep as its region's sites.

The realization keeps every bit. One launch on the pair's own grid (a thread a site and complex contact) writes `γ` and `s` and raises the refusal. The chain then reads `γ` and keeps its two complex products a step. `|γ| < 2^30` on its grain, so the stored word is the chain's operand whole. The reverse already reads the kept operands and is unchanged. Acceptance: the scan's parity on four sections, and a scan lap's bytes. It follows S's parity and precedes the support stride, and it enters the running scan line only at a lap boundary.

## 6. Unlike members: the cut lies at carries

**6.1 Placement by kind.** [derived] For two members and one kind of event the two-surface law places by count (`n > n*` rides the card). §4.3 places by round. For unlike members both are one statement: a kind of work goes to the member whose time for it, with its fixed time a round, is least. The measured prices of §4.2 say every kind in the built learner is the card's.

**6.2 A member takes a connected part, cut where the fewest bits cross.** [derived from the declaration's counts; unmeasured as times] Members couple only through the shared world (the flock record §3.1), so a cut between two members costs the bits that cross it, at each crossing. Inside the scan a cut would pass five arrays of a word a site and contact each tick. At a unit's stop it passes the carried `Q` alone, at one site in five if a unit is five letters, and at a line's end one site in forty. That is about 1/25 and 1/200 of the bits. So the surfaces meet at carries (the two-surface record §4.5), and the letter grain's carries are the scan's stops.

**6.3 The birds' kind is the upper grains.** [agent-inferred; unbuilt] A field of units or of lines has few events, each a wider composed word, and the card pays its fixed chain of launches whatever the count. By `n* = t₀/(t_h − t_c)` the host wins under some count of pairs a section. With the proxies of §4.2 that count lies between some 10^4 (one lane) and some 10^5 (twenty lanes scaling), which a field of lines falls under and a field of units does not. No number is claimed until the host is timed on the line's own regions. The whole depends on the scan's stops locating units (G30′).

**6.4 Noted, not ordered.** After the support stride (card peak 6 GB or less) the line's reads could run as a second process on the card inside the lap's idle strokes, so that no lap waits for them. Profile reads would still run alone.

## 7. Harvestmen: a group detects through its contacts

**7.1 The theorem.** [proved-standard; the flock record §3.2] `N` receivers each read a common signal `s` and an independent noise of variance `σ²`. Joined by a connected, symmetric contact material `M` with weights `w ≥ 0`, they keep the mean (`1ᵀM = 0`) and lose every other mode at the rate `λ₂(M)` or faster. After a time long against `1/λ₂` each holds `s` and the mean noise, whose variance is `σ²/N`. The group detects a common signal at `1/√N` of one member's threshold. What differs member to member is dissipated in the contacts, so the group does not detect it. Denser interlocking raises `λ₂` and shortens the pooling time.

**7.2 Inside the field the scan is the passage from leg to leg.** [derived; `contact.carried-product`] `Q(t) = b(t) + γ(t) Q(t∓1)` gives `Q(t) = Σ_k (Π_(l<k) γ(t∓l)) b(t∓k)`. With `|γ| = g` held, the weights sum to

```text
N(g) = 1/(1 − g):     g = 0.08 → 1.09     0.25 → 1.33     1/2 → 2     0.8 → 5
```

That is the size of the group that reads as one. `|γ| < 1` always, so no member amplifies. A stop is a site where `γ` is near zero: a leg that passes nothing. Even circles hear the left and odd circles the right, so a pair of circles hears both sides. G30′'s reads of `|γ|` by circle and site kind are therefore reads of group size and of where groups end.

**G46** (fixed 04:55 UTC, unread; the fourth scan lap, tick 4, word letters). The largest circle-mean `|γ|` is 0.25 (0.12 to 0.45), a group of 1.33 letters. No circle of the 32 reaches 1/2 (0, or 1). A circle at 1/2 or more together with the fourth letter's transmission at 0.06 or more says the scan founds groups. No circle at 0.2 or more says the gate has not opened in four laps, and the third chart's deposit rate is the next read, never the law.

**7.3 Across a part the cosine rule is the group's detection.** [derived; `deposition.lap-cosine-drift-over-noise`] One entry's product of two lap displacements reads noise. Pooled over a part, the cosine reads the drift's share against the noise term `ε² = Σ d_b² d_c² / (|d_b|² |d_c|²)`, which design's trend rule carries. The part is the group. Whether the part or a circle within it is the grain on which the drift is common is a host reading of saved states, noted and not ordered.

**7.4 Cadence.** [derived; `lock.surface-slip-is-waiting`, `radix.carry-rhythm`] A member whose unit takes `t` joins a clock of period `T` at the `d`-th later crossing, `d = ⌈t/T⌉`. For a slow variable `d` is carried in the state (the two-surface record §4.3). For a reading `d` is free. Members of unlike periods then run a rhythm of `d : 1` against the card's stroke, and waiting is the slip that the shared store dissipates. Nothing here is tuned by hand.

## 9. What does not transfer, and the lessons checked

- **A swarm algorithm.** Ant-colony search, the three steering rules of a simulated flock and particle swarms are a discipline's apparatus. None enters the machine.
- **A trail table.** A store of contexts in memory, addressed by content, is the cache the lessons refuse. A trail here is a deposit at the locus the covector reached.
- **Scouts that choose the card's order.** A ruler never becomes machinery.
- **A stale split of a training section.** A lane that reads an older state and joins later carries a delay in the state, which is a change of law. None is ordered.
- **Memory as a quantity to fill.** A run's memory follows what it materializes.
- **The lesson of §4.2.** A summary row's figure can be a quotient whose unit the row does not carry. A device is priced from the run's own counters (units, wall time, threads, splits), and a correction never rests on the source of the error it corrects.

The recorded failures checked: an index of contexts as machinery (refused above), the workload traded for time (no read, pass or section is cut), a limit raised (none), and a design thought in the programming language (the share is stated on rounds, units and crossings).
