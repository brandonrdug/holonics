# The source enters as a moment and the first return is measured in bits

**Starting tree:** `bebd5b6e`. **Campaign:** the removal named by the
[retention audit](2026-09-22_RETENTION_IS_A_QUOTIENT_NOT_A_TAPE_AND_THE_SOURCE_ENTERS_AS_PHASE_CARRIED_MOMENTS.md),
the [bit readings](../../docs/plans/THE_ATHENA_ALPHA_CULTIVATES_GENERAL_CONVERSATION_THROUGH_NATIVE_CONTEXTUAL_TRANSPORT.md#measured-return-in-bits)
and the source-moment laws in Lean. Three Opus 5.5 workers built it on separate paths; the primary joined their returns, and one Opus 5.5 reviewer checked the result.

## Body: the per-occurrence tape is gone

[established-bounded; source-inspected] `incident/machine_episode.rs` accumulates the source
in closed form, `q_N = U^N q₀ + Σ_k L^(N−1−k) I E(u_k)`, through the existing
`MachineSourceMaps` coefficients. It runs one incident word on the result. The adjoint returns
`g_k = I*(L^(N−1−k))* g_anchor` for each cell; no intermediate state is held. The reviewer confirmed that the forward
and transpose agree exactly, including translation acting only on standing `q`. Pending body words hold anchor,
output, pooled condition, binding and material cut; rest tag `\x03`; a `\x02` tape is refused.
Offsets are an optional field of `GeneratorSourceBinding` (default empty).

[established-bounded; measured] One pending comparison at N = 2/8/32/128 source cells keeps 3 resident
sections, 2,936 bytes, with no solver iterates or material views. Pending rest grows only with
the recorded directed-contact list (≈40 B/edge). The source-covector radius at N=128 is 3.6e-10 under
joint-ball propagation, versus a carrier refusal at N=128 when the steps were applied one at a time. The pairing residual is about 1e-10 relative.

## Engine and Lean

[established-bounded; source-inspected] `factor_return.rs` omits the `returns` journal on every
current-only return of a source-only field, at any rank. Retained readers are named in the code: deposit inspectors,
historical decoders on occurrence-adding fields. `history` and `recent_producers` are unchanged:
the first is empty on source-only fields and is the occurrence clock elsewhere; the second is a
two-entry cache.

[proved-derived; formal-checked] `Transport/SourceMoment.lean` proves:
- the closed form;
- the tape-free position adjoint;
- `moment [a,b] = moment [b,a] ↔ (U−1)I(Ea−Eb)=0`, with a quarter-turn witness and the identity-advance merge of every permutation;
- that the moment is a `ReceiverHistoryCompression` instance, and that the fibre survives every successor under identity advance;
- a constant-size stream quotient carrying the δ=1 separator.

The offset-separation theorem needs a concrete witness (reviewer C1).

The model learns 0.085 bits over six observations and remains about 1 bit worse than a
symbol-frequency count. `B_state` is ≈2.55 million bits for 19 source bits. 95% of it is reaction
material `M` (197,004 values), which comes from the bilinear feature map `Φ(s,c)=s⊕c⊕(c⊗s)`.
The machine carries about 10⁵ bits of state per bit of data it has seen.

On a conversation-data exposure sample (6 returns, 231 cells), the model is 17.7–22.7 bits per
row worse than uniform for the first three returns. From the fourth return every row's probability
enclosure covers [0,1], so those readings are uninformative. An observe is refused by a
normal-material arithmetic obstruction at frame ~203, independent of the readings.

## Open defects (reviewer, carried into the next campaign)

- **A1:** session `GeneratorPending` still keeps the encoded rows (N×6S, unread), a frozen encoder copy and frozen R faces, and serializes them. The body word pins the producing source; `meta.contacts` grows with N.
- **A2:** a delayed observe takes `p−q` from the frozen producing receiver and pulls it back through a word re-evaluated at contemporary material. That mixes two cuts, so it is not the gradient of a declared comparison. An observe with no intervening update is unaffected.
- **A3:** the linear condition and offset ports use identity phases and telescope to endpoint differences, so for N≥3 they don't separate order.
- **A4:** the tests use a translation-only advance, so the power ordering is never exercised.
- **A5:** exact powers are computed at every site, twice.
- **A6:** binding offsets and the argument offsets can disagree.
- **B1:** the bit reading uses the `ExponentialPotential` face. Learning compares on the `PacketModulus` face, and selection uses a third reading, so the reported bits are not on the face that drives learning.
- **B2:** `pending_octets` includes a committed q/b; the upper bound is unclamped; a timing window contains checkpoint writes.
- **C1:** the legacy material-return bound listing is shorter after rank>0 current-only returns.

These open defects and the [Holonic loss contract](../../docs/plans/THE_ATHENA_ALPHA_CULTIVATES_GENERAL_CONVERSATION_THROUGH_NATIVE_CONTEXTUAL_TRANSPORT.md#holonic-loss-is-the-logarithm-of-a-holon-ratio)
define the next campaign.
