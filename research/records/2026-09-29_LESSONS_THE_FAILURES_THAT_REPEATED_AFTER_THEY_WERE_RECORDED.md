## 2. Why today's builds did not unify

Two independent reviews read today's code; they agree.
- **The learner was counts, not the Holon.** The founding (`found_passage`) is a suffix-automaton
  context model priced by KT continuation counts; its units are born only when a one-step count gain
  pays a per-class charge, on a ladder that expands only from newborn classes, so nothing longer than
  a fragment survives. It is the byte-tree line re-entering through the encoding, and it bypasses
  deposition (the chart is founded once, outside the constitution), Birth's own trigger (a reached
  covector) and key location (placement by first arrival).
- **The seams had no shared law.** Constituents were labels, not a ring's locked modes; the moment
  counted bytes while classes only stepped the lift; the latent was pseudo-random signs, not keys
  located by the Bombe (`hnn::keys` unused); refinement over `K` words fed a one-word, unit-scale
  step; the receiving tree's logits were added to the field's with no flow and effort.
- **The unified form** (Astra, agent-inferred): the coupled motion of loaded rings and contacts with
  its deposition is the learner, under `learned_energy_balance`; keys are located by loop closure;
  a unit is founded from a reached separating covector under the field's actual transports; a longer
  unit survives because its composed transport returns stably, not because a substring pays; the
  step reads a certified curvature bound over every re-entry and station. `learned_energy_balance`
  is a balance, not a stability theorem; the stability is owed.

## 3. The lessons

1. **Read the lessons before designing.** Before any design, brief or build: read this record and
   the September 24 lessons, and name which recorded failure each choice could repeat. A choice that
   repeats one is refused before it is built.
2. **One construction across modalities.** State every design as one Holon-level construction and
   check it against text, image, acoustic and motor input before writing code. Anything specific to
   one modality lives only in a boundary codec. A design that makes sense only for bytes is wrong.
3. **A located cause stays open in its owner until it is repaired there.** Every new consumer of that
   owner names it as its expected blocker before its pin. No new consumer is built on an owner whose
   located cause is unrepaired.
4. **No recitation and no index.** Reproduced source is contamination. An exact realization of a
   passage is a tape. Retained state is a quotient sufficient for the admitted future, reported
   against the data it read.
5. **No authored routine** (the governing law "No catered machinery").
6. **A deposition step is certified.** It reads a bound on the covector reaching its locus, over every
   station and re-entry, and sets its step from it; the energy bound is enforced at the commit, not
   only recorded.
7. **Output decides generation.** Bits are reported beside a generation, never as its success. A local
   pass on a new small example does not replace the standing failure.
8. **Unseen means unread by any run.** Transfer is graded only on material no run has read, and the
   data a generation needs is stated in its acceptance.
9. **A refusal changes the law or the partition, never the limit.**
10. **Report substance.** The output as it is, the mechanism of a failure, and the experiment that
    will explain it; no neutral list of blockers, no hedging, no apology, no reactive rule. Decide, and
    never hand Brandon a decision or a document to review. After a compaction, act on the newest
    message and the plan; never re-answer an old one. Take his words as written.
