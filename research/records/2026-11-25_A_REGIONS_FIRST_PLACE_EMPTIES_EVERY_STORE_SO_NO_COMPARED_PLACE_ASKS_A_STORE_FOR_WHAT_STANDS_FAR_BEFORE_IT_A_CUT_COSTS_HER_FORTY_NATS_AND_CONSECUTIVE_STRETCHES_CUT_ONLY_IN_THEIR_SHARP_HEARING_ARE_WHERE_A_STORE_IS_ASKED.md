## 6. Why her stores hold thirty places: nothing compared asks them for more

[interpretation, 0.75] In the pass as it stands a compared place is in one of two cases with respect to a source far before it.

- **The source stands inside the place's region.** The open place hears it one by one. Whatever a store also holds of it is a mode
  dependent at the receiver: the face it moves is moved by the hearing already (`compression.redundant-mode-kernel`).
- **The source stands before the region.** Every store was emptied at the region's first place, so two passages that agree from
  that place on read the same at every later place (Lean `Holonics.HNN.StoreCut.emptied_at_cut`): what stood before is in the kernel
  of every face of the region.

In both cases what a store holds of a far source moves no compared face. A deposit is linear in the covectors that reach a locus
(`deposition.cost-follows-surprise`), and none reaches a store's far content, so nothing charges it. The retention law says the
same from its side: retention is the quotient sufficient for the admitted future, and the pass admits no future in which a store's
far content is needed. What the stores do hold, the last thirty places, is what the hearing brings least well: the order of the
nearest places, which the moments carry by phase and the near field by contact.

This is two paths from one source to one receiver, a contact that carries the source whole and a store in parallel with it. The
flux divides by what each path removes of the covector, and the store's share of a source the contact already carries is nothing.
Where the contact is cut and the store is emptied with it, the store's share is nothing again.

Three readings this places:

- **The fade family's purpose clauses.** The night's arms and W117q lowered her rings' fades, founded a second fade and read for a
  ring carrying a far count; each purpose clause missed (the lead's reads; §3's second row). A slower fade lengthens what a ring
  could hold. No compared place pays for it, so the deposit trims it or leaves it, as the morning's record read on text.
- **The frontier record's far read.** With the far field cut, more known places read worse (§2). The moments and near states were
  never asked to carry what the far rows carried.
- **The calendar record's reads of November 24.** She holds a rate and no count; a ring's whole turn gave no count. A count that
  stands across many places is a store's far content.

What would move the weight: an arm in which a place needs a source it cannot hear one by one while a store that met the source
still rings (§7). If her stores' horizon does not grow there, the weight goes to the stores' form (how long a ring's fade lets it ring, the
level's one winding).

## 7. The construction: consecutive stretches in one region, cut only in their sharp hearing

The pass's stretches are already consecutive places of one piece (§2). The construction leaves a stretch what it is, the span in
which an open place hears every site one by one, and changes what stands at its first place:

- **One region holds a slice's consecutive stretches**, up to the reach (3,771 places, where its frames would repeat: about
  seventeen stretches of the pass's mean).
- **The stores run the region whole.** The moments, the opening's rows, the circles' charge, the level and the near field are not
  emptied at a stretch's first place. By the hand-off law (Lean `run_from_cut`) a store run from a cut with the state it held there
  is the whole run, and its state at the cut is sufficient for what stands before (`state_at_cut_sufficient`).
- **An open place hears across its stretch's first place as a definite receiver hears far**: the definite sites in the classes its
  probe names, one by one, and each octave's crowd at its mean word. Inside its stretch it hears as today.
- **The return runs the region whole**, through the same supports.

This is the ruler's mask 191 at every stretch's first place, made the region's law. What it is and is not:

- *Every open place hears all it hears today, and more.* Nothing a stretch has is taken from it.
