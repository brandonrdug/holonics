## 1. Why this record exists

Brandon holds self-induction as his own postulate (18:21Z: it is asserted from experience, never a "test" a reading could fail), and on
21:12Z gave its lens for a loop that runs off: overconfidence reinforces itself until it is wrong, and then the evidence should shatter the
source of the run-away in a snap, the way lightning and plasticity break and reform; it is not to be damped before that is seen. The
junction record's §8 named what such a loop needs in this build: a store of the rate, its own echo known as its own, and a drive. The
built tick has the first two only weakly (G151: a written row's echo is 0.00068 at the median) and no drive. This record reads the drive
and the snap, and says what the readings leave for the next design.

## 2. What the readings show

- **A reader acting on its source at a tick's rate does not ring.** The read's mirror (`Field::mirror_route`: the forward read taken by each
  source of its readers' rays, a pure strength `s`) has a large first order on the line's own text (G160: `+0.12` nats a place a unit of
  `s` in heads 0 and 2, three to five times its random-sign floor), but at finite strength every uniform `s` from −0.2 to +0.1 raises the
  code on the regions the line deposits from (G161), and run past the trained depth with it on, the free iteration settles without
  ringing at every strength read: a turn slows the settling, agreement hastens it (G162). The tick's own relaxation damps a turn added at
  its rate.
- **The field's own release, fed back, rings.** Opening each open place on its face from the pass before (`read-release`), a place that
  hears only its own release flips between two faces every pass, the successive changes pairing at −0.77 at pass 3 and −0.99 from pass 8 to
  pass 32 in every slice (G163, G166). That is a period-doubled response to a period-one drive: the parametron's subharmonic, its two phases
  a bit, and the synchronous two-cycle of a symmetric map iterated in step (`tower.field-echo`). It is the first ring this build shows.
- **Mutual, it runs off and then locks.** The open places of the cut's masks made sources at their releases reinforce one another: the code
  climbs from 3.01 to 8.50 nats a place while the expected code falls from 3.04 to 1.32, and from pass 21 the whole loop itself falls into a
  two-cycle at its saturation (G166). No pass snaps back: nothing in the loop compares a release with what the passage holds.
- **The evidence is inside the field.** Read through the readout at the written places, whose classes the passage holds, the known places
  code 3.95 nats a place at the first pass and worsen in step with the runaway, +0.28 by pass 16, 0.59 of it by pass 4 (G167).
- **A snap limits the harm and buys nothing.** Shattering a release (its opening and its voice returned to the built read) where the known
  places of its near field turn worse catches 43% of releases at the first chance and holds the known places, but the surviving releases
  mislead the open places as fast as before, and a second pass reads worse than the first at the open places pooled (3.44 against 3.01),
  so no snap at any pass recovers more than the first read (G168). Damping instead settles the lone loop onto a point 0.61 nats a place worse
  than its first read (G164).

## 3. What follows

The trained field cannot use its own release: it was never trained through a carried opening (the carried release was retired on
November 3, the record of November 1 §16.27), and at a place opened on its own face its readout disbelieves it, which is the two-cycle. So
the snap the lens names is, on the objects, not a cut inside an untrained loop but the evidence reaching the parts that produced the
runaway: the deposit through the loop, plasticity beside the lightning (agent-inferred). The loop's ring is real and the loop's use is
owed to training.

## 5. Heard as written text the loop runs off at any temperature it holds; what bites it is a warmer field, and the evidence waits on training

second lap; host, 8 threads; receipts `g169_redraw.txt` to `g173_before.txt` under the junction record's receipts, ruler

- **The soft loop of §2 is mean field.** Its carried faces are averages and its update is deterministic, so a metastable lock has no
  fluctuation to decay by: escape over a barrier needs noise, and at zero noise its rate is zero (Kramers; agent-inferred). That is one
  reason no pass of §2 snapped back.
- **Drawn and heard as written, the loop runs off too (G169, `read-redraw`).** Each open place is drawn from the field's own face, and
  every read is a mask state the field trained on: the open places of one residue modulo `NEAR` opened at a time, the others written at

  nats a place, the truth at 7.65, 72% confidently wrong) and coarsens: its changes fall from 28% to 1.4% of the places and onto the
  walls between ordered runs, where neighbours change together 19 times as often as chance. So noise does not bite either.
- **Why: the field takes its own news for the passage's.** The receiver's news is `κ = S ⊖ E`, what it already predicts cancelling (the
  laboratory's foil). A release is the receiver's own `E`. Heard as a written class it arrives as `S`, with the weight of evidence, and a
  field trained only on written neighbours trusts a neighbour fully, so a self-consistent release is a fixed point however wrong, and
  its confidence is bought from the field itself. That is the junction record's §8 item, its own echo known as its own, and no
  temperature the field holds supplies it (agent-inferred).
- **What could bite it, recovered.** October 8 read this lens on the constitution: a learning medium of joints driven by the reached

  the evidence returns, which is largest where a confident release contradicts what the passage holds.
- **Read: the bite is a warmer field, not yet the evidence (G170 to G173, `read-breakdown`).** After each sweep a copy of the learner

**What follows.** Self-induction in this build is limited by what the field can hear, not by how hot it runs. Heat lowers the level a
