## The failure this step works on

On GSM8K the byte route's tower codes a calculation's result digits at 1.23 bits each where the same
expression was calculated in training and 4.10 where it was not, above a blind guess among ten
digits (3.32), with the scale zeros of each result read apart (0.69 and 1.38). It holds the faces it
met and has located none of the cycles that make new ones. Brandon, October 23: whether the arithmetic
is a failure turns on whether the machine still fails after it has met many interacting digits, and a
number is its digits on cycles at a level of the base's tower, the level only which step.

## The claim fixed first

On a generated terrain of sums, differences and products with operand pairs held out, the tower's
code of a held-out result's content digits falls below a blind guess, 3.32 bits a digit (the
terrain's own marginal of result digits is 3.22).

## The terrain and the read

`tools/arithmetic_terrain.py` generates every `a+b=c`, `a-b=c` (`a ≥ b`) and `a*b=c` for operands
`0..99` (uniform digits, so the carry is exercised: the GSM8K passage's own sums carry out of 21% of
places), one a line, and holds out a keyed one in ten of the operand pairs (the unordered pair for
`+` and `*`, so a commuted pair is never seen): 22,551 training lines (216,131 bytes) and 2,499 held
out. The truth is generated by exact integer arithmetic; a terrain may generate its truth, and only
its text reaches the machine, which gets no calculator, digit table, place counter or carry routine.

The tower is the gated moment tower of the previous records at the byte classes (`hnn::tower`,
width 64, 64 modes, 256 contacts, two levels, eight streams, sections of 128, rate `2^−7`). The new
exterior read `read-lines` (`examples/solenoidal_alpha/complete_language/tower_train.rs`) reads each
development line alone from rest through its `=`, codes the result class by class, and lets the tower
write its own result by four agreeing draws; `tools/arithmetic_lines.py` reads the codes by operation
and place, the units place 0, a result's trailing zeros (its level) apart.

- **A product is written at the right size and level, and wrong on its cycles.** The length, the
  leading digit and the zeros a shared prime forces are faces of size and collapse; the residue of a
  product of units is a turn (on the units of `ℤ/q` the index chart turns `×` into `+`), and every turn
  of a product is at chance: the residues mod 9 and 11 and the units of a product of units.
- **A sum's tens are composed and its carry dropped.** Where the units carried and the tens are
  wrong they are the tens without the carry in 22 of 41 lines, and in the duplex in 36 of 37: the
  tens digits' turn on the ring of ten is held, the carry (the cocycle between places) is not.
- **A one-digit difference opens on the face of a leading digit.** Whatever the units are, it is
  written as 1 or as a two-digit number beginning with 1; a difference's wrong units are not the
  operands' sum's (at or below the chance of one in nine), so the operation's sign is not the slip.
- **Reading from both sides creates no face neither side holds.** The duplex's meeting codes a
  product's tens at 3.41 bits a digit against its forward's 3.40, and of 224 products only 99
  refinements settle within eight sweeps. A held face gains the same bits in any reading order (the
  chain rule), so the reading order is not why the middle places are blind; what is missing is the
  composition of the turns the operands carry.

The cause in the tower's law: a gated mode's symbol keys only its modulus, `λ_t = |λ|^(c r_t) u`, so
each mode turns at its founded rate whatever arrives, and the arriving symbols only open and close
it. A residue is an angle accumulated from the turns its digits carry; no mode is turned by a digit.

## Why a written product is near its value and a difference can end early

[agent-inferred, October 23, from the reads above] Every face the gated tower writes right is
constant over the met pairs around an unmet one; every face it misses is a phase that turns from one
met pair to the next.

- **Near on size, off on its cycles.** An unmet product is read through the met pairs around it.
  Their sizes agree, `log ab = log a + log b` moving little from one pair to the next, so the length
  (209 of 224 after twelve passes), the leading digit and the level add up. So do the collapses: an
  operand sharing a prime with `q` zeroes the product on `ℤ/q` whatever the other operand (146 of 156
  on 2, 78 of 84 on 5), and the units of a product of units of `ℤ/10` is one cell of a ten-by-ten
  crossing every cell of which was met (28 of 40). A residue on a ring whose cycle spans the whole
  operand is a character `e^(2πi k ab/q)`; over neighbouring pairs it turns, and summed it cancels
  unless `kab/q` is nearly constant there, so the met paths interfere destructively and the residue
  is drawn at chance: 30 of 224 on 9, 20 of 224 on 11. A product's tens digit is such a phase (the
  cross crossings `a₁b₀ + a₀b₁` and the carry out of `a₀b₀`) and costs 3.30 bits. `92*65=6010`
  (5,980) has the size, the length and both collapses, and draws its middle digits.
- **A difference opens on the caustic of its leading place.** Writing from the left, the tower draws
  a difference's first digit before it has located the level. Over the terrain a difference's
  leading digit falls off linearly from 1 (954 of the 5,050 differences lead with 1, 146 with 9),
  and where the operands' tens are equal or adjacent both levels' paths pass through 1: the
  one-digit result `1` and every two-digit result `1x`. The first written digit is the face where
  those paths converge: 22 of the 25 unseen one-digit differences are written beginning with 1
  (`74-72=1`). With a first digit drawn, the line's end is drawn at a level not yet located, and a
  two-digit difference can end after one digit (`99-56=5`): 21 of 135 differences are written at the
  wrong length, against 15 of 241 sums and 15 of 224 products. A difference's wrong units are its
  operands' sum's units in 4 of 65 lines, under the chance of one in nine: the sign is read, and the
  slip is the level.
- **A sum's places are turned and their coupling dropped.** A sum's units cost 0.50 bits and its tens
  1.37; where the units carried and the tens are wrong, they are the tens without the carry in 20 of
  30. Each place's turn on the ring of ten is held; the carry, the winding that couples the units'
  circle to the tens' (the helix's lift), is not. `71+41=102` writes the units and the hundreds and
  draws the tens.

## The turned mode

[definition; agent-inferred, October 23] The cause named above is in the law: a gated mode's symbol
keys only its modulus. A turned mode (`TowerDeclaration::turned`, the module's docs; atlas
`hnn.tower-turned-mode`) is keyed on its turn too, `s_t = 2^(−⌈log₂ w⌉) (W_φ x + b_φ)` half turns
and `λ_t = ρ_t u e^(iπ s_t)`, so a mode can carry a residue as its accumulated phase. The turn opens
at zero, so the turned tower opens on its gated twin; `|e^(iπ s)| = 1`, so the gated mode's passivity
bound holds unchanged and no new Lean statement is owed for it. Its covector, the move's turn share
times `π`, agrees with the code's difference (tested).

## The pumped mode

[definition; agent-inferred, October 23] The lock is the parametron's pump, so a pumped mode
(`TowerDeclaration::pumped`, the module's docs; atlas `hnn.tower-pumped-mode`) mixes a symbol-keyed
reflection into its turn: `h_t = α_t h_(t−1) + β_t conj(h_(t−1)) + γ_t B x_t`, `α_t = ρ_t(1 − |κ_t|)U_t`
with `U_t` the turned unit, `β_t = ρ_t κ_t e^(iπ p_t)`, the depth `κ_t = 2 r_t − 1` from a base-two
logistic of `W_κ x + b_κ`, the axis `p_t` read as the turn is. As a real `2×2` move its determinant is
`ρ²(1 − 2|κ|)` and its trace `2ρ(1 − |κ|) cos θ_U`: it turns while `(1 − |κ|)² cos² θ_U < 1 − 2|κ|`, and
past that its in-phase axis boosts and its quadrature is shed, so the mode's phase settles on one of
two sheets across the pump's axis; on the edge it shears. These are the Swing's three kinds, keyed
cell by cell. The move's real-linear norm is `|α| + |β| = ρ_t`, so the gated passivity bound holds
with the same `γ_t`; the norm identity is owed in Lean (#410). The pump opens at zero, so a pumped
tower opens on its turned twin (tested: their codes agree exactly), and its covectors agree with the
code's difference (tested).

## Looking again

[agent-inferred, October 23] The near misses vary with the state a line meets: across the eight
reads above, 73+31 is written 104 in four and 105, 106 or 114 in the others, and 71+41 is written
112 in four and 102, 113 or 122 in the others. The right result is within reach of the moment, and
each read gives it one look. `read-lines-again` gives it more: after the fork writes its rest in the
stream, it carries what it wrote, its line's end and the line's head once more, and releases again,

is read apart from drawing again. The first look spends the key file as the stream read did, so its
releases are that read's (a check on the read); the later looks draw from the file's second half.
None of the towers trained on a line followed by itself, so a look may only repeat the one before.

## Looking inside training

[agent-inferred, October 23] A look the tower never trained on can only repeat its first: nothing in
its passage ever followed its own answer. The look moves inside training
(`train-looking-pumped-tower`, `Looking` in the training example). At each moment the terrain
declares (the `=` at which a line asks for its result), the tower releases its own result from the
state its stream has reached, by four agreeing draws from its stream's share of a key file; that
release and its line's end re-enter what the stream reads, the line's head arrives again, and then
the line's true result. Only what arrives is charged (`TowerLearner::section_charged`): the
release is the tower's own and brings no news, so its faces send no covector and the certificate
does not count them (the reafference row, `receipt.reafference-lag`: a receiver cancels its own
release at its own clock's phase). The true result's covector returns through the state the
tower's own release moved, so what the tower learns is what a look after its own should write.
This is the tower's own output reconfiguring its next input, with nothing authored about right or
wrong: the terrain's line arrives as it always did.

Frustration is what the looks read [agent-inferred, joining Brandon's lens of October 23]. A loop
of locks whose couplings multiply to −1 has no placement that satisfies every lock, and its least
energy is lifted by 2 for each such loop, declared from the loop's parity
(`holon::parametron`, `the_least_locked_sheet_energy_is_twice_the_frustration_less_the_edges`). In
learning it is the part of an arrival that no placement of the navigators reaches: what the true
result still costs however often the tower looks. `read-lines-again` now writes that cost from
where each look stands, so every line reads two numbers, the floor (the least cost any look
reaches: the frustration) and how fast the looks fall toward it (the gap). Many wrong placements at
nearly one cost close the gap, which is what a product wandering through 470, 410, 420, 410 and
490 reads as. Coprime cycles cannot all close together (`ratio.comma-never-closes`), and in a
product's digits the middle place stays frustrated until the carries below it are placed: the
place where every tower here is still near blind.

The claim fixed before the run: a pumped tower trained this way for 12 passes, with `ap_t1`'s
declaration, terrain, founding keys and rate, read by `read-lines-again` over first-met unseen lines
(at most eight looks), writes more whole results right at its last look than at its first in each
operation; more of those lines turn right than turn wrong; and in each operation its last look
writes at least as many right as its own redraw's.

A second read was declared during training, before any look was read: `read-lines-carried`, which
looks as `read-lines-again` does and whose stream carries each written line's first look, line end
and head again before the true result, the passage the tower trained in. In `read-lines-again` the

the carried read is reported beside it.

- **The look learned to repair.** Trained on its own attempts, the tower's looks carry the true

  tower's own release, and an uncharged byte sends no covector. So the prediction the tower makes
  where a question first arrives was never compared with anything: the truth was charged only after
  the attempt, its line end and the head again. The tower learned to answer a second time and
  never the first, and the plain held-out text, where every question arrives once, rose to 9.06

  compared nothing.
- **Frustration reads as floor and gap.** The plain tower's floor is 0.54 bits a line on sums and
  5.36 on products, its first look within 0.12 and 0.33 bits of it: the gap is closed before it
  looks, so its looks wander among placements of nearly one cost (the products' 470, 410, 420,

The next loop compares the attempt with the truth where the attempt stands: at each byte the tower
releases, the declared comparison is that byte's face against the true line's byte at the same
place (the true line end where the attempt runs short), its covector `p − q` charged, while the
stream still reads the tower's own byte. The look then trains the first arrival and the repair
together, and its acceptance is this claim plus the plain tower's first-look counts.

## The recorded failures this step could repeat

- **Catered machinery.** The terrain's truth is generated exactly, outside the machine; the tower
  receives only text and locates every relation itself.
- **Seen material graded as unseen.** Held-out pairs are keyed on the unordered pair for `+` and
  `*`; the development text alternates held-out and seen lines so both are read alike.
- **Bits read as progress.** The claim was fixed before the run and is a code of unmet results
  against a blind guess; the written lines are shown as they came, ungraded.
- **Text run on its codec's grain as the exception.** The terrain is text; nothing in the tower or
  the read names a digit or an operation, and the place read is an exterior partition.
