Refs #405 #404. Follows [the letters' copy](2026-10-23_THE_LETTERS_COPY_LOWERS_THE_CODE_AND_THE_ANSWERS_STILL_OPEN_ON_NAMES_OF_THEIR_OWN.md)

## The failure this step works on

On GSM8K the byte route's tower codes a calculation's result digits at 1.23 bits each where the same
expression was calculated in training and 4.10 where it was not, above a blind guess among ten
digits (3.32), with the scale zeros of each result read apart (0.69 and 1.38). It holds the faces it
met and has located none of the cycles that make new ones. Brandon, October 23: whether the arithmetic
is a failure turns on whether the machine still fails after it has met many interacting digits, and a
number is its digits on cycles at a level of the base's tower, the level only which step.

## The claim fixed first

On a generated terrain of sums, differences and products with operand pairs held out, the tower's
code of a held-out result's content digits falls below a blind guess, 3.32 bits a digit (the
terrain's own marginal of result digits is 3.22).

## The terrain and the read

`tools/arithmetic_terrain.py` generates every `a+b=c`, `a-b=c` (`a ≥ b`) and `a*b=c` for operands
`0..99` (uniform digits, so the carry is exercised: the GSM8K passage's own sums carry out of 21% of
places), one a line, and holds out a keyed one in ten of the operand pairs (the unordered pair for
`+` and `*`, so a commuted pair is never seen): 22,551 training lines (216,131 bytes) and 2,499 held
out. The truth is generated by exact integer arithmetic; a terrain may generate its truth, and only
its text reaches the machine, which gets no calculator, digit table, place counter or carry routine.

The tower is the gated moment tower of the previous records at the byte classes (`hnn::tower`,
width 64, 64 modes, 256 contacts, two levels, eight streams, sections of 128, rate `2^−7`). The new
exterior read `read-lines` (`examples/solenoidal_alpha/complete_language/tower_train.rs`) reads each
development line alone from rest through its `=`, codes the result class by class, and lets the tower
write its own result by four agreeing draws; `tools/arithmetic_lines.py` reads the codes by operation
and place, the units place 0, a result's trailing zeros (its level) apart.

Read in the base's tower:

- **The units crossing is located where the level is known.** A four-digit product's units digit
  costs 0.35 bits: the crossing of the operands' units digits on the ring of ten. A difference whose
  result is one digit costs 3.97 bits at that digit: writing from the left, the tower has not located
  which step the result sits on, so it codes its first digit as a tens digit.
- **The leading face is located.** A product's leading digit costs about one bit.
- **Where the crossings pile up it is blind.** A product's tens digit is the units of the two
  cross crossings `a₁b₀ + a₀b₁` plus the carry out of `a₀b₀`, and it costs 3.2 to 3.5 bits at
  every length.

## GSM8K with a terrain of its own calculations

The same tower trained three passes on the prepared GSM8K training text (3,528,168 bytes), and on
that text with 24,000 drawn calculations spread evenly through it as paragraphs of
`<<a op b=c>>c` lines (`arithmetic_terrain.py drawn 3 6000`: operands of one to three digits with
uniform digits, the four operations, every operand pair that the held-out text calculates left out;
418,649 bytes more). The held-out text is the 747 problems; its calculations' result digits are
partitioned by whether their expression was calculated in training (`tools/gsm8k/calc_roles.py`).
Bits a byte after three passes:

| | seen results' digits | unseen results' digits | results' scale zeros | everything else | all |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GSM8K alone | 2.945 | 3.536 | 1.423 | 2.252 | 2.260 |
| with the terrain | 2.864 | 3.208 | 1.686 | 2.353 | 2.358 |

The terrain lowers the unseen results' digits by 0.33 bits, to just under a blind guess (3.32), and
raises everything else by 0.10 (agent-inferred: the terrain's sections take part of each pass's
deposits from the prose). Meeting more interacting digits did not locate the middle places' cycles:
the terrain of the previous sections shows the same tower blind at a product's tens after 24 passes.

## The recorded failures this step could repeat

- **Catered machinery.** The terrain's truth is generated exactly, outside the machine; the tower
  receives only text and locates every relation itself.
- **Seen material graded as unseen.** Held-out pairs are keyed on the unordered pair for `+` and
  `*`; the development text alternates held-out and seen lines so both are read alike.
- **Bits read as progress.** The claim was fixed before the run and is a code of unmet results
  against a blind guess; the written lines are shown as they came, ungraded.
- **Text run on its codec's grain as the exception.** The terrain is text; nothing in the tower or
  the read names a digit or an operation, and the place read is an exterior partition.
