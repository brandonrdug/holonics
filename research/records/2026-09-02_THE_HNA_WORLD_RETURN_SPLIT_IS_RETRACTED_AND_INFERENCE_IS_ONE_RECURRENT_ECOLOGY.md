## Ruling

[definition] The result of one holonic operation is the successor ecology used by the next
operation. This is the inference loop. An LLM output being appended or re-presented for the next
token is an ordinary example. A user message, tool result, compiler response, theorem-kernel
diagnostic, or sensor reading may likewise become the next occurrence. None is a privileged
`WorldReturn`, reward, teacher signal, or permission to update morphology.

[definition] An operation is a new causal occurrence even when one receiver reports equal values
before and after. The complete ecology advances in chronology and lineage. Its activation/current
projection may change while its constitutive morphology remains equal; another operation may also
change morphology. Neither equality nor inequality of serialized bytes defines inference or
learning.

## Formal correction

[definition] The HNA primitive is:

```text
operate : Ecology -> Occurrence -> Emission x Trace x Ecology
```

with recurrence law

```text
successor(step_t) = predecessor(step_(t+1)).
```

The trace must retain the local-current, aggregation, reaction, chronology, incidence, receiver,
and open-obstruction testimony actually used by the operation. It may not be manufactured after an
address-only table lookup.

[definition] At a finite neural chart:

```text
J_(E,g,x)(i,j) = LocalCurrent(E.morphology, g, x, i, j)
x'(i)          = Reaction(E.morphology, g, i, sum_j J_(E,g,x)(i,j))
E'             = Advance(E, occurrence, x').
```

A weight matrix may present a constitutive cross-section `W`; `W x` is current. Bytes, byte
differences, identifiers, counts, statuses, scores, and losses are receiver/application faces until
a complete constitutive map states otherwise.

[definition] The existing `InferenceCirculationReturn.morphologyFixed` and
`CultivationPassage.returnIsLater` formal split does not govern HNA. HNA0 must return a recurrence
owner which admits both fixed-morphology and morphology-changing operations. Remount, ablation,
withdrawal, replay, source-access, and held-out comparison remain optional receivers for the narrow
storage, attribution, compression, or evaluation claims they actually answer.
