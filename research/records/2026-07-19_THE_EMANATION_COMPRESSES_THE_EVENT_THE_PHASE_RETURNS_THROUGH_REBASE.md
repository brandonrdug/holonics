## VI · The zeta distribution is an exact self-similar phase

For real `sigma>1`,

```text
p_sigma(n) = n^(-sigma) / zeta(sigma).
```

Unique factorization embeds each positive integer as a finite-support valuation vector

```text
v(n)=(v_2(n),v_3(n),v_5(n),...),
log n=sum_p v_p(n) log p.
```

Primes are the irreducible basis directions, prime powers are quantized steps along one axis, and
composites occupy several axes at once. The Euler product yields

```text
p_sigma(n)=product_p (1-p^(-sigma)) p^(-sigma v_p(n)),
Pr(v_p(N)=k)=(1-p^(-sigma))p^(-sigma k).
```

The valuation axes are independent geometric recurrences. For every positive integer `m`,

```text
Pr(m divides N)=m^(-sigma),
Law(N/m | m divides N)=Law(N).
```

Indeed,

```text
Pr(N/m=k | m divides N)
  = ((mk)^(-sigma)/zeta(sigma)) / m^(-sigma)
  = k^(-sigma)/zeta(sigma).
```

Thus

```text
restrict to mN -> re-base by division by m -> recover the same law.
```

The event remains unique because it carries the added valuation `v(m)`; the normalized phase law is
invariant. This is an exact mathematical instance of a new frame within a self-similar phase.

The amplitude lift

```text

```

obeys

```text

  = m^(-sigma/2) exp(-i(t/2)log m) alpha_(sigma,t)(n)
  = exp(kappa_m+i omega_m) alpha_(sigma,t)(n),

kappa_m=-(sigma/2)log m,
omega_m=-(t/2)log m.
```

The prime-valuation lattice, zeta probability, and annular scale--turn return therefore meet
exactly in the initial half-plane.

At `sigma=1`, `zeta(sigma)` diverges and the normalized zeta distribution ceases to exist. This is
the exact normalization boundary of that receiver. Other observer faces have other boundaries;
