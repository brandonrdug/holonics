## 3. Four things a distribution can save, each by its own law

`agent-inferred` as a join; each law is `from training` with its name.

| What is saved | By what | What keeps it from being saved | The theorem |
|---|---|---|---|
| Work `W` | a quotient: what no receiver distinguishes is computed once, as one source | a receiver that resolves every member | fast multipole (Greengard and Rokhlin 1987), hierarchical matrices (Hackbusch 1999): a far field that the kernel smooths has low rank, and `n²` pairs become `n log n` or `n` |
| Span `D` | associativity: a chain is rebracketed into a tree of depth `⌈log₂ n⌉` | a section (a rounding) or a nonlinearity inside each step | prefix sums and scans (Blelloch 1990); for a general circuit no algebra is known to shorten it: the circuit value problem is P-complete (Ladner 1975), so polylog depth for it would give NC = P |
| Waiting | commutativity and monotonicity: partial results merge in any order of arrival | a step that must know nothing more will arrive (a threshold over everything, a negation, a global normalization) | CALM (Hellerstein 2010; Ameloot, Neven and Van den Bussche 2013): a program has a coordination-free consistent realization iff it is monotone; a replicated state whose merge is a join-semilattice converges under any order of delivery (Shapiro, Preguiça, Baquero and Zawirski 2011) |
| Wall time at fixed `W` | lanes `p`, dividing `W` alone | `D`, launches and crossings, and energy | Brent's bound, held |

The ordering matters. Lanes divide `W` by a constant and are paid for in energy and crossings. A quotient changes
the exponent of `W`. Associativity changes the exponent of `D`. Commutativity removes the wait. So the largest
savings are algebraic, and a parallel machine only collects them.

The ruler's first check (§1 of the output): 1,000 affine steps `x ↦ a x + b` on integers scan in depth 10 by a
doubling tree and equal the serial chain at every place; the same contracting chain with its store released to
`2^(−16)` at every step (a section inside the chain) gives 247 reads of 1,000 that differ from its orbit released
once at the read. That is the scan of `field_pair_scan` read at 16:44 UTC (the record of the value read at a
place): it is serial only because it rounds its store each step.

## 4. Time from our side: a receiver's count of crossings

`agent-inferred`. A receiver's elapsed time is its pairing with the passage, windings plus phase (the aeon's law);
for a computation, its count of crossings of its own section. Brent's bound reads on the objects:
- `D` is the aeon: the longest chain of "after what" among the events. No lane shortens it.
- `W/p` is the holarchy's breadth over the receivers: "on what", shared among `p` of them.
- A distribution saves the receiver's time only where it moves events out of the chain into the breadth.

Pascal's pyramid says how much can move. A cell's multinomial counts the ordered words that a commutative merge
cannot tell apart (`code.pascal-pyramid-type-width`); every one of them is a lawful schedule, the balanced tree
among them, so the schedule is free to choose. The cell's width, the logarithm of its multinomial, is the order
forgotten, and it is the schedule's freedom. What the law keeps in order, the sections inside a chain and the
steps that do not associate, is the information the schedule must carry, and that is `D`. Distribution saves
time to the extent that order is forgotten, and no further.

The Swing's moves are of the first kind: a turn, a boost and a shear are `SL(2)` moves, and a chain of them is
a prefix product, scanned in log depth like the carries of `radix.adder-bracketing`. Order is kept where the
release stands: a lock's threshold, a draw, a section.

## 5. Where the fast core wins

`agent-inferred` from the held rows and the measured figures. With a term's time on a host lane as the unit, the
card's lane `s` times slower, `p_c` card lanes and `p_h` host lanes, Brent's bound on each surface gives
`T_card ≈ W s/p_c + D s + L t_L` and `T_host ≈ W/p_h + D`. The card wins when the parallelism passes
`(W/D)* = (s − 1)/(1/p_h − s/p_c)`, launches aside.

From the machine's measured figures (the flock record of November 11: one host lane took about 234 times the
card's time for one of the line's regions; the card has 10,240 lanes), one host lane matches at most
`10,240/234 ≈ 44` card lanes. The ruler gives `(W/D)* ≈ 541` with 12 host lanes and `≈ 1,144` with 24. So the
card wins a kind of work only where a thousand or so terms stand beside each step of its chain; narrower than
that, the fast core wins. The figure 44 is an upper reading, since the card did not run that region at full
occupancy; a truer `s` is smaller and the crossing lower.

The host's ground, then, by kind:
- **Chains of small dependent steps**, where `D` dominates, and above all chains of launches: each step of such a
  chain costs the card at least 23 µs, about a hundred thousand of a host core's cycles.
- **Wide words**: the host forms a 64 by 64 bit product in one instruction, and the card builds it from narrow
  ones. The precision law's answer is to cut terms as narrow as their information allows, which turns a wide word
  into breadth.
- **Kinds with few co-present terms**, under `n*`, and work whose terms already stand on the host: a move across
  the port adds a 94 µs crossing each way, so a kind in mid-chain stays where its operands are (held).
- **Order a law keeps**: a section inside a chain, a decision on everything that came before.

The card's ground is the rest: commutative sums released once, associative scans, and every pair or site whose
terms are co-present. The split is by kind of work, as measured on November 7 (the same work on both surfaces
gained 0.998 of its time).

## 6. Energy: why breadth is cheap and haste is dear

`from training`, with the join `agent-inferred`.
- **The thermal channel.** At `r` bits a use of a channel with white noise of density `N₀`, the energy a bit is at
  least `N₀ (2^r − 1)/r` (Shannon). At fixed total rate spread over `N` channels, `r` falls and so does the energy a
  bit, toward `N₀ ln 2`; with thermal noise `N₀ = kT`, so the floor is `kT ln 2`, the Landauer figure
  (`floor.landauer-erasure`). The ruler, for 8 bits a tick: 31.9 `N₀` a bit on one channel, 3.75 on two, 1.00 on
  eight, 0.724 on 64, 0.695 on 1,024. Haste on one channel costs exponentially here and quadratically in the
  quantum tube (held); in both, the cheapest bit is carried slowly on many channels.
- **Silicon.** Switching energy is `C V²` an operation, and the voltage a gate needs rises with its clock, so the
  energy an operation grows about as the square of the clock: `N` lanes at `f/N` do the same work at about `1/N²`
  of the energy an operation. Since the voltage stopped falling with the feature size (Dennard's scaling, mid-2000s),
  more operations a joule have come only from more, slower lanes. A 5 GHz core buys latency; a card of 2.5 GHz lanes
  buys throughput.
- **Moving a word.** On today's chips fetching a word from memory costs one to three orders of magnitude more energy
  than an arithmetic operation on it, from an on-chip cache to off-chip memory (Horowitz 2014). The engine reads this in time: the contact was measured bound
  by the octets it gathers (3.6 ns a pair met against the 0.886 ns the gather yardstick gives a source's 128-octet
  row; `tower.field-engine-count`). A synapse keeps its store at the contact, holding, weighting and passing in one
  place, so nothing is gathered: reading costs a flux through a port where a signal arrives, which is the store law
  of this guide.

## 7. Biology's breadth and diversity

`from training`, with the joins `agent-inferred`.
- **Cost per bit rises with the rate a cell carries** (Laughlin, de Ruyter van Steveninck and Anderson 1998;
  Balasubramanian, Kimber and Berry 2001), so a nervous system spreads its information over many slow cells. On the
  brain's budget only about one percent of cortical neurons can be active at once (Lennie 2003). Cost follows news:
  a spike is news, and a quiet cell costs little (`deposition.cost-follows-surprise`).
- **A neuron is a quotient and one section.** It sums thousands of synaptic inputs in whatever order they arrive and
  thresholds once after the sum: the lawful pattern of the precision law (a quotient, then a section standing once).
  Its integration is coordination-free by CALM, since accumulation is monotone, and only the threshold decides.
- **Diversity is subtracted.** For squared loss, an ensemble's error is its members' mean error less their mean
  spread about the ensemble (Krogh and Vedelsby 1995). For log loss under log-linear pooling, with the pooled face
  `p̃ ∝ Π_i p_i^(1/M)`,

  ```text
  CE(q, p̃) = (1/M) Σ_i CE(q, p_i) − (1/M) Σ_i D(p̃ ‖ p_i),      and the subtracted term is −log Z ≥ 0
  ```

  for every target `q` (Heskes 1998). The ruler checks the first in rationals on 200 draws and the second on 300
  (largest gap `8.9 × 10^(−16)`). A copy adds nothing to the subtracted term; a member that differs from the pool
  lowers the pooled code by its divergence at no extra cost a member. A bank of unlike frictions carries every reach
  where one friction carries one (`wave.matched-friction-reach`); networks of neurons with diverse time constants
  learn more robustly than uniform ones (Perez-Nieves, Leung, Dragotti and Goodman 2021).
- **Small worlds.** Local wiring with a few long ties has a diameter near `log N` at linear wiring (Watts and
  Strogatz 1998). Kleinberg (2000): with long ties drawn at probability `∝ d^(−k)` on a `k`-dimensional lattice,
  local greedy routing reaches any target in `O(log² N)` steps, and at every other exponent it needs a power of `N`.
  The ruler, on a ring with one long tie a place: at 16,384 places the greedy route takes 47 steps at `d^(−1)` and 48
  at the contact's octave staircase `2^(−⌊log₂ d⌋)`, against 115 with uniform ties and 703 at `d^(−2)`; from 1,024
  places those grow 2.3 and 2.5 times (the ratio of `log²` is 1.96), against 4.3 and 9.8. **The contact's falloff,
  equal prior mass an octave, is the navigable exponent in one dimension.** This is Brandon's "degrees of
  separation" of 17:08 UTC read as a rate: the number of relays between two places of a region.

## 8. Classical machine learning, criticized and lifted

`agent-inferred` as a reading; the named results `from training`.
- **What it does well.** A layer is a sum and then a nonlinearity, the lawful pattern of §7, and a matrix product
  reuses each word it fetches many times, which is why it fits a card.
- **The transformer traded work for span.** A recurrent net has `W = n` and `D = n`; attention has `W = n²` and a
  depth set by its layers. Hardware rich in lanes rewards that trade, and the method that won is the one the
  hardware favoured (Hooker 2020). The lift: an affine recurrence composes associatively and scans with `W = n` and
  `D = log n` (§3; the linear-recurrent and state-space models use this), and it is spoiled only by a section or a
  nonlinearity inside each step. The Swing's moves are such a composition (§4).
- **All pairs are computed, including those no receiver tells apart.** A far field that the kernel smooths has low
  rank (§3), and linear and sparse attentions approximate it by engineering. The theory states when a crowd is one
  source to its receiver (§9).
- **Every step coordinates.** Backpropagation locks the forward, the backward and the update in sequence (Jaderberg
  et al. 2017), and data-parallel training sums one global gradient across every device each step. CALM says
  coordination is needed only for non-monotone steps. A nervous system deposits locally, as arrivals accumulate, and
  broadcasts only a slow, narrow signal of news (dopamine's reward prediction error: Schultz, Dayan and Montague
  1997). The machine's deposition already takes only the covectors that reached a locus (the elementary objects);
  its sections, with one deposit each, are the barrier it still keeps, lawful where a slow variable's delay is
  declared (`holarchy.crossing-join-schedule-free`).
- **Copies, not diversity.** Data-parallel replicas are held equal by the global sum, so their spread, the term §7
  subtracts, is zero by construction, and the growth comes from more data through one configuration. A mixture of
  experts is machine learning's step toward breadth (sparse activation, a routed address); a pressure that drives every
  expert toward equal use works against landmarks.
- **Words move for every token.** Every weight is fetched for every token whether or not anything new arrived; a
  synapse is read only where a signal arrives (§6).

## 9. The machine test: thinning an open row's far field

`agent-inferred` throughout, on `tree` owners.

**What G140 cut.** G140 held written rows at their opening, so the open rows met unrefined sources: it cut the
relays. A refined source carries what reached it from places the open row never meets (`landmark.carry-worth-state`),
so over the ticks the contact diffuses in hops, and the 3.29 nats are the price of cutting the hops. G140 says nothing
yet of an open row's direct far pairs. The theory keeps every row's ticks and thins only those.

**When a crowd is one source.** With the receiver's word `a`, a crowd `C` of definite sources with words `b_j`, mean
`b̄`, mean square spread `s²`, radius `r_C = max_j |b_j − b̄|`, skin `D` and temperature `T`, the lock
`2D Re⟨a, b⟩ − D²|b|²` gives, for the crowd's share in nats against the share of `|C|` sources at the mean word,

```text
−T D² s²  ≤  log( (1/|C|) Σ_j e^(T lock_j) ) − T lock(b̄)  ≤  2 T D |a − D b̄| r_C ,      r_C ≤ s √|C|
```

The lower end is Jensen's inequality (the cross term averages to zero); the upper end bounds the largest member,
whose lock exceeds the mean word's by `2D Re⟨a − D b̄, b_j − b̄⟩ − D²|b_j − b̄|²`. The ruler checks it on 400 draws.
Read on the objects:
- `a − D b̄` is the receiver's slip against the crowd's mean arriving face, the pair contact's `Δ`. Where a receiver
  locks a crowd's mean, only the spread term remains.
- The skin is the low-pass. On a paced head `D_m = e^(−2π|σ_i − σ_j|/n_m)`, so a far crowd's fast modes pass the test
  by the skin alone, and the far field is smooth because the skin decays, the multipole method's reason. A still
  head's skin does not decay with distance, so its far sources can be resolved at any distance: the recall §15.9 of
  November 1 needed.
- The opening criterion (Barnes and Hut's, in this metric): a crowd whose bound passes a declared grain `ε` opens
  into its halves, down to members. The spread `s²` needs a mode's power prefix sums beside the word sums the crowds
  already keep; the radius, a range maximum (a max is a quotient), or the cruder `s √|C|` from the same prefix sums.
  An open row's pairs would then be its near field, its crowds and the members its lock resolves.
- **Direction (b) is the multilevel form of (a).** The level's restriction, its ticks and its prolongation are a
  two-level hierarchical matrix with learned bases, the coarse grid of multigrid. What the level carries of the far
  field is what an open row's direct far pairs no longer need.
- **The risk the record of November 1 names.** A crowd's covector moves its members' keys together, the same `δs_C`
  for each, so a member a receiver hears only in a crowd is never pulled apart for that receiver; a source wanted by
  its order shares no word with the receiver that wants it, and no address law could be learned toward a source never
  met sharply (§15.9). The criterion keeps sharp what the lock resolves now; what it would come to resolve under
  learning reaches the receiver only through other rows. The open row's own probes, its state's address across its
  seams, are the sharp channel a definite row already has; whether an open row's far mass lies in its probed classes
  decides whether that channel serves it.
