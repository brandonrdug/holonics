memory. A material world may know that one event incidence caused another because that relation is
part of its enacted law. The membrane must transport that knowledge without inventing it:

```text

world-local enacted law -> explicit directed incidence
native organ cut        -> live lineage capabilities
host or CUDA executor   -> receiver-relative meeting

```

The event-local position of a current is only an address into the borrowed cut. Numeric order has
no temporal or causal meaning. The separate relation row carries `from -> to`. Before conduct, the
mouth translates those two addresses to the already-opened or newly-bound lineage capabilities.
After conduct, the addresses and rows disappear. Only changed standing topology can affect later
machine conduct; the complete contact returns outward.

Empty relation population means actual absence. Repeating a declared row means plural incidence.
An unresolved directed meeting remains one complete open contact and contributes no fabricated
deed. These are different material cases and none can be reconstructed from the others.
