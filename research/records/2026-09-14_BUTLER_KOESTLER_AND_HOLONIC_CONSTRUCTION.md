# Holor Calculus, SpiralOS and Holonic Construction

[established-bounded; source-inspected] Carey Glenn Butler's public corpus contains a substantial
philosophical programme, increasingly explicit geometric proposals, numerical mathematical
prototypes and a large body of human–LLM dialogue. Its overlap with Holonics is substantial:
whole/part organization, interior/exterior relations, perspective, phase, memory, curvature and
recursive generation. The public implementation is less complete than several descriptions of
SpiralOS and SpiralLLM suggest. Reading its actual equations reveals both useful constructions
and specific mistakes that can be repaired. Neither a dismissal of the entire programme nor an
identification of it with a completed HNN engine is supported.[^1][^2]

[interpretation] The most productive common question is how a situated object can carry its own
organization while participating in a larger interaction. The proposed correspondence is between
Butler's enriched holor and Holonics' incidence, material, current, source/receiver and preimage
structure. Its test is an actual transformed face: change the relevant frame, phase, boundary or
material and determine the consequence. Shared terminology or a declared awareness attribute
cannot discharge that test.

## Koestler supplies an important structural foundation

[established-bounded; source-inspected] In *The Ghost in the Machine*, Koestler places the holon
between an isolated atom and an undifferentiated whole. Its relative autonomy coexists with
participation in larger organization. His later SOHO formulation combines relatively stable
rules with flexible strategies and explicitly joins nested organization to lateral networks.
This is richer than a tree of sealed software modules.[^15][^16]

[established-bounded; source-inspected] SOHO's input/output discussion is particularly relevant:
reception abstracts according to a system's relevance criteria, while an implicit organization
can be elaborated into complex output. Section 6.9 joins perception to action and even names
humming a tune to assist auditory recall. Koestler's specific brain-evolution and social hypotheses
belong to their historical argument; they are not needed to reuse these structural distinctions.[^16]

[interpretation] A computational realization should therefore make the boundary, local law and
cross-boundary coupling explicit. A holon can change roles when the receiving boundary changes.
Its abstraction need not materialize every lower-scale degree of freedom; it must retain the
relations needed for the requested continuation. Internal imagery, heard sound and emitted sound
can be different receiving faces of the same generating activity. This connects Koestler to the
existing [receiver and music synthesis](2026-09-14_HEAR_THE_MUSIC_SITUATED_RELEASE_AND_SELF_MOTION.md)
without imposing a rigid hierarchy on HNN.

## What the mathematical programme actually constructs

[established-bounded; source-inspected] The foundation progresses from a proposed rotation law to
an enriched geometric configuration: section, metric, connection, curvature, trace data and
stance. HC IV makes the section–connection pair particularly explicit, using ordinary gauge
curvature and path transport. The useful question is which supplied variables represent an
actual observer or learning system, and which equations connect their transformations to a
received consequence.[^3][^4][^6]

[definition] A context-dependent generator $J$ with $J^2=-I$ supports the familiar identity
$\exp(\theta J)=\cos\theta\,I+\sin\theta\,J$, by separating the even and odd powers of its
series. Changing $J$ supplies an oriented family of complex structures. A general Lie-algebra
element instead has its own exponential; it does not automatically satisfy this two-term formula.
In Holonics these are constraint-defined modes with declared generators, normalization and
branches. There is no need to promote a decimal approximation to a fundamental constant.

[established-bounded; source-inspected] HC II and III introduce energy shaping, projected
optimization and a map from model parameters into holor configurations. The attention appendix
contains a usable regularizer sketch: entropy-band penalties, short-loop attention penalties
and a supplied ethical mask. Those are concrete proposed functions. They do not derive an
awareness manifold, a physical torsion field or the meaning of the mask from attention alone.[^4]

[interpretation] This distinction matters for the computational object. Ordinary tensors and
bundle sections can represent internal states and receiving apparatus. A holor can legitimately
add structure, constraints and permitted transformations, but that added structure must be
specified. The contrast between tensors that merely simulate and holors that participate does
not itself provide a realization map. HNN's distinction lies in its actual current/material/
incidence and source–receiver operation, not in whether its mathematics is executable.

## Independent mathematical checks and constructive repairs

[definition] The following calculations examine explicit source statements, with their versions
and conventions retained. They do not turn a local error into a verdict on every idea in the
corpus. [Independent rational checks](../experiments/butler_review/README.md) accompany the
arguments; no external SpiralOS code is executed by those checks.

### 1. Recover the dependent octant coordinates

[established-bounded; source-inspected] CI II's table has eight rows: every interior row has
Depth, and every exterior row has Scope. HC I subsequently writes the stance set as a Cartesian
product of four binary factors while still calling it eight-element. The earlier table supplies
the dependency needed to repair that presentation.[^7][^4]

[proved-derived] Encode the four displayed fields as bits $(i,m,p,f)$. The independent product
has $2^4=16$ members. The table's restriction $f=p$ has $2^3=8$; flipping all four bits
preserves that restriction. Thus eight is coherent as a declared dependent chart. The assertion
that it is the unrestricted four-factor product is the problem. This is directly relevant to
implementation: expanding coordinates without retaining their joint constraint changes the
represented object. It is not evidence that eight is the universal dimension of awareness.

### 2. Distinguish an identity from a regulating law

[established-bounded; source-inspected] HC I defines Micro as reciprocal Scope and Macro as
reciprocal Depth; the stated IAR ratio then follows identically. HC II assigns energy to a
mismatch between those ratios.[^4]

[proved-derived] With those definitions maintained exactly, the mismatch and its derivative
are zero everywhere. That term cannot regulate a depth/scope trade-off. It can become a useful
consistency loss if Micro and Macro are independently measured quantities, or a resource law
can separately constrain attainable depth and scope. These are different constructions.
Foveated sampling provides an example of the latter: resolution follows a calibrated receiver
and its sampling budget, rather than a reciprocal identity alone.

### 3. Repair the displayed connection before using its physical interpretation

[established-bounded; source-inspected] HC I §7.2 and its v1.1 republication specify a flat
Euclidean metric with only $\Gamma^x_{tx}=\tau/2$ and $\Gamma^x_{xt}=-\tau/2$, then describe the
connection as metric-compatible and affine-flat. The published PDF confirms those coefficients.
The later corpus does not replace this example with a derivation establishing those properties.[^4]

[proved-derived] Use the convention
$\nabla_\mu v^\lambda=\partial_\mu v^\lambda+\Gamma^\lambda_{\mu\nu}v^\nu$.
The connection matrices are

$$
\Gamma_t=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\0&\tau/2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\Gamma_x=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\-\tau/2&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

For constant $g=I$,
$(\nabla_tg)_{xx}=-\tau$, while
$[\Gamma_t,\Gamma_x]^x{}_t=-\tau^2/4$.
For nonzero $\tau$, these respectively refute metric compatibility and flatness. Computing
the divergence with the Levi-Civita connection instead silently changes the specified operator.

[proved-derived] A consistent replacement is
$\Gamma_t=0,\;\Gamma_x=-\tau J$, where
$J=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$.
Both connection matrices are skew, and their commutator is zero. The connection is therefore
metric-compatible and flat, with $T^x{}_{tx}=\tau$. A constant current $\Phi=(1,0)$ has affine
divergence $-\tau$. Pair torsion with the dual antisymmetric tensor having
$\chi_x{}^{tx}=1/2$, $\chi_x{}^{xt}=-1/2$: its scalar pairing is $\tau$.
With zero residual-curvature term, this particular HSE balances exactly. This repairs a
mathematical model; a measured interpretation as awareness or physical matter still requires
its constitutive and receiving map.

[established-bounded; source-inspected] The chronology also contains partial repairs. HC IV uses
a proper complex Hermitian pairing, improving HC I's real-valued transpose formula. HC II §5.2
writes the chirality tensor with dual indices. Those improvements should be retained, while the
remaining antisymmetric normalization and later mixing of scalar torsion with curvature forms
must be typed at their actual occurrence.[^4][^6]

### 4. Check diffusion signs and the discrete projected step

[proved-derived] On a flat periodic domain, hold the other HSE terms fixed at zero and write
$H=\operatorname{div}\Phi$,
$E=\tfrac12\int H^2$. Integration by parts gives
$\delta E/\delta\Phi=-\nabla H$. Its gradient descent is therefore
$\Phi_\tau=+c\nabla H$, for positive mobility $c$.
The negative sign written in HC II's proposed local term gives the opposite behavior: a sine
mode grows instead of diffusing away. Extra couplings may change a full system, but the displayed
term alone is not a descent term for that energy. The corrected term acts on the longitudinal
Hodge component; curl/shear dynamics requires its corresponding constitutive terms.[^4]

[counterexample; computational-witness] HC III §2.6 gives a discrete energy-decrease estimate
using the tangent-cone projection of the gradient itself. Let
$C=[0,1]$, $F(x)=1-x$, $x=1$, $\eta=1/2$, and take the valid Lipschitz bound $L=1$.
The projected step remains at one, so its loss stays zero. The gradient is $-1$,
which lies in the tangent cone and has norm one; the published estimate demands loss at most
$-3/8$. All these choices satisfy the theorem's stated setup, including a zero holor
regularization weight. The estimate is false as written.[^4]

[proved-derived] For a smooth objective with an $L$-Lipschitz gradient and a closed convex
set, use a fixed $0<\eta<2/L$ and retain the actual step
$x^+=P_C(x-\eta\nabla F(x))$. Projection optimality and the descent lemma give

$$
F(x^+)\le F(x)-\left(\frac1\eta-\frac L2\right)\|x^+-x\|^2.
$$

The projection inequality is $\nabla F(x)\cdot(x^+-x)\le-\|x^+-x\|^2/\eta$;
combining it with the smooth descent bound proves the displayed estimate.
The corresponding gradient mapping is
$G_\eta(x)=(x-x^+)/\eta$.
This gives a valid discrete residual and, with the stated compactness assumptions, the usual
stationarity conclusion. It does not say that every individual penalty decreases, that every
stationary point has zero HSE residual, or that no further learning is possible under another
representation or receiver.

### 5. Preserve compositional history in the morphism that needs it

[established-bounded; source-inspected] *Holors and Tensors* assumes admissibility is closed
under composition, then illustrates two admitted arrows whose direct composite is forbidden.
Its stated morphisms contain the underlying map and endpoint admissibility, so the illustrative
case conflicts with that closure assumption as written.[^17]

[proved-derived] There is a useful repair. An arrow can contain the underlying transformation
and an admitted path or sufficient composition witness. The composite path $A\to B\to C$
then exists even when a different shortcut $A\to C$ does not. Both can project to the same
underlying tensor transformation without being the same enriched arrow. Concatenation provides
composition; the forgetful functor preserves it. This is the role already served by addressed
Holonic spans and their retained joining equality. A relevant path class, generator or residual
may suffice; a complete raw event archive is not required by this argument.

[proved-derived] The related RTTP issue is equally concrete. A natural transformation
$\mathrm{Id}\Rightarrow U E$ need not be invertible. If extraction maps two objects to the
same tensor while a future receiver distinguishes them, no decoder of that tensor alone can
recover both. The missing distinction must remain in an auxiliary fibre or the receiver claim
must be restricted. Holonics' future-receiver compression and dynamic commuting equation address
exactly that condition. A stipulated return protocol is not itself its proof.[^4][^9][^17]

### 6. Develop a real consequence of the golden emitter series

[established-bounded; source-inspected] The X–XI materials propose
$T_\varphi=\sum_{k\ge1}\varphi^{-k}\zeta(\varphi^k)$ for the golden mode. This is a meaningful
scalar series, independently of its proposed interpretation as an emitter.[^18]

[proved-derived] For real $\varphi>1$, zeta is positive and decreasing on its real domain, so
$\zeta(\varphi^k)\le\zeta(\varphi)$. Geometric comparison proves convergence. More usefully,

$$
0<T_\varphi-\sum_{k=1}^N\varphi^{-k}\zeta(\varphi^k)
\le \zeta(\varphi^{N+1})\frac{\varphi^{-N}}{\varphi-1}.
$$

The integral bound $\zeta(s)\le1+1/(s-1)$, together with the golden constraint
$\varphi^2=\varphi+1,\varphi>1$, yields explicit rational upper bounds using $\varphi>8/5$.
Since each positive factor decreases as $\varphi$ increases, a completely rational bound is
$$
\operatorname{tail}_N\le
\left(\frac58\right)^N\frac53
\left(1+\frac{1}{(8/5)^{N+1}-1}\right).
$$
The inequality $\varphi>8/5$ follows by evaluating $x^2-x-1$ at $8/5$, obtaining $-1/25$,
and using its strict increase for $x\ge1/2$. The preceding analytic argument proves the bound;
the companion calculation evaluates its rational right-hand side at integer truncation levels.
This is a reusable generating relation with a remainder. A physical emission channel would additionally
specify units, transport and a receiving coupling.

[proved-derived] Neither this series nor mirror symmetry settles RH or Goldbach. A finite
prime-pair statistic is zero when its defining pair set is empty; its definition cannot establish
that the set is always nonempty. Likewise, a polynomial in
$z^2=(s-1/2)^2$ can have symmetric zeros off the central line. The actual zeta/xi source requires
more than reflection symmetry. HC VII's more careful meta-level discussion similarly supplies
extensions of viewpoints/theories, not a refutation of incompleteness for a fixed system under
Gödel's hypotheses.[^18][^19]

### 7. Preserve the analytic source in a phase interpretation

[established-bounded; source-inspected] The separate Riemann Return package's Derivation1
drops the magnitude weights from a zeta-series expression for conceptual clarity, then uses
critical-line symmetry to explain cancellation. Its confirmation appendix attributes a full
rigor endorsement to Grok. These additions are part of the published record, not independent
verification of the derivation.[^23]

[proved-derived] For $s=\sigma+it$, the factorization
$n^{-s}=n^{-\sigma}\exp(-it\log n)$ contains both magnitude and phase.
The ordinary series of unweighted phases cannot converge: every term has modulus one, whereas
convergence requires successive terms to tend to zero. It also contains no $\sigma$, so it
cannot distinguish one real-part locus from another. The ordinary absolutely convergent zeta
series belongs to $\sigma>1$; using its analytic continuation in the critical strip requires
a valid continued expression and its domain.

[proved-derived] A concrete repair in $0<\sigma<1$ is to use the convergent eta construction
and its actual factor:
$\zeta(s)=\eta(s)/(1-2^{1-s})$. The denominator is nonzero there because
$|2^{1-s}|=2^{1-\sigma}>1$. The existing Holonics analytic-source/jet and remainder owners
already provide the corresponding source-qualified route. Keeping that source permits a real
phase/current interpretation; deleting its weights or continuation does not. Symmetry still
does not prove that all zeros are fixed by the reflection.

## What is implemented, and what the reported metrics establish

[established-bounded; source-inspected] The repository is organized primarily as a publication,
specification and dialogue corpus. Its own map describes a future runtime layer. Standalone
source includes a YAML-to-JSON lattice builder, site/HUD utilities, a registry and provenance
helpers. That is executable infrastructure, but the lattice builder does not compile a field
operator merely because its name says “compile.” The sandbox registry's signing routine is a
placeholder even though its acknowledgement uses a verified status.[^2][^20]

[established-bounded; source-inspected] There is additional mathematical code in archives.
The HC VII test archive contains exactly 320 test methods across 14 files. Recovered package
members implement NumPy/SciPy chiral objects, path and simplicial operations, information geometry
and a reasoner dispatcher. The source ZIP has header/CRC defects, so a complete intact package
cannot be inferred from its central-directory list. These are real mathematical prototypes;
solver computation is not excluded from intelligence simply because it is not a language model.[^21]

[established-bounded; source-inspected] The demo uses synthetic in-memory examples. It does not
supply a trained language model, cultivation corpus, model checkpoint or the experiments needed
to reproduce the published large-scale hRAG/hCAG and kinfield metrics. Static presence of 320
methods verifies their count, not a 320-pass run or a coverage percentage. The recovered dispatcher
can also report verification when no verifier is registered. The published percentages therefore
remain author-reported results at this public evidence boundary.[^21]

[interpretation] The engineering lesson is not to reject prototypes. It is to connect each
capability claim to the operation actually returned: computed invariant, fitted relation,
generated field, measured reconstruction or explicit obstruction. Names, status fields and
passing tests answer their own questions. They do not establish a different mathematical or
product consequence merely by appearing together.

## The dialogue records expose a recognizable workflow failure

[established-bounded; source-inspected] In the April 11 knowledge dialogue, an LLM response
promotes an awareness-based definition to a claimed solution of the Gettier problem and then
to a complete canonical formulation. Butler later adds the self/other relation; the assistant
then describes the prior formulation as incomplete. This is direct evidence of premature closure
and retrospective accommodation in the assistant's prose, not a diagnosis of the author.[^22]

[counterexample] Reflexive accuracy about a representation does not alone guarantee that its
external content is true. Take an external Boolean fact $p=0$, an internal belief $b=1$, and an
accurate metareadout $A(b)=b$. The metareadout correctly represents the belief while the belief
misrepresents the external fact. A new definition of knowledge can be philosophically useful,
but relating it to traditional propositional knowledge requires a further bridge. Renaming the
target does not resolve the earlier counterexamples.

[interpretation] Several failure mechanisms follow from this case and the checked equations:
provisional language is promoted to canon before its discriminating example; a later revision
changes a dependency without revising its consumers; a metaphor obtains notation before its
operands are defined; and an implementation label substitutes for the behavior it names.
These mechanisms are testable in a workflow by following a single relation from the original
statement through its formalization, code and returned product. They need no speculation about
private mental states or whether a human author “really” understands an idea.

[established-bounded; source-inspected] The later repository also contains deliberate attempts
to improve this practice: frontier/FHS separation, clearer Self/Other qualifications, and
explicitly unfinished interface notes. These are useful corrections. They do not automatically
repair an older proof or supply a runtime, but they provide a better basis for doing so.[^5][^13]

## The current interface notes sharpen the philosophical distinction

[established-bounded; source-inspected] The recent Calls, Fascia and Peering notes are more
explicit about their unfinished status than the early sweeping formulations. Calls organizes
threshold, exchange, boundary, orientation and grounding roles. Fascia retains connection without
fusion. Peering distinguishes attending to another perspective from imposing a prior interpretation
on it. Self/Other and Awareness/Boundary supply the proposed orientation and movement relations.
Several placements and the full operator algebra are still declared frontier work.[^13]

[interpretation] These are useful design intentions when translated into concrete participants,
maps and changes. “Peering” can motivate retaining both receiving frames and compatible source
states. Its philosophical criticism of projection should not become a ban on mathematical
projection: a scoped readout with its fibre retained can respect the other participant. Likewise,
fascia can be realized through an existing boundary/transport and memory law; its name does not
require a separate engine module. A policy governing voluntary requests can be modeled explicitly
without making consent a prerequisite for every physical perturbation.

## Consequences for Holonics, predictive release and self-awareness

[interpretation] The source comparison supports one coherent construction: a participating
field has internal organization, a changing boundary and receiving apparatus, and an action that
transforms both its own current and its relations. Butler's section/connection and enriched-object
ideas can map into these existing HNN operands. His covenant and voluntary participation rules
are a separate normative programme; they cannot be assumed to govern every physical reception
or involuntary emitted cue.

[definition] The receiving Holon therefore carries its own frame and material. For
$y=\rho(x_S,x_R,t)$, its differential includes both participants:

$$
