[definition] Brandon asks which work is ours, what is mathematically distinctive, what to
publish and which external resources would improve the next campaign. This is a source and
publication-positioning review at `4e138f8b` plus the current mass/navigation return. It

## Three different meanings of “ours”

[established-bounded; source-inspected] The project authors its Lean source, exact Rust/CUDA
implementations, source/receiver conventions, experiments, records and manuscripts. The
workspace declares `MIT OR Apache-2.0`; separate third-party notices remain attached to their
material. Lean/Mathlib and other library/tool dependencies, imported source records and cited
mathematical/physical results are not originated by Holonics merely because the project uses
or formalizes them. Git provenance identifies the project artifact; it does not settle who
first discovered the underlying theorem.

[definition] Separate **authored artifact**, **specific mathematical construction**, and
**scientific priority**. An implementation or formal proof can be an original project artifact
while its theorem is classical. A particular combination, executable representation or
counterexample can be worth publication without claiming the general idea was unknown.
Conversely, a new label or an absence of a matching phrase in a search establishes no priority.

[definition] Some useful distinctions are mathematical rather than terminological. A span
retains the population of occurrences joining two states; a cospan can describe how interfaces
embed into an open component. A relation may forget occurrence multiplicity. A present-output
quotient may fail under a future generator; a future-stable quotient must commute with the
admitted dynamics. A statistical predictive state and an exact source fibre have different
tasks. These comparisons should be written as explicit maps and examples, not as claims
that the adjacent theories overlooked them.

## The distinctive programme and its demonstrable value

[established-bounded; source-inspected] Holonics consistently carries the following through
its particular source and implementation combination:

- Situated comparison retains the source, receiver, orientation, conditions and causal origin.
- Occurrence composition preserves actual joining constraints and source populations.
- Dynamic compression is expressed by generator/receiver commutation, with fibres or
  remainders retained in a representation appropriate to the requested continuation.
- Constitutive mechanics, quantum/phase transport, exact algebra and learned formation use
  this same source/receiver discipline, while keeping their actual equations and units.
- Exact formal returns have executable consumers and resource measurements at named scopes;
  native current ownership prevents historical replay from silently becoming the mathematics.

[definition] This combination is the project's identifiable research programme. Its strongest
publication claim is that the specified shared construction produces useful formal and
computational consequences across the exhibited domains. Claiming a uniquely complete theory
of all physics, or that academia has no comparable broad frameworks, would exceed this review.
The existing open-systems, behavioral, coalgebraic and physical literature demonstrates real
predecessors to several of its organizing ideas.

[interpretation] The working ontology is economical when a small collection of constructions
explains several source-qualified results without erasing the distinctions those results need.
That is testable through commuting diagrams, retained counterexamples, successful reuse and
measured reductions. Feeling less need to separate subjects is useful research guidance;
the publishable account shows exactly which assumptions disappear and which consequences
follow. Breadth should generate those consequences, not become a claim of priority by itself.

[interpretation] LLM access and Brandon's sustained cross-domain questioning plausibly lower
the practical cost of finding, translating, implementing and checking connections. The logs
document that working process. They do not establish a historical explanation for why every
other researcher did or did not construct something similar. The defensible explanation of
