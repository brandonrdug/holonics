The language of geometric currents supplies a useful formal instrument: generalized oriented

Three operations must not be conflated:

1. **compression** is the event's outgoing factorization, retaining every distinction still
   consequential at the declared successor boundary;
2. **oriented annihilation** composes opposed contributions in one declared fiber to an actual zero
   resultant; and
3. **constituent departure** releases a lower element whose relation has completed or ceased to
   affect the continuing higher construction.

Loss is the oriented difference not carried through a declared fold or transport. It is neither a
reward nor a punishment. It may record coherence, decoherence, changed boundary, untransported
hand, released constituency, or unresolved residual. Whether the change is harmful is a later
observer relation, never an intrinsic sign attached by the engine.
