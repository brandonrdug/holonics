implementation plan after the computational Holon, intrinsic field, receiver/plate, fluid
and RH-current work. The deliverable is a consolidated executable construction plan. This
return changes the roadmap, specification and pickup; it does not claim to have implemented
the new model during a planning turn or resume the old timed goal.

## First operation and reviewed implementation constraints

[definition] The chosen initial split is

```text
q = joined actual field source and prepared input/boundary,
s=P_s q,             h=P_h q,
r=R_M(s,h),          u=E_B input+r,
(w,b_next)=S_D(u,b),  y=P_R w.
```

The fixed-incidence/finite-section aperture and reaction-before-scattering order are explicit
implementation choices. D and M are parameters of the same model, fixed during its first
generation and developed on actual observed returns. The field is not replaced by a predictor
trained to imitate its outputs. Later changing incidence/material and multimodal receivers
extend this same construction.

[established-bounded; source-inspected] A bounded Luna review identified three places where
an enum attachment alone would not implement this plan: the producing-handle sequence, the
resident joint output receiver, and the actual field→reaction port/composition. Root followed
their source and made these edits explicit in the first-increment contract:

- `field/resident_input.rs::advance_current_resident` already admits generated point currents
  without a host readout. The first inspection of only `NativeFieldOccurrence` constructors
  would have missed it. Reuse its actual input chart.
- `NativeFieldCurrentSource` retains outgoing report, operative b, producing sections, births
  and ownership behind its outer enclosure. The preserved `reflect` draft applies the actual
  D to an enclosed joint current without mutation. Review/integrate that staged application
  with a matching current-publication port owned by the field.
- `ResidentGeneratorNeighborhood` needs the declared field/input source and condition maps
  aligned with its actual action domain. Use a staged read of the prepared condition/action
  for forecasts; do not mutate condition merely to inspect a prospective operation.
- The first public receiver reads the requested complete outward block through an identity
  or explicitly configured projection while retaining the joint source witness. It is not
  only a continuation ID, and marginal coordinate enclosures do not define independent outputs.
- The application retains the producing source/reaction cut. A target arrives as a new
  receiving occurrence linked to that source. `respond_to_material_observation` requires the
  latest receiving cut and commits its response internally; applying it again would duplicate
  the update. The wave prediction and field pending representations must not advance independently.

[definition] The first return must include an actual public training/generation invocation,
input and target sections, supplied configuration versus inferred material, held-out output,
the composed derivative/update checks, a local trained artifact/reopen, and setup/resident/
delivery measurements. A working helper or merely changed coefficient does not close it.
The first usable Athena release additionally owes its contextual conversation/code/application
evidence and the durability/resources needed by those tasks. Frontier-level usefulness remains
the broader empirical objective.

## Downstream scope retained without restoring obsolete engines

[established-bounded; source-inspected] The review followed the public SDK, native Workbench
stream, prepared conversation reader, current body rests, older text application, modality
receiver owners and package exports. The plan uses their actual scopes:

- Conversation preparation and `alpha::exposure` provide source/provenance and a development/
  evaluation boundary, not native training by themselves.
- `text_session.rs` currently depends on `AthenaTokenApplication` and older HNP model semantics.
  Its existence does not justify restoring next-token evolution inside the new field model.
- Native ecology, field/operative, coupled-body and inherited full-session rests are distinct
  payloads. Extend the one belonging to the assembled body and its actual continuation.
- Acoustic/optical receiver mathematics is reusable, but its historical source-neutral
  morphology types require actual source/axis maps from the new emitted field.
- The custom ONNX/Safetensors package is not a standard executable graph. Export/backends
  owe target execution, state/parameters and numeric/receiver comparison.

[definition] The plan retains exact native arithmetic, GPU residence, joint source fibres,
changing receiver/material derivatives and source-qualified memory. Physical/analytic research
remains authorized at its requested scope. RH, a full fluid/GR endpoint, universal intelligence,
perfect compression and all possible modalities are not prerequisites for the first useful
model/application return.
