### 2.3 Pair contacts hold a conditional only up to its three-body part

[proved-standard] Let `L` and `R` be the blocks left and right of a place `x`.

```text
log P(L,x,R) = f(L,x) + g(x,R) + h(L,R) + c   ⇒   P(x | L,R) ∝ e^(f(L,x)) e^(g(x,R)),
I(x; L,R) = I(x; L) + I(x; R) − I(L; R) + I(L; R | x),
L ⟂ R | x   ⇒   P(x | L,R) ∝ P(x | L) P(x | R) / P(x).
```

A law with pair terms only has a conditional that is a product of a left contact and a right
contact (`h` cancels). Functions on `L × x × R` modulo pair terms have dimension
`(n_L − 1)(B − 1)(n_R − 1)`: that quotient is the three-body part. Parity is its plainest case:
with `x = L ⊕ R` on fair bits, every pair is independent and the three together hold `ln 2`. The
linking chart holds the same fact: the Borromean rings have every pairwise linking number zero and
a triple number of ±1 (Milnor, 1954), and `ns.helicity-linking-form` already proves the pair form
reads zero on them. Atlas `code.pair-contact-conditional`.

### 2.5 Held, endless and closed are one boundary response

[proved-standard] A string of tension `T` and line density `μ` has speed `c = √(T/μ)` and impedance
`Z = √(Tμ)`. At a cut:
- an endless continuation presents `F = Z v`, a resistance: everything sent is absorbed (`Γ = 0`);
- a rigid grip presents `v = 0` and returns everything (`Γ = −1`); a load `Z_L` returns
  `Γ = (Z − Z_L)/(Z + Z_L)`;
- a partition read for less than the echo time `2d/c` of its nearest unmatched end cannot tell
  these apart;
- a closed loop of length `ℓ` has the modes `k_n = 2πn/ℓ` in both hands, holds tension with no
  grip only against a curvature load `Tκ` per length (a spin, a pressure, or a closed space it
  winds), and carries an integer winding that only a slip changes.

So the three cases are one family, the boundary response at the partition's cuts
(`tube.dn-elimination`), and they differ in what returns and when. Atlas
`wave.boundary-response-family`. A current in a superconducting loop persists for the last reason:
its phase winds an integer number of times, the fluxoid is quantized, and only a phase slip changes
it. On the objects that is the harmonic part of motion, which has no boundary and no coboundary to
dissipate through. It stores and returns; it supplies no work without a port, so it is no
perpetual motion. A driven region that holds its order exports entropy to do it
(`dS = d_iS + d_eS`, `d_iS ≥ 0`): that is the lens's outrunning.

### 2.6 Widths add over branches and multiply along registers, and their cells add

[proved-standard] Registers of bases `b_1 … b_m` in series have `Π b_i` configurations and hold
`Σ b_i` cells. A configuration's value is `Σ d_i w_i` with place weights `w_i = Π_(j<i) b_j`, and
the top value is `Π b_i − 1`: three registers of base 2 have weights 4, 2 and 1 and reach every
value to `7 = 4 + 2 + 1`. At a junction where one branch is taken the configurations add,
`N = Σ N_k`. The leaves of a tree are therefore a sum of products, every integer is a width, and
an integer `N` has `2^(N−1)` compositions. The least cells for `N` configurations is `e ln N` at
base `e`, and among integers it is reached by threes (`radix.economy`). Atlas
`radix.register-composition`.

[correction, 21:10 UTC] The chain record's item 4, "growth is sized in doublings", is withdrawn.
It took a ratio of numbers for the unit of growth. A width is a composition: it grows by the part
that is lacking, in any integer count, and the grip widths of 2.5 (10, 5, 2.4, 1.44, 1.14) are the
measured case, none of them a power of two.

[interpretation; agent-inferred] A dynamic register is a grip that opens or closes. A held place
of leak near zero splits a chain into parts whose configurations add where they multiplied before,
and its leak is the width that still joins them. The release's locks are those grips, so the
release re-partitions its own registers as it goes.

### 2.7 Plasticity is anisotropy: deposition leaves dyads

[proved-standard] Deposits along unit directions `u_f` with weights `w_f ≥ 0` leave
`M = Σ_f w_f u_f u_fᵀ ⪰ 0`. `M` is a multiple of the identity if and only if the weighted directions
form a tight frame, and `a(M) = (tr M)²/tr(M²)` lies in `[1, n]`, with `n` at isotropy and 1 at a
single direction. Three results of crystals restate on it:
- a rotation that holds a lattice has order 1, 2, 3, 4 or 6 (`winding.crystallographic-restriction`);
- a drive `σ` moves a slip system (plane `n`, direction `s`) by its resolved part `τ = nᵀσs`, at
  most half a uniaxial drive (Schmid, 1924);
- slip crosses a face between two grains by the alignment of their frames, and yield rises with
  finer grains as `σ_y = σ_0 + k d^(−1/2)` (Hall, 1951; Petch, 1953).

Atlas `deposition.dyad-anisotropy`.

[interpretation; agent-inferred] `contact.material` is this `M` with `u = J_f` and the contact's
dissipation as weight, so the constitution that deposition leaves is anisotropic wherever the flux
that reached it had preferred directions, and isotropic only by a tight frame. Plasticity in the
lens's sense is the growth of that anisotropy. Hardening is its other face: the directions that
carried flux conduct, and the faces between differently oriented regions resist. The rate-by-part
arm now running is the resolved-drive law: a founded part whose lap displacements keep their
direction (cosine above one half) moves an octave faster, and one whose displacements turn moves
slower. A part read at two laps is the hair of the lens, one receiver and two times.

## 5. What does not transfer

- Eyes, hair cells, muscle and cortex. Only where readings are joined, and that a difference has
  two ends, are taken.
- Point groups, slip systems and lattice tables as authored layouts. Nothing in the machine is
  given a lattice.
- Superconducting materials, and any claim of motion without loss. The machine's laps dissipate;
  2.5 names what persists (a winding) and claims no more.
- The number two. No part of the machine is paired because nature pairs.
- An authored tree, grammar or joint list. The tree is whatever the located contacts make.
- The Borromean rings beyond their statement: no link invariant is computed by the machine.
- The rulers. The joint tables, the branch product and the fitted pair reader measure the text.
