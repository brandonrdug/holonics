## 3. The return on the clock of news

`agent-inferred`, with its readings `from training` where named.
- **Object.** A compared place's news `c_t = −ln p̂_t(u_t)` and its covector `g_t = p̂_t − q_t`. Since
  `‖g_t‖₁ = 2(1 − p̂_t(u_t)) ≤ 2c_t`, a dark place's deposit is already bounded by its news.
- **The law.** In each region, `S_t = Σ_(k≤t) c_k` (a scan at one grain, released once, so schedule-free). A place is
  returned `Δτ_t = ⌊S_t/σ⌋ − ⌊S_(t−1)/σ⌋` times at weight `w_t = σΔτ_t/c_t`, and the region's end closes its last partial
  stride at weight `r/c` with `r = S_T mod σ`. The weight is a ratio applied once to a term before the sum, so it is a
  section that stands once and leaves the sum's order free.
- **What it keeps.** `Σ_t w_t c_t = S_T`, on integers at the news grain: every unit of news is returned once and the
  remainder is carried to the region's end, never dropped. Every bright place (`c_t ≥ σ`) is returned at a weight within
  `1 ± σ/c_t`, and at most `S_T/σ + 1` places are returned in all. The proofs are one line each: the `Δτ_t` telescope to
  `⌊S_T/σ⌋`, so `Σ σΔτ_t + r = S_T`; a step of `c_t ≥ σ` crosses at least one multiple of `σ`, and `σΔτ_t` lies strictly
  within `σ` of `c_t`; a returned place has `Δτ_t ≥ 1` or is the end; and `w_t‖g_t‖₁ ≤ (σΔτ_t/c_t) · 2c_t`.
- **What it is.** The deposit becomes a quadrature of `∫ Jᵀ(g/c) dS` on the coordinate of news, sampled once a section.
  Its integrand is bounded (`‖g/c‖₁ ≤ 2`), so no weight blows up: `‖w_t g_t‖₁ ≤ 2σΔτ_t`. It is sampling with probability
  proportional to news, the density under which the integrand is bounded (Horvitz and Thompson 1952), made deterministic
  by the carry as sigma-delta modulation is (Inose and Yasuda 1962); an integrate-and-fire unit is the same odometer, and
  so is the Bresenham line.
- **What a drop loses.** A plain drop at `σ` deletes the dark places' push toward their own prediction, the same push
  every section, so its error is a bias. The clock's error is a variance that averages across regions.
- **The grain has a reason, read before it is used.** `σ` is the news's own standing spread, `|c_end − c_mean|` at
  compared places between a lap's end state and its mean, by region and fifth. That is the reason today's lock grain had
  (the distribution record's §12, `deposition.rate-temperature-standing-spread`): a misweighting inside the field's own
  temperature is one the line already pays between its draws. It is carried in the learner's state, never set by hand.

## 4. A dark face frees its top tick only, and a held tick frees both ways

`tree` for the build thread's point (20:37 UTC); `agent-inferred` for the rest.
- **The point.** A dark place's skipped covector removes its own face's return at the last tick. At every tick below, a
  site's covector collects from every returned place that reads it through the contact, so with half the places returned
  nearly every site still carries a covector, and the rows, columns, skin and maps below the top run at full count.
- **The section, restated.** With the law on the top tick alone, a section reads `1 − R_top(1 − f)`, where `f` is the
  returned share and `R_top` the top tick's face part (the comparison, the face's maps, the top rows at dark receivers).
  The first guess, `1 − R(1 − f)` with `R` the whole return's share, is withdrawn.
- **Two forms for the ticks below.**
  - *(a) The same clock on the reached covector.* A site is returned at tick `k` once a section of the covector mass that
    reached it, at weight `σΔτ/‖δy‖`. It thins little, because the mass reaching the lower ticks is mostly the bright
    places' covectors, of order one, spread by the shares.
  - *(b) The forward on the clock of news, at the place level.* A site whose update at a tick falls under `σ` holds its
    current. This is the October tower's held current brought down from the levels to the places. A held tick computes no
    contact row forward, and its return is the identity, so both directions thin together and the forward's cost follows
    news, which is what streaming asks. Its bound is the slip bound's (`contact.crowd-slip-bound`): a held site's word
    differs at its readers by at most its skipped update. Theory prefers (b).
- **What is read first.** Per site and tick, by region and octave: the update's size in the current's own metric, the
  reached covector mass, each as its share under `σ`, and the profile's return by tick. No guess on the lower ticks is
  fixed until those are printed.

## 9. The unit is the composed shape, not the codec's digit

`tree` for the lens; `agent-inferred` for the rest. Brandon, 20:42 UTC, in the build thread (a paraphrase). A place is no
small section of bytes: shapes coarse-grain, and that is compression. A form is compressed by what it does in the
contexts it is used in, as an arithmetic operator is, so two forms of different length that do the same thing in an
exchange are one, and the depth of the words is not paid where it need not be. His examples are examples, not the
subject.
- **The reading.** A byte is the codec's digit. The unit is the composite the field's own news shapes, and a composite is
  coded by what it does at its receiver: forms that every admitted later receiver reads alike are one class, the kernel
  of Holonic Compression, and only the choice among classes is news (the rule of November 14 that equivalent forms are
  not ranked by the comparison). The composite's remaining digits are dark. §3 and §4 price a place; this section moves
  the price to the unit.
