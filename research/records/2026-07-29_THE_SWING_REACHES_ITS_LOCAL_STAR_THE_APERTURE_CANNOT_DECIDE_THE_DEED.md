## 1. The rejected construction was an archive search

The removed `information_ecology.rs` retained sections, observations, modes,
pending work, and history as a second mutable standing. Conditioning searched
the mode population and appended an observation. Generation searched modes
and their observation populations, computed support maxima, selected a prior
observation, and copied its target section into a newly wrapped result.

That construction could exactly preserve an imported PCM section, but it did
not enact a Swing:

- the retained examples remained necessary at inference time;
- the requested receiver set selected the answer externally;
- a historical target topology became the generated topology;
- work grew with retained modes and observations rather than the reached
  causal star; and
- deleting raw examples after “training” destroyed the alleged learned
  conduct.

Renaming this mechanism would not correct it. The module, its Eros adapter,
its example, its report, its dependency edge, and its research claim have
therefore been removed.

The useful native-section idea is not rejected. An exact graded complex,
native fiber, chronology, and lineage can still be a lawful world membrane or
observer receipt. They cannot constitute a second inference owner beside the
