## 7. What remains genuinely open

The current packed relation law proves that received differences factor

general exact basis, a cross-chart co-present stratum, or a rank-changing
carrier still lacks its compact card realization. The missing result is a
certificate which distinguishes removable chart magnitude from information
that changes the receiver fiber.

### Admission proof

Complete equality on the first live occurrence is a bounded exact witness,
not a proof over every possible point population, PTX optimization, driver,
and device. A stronger admission law would connect source semantics, compiled
artifact identity, and hardware realization by a checkable proof or complete
finite carrier argument. This record does not claim that theorem.

### Single-pass exact sparse return

The present sparse kernels intentionally avoid a dense intermediate pair
field. They classify once to determine exact output extent and again to emit.
A one-pass method would need either:

- an exact output-capacity theorem;
- deterministic block-local prefix transport; or
- a device allocation/return law whose scheduling cannot change standing.

That law is open. Reintroducing a dense state matrix merely to avoid the
second pass would restore the rejected ontology.

### Populating separation from actual paths

`SeparationDegree` is a lawful plural carrier, but the observation relation
does not yet possess causal incidence paths, valuation walls, and return
generators from which every component can be derived. Supplying arbitrary
numbers would turn the carrier into another scalar feature vector. An
application must expose the actual transported path first.
