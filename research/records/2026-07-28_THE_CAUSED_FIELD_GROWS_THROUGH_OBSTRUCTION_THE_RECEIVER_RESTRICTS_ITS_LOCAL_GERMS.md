multiscale differences, selected local extrema, joined selected extrema into
an observer-relative graph, completed graph cliques into triangles, assigned
projected edge crossings a front/back order, and displaced the resulting graph
through one exact scalar potential.

The output retained useful image correspondences, but Brandon identified that
the construction had repeated the lower-dimensional habits it was meant to
correct:

- the logical population was bounded by an authored maximum locus count;
- every event rebuilt an independent finite scene instead of growing standing;
- fixed candidate and enacted degrees selected topology in advance;
- a sensor address was promoted into the only available geometric chart;
- abstract tetrahedral incidence was treated as realized hypergeometry;
- plural depth was reduced to binary order at already-planar crossings;
- one scalar graph potential was treated as relief;
- terminal barycentric and nearest-locus interpolation reconstructed the image;
  and
- every edge was assigned the same display color rather than returning
  transported phase and gradient.

The corrected question is:

> Can `holonic-engine` own a growing causal field atlas whose logical
> population is caused rather than capped, whose loci are exact local field
> germs carrying curvature, phase, lineage, and plural receiver depths, and
> whose finite executor residency never becomes a limit on the world—while an
> image or video remains receiver testimony rather than a precomputed mesh?

This is a production-owner question. A renamed experiment, a denser feature
graph, a larger locus ceiling, a three-dimensional drawing, or a new color
scheme cannot answer it.

## 2. Logical population and physical residency are different receivers

Let the local sections directly caused by an occurrence be \(C_0\). Define

\[
C_{n+1}
=
C_n
\cup
\operatorname{dependencies}(C_n)
\cup
\operatorname{emanations}\!\left(\operatorname{obstructions}(C_n)\right).
\]

The logical ecology is

\[
C=\bigcup_{n\ge 0} C_n.
\]

One occurrence may reach exact rest, retain an open frontier, or expose exact
recurrence. There is no authored maximum population. A physical executor may
hold only one finite page or antichain at a time, but paging, eviction,
remounting, and worker order must return exactly the same standing and query
receipt.

An allocation failure may pause physical realization while preserving the
last exact standing. It may not redefine the population that lawfully exists.

## 3. A locus is a local analytical germ

A local field germ is represented schematically as

\[
g_i=(U_i,F_i,dF_i,d^2F_i,\Phi_i,\ell_i),
\]

where:

- \(U_i\) is a receiver-local chart or boundary-closed region;
- \(F_i=0\) is an exact implicit support law or an exact open coefficient
  fiber;
- \(dF_i\) carries local orientation and normal variation;
- \(d^2F_i\) carries curvature and independent tangent directions;
- \(\Phi_i\) is a family of exact receiver-relative phase laws; and
- \(\ell_i\) is complete source and self-emanated lineage.

An oriented surface sample constrains an implicit coefficient fiber. It does
not arrive as a preconstructed mesh. Independent samples refine that fiber.
When the fiber has one nonzero projective coefficient direction, the engine
may materialize its exact implicit support. When a new sample cannot inhabit
the current germ, the obstruction founds a descendant germ rather than
destroying, truncating, or averaging the prior standing.

Source-declared exact geometry remains lawful material, but it must retain
source provenance and cannot be reported as learned or self-emanated.

## 4. Causal cones are not screen-space fans

For a germ \(g\), its causal past and future are

\[
J^-(g)=\{h:h\longrightarrow g\},
\qquad
J^+(g)=\{h:g\longrightarrow h\}.
\]

These sets are generated by actual occurrence, refinement, obstruction,
declared interaction, and return. Their divergence is the population of
independent transported residual directions. Their reconvergence requires
exact gluing under a declared interaction; incompatible arrivals retain their
oriented residual.

A terminal observer may project this cone into a plane, but the plane does not
own the cone. Straight diagnostic edges cannot stand in for the continuous
supports or their connection.

Primary source:

- Kimi Team, [Kimi K3: Open Frontier Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.24653),
  2026.

### InfiniteDiffusion

InfiniteDiffusion defines an implicitly unbounded output while materializing
only the finite dependency closure required by a local query. Cache residency
does not define the object, fixed-location passive queries are seed-consistent,
and co-present work at one diffusion step is parallelizable.

The holonic construction does not inherit Gaussian founding, fixed sliding
windows, image-grid ontology, floating weighted averaging, or seed identity.
It inherits the separation between an implicit unbounded law, a finite caused
query closure, and a bounded transient physical cache. Passive restriction of
one standing must be query-order invariant; an enacted observation event may
still lawfully change later standing.

Primary source:

- Alexander Goslin,
  [InfiniteDiffusion: Bridging Learned Fidelity and Procedural Utility for Open-World Terrain Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08309),
  2026.

### Points as Tori

Points as Tori replaces an isolated sample with a local analytical surface
proxy carrying orientation, first and second fundamental forms, principal
curvature directions, and a closed-form distance field. The global field is
queried at arbitrary points without first tessellating one global mesh. The
paper also relates signed eikonal transport, screened Laplace equations,
winding numbers, Poisson reconstruction, and phase fields; it explicitly
proposes adding support in regions whose reconstructed field remains strongly
non-eikonal.

The holonic construction does not inherit fixed-size neighborhoods,
floating-point exponentials, a pretrained coefficient predictor, or
approximate blending as standing. It does inherit the decisive ontology that
a point can be a curvature-bearing local field germ, that arbitrary-resolution
queries need not make a spatial grid fundamental, and that unresolved field
behavior can cause additional local support.

Primary source:

- Nicole Feng, Ioannis Gkioulekas, and Keenan Crane,
  [Points as Tori: Fast Pointwise Signed Distance for Point Clouds](https://nzfeng.github.io/research/PointsAsTori/PointsAsTori.pdf),
  2026.
