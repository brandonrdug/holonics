## Biological correction: silk is a constitutive process

Spider silk makes the missing seam unusually visible.

- Amino-acid sequence does not by itself constitute the final fiber. Crystalline and amorphous
  domains, water, deformation rate, and viscoelastic dissipation jointly determine the returned
  stress-strain body.
- Liquid-liquid phase separation, nematic preorientation, ions, pH, water extraction, shear, and
  draw condition the dope into another morphology. The dope is not a frozen answer awaiting a
  renderer.
- Low-abundance silk components can cause disproportionate tensile changes. Frequency is not the
  same object as constitutive importance.
- The attachment and surrounding web change the effective capture response. The exterior sees a
  boundary response of spider, gland, fiber, junction, anchor, prey, and environment, while failure
  can migrate between those bodies as water or loading changes.

This supports the architectural requirement without claiming that software literally is silk:
hierarchical constituents become useful only through a conditioned, path-dependent body whose
