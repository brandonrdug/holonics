## Navigation discipline inherited from GPS and software navigation

This construction does not claim that a code repository is physically a satellite constellation.
The navigation discipline is nevertheless concrete.

GNSS retains distinct observables because they answer distinct receiver questions: code gives an
apparent range, carrier phase gives a more precise but integer-ambiguous phase relation, and
Doppler reports relative motion. Receiver and satellite clocks, propagation media, instrumental
delay, multipath, and noise remain explicit terms rather than being collapsed into one coordinate
([ESA, *GNSS Basic Observables*](https://gssc.esa.int/navipedia/index.php/GNSS_Basic_Observables)).
Repository lexical contact is therefore only one observable. Path/span, syntax, type/call,
ownership, test, historical lineage, theorem/proof, and CPU/card testimony remain distinct
channels.

Coordinates are meaningful only in a declared frame and epoch. A satellite coordinate computed
in the emission-time ECEF chart must be transported into the reception-time ECEF chart because
the Earth rotated during signal travel
([ESA, *Satellite Coordinates Computation*](https://gssc.esa.int/navipedia/index.php/Satellite_Coordinates_Computation)).
Likewise a source span, syntax node, generated artifact, and kernel-return face may be co-present
without being interchangeable. Their transport must name source and target codecs, revision,
aperture, and exactness.

Positioning is a local constraint problem rather than a ranking of every point on Earth. At least
four independently situated pseudoranges constrain receiver position and clock offset, and an
overdetermined system is refined around a contemporary local estimate
([ESA, *Code Based Positioning*](https://gssc.esa.int/navipedia/index.php/Code_Based_Positioning_%28SPS%29)).
A repository question likewise launches independent currents from its available landmarks and
intersects their transported local sections. It preserves plural admissible fixes when the
constraints do not yet distinguish one.

Navigation also owes integrity. Residuals, fault assumptions, exclusion, and protection bounds
remain distinct from coordinate accuracy
([ESA, *RAIM Fundamentals*](https://gssc.esa.int/navipedia/index.php/RAIM_Fundamentals)).
The repository return must therefore expose stale charts, failed transports, inconsistent loops,
and open alternatives; a fluent generated sentence cannot erase them.

Moving-feature standards distinguish leaves, trajectories, prisms, and foliations and encode
chronological changes without repeatedly materializing the entire interior
([OGC Moving Features Access 1.0](https://docs.ogc.org/is/16-120r3/16-120r3.html)).
The analogous repository body retains source occurrence leaves and caused chart trajectories;
receiver queries request the relevant local foliation.
