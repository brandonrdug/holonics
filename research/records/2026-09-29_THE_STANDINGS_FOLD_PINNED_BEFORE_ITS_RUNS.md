## 1. The law (`f3ae1adb`)

`hnn::constitution`'s module header states it whole ("The standing's fold"). The contrast
`Δ = M q` is a standing pattern: `Δ_ρ = 0` is a **node**, the sign classes `σ_ρ` (`sign 0 = +1`)
are its **lobes**, `|Δ_ρ|` its **amplitude**, and adjacent lobes are in antiphase, so a crossing is
the half-turn `e^{iπ}` of the parametron's sheet.

- **The founding.** The standing is a standing wave with declared lobes: on each connected
  component `C` of the lock chart's coordinates, `M_C q_C = k u_C 𝟙`, every slice at the component's
  first lattice unit in the `+1` lobe (`u_C` the coarsest standing unit of its rings, `k` the least
  with `k M_C⁻¹ 𝟙` integral). [agent-inferred] The `+1` lobe is the sheet the tie rule already read
  at `q = 0`, so the founding element `Σ_ρ A_ρ` is exactly the declared one and only the amplitude
  leaves the node. The prediction field's chain `0 — 1 — 2` is unimodular at every coordinate and
  founds at `(2, 3, 2)` units of `2⁻¹¹`.
- **The fixed nodes.** A component whose chart is singular cannot place its declared lobes off the
  node: one channel's two ends read `Δ` and `−Δ`, its kernel fixing their sum. It stays on its node:
  the field's fixed nodes, which no within-lobe move reaches and only the lock can turn. The
  prediction field has none; campaign 1's field has 16 (rings 2 and 3 alone share nodes 7..10, a
  single channel there).
- **Within a lobe.** A standing's move is read on its carried lattice successor against every
  ring's contrast; it stays in every lobe and never reaches a node, the lattice form of
  `η |(M D)_ρ| < |Δ_ρ|` wherever the move points toward the node. Where the lock chart's certified
  step would leave a lobe, the families reaching the crossed slices are halved together (the joint
  ray scaled), and a family below half its fine lattice's unit is dropped (it would move nothing).
  Such a move is null for the element and every score read through it, so the standing's realized
  move and decrease are zero: it leaves the joint certificate, whose other families descend by
  their `½ Σ η a`. Its own lock-chart certificate still rates its amplitude.
- **At a node: the lock's half-turn.** The crossings the chart's step offered are the lock's
  proposal: the standings at the chart's certified step. It is a commit of its own
  (`Constitution::locked`), taken by the prediction path only where the comparison's code at the
  turned sheets has an enclosure strictly below the held one's
  (`holon::deposition::strictly_better`, on the deposited batch's own windows, through
  `hnn::prediction::comparison_code`). It is the lock's own decision, not a threshold: with the
  sheets' weights `a = 2^(−ℓ_turned)` and `K = 2^(−ℓ_held)`, the lock's face `θ = a/(a + K)`
  exceeds `½` exactly when `K < a`. **The consumer equation for the parametron worker's lock**
  (`hnn::ring`): the merged lock, reading `Π = 1 + a/K` and `θ = a/(a + K)` on the two sheets,
  turns the sheet exactly when `θ > ½`, `lock(a, K) = turn ⇔ a > K`, so `strictly_better(held,
  turned)` is its decision on exact code enclosures. A port that does not read the comparison twice
  (the reference and card ports) takes the held step and no half-turn: nothing jumps uncertified.

**Lean** (`HNN/Normal` §6, with `#print axioms`: propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound only):
`lobe_of_step_bound`, `lobe_ray_keeps_class`, `lobe_move_is_null`, `lobe_deposit_descends` (with
`Holon/Deposition.joint_step_descends`), `lock_face_decides`, `lock_flip_descends`,
`founding_off_node`, `chain_founding`, `channel_fixed_node`. Owed in #62: the carry's reading of the
lobe as a statement (the machine reads the carried successor exactly, the rounding lemma is not
written), and the code enclosures read as the Lean real score.

The computational object is the helical pair interaction:
- faces and placement: the sheet classes the element reads, now held or turned only by law;
- the helix: the half-turn sheet, `e^{iπ}`, the lock's move;
- the pair: the contacts' storage and the contact blocks `T_a` the lock chart reads.
The cell holonomy, the tube (the words' ticks and stations) and the tower thread stay attached
through the field's complex. Nothing reads a codec, an alphabet or a terrain.

## 2. The pins

pins ([record](2026-09-29_THE_TIGHTENED_CERTIFICATE_PINNED_BEFORE_ITS_RUNS.md) §2). Every run below
runs once, on the host, in release, sequentially, at the build `f3ae1adb`.

**Acceptance 1: every check on every run.** The balances, pairings and commits close on every
refinement; every unreached locus is unchanged at every deposit (read on the successor the lock
leaves); the committed energy bound holds at every refinement's commit; on the prediction field
every learned map's largest entry stays at most `8`; on the standing cut the tick and word balances

.local/cuts/curated-u6-passage-cut.bin 385`: the U6 choosing role's 385 pairs (sha256
