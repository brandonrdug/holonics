# THE CORPUS CURRENT COMPLETES THE SEGMENT; THE LATER UTTERANCE TESTS THE CONSTRUCTION

**Status:** BRANDON-RATIFIED PLAN / BUILT / ONE FROZEN HOST RUN / DIRECT COMPLETION AND
DEPARTURE OBSERVED / SELECTIVE LINGUISTIC COMPOSITION NOT OBSERVED

## Present question

Can ordinary exposure to an existing text ecology cause Eros-completed regions to become
higher-grain Standing which participates in the conduct of a nonidentical later utterance?

The fixed-byte text cell already measured exact local recurrence and chronology sensitivity. The
older Holobrochos record also observed variable cohered segments, but its historical segmenter and
apparatus are not production owners. Neither result establishes that the direct machine composes a
tokenized corpus across utterances. Path depth, readable fragments, and final body differences
cannot answer this question.

## Theory-to-structure relation

The tokenizer is inherited source technology: it maps source text to categorical addresses. It
does not name meanings or decide which complete constructions are identical.

One corpus line is one source-owned utterance worldline. Its tokenizer occurrences are co-present
current, and only adjacent occurrences inside that line receive source-declared oriented
incidence. No adjacency crosses a line boundary. The utterance ends after the event. Its token
carriers therefore depart, while the direct machine may retain the completed regional
constituent.

```text
tokenizer-addressed utterance current
  -> source-declared ordered adjacency inside that utterance
  -> direct regional completion against one Standing-before
  -> touched earlier constituent(s) + one atomic successor
  -> ended occurrence carriers depart
  -> a held-out utterance meets the retained construction
```

Repeated exposed adjacency is only an interface through which two constructions may meet. One
shared pair does not by itself prove a phrase, meaning, rule, or linguistic completion. A
linguistic result requires the resulting higher construction to participate in changed later
conduct under the frozen contrasts.

- training corpus: `experiments/corpora/diet/child.train.txt`, complete lines up to 8,192 OLMo
  tokenizer occurrences;
- validation corpus: `experiments/corpora/diet/child.val.txt`, complete lines up to 2,048

  `48d788eca847d4d7548f375ad03d3c9312f6139e`;
- no special tokens are inserted;
- a tokenizer address is shifted by one only at the nonzero `RelationAtom` membrane;
- the source-only fixture freezes four probes, in validation order, for each of:
  exact recurrence, single-slot recombination, a familiar two-token segment in a changed wider
  field, and an unseen adjacent pair;
- each probe freezes its actual continuation and one source-derived sibling continuation before
  Eros runs.

The selection program must stop if any cohort has fewer than four members. Corpus size, tokenizer,
probe membership, continuation siblings, and event law cannot change after the first machine
result.

## Controls

Three independent bodies receive:

1. the chronological utterances;
2. the same tokenizer inventory and line boundaries with order reversed inside every utterance;
3. no training utterances.

Training inside one body remains sequential because source chronology is material. Independent
bodies and probe remounts may use separate CPU cores. Every probe candidate begins from an exact
remount of its body checkpoint; actual and sibling candidates never train one another.

## Primary observable

For the first 32 training utterances and every probe, retain:

- the source utterance/token current;
- the exact earlier Standing constituents touched;
- their external source-testimony ancestry;
- the returned constituent grain, axes, cells, incidence, pins, complete boundaries and paths;
- RIDE, FOUND, or OPEN at every boundary;
- the successor constituent population and lower-constituent departure; and
- the actual-versus-sibling contrast under chronological, reversed, and virgin Standing.

An earlier relation counts as learning only when training-ancestried Standing actually enters the
later replacement. A changed hash or scalar count without that participation is insufficient.

## Interpretation boundary

- exact spans only: segment memory;
- several training utterances with different slot material entering one held-out recombination:
  evidence of higher-grain composition;
- different conduct for the same local segment under a different wider field: contextual
  division;
- equal actual and sibling conduct: a plural afforded field, not failure and not correctness;
- trained/virgin difference without training ancestry: prefix-local construction, not learning;
- chronological/reversed equality: order was not consequential at this cut;
- deep paths or readable concatenation without the causal relation above: the historical
  complexity-without-language failure;
- lower interiors departing while the later boundary remains effective: completion/rebase at this
  bounded ecology.

The strongest accepted signature is a later held-out continuation whose replacement actually
imports one higher constituent with ancestry in several distinct training utterances carrying
different slot material. The experiment does not claim English, response policy, semantic
correctness, or general training.

## Measured disposition

The frozen source, direct host construction, exact physical record, and causal interpretation are
deposited at
[`eros-cohered-corpus-01`](../observations/eros-cohered-corpus-01/RESULTS.md).

Corpus exposure did complete and replace prior regional constituents. The chronological body
reduced 1,556 utterance events to 166 final constituents, and one higher constituent carried exact
testimony from 1,121 source lines. Later nonidentical probes physically imported trained Standing;
the virgin control did not, and reversing within-utterance order changed the result.

The strongest planned linguistic signature was rejected. One matching exposed token pair could
recruit the 1,121-line constituent, so actual and sibling probes frequently imported the same
indiscriminate field. Observed pairs that no longer recruited were still present in its interior
but no longer exposed. The result establishes direct completion, departure, chronology, and
phase-local availability; it does not establish selective phrase/frame composition.

The 32-line preflight also underestimated both later closure cost and observer-report expansion.
The accepted scientific run remained bounded, but its ignored exact report is 1,058,200,985
bytes because complete large constituents were serialized repeatedly. The result records this as
an observer-artifact defect; it is not a claim that the live body itself occupied that extent.
