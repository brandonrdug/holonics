## The generating relation actually invoked

[definition] For the declared symbol chart, e(c) is a unit complex basis vector. The incoming
request x is the ordered direct sum of its symbol vectors, with zero inclusion into unused
boundary channels when required. The context is the ordered tensor
`h=e(c_m) ⊗ ... ⊗ e(c_1)`, constructed on device through the existing bilinear feature operation
and a coordinate restriction. Source text, context parts and target text are separate operands.
Neither file paths nor episode identifiers become amplitudes or choose a model branch.

[definition] The application selects the incoming-boundary restriction for the local reaction:

```text
r = R_M(x,h) = A(h)x+c(h)
u = x+r = (I+A(h))x+c(h)
(w,b_next) = S_D(u,b)
response = codec(nearest-unit-basis sections of w)
```

The earlier continuing-boundary model remains available and is the default of `from_field`;
its saved numerical task retains its meaning. `NativeFieldReactionPort` records the chosen
operand and persists with the same body. A requested text section is one generated field;
selection does not schedule a next-symbol feedback loop.

[proved-derived] The identity term and reaction share x, so their source uncertainty is bounded
through `I+A(h)` before reception. When `A=-I`, that dependence cancels exactly; summing two
independent source balls would miss the cancellation. The native conditional kernel contracts
the identity into the coefficient before rounding. Its fixed-h gain bound, known external
current and continuing internal b enter the existing complete constituted reflection. A
separate external input and the existing b are joined with their admitted outer bound.

[definition] Each output position chooses the closest declared unit basis vector to its part
of the same computed joint centre. For real unit vectors in a complex chart, minimizing squared
distance is equivalent to maximizing the real coordinate: the complete norm and imaginary
terms cancel between candidates. The device returns every selection in one operation, with
ties, the source radius and a conservative robust-margin flag. The full joint source stays
attached; the selections do not identify independent possible points from marginal balls.

## Producing material survives interruption

[definition] A pending comparison needs the operation that produced it. Its cold witness
contains the original field source, D/moment sections, reaction source/condition/material,
scattering input and output, epoch and handle. It contains no second live field ecology.
Remount rebinds these immutable sections to the same field owner after incidence/grain/source
checks. Its D/M may precede contemporary material; substituting the current map would change
the adjoint. The actual producing outputs are checked against their retained actions on reopen.

[established-bounded; source-inspected] `NativeFieldCurrentSourceRest` and
`FieldReactionEnclosureRest` reuse existing section/material codecs. `NativeFieldModelRest`
extends its original payload with an explicit reaction-port/pending-comparison suffix; an
unextended old payload retains the continuing-boundary interpretation. `NativeFieldSavedSession`
adds the codec and existing `HnaStreamState`. Failed output draining retains the encoded response
and model comparison, rather than executing generation again. A receiver failure after native
commit reports that committed status and comparison explicitly.

## What the application learned and returned

whole-section replacement. The native model sees unit currents and tensor contacts; the
exterior preparation script alone knows how its supervised targets were authored. Fifty-four
distinct off-diagonal requests cover nine context pairs. Diagonal requests are withheld from
updates. One pass returns 21/27 overall but none of the six required edits. Eight passes plus
two resumed development comparisons (434 total targets) return 27/27, including all six edits.
The held-out population is recombination validation used during iteration, not a blind final
benchmark. Both failures and successes remain in the evidence.

[established-bounded; measured] A further 27-case committed validation sequence also returns
all expected text while advancing the field between requests, with no material targets.

complete joint/boundary currents and radii all agree.
