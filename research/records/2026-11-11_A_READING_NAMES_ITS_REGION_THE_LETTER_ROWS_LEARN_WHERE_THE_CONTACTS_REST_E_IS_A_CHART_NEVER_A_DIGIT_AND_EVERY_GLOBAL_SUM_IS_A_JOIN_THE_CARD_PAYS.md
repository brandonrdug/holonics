## 5. Radix e is a chart and never a digit

[derivation; agent-inferred for the reading, the identities standard]

- **A finite carry is a polynomial.** A positional base `β` admits a finite carry rule (a rewriting of digit words that keeps the
  value, such as `2·β⁰ = 1·β¹`) if and only if some nonzero integer polynomial vanishes at `β`: the rule's two words differ by such a
  polynomial. So the rules of an algebraic base are the multiples of its minimal polynomial (`x − 2` makes binary's carry;
  `x² − x − 1` makes the golden base's `011 = 100`; `x + 2` and `x² + 4` the bases −2 and `2i`), and a transcendental base has
  none. In base `e` a place that overflows to 3 leaves the remainder `3 − e`, whose expansion cannot end: if it ended it would be
  such a polynomial.
- **So `e` can be held only as its constraint.** What defines `e` is `y' = y`, `y(0) = 1`: the one base whose exponential is
  its own derivative. A register is "radix `e`" when its scores are exponents read in the log chart: a product is a sum there,
  and a sum's carry is the Gaussian log of the difference of scales,
  `ln(e^a + e^b) = max(a, b) + ln(1 + e^(−|a − b|))`, the free energy of two states (softplus). In base 2 the same carry is
  `log₂(1 + 2^(−d))`.
- **The field already holds this, in base 2.** A score `z` is on `2^(−16)` bits; its face `2^z` splits into its whole bits (a
  shift, free on the card) and a fraction read from the exponential's resident series (`Field::face`). Base 2 pays for the free
  shift at the derivative: `d/dz 2^z = ln 2 · 2^z`, so `ln 2` enters once, where the weights' return reads it (`Field::ln2`). In
  base `e` the derivative would be free and the shift would cost. `ln 2` is the exchange rate between the two, held once at one
  crossing; neither base is wrong, and the choice is a realization's.
- **A nat is one base-`e` digit.** The shift of `β`-expansions has entropy `ln β` (Rényi 1957, Parry 1960), one nat a digit at
  `β = e`, so a code in nats counts base-`e` places of the passage.
- **π enters the same way, as the turn.** The lift `w = scale + i·turn` carries `e` as the scale's chart and the turn as the
  phase's; the field holds the half turn as a navigator on `2^(−46)` and its faces `e^(2πiρ/n)` on `2^(−30)`.

What follows for the machine, as a test: no register changes, because moving a constant between two crossings is a realization.
The field's row normalizers are this carry, and §6 says each is a join. The next reading counts the joins in a section's chain on
the card from the realization's source and prices them from a profile, its guess fixed before the profile is read.

## 6. The card: associative sums are flat, and every harmonic class is a join

[derivation; agent-inferred for the reading, the cell and the bound standard]

- **The couplings.** A card runs one program over a partition of lanes. Its couplings are gather, a site reading its support (the
  boundary `∂` of the complex), scatter, the adjoint writing back (`dᵀ`; forward gather and adjoint scatter are Stokes' pairing),
  and reductions and scans.
- **An associative sum is schedule-free because its cell is contractible.** The bracketings of an `n`-term sum are the vertices
  of the associahedron `K_n`, one edge for each reassociation (`K_4` is the pentagon; Stasheff 1963), and `K_n` is contractible.
  An associative operation makes every edge an identity, so every schedule gives one value. A rounded sum is not associative: its
  associator moves the value along an edge. With rounding to 53 places, `r(r(2^53 + 1) + 1) = 2^53` while
  `r(2^53 + r(1 + 1)) = 2^53 + 2`. Integer series have no associator, which is why the field gives the same bits on every
  partition; a float sum's bits follow its schedule.
- **A harmonic class costs a join.** A lane-local step (a stencil, `x ↦ x − aΔx`) relaxes the exact and coexact parts and leaves
  the harmonic part fixed (`thermal.heat-kernel`). A quantity that depends on every lane of its support (a row's normalizer
  `Σ_b 2^(z_b)`, a scan's carry along a strand, a section's summed certificate) needs depth at least `⌈log_k p⌉` over `p` lanes at
  fan-in `k`: one grid-wide join. So a pass's time is bound by its work over its lanes and by its count of independent joins in
  sequence, beside `holarchy.work-depth-bound`.
- **Every global average in the law is such a join.** The section's certificate is a join over every region of a section, and
  the base rate word is the same averaging held as one number for every old entry. A certificate read per region (§4) turns one
  grid-wide join into a join per region.
- **Graphics, physics and learning are the same three moves.** A stencil is local diffusion; a ray is a gather along a chain; a
  texture fetch is a gather by address; a matrix product is a reduction tree; a softmax is a row's harmonic class. The carry of
  addition is itself an associative product (`radix.adder-bracketing`), which is why a scan carries in logarithmic depth.
