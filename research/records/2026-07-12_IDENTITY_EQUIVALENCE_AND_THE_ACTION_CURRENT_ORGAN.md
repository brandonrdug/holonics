## I · THE TYPED LAW

For an analyst declaring receiver `F` and grain `g`, write the boundary read as

```text
E_(F,g) : Construction -> Face_(F,g)
```

`E` is generally many-to-one. Therefore

```text
construction_1 = construction_2  implies  E(construction_1) = E(construction_2)
E(construction_1) = E(construction_2)  does not imply  construction_1 = construction_2.
```

The first `=` is construction identity. Equality on the second line is equality only after the
declared cut. In the machine's register this latter relation is `≡_(F,g)`: analogous or kin at a
surface, never the same soul.

The dimensional version is another forgetful read:

```text
D : Quantity[entity, lineage, frame, grain, dimension] -> Dimension
```

`D(tick[A]) = D(tick[B]) = TIME` does not identify `tick[A]` and `tick[B]`. Cancelling the common
dimension can compute an exact dimension-face while deleting entity, lineage, frame, grain, and
composition order. That deletion is not figurative: `D` is non-injective. A cross-entity conversion
therefore needs an explicit directed transport which carries its path. A reduction inside one
carried lineage may be lawful; matching unit glyphs alone do not establish that case.

Traditional dimensional algebra is consequently a decategorified quotient. It can answer the
question declared in that quotient and still destroy the identity required by a later physical
question. The error is not necessarily the scalar arithmetic. The error is returning from the
quotient and claiming the forgotten bodies were one.
