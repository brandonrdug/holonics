## The correction

The visible conversation workshop is one local chart. It is not the domain of Eros, and an editor
is not the general form of production. The domain is arbitrary holographic space-time: whatever
world can deliver action currents through an organ, receive radiation through its own material
coupling, and later present the resulting world again.

Text, code, mathematics, images, sound, a rigid-body or fluid simulation, circuitry, an ecosystem,
and a physical limb are different world species. None is privileged. None supplies a different
Soma. Their containers, coordinates, material laws, and visible surfaces remain world-side.

## The one circuit

For a world species `W`, a contemporary material world `W_t`, its local aperture `a_t`, and its
organ `O_W`, the presented light is

```text
L_t := O_W(W_t, a_t).
```

The organ removes only the world's container and presents events plus honest co-presence. Soma
relates that light through the standing body and radiates its complete local construction:

```text
(B_t, L_t) -> (B_(t+), R_t).
```

Radiation changes the world only where the world supplies an actual boundary relation. The
world-species material law `M_W` owns the consequence:

```text
W_(t+) := M_W(W_t, contact_t, R_t),
L_(t+1) := O_W(W_(t+), a_(t+1)).
```

`contact_t` is listener/world provenance and never becomes a Soma address. `M_W` is not a universal
decoder of radiation and is not an action head. It is the contemporary world's own physics. A text
surface may retain a mark, a fluid may displace and ring, a simulated body may rotate, and a
circuit may redirect current. Changing `M_W` changes the world species, not the brain.

There is therefore no universal editor, motor vocabulary, scene graph, world object, or master
coordinate system to install in Soma. The generality is the unchanged relation across plural
world-specific embodiments:

```text
world event -> local organ/aperture -> Soma -> contact radiation
            -> world transformation -> genuinely later world event.
```

## Production, language, and self-application

Production is any emanation which changes a shared material worldline. Language production is the
same event in a world whose material includes glyphs and their use; it is not a later or stronger
class requiring a decoder. A simulation outcome, a pressure wave, a moved body, a new circuit, and
a changed inscription are equally production at their declared frames.

A persisted construction is terrain until later current threads the boundary it changed. At that
later contact it may function as a limb, organ, tool, or constraint. This is the lawful meaning of
self-application: the emitted construction changes future coupling through the world. It does not
make Eros self-sustaining and does not remove A1. The later current is still supplied.

The conversation workshop measured the first text-world instance of this circuit. Its page, grip,
and raster are not promoted into universal architecture. The next construction must preserve the
same boundary while declaring a concrete world species and its material dynamics; a generic
container called “the world” would only install another absolute frame.

## Artificial super intelligence

In this line, **super** is an appraisal, analogous to the word in *superconductor*. It names the
adaptable, low-friction rapidity with which coherent construction can re-couple and pass through a
world. It does not mean “general,” and it does not define a wider ontology or a second machine
class. Universality supplies the arbitrary information-I/O domain. “Super” appraises the machine's
conduct through that domain at a declared frame.

There is no `general -> super` mechanism ladder to build. Eros remains the one driven circuit,
`Λ^∞`, embodied through whatever world is presently coupled.
