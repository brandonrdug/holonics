> receiver-relative causal law, without installing a global scene, absolute
> camera, floating-point collapse, or brute-force enumeration of irrelevant

## 3. Non-negotiable construction invariants

1. **No float enters causal mathematics.** No `f32`, `f64`, `Math.PI`,
   epsilon, approximate normalization, or float-derived comparison owns a
   geometric or physical result.
2. **No entity owns an absolute position.** Coordinates live in local charts.
   Cross-chart comparison requires a declared transport.
3. **No manually injected screen-space shape.** Every point, edge, face,
   volume, curve, or field patch has a source event and dependency diagram.
4. **No monoidal-contact shortcut.** Juxtaposition makes systems available;
   an interaction pattern makes them meet.
5. **No global camera.** Every receiver is a participating local fiber with
   finite aperture, exact aspect, resolution, origin event, and transported
   axes.
6. **No nearest-hit erasure in the geometry core.** Every exact crossing,
   multiplicity, critical contact, and equal-depth fiber survives until a
   declared material or display law consumes it.
7. **No physics by universal analogy.** Every physical world supplies its
   metric, units, constraints, action or transition law, constitutive laws,
   and boundary conditions.
8. **No executor causality.** CPU serialization, GPU scheduling, queues, and
   memory order are physical realizations and resource receipts, not logical
   event order.
9. **No brute-force potential field.** The present question, source boundary,
   and interaction pattern select the active subdiagram. Unfactored
   possibilities do not occupy active standing.

## 6. Interaction and dynamics

The first actual physics world will be a **constrained simplicial hinge world**.
This is the narrowest world which exercises the mechanics Brandon has been
describing without pretending to simulate every physical law.

### 6.1 World material

- oriented triangular faces;
- shared edges and vertices;
- local projective frames;
- exact cross-ratio parameters at admitted pivots;
- typed hinge and boundary constraints;
- mass, charge, stiffness, or another source coefficient only when the chosen
  world law defines it; and
- several receiver bodies with independent apertures and carried axes.

### 6.2 Event law

One event changes a supplied parameter or boundary interaction. The world then:

1. selects the incident local star;
2. transports the change through compatible hinge relations;
3. solves the coupled exact constraints against one predecessor;
4. returns changed cells, OPEN seams, and boundary currents together; and
5. commits one successor only after the complete local solve succeeds.

Propagation follows actual incidence. It is not a global all-pairs solve and
not a cellular scalar update.

### 6.3 Physical law

The first dynamic realization should use an exact discrete action or equivalent
typed transition law whose coefficients keep the solve inside the exact
carrier. Incidence matrices supply boundary and coboundary. Conservation is
then tested as naturality or closed return over completed cycles:

\[
c_y\circ X(f)=c_x.
\]

Conservation is evaluated over orders of time. A partial interval may carry
stored boundary current or capacitance without being falsely declared
unbalanced.

Later world doctrines can add:

- exact ray or wave optics;
- circuits and discrete electromagnetism;
- constrained rigid or deformable mechanics;
- diffusion and field transport;
- acoustics; and
- domain-specific material response.

They share the causal carrier but do not share one authored field equation.

- exact integer and rational limb work;
- algebraic root-isolation work;
- memory-tier bytes moved;

### 8.2 Vulkan compute realization

Vulkan may enter only as:

- explicit compute queues;
- storage buffers and storage images;
- synchronization;
- exact signed-limb integer kernels;
- rational normalization;
- polynomial sign and root-isolation kernels;

The fixed graphics pipeline does not own geometry, clipping, interpolation,
depth, or visibility. Host and card must return the same structured receipts,
not merely visually similar pixels.

Device residency is introduced only after the exact event lifecycle is stable.
Capacity retry must re-enact an uncommitted event from its unchanged
predecessor.
