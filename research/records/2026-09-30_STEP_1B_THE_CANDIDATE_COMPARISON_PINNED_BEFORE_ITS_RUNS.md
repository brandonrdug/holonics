## 2. The candidate, stated in the objects [definition; agent-inferred]

### 2.1 The lock, whole

At a refinement `r` of a request's release, the placed section `S_r` (the cells locked before `r`),
an open station `j` and each class `x ∈ A` (the alphabet with the termination, `|A| = 5`), the
candidate's storage is the release's own, `z_(S_r, j, x)` (`BankPlacement::storage`), and its
reading the executed growth `a_(r,j,x) = max_m ρ(M_m(z))`, enclosed `[L, U]`
(`ReceivingBank::read_turn`): one turn's cell holonomy, the joint over the bank's members.

The station's decision is a lock (`Objects/ParametronLock`, the elementary object's Ising site).
Each class is a **biased sheet** of weight `a_(r,j,x)`. The **resting sheet** has weight one: the
lossless ring's growth, at the bifurcation, the weight the release's threshold compares against
(`Growth::is_locked`, `L > 1`). The lock's exchange read at its resting sheet and its face are

```text
Π_(r,j)   = 1 + Σ_(x∈A) a_(r,j,x)                       (the partition function of the lock's sheets)
θ_(r,j,x) = a_(r,j,x) / Π_(r,j),   θ_(r,j,∘) = 1/Π_(r,j) (each sheet's share; ∘ the resting sheet, the hold)
```

For the target `t = t_j` the rest of the lock is its resting weight `K = 1 + Σ_(x≠t) a_x`, so
`θ_t = a_t/(a_t + K)`: the two-sheet face of `parametron.lock-exchange-polynomial`, whose flip is
`θ_t > ½ ⇔ a_t > K`.

### 2.2 The comparison: the log of the whole lock over the target sheet

```text
ℓ_(r,j) = log(Π_(r,j) / a_(r,j,t)) = −log θ_(r,j,t)          nats, > 0
∂ℓ/∂ ln a_x = θ_x − q_x,   q the target's indicator, q_∘ = 0   (the resting sheet does not move)
```

- **Loss is the logarithm of a ratio of Holons.** `R = Π/a_t` is the ratio of the receiving lock
  read whole (every sheet's executed growth, the resting sheet's included) to the target's sheet,
  on one receiving face; `ℓ = log R`. Its logarithmic derivative on the log-readings is the
  covector `θ − q`, the governing law's `p − q` with the face `p = θ` (Lean `lockFace_covector`,
  `HNN/BankFace.bank_face_covector` with one more sheet that does not move).
- **The classical loss is the perceived difference.** `−log θ_t` is the surprise of the target at
  the lock's face: what crosses the receiver's section when the target arrives. What the lock
  already predicts cancels: where `θ_t → 1` the covector vanishes.
- **Cross-entropy is physical.** `ℓ = D(δ_t‖θ)`, the divergence of the target's sheet from the
  lock's face, which the governing law reads as the free energy of the target state over the lock's
  equilibrium, `F(δ_t) − F(θ) = k_BT · D(δ_t‖θ)`, with `Π` the lock's partition function at its
  resting sheet (`θ = a Π′/Π`, `lockFace_eq_bias_logDeriv`). [interpretation] Reading the sheets'
  weights as Boltzmann factors at the lock's own temperature is `Objects/ParametronLock`'s stated
  interpretation, not a theorem. The phase part of the ratio is not read: the release decides on the
  dominant multiplier's modulus alone (`log|μ|`, whose covector is `Re(μ′/μ)`). The comparison is
  incomplete by that part, as the governing law says of any classical loss, and it is the part the
  release executes.
- **To `E`.** Each sheet's covector is the executed growth's own (`read_turn_covector`: the simple
  dominant multiplier's eigen-derivative through the executed tick and its solve), carried through
  the candidate's placement and the request's phases to the source port (`returns`, unchanged).

### 2.3 The margin, derived: the solved level is the lock's flip

```text
ℓ < log 2  ⇔  θ_t > ½  ⇔  a_t > 1 + Σ_(x≠t) a_x                      (lockFace_lt_log_two_iff)
on enclosures:  1 + Σ_(x≠t) U_x < L_t  ⇒  U_x < L_t ∀x ≠ t,  1 < L_t,  ℓ < log 2   (lockFace_enclosure_sublevel)
```

The level is the lock's own flip (the half of the face at which one sheet outweighs the rest), not a
chosen number, and the resting sheet's weight is the release's own threshold. The test is one exact
rational comparison of the enclosures' endpoints: no logarithm is enclosed to read it. It implies
the release's class predicate (`Growth::exceeds`) and threshold (`Growth::is_locked`), so the
target is the station's top and eligible (`class_top_is_target`). The margin it holds on a right
decision is the rest of the lock taken together: the target must outweigh the resting sheet and
every rival at once, up to `log |A|` nats above the leading rival (`(1 + 4a)/a ≤ 5` for four rivals
at `a ≥ 1`). Beyond the level the pull continues, weakening as `1 − θ_t`, and never vanishes
(`lockFace_share_lt_one`, `lockFace_strictAnti`): a right decision keeps resisting a move of a
shared locus that would lower it. A tie of the five candidates reads `ℓ = log(5 + 1/a) > log 5`,
and any rival at or above the target reads `ℓ ≥ log 2` (`lockFace_ge_log_two_of_rival`): no tie is
a minimizer, and the comparison has none (`lockFace_pos`, `lockFace_strictAnti`).

### 2.4 Where it is read: each station once, at the release's decision, while the release agrees with the key

The release from the open section runs refinements `r = 0, 1, …, r_end` (`bank_release`, the one
owner of the lock iteration), each locking the eligible stations of the largest gap. Let `r*` be
the **last refinement whose placed cells all equal their targets** (the key-consistent prefix is
`0, …, r*`; `r* = r_end` when every lock is right). Station `j` is read once, at

```text
d(j) = the refinement that locks j,  if that refinement is before r*;
d(j) = r*,                           otherwise (j locked at r*, later, or never).
```

At `r*` every station still open is read, the wrong lock decided there included. The request's
term is `Σ_j ℓ_(d(j), j)`: exactly `m = 8` terms a request, whatever the trajectory.

- **Open section, not partition.** The contexts are the machine's own sections, placed by its own
  locks in its own order: the contexts the release executes. A partition with the targets placed
  is a context the release never meets (the diagnosis §3: the float fit's teacher-forced 869 of
  1,024 released 17 whole of 128 from the open section; Stage 2's partition arm released 0).
- **Only while the release agrees with the key.** The ideal listener's observations are pairs of
  the terrain's passage (the declared family's emitter reads `x_(t−ℓ)` from the request or the same
  request's earlier stations, all truth). A refinement whose placed cells all equal their targets is
  such a passage, and its target is an emission of the key from the cells the storage holds. After a
  wrong lock the storage holds the machine's own wrong cell, and the target there is not what any key
  consistent with the context emits: for order-2's key `(2, a ↦ a + 1)` a wrong class `c` at station
  `j − 2` has the key emitting `c + 1` where the target says `x_(j−2) + 1`. Such a reading carries
  information against the key. It also cannot change whether this section is whole: its section is
  already wrong.
- **Each station once, at its decision.** The release decides a station once, at the refinement that
  locks it; that refinement is where the comparison the release executes for it lives. One term a
  station keeps the count of terms fixed (8 a request), so the strict decrease compares like with
  like, and a move that corrects a lock is not charged for the refinement it opens (reading every
  open station at every consistent refinement would add terms exactly when a lock comes right).
- **Sufficiency.** If every decision term is in the solved level, every lock is at its target
  (`decisions_release_the_section`, by induction on the refinements, with
  `lockFace_enclosure_sublevel` and `class_top_is_target` at each decision), and at `r*` every
  station still open is eligible, so the release cannot hold there while a station is open (a
  refused Floquet certificate stays a commit guard): the section is released whole (with
  `predicates_release_the_section`).
- **What a reading carries at its operating point.** The face's susceptibility is `θ(1 − θ)`
  (`bias_susceptibility`): the covector `θ − q` is largest on wrong decisions and on right ones held
  by a small share, and fades only as the target takes the whole lock. Far past threshold the
  resting sheet's share `1/Π` is small and the face reads the sheets' relative growth, which is
  what decides the class: the comparison is not blinded by every candidate being locked, where the
  threshold carries nothing.

### 2.5 The composition, enclosed

```text
L(Θ; epoch) = Σ_(requests) Σ_j ℓ_(d(j), j)
ℓ ∈ [ln(1 + (1 + Σ_(x≠t) L_x)/U_t),  ln(1 + (1 + Σ_(x≠t) U_x)/L_t)]      (ln_enclosure on the declared grid)
X(Θ; epoch) = Σ_(requests) Σ_j (ℓ_(d(j), j) − ln 2)₊                      (the excess over the solved level)
```

`L` is one scalar of the epoch: a composition's descent, coherent on shared loci (the diagnosis §4).
Reported beside it: the stations in the solved level (the rational test), and the class,
threshold, order and section predicates exactly as now.

### 2.6 How the certified move and its guards consume it

`executed_move`'s law is kept; three parts read the lock face.
- **The proposal.** Every term's every candidate contributes its leading active member's storage
  covector at its section `S_(d(j)) ∪ {j ↦ x}`, weighted `c_x = θ_x` for `x ≠ t` and `c_t = θ_t − 1`
  (each at the dyadic face of its enclosure's midpoint; the direction needs no certificate, the move
  does). The weights take the place of the hinge's `±1` signs (`Contribution::sign`). `returns`, the
  source port's normal law (`Constitution::stepped_source`), and the modulus's least-squares step
  (`γ_ρ = Σ_c c ⟨ĝ_c, ∂z_c/∂ρ⟩`, `Δρ = −γ_ρ/G_ρ`, Lean `modulus_least_squares` for any covectors)
  are unchanged.
- **The first-order certificate.** On the carried move's exact storage moves `Δz`, each candidate's
  directional range `D_x = [min_(m∈A_x) ⟨ĝ_(x,m), Δz_x⟩⁻, max_(m∈A_x) ⟨ĝ_(x,m), Δz_x⟩⁺]` over its
  enclosure-active members `A_x` (the truly active member is among them), and each term's bound
  `Σ_(x≠t) sup(θ_x · D_x) + sup((θ_t − 1) · D_t)` in interval arithmetic with `θ`'s enclosure
  `[L_x/(1 + L_x + Σ_(y≠x) U_y), U_x/(1 + U_x + Σ_(y≠x) L_y)]`. The sum over terms must be certified
  negative (Lean `lockFace_first_order`, `sum_upper_dini_descends`). **A term with an unresolved active
  member refuses the trial** (`TrialRefusal::FirstOrder`, typed), never bounded by its resolved
  members alone.
- **The ladder's start**, one law for both compositions: the first-order zero of the comparison's
  excess over its solved level, `η₀ = 2^⌊log₂ min(X⁻/(−s⁺), ½/u)⌋` with `s` the joint unit move's
  certified slope of the comparison and `u` the unit move's largest entry change; the entry scale
  `½/u` alone when `X⁻ = 0`. For the hinge the solved level is zero and `X = F`: today's law. For the
  lock face the infimum zero is never attained, so the first-order zero of `L` itself would always
  overshoot; the excess is what the linearization can reach.
- **The guards, unchanged as commit guards**: the entry bound `2³`; every candidate crossing
  admissible; every lock's Floquet certificate; the first-order certificate negative; the
  constitution's own guards; and `L(Θ′)⁺ < L(Θ)⁻` by disjoint enclosures, `L(Θ′)` read on `Θ′`'s own
  release and its own decisions (a changed lock order is read, never assumed). The ladder's depth
  stays 8.

### 2.7 The law as continuing deposition

There is no training phase in the law. The machine meets its passage in epochs (divisions of the
aeon at the receiver's section: the cut at which the constitution is read), each of 8 requests in
the pinned runs, the requests of an epoch co-present regions over the one constitution. At each
epoch:
1. every request is released from the open section at the current constitution: the machine's
   output, and the only forward reading (the release's refinements are the comparison's contexts;
   nothing is re-run to train);
2. the targets arrive: the terrain's continuation, the comparison's declared input;
3. the comparison is read at the release's own decisions (§2.4) and returns its receipt;
4. the certified move deposits, adopted only under every guard; a refused move changes nothing;
5. the next epoch meets the successor.

The retained state is `E`, the modulus `ρ` and the source port's normal law (its carried Gram); the
comparison's operands, covectors and contexts are transient, and no tape, journal or list of
epochs is kept. Validation and confirmation reads are exterior receipts at cuts of the passage:
they read the retained constitution the next aeon would meet and deposit nothing. "Sixteen moves"
is the pinned passage's length; the law continues on any passage.

### 2.8 The no-leakage boundary

- The receiver, its opening (`E₀`, `ρ₀ = 102837/131072`), its update and its release read no key
  family, survivor, true lag or admitted continuation. Those are computed only by the counts mode
  from the terrain's pairs, as in 1a; the crate's `hnn::executed` owner has no type of them in scope.
- The comparison's use of the terrain's targets is its declared input: it reads `Request::targets`
  for `q` and for the key-consistent prefix (`r*` compares placed cells with targets). The release
  never reads a target; the causal reading (I6) reads targets as an exterior receipt, never in an
  update.

## 4. The alternatives considered, and why each was not chosen

1. **The hinge with a margin**, `Σ (f + m)₊`. Its zero set is the open predicate set with margin
   `m`, so ties are no longer minimizers. Not chosen: no law fixes `m` (the grain's `2^(−16)` nats
   would move the tie pathology to the margin's boundary; `log 2` borrowed from the lock is the
   candidate's level without its face); its covector is a sign on one leading branch, dropping every
   other rival (a move that lowers the leading rival and raises another is invisible to it); and a
   decision past the margin leaves the composition, reopening the trade of right decisions on
   shared loci that 1a measured (83 right→wrong against 98 wrong→right).
2. **The hinged lock face**, `Σ (ℓ − log 2)₊`. It has a genuine zero set (the solved level) and the
   same face. Not chosen: the hinge is a construction the governing law does not state (the
   classical loss is `−log θ_t` itself); its flat region makes the descent blind beyond the level, so
   right decisions stop resisting moves that lower them until they fall back below it. Its excess is
   kept where it is lawful: as the ladder's start (§2.6).
3. **The face path** (cross-entropy over the bank's second-order reading `A`). Measured and retired:
   misaligned with the executed growth past threshold (the diagnosis §3, cosine `39/512`; Stage 2's
   `face-open` 0 whole). The candidate keeps its cross-entropy form and replaces the reading with the
   one the release decides by.
4. **Key location by loop closure** (`hnn::keys`, the Bombe). It locates the source rings' initial
   configurations from the crib that closed an aeon, over a menu of contacts, and re-keys their
   phases; it does not move `E` and does not change the comparison whose descent 1a measured
   shrinking toward ties, which is the subject 1a names. Taking the terrain's declared lag-map family
   as its menu would hand the receiver the reference family (catered machinery). The owner stays kept
   (THE_REBUILD's batch H keep-list) for the continuing field's ring keys.
5. **Moving the operating point near resonance** (re-founding so readings sit near `θ = ½` of the
   threshold lock). It changes the opening and the release's threshold, not the comparison, and the
   threshold is the bifurcation, not a chosen level. The lock face reads the sheets' relative growth,
   which carries the class information at any common level (§2.4).
6. **The energy face** (`a²`, the lock at half the temperature). The same law at another temperature;
   the release decides by `a` and the lock's face is defined on its readings' weights, so the face
   reads `a`.
7. **Other contexts**: every refinement of the trajectory (1a's convention, kept as the arm `L-all`

## 8. The independence tests: what could show the candidate works for another reason

- **I1, the old law on identical material** (F-all against L-dec): whole sections and stations on
  the final confirmation, per-move flips and margins. If F-all does as well, the candidate adds
  nothing to 1a's law.
- **I2, the context alone** (F-dec against L-dec): if the hinge read at the decisions does as well,
  the gain is the context rule's, and the lock face's claim is unsupported.
- **I3, the objective alone** (L-all against L-dec): if the lock face read at every refinement does
  as well, the context rule's claim ("read where a reading carries information about the key") is
  unsupported.
- **I4, does descent flip decisions right, per move**, every arm: at the epoch's open section and at
  its decisions, the stations wrong→right and right→wrong from `Θ` to the adopted `Θ′`, and summed.
- **I5, does the margin hold on right decisions, per move**, every arm: at the open section and at
  the decisions, the right decisions' count, their mean class gap's and mean `θ_t`'s lower ends, and
  the stations in the solved level.
- **I6, the lock order**: the causal reading on validation (order-2): for each request and station
  `j`, the storage at `j` with stations before `j` placed at their targets and the rest open, its five
  candidates read by the joint growth; per constitution, the stations whose class predicate holds,
  whose threshold holds, in the solved level, and the target's rank. It reads the per-station
  relation with no lock order. Whole sections that rise while the causal count stays at the
  opening's would place the gain in the release's order, not in the relation. Beside it, as in 1a:
  the first lock's station and whether it is right.
- **I7, the relation that needs no request**: per move at the epoch's open section and per
  validation constitution, the termination's rank among the five and the target's rank among the
  four symbols. It separates the key's relation from the termination ranked last (Stage 2's only
  learned relation).
- **I8, the ideal listener's information per epoch** (bits, its own unit), as in 1a: on 1a's passage
  it took all of `log₂ 10240` bits on the first epoch and none after; descents after the listener's
  last informative epoch carry no key information it lacked, and are read as fitting the passage
  unless the validation moves.
- **I9, unseen**: order-2's final confirmation checked exactly for content shared with its training
  and validation requests before its read.

## 10. The prediction and the falsifiers

**The prediction** [agent-inferred]: on order-2, the candidate's descent moves decisions where the
hinge's shrank contrasts: summed over 16 moves at the epochs' open sections, wrong→right at least
`3/2` of right→wrong (1a: 98 against 83); the right decisions' mean class gap at moves 12–15 at least
half its mean at moves 0–3 (1a: from `244/256` to `26/256`); the wrong decisions at moves 12–15 below
`3/4` of moves 0–3 (1a: 203 against 213); and on the final confirmation more whole sections than
both openings (0 at both at every earlier order-2 seed read), more than F-all, F-dec and L-all. The
regressions hold. The acceptance's bar is low (one whole section above 0); the mechanism readings
are what distinguish learning from luck, and they are read as written.

**The falsifiers** (each read as written and kept as it fires):
1. **The acceptance**: L-dec's order-2 `m16` releases no more whole sections on the final
   confirmation than the better opening.
2. **The mechanism, decisions**: summed over the 16 order-2 moves at the epochs' open sections,
   wrong→right at most right→wrong in L-dec.
3. **The mechanism, margins**: L-dec's right decisions' mean class gap (lower end) at the open
   section at moves 12–15 below half its mean at moves 0–3.
4. **The termination only**: at L-dec's `m16` on validation, the target top among the four symbols
   at no more open-section stations than at the founded opening, while `L` fell on the fresh epochs.
5. **The context, not the objective**: F-dec's `m16` releases at least as many whole sections on the
   final confirmation as L-dec's.
6. **The objective alone**: L-all's `m16` at least as many as L-dec's.
7. **Nothing over the old law**: F-all's `m16` at least as many as L-dec's.
   (5–7 are attributions: read when the acceptance holds, reported as readings when it fails.)
8. **The order, not the relation**: L-dec's `m16` causal class-holding stations (validation) no

9. **A regression**: the alternation's or the line's L-dec `m16` releases fewer nonconstant whole
   sections on the final confirmation than its better opening.
10. **Certification**: an adopted move fails a guard, a trial is adopted with an unresolved member,

11. **Unseen**: order-2's final confirmation shares a request's content with its training or

12. **Resources**: a run passes its deadline (reported incomplete, not rerun).
