## Confirmed defects and repairs

[established-bounded; source-inspected] The review repaired these concrete boundaries:

1. **A declared probe could become a universal equivalence claim.**
   `relation_ladder::classify` trusted `probe_is_the_whole_carrier`. It now checks that both
   occurrences belong to a finite carrier closed under every generator and both symmetry maps.
   The generator squares and receiver triangles then apply to every continuation inside that
   carrier. Empty, incomplete and escaping probes stay probes; a checked invariant subcarrier
   can certify useful future equivalence without enumerating its entire ambient type.
2. **Device receipts could describe different tables from those executed.**
   `SectionDeviceTables` now retains borrowed layout/operator/colouring provenance. Enactment
   rejects a different incidence, offset layout or colouring even when every buffer extent
   agrees. Staging checks the colouring before allocation. `SectionApparatus::apply_once` now
   selects its explicitly supplied context before allocation and submission.
3. **Tube receipts could count faces they never inspected.**
   Square and circuit checks reject a supplied face outside their declared chart aperture.
   Profile consumers must pass the faces of the aperture they actually request.
4. **A materialized face could carry an unrelated fibre.**
   `MaterializedFace::found` now checks chart and face agreement with its retained observation
   fibre; the fields are read through accessors.
5. **Remount bypassed grain constructors.**
   Grain faces, towers and selections deserialize through their invariant checks. Unknown tower
   schemas are refused. Selection maps must agree with grain projection and with their inverses.
6. **Declared topology metadata could alias distinct occurrences.**
   `ApertureFiltration::found_declared` rejects repeated occurrence addresses, negative aperture
   ceilings and component maps that do not cover exactly the declared population.
7. **Hodge and rigidity could see different incidence in the same remounted member.**
   `GradedConstraintComplex::validate_structure` checks cell-map coverage, grades, boundaries,
   provenance and open-resolution coherence; both `from_member` consumers invoke it. This
   verifies internal structure, not the historical physical evidence behind a serialized report.
8. **Manually assembled transformations bypassed the passage law.**
   Both the design-situation and evaluation-lineage consumers now validate mutation declarations
   through the existing horizontal-passage constructor, including the actual environment and site.
9. **Junction declarations admitted mismatched cochains and units.**
   Tangential, normal-jump and source maps must name the same joint. Source and flux have equal
   units for the declared integrated discrete cochains. This is not a claim that taking a
   pointwise continuum divergence preserves physical dimension.
