## Closure is not yet whole-question coherence

The passage exposes a precise language boundary. Exact local-region closure is
necessary, but the present answer receiver can close after independently
relevant phases cover its regions. It does not yet require those phases to glue
through a common causal role structure over the whole interrogative.

In less compressed terms, the machine currently knows that one phase reaches
`change` and another reaches `exact suffix`; it does not yet require the first
phase's changer, changed body, and consequence to be the same causal bodies
constrained by the second phase. This is neither a missing scalar score nor a
request for softmax. The missing conditioning capability is an exact
cross-phase relation fiber which either glues the subject/action/object roles
across the complete question or leaves their attempted composition OPEN.

## Returned answers remain too coarse as later organs

The next question was:

> What is ExactSuffixEcology?

Before correction, the body repeated both sentences. The previous answer had
returned as one answerable episode, so reaching the relevant second phase also
made its unrelated first phase available as part of one already-contiguous
standing surface.

The original answer preserved both phases in its generated receipt, but its
later episode passage flattened their outer surface. The next construction
must retain answer-internal phase cells and their incidence as operative later
morphology. A later question should be able to recruit the suffix-definition
phase without inheriting a co-present policy sentence merely because both were
once spoken in the same answer.

## Contextual recruitment is visibly overpermissive

In the same continuing session, the unrelated question:

> What is photosynthesis?

failed direct grounding. The current contextual rule then recruited the prior
question and answer. Eros repeated the prior suffix answer. The new membrane
reported:

```text
context=2

  operator-question-0
  episode-0/answer
world deed: none
```

After `/reset`, the same question did not repeat that answer; it returned an
explicit clarification. Thus the repeated answer is not hidden knowledge or
randomness. It is exactly caused context leakage.

The correction is now concrete: a novel surface belonging only to the new
question must remain an unmatched boundary under contextual enlargement.
Prior dialogue may help route or resolve that boundary, but it cannot erase it
by supplying a larger prompt whose old regions close. `photosynthesis` must
remain OPEN until some receiver actually returns a relation carrying it.

## Feedback and rest remain operative

An operator correction addressed the exact repeated answer episode and

caused a new repository deed rather than directly becoming the selected
answer, which is useful negative evidence: feedback changes admissibility, but
the present query-to-correction coupling is not yet uniformly strong enough to
serve as the answer path itself.

The corrected 17-occurrence session remounted exactly. A separate reset
session containing an unresolved clarification also remounted exactly with its
pending clarification preserved.
