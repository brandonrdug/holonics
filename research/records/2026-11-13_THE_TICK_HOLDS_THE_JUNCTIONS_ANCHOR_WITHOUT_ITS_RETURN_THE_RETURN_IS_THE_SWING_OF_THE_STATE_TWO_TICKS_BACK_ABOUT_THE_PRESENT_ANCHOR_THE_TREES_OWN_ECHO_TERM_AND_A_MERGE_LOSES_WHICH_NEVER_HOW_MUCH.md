## 2. A merge loses which, never how much

**Statement.** Let `Φ` be any map of states (a tick, a release, a rounding, a fold) and `q` a
quantity it conserves, `q ∘ Φ = q`. If `Φ` merges two states, `Φ(a) = Φ(b)`, then `q(a) = q(b)`.

Proof: `q(a) = q(Φ(a)) = q(Φ(b)) = q(b)`.

So every fibre of a merge lies inside one level set of every conserved quantity. What a merge
annihilates is the position inside the level set (which state it was). The level (how much) it
cannot touch, and the level obeys a continuity law, so its disturbance travels by the medium's
flux whatever was merged. That is the correction's content on the objects.

What it changes in the records. The summary line of October 22 ("information dissolves into a
receiver's kernel, never annihilated"), repeated in the records of October 23 and 26 and of
November 7 and 10, states a property of one design as a law of the world. The design is the
carried release (`release.carried-residue`: a stream released with its residue carried conserves
and keeps the orbit). The world also holds the other map: `release.plain-rounding-annihilates`
already says a plain rounding annihilates a constant reading below half a step at every length.
Both rows stand. The slogan is retired in this form: a merge annihilates which and conserves how
much, and a design chooses, release by release, whether the residue is carried.

A merge takes time. At a junction of restitution `Γ` (§6) each arrival's departure from the

the differences the medium has not yet merged, and no more: the lens's remark that a near and

## 3. What a place carries is bounded by its state's surprise to its reader

`landmark.pin-worth-surprise` bounds what a pin `x` is worth to a target `y` under a context `c`:
`ln(p(y|x, c)/p(y|c)) ≤ −ln p(x|c)`. The candidate law of the design record §4.8 took the pin to
be a written place's symbol and concluded that a written place of low news needs no row of its
own. G140 read it before any build and it missed. On 100,508 held places opened alone, with
every written row held at its opening through the ticks the open rows read 3.292 nats a place
worse than the line's own read; with the count reader's landmarks among the written rows
computed as well (0.28 of the rows) they still read 1.604 worse; and as many written places
drawn at random read 0.052 better than the landmarks, so the landmarks ranked no receiver.

**The bound that holds.** Apply the same inequality with the pin a written row's state `z` after
its ticks and the context the reader's own `c′`: `ln(p(y|z, c′)/p(y|c′)) ≤ −ln p(z|c′)`. A row's
worth to a reader is bounded by the surprise of the row's state to that reader. The place's own
news does not bound it. Witness: let `c = (c′, f)` with `f` a fair bit far from the reader, the
place's symbol `x` constant, the row's state `z = f` and the target `y = f`. The place's news is
`−ln p(x|c) = 0` and the row is worth `ln 2`.

So a place's news bounds what it says, and its width bounds what it carries. Cost may follow
surprise only as the surprise of a state to its reader, which no locale holds before the meeting.
G128 b's reading agrees from the other side: on 100 sections no row at any tick moved by less
than `2^(−8)` of its chart's largest entry, so no row was idle to be skipped. A place is never
pruned by its own news.

## 6. The junction in the law, and in the built tick

**The law.** At a junction `r` with a store `s_r` of admittance `Y_r` and arrivals `a` of
conductance `G_a`, the participation anchor is `v_r = (Y_r s_r + Σ_a G_a a)/(Y_r + Σ_a G_a)` and
the junction returns every arrival half-turned about it: `o_(r→a) = 2v_r − a`, `b_r = 2v_r − s_r`
(`hnn.junction-scattering`; `junctionScattering_involutive`, `junctionScattering_isometry`). It
is power-neutral, as the Holon's interconnection is. Reflection is in the law three times over:
this involution; the two-port's `Γ = (R − M)/(R + M)` with `T + Γ² = 1` and `⌈1/T⌉` rounds of
service (`wave.junction-reflection`); and the pumped mode, a transmission and a reflection whose
bifurcation is a standing wave (`hnn.tower-pumped-mode`, `tower.participation-pumped-frame`).

**The built tick** (`field.rs`, the module's law): the current is the normalized weighted mean of
the sources' values, `x ← x + O y + b_o`; the strand's pair contact and the triads add products;
`e_ii = 0`; `K` ticks of one `Θ`. A search of `field.rs`, `field/moment.rs` and `field/scale.rs`
finds no scattering, reflection or impedance, and the tower does not consume `scattering_about`.
Each tick forms a site's meeting, the anchor's analogue, and adds its image to the site's state
(this sentence first said "sets the site to it", the error §13.1 corrects). The state of
the tick before enters the next only through the present state. The junction's mean was kept
and its return was dropped.

**A family that holds both.** Write `P` for a junction's projection onto its anchor, `(Pa)_b = v`
for every port `b`, with weights `π_a` summing to one. For a restitution `Γ` let

```text
S(Γ) = P + Γ(P − I)        (S a)_b − v = −Γ (a_b − v)
```

the Swing of each arrival about the anchor, shortened by `Γ`. This `Γ` is a restitution about the
anchor. What a transmission line calls a port's reflection is the port's own return of item 2.

1. `S(Γ)` has the value 1 on the anchor and `−Γ` on every difference of arrivals:
   `Σ_b π_b |(Sa)_b|² = |v|² + Γ² Σ_b π_b |a_b − v|²`. The anchor always passes whole. The
   differences return half-turned and shortened by `Γ`. This is §2 at a junction: how much
   passes at every `Γ`; which is merged wholly at `Γ = 0` and kept wholly at `Γ = 1`.
2. A port's own return, the share of `a_b` in what is sent back along `b`, is `(1 + Γ)π_b − Γ`.
   Three values are named, for `d` equal ports:

   | `Γ` | junction | own return | power kept on the differences |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | 0 | the anchor broadcast to every port: the built tick | `+1/d`, an echo in phase | none |
   | `1/(d − 1)` | no port hears itself: a matched splitter; a step that does not go back the way it came | 0 | `1/(d − 1)²` |
   | 1 | the half-turn `2P − I`: the law's junction | `(2 − d)/d` | all |

   At `d = 2` the last two coincide: a line passes a wave with nothing returned. At a
   bifurcation, `d = 3`, they cannot: no three-port is lossless, reciprocal and free of return at
   every port at once (microwave network theory; from training, and the proof is three lines: a
   symmetric unitary `S` with a zero diagonal has `S₁₂ conj(S₁₃) = 0` and its two fellows, so
   two of the three couplings vanish and a row is left empty). A lossless tee returns a third of
   an arriving amplitude inverted and sends two thirds down each branch; a splitter that returns
   nothing keeps a quarter of the power of the differences. This is the answer to the question
   in its own chart: the law's junction reflects as a tee does, and the built tick is neither
   device. It returns each arrival's echo in phase and absorbs every difference each tick.
3. **With one tick of transit the family closes on the sites.** Let waves travel a contact in
   one tick, `a_(j←i)^(k+1) = (S(Γ) a_i^k)_(i→j) = (1 + Γ) v_i^k − Γ a_(i←j)^k`, with reciprocal
   contacts and weights `π_ij` that stand over the two ticks, and `A = (π_ij)`. Then

   ```text
   v_i^(k+1) = (1 + Γ)(A v^k)_i − Γ v_i^(k−1),     that is     v^(k+1) − A v^k = −Γ (v^(k−1) − A v^k)
   ```

   Proof: `v_i^(k+1) = Σ_j π_ij [(1 + Γ) v_j^k − Γ a_(j←i)^k]`, and
   `a_(j←i)^k = (1 + Γ) v_i^(k−1) − Γ a_(i←j)^(k−1)`, whose weighted sum over `j` is
   `(1 + Γ) v_i^(k−1) − Γ v_i^(k−1) = v_i^(k−1)`.

   The junction's return, read on the sites, is the Swing of a site's state two ticks back about
   its present anchor, shortened by `Γ`. At `Γ = 1` on a line it is
   `u_i^(k+1) = u_(i+1)^k + u_(i−1)^k − u_i^(k−1)`, the wave equation's own step. At `Γ = 0` it
   is `v^(k+1) = A v^k`, the mean alone: the built tick's form.
4. **The modes.** On a mode of the mean with value `λ` the step's roots solve
   `z² − (1 + Γ)λ z + Γ = 0`. Where `(1 + Γ)² λ² < 4Γ` they are a conjugate pair of modulus `√Γ`:
   the mode turns. Near the conserved mode, `λ = 1 − ε`, the slow root is `1 − ρ ε` to first
   order with `ρ = (1 + Γ)/(1 − Γ)`: it still settles, `ρ` times sooner. At `Γ = 0` every root
   is `λ` itself, and for reciprocal weights `λ` is real (`A = D⁻¹G` with `G` symmetric is
   similar to a symmetric matrix: a network of resistors and stores of one kind relaxes and
   never rings; from training). A mean with no return has no turn to carry.
5. **The two-tick map merges only what its friction declares.** `(x⁻, x) ↦ (x, (1 + Γ) F(x) − Γ x⁻)`
   inverts for every `F`, rounded or not, while `Γ ≠ 0`; at `Γ = 1` it is a shear and a swap
   (`arith.shear-merge-cost`) and loses nothing, and under it the only release with a dropped
   remainder is the product by `Γ`. At `Γ = 0` the pair `(x⁻, x)` is sent to `(x, F(x))` and the
   state before is gone.

## 7. The return, placed in the tick

**The law** (a design; `tower.field-tick-return`). With `v^k = F_Θ(x^k)` the built tick's whole
output at tick `k`,

```text
x_t^(k+1) = v_t^k + Γ_t^k (v_t^k − x_t^(k−1))        Γ = (ρ − 1)/(ρ + 1),   ρ = 2^ℓ,   ℓ_t^k = L r̂(x_t^k) + ℓ_b
```

one real `ℓ` a site a tick, read from the site's own pair chart as the carry's leak is
(`FieldLayout::carry`, `carry_friction`), on the same log chart.

- **The chart is the site's impedance.** `ρ` is the ratio of the load on the site's store to its
  contacts, and `Γ` its reflection, the Cayley map of a positive real as the carry's turn is the
  Cayley map of an imaginary one. `|Γ| < 1` at every finite `ℓ`, so the step is passive by its
  form and a store's quality stays finite (`store.finite-quality`). One unit of `ℓ` doubles the
  rate at which the slow modes settle (§6 item 4), and `ρ` is twice the relaxation time of the
  flux in ticks. `ℓ > 0` returns in phase and carries a rate; `ℓ < 0` returns the difference
  unturned and holds the site against its arrivals, `x^(k+1) = (1 − |Γ|) v + |Γ| x^(k−1)`.
- **Founded at the matched load.** `L = 0` and `ℓ_b = 0` give `ρ = 1` and `Γ = 0` in integers, so
  a grown state reads every face of its source bit for bit and the built tick is the law's case.
  The covector stands at the founding: `δℓ = (ln 2/2)(1 − Γ²) Re⟨λ^(k+1), v^k − x^(k−1)⟩`, at
  most `ln 2/2` a unit of the pairing. This is first order at every tick, so no later term waits
  behind an earlier one, which was the fault of the carry founded at total friction through a
  gate whose terms multiplied (the follow-up record of November 11 §8.5). And it is not the
  founding at zero friction either: `Γ = 1` is not reachable.
- **The opening is at rest.** No momentum is authored: by the reflection of time about the
  opening, `x^(−1) := x^1`, which gives `x^1 = v^0` at every `Γ`. The first tick is the built one
  and the return acts from the second.
- **The covector returns by the same Swing.**
  `λ^k = (1 + Γ^k) J_F(x^k)ᵀ λ^(k+1) − Γ^(k+1) λ^(k+2)` and the chart's term, with the operands the
  forward already holds and one more row a site a tick (the covector two ticks on).
- **Bounds and cost.** Since the charts read `r̂`, a tick's step is bounded by some `c` whatever
  the state, and `|x^(k+1) − x^k| < 2c + |x^k − x^(k−1)|`, so `|x^K − x^0| < K² c` against `K c`
  for the built tick: at four ticks a factor of four, two bits of the state's carrier, to be
  read against the carrier's declared room before a build. The cost is one row of the state's
  length for `ℓ` and one scaled difference a site a tick, beside a row's pair terms; the part
  has `D + 1` entries where `D` is the state row's length.
- **What it is not.** The return lets a tick carry a rate. It does not make a free ring stand.
  A mode with friction rings down unless a pump pays the friction, and the pump of the law is a
  periodic change of the constitution (`parametron.floquet-decision`). In the two-tick form the
  anchor's change from tick to tick is that modulation, a moving grip, and it does work through
  the `½ xᵀĠx` term of the Swing's energy law. A mode of first order cannot be pumped at all:
  under any periodic change its multiplier is a product of positive reals, with no sheet to
  cross to. So the return is the term without which a pump has nothing to act on, and it is not
  the pump. The pump the tree holds below the standing bifurcation is the rotating one
  (`parametron.rotating-pump-resonance`, open: a quarter-turn pump grows where the standing pump
  is passive), and it is unbuilt in the tower (`tower.participation-pumped-frame`: no race pumps).

**The value the constitution itself names.** For a mean with shares `p_ij`, a site's state of two
ticks before returns to it with the share `B_i = Σ_j p_ij p_ji`. Taking the echo out and
bringing the remaining shares back to one gives the same step with

```text
Γ*_i = B_i/(1 − B_i),        ρ*_i = 1/(1 − 2 B_i),        ℓ*_i = −log₂(1 − 2 B_i)
```

For `d` equal reciprocal partners `B = 1/d` and `Γ* = 1/(d − 1)`, the walk that does not

### 13.6 A one-way read is free fall, and the reader's reaction makes it a turn or a boost
