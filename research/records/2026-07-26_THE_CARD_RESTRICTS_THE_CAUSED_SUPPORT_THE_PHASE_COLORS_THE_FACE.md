## Question and correction

The desktop receiver remained slow after terminal-neighborhood propagation was
removed. The question was whether the expensive exact receiver restriction
could cross the GPU without restoring a classical float raster pipeline,
display-owned causality, or one cell as one world event.

The first CUDA passage moved conic support alone. It remained approximately
370 ms at a \(640\times400\) aperture because exact recursive triangle-edge
restriction still ran on the host. That diagnosed the actual bottleneck.
Triangle and conic support now cross the card together.

Vulkan was inspected and rejected for this passage. Its queue/device boundary
adds an unrelated graphics API and a floating-point queue-priority field even
for compute. The engine instead owns a small CUDA Driver boundary and compiles
its CUDA source into PTX during the Rust build. CUDA supplies allocation,
conduct, and parallel physical fibers; it does not define the geometry.

## What the card computes

The source successor still precedes the finite presentation:

\[
S_t
\longrightarrow
\{S_{t,r}\}_{r\in R}
\longrightarrow
\{S_{t,r}\cap D_{\lambda,r}\}_{r\in R}.
\]

Every projected segment is first split exactly at the projective horizon and
clipped to the finite receiver aperture on the host. The resulting finite
endpoints are carried to the card as normalized integer homogeneous points.
Every conic is transformed exactly into the terminal chart; rational
denominators are cleared by their least common multiple and common integer
content is removed.

The card does not dispatch one fiber per display member and does not sweep
radially. Each primitive is restricted first against a sparse population of
\(16\times16\) local aperture sections. A section which the exact segment or
quadratic cannot cross departs immediately. Only an admitted section descends
to its finite members. Multiple edges of one simplex primitive write one
primitive-support carrier, so a vertex shared by two edges is not falsely
counted twice. Primitive carriers are combined into receiver-relative support
multiplicity only after the card returns.

Conic coefficients and homogeneous segment coordinates use signed-magnitude
128-bit device carriers. Before launch, arbitrary-precision host arithmetic derives
the maximum intermediate magnitude for the complete contemporary face. The
executor then selects exact signed `i128`, 192-bit, 256-bit, or 384-bit arithmetic for
all orientation, stationary-point, determinant, and quadratic-range work in
that frame. A law beyond the admitted 128-bit carrier / 384-bit intermediate
boundary is refused; it is never truncated or converted to a float.

The first executor is not admitted by availability. Its complete returned
addresses, primitive multiplicities, and primitive count must equal the
arbitrary-precision host authority. A second physical hinge event then changes
the conic ecology and must again return the same complete face on host and
card.

## Carried physical continuation

The desktop world now uses the exact declared recurrence

\[
q_{k+1}=q_k-q_{k-1},
\]

obtained from the discrete variational law with \(I=K=2\). A boundary impulse
starts one carried current. Subsequent zero-boundary events continue that
current exactly; the six-event recurrence neither damps it nor grows
denominators. Each successor changes the hinge-dependent conics before the
plural receiver assembly, and the card restricts that new caused face.

This is discrete causal continuation, not wall-clock numerical integration.
No elapsed seconds, frame delta, float velocity, or display adjacency enters
the physical law.

The interactive run exposed a distinct temporal defect. The variational
successor previously advanced a trajectory only when its projected kinematic
coordinate differed. At a turning event with an equal coordinate, the elapsed
event was erased and the incoming boundary current could be lost. The physical
worldline now always advances its selected
\((q_{k-1},q_k)\) carrier on a completed event. Coordinate equality is not
event equality.

The desktop also no longer prints one continuation every 60 events. Since 60
is divisible by the exact six-event recurrence, that logger always observed
the same phase and made a periodic orbit look stationary. An exact full-state
observer now reports the first actual return and its period. Boundary input
starts a new observed segment. Complete event testimony is written as exact
ratios to `target/holonic-engine/desktop_receiver_trace.tsv`; the trace includes
the receiver standing, all hinge trajectories, orbit invariant, support
counts, arithmetic tier, intermediate bound, and wall receipt.

A future exact range refusal no longer closes the desktop process. The
physical and receiver standing remain carried, the last valid presentation
remains visible, and autonomous continuation pauses. A later input may return
the world to an admitted presentation region. The refusal is printed; it is
not clipped, wrapped, or approximated.

\[

J_t=J_t^{\mathrm{internal}}+J_t^{\mathrm{external}}.

Each receiver transports this triple through its own standing projective

## Ownership boundary

one complete finite receiver face. The host still owns:

- the small six-hinge arbitrary-rational variational successor;
