## What now governs reasoning

[project-postulate] Difference comes before its measured value. A comparison retains its
comparands, reference, conditions, orientation and causal history. A zero receiver result is a
scoped agreement; it does not identify all causes or all future conduct. Numerical subtraction
is one additive chart, not the entire difference ontology.

[project-postulate] Relevance and relativity are constitutive. Everything participates in
oriented, conditioned causal relations. Frame comparison formalizes one aspect of that
participation. Actual interaction and future consequence determine the relation; a label,
universal all-to-all coupling or scalar relevance governor does not supply it.

[project-postulate] Mathematics, engineering and cartography are connected constructive activity.
A result carries the further inference and construction it enables. Treat a new material as
an opportunity to recover and instantiate a shared relation; preserve the work already engaged.
An apparent obstacle calls for investigation of the actual source, chart or missing distinction.

[project-postulate] The causal ontology approaches apparent randomness through unresolved
conditions and receiver scope. Probability and statistics remain lawful charts. Compression,
intelligence and navigation name the reuse and refinement of consequential relations. Keep
compatible Preimage Fibres without requiring perfect inversion of a microscopic past.
