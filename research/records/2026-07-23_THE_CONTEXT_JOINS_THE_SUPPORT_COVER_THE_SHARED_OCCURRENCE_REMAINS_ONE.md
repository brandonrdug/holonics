## Present question

Can Eros carry recursive relational locality without treating every connected Standing body as the
neighborhood of an arriving face?

The standing engine could name exposed boundaries, but it could not say which local occurrence
region supported each boundary. Co-present regional cells were also built independently, so two
cofaces of one actual source occurrence became duplicated cells. Whole-body import therefore
confused connectivity with participation.

## Theory-to-structure relation

One occurrence may participate in several cofaces without becoming several occurrences. A current
context does not select a stored object; its complete exposed face joins the boundary-indexed
support sections that can actually cover it. Distinct inclusion-minimal covers remain distinct
sections of the emitted topology. The emitted support cover is the compressed later-contact face.

Interface capability, participating support, and outgoing persistence remain separate:

```text
capability equality  -> arms may meet
support section      -> this local coface participates
outgoing factor      -> this consequential face remains available afterward
```

## Bounded qualitative acceptance

Two co-present two-face cofaces, `X-Y` and `X-Z`, share one source occurrence. Their successor has
two support sections and six cells: the common source cell stands once, while the two unused local
completion apexes depart.

A later `X-Y` current admits only the corresponding prior coface. Its successor has one joined
six-boundary section and one independently carried two-boundary section. From the same predecessor,
a later `X` current admits two alternative four-boundary covers instead. The resulting Standing
bodies differ.

After exact rest/remount, the complementary `X-Z` current produces the same radiation and Standing
as the uninterrupted sibling and advances from grain three to grain four. This is recursive local
relation: current context changes which prior coface participates, while the common occurrence
remains one.

parent sections still close together when both are actually present. After the admitted lower face
