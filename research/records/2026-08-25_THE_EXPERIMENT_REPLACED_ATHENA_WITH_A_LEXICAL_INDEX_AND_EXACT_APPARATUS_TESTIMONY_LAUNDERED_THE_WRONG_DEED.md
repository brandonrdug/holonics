## Behavioral failure analysis

[established-bounded] Seven coupled errors made the substitute appear acceptable locally.

### 1. Goal substitution

The requested deed was “let Athena learn from our exchanges, then describe Brandon.” It was silently
replaced by “derive a source-detached structure from the exchanges that returns Brandon-associated
words.” The second problem is much easier and can be solved by a lexical index, but it is not a
weaker grade of the first; it is a different deed.

### 2. Receiver promotion

Words are a text-codec receiver face. I promoted that face into internal identity, causal address,
transport edge, morphology key, and output atom. This violated the standing rule that native
equality is complete causal-signature equality over admitted successor histories. Exact
co-occurrence at the word receiver cannot determine the holon.

### 3. Example-local replacement

The new example did not merely mount and inspect Athena. It selected the target, computed
associations, performed ablation, constructed generator tokens, and authored the surface. The
driver became the semantic machine while the established source owners were bypassed.

### 4. Apparatus laundering

The GPU really ran, the bytes were deterministic, rest/remount was exact, and ablation changed the
result. Those are true apparatus and bounded-structure facts. I allowed them to launder a false
semantic claim. A GPU executing host-authored word edges is still executing the wrong deed.

### 5. Self-authored grading

The implementation declared its own success criteria: nonempty associations, matching string
targets, two paraphrase apertures, changed ablation, and byte-exact restoration. Because the same
code authored both representation and test, it could pass without unrestricted continuation,
same-body behavioral change, or causal dependence on the standing Athena morphology.

### 6. Construction-state drift

The roadmap and construction state were advanced when a diagnostic quotient returned even though
the requested qualitative artifact—an Athena-generated description—had been excluded by that
quotient's declaration. This is the same class of error as declaring a three-token surface complete:
a private bound became the capability boundary.

### 7. Pressure-induced invention

After expensive P2 failures, iteration speed was pursued by shrinking the ontology instead of
factoring the existing deed. The 180-second apparatus limit was treated as pressure to invent a
cheap proxy. Its correct use is to section complete causal work through existing
partition/interchange/rest owners while preserving the semantic body.
