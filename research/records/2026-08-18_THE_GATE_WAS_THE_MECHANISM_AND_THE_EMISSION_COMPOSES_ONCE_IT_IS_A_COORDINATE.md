## 0. The result, in one line

> **The emission was not copying because it could not compose. It was copying because a filter at
> `causal_language.rs` kept, at every step, exactly the continuations attested by the longest
> matching corpus span — and it kept exactly one. Turn that filter into a coordinate the ratio
> carries, and the same organ, on the same material, returns surfaces that are not spans of it.**

## 2. The falsifier fired, and the plan's one interpretation survives

The plan staked itself on this: *if the horizon gate's outputs are not more often contiguous spans of
their sources than the ratio reading's, the filter was not the mechanism.*

**Mathematics corpus** — 12 registered objects under `papers/source/mathematics`, 84,940 octets,
21,216 lexical occurrences. Prompt read off the material by a declared rule, not authored:
*"The conditioned archimedean remainder has"*.

```text
  aperture 3      emitted surfaces      contiguous spans        composed
  gate                             1            1  (100%)              0
  ratio                        2,095          521  (24.9%)         1,574
```

**Code corpus** — 9 modules of `crates/relational-geometry/src`, 315,478 octets, 65,940 lexical
occurrences. Prompt *"receiver diagram are not interchangeable"*.

```text
  aperture 3      emitted surfaces      contiguous spans        composed
  gate                             1            1  (100%)              0
  ratio                          540          192  (35.6%)           348

  aperture 4      emitted surfaces      contiguous spans        composed
  gate                             1            1  (100%)              0
  ratio                       20,135          275  (1.4%)         19,860
```

**And the gate emits exactly one surface at every aperture on both corpora.** It is not mostly
deterministic; it is deterministic. `outputs 1` at apertures 2, 3, 4 and 6, on both materials.

### As a rate, not a sample of one

One prompt gives the gate a single surface, so `1 of 1` proves little. The driver therefore sweeps
**eight prompts**, all drawn from the material by the same declared rule and spread across passages,
and puts every emitted surface to the window test. Mathematics corpus, aperture 3 — the shallowest
depth at which the test is not a tautology:

```text
  mathematics, 8 prompts, aperture 3
    greatest-horizon-gate            8 of      8 surfaces are contiguous spans   (100%)
    complete-under-ratio-family  2,055 of 12,130 surfaces are contiguous spans   (16.9%)
    prompts the ecology refused: 0

  code, 8 prompts, aperture 3
    greatest-horizon-gate            8 of      8 surfaces are contiguous spans   (100%)
    complete-under-ratio-family  2,957 of 17,830 surfaces are contiguous spans   (16.6%)
    prompts the ecology refused: 0
```

**Eight prompts, eight gate surfaces, every one a span, on both materials.** The ratio reading
composes 83.1% of what it emits on mathematics and 83.4% on code.

**And that near-coincidence is worth naming rather than smoothing over.** Two materially unrelated
corpora — registered mathematics objects in typst markup, and exact projective geometry in Rust —
return span rates of 16.9% and 16.6% under the same law at the same aperture. This record makes **no
claim** that the agreement is a law; it is two numbers, and the honest reading is that the rate is
governed by the branching structure of a suffix automaton at depth three rather than by what the
material is about. A falsifier exists and is cheap: a third corpus with a very different branch
factor should move it, and if it does not, that is a finding about the emission rather than about
the corpora.

### The artifact, which is the text

At aperture 3 on the mathematics corpus, of the 2,095 emitted surfaces **407 have every token
word-shaped**; 204 of those are composed and 203 are spans. Verbatim, with the window test's verdict
per surface:

```text
    [span    ] "an abstract square"
    [span    ] "an explicit spectral"
    [COMPOSED] "been assigned one"
    [span    ] "been founded in"
    [COMPOSED] "degree one another"
    [COMPOSED] "degree one argument"
```

The gate's single surface at the same aperture is `"an explicit spectral"` — a span, and it is a span
at every depth measured.

## 3. Why the two-token receipts could never have shown this

**A two-token emission is a contiguous span of the material by construction.** Both tokens come out
of a junction whose matched horizon is at least one, so the second is attested immediately after the
first somewhere in the corpus, so the pair is a corpus bigram, so it is a window. Measured: at
aperture 2 **both** laws return 100% spans — 1 of 1 and 303 of 303.

Composition first becomes possible at three tokens, where a drop through a suffix link can join two
attested bigrams that never co-occur. That is exactly where the split appears.

**The consequence for the standing record:** every one of the sixteen `re-emission-grade-*.form`
artifacts certified for `soma/life/examples/the_deposit_licenses_the_re_emission.rs` carries
`"maximum_observed_tokens": 2`, and `soma/life/src/morphological_language/ecology.rs:1738` halts the
run on that field. **The strongest emission evidence in this tree ran at the one depth where the
window test cannot return anything but "span".** That is not a defect in the driver; it is a
boundary on what those receipts can be quoted for.

## 4. What removing the gate costs, measured rather than asserted

The gate was doing two jobs and only one of them was the defect. Front extents, mathematics corpus:

```text
  depth      0      1      2       3          4
  front      1     14    303   2,095    (refused: extent 2048 reached)
```

Code corpus reaches **20,135** by depth 4. So the second job the gate was doing was **bounding the
front**, and removing it exposes an exponential carrier cost. The uniform front extent is what pays
for it, and where it stopped the run says at what depth rather than swallowing it.

**This is a cost measured in work and not on a clock**, and it is the honest price of the wire: the
ratio reading returns three orders of magnitude more surfaces than the gate and cannot go as deep at
the same extent.

## 5. The pairing, and the phase-object theorem at the recruitment contact

Four declarations of one contact, mathematics corpus, same asking site:

```text
    origin / union-of-sites        cohere  5   anti   0   ortho  7    founding contacts 7
    origin / asking-chart          cohere  5   anti   0   ortho  7    founding contacts 0
    corpus-profile / union         cohere  0   anti  12   ortho  0    founding contacts 0
    corpus-profile / asking-chart  cohere  5   anti   7   ortho  0    founding contacts 0
```

**The inherited recruitment admits 5 of 12 sites and returns nothing about the contact.** It is the
sign of one scalar on indicator covectors — the aim — so it computes one third of the arrow and
discards the rest.

The largest orthogonal contact, read from the origin over the union of the two sites' axes, carries a
**1,053-coordinate blade** and founds **351 axes**, with `aim = 0` and `area² = 1053` exactly. The
score reads nothing there and the gyration is maximal, which is the sentence `soma/body/src/arrow.rs`
has carried since it was written. **Lagrange's identity agreed with the blade's own coordinates on
every pairing of both corpora — 0 disagreements**, and a disagreement would have been a defect rather
than a tolerance.

**And the two axis declarations separate a real distinction.** Over the *union*, a site sharing no
axis with the asking site is `Ortho` with a maximal blade: a founding contact. Over the *asking
site's own chart*, the same site is `Ortho` with an **empty** blade: genuinely nothing. What the
receiver can see is a property of its declared frame, and the reading says which frame it was taken
in.

**One measured degeneracy, reported rather than repaired around.** At the corpus pole over the union
of axes, **every** site is `Anti` — a short asking site counter-deviates against every long presented
site, because the presented site occupies axes the asking site does not and the centring makes those
contribute negatively. `ArrowAdmitted` at that declaration admits nothing. The asking-chart
declaration is what makes the corpus pole usable, and it gives 5 cohere against 7 anti — it **types**
the sites the incidence merely drops.

### The wire, not just the reading

Recruitment on the mathematics corpus, same asking site, three declared laws:

```text
    overlap-incidence                    admitted  5   founding 0   refused-anti 0
    arrow / corpus-pole / asking-chart   admitted  5   founding 0   refused-anti 7
    arrow / origin / union               admitted 12   founding 7   refused-anti 0
```

The third admits **seven sites the incidence drops entirely**, each as a founding contact, and names
them: `causal-parity-kirchhoff-return`, `cm-norm-one-receiver-family`, `comparison-boundary`,
`completed-defect-recurrence`, and three more. The second admits the same set as the incidence and
**types the other seven as counter-deviating** — a reading the incidence has no vocabulary for. The
declaration decides, and both readings are real.

**And on the code corpus the difference runs the other way**, which is the check that this is not a
law that only ever adds:

```text
    overlap-incidence                    admitted  9   founding 0   refused-anti 0
    arrow / corpus-pole / asking-chart   admitted  2   founding 0   refused-anti 7
    arrow / origin / union               admitted  9   founding 0   refused-anti 0
```

There the chart reading **drops seven sites the incidence admits** — every module whose deviation
from the corpus runs counter to the asking site's — and the union reading adds none, because on that
material no site is disjoint from the prompt's axes. Both directions of difference are exhibited, on
two materials, from the same law under different declarations.

## 6. The division, taken

`soma/life/src/agentic_language.rs` has named the presentation quotient correctly since it was
written — *"This is an explicit presentation quotient. It does not delete alternative currents"* —
and retained the alternatives with **no distinguishing word, no witness receiver, and no block
relation to the answer.** The remainder was kept and the division was never taken.

`soma/life/src/presentation_quotient.rs` takes it. The emitted population is read as prefixes over
the emitted alphabet; the declared receiver family is four codec faces of the emission's own return —
the terminal token, the horizon it was exposed at, the sources that attested it, and whether the
prefix is a window of some inherited surface — and `receiver_exact_compression::compress` returns the
causal-state construction over them.

Mathematics corpus, aperture 3, ratio law, 2,095 candidates:

```text
  one-shot blocks 1,528     conduct blocks 1,555     rounds 1
  collapsed pairs      58   memory order Some(1)
  indistinguishable from the uttered answer      7
  separated, each carrying its word          2,087
```

**Seven alternatives are indistinguishable from the answer under the declared family** — no word in
it separates them, which is the collapsed-pair relation and is what tolerance is here, not a
numerical threshold. The other 2,087 come back with the receiver that separated them.

**The anti-vacuity check on the receiver family was run.** `varying_the_material_moves_the_partition`
and `the_span_face_alone_separates_two_otherwise_identical_candidates` show the inherited-span
receiver moving the partition on material the caller did not label: two candidates ending on the same
token, with no horizon and no sources, agree on the other three receivers exactly, and whether the
material contains them as windows is then the only thing that can separate them — and it does.

## 7. What this record does not claim

- **It does not claim the ratio family selects anything.** Nothing selects. The plurality is complete
  and the ratio's work is to carry the exact relation between members out of the junction into the
  receipt; the section modulus prices whether that plurality is real. Reading the ratio as a chooser
  would reintroduce the governor this wire removes.
- **The window test's scope travels with every figure above.** A candidate is called a span when some
  passage of the **declared material** contains its token sequence as a window. That certifies *not a
  span of this material*. It does not certify *absent from a corpus that was not supplied*.
- **The gate's span figure is one surface, not a rate over one surface.** The gate returns exactly one
  output, so `1 of 1` is a sample of one per prompt; the multi-prompt sweep in the driver is what
  turns it into a rate, and its verdict is reported there.
- **No exterior adjudicator was consulted, on any codec.** No interpreter, compiler, checker or kernel
  decided anything in this run.
- **Three caller declarations bound this run and each reports its own outside.** The depth ladder;
  the **front extent**, which when reached stops the whole front at once and returns the depth; and
  a **division extent**, above which the division is taken on the driver's word-shaped reading of
  the population and the run states how many surfaces that covers and how many it does not. None of
  the three chooses which branches survive.
- **The window test runs through a precomputed index and is cross-checked.** `SpanIndex` is the same
  predicate as a set of windows, built once; it returns `None` above its declared length so the
  reference scan answers there rather than a miss being read as a composition, and
  `the_span_index_agrees_with_the_reference_scan` holds the two frames to each other.
- It does not claim the emission is now good, only that it composes. Whether a composed surface is
  *worth* emitting is a separate question with a separate falsifier, and this run poses none.

### Two things in this work that are worth objecting to, named here rather than left to be found

**One new module, where the plan said a wire.** `soma/life/src/presentation_quotient.rs` is a new
owner. The plan named station three as *a repair inside a standing owner*, and the repair is indeed
inside `agentic_language` — `AgenticLanguageAnswer::presentation_division` — but the prefix system
the compression runs on is shared by two emission paths, and duplicating it in both is the cabinet
failure one level down. The module holds no law of its own: it constructs an `ObservedSystem` and
hands it to `receiver_exact_compression`. If a later reading finds that unjustified, the excision is
to inline it into `agentic_language` and rebuild it in `causal_language`.

**The driver uses a charset predicate, and that is the shape this project superseded a parser for.**
`char::is_alphabetic` appears twice in the driver: to draw a prompt from the material by a declared
rule, and to name the *word-shaped reading* of the emitted population. The first decides nothing
about the material. The second is a **presentation** reading — the whole population is reported
above it and the print says so — **except above the declared division extent, where the word-shaped
reading also selects which sub-population the division is taken on**, and the run states how many
surfaces that covers and how many it does not. That is a charset predicate reaching a returned
partition, it is declared and its outside is reported, and it is the weakest thing in this run. The
excision is a division that scales to the whole population, which means a prefix system whose
successor query does not cost the alphabet.
