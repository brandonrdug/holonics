## Present question and result

Does Eros require a recursive relation among occurrences in a receiver-relative “nearby” sense,
and does the current engine actually embody it?

Yes, but only half of that relation is currently embodied.

The direct machine already closes actual lower regions into higher-grain occurrences, retains
their exposed boundaries, lets lower constituents depart, and permits a genuinely later Current
to meet the higher Standing. The bounded prime hand-up physically demonstrated a declared
`grain 3 -> grain 4 -> grain 5` relation.

The missing half is **recursive relational locality**. A later contact currently finds an exposed
arm on one Standing constituent and imports that complete constituent. It has no exact
boundary-indexed section saying which local support realizes that arm. The corpus experiment
therefore let one adjacent-token interface recruit a connected constituent containing 1,121
utterances.

The correction is not a semantic selector, fixed token window, attention governor, distance
threshold, or search over every possible subcomplex. It is a direct geometric distinction:

```text
interface admission   says whether two actual boundary arms may meet;
local support         says which occurrence neighborhood participates in that meeting;
outgoing factor       says which consequential face remains active afterward.
```

These three relations may agree in a tiny fixture. They are not one law.

## I · Nearby is typed incidence, not a scalar distance

At one actual event cut `e` and organizational grain `g`, let

```text
K_(e,g) = (O,I,D,T)
```

be the participating occurrence complex:

- `O` is the finite population of actual occurrences;
- `I` is oriented boundary incidence;
- `D` is actual dependency incidence; and
- `T` is local transport.

For an actual occurrence `sigma`, define its event-local coface population

```text
Cof_(e,g)(sigma)
  = { tau in O | sigma actually participates in tau
                    by admitted incidence, dependency, or interface transport at e }.
```

Its **closed relational star** is the coface population together with every actual face needed to
make those cofaces an incidence-closed local region:

```text
StBar_(e,g)(sigma)
  = FaceClosure({sigma} union Cof_(e,g)(sigma)).
```

In a simplicial chart its **link** is exactly

```text
Lk_K(sigma)
  = { eta in K | eta is disjoint from sigma
                 and eta union sigma is in K }.
```

For a general cellular or hypergraphic source, the **relational link** is the corresponding
population of contextual faces in the star which do not contain the focal occurrence, carried
with their actual attachment maps to cofaces which do. `FaceClosure` and the link use only actual
supplied incidence. They may not invent missing simplices merely because all pairwise labels or
coordinates happen to agree.

The complete nearby face is therefore structured:

```text
Near_(e,r,g)(sigma)
  = (StBar_(e,g)(sigma),
     Lk_(e,g)(sigma),
     I restricted to StBar,
     D restricted to StBar,
     T restricted to StBar,
     receiver r).
```

There is no required scalar radius. Several distinct notions remain typed:

- **incidence-near** — two occurrences share an actual face or coface;
- **dependency-near** — one occurrence materially participates in another event;
- **transport-near** — a lawful local path connects their carrier faces;
- **grain-near** — one occurrence is a face of a completed higher construction; and
- **metric-near** — a later optional chart supplies lengths or conductance.

The first four do not require the fifth. Drawing two points close together supplies none of them.
“Local” also does not mean few rows or small physical volume. It means that the relation factors
through a named focal occurrence and its contemporary boundary. A highly recurrent occurrence
can have a large plural star without making every coface one identity.

## II · One star can carry a plural fan

One occurrence can belong to several actual cofaces. Its star is then a fan, not one private cell
state and not a forced choice of one continuation:

```text

                         /
focal sigma -----------+--- link face c_2
                         \
                          link face c_3.
```

Every coface remains a distinct occurrence. Equal carried values do not identify them. The shared
focal occurrence is retained once as shared incidence rather than copied into three unrelated
bodies.

This is the exact geometric basis for a first-person field of recurrence. The field is the plural
population of situated links around a received face. It is not yet a probability scalar, and it
does not require one coface to win.

## IV · Context is the relational join of co-present faces

Suppose one contemporary Current presents an ordered population of actual faces

```text

```

Each face admits zero or more Standing boundary occurrences through exact source capabilities.
For one face, the candidate support fan is

```text

  = { U | some b in boundary(U) is actually admitted by a at e }.
```

Context is not a static stored edge chosen by a score. Its presence restricts the fan by a
relational join over the actual boundary-to-section incidence:

```text

  = { U | for every a_i there is an admitted b_i in boundary(U),
            and (b_1,...,b_m) participate in one actual link/coface of U }.
```

Categorically this is a pullback of the current-to-boundary admission relation along the
boundary-to-section incidence. When several sections are literal subcomplexes of one shared
occurrence complex, their physical overlap is an intersection and the atlas overlap maps preserve
those shared occurrences. Analogous sections from different histories remain distinct even when
they satisfy the same current faces.

The outcomes remain geometric:

- an empty joined population is an OPEN contextual relation, not deletion of any section;
- one connected overlap is one locally supported continuation;
- several disconnected overlaps remain plural;
- an oriented or transport mismatch remains a complete residual; and
- a genuinely supplied higher coface may close the overlap and hand it upward.


This is the constructive content of “context reorients the field.” Adding a co-present face

## VI · Recursion requires re-base, not transitive component expansion

Let `U_g` be one locally supported region whose interior boundary has actually closed relative to
its exposed link. Completion owes an actual hand-up:

```text

  --close at event e-->

```

The higher occurrence `u_(g+1)` then participates in another star and link at its new grain:

```text

```

This is recursive relation among relations. The lower occurrence is not merely relabelled; a new
occurrence exists with its own lineage and exposed link.

Several hand-ups may belong to one atomic event when every required lower and parent interface is
actually co-present. No intermediate completion becomes Standing. Otherwise the next hand-up
requires genuinely later Current. The existence of a closed grain-`g+1` body does not synthesize
a universal parent port for grain `g+2`.

The distinction from graph percolation is decisive:

```text
rejected:
  one match -> transitive reachability through every connected interface -> one growing body

required:
  one match -> its supported star closes -> re-base as a higher occurrence
            -> its exposed link participates at the next grain.
```

Recursive breadth comes from plural cofaces. Recursive depth comes from actual hand-up. Neither is
an unbounded same-grain flood.
