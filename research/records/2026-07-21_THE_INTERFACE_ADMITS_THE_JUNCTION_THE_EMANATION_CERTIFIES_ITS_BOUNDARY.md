## 1 · Three independent coordinates

`EventCell` and `LiveCell` now keep three separately supplied coordinates:

- `dependency_rank` orders the source-declared event-local dependency;
- `dimension` is oriented boundary degree; and
- `grain` is receiver-relative constituent scale.

Boundary incidence holds dependency rank and raises dimension by one. Ordinary dependency raises
rank. `RewriteInterface` raises rank while holding dimension. Grain is free to change under either
when the source world declares that situated occurrence.

`EventCell::new` remains an explicit aligned-chart convenience in which dimension and grain agree.
`EventCell::situated` is the general constructor. No runtime inference relates the three.

## 2 · The source names the interface

`InterfaceCapability(namespace, local)` is an opaque world-declared identity. Equal values,
coordinates, reaches, grains, faces, or camera positions do not manufacture it. Regional arms can
join only when both carry the same declared capability.

This removes the former `reach + grain` admission heuristic. A source may lawfully reuse one
capability across later events when it is the continuing material seam. Chronology itself is not
that identity.

The complete face pair is retained as

```text

```

where `through` and `direct` are primary. `chi` is an optional projection available in Soma's
current Face carrier. A comparison which has no `Chi` projection remains an open joined interface;
it is not dropped or converted to zero.
