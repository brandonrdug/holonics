## The distinction is an enacted relation, not a data format

Information is not intrinsically either “reference” or “training data.” The distinction is what
the contemporary receiver does with one caused occurrence.

A **reference** is consulted through a local aperture while answering the present problem. It may
restrict the present calculation and its lineage must remain visible in the answer, but the act of
consultation does not by itself change the standing morphology. Closing the reference membrane
leaves the intermediate body equal to its predecessor.

A **conditioning occurrence** returns through the body's causal mouth. It changes incidence,
version fibers, recurrence, obstruction, phase, or permitted transport, and therefore changes how
later problems conduct. Inherited corpora and demonstrations are initial conditioning when they
are used to found standing. An imported landmark is also conditioning if it enters standing and
restricts a version fiber; calling it imported does not make it a read-only reference.

The same external inscription can lawfully participate in either relation. A theorem consulted
during one proof may remain a reference; a returned worked passage may later condition a reusable
proof morphology. The distinction must be present in the enacted lineage rather than inferred
from filenames, dataset splits, or an application label.

There is consequently no train/freeze/infer lifecycle in this construction. There is one
continuing intermediate body. Every caused return may cultivate its contemporary morphology, and
every later deed uses the resulting successor. A self-emanated answer already conditions later
dialogue topology as an answer occurrence, but it does not certify itself as exterior truth;
acceptance, correction, refusal, and world return remain different caused roles.

## What this establishes

- continual learning by exact causal succession rather than a frozen parameter snapshot;

that grammar; they do not induce arbitrary machine code or certify an exterior program outside
