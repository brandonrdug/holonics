## Correction

An invariant is not a property declared unchanged everywhere. It is a relation which remains
sufficiently unchanged under a named transport through a bounded arc of history. Change the
receiver, medium, measurement construction, objective, grain, or interval and the former invariant
may split into different faces. Those faces can still support an analogous inference without
becoming one identical object.

This is the missing precision in “context bends the word.” Context is not a label attached to
material. It is the lived causal arc in which material stands, recurs, meets other construction,
and has consequences.

## The typed arc

Write one observational arc as

```text
A = (B, F, g, M, O, [t0,t1], H)
```

where `B` is the admitted boundary, `F` the receiver frame, `g` its grain, `M` the material medium,
`O` the consequential objective or world law, `[t0,t1]` the measured interval, and `H` the carried
history entering it. A relation `I` is invariant only with respect to a declared transport `T`
through `A` when the faces required for the contemporary deed and its consequence carry under
`T`. Nothing in that statement licenses a frame-free invariant.

The invariant is therefore closer to a stable axis of action than to a frozen value. It is what a
current can pivot from over this arc. The same relation can cease to be a useful pivot when later
contact changes the population, boundary, or required consequence.

## Measurement methods produce distinct faces

Two instruments never literally perform one event. Each has its own material body, preparation,
aperture, calibration, receiver grain, and contact history. Agreement means that a lawful
translation carries enough relation between their distinct output faces for one declared use. It
does not make the faces identical. Disagreement can expose receiver construction, a changed
population, or a real difference in the observed world; the full methods must remain beside the
results before those possibilities can be distinguished.

The cosmological-constant intuition belongs here. The calculus may not assume that a value which
appears stable inside our observable cosmic manifold and era is globally constant. Different
measurement methods are genuinely different receiver charts and can expose different magnitudes
of residual. Whether the physical cosmological constant varies is an empirical cosmology question;
the laboratory law is already definite: neither agreement nor disagreement permits erasing the
method, frame, interval, or received face.

## Statistics is the exact distribution of an arc

For a deterministic history population admitted by `A`, a tendency is the exact quotient of deeds
or surfaces satisfying a named relation inside that population. It may be highly stable over
`[t0,t1]` and still change when the arc grows. The distribution is useful precisely because it
compresses what has remained relatively invariant in that region; it is not a timeless command
over future output.

Thus “standard English expression is usually wrong inside Rust syntax” is a lawful local tendency,
not a contradiction. English prose and Rust source are different material worlds with different
expression laws. Correctness means continued consequence under the contemporary world boundary.
A sentence can be well-formed prose and compiler-refused code; a Rust expression can be lawful code
and poor exposition. Neither world owns a universal grammar.

An individual's prose also has local law. Word choice, punctuation, indentation, paragraph
separation, cadence, and the proximity of repeated surfaces form a history-conditioned

## A prior occurrence becomes a standing pivot

Let two occurrence events carry the same visible surface `s`:

```text
e_i = (s, A_i, K_i, consequence_i)
e_j = (s, A_j, K_j, consequence_j),  j > i.
```

Their equal face does not imply an equal deed. Once `e_i` occurs, “`s` occurred here” is a durable
fact of the later arc. The later current can pivot from that standing occurrence.

Close repetition tends to be redundant when the same surface attempts to carry the same relation
again without a changed neighborhood, foil, objective, or consequence. Repetition becomes useful
contrast when the later occurrence changes what the surface is related to, exposes another face,
or deliberately establishes recurrence as the subject. Redundancy is therefore not a word-count
penalty. It is an oriented residual between repeated material and the new relation it makes
available.

This explains the writing heuristic without installing it as a rule. A nearby repeated word often
offers little new path because its former use already stands as a local axis. A writer can make the
same word necessary by changing the context so that the two occurrences foil one another. The
second use then carries a distinction which a synonym might erase.

## Translation changes the medium law

To translate code into English exposition is not to preserve glyphs. It is to carry a chosen
relation—intention, operation order, constraint, or world consequence—through a transformation
into a medium with different local expression laws. The source and translated faces are
objectively different. Their correspondence is established by what relation survives and what
residual returns, not by surface similarity.

This makes translation an ordinary Swing:

```text
source construction -> source-world consequence
        | transport through another medium
translated surface  -> receiving-world consequence
```

Analogy carries what survives; foil retains the difference. Repeated translation and return grow a
local cross-medium path ecology. No dictionary, grammar, or authored semantic coordinate is owed
inside Soma, although dictionaries, grammars, compilers, and human testimony may all cross as
ordinary world material and consequences.
