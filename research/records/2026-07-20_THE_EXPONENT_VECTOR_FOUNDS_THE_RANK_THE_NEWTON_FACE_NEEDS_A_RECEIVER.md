## II · An exponent sign is not itself a new dimension

For an algebraic torus `T=(G_m)^d`, a Laurent monomial is

```text
x^alpha = product_j x_j^(alpha_j),     alpha in Z^d.
```

The exponent vector is the exact lattice coordinate.

- `alpha_j>0` moves along the positive character direction of axis `j`;
- `alpha_j=0` makes that axis inactive in the monomial;
- `alpha_j<0` is a reciprocal or dual direction on the same axis; and
- rank increases only when a previously absent independent basis component becomes nonzero.

Thus `x^a -> x^-a` reverses within one Laurent direction. `x^a -> x^a y^b`, with independent
`y` and `b!=0`, changes support rank. A negative exponent can look like leaving an affine chart
because the coordinate hyperplane `x=0` is excluded from the torus, but it does not by itself add a
dimension.

For integers, the exact valuation coordinate is

```text
nu(n)=(v_p(n))_p.
```

It has finite nonnegative support. A prime `p` is the primitive basis vector `e_p`; a composite is
the joined exponent vector supplied by unique factorization. Extending from positive integers to
nonzero rationals permits negative valuations and therefore reciprocal motion along already-founded
prime axes.

Residue phase, valuation exponent, and radix glyph remain different charts. Decimal terminal
digits arise from the `2`-by-`5` residue quotient, not from the sign of a valuation exponent.

## III · A Newton vertex needs a declared expression

For a Laurent polynomial

```text
f(x)=sum_(alpha in A) c_alpha x^alpha,
```

the support `A` is the finite set of exponent vectors with nonzero coefficients. Its Newton
polytope is

```text
Newt(f)=conv(A).
```

Vertices and faces are therefore exact, computable relations among the monomials of one declared
receiver. They are not universal glyphs assigned to primes. A change of polynomial, coefficient
valuation, objective, or projection can change the visible face without changing the prime.

Tropical geometry supplies a lawful kink: after a declared valuation and weight vector, the
piecewise-linear potential bends where at least two monomial weights tie. That tie locus is an
exact discriminant. A line segment drawn merely between modular points has no such warrant.

Prime hyper-shapes can consequently be real in several typed senses:

1. a prime is one primitive vertex in the valuation lattice;
2. a finite receiver selects a support whose convex or cellular hull has exact faces;
3. simultaneous residues give a point on a product of cyclic fibers;
4. an ordered divisor test gives a path through those fibers; and
5. a polynomial receiver gives a Newton/tropical species at that prime.

None of these is the single absolute shape of a prime. They can be transported and compared when
the common receiver relation is retained.
