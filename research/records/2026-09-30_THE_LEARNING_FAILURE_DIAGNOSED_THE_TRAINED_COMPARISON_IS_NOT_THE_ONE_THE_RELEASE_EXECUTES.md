## 3. The learning failure, separated

Measured on the order-2 terrain, an alternation and a spectral line; floats appear only in exterior
probes, and every fitted constitution was re-read by the exact owner.
- **The trained comparison is misaligned** (verified). The trained face `A = Σ p²|W|²` over candidates
  and the executed bank's growth `a = max_m ρ(M_m)` agree on the top candidate at 2,517 of 9,216
  decisions (chance 1,843); the cosine between their descent directions is `39/512`. Past threshold
  (10,179 of 10,240 members) the face's second-order term does not order the executed growth.
  Descending the face leaves the alternation's executed decisions unmoved (552 → 547 of 1,024).
- **Descending the executed decision instead reads the rule** on those populations: order-2 141 → 869,
  alternation → 1,019, line → 1,004 of 1,024 held-out decisions, the fitted `E` re-read and certified
  by the exact owner. This is existential and local: some `E` works for these populations and
  contexts; it does not show that native deposition reaches it.
- **Movement is the proximate cause of "nothing changed"**, not the root: the certified step moves
  `E` by `[2⁻⁹, 2⁻⁸)` a deposit while a lock needs `2⁷`–`2⁹` times that along the direction taken.
  The step's length is set by the readout's curvature and its direction by the face; neither is the
  executed decision's.
- **The request is under-weighted where generation begins**: request cells enter at `ν̂(40)` against
  placed stations at up to one; the first two stations read 72 and 53 of 128.
- **The release's order is not trained**: training compares partition contexts, the release locks from
  an open section; the first lock lands on a request-reading station in only 44 of 128 sections.

## 4. Astra's challenge, adopted

- The overclaims are withdrawn: the evidence does not establish "representation refuted" or
  "transfer refuted" in general, and the fold's 15-against-16 split shows only that training-code
  improvement did not predict improvement on that probe.
- The stability proposal is not adopted as the next change. A growth bound with `ρ > 1` bounds growth
  and does not prevent it; bounded energy alone does not make learning; and per-comparison descent is
  incoherent on shared loci (two receivers can require opposite moves of one parameter). What
  survives is descent of a declared receiving composition, with its complete bound.
- Retention's future-sufficiency supplies no generalization theorem; the learning-path record's
  implication from it is withdrawn.
- Guards that are counted but not enforced are to become commit guards: the notebook's energy
  comparison and entry bound, and release after an uncertified Floquet certificate.

## 5. The decision

**The missing connection:** no learning signal of the comparison the release executes reaches the
learned loci. The release reads each open station's candidates by the largest member's executed growth
from its actual storage, requires the target's lower endpoint above one and above every rival's
upper endpoint, and locks the stations of largest positive gap from an open section. Training
descended a different quantity in different contexts.

**The next loop** (Astra's smallest decisive loop, agent-inferred): join that one executed comparison
to its exact pullback, a carried update and a full release receipt, at `E` alone, with the pumps, the
other families and the fold held fixed.
- The comparison is the release's own predicates: class, threshold and order comparisons from the open
  section, certified on the actual carried successor by disjoint exact enclosures. Its covector is the
  simple-root eigen-derivative of the executed monodromy (through the executed tick and its solve),
  valid only with certified root separation; collisions and dominant-branch ties return a typed
  unresolved result.
- Then, on the order-2 terrain, face against executed comparison crossed with partition contexts
  against the machine's own open-section trajectory, with identical material and work bound; the
  alternation and line as regressions; whole released sections shown.

**What would show this explanation wrong:**
- the native certification cannot reproduce the exterior fitted endpoint (the fit was not native
  capacity);
- the covector predicts descent and the certified carried successor increases the declared comparison
  (the derivative or certificate is wrong);
- the executed comparison succeeds under partitions and fails from the open section (context and
  order, not alignment, is the obstruction);
- training improves and untouched confirmation does not (not sufficient for transfer);
- two requests with equal retained moment need different targets (representation is obstructed).

The corpus pass and the card realization wait for this loop: the new comparison changes the cost
(for text, `257·32·33/2 = 135,696` candidate turns a request), so its projection is made from the
repaired path, not the old one.
