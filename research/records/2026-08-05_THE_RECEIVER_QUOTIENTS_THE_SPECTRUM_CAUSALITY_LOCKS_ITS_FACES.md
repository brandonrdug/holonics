## 1. Colour is a kernel

`[proved-standard]` A receiver with `n` photoreceptor classes projects the spectral power
distribution — a function of wavelength — onto `n` numbers. Human trichromacy makes this a map onto
three. The map has a large kernel; two physically distinct spectra in the same fiber are
**metamers**, indistinguishable to that receiver and distinguishable to another. This is ordinary
colorimetry.

`[interpretation]` Metamerism is the physically constructible witness for
`canon/08_CORE_MATHEMATICAL_INSTRUMENTS.md` §1: receiver-face equality is strictly weaker than
occurrence equality, and the gap can be **built**, not merely argued. Colour is therefore not a
property of light. It is a name for a fiber of a receiver's response map.

## 2. The composition law belongs to the medium

`[proved-standard]` Additive composition (emitters summing in the light before the receiver) and
subtractive composition (a material removing bands from a broadband illuminant, multiplicative in
reflectance) are different operations. Red and green light compose to yellow; red and green pigment
compose to a dark neutral.

`[historical]` The laboratory's existing law already states the ordered stages:

> coherent paths superpose **before** intensity; incoherent spectral populations add **after** the
> relevant quadratic response; receptor sensitivity forms the colour equivalence.

`[interpretation]` The quotient is therefore **layered**, and each layer forgets something
different: coherent-to-incoherent forgets phase; incoherent-to-receptor forgets spectral shape. No
composition of two informants is well posed until the stage is declared.

## 3. What evolution varies in a receiver

`[proved-standard]` Stomatopods carry twelve to sixteen photoreceptor classes and discriminate
wavelengths only about 25 nm apart, materially worse than trichromatic humans and than bees. The
published interpretation is a serial spectral readout rather than opponent comparison
([Thoen, How, Chiou and Marshall, *Science* 343:411, 2014](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1245824)).

`[interpretation]` **Channel count is not resolving power.** The architecture of comparison
determines what is discriminable. This is the same distinction as active versus total parameters in
a large model.

`[proved-standard]` Carotenoid-pigmented oil droplets in avian and reptilian cones act as long-pass
cut-off filters, narrowing each channel's effective sensitivity and improving colour discrimination
at a cost in absolute sensitivity.

`[interpretation]` **Refusing more light distinguishes more.** A narrowed aperture is a
discriminating aperture.

`[proved-standard]` In *Habronattus* jumping spiders a localized long-pass filter over part of an
existing green-sensitive receptor population yields a third effective spectral channel without a
third receptor class
([Zurek et al., *Current Biology* 25:R403, 2015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2015.03.033)); the
principal retinas additionally move across the visual field.

`[interpretation]` **A filter can found a channel.** A new receiver distinction may come from
re-charting a standing organ rather than from founding a new one — a RIDE producing what appeared to
require a FOUND.

`[proved-standard]` Dichromatic observers detect some colour-camouflaged targets that trichromats
miss ([Morgan, Adam and Mollon, *Proc. R. Soc. B* 248:291, 1992](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.1992.0074)).

`[interpretation]` **Camouflage is an engineered collision in a particular receiver's kernel**, and
counter-illumination — matching emitted downward light to downwelling daylight so a silhouette
vanishes to an upward-looking predator — is its active form. Evolution performs adversarial
metamerism. **Any declared receiver family has an exploitable kernel, and something will occupy it.**

## 4. Three couplings of organism to light

`[proved-standard]` Photosynthetic antenna complexes funnel excitation from a broad pigment array to

`[interpretation]` The antenna is a condensation of a far population into a compact realizer for a
declared consequence. And **the receiver family is a fingerprint of the channel's transfer

`[proved-standard]` Bioluminescence emits through a luciferin/luciferase reaction. Its ecological

`[interpretation]` The same emission mechanism serves attraction and deterrence; which one it is is

`[proved-standard]` Scorpion cuticle fluoresces under ultraviolet illumination through β-carboline

`[open]` The adaptive function is unsettled. Whole-body photon collection for ambient light sensing,
signalling, and a byproduct of sclerotization all remain live. Visible fluorescence establishes

ultraviolet response is incidental — is among the live readings.

`[interpretation]` Structurally, fluorescence is a **frequency-shifting relay**: a receiver that is
also an emitter, with a declared transfer between charts. It changes the chart, not the value. That
is a transducer in the project's exact sense.

## 5. Holomorphy and spectroscopy are one subject, and causality is the bridge

`[proved-standard]` A holomorphic function is conformal where its derivative is nonzero, analytic,
and by the identity theorem determined on a connected open set by its values on any subset with a
limit point. Cauchy's integral formula determines interior values from a contour.

`[proved-standard]` Causality — a response cannot precede its stimulus — implies that the complex
susceptibility `χ(ω)` is holomorphic in the upper half plane, which implies the **Kramers–Kronig
relations**: its real and imaginary parts are Hilbert transforms of each other,

```text
Re chi(w) = (1/pi) P integral  Im chi(w') / (w' - w)  dw' .
```

Absorption over all frequencies determines dispersion. `[proved-standard]` The resonances are poles;
a Lorentzian line shape is a simple pole viewed from the real axis, and pressure and Doppler
broadening displace and smear it.

`[interpretation]` Therefore **spectroscopy is the study of a causal transport's singularity
structure read from its boundary**, and it is the strongest available form of *let the boundary
carry the interior*. The engine already owns this mathematics under other names: regular-singular
continuation, residues and monodromy, Gauss--Manin transport, and the hypergeometric navigation of
`canon/08` §6, whose characteristic exponents at a singular point are that point's lines, whose
monodromy is the holonomy, and whose connection problem is transport between receiver charts.

## 6. The symbol ladder and where it terminates

`[historical]` Brandon's argument: in `let a, b, c = alpha(t), beta(t), gamma(t)` the glyphs carry

`[interpretation]` The number of distinguishable variables on a page is **receiver-relative**: a

`[interpretation]` The ladder terminates in a named object. Coloured symbols are a fiber; matrices

`[interpretation]` **Shape is the spatial spectrum; colour is the spectral spectrum.** Both are

concurs: an aperture's diffraction pattern is its spatial Fourier transform, and vision carries
spatial-frequency channels alongside wavelength channels.
