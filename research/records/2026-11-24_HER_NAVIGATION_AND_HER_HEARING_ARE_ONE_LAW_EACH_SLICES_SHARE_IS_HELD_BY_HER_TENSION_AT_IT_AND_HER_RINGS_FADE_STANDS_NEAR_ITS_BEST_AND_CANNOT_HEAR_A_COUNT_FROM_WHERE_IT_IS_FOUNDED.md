## 1. Brandon's correction and his lens

At 21:54 UTC he asked what "a percent of a pass" meant and where her autonomy meets the pipeline that first shows her the material.
The plain answer: the store is one passage of about 15 GB, the trainer feeds it a section at a time (about 13,500 new places,
interleaved across the slices by their shares), a lap is 3,000 sections (about 40 MB), and in four laps since the collection's join
she had read about 1% of it. The lead then said that her navigation does not reach what she is shown. At 21:57 he corrected that ("this
is something you've already built"), and it was: the terrain world's share of a section has followed her tension at it against the
passage's since November 21 (atlas `receipt.terrain-held-by-its-tension`, the `next_share` of every lap's `STORE_TERRAIN` line), the
October tower looked again at moments it located itself (the records of October 23 and 24), and her paces are located by deposition.
What did not reach the passage was the slices: they were drawn by their octets' quotas alone. The lead's error was a mechanism called
absent before the atlas was searched, which the guide's first practice rule bars.

At 22:04 he joined the two halves: being carried through the material without choosing what to look at while ringing with it, and
choosing what to look at while ringing too briefly to hear it, are one problem. On the objects: her tension at a slice is the rate at
which news reaches her there, and a ring's fade is how long that news keeps sounding once it arrives. What dwelling gains is their
product, news that arrives and stays heard long enough to deposit.

## 2. The slices held by their tensions (built)

`StorePass::slice_tension` (`crates/engine/src/learner.rs`): each slice's code at its open places in the last section that read it, in
millionths of a bit a place, carried as a `tension` line after the pass line. Where it is set, the cut (`Cut::next`,
`crates/engine/src/passage.rs`) takes the slice with least `taken_s / (quota_s · T_s)`. A slice with no reading yet weighs as the
mean of those with one, and where none has a reading the quotas alone apply. Every piece is still read once a pass and in order; only
the interleave moves. The trainer (`train-store`) carries each section's readings forward and prints them (`STORE_TENSION`).
`set-tension` founds the line on a state with no reading. A join reads its new slices afresh. Without the line, the cut and every byte
of the state are as before. A test (`the_cut_held_by_its_slices_tensions_…`) holds the choice at fixed counts and the line's round
trip. This is the terrain's law of November 21, read between the passage's slices; its arm, G-S1
([`GUESS_S1.txt`](receipts/2026-11-24_hearing/GUESS_S1.txt)), runs W113s beside its control W113 from W112's end. Gate 3 of the build (`e5s`) against the line: the parity of three sections, the
reproduction of 24 and the held read each EQUAL ([`gate3_e5s.txt`](receipts/2026-11-24_hearing/gate3_e5s.txt)).

## 3. Her face on a period, and her rings' fade, read before a fade is a law

On the period rows, every change that lets her rings hear further makes the spaces after long words cheaper

[`tension2_period.txt`](receipts/2026-11-24_hearing/tension2_period.txt),

an octave slower, 0.29 to 0.41 at `fade:16:−1000` and 0.15 to 0.21 under `skin:places`, while the letters there grow dearer by 0.04
to 0.12. The lead read that at 22:15 UTC as a trade of timing against letters and told Brandon that a fade read from her state was
asked for. The formal thread cut the same codes finer that hour (the calendar record, the coordinator's relay of 22:24), and the
reading does not stand:
- **The cheaper breaks are not timing.** Each kept space beside its loose twin's after the same `L` letters falls alike (at
  `fade:16:−100`, kept −0.026 to −0.049 and twins −0.029 to −0.057 at `P1` = 9 to 16), and what a shown period gives her moves by 0.00 to
  0.03 at every ±100 setting. What moves is her density of a break in rows of long words, with a period or without.
- **On the held stretches no class gains from hearing further.** At `fade:16:−1000` spaces cost +0.056, line feeds +0.104 and letters
  +0.062; at ±100 every class is dearer slower and cheaper faster. There is no trade on her passages.
- **The fade stands near its best.** A quadratic through the ±100 reads puts the rings of 16 and more stationary a seventh of an octave
  faster, worth 0.0011 nats a place; all rings at +0.04 octave, worth 0.0003.
- **At the founding the fade cannot hear a count.** Its covector at a pair `s` radians apart is `−s e^(−γ s)`; at `γ = 1` a pair one
  turn apart reaches it at `2π e^(−2π) = 0.012`, a thirtieth of a pair a radian apart.

So a fade a ring founded at the law's bits, or a gate founded at zero, has about nothing to descend, and the gated fade is not built.
The 2×2's fade cell is a ring's fade as its own state founded displaced on part of a tower, near `γ = 0.11` where a ring keeps half of
itself over a turn, with a guess on the laps that win back what the displacement costs at founding. The build's covector comes from
the skin's homogeneity: the skin depends on her clock only through `γ |σ_i − σ_j|`, so `∂L/∂ℓ = ln 2 · Σ_pairs s ∂L/∂s`, with the
pairs' `∂L/∂s` already formed slot by slot in the pace's return.

## 4. The failures this could repeat

- **A mechanism called absent before the search** (the first practice rule; November 12). The terrain's tension share was in the atlas
  and in every lap's log; the lead said her navigation did not reach the pipeline. Repaired in the same hour, and written into the
  project's memory.
- **A guess that follows the hope, not the slope.** G-H9's arm was a fade a ring founded at the skin, and the slope where the line
  stands is flat. The arm is founded displaced before it is built.
- **A reading of one column.** The 22:15 line to Brandon, a trade of timing against letters, rested on the kept rows alone; their twins
  and the held text read otherwise. It was corrected to him that hour.
- **A ruler's null read short.** These are first-order reads on one trained state. A lap that trains with a law can move where a
  perturbation of a fixed state cannot (rule 10), so the 2×2 trains each cell, and these reads only set its law's form.
