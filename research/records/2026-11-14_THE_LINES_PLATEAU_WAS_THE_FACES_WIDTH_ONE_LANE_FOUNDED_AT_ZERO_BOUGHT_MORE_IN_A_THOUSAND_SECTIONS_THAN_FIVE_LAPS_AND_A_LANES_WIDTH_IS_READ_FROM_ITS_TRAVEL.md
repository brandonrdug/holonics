## 3. The lane (G188)

A copy of a lane's width is barred as a law (the guides: a width belongs to its part and is a partition sized from a need). It was built as an
instrument, to test the premise of November 12 (§4: the level is set by the constitution's size) at the face before any lane is sized.

- **The founding** (PR #801). `Field::laned` now grows a tower's fine level: the level's field is kept and the glue's reads of the lane (`A`'s
  columns of each harmonic) and writes into it (`B`'s rows, `b_B`) stand at zero; it carries the opening's lift, the junction's row and the
  mark's strengths, which it had left behind. Host: every face and every standing covector entry kept on a field with the opening's block, its
  tower, a marked tower and a field with the junction's return. Card: the laned lap-7 mean's held read is the line's own byte for byte. The
  build reproduces lap 8's first 24 sections. The constitution grew from 221,881 entries to 305,145.
- **The read.** Trained for the card time of the line's lap 8 from the same state (640 ms a section against 587, 1.09 times; 2,732 sections
  to 3,000), at equal card-time windows the laned line stands 0.0086 under lap 8 at one-place masks and 0.0129 under at whole masks, every slice
  under at both; as the guess was fixed, against lap 8's later window, 0.0071 and 0.0118 (wikidata 0.0055 over at whole masks there). One lane
  in about a thousand sections bought more at whole masks than the last five laps together.

So the plateau was the face's width, and width costs the card little (the pairs of sites, not the width, hold the card's time and memory).

## 4. What follows: a lane's width read from its travel (design, agent-inferred)

The width a reading names (atlas `tower.face-lane-composition`) is now asked of the deposit itself. A lane is a register of the face read
as a ratio to its own power (`lane_chart`). Its readers' need shows in what the deposits do to it: over two lap means the travel of the
blocks that read the lane (its columns of the key, value and probe charts and of the pair drives, its rows of the letter encodings) spans some
count of directions. The census of November 12 (G114) read that count on whole blocks and found every block full; its ruler, drawn on spans
of known rank, tells a confined column space from a full one and nothing else. That is exactly the question for a founded lane: if its
travel fills its 32 directions at the ruler's full-rank level, the need is at least that and a further lane is founded beside it; if the
travel is confined to `k` directions, the lane's width is `k`, and the composition holds a lane of `k`. The next build is the composition of
lanes of unequal width (the lane chart over a composition, a lane founded at a declared width, each block reading the lanes it declares),
entering at `(32, 32, 32)` by reproducing the laned line, then the census of the third lane's travel read as its guess fixes. The laned line
continues meanwhile as the instrument the census reads (its laps are the travel); it is named an instrument and enters no claim of a law.
The broader passage (tier B, 8 GB) waits on the composition, as the November 12 record ordered.

Which recorded failures this could repeat, and why it does not: a width as a dial (the lane's width is read from the travel, never doubled
until a code improves; the copied lane is an instrument and says so); a refusal answered with a larger limit (the lane is a part founded at
zero with every face kept, not a raised bound); bits read as progress (the decision is the travel's rank, with the codes by slice beside it);
a design thought in arrays (a lane is a register with its own power, and its width is a count of directions the deposit fills).

**4.1 Amended the same day: the census does not size a lane, and the capacity price does** (receipt `census_lanes.txt`). Drawn on spans of
known kind at the lanes' shapes, the census's `k` reads 22.9 to 23.8 for two full-rank spans of 32 columns and 18 to 21 for spans confined to
8, 16 or 24 directions with modest noise beside them; a count above a row-shuffled null reads 21.6, 12.2 and 6.5 for one full-rank shared
span at 245, 128 and 64 rows. Neither tells a confined lane from a full one on these shapes, so neither sizes a lane (the fourth pass's two
lanes read `k` = 17 to 25, inside both ranges). The atlas already holds the reading that does: the capacity price (`code.capacity-price`),
`π(V) = −dC/d ln V`, the code's fall a nat of cells. G188 reads it at the face: the constitution grew by a factor 1.375 (ln r = 0.319) and
the code fell 0.0129 nats a place at whole masks and 0.0086 at one-place masks in the lane's first thousand sections, `π` ≈ 0.040 and 0.027
(a lower bound while the lane is still opening). So the face grows by partition while a founded lane's price, read against its cost in card
time (1.09 times a section here), stands above the price of the same card time spent on more sections; a lane's width is then the grain of
the purchase, and the composition of unequal widths is needed only where two parts' prices call for different grains (agent-inferred). The
next readings: the third lane's price as it opens (the laned line's laps against the line's last level), and the price of a part other than
the face (the contacts' growth of November 10 bought 0.013 in four laps) on the same scale.
