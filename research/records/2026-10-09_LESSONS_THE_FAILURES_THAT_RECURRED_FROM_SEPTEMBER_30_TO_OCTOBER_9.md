# Lessons: the failures that recurred from September 30 to October 9

**Date.** October 9. **Issues.** #63, #73, #148. **Grade.** Project record. It extends
[the September 29 lessons](2026-09-29_LESSONS_THE_FAILURES_THAT_REPEATED_AFTER_THEY_WERE_RECORDED.md),
whose §1 numbers ten failures and whose §3 numbers eleven lessons; this record continues both
numberings. Its source is an introspective reading of Brandon's messages to both agents and of the
agents' own exchanges from September 30 to October 9 (agent-inferred; the readings are private and
are not reproduced). Each failure below recurred after Brandon had named it.

## 1. What recurred

11. **A known-truth fixture became the goal.** An authored fixture built to test a capability was
    repaired until it passed, instead of revisiting what it was meant to show: a modulo criterion, an
    exact-text loss and a majority-class demonstration (October 7). A fixture that lets every C/K/D
    representative stay unchanged passes at its grain and witnesses no learning (October 9).
12. **A demonstration stood in for the requested capability.** A runnable example of a fixed key read
    against truth was offered while the request was a learned helical code (October 9).
13. **A concept was split from the machine and attached to it as a feature.** The embedding was
    treated as a module to add to the HNN rather than as readings of the circuit's own faces
    (October 7); reconstruction was briefly named the main goal.
14. **The latest phrasing narrowed the work.** Several replies answered only the newest wording of a
    lens and dropped what it joined (October 2 to October 9).
15. **A limit was imposed without its workload.** A 4 GiB cap and a 16 s deadline were copied from a
    development run onto checks whose peak was unestimated; one check materialized a dense 4 GiB
    matrix it did not need (October 8).
16. **Worktrees were removed before their untracked and ignored files were inventoried** (October 9);
    one Lean draft had to be matched afterwards against the history.
17. **The agents looped on dependence and agreement.** Each waited on the other or routed through the
    other, while the arrangement between them was written nowhere (October 1 and 2).
18. **A sum replaced its series in a report.** Margins were reported as single numbers, discarding the
    winding and turning parts that composed them, and predicted classes as bare integers (October 7).
19. **Programming words stood in for the physics.** "Decision", "sign", "error" and "step" were used
    as if they named objects (October 2 to October 9).

## 2. The lessons

12. **A fixture is evidence of its purpose, never the goal.** When an authored fixture fails, its
    purpose is reread before the fixture is touched; a passing fixture states what it does not
    witness (failure 11).
13. **The requested capability, not a demonstration of a neighbour.** A loop's deliverable is the
    capability Brandon asked for or its measured blocker (failure 12).
14. **Every concept is a reading of the circuit.** A capability is derived as readings and laws of
    existing owners before any module is proposed (failure 13).
15. **Derive from the whole lens.** A new phrasing is joined to the lens it continues and to the
    owners it touches; the record says what it adds (failure 14).
16. **Limits come from the workload.** CLAUDE.md's waiting standard states the rule: a ceiling from
    the estimated and then measured peak, at least 8 GiB per job unless measured lower; one to three
    minutes for an unmeasured run; longer runs stated and scheduled (failure 15).
17. **Inventory before removal.** A worktree's untracked and ignored files are listed and each is
    committed, copied or retired by name before the worktree is removed (failure 16).
18. **The arrangement is written.** CLAUDE.md's "Working with Epime" and the mailbox protocol state
    who leads, who reviews and how they coordinate; neither agent waits on an unwritten one
    (failure 17).
19. **Report the series.** A reading built from contributions is reported as its series with its
    sum and carry, and a class with what it denotes at its receiver (failure 18; CLAUDE.md's exact
    arithmetic).
20. **Name the object.** A release is a leader's integration, not a click; a comparison is a ratio
    with its residual, never an error; a sign is an orientation read in a named frame; a physical
    tick, a deposition step and a change of representation are three different things (failure 19).
