## Direct ruling

[project-postulate] Brandon's correction is that the siphonophore's primary significance is the
word **holon** itself. A siphonophore makes physically observable the simultaneous whole/part
status that is present in every bounded living system, although it is less visually obvious in an
ordinary animal. A holon is a whole unit and a part at once; its sub-parts are themselves holons,
and this recursion continues through organs, tissues, cells, molecular complexes, repositories,
library modules and mathematical structures.

[project-postulate] The colonial-versus-organism question is therefore not a paradox requiring one
side to win. The colony and its zooids can both be valid holons at their respective boundaries,
receivers, material laws and continuation horizons. The same statement applies to an animal and
its organs, an organ and its tissues, a tissue and its cells, a repository and its modules, and a
mathematical object and its subobjects. Whole and part are relational roles in one nested
construction, not mutually exclusive intrinsic identities.

## The siphonophore makes the general fact visible

[established-bounded; source-inspected] Siphonophores are colonial hydrozoans composed of
functionally specialized zooids. Their colonies can combine feeding, propulsion, defense,
reproduction, buoyancy and orientation in one continuing arrangement. The ordinary overview
therefore describes an organism that is simultaneously a colony of organism-like units and one
coordinated animal-like system: [Siphonophore overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siphonophore).

[established-bounded; source-inspected] The *Physalia utriculus* multi-omic preprint makes the
same relation visible at molecular scale. Zooid identity is associated with structure-specific
regulatory and chromatin-accessibility programs built from conserved developmental machinery,
while venom chemistry includes lineage-specific SOUL expansion and host-surface dependence. The
same colony is consequently one object at the ecological receiver and a population of distinct
material/functional faces at finer receivers. This preprint has not been peer reviewed and is
retained at that scope:
[González-Rajal et al., *Integrative genomics of the siphonophore Physalia utriculus*](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.01.742136v1.full).

[interpretation] The visually striking feature is not that siphonophores alone possess
whole/part recursion. Every animal has nested current-bearing boundaries. Siphonophores expose
the issue because the parts retain specialized morphology and coordinated conduct that resemble
organs or independent organisms, while the colony remains a physically coupled reproductive,
nutritional and motile ecology. They force biological language to acknowledge that “organism” is
already a receiver-relative cut through a continuing object.

## Holon, occurrence and receiver

[definition] The repository's shared carrier can express the ruling as a recursive situated
object. For a declared receiver family, let

```text
H = (I, X, G, R, P, L, W)
```

where `I` indexes grains, stations, environments and apertures; `X` carries situated faces; `G`
contains generators and passages; `R` contains receivers; `P` contains presentations/codecs; `L`
contains lineage; and `W` carries cost. A sub-holon `h ⊂ H` is not a detached copy or a label. It
has its own situated presentation and continuation while participating in `H` through an admitted
incidence, contact, current, constitutive law or boundary exchange.

[definition] Identity belongs to an occurrence, persistence to lineage, and sameness to a declared
receiver. Whole/part status is another typed relation over that same object. A nectophore can be a
whole actuator under its local fluid receiver, a part of the nectosome under its propulsion
receiver, and a descendant in a colony lineage under its developmental receiver. None of these
readings silently identifies the others.

This is the same reason that:

- a repository module can be a complete library owner and a part of the engine;
- a theorem can be a complete formal object and a lemma inside a larger development;
- a cell can maintain local conduct and remain part of a tissue;
- a molecule can be a complete chemical object without being an organ of the organism;
- a microbial colony can be a living ecology inside a gut without each enzyme instance becoming
  an organ.

The `receiver_atlas`, `physical_occurrence`, `standing`, `receiver_release` and shared-carrier
owners already carry the needed separation. No universal organism label should be added above
them.

## Thermodynamic criterion for an organ

[definition] “Organ” is narrower than “part” and narrower than “holon.” A candidate `u` is an
organ of a higher holon `H` only relative to a declared environment `eta`, receiver family and
continuation horizon `T`. The candidate must satisfy two classes of conditions:

1. **Internal composition.** `u` is spatially and causally inside the incidence/material complex
   of `H`; it is not merely co-present in the same medium or adjacent to the boundary.
2. **Reciprocal cycle dependence.** Removing `u` changes the continuation of `H` and the
   continuation of `u` itself. In the declared regime, the intact higher system sustains its
   characteristic heat/energy/current/entropy-diffusion cycles, but the excised higher system
   cannot maintain the same cycle family; conversely, `u` cannot maintain the same classified
   conduct after separation from `H`.

This gives a receiver-relative organ predicate rather than an anatomical slogan. A useful
conditional form is

```text
Organ_T(u, H; eta, R, ε)
  iff Internal(u, H)
   ∧ Defect_H(excision(u), T; eta, R) > ε_H
   ∧ Defect_u(separation(u, H), T; eta, R_u) > ε_u.
```

`Defect` is not an authored score. It is the declared difference between the intact and
excised/remounted continuation families, measured through the relevant flux, boundary, heat,
entropy, material or future receiver. The tolerance, horizon, environment and units belong to
the claim.

[conditional] The physical balance behind this predicate is already in the biological carrier:

```text
∂ₜ a + div j_a = r_eta(a, q)
∂ₜ s + div j_s = σ_eta + r^s_eta,
```

with `a` a stored quantity/current chart, `s` an entropy chart, `j_a` and `j_s` oriented fluxes,
and `σ_eta` the declared entropy-production term. Across an interface `Σ`, the relevant exchange
is a boundary flux such as

```text
J_a(Σ) = ∫_Σ j_a · n dA,
J_s(Σ) = ∫_Σ j_s · n dA.
```

The criterion therefore concerns sustained nonequilibrium or dynamic-steady cycles, not merely a
static zero-flux equilibrium. A temporarily surviving separated fragment may still be a holon,
but it does not thereby retain the same organ relation; the receiver horizon distinguishes
delayed failure from continued independent conduct.

[interpretation] If the higher object remains within the same declared continuation family after
removing one molecule or enzyme instance, that instance is not an organ. It may still be a holon
and may participate in a larger ecology. A gut microbial community may be a nested living holon
and a material contributor to gut conduct, while one lactase molecule is not an organ of the gut.
Whether the community itself is an organ-like constituent is a separate reciprocal-cycle claim:
the host and the community must each fail to retain their relevant classified cycles after the
declared separation. Spatial interiority alone does not decide it.

This distinction prevents two opposite errors:

- treating every internal object as an organ merely because it is nearby or functionally useful;
- treating every internally coupled object as an independent organism merely because it has a
  local current or can persist temporarily after separation.

## Why siphonophore dynamics affirm the criterion

[established-bounded; source-inspected] The swimming literature supplies a direct physical case.
Nectophores on the same side of the nectosomal axis pulse with metachronal timing, while
opposite-side groups share frequency without complete phase locking; the colony's trajectory is a
receiver of the coupled pulse family, not a sum of independent jets. See [Strock et al.,
*Nectophore coordination and kinematics by physonect siphonophores*](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37655651/).

[established-bounded; source-inspected] The multi-jet fluid experiments show that nearby pulsed
wakes alter one another's velocity and thrust through local vortex induction and streamline
curvature, with the measured interaction depending strongly on spacing and pulse timing. See
[Athanassiadis and Hart](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1604.05382).

[interpretation] These are not independent propulsors placed beside one another. Their coupling
is a constitutive relation through a shared medium. Removing or mistiming one member changes the
continuing flux and wake family of the larger swimming holon. The exact degree to which a
nectophore qualifies as an organ, however, remains a declared physical question: it depends on
the species, developmental stage, receiver, time horizon and whether the nectophore can maintain
its own classified conduct after separation.

The same logic applies to the gonodendron. Its detachment, developmental state and internal germline
and somatic populations are not resolved by saying “colony” or “organism.” They are a passage to a
new environment and receiver family. A detached structure may retain lineage and internal
standing while losing the higher colony's constitutive cycle; the resulting difference is the
relation to be measured.

## The broader biological and computational consequence

[project-postulate] Holonics should not inherit the biological habit of choosing one privileged
boundary and calling it the organism. The correct question is:

```text
At which boundary, under which constitutive law, receiver and horizon,
does this continuing family behave as a whole, and through which interface
does it participate as a part of the next holon?
```

The siphonophore is a validation case for this general method, not a special exception. The same
method applies to:

- a host and its gut ecology;
- a tissue and its cells;
- an organ and its vessels, nerves and microbiota;
- a molecule and its complexes;
- a repository and its modules;
- a mathematical carrier and its charts, fibres and receivers;
- an HNN field and its local currents, modes and boundaries.

[interpretation] Molecular phylogenetics contributes an important warning here. Sequence alignment
and tree reconstruction are receivers over molecular differences; species trees, gene trees,
haplotypes, regulatory states and organismal conduct are not one identity object. Gene-tree
discordance and lineage-specific duplication are exactly the sort of source/lineage distinction
that Holonics preserves. The general overview of the method is [Molecular phylogenetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molecular_phylogenetics).

The *Physalia* preprint's distinction between conserved developmental machinery, zooid-specific
regulatory landscapes and lineage-specific SOUL expansion therefore reads naturally as one
continuing holon with multiple material and evolutionary charts. It does not require a separate
engine for each zooid, and it does not make a sequence or chromatin face the intrinsic identity of
the colony.
