## Circulating interiors and changing mode encodings

[proved-derived; formal-checked] The existing `Physics/ReflectedBoundaryMemory.lean` now proves
`boundary_reduction_iff`. For linear boundary/interior action `x_next=A x+B z`, the equation

```text
∀x,z, E_next(A x+B z)=U(E x)
```

holds exactly when `E_next A=U E` and `E_next B=0`. `boundary_reduction_with_forcing` adds the
transformed boundary drive. `moving_boundary_rate` includes E_dot in the continuous chart.
The proof uses linear-map extensionality and the two independent operands; no new
noncomputable inverse, physical material assumption or experimental success is asserted.

[conditional] Applied at every interaction, these equations give identical encoded boundary
evolution by induction. The unconstrained-interior criterion is not necessary for every
restricted physical family; a constrained interior uses its actual joint family. When the
criterion fails, the existing dynamic reflection equation gives the returning mode/memory.
This makes the eddy/no-eddy question a definite boundary-response comparison. Instantaneous
divergence alone does not decide it.

[established-bounded; source-inspected] The existing induction formalization already proves
that a changed face flux cannot be an exact nodal drop and separately defines conductive
eddy circulation. The material comparison additionally needs conductivity, geometry,
frequency and magnetization law. TDK describes the resistivity mechanism behind suppressed
eddy loss; Goto supplies the nonlinear pumped resonator and phase readout. These ground
the physical explanation instead of treating ferrimagnetic/ferromagnetic labels as an
equivalence or chaos theorem.
