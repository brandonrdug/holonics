## 5. Training grade and open relation

The experiment establishes training as ordinary lawful experience changing Standing and later
conduct. It establishes exact local continuation recurrence and chronology sensitivity. It does
not establish a general language learner.

Both `r` and `w` were actual training continuations of each held-out terminal two-octet edge, so
both touched after forward passes 1 and 3. No authored target became unique. Earlier `one/two`
history changed complete receiver geometry, but the same candidate differences also existed while
processing an untrained prefix. The observation therefore does not show that training bound the
earlier number word to the later verb.

The smallest relation left open by this particular foil is a wider lawful composition in which an
earlier regional factor remains consequential at the continuation boundary. A wider tokenizer,
longer context, attention mechanism, pretrained language model, or learned transducer may be a
lawful inherited constituent of a more complete machine, but none retroactively demonstrates that
composition in this fixed byte-chart observation. The measured claim remains only local recurrence
under its declared source chart.
