# Receipts: host rulers of November 10 (night) and November 11, a grip of letters and a founded unit

Rulers on the host, never machinery. None used the card and none is a learner run. The `cap_*.py`
scripts read the python-codes passages under `${HOLONICS_ROOT}.local/`, which stay private; only
aggregate figures are written here. They continue the receipts of the chain record of the same
day, whose `yard.py` (the loader and the Witten-Bell tables) they `exec` and whose `train.npz`
they read. Floats, Adam and tables indexed by letters are the rulers' own.

The first ruling (the record's §1 to §7):

- `guess_capacity_28_30.txt`: guesses G28 to G30, each with the UTC time it was fixed.
- `cap_n.py G R SECONDS`: the pair-only reader (`R = 0`) and free factors, one table an offset.
- `cap_o.py G R SECONDS`: the word of shared strides, one stride and one deposit a letter composed
  in passage order; its gradient was checked against finite differences before the first fit.
- `grip_readers.log.txt`: the final line of every fit of the two.

The lens of 23:49 UTC (the record's §8):

- `guess_capacity_32_36.txt`: guesses G32, G34, G35, G36, G30′ and G33, each with the UTC time it
  was fixed. G30′ and G33 are design's reads and were unread when this was committed.
- `cap_p.py strides|units D`: the moduli of the strides `cap_o.py` fitted, by kind of letter; and
  founded units by count, a context tree pruned at `c` meetings. Logs `cap_p_strides.log.txt`,
  `cap_p_units.log.txt`.
- `cap_q.py L N|U K|read`: the letter chain, and the unit chain whose contexts end at unit
  boundaries. The cut into units (a run of word letters, a run of one repeated space-class letter,
  or one other letter) is the ruler's own. Logs `cap_q_letters.log.txt`, `cap_q_units.log.txt`,
  `cap_q_read.log.txt`.
- `cap_q_read_leaked_first_run.log.txt`: the first run of `cap_q.py read`. Its contexts were cut
  with the letter being read, which told them that letter's kind, and it read 0.98 nats at four
  levels. It is kept as the record of the error; the script here is the corrected one, whose
  contexts are functions of the letters before the place alone.
- `cap_r.py STATE…`: design's locked visits (`locked-visit-v1`: a record a place with the stored
  face), read from the directory named by the environment variable `HOLONICS_VISIT`: the locked
  code, its correlation with itself `k` places on (G31), its split at unit starts and by kind of
  letter, and its correlation with the count law of two letters each side (G35). It reads
  `cap_m_auto.npy`, which `cap_m.py auto` of the navigation record's receipts writes. Log
  `cap_r.log.txt`.
- `cap_s.py STATE…`: the field's stored faces multiplied into a count law's whole distribution and
  renormalized (G36). Log `cap_s.log.txt`.
