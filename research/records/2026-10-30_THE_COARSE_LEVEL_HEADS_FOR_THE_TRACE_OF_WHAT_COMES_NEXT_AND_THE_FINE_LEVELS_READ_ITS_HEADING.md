## 4. The law

[definition; agent-inferred, October 30] **A clocked level carries a heading.** On a tower with
packets whose news clock begins at level `F`, each level `l ≥ F` reads its modes through its own
heading material `C^→_l` (complex, `w × n`, beside its read):

```text
ĥ_l(t) = r̂(Re(C^→_l h_l(t−1)))          the heading, read from the modes before arrival t
```

Between its ticks a coarse level's modes do not move, so its heading is held, and the heading at a
tick is the one it held since its last tick.

[agent-inferred, at the build] `C^→_l` opens as a copy of the level's read `C_l`, as
participation's read opens: a heading at zero is no ratio, so its closure would carry no covector
into `C^→_l`, and the copy opens it on what the level's contacts already read.

**The trace of a span.** Each clocked level carries the open trace of the arrivals since its last
tick, the sum of their encoded faces:

```text
o_l ← o_l + E(u_t)                        at every arrival
τ_l(j) = r̂(o_l),  then o_l ← 0            at tick j of level l
```

The span is the packet's span, closed by the same tick. Order inside it is what the coarse scope
does not tell apart; the trace is the span's colourless face (the Galois-gap record's §12, the trace
over the span's translations). `o_l` is the present span, released at the tick, not a store.

**The heading meets the trace through a pair contact over the passage's menu.** The menu of a tick
is the traces of the same stream at the same level in the learner's section, `M_l`. At C′ (the
clock's first section `B = 8` bits, about two bits a class) level 4 ticks about every four classes
and level 5 every eight, so a section of 128 holds about 30 and 15 traces a stream [estimated from
the clock's sections]: the heading has about five and four bits a tick to locate. With the pairing read on the bits' grain,

```text
z_ji = 2^(−s) ⟨ĥ_l(t_j), τ_l(i)⟩ / 2^(2·16),   s = ⌈½ ⌈log₂ w⌉⌉     (bits; i ∈ M_l)
e_ji = 2^(z_ji − max_i z_ji),   ℓ_j = −log₂ (e_jj / Σ_i e_ji)       the closure's code at tick j
```

The loop closes when the heading the level held picks out the trace of the span its own tick
closed, against every other span of the passage: the Bombe's menu test, read as a Gibbs face. `s`
is the scale at which a heading and a trace with independent directions pair with unit spread
(each `r̂` has `|r̂|² = w` on the face's `2^(−16)`), so the face opens neither flat nor sharp
(`s = 3` at the race's `w = 64`).

**Both sides change.** The closure's covector is the classical descent covector of its declared
comparison, `g_ji = e_ji/Σ_i e_ji − [i = j]` (in bits), reaching
- the heading, `Σ_i g_ji τ_l(i)`, and through `r̂`'s adjoint `C^→_l` and the modes `h_l(t_j − 1)`
  (whose covector the level's own reverse then carries into its moves, its push and its packets);
- each trace, `Σ_j g_ji ĥ_l(t_j)`, and through `r̂`'s adjoint every arrival's encoding row in that
  span.

The target is the arrivals' trace, encoded through the same `E` as the source (the loss law), never
a packet the fine levels formed: a packet is moved by the fine levels, which read the heading, so a
heading compared with a packet could close its loop on its own echo.

**The fine levels read the heading.** The residual stream opens on the encoding plus each clocked
level's heading through its descent material `D_l` (real, `w × w`, opening at zero):

```text
x⁰_t = E(u_t) + Σ_(l ≥ F) D_l ĥ_l(t)
```

so every level's push, gate, turn and contacts read where the coarse levels are heading, and the
tower opens on C′ face for face. During release the same read steers the fine levels toward the
trace the coarse level heads for: the release descends through the field the coarse level holds.

[agent-inferred] **The heading is located by its closure alone.** The fine levels' covector stops at
`D_l`: `G_D += δx⁰_t ĥ_l(t)ᵀ`, and the heading is a given input to the fine levels' reverse.
Carrying that covector into the heading would need one reverse across the levels in time (the fine
scan at `t` would reach the coarse modes at their last tick, whose packet read the fine level before
`t`), and the question this loop asks is whether a heading located by closure steers. Under the
news clock the section's opening is already a constant; a heading read from the opening modes
reaches only `C^→_l` and its trace.

**The certificate reads the whole code.** A section's code is the arrivals' code plus every tick's
closure code, the faces `(e_jj, Σ_i e_ji)` joining the arrivals' faces in the exact comparison of
products, so the deposit is certified on the comparison it descends.

## 9. The face reads the heading

[agent-inferred, October 30] **The cause.** §4 stopped the fine levels' covector at `D`: once `D`
moves, every step on `C^→`, on the clocked levels' material and on what feeds their modes moves
`x⁰` through `D ĥ` along a path the covector does not carry. The certified step is then not descent
on the arrivals' code, and the certificate refuses it and halves the whole step, so every family
takes about a seventh of its step. The certificate did its work; the covector was incomplete. With
`D` held at zero the covector is exact, and the refusals and the arrivals' cost go with it.

**Where the covector is exact.** The reverse runs level by level from the top, each level scanning
its cells backward. The covector of `x⁰_t` exists only after every level's scan, while the heading
it read is the coarse level's modes at `t − 1`, whose scan ran first: carrying it at `x⁰` needs one
reverse across the levels in time. The one covector the reverse holds before any level's scan is the
top's, `δx^L_t = Uᵀ δz_t`. So the heading enters there:

```text
z_t = U (x^L_t + Σ_(l ≥ F) D_l ĥ⁺_l(t)) + c,     ĥ⁺_l(t) = r̂(Re(C^→_l h_l(t)))
G_D += δx^L_t ĥ⁺_l(t)ᵀ,     δĥ⁺_l(t) = D_lᵀ δx^L_t
```

## 17. The step's parts meet through the receiving face's metric

[agent-inferred, October 30; the law derived in the unified-theory thread the same day, its toy
receipts there] **What is owned, and what is missing.** A certified step bounds each locus's own
curvature along its ray and charges the cross terms between loci at their Cauchy–Schwarz worst
(atlas `holon.joint-step`); the tower's certificate instead reads the actual change and halves
every family together when the code rises. §13 and §16 located the refusals in the cross terms
themselves: each family's step alone lowers the arrivals or nearly does, and together they rise,
through the clocked levels' inputs and not their ticks. The receiving face's own curvature is
owned: the code's Hessian in its logits is the face's Fisher form, `ln 2 (diag p − p pᵀ)` in
base-2 logits (atlas `receiver.fisher-certificate`), and the form pulled back through a family of
moves is the witness's Gauss–Newton form (`hnn.witness-plane`). The missing term is that form read
between the step's parts at the refused step, and a scale for each part chosen from it.

**The law.** At the first refused halving `h₀` of a section, with the five parts of §13 (the
descent, the heading's read, the readout with its bias, the clocked levels' material, and the
rest with the dormant returns and a founding's opening), let `s_i` be part `i`'s step released at
`h₀` and read the faces of five candidates, each the tower stepped by one part alone. At every
compared cell `t`:
- `z_t` is the opening face's base-2 logits on `2^(−16)` bits, `δz_t^(i)` part `i`'s change of
  them, and `p_t` the opening face's masses partitioned on `2^(−32)` so they sum to one exactly
  (each mass's floor, then the remaining grains to the largest remainders, ties to the lower
  class).
- `c_t^(i) = Σ_c p_tc δz_tc^(i)`, and the cell's Gram form
  `G_t^(ij) = Σ_c p_tc δz_tc^(i) δz_tc^(j) − c_t^(i) c_t^(j)`, the covariance of two parts'
  logit changes under the face: positive semidefinite by construction, a square and not an
  assembly of measurements.

Summed over the section, `g_i = Σ_t (c_t^(i) − δz_(t,target)^(i))` is part `i`'s first-order change
of the arrivals' code and `G = Σ_t G_t` the meeting form. The model of the code's change under
`Σ_i α_i s_i` is `m(α) = Σ_i α_i g_i + ½ ln 2 · αᵀ G α` (`ln 2` its navigator's face on
`2^(−30)`), and the scales are `α* = argmin over [0, 1]⁵ of m`, solved exactly over the `3⁵ = 243`
faces of the box (on each face the free scales solve their block of `ln 2 · G`, a face whose block
is singular or whose solution leaves the box is passed over, and the least `m` is taken, the first
face in order on a tie). The applied scales are `α*` released to `2^(−32)`, and `m` is read at them.

The section steps by `Σ_i α_i s_i` and accepts when the certificate admits it (the arrivals' faces
fall exactly) and the actual change is at most a quarter of `m`, both below zero. Otherwise the
region shrinks: at `h₀ + k` the parts are `s_i / 2^k`, so the model is the same form read with
`g / 2^k` and `G / 4^k`, solved again, and the faces are not read again. The section deposits
nothing once the scaled step releases to zero. A section with no refused step steps as before.

**The Schur reading.** Part `r`'s descent after the others have moved is
`g_r − G_ro G_oo⁻¹ g_o` with curvature `G_rr − G_ro G_oo⁻¹ G_or`: a part whose conditioned descent
is gone adds nothing once the others move, however much it descends alone. That is the box solve's
face for the others free.

**Choices, and their reasons.**
- Five parts and one block each: the toy's two-block form (the readout against the rest) ended
  above halving in two of five settings; one block a family is the least resolution that read the
  meeting in §13.
- The share a quarter: any share in `(0, 1)` keeps the descent (an accepted step falls, a refused one
  shrinks until the first-order term dominates), and the toy never ended above halving at any share
  from an eighth to three quarters; a quarter is the usual trust region's.
- The form read once a refused section, the region shrunk without a new read: the model is the
  second-order expansion at the opening, which only improves as the step shrinks, and a new read
  costs six forwards. A smooth meeting falls by a quarter a halving, as the form scaled by `4^(−k)`
  does.
- The scales at most one: the law never steps past the learner's own step, so no refusal is
  answered with a larger step.

**The read.** `PassRealization::logits` reads every compared cell's face logits forward from the
opening, kept in no slot: the host from its cells, the card from `tower_forward`'s read 3, which

## 22. The clocked levels' credit crosses the section's edge

[agent-inferred, October 31] **The located cause** (§21). A section's reverse opens at zero at its
end and stops at its opening, and the race carries no passage credit, so a clocked level's
recurrence learns only from its own section's ticks. The frozen-material read (c6ce14204, C′'s saved
learners, 16 streams, B = section 16 read alone and after A of 1, 2 and 4 sections compared with
nothing) put the omitted return at 5.94 times B's own covector on level 5's moves at lap 0 and 1.66
at lap 45, 1.16 on level 4's moves at lap 45, and 0.76 and 0.57 on level 5's push and gate. The
clocked levels' slowest modes hold 48 to 188 of their own ticks (laps 45 and 23), about 200 to 1,500
arrivals at one tick in four and in eight, against a section of 128.

**What is owned.** The passage credit (`LevelCredit`, atlas `hnn.tower-passage-credit`, Lean
`HNN/PassageCredit`): a stream carries the derivative of a level's modes by the level's own
recurrence families as phase-carried moments, and at a section's opening the covector reaching the
carried state meets them. It refuses a news clock (its moments over a clocked move were owed), the
card carries none, and on October 23, on the gated tower whose levels move at every arrival, it
read 1.1% and 0.9% worse after passes 1 and 2.

**The law.** On a tower whose news clock begins at level `F`, the credit is carried at the clocked
levels `l ≥ F`.
- *A clocked move.* At cell `t` the level moves `Δτ_l(t)` times with the arrival held,
  `h⁽ʲ⁾ = λ_t h⁽ʲ⁻¹⁾ + γ_t p_t`, and every moment takes the same sub-steps:
  ```text
  P ← λ_t P + γ_t x̃_t                       K ← λ_t K + i λ_t h⁽ʲ⁻¹⁾
  A ← λ_t A + v⁽ʲ⁾ c r_t ρ_t/|λ|            Q ← λ_t Q + v⁽ʲ⁾ ∂ρ_t/∂q
  W ← λ_t W + v⁽ʲ⁾ (∂ρ_t/∂q) x̃_t            v⁽ʲ⁾ = u h⁽ʲ⁻¹⁾ − (ρ_t/γ_t) p_t
  ```
  `x̃_t` is what the push and gate read at the tick (the packet's read), and a cell that ticks the
  level no time moves no moment. Over a section `P_T = Λ P_0 + Σ_s π_s c_s x̃_s`, with
  `c_s = γ_s Σ_(j<Δτ_s) λ_s^j`, `π_s` the later cells' turns `λ^Δτ` and `Λ` the section's (`W`
  likewise with `Σ_j λ_s^(Δτ_s−j) v⁽ʲ⁾ ∂ρ_s/∂q`). It is the existing law's sequence of moves, which
  `HNN/PassageCredit.hasDerivAt_state` holds for any moves, so no new statement is owed.
- *The packet a clocked level carries up.* Level `l`'s moves carry level `l+1`'s open packet from
  rest since that level's tick by the same sub-steps, so the packet has moments of its own by level
  `l`'s families (the recursion with the packet in place of `h`), rested with it at level `l+1`'s
  tick. Level `F`'s packet, carried by level `F−1`, which moves at every arrival, has none: the
  credit begins at the clock.
- *The meeting.* At the opening, the covector of each credited level's carried modes,
  `a_l = conj(λ_0)^(Δτ_l(0)) G_h(0)`, meets the level's moments in the shapes of the section's own
  terms, and the covector of the packet it carries, `ā_l = conj(λ_0)^(Δτ_l(0)) G_a(0)`, meets the
  packet's moments into the same families.
- *Held as given* (declared). The input a level hears (the packet's read, the passing current) and
  everything below the clock: what reached a clocked level's state through the packets it read in
  an earlier section returns into its own families only. So the top level's families meet their
  whole return across the edge, and a lower clocked level's meet theirs through its modes and its
  open packet. The certificate still rereads the section from its opening: the certified step is
  descent on the section under the passage's direction.

**Why the clocked levels alone** (agent-inferred). The located cause is theirs; their memory runs
two to twelve sections past the edge while the arrival levels' slowest modes hold 26 to 179
arrivals; and the credit on levels that move at every arrival read worse on October 23. The
arrival levels' omitted return is read beside, so a later loop joins them on its measurement.

**The carrier and its memory, projected at the race** (`w = 64`, `N = 128`, `F = 4`, six levels,
320 streams). A credited level's `P` and `W` are `N × w` complex moments and `A`, `K`, `Q` are `N`
each, 268,288 bytes a stream; level 4's own and its packet's and level 5's own make 804,864 bytes a
stream, 245.6 MiB over the streams. A pass opens its streams at rest, so the credit opens at zero

the opening's and the ends), about 737 MiB. The card keeps the credit resident between sections: the

**The card.** The forward writes, at each tick of a credited level, each mode's turn `λ_t^Δτ` and

by the cell recursion; a credit kernel after it sums `P` and `W` in closed form into its read's
advanced credit. The reverse writes each credited level's opening covectors from its modes' scan,
and a meeting kernel adds each stream's meeting to the covector before the outer sums are read

## 29. The fine levels move under the heading

[agent-inferred, October 31] **What the heading is now.** Since §11 the heading is the coarse
level's read that the face takes, `z = U(x^L + Σ_l D_l ĥ⁺_l) + c`. Between a level's ticks its modes
stand, so the heading a tick leaves is the one the face reads for every arrival of the span the
next tick closes (§19): it is trained by every class of that span, a forecast of the span in the
face's own terms, and since §22 and §24 its families carry their return across the section's edge.
The closure reads it against the span's trace in the encoding's terms, a space the heading is never
asked to enter, so the closure sitting above flat says the heading is not the trace, not that it
forecasts nothing. What the heading still does not do is move anything: the fine levels' pushes,
gates, turns and contacts never read it, so the coarse levels' long memory reaches the released
class only as a bias on its face and never the motion that forms the next class. The review's point
(the coarse state is read at the output and not used as motive) and Brandon's lens (an NPC whose map
steers each step, an auto-gradient with its terrain) name the same missing term.

**The law.** The coarse field enters the fine field's motion: each clocked level's heading enters
the residual stream's opening through its own descent into the stream, `D↓_l` (real, `w × w`,
opening at zero, beside the face's `D_l`):

```text
x⁰_t = E(u_t) + Σ_(l ≥ F) D↓_l ĥ_l(t),     ĥ_l(t) = r̂(Re(C^→_l h_l(t−1))) = ĥ⁺_l(t−1)
```

`ĥ_l(t)` is the heading the level held before arrival `t`: the closure's heading, the face's
heading after the cell before, and at a section's opening the heading of the carried modes. The
arrival's encoded face is the coloured part of the opening, the coarse level's direction the
colourless part that every arrival of its span shares (the Galois-gap record's §12: the coarse
scope is the trace of the fine field, and here it is returned to it); in the fluid reading it is the
coarse correction carried back into the fine state, a body force on every fine motion, where §9
placed it only in the observation. Every level's push, gate, turn and contacts then read where the
coarse levels are heading, and so does the release: each drawn class enters a fine field already
moving under the heading.

**The covector, and why §4's version of this read failed.** The forward is a loop within a section:
the coarse modes at `t` read the fine levels' motion up to `t` (the packets), and the fine levels at
`t + 1` read the coarse modes at `t`. The reverse runs level by level from the top, so the fine
levels' covector of `x⁰` exists only after the coarse levels' scan has run. §4 stopped the covector
at `D`, which is the loop's series cut at its first term: every step on `C^→`, on the clocked levels'
material and on what feeds their modes moved `x⁰` along a path the covector did not carry, and §8–§9
measured the result (a refused step 2.8 times a section). The reverse is linear in its sources given
the forward's carriers, so the whole covector is the series

```text
δ = Σ_(k ≥ 0) δ_k,    δ_0 = R(top),    δ_(k+1) = R(a_k),
a_k(l, t) = r̂'(ĥ_l(t))ᵀ D↓_lᵀ δ_k x⁰_t     (entering the modes after cell t − 1)
G_(D↓_l) = Σ_k Σ_t δ_k x⁰_t ĥ_l(t)ᵀ
```

`R(·)` is the existing reverse (every level's scan, the packets' returns, the opening's meeting with
the credit) with the named covector as its only source: `δ_0` is today's reverse, and each later
term carries the covector once more round the loop through the heading. A term entering the modes
after cell `t − 1` reaches the fine levels' inputs only at cells up to `t − 1`, so each term reaches
at least one cell further back and the series is finite within a section (its length at most the
section's cells). It is released when its term is zero at the covector's grain, the grain on which
every term is already carried; no tolerance is chosen. At the opening (`t = 0`) the term enters the
carried modes, whose covector meets the every-level credit (§24), so the read's return also crosses
the section's edge.

  section's one certificate, like every family.
- *A refusal answered with a larger limit*: the series' length is the mathematics' (zero at the
  grain), read and reported; the race's deadline is fixed from a 64-section read before launch.

- *Text as the exception*: `x⁰` is the encoded arrival of whatever medium.

## 30. The heading drives the fine modes; it does not pump them

[agent-inferred, October 31] **The cause, read from §29.** `x⁰` is what every arrival level's push,
gate and turn read, and what the clocked levels' contacts hear as the passing current. Added there,
the coarse field did two different things to the fine field: it drove its modes (through each
level's push, `γ P x`), and it modulated their constitution, each cell's decay and turn through the
gate (`|λ|^(c r)`, `r` the gate's ratio) and the turn, which §29's law never meant. In the objects
these are different elements: a source drives a mode at its port, with power `⟨e, f⟩`; a
modulation of the constitution is a pump, whose work is its own account (the parametron's pump is a
periodic modulation of the constitution, certified by its monodromy). A body force in a fluid enters
the momentum balance as a source; it does not change the viscosity. §29's lift pumped every fine
mode's dissipation from a field that the fine field itself forms, a closed loop through the gate's
exponential, and its own step overshot (the parts, above).

**The law.** The heading drives: it enters each arrival level's push and nothing else.

```text
h_l ← λ_l(x_l) h_l + γ_l P_l (x_l + D↓ ĥ(t))     for l < F (the push reads the force beside the stream)
q_l, s_l read x_l alone                           (the gate and the turn read the stream, not the force)
x⁰_t = E(u_t)                                     (the stream opens on the arrival)
```

`D↓ ĥ` is the coarse field's effort in the stream's coordinates and each fine level's own port `P_l`
converts it into its modes' drive, scaled by each mode's own coupling `γ_l = √(1 − |λ_l|²)` (a slow
mode answers a force weakly; no scale is chosen). The clocked levels' push reads the packet, which
carries the fine levels' motion, as before. The covector is the same series (§29), its source the
force's covector: `δ(D↓ ĥ)_t = Σ_(l<F) P_lᵀ γ_l G_l(t)`, the push's input covector at each arrival
level, in place of `δx⁰`.
