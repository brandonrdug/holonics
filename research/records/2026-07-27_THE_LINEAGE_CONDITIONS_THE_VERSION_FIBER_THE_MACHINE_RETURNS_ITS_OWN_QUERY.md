## Present question

Brandon's preceding correction was that the holonic machine should not avoid
complex black-box experiments merely because their governing algorithm is
initially hidden. Bitwise operations are a particularly important boundary:
they are both finite exact geometry and the material from which an actual
processor realizes algorithms.

His further correction is:

- inherited lineage is material the contemporary machine may pivot from;
- what would traditionally be called statistics need not enter as an
  observer-free fact;
- an imported distribution may instead be a context-relative landmark from
  which the machine derives and tests its own topology; and
- floating observations should not enter yet, before the exact landmark,
  reconstruction, and realization laws are understood.

The construction therefore asks:

> Can the production engine reconstruct the exact semantic class of an
> opaque recurrent bit transducer from source-marked lineage and its own
> receiver queries, retain the complete compatible family until the oracle
> returns every admitted finite face, derive higher-order Boolean geometry,
> and realize the result as actual machine code without receiving the target
> program, a truth-table answer, a floating loss, or an application-selected
> query?

This is the first bounded answer. It is not a claim that arbitrary programs
or arbitrary physical devices can now be reversed.

## 2. The declared recurrent bit ecology

For a receiver of width \(w\), a query is

\[
q=(s,x)\in Q_w
  =\{0,1\}^{w}\times\{0,1\}^{w}
  \cong\{0,1\}^{2w},
\]

and its response is

\[
F_w(q)=(s',y)\in\{0,1\}^{2w}.
\]

One admitted program is the five-event causal diagram

\[
\begin{aligned}
m &= B_{\mathrm{in}}(s,x),\\
t &= \operatorname{rot}_{r_s}(m),\\
s'&=I_c(t),\\
u &= \operatorname{rot}_{r_x}(x),\\
y &=B_{\mathrm{out}}(s',u).
\end{aligned}
\]

The available binary laws are exact bitwise XOR, AND, OR, modular addition,
and modular subtraction. The immediate law is XOR or modular addition.
Rotations and the immediate are part of the candidate, not fitted floating
parameters.

At maximum width eight, canonicalizing the two identical zero-immediate
cases gives

\[
5\cdot 8\cdot(256+255)\cdot5\cdot8=817{,}600
\]

declared programs. The law begins with this complete family. The opaque
instrument's target is a member of the family, but its membership and
parameters do not cross the world membrane.

This grammar is inherited mathematical ecology. It is not called a fact
about the opaque source. Testimony determines which part of it remains
admissible.

## 3. Lineage conditions a version fiber

Let \(G\) be the declared program ecology and let an exact testimony history
be

\[
E=\{(\lambda_i,\sigma_i,q_i,r_i)\}_i,
\]

where \(\lambda_i\) is a lineage identity, \(\sigma_i\) distinguishes an
imported landmark from a returned receiver occurrence, and \(r_i\) is the
observed response.

The contemporary version fiber is

\[
V_E=
\left\{
P\in G:
P(q_i)=r_i\text{ for every }i
\right\}.
\]

Receiving one more observation restricts it by inverse image:

\[
V_{E\cup\{(\lambda,\sigma,q,r)\}}
=
V_E\cap\operatorname{ev}_{q}^{-1}(\{r\}).
\]

This gives the imported data its intended ontology:

- it is immutable caused testimony;
- its source and chronology remain inspectable;
- it conditions a contemporary family;
- it need not be promoted to an absolute assertion outside that family; and
- contradictory testimony is an obstruction, not an averaging request.

`ImportedLandmark` and `ReturnedReceiver` remain distinct history sources.
Neither is silently rewritten as the other. Founding a wider receiver
enlarges the domain on which surviving programs may be compared; programs
which were identical on a lower-width quotient may split there.

The cardinality \(\lvert V_E\rvert\) is an exact refinement receipt, but it
is not yet a probability or loss law. No uniform prior over programs is
asserted.

## 4. The machine owns the search

When at least two candidates differ on an active receiver, production finds
an exact witness. It:

1. retains a reference candidate and compares it with another surviving
   program;
2. traverses each active Boolean cube in deterministic Gray order;
3. finds the first vertex at which their complete responses differ; and
4. emits that vertex as `next_query`.

The world may return an answer only to that exact query. A different query is
atomically refused. The returned response restricts the entire version
fiber, after which production derives the next witness.

This is a bounded abductive/deductive cycle:

- disagreement search proposes a receiver occurrence capable of separating
  live explanations;
- returned testimony deductively removes incompatible explanations; and
- the surviving causal diagram may become induced reusable material only
  after finite semantic certification.

Gray traversal is an exact elementary path through the cube. It is not a
claim of optimal experimental design: the current selector does not maximize
information gain or minimize the number of oracle calls.

## 5. Agreement is not certification

Candidate agreement inside a grammar does not by itself certify the opaque
world. The first implementation attempt would have stopped when one
semantic class remained. That was rejected.

Once no distinguishing candidate query remains, production requests the
complete response table for each active width. A width-\(w\) return contains
exactly

\[
\lvert Q_w\rvert=2^{2w}=4^w
\]

responses in canonical binary-address order. The law validates the table
shape, range, requested width, and every candidate response. Only after all
active widths have returned may it emit `BitSemanticCertificate`.

For widths one through eight, the complete certified domain has

\[
\sum_{w=1}^{8}4^w=87{,}380
\]

vertices.

The certificate therefore says something stronger than “one candidate
survived the selected probes”: the representative and the opaque receiver
agree on every point of every admitted finite receiver. It says nothing
about widths beyond eight or behaviors outside the declared two-word
transducer membrane.

## 7. The truth table is a higher-dimensional face

At width eight, the transition is an exact map

\[
F:Q_{16}\longrightarrow Q_{16}.
\]

For input direction \(i\) and output coordinate \(j\), define the Boolean
edge derivative

\[
\Delta_iF_j(v)=F_j(v)\mathbin{\operatorname{xor}}F_j(v\oplus e_i)
\]

and its directed edge count

\[
C_{ij}=\sum_{v\in Q_{16}}\Delta_iF_j(v).
\]

The production geometry receipt retains the complete \(16\times16\) integer
matrix \(C\). Its entries distinguish:

- 70 absent relations, with \(C_{ij}=0\);
- 26 invariant flips, with \(C_{ij}=65{,}536\); and
- 160 context-dependent relations strictly between those values.

It also applies the exact Boolean Möbius transform to each output coordinate.
The algebraic-normal-form degrees are

\[
(4,5,6,7,8,1,2,3,\;4,5,7,9,11,13,14,15).
\]

The first eight coordinates are the returned state and the final eight are
the output. Degree 15 is direct finite evidence that modular carries create
high-order incidence across the 16-dimensional input cube. The geometry is
not a plane drawing, a point cloud, or a scalar accuracy score.

This also makes the earlier “difference of faces” language concrete. A bit
coordinate is not interpreted alone; its relation is the exact change
induced along every oriented elementary input direction and the higher

The 54-byte image is not claimed to be minimal, fastest, or a universal

## 10. Relation to the broader research trajectory

### Receiver-relative origin

The zero landmark is not interpreted as “nothing.” It is one observation at
the local origin of a width-specific receiver basis. Its consequence depends
on the active program ecology and the later receivers through which that
ecology is tested.

### Deterministic diffusion

Testimony propagates through every compatible candidate by exact
restriction. The changing version fiber is diffusion through incidence, not
random perturbation. Wider receivers expose relations which lower quotients
could not distinguish.

### Information and its decoder

The opaque responses, causal program, x86 bytes, and System V ABI are
different caused objects. Their mutual relation makes the bytes executable.
This instantiates the earlier observation that encoded mass is informative
only for a receiver carrying an appropriate decoding method.

### Loss and probability

Forgetting a testimony enlarges the version fiber; receiving one restricts
it. That provides an exact topology of retained and lost distinctions. It
does not yet define probability. In particular, candidate count alone is
not promoted to likelihood, confidence, or semantic importance.

### Induction without representative guessing

The machine does not jump from several points to one favored answer. It
retains the entire compatible family, asks where live alternatives differ,
and accepts one causal diagram only after the world returns every admitted
face. The resulting diagram can then be compiled and reused.

## 11. Exact boundary of the result

- The admitted source grammar is a fixed five-event recurrent transducer,

- The result does not establish general black-box inversion, cryptanalysis,
