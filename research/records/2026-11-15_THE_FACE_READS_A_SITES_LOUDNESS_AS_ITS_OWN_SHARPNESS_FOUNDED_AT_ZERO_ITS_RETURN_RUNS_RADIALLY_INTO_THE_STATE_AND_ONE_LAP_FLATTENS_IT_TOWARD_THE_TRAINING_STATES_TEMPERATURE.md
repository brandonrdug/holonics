## 2. The law

At each open site, `v_a = release(⟨E(a), y⟩, 16)` the plain face, `λ_ℓ = log2_grain(s_ℓ)` the log of lane `ℓ`'s power on the face's
grain at the stage the face reads, and

```
β = 2^16 + release(Σ_ℓ κ_ℓ λ_ℓ, 16) + b,      z(a) = release(β v_a, 16) + c_a.
```

`κ_ℓ` (one a lane) and `b` are deposited material, part 26 (`loud`), founded at zero (`Field::with_loud`, `found-loud`), so at the
founding every face is the source's bit for bit. A blank's clock and the opening read their faces at temperature one, each its own
receiver (`agent-inferred`).

The return: `δc = δz` as before; `δv_a = release(β δz_a, 16)` in `δz`'s place for the encoding and the lift; `δβ = release(Σ_a δz_a v_a,
16)`; `δκ_ℓ = release(δβ λ_ℓ, 16)`, `δb = δβ`; and `δλ_ℓ = release(δβ κ_ℓ, 16)` returned radially into the state the face read,
`∂λ_ℓ/∂x_i = r̂_i/(W s_ℓ ln 2)`, beside the chart's own adjoint, which keeps only the part off the ray.

Scale (Fable's records #823, #825): the law reads the log of power, so a stretch of every site's power shifts `λ` by a constant that
`b` absorbs; no scale is privileged, and the reference loudness `−b/κ` is located by deposition. `κ` and `b` move only from places that
occurred and were compared (Hodge's side: no count of nothing).

The edge (Brandon 03:01Z, the de Bruijn–Newman constant): `Λ = 0` holds ξ at its critical temperature with no margin, so any
sharpening moves a zero off the line. The temperature that minimizes cross-entropy is the edge where neither sharper nor flatter lowers
the code (calibration), and `β` is set by descending the code, so the deposit parks it at the edge instead of running it to sharpness.
