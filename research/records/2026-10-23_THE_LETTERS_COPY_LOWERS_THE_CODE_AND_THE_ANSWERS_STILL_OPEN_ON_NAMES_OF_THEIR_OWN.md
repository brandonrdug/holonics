## The failure

The copy contact's samples reuse the question's pieces, but none answers about John: each opens on
a name of its own. The record located the cause in the units: `?\n` and `\n\n` are two units, so the
answer's opening meets no unit context that its question's opening met.

## What was built

The law is in the consumer's module doc (`examples/solenoidal_alpha/complete_language/unit_copy.rs`,
"The located copy at the letters"; commit `1c783005c`); the passage tree gained a lock-only read
with the visits of the node it locates (`PassageTree::lock`, atlas `hnn.passage-node-lock`). A
second passage tree reads the same passage at the letters, and its lock, run forward through its
own kept classes and read in the units, proposes the next unit. Both passages open at each problem
on the frame the prepared text puts before every question.

## The recorded failures this step repeated

the law is simple, symbols placed on cycles. That is the recorded failure
"an index of contexts counted as generation" and "a design thought in the programming language

letters' copy stays (it lowers the code by 4.3% and carries the question's pieces); the subject is
the problem's cycle read one turn later, and it is taken again after the arithmetic, in those terms.
