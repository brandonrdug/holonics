## 4. Carry: a cycle with a hand

Where a medium's dissipation dominates its storage, momentum carries nothing: what was pushed stops when the push stops. The
morning's record gave the measure, the share that fades in a turn (`wave.one-propagation-two-ratios`). In that regime the equations
of motion hold no rate, so a sequence of shapes run twice as fast moves the same amount, and a sequence run backward moves it back.
A stroke that goes out and returns along the same shapes therefore moves nothing at all, however fast one half is and however slow
the other (Purcell 1977). What a cycle of shapes moves is a property of the loop alone: for a small loop, a curvature times the area
it encloses (Shapere and Wilczek 1989), and for a pump carried once around a gapped constitution, an integer (Thouless 1983,
`pump.thouless-integer`).

`sweep p q` in the Lean is what a straight stroke from shape `p` to shape `q` moves under the area's own potential. Out and back
cancel. A loop through three shapes moves its signed area. The loop run the other way moves the opposite. Shapes scaled by `c` move
`c²` as much, and no rate appears in any statement. Two states have no loop that is not its own reverse, and three are the least
that have one. That count is why a clock that moves charge along a line has three phases, why a nerve has a state after firing in
which it cannot fire, and why a screw, a wave along a filament and a train of contractions are one machine: each is a cycle through
three or more states with a hand, and the hand is the sign of what is carried.

On the objects this is the helix: a circle and its carry. "No privileged clock" is the law's indifference to speed, and the carry
is the loop's holonomy. The carry circles of the engine are this object. Read on November 22 at the line's state W91, their moves
were turns at 0.475, locks at 0.201 and moves of negative determinant at 0.325, with 486 of 512 circles committed to one hand and
the hands evenly split across circles. A move of negative determinant is a mirror, and a mirror on the way round is a retraced
stroke: what the circle had carried up to it is carried back.

## 5. Kept: a count, or a pair of opposite hands, and a level as a difference tone

Turn every member of a set by one unit `z` of the circle. Three things can be asked of a read (Lean, section 3).

- A linear read `Σ c_i h_i` carries the turn once. It holds nothing that outlasts the turn.
- A product of one hand, `h_a h_b`, carries the turn twice.
- A product of opposite hands, `conj(h_a) h_b`, does not carry it. `p` of one hand against `p` of the other is a singlet of the
  circle, and one hand alone is not.

So under a common turn two kinds of thing stand: a closed cycle's whole windings, which no continuous turn changes, and opposite
hands joined by the transport between them. The tree's Ratio is the second kind, and its statement that orientation exists only in
the pairing is this one. It is why a pair of wires twisted together rejects what reaches both, why an electric sense reads the
difference between two pores, and why a grain is a relation between grains (`radix.grain-is-a-relation`): the common part of two
grains is the gauge, and the relation is what is kept.

Give the members rates. A product of one hand of rates `ω₁` and `ω₂` stands at `ω₁ + ω₂`, a finer grain than either. A product of
opposite hands stands at `ω₁ − ω₂`, which can be slower than both. A linear read of members that all turn at least `ω` a second
holds nothing slower than `ω`. **A level whose clock is slower than every part under it is therefore a product of opposite hands of
those parts, or it holds nothing of them.** The ruler's section F shows the case a listener knows: partials 3, 4 and 5 of a unit
hold the unit at 0.000000 in a linear read and at 2.000000 in the square, and a turn of 0.7 applied to all three moves each
partial's phase by 0.7 and leaves the difference tone where it was. The ear hears a fundamental no partial holds; a mean flow is
driven by the mean of a product of fluctuations; an envelope is what a detector of squares returns.

**The built level, read against this.** The restriction of `field/scale.rs` is `x¹_j = A m(j) + b_A` with
`m_k(j) = Σ_(t∈e_j) h_k(φ_t) r̂(x_t)`: the place's read times a chart of the news phase, summed over the epoch. That is a product,
of the read with the level's own clock, and it brings what turns `k` times an epoch down to the epoch's rate. The law is built to
that extent. What the moment does not hold is a product of two parts of the read with each other. The tree had measured the
consequence on November 17 and 18: the moment holds an epoch's symbols as a bag at 721 thousandths and the epoch as one identity
at 310, and at the level's locus nothing linear in a unit's symbols, in the unit before or in their moments relieves the fine
comparison, "the unit bound, which a linear read of its symbols does not hold"
(`tower.level-restriction-is-its-draw`, `deposition.no-linear-tension-at-the-levels-locus`). The unit's bound is the relative phase
of its members, which is the pair of opposite hands. The same rows hold the other kept thing as a loss: in the epochs that wind
past a section the chart's fold costs the leading symbol 507 against 329 and the last 518 against 223. That is a count dropped.
The two things the law says are kept are the two things the November rows found missing.

## 6. Nested-depth: composition is a shift with a floor

A circle's winding can go up or down without end, and a turn forward is undone by a turn back in either order. Give the winding a
floor, the empty stack, the trunk of a body. Then a push followed by a pop is the identity, and a pop followed by a push is the
identity less the floor: `S*S = 1`, `SS* = 1 − P₀` (Lean: `pop_push`, `push_pop`, `push_pop_loses_the_floor`). Every algebra made
by one such pair is the same algebra (Coburn 1967), and its words reduce to `a` closers then `b` openers with the product that
cancels an opener against the closer after it. The Lean's `bmul` is that product: it is associative, the depth `b − a` adds along a
word, an opener then its closer is the empty word, and a closer then an opener is a word of depth zero that is not the empty word.

Three things follow.

1. **Nested-depth is an index.** The depth is the one additive count a word of brackets has, and for the shift it is the index: the
   winding of the symbol read with a floor (`cell.toeplitz-index`). A ring alone, however many, is a machine of finitely many
   states. A winding with a floor is a stack. Every nested language is a language of finitely many states cut with a language of
   balanced brackets (Chomsky and Schützenberger 1963), so the whole of nesting is rings and one more thing, and the one more
   thing is this.
2. **A word holds more than its depth.** `close_then_open` is the finite statement of Brandon's point about a net hand: a compound
   whose count from outside is zero is not nothing. The count is the image of the word under the one map that forgets order.
3. **With `p` kinds of bracket the address is a `p`-adic number.** `p` pushes with ranges that do not meet and together fill the
   space (Cuntz 1977) are "write digit `i` in front", and an unending word of them is a path down a tree of `p` branches a level,
   each ball inside the last. That is the tree's fractal navigator (words, restrictions, a source word as an address) and
   Ostrowski's reading of a number at a prime (`prime.ostrowski-first-differing-clock`, `tower.first-shared-level-distance`): two
   addresses are near when they share their first places, and the distance between two parts is the depth at which they first
   share a cell.

## 7. A chain of relative moves, a link asleep, and why no hand is net

A part's place is the product of the relative moves of every joint between it and the trunk. That product is a word, its length is
the nested-depth of the part, and it is the tree's serial screw word, the chain of the pair contact (the motor chart of the
elementary objects). Four finite statements say what Brandon reported of his arm (Lean, section 9).

- A change of the frames inside the chain leaves the product as it was (`chain_regauge`). No frame along the arm is privileged,
  and the place is the same from each.
- With one link unread, the far part is still read against itself: `(a x b)⁻¹ (a x b c) = c` whatever `x` is
  (`asleep_link_keeps_the_far_part`). The hand is felt, its fingers against each other, with the upper arm gone.
- The far part's place varies one for one with the unread link (`place_tells_the_link`). Its place is lost as a whole, by that one
  factor.
- One closed loop through the link returns it: `x = a⁻¹ t b⁻¹` from one touch `t` (`touch_recovers_link`; the ruler's section G
  draws 1,000 planar chains and returns each link to sixteen places).

Moving is closing a loop. The same statement is the Bombe's (a menu of contacts closed pairwise), a map closed on a place visited
twice, a correction of a code by its checks, and play as loops a learner chooses. The tree's row on the syndrome adds what a single
loop cannot do: a loop detects that some link on it is unread, and telling which needs a menu of loops that separates the links,
two readings to detect and three to locate one (`attribution.syndrome-localization`).

**No hand is net.** A sum of the parts' signed turns, seen from outside, is the depth of §6 and no more. It can vanish while every
part keeps its turn against its neighbour. The product formula is the statement for a number: its sizes at the real place and at
every prime multiply to one, and each prime keeps its own valuation (`tower.product-formula-exchange`). For a body it is the
tree's row on chains: a chain composes into one whole exactly where its contacts meet opposite hands, and a compound's own charge
is carried in by its parts or made at a crossing (`holarchy.chain-composes-opposite-hands`). The information is in the parts and
is reached along the chain.

**A budget of receptors is a lattice.** A field of bounded detail sampled on a lattice repeats its spectrum on the dual lattice,
and the fewest samples are where the repeats pack densest (Petersen and Middleton 1962; the tree cites it at
`cell.hexagon-three-classes-mod-common-mode`). So at one count of receptors a volume, layouts differ in what they resolve, and a
density cannot stand in for a layout. The ruler's section C gives the sizes (§8.3).

## 8. Implicit units, the Comma, and an address in a ratio's own remainders

### 8.1 A unit is a loop that nearly closes

Nobody declares twelve. Take two parts, an octave `2:1` and a fifth `3:2`, and ask how many times the smaller fits the larger. Once,
and a fourth `4:3` is left. The fourth fits the fifth once, and a tone `9:8` is left. The tone fits the fourth twice with the leimma
`256:243` left, the leimma fits the tone twice with the comma `531441:524288` left, and so on without end, since no power of three
is a power of two (`ratio.comma-never-closes`). The ruler's section A runs it in factored integers:

| step | fits | remainder | nats | unit (fifths) | laps before the comma is one own step |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | `2² : 3` | 0.287682 | 1 | 2.41 |
| 2 | 1 | `3² : 2³` | 0.117783 | 2 | 2.94 |
| 3 | 2 | `2⁸ : 3⁵` | 0.052116 | 5 | 2.66 |
| 4 | 2 | `3¹² : 2¹⁹` | 0.013551 | 12 | 4.26 |
| 5 | 3 | `2⁶⁵ : 3⁴¹` | 0.011463 | 41 | 1.47 |
| 6 | 1 | `3⁵³ : 2⁸⁴` | 0.002088 | 53 | 6.26 |
| 7 | 5 | `2⁴⁸⁵ : 3³⁰⁶` | 0.001022 | 306 | 2.22 |
| 8 | 2 | `3⁶⁶⁵ : 2¹⁰⁵⁴` | 0.000044 | 665 | 23.88 |

Three readings of the table.

- **A unit is implicit.** Each count of fifths in the fifth column is a loop of the two parts' own ratios that nearly closes, and
  the remainder beside it is what the loop misses by: its comma, the holonomy of the loop, which is the coordinator's second guess
  and the tree's reading since October (`rotation.fifth-gap-recursion`: each level's remainder is the next level's gap). The units
  are found by the parts and never handed in. They nest, each built of the ones before it, and that nesting is nested-depth again.
- **The list of fits is the address.** `[1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 1, 5, 2, 23, …]` places the fifth in the octave to any grain, in the
  ratio's own remainders. Nothing stands beside it: no base, no width, no place of a point. Any reader who holds the two parts
  derives the same list (`lock.run-word-address`, `lock.euclid-address`).
- **A unit's quality is the next entry of its address.** The laps a unit runs before its comma amounts to one of its own steps lie
  between the next count of fits and that count and one: 4.26 for twelve (next 3), 6.26 for fifty-three (next 5), 23.88 for 665
  (next 23), and 1.47 for forty-one (next 1). A large entry in an address is a place where a reader may stop with little left
  over. That is a landmark in the tree's sense, a face where paths converge, and it is why twelve is a scale and forty-one is not.

A unit that misses its closure by a comma misses by `n` commas in `n` laps (`comma_accumulates`): a helix. A zero comma is a circle,
which returns and carries nothing forward. No closure at all keeps nothing. A small comma is what is both kept and advancing.

### 8.2 What the address costs

The ruler's section B. For a ratio drawn at large, an entry of the address has an entropy of 3.4325 bits, costs 3.5070 bits written
with its own length, and fixes 3.4237 bits of the ratio (`π²/(6 ln 2)` nats; measured on 478,311 entries of 200 drawn pairs of
4,096-bit integers: 2.3740 nats fixed an entry, 3.5082 bits written). So an address in a ratio's own remainders pays 0.08 bits an
entry over what it fixes, and holds nothing beside itself. A radix digit pays what it fixes with nothing over, and holds its base,
its width and the place of its point beside it, for every value, in a table.

The price is elsewhere, and the tree has it: the walk that derives the address pays the square of the logarithm in unit moves,
through a heavy tail (`lock.euclid-walk-cost`), and arithmetic on such addresses waits on entries of no bounded size (Gosper 1972).
Storing and computing exchange, as the precision law says. An address of this kind stores least and navigates most.

### 8.3 Several parts: a unit, a packing, an address and a correction are one object

With more than two parts the remainders form a lattice. The intervals of 2, 3 and 5 are points `(v₂, v₃, v₅)`, a loop that nearly
closes is a short vector, and a temperament is a covector whose kernel is the commas it calls zero
(`ratio.temperament-kernel-commas`). Read as a general object:

- **an address** is a configuration on which every loop of the menu closes;
- **a comma** is a loop's failure to close, which a code calls its syndrome (`attribution.syndrome-localization`);
- **a unit's quality** is the least comma among the loops through it, which a packing calls its least distance;
- **a correction** reads the commas and moves the parts until they close, which is Brandon's arm woken by moving it;
- **finding the nearest closed configuration** is hard in general and immediate on a basis that fits, where checking one is a
  multiplication. That is his remark on shortest paths, and the tree's rows on finding against checking stand under it
  (`key.kernel-of-a-locale`, `key.consistency-with-carried-residues`).

A layout denser than a grid is made exactly this way: the points of a grid whose low places satisfy a set of checks, each check a
loop of residues that must close. The ruler's section C prices it, against the cubic layout that scalar digits are:

| layout | dimension | cells at one least separation, against the cube | bits a coordinate | rounding gain, bits a coordinate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| hexagonal | 2 | 1.1547 | 0.104 | 0.028 |
| face-centred | 3 | 1.4142 | 0.167 | 0.041 |
| E8 | 8 | 16 | 0.500 | 0.109 |
| Leech | 24 | 16,777,216 | 1.000 | 0.171 |

As a code of values, where a value is rounded to its nearest cell, scalar digits lose at most 0.2546 bits a coordinate against any
layout in any dimension. As a menu of states told apart at one separation, which is what an address is, the cubic layout holds
half a bit a coordinate less than E8 in eight dimensions and a whole bit less than the Leech lattice in twenty-four (both proved
the densest: Viazovska 2017; Cohn, Kumar, Miller, Radchenko and Viazovska 2017). This is the size of what Brandon points at when he
sets primes, packings and scores against strings of digits. The loss of scalar words is small where they round and large where
they address, and what recovers it is couplings between the coordinates' low places, each a loop that closes.

### 8.4 Naive compression is a temperament

A temperament declares a comma zero. Twelve equal steps do it for `3¹² : 2¹⁹` and for two commas of five, and the tree prices where
the remainder is then placed (`ratio.comma-placement-gauge`: spread over twelve fifths it costs a twelfth of what it costs in one).
A compression that drops a remainder because it is small is a temperament: it is lawful as a receiver's quotient, when the kernel
is the receiver's, and it is naive when the kernel is the compressor's. Holonic Compression keeps the remainder as the cokernel,
emanated or retained, and lets the receiver's grain say when it counts.

## 10. Mutual loss

Two readers each hold a share for every symbol, `p` and `q`. What `p`'s reader pays to read with `q`'s shares is `D(p‖q)`, and
what `q`'s pays to read with `p`'s is `D(q‖p)`. Their sum is `Σ (p − q)(log p − log q)` (`mutual_loss_two_way`). It is the same
from either side, it is never negative at any symbol, and it is positive wherever the two differ (`mutual_loss_symm`,
`mutual_loss_nonneg`, `mutual_loss_pos`; the ruler's section E on a drawn pair: 0.015174 and 0.015287 nats, sum 0.030461).

It is the comma of the loop the two readers make: send a symbol from one to the other and back, each coding with its own shares,
and this is what the round trip misses by. It privileges neither reader, which a one-way cross-entropy does. Read with §8.1 it is no
loss in the sense of something taken away. Two readers with a zero comma are one reader twice, and the loop carries nothing. Two
with no closure share nothing. A small comma is a helix: the loop is kept and something advances each time round.

Brandon's two classes of molecule are a loop of two readers, each the other's key: one is read into the other, and the other does
the reading. Neither closes a loop alone, so neither is a unit, and the question of which came first asks for a first member of a
loop, which a loop does not have (`loop_has_no_first`, `loop_start_conjugates`: read from another of its parts, a loop's remainder
is a conjugate of the same remainder). The unit is the loop, and it is pumped from outside itself, as the Earth's loops are by the
Sun, which is §4's cycle in a medium that fades. Nothing of biology is built or proposed here; the statement is of loops.

## 20. A constant is a state read as a number, and an overture is its inverse

Brandon's fourth note (21:39 UTC), on §19's fourth point, in this record's words. The reading of the skin's constant is likely
right and should have been plain. It is the kind of thing a gravitational constant is, an index of refraction, a cosmological
constant: the units of the axes of time vary. An overture is the inverse kind of thing. The underside of a face is the underside
of time, and that is the lock.

**A number that survives every change of units is a state.** The test of November 21 says that a statement which changes when a
part's unit and its reader's reading are scaled inversely was counted from a gauge. Read the other way it tells a state: a number
that no such scaling moves is no gauge. A speed and a coupling that turn one unit into another are units, and can be set to one.
An index of refraction is a ratio of two clocks and stays what it is. The skin's number is e-folds a radian of the ring's own
turn, a ratio of a fade to a turn, the same in every unit of length or of time (`fade_over_turn_is_unit_free`). So it is a state of
the medium, and by the store law it has a balance: what charges it, what leaks, at what rate.

**The index is complex, and the engine holds half of it.** A wave number is `k = (n + iκ) ω/c`: a turn a length and a fade a
length. The engine holds the turn as a state, since a ring whose clock is its own turns by its pace between two sites (`skin_of`
takes the turn's variation). It fixes `κ/n = 1`, an angle of 45 degrees, for every ring. That is the
index of a good conductor. A clear medium has `κ` far under `n`. The morning's row gives the same angle as the end of no quality
(`arg k = ½ arctan(1/Q)`, `wave.fade-per-turn-is-the-quality`).

**The lock: a response one-sided in time is fixed by its face** (Lean, section 12). Split a response into the part even in time
and the part odd in time. The even part is what a reading from both sides returns, the face. The odd part is its underside.
Where nothing answers before the act, the underside is the face after the act and the face's negative before it
(`one_sided_under_is_face`, `one_sided_under_before`): the face times the sign of time. So the face alone gives the response
(`one_sided_from_face`), and in the frequencies a medium's turn and its fade are two parts of one function (Kramers 1927; Kronig
1926; Titchmarsh 1948). "The underside of a face is the underside of time" is this theorem. Four of the tree's statements are
charts of it.

- A lock and its key are one face read from its two sides (`key.mirror-separates-finding-from-checking`, November 22).
- At a boundary the reciprocal half of a response is the part even under a swap of two acts and the handed half the part odd, and
  order lives in the odd part (§11).
- The scale part of a ratio's logarithm is the underside of time, a count where the turn part is a clock (the record of
  November 11, §14.3). Here the fade is the scale part and the turn the clock part, and one-sidedness is what ties them.
- The completed zeta function is a factor of one hand added to its mirror (§12). Its face on the line fixes its causal half, and
  the zeros stand on the line when that half is passive (`key.half-line-circle-contraction`, `rh.causal-impedance-route`).

For the engine the lock costs nothing to keep: a one-sided strand's kernel `e^(−γ s) e^(iθ s)`, `s ≥ 0`, is causal at any fade
`γ ≥ 0`, so a ring's fade can move as a state and the turn and the fade stay one function by construction.

**An overture is the inverse of a constant.** A constant is a state read as a number: it moves slower than its reader's clock,
and the reader writes down what it sees (the guide: "stored" says that a mode is slow against its reader's clock). An overture is
a number entered once as a state, after which only deposition moves it (the record of October 29; the gene of November 16 and
17, whose form is handed at founding, the same for every stream). The two are one face from its two sides: a reading down to a
number, and a founding up from one. So the skin's number is lawful as an overture, each ring's fade founded at one e-fold a
radian, and it stands in the engine as a constant that nothing has moved since November 1.

**The arm this settles** (a construction; the engine's and the lead's). The fade is founded at the present number, so the build
with its new law off reproduces the line's lap (rule 2 of a line's lap). The covector is in hand: a source at turn `s` is weighed
by `e^(−γ s)`, whose slope in `γ` is `−s e^(−γ s)`, the adjoint's return weighed by each source's own turn. Its rate is a store's
state like any deposit's. Guess G-H9, fixed at 21:42:26 UTC on an arm nobody has built: the word before at a word's third letter at
0.10 or more (0.55); the located fades leave their one value, with the fade a radian under half the founding's on at least half
of the rings of period 128 and more (0.8); every held register within 0.003 nats a place of its control (0.7).

**What does not transfer.** Nothing is claimed here of any constant of physics. That a medium is a metric to what crosses it
(Gordon 1923), that a coupling can be read as a medium's stiffness counted in its modes (Sakharov 1967) and that a cosmological
term can enter as a constant of integration (Einstein 1919) are the literature's, named as the charts the note points at; the
tree's own reading of the last is the record of August 25. What transfers is the test of the first paragraph and the split of the
third.
