## 4. Source facts the construction turned up, each a `SourceTestimony::Implementation`

- **The contact carries no scale.** `Gemma4TextAttention.__init__` sets `self.scaling = 1.0`. The
  paused site's `BASE` candidate divided by `sqrt(256)`; the source does not.
- **The values are RMS-normalized without gain** (`v_norm`, `with_scale=False`) before the contact;
  the paused site carried no value norm.
- **`layer_scalar` for layer 0 is `0x3d7a = 250·2^-12 ≈ 0.061`**, and it multiplies the layer's
  complete return last. It is not `1`.
- **The entering scale is `bf16(sqrt(2560)) = 50.5`**, cast by the source at law level.
- **Layers 24–41 store `k_proj`, `v_proj`, `k_norm`, `v_norm` that the source ignores on load**
  (`_keys_to_ignore_on_load_unexpected`); their K/V is reused from the last non-shared layer of the
  same type. Realization testimony for a later station; it licenses no ablation here.

## 6. What this does not claim

- Not Phoenix inference, not a native model, not a rest, not an emission. One layer of forty-two, the
  sliding-attention species only; the full-attention layers (head 512, proportional RoPE with a
  quarter rotated) are not enacted; no tokenizer codec; two tokens.
- Not exactness in the source's own runtime: the enclosure encloses the exact mathematics of the
  stored map, and the source's bf16 arithmetic sits `0.09` away from it.
- Not a tight enclosure by a magnitude receiver's standard: `2.5·10^-4` at `2^-48` after one layer,
  and the amplification through the layer is the dependency of interval arithmetic, reported as
  such. A finer aperture nests, and that is the law the driver checks.
- Not the four resource obstruction repairs: a placement on the CPU chart refuses rather than
  computes, and a carrier-range refusal names its octaves; neither is answered here by changing the
  partition, only by refusing.
