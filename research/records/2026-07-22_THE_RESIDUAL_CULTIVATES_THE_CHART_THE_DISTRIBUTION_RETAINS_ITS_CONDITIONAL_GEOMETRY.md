## Exact conditional distinction

For a chart population `S`, feature `f`, and observed value `v`, define:

```text
N_(f=v)(tau) = # {e in S : I_e(f)=v and tau_e=tau}
Q_(f=v)(tau) = N_(f=v)(tau) / sum_u N_(f=v)(u).
```

Feature `f` is conditionally material at that chart exactly when two observed values have unequal
exact quotients:

```text
exists v,w : Q_(f=v) != Q_(f=w).
```

This quotient licenses a split but does not replace its geometry. The resulting branches and their
further conditional structure remain part of the chart.

Two regions can have the same marginal distribution while differing in how that distribution is
composed. Collapsing both to the same scalar probability loses their conditional topology. The
diagram therefore carries counts, branch incidence, and shared continuation—not only normalized
weights.
