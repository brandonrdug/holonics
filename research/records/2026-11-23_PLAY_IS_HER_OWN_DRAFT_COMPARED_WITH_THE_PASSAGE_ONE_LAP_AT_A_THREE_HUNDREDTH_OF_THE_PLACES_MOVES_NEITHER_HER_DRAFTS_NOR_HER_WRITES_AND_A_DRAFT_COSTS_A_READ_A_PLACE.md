## 4. What it says

At a three-hundredth of the places, one lap of her own drafts moves neither the drafts (7.2% to 7.6% agreeing) nor the writes, and it
costs the theory's code a hundredth of a nat. By G-P1's rule the dose is the next change, and the dose is priced by the draft: a draft
of `RUN` places is `RUN` reads of the field, one place a read, because the act is released by the writer's sweeps
([`Constitution::release`](../../crates/engine/src/release.rs) with no run bound). The release already holds the other form: a read
releases a run, its places drawn together from one read's faces while the entropies they add stay within a bound, and refine passes
reopen and redraw ([`Constitution::sample_runs`](../../crates/engine/src/release.rs)), which is the generation law's own wording
(generation refines a joint field and releases its boundary). A draft opened whole and settled over a few passes costs a few reads
in place of `RUN`, so the dose can rise tenfold at this lap's cost. That is the next loop, read beside a control.
