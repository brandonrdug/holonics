## 3. The object

A level is a codec pivot that carries its decoder, with a transport of its own. In the engine's notation:

```text
E   the restriction     x¹_j = A m(j) + b_A,   m_k(j) = Σ_(t∈e_j) h_k(φ_t) r̂(x_t)        an epoch's moment on the news phase
U   the level's transport over epochs, its field's ticks on the clock of news between its sites
D   the prolongation    x̂_t = Σ_k h_k(φ_t) v_k(j),   v(j) = B r̂(x¹_j) + b_B
F   the receiver's face at the symbols, the encoding's readout
```

Its law is two equations at its consumer, the two this guide gives as its examples of a relation stated with its
consumer:

```text
the closure     D E = ρ               the decoder after the encoder is the projection onto what the admitted future distinguishes
the square      E A_f = U E           restricting the passage's next epoch is the level's own transport of this epoch's state
its read leg    read′(π σ) = ρ(read σ)   the level's state, read through its own decoder, gives the receiver's reading
```

- **Each defect is read at the receiver's face.** The closure's is the cokernel's news, the level's own density at the
  places of its own epoch, `d_t = F(D E x)_t` and `ĉ_t = −ln d_t(u_t)`. The square's is the same reading one epoch on,
  `d⁺_t = F(D U E x)_t` at the places of epoch `j + 1`. The target is the written symbol through the same encoding, the
  loss law's own requirement.
- **Why at the face and never in the level's coordinates.** A square in coordinates is closed by the constant map, the
  coarsest congruence, so a level compared with its own restricted future can agree with itself about nothing. The read
  leg anchors it. October 30 gave the same reason for its target: a heading compared with a packet the fine levels
  formed could close its loop on its own echo.
- **Why a density over the symbols and no menu.** A contrast against a section's other spans locates what tells those
  spans apart, and its target was another function of the terrain than the arrivals' code, which is where October's
  closure cost the arrivals. A density at the symbols has the arrivals' own target, so on terrain the two comparisons
  share their covectors descend one code. Whether a shared locus still costs is a reading and is not assumed (§6).
- **What "its own grain" means.** Two things. The route: the level's state alone, through `D` and `F`, with no fine tick
  after it. The sum: over the unit, `C¹_j = Σ_(t∈e_j) ĉ_t`, the unit's news under the level, one symbol of the level's
  own passage. The second gives the recursion its ground: a second level's sites are the landmarks of `C¹`. A level
  with no face has no news, so the law "a level's sites are the landmarks of the news below it" has nothing to cut above
  the first level, which is where the built tower stands.
- **The kernel.** The closure alone keeps two spellings apart: an identity suffices for the future and is not the
  least. The square pulls together the states of units the future reads alike, and the quotient forms where the state is
  short. Brandon's example of the evening is the case: a worded operator and its sign do one thing in an exchange and
  are one class at the level, two strands below it. It is an illustration and no reading is ordered on it.
- **What the fine level then is.** The cokernel's carrier: it reads what the level's density leaves. The design of
  November 15 states its cost (a place runs whole where the unit's news under the level steps its clock). Nothing of
  that is ordered here.
- **The backward pass, by analogy.** Symbols into a state and back to symbols is the round trip `D E`, and what survives
  it is `ρ`. The night's trace was that round trip read through the level: 68 to 171 thousandths. The analogy lands on
  the codec's own equation, which the tree held.
- **No square is promised to close.** `tower.shift-no-coarse-navigator` holds a reduction with a defect under every
  coarse navigator. A defect that stands is the fine level's to carry and is read as the level's news; it is no failure
  of the build.
