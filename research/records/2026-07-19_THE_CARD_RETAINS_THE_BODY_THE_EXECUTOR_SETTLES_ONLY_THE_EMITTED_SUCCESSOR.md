## Theory-to-structure relation

Residency changes where the already-live relation is physically carried. It does not change who
owns it:

```text
logical standing-before + live carrier-before
  -> executor stages out-of-place physical enactment
  -> LiveCurrentMachine validates every returned current and hand
  -> LiveCurrentMachine prepares the complete successor
  -> executor settles that already-prepared successor into discardable residency
  -> LiveCurrentMachine commits standing, continuations, departures, and radiation.
```

The executor has no standing-after construction law. It cannot admit a current, choose a
contribution, resolve chronology, commit a lineage, or recover from testimony. Its settlement hook
runs only after the machine has closed every fallible logical allocation and validation. A failed
physical settlement leaves the prior resident buffers intact and the logical body unchanged.

## Out-of-place carrier continuation

Each event launches against immutable committed device buffers. For a matching live lineage, the
executor allocates its result aperture and copies only the active carrier, overflow counts, and
occupied overflow rows device-to-device. Event-local control, deeds, contacts, and bounded outputs
remain event-local.

If actual kernel pressure requires a larger aperture, the failed attempt is discarded and the new
aperture is populated again from the unchanged committed device predecessor. Only a complete
kernel return becomes staged. Terminal lineages are marked for release rather than retained.

After machine validation, settlement promotes all staged continuing carriers together and removes
all staged departures. No partial result becomes the next input.

## Resident standing successor

The standing-before allocation is read-only during conduct. If the prepared successor is exact,
the allocation remains in place. At the same flat-compatible rank, settlement creates a new sparse
standing allocation out of place:

- its header and changed or newly occupied rows cross host-to-device;
- unchanged rows cross device-to-device from standing-before; and
- the prior allocation remains committed until every transfer succeeds.

A genuine rank change or coherence break performs one full physical remount. This is a phase
boundary in the device layout, not permission to upload the body on every event.
