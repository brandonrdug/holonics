## 1. The correction

A square which fails to commute does not itself choose a contextual branch, found a new predicate,
negate either path, or declare an error. It exposes an **OPEN comparison at the contemporary
frame**. The routes and their difference are known; the relation which would reconcile, explain,
or carry them together is not presently available.

The OPEN is not empty. Its boundary is exact information:

```text
what entered,
which paths conducted,
where they landed,
which interface they shared,
how their returned faces differed,
which frame/objective/receiver made that difference consequential.
```

Another relation may later fill, rebase, bypass, contextualize, or render that opening irrelevant.
Nothing about present noncommutation entails that no such relation exists. “Yet” is part of the
type: OPEN belongs to the current Standing, frame, objective, receiver, and grain.

This corrects the closing alternative in the preceding discussion. A noncommuting square does not
automatically decide among retaining a residual, dividing an antecedent, or founding a higher
relation. It emits the OPEN boundary. A genuinely later event may do any of those things through
actual new Current.

## 2. Predicates name local relational faces

Let `Sigma` be a many-sorted local signature. An ordinary `n`-ary relation is interpreted as

```text
P^M subset X_1 x ... x X_n.
```

The Cartesian product names possible tuples in that chart; `P^M` names admitted tuples. Eros must
also preserve parallel equal occurrences, so its more faithful relational carrier is an
occurrence-indexed multirelation

```text

```

Distinct members of `E_P` may have the same image without becoming one event. Predicates therefore
do not install intrinsic properties on isolated objects. They expose receiver-, frame-, and
world-relative relations among actual occurrences.

An antecedent is a finite relational pattern, for example

```text
A(x) = P(x_1,x_2) tensor Q(x_2,x_3) tensor Constraint(x_1,x_3).
```

`tensor` is written to emphasize resource-sensitive co-presence. Ordinary conjunction is a lawful
truth projection, but it does not say whether an occurrence may be copied, discarded, consumed, or
preserved.

## 3. Transport is grounded, proof-relevant implication

Classical material implication

```text
A -> C  ==  not A or C
```

is too collapsed to be Eros transport. It is true whenever `A` is absent and retains no witness of
how an actual `A` became `C`. The transport face is instead a resource-sensitive sequent or rewrite
schema

```text
rho : forall x. A(x) -o exists y. C(x,y)
```

together with an actual grounding `m` of `A` in contemporary Current. The pair `(rho,m)` is a
transport occurrence. Different groundings and different derivations remain distinct even when
they expose an equal consequent face.

The equivalent local-rewrite presentation is

```text
rho = (L <- K -> R).
```

`L` is the antecedent neighborhood, `R` the consequent neighborhood, and `K` the carried interface.
`L \ K` may cease participating, `R \ K` may be produced, and `K` continues without requiring a
crystal copy of either interior.

The logical consequent and the Eros consequence remain distinct. `C` is the afforded right-hand
pattern. It becomes a material consequence only when a grounded event conducts, changes its world,
and may return as genuinely later Current.

At this cut:

- **RIDE** — the grounded consequent factors through transport and witnesses already available in
  Standing;
- **FOUND** — an actual event attaches a new witness, occurrence, interface, axis, or higher cell;
  and
- **OPEN** — the available relation does not presently complete the comparison or consequent.

Failure to derive is not derivation of negation. Explicit negation and incompatibility owe their
own local predicates.

## 4. Analogy is a comparison of transports

For two antecedent-consequent transports

```text
r_1 : A_1 -> C_1,
r_2 : A_2 -> C_2,
```

let `u : A_1 -> A_2` relate their antecedent faces and `v : C_1 -> C_2` relate their consequent
faces. Their comparison is the square

```text
A_1 --r_1--> C_1
 |u            |v
 V             V
A_2 --r_2--> C_2.
```

The complete comparison is

```text
Chi_square = (v circle r_1, r_2 circle u).
```

Where the pair agrees at the declared receiver, the square commutes and witnesses a transported
analogy, rebase, or dual description. Where it does not agree, the square is OPEN. In an additive
carrier one may project the residual

```text

```

but `delta_square` is the oriented boundary datum, not the missing explanation and not a scalar
command.

The triangle in `ELEMENTARY_MECHANICS.md` is the special case comparing a two-step composite with a
direct transport. A triangle, square, or higher comparison may therefore be read as:

```text

2-cells  comparisons among transports,
n-cells  coherence among lower comparisons.
```

This is one lawful higher-relational or simplicial chart. It is not a requirement that every world
arrive pre-triangulated.

## 5. The hole has two typed faces

The intuition of a hole can be stated exactly without collapsing incidence and transport.

### Combinatorial OPEN

An oriented comparison boundary may form a cycle `z` while no admitted higher occurrence `c`
satisfies

```text
partial c = z.
```

Then the contemporary complex has an unfilled incidence cycle. If later Current founds such a
`c`, the old cycle becomes a boundary in the enlarged successor. The earlier occurrence was still
a real hole at its own event cut.

### Transport OPEN

A higher incidence cell may already exist while its parallel transports remain noncommuting. The
cell's topology is present, but no admitted equalizer, homotopy, rebase, or higher transport relates
the two carried paths at the selected receiver. Its `Chi` remains open transport or curvature.

These two openings can coincide, but neither implies the other. Adding a drawn face does not solve
a transport mismatch; equal transport does not invent a missing occurrence.

## 6. Foil exposes the opening; it does not pronounce a verdict

A foil is a controlled sibling or path comparison which preserves a declared boundary while
changing one named relation:

```text
F : (M,K) -> (M',K).
```

If their transported consequences agree at the selected receiver, that foil is gauge there. If
they differ, it exposes an OPEN. The foil is the contrast construction; noncommutation is its
result. Neither branch must be globally false.

A declared incompatibility is additional structure, for example

```text
C_1 tensor C_2 |- bottom_r.
```

This states that `C_1` and `C_2` cannot jointly occupy receiver `r` under a named constraint. It
does not fill their noncommuting square and does not license explosion into unrelated conclusions.
Distinct contexts or lineages may retain both until a later relation changes their joint boundary.

The base mechanics is therefore open-world and non-explosive:

```text
absent relation != negated relation,
failed route != impossible future route,
local incompatibility != universal falsehood,
noncommutation != automatic FOUND.
```

## 8. Failed navigation is valuable situated terrain

For current hexis `H_t`, frame `F_t`, objective `Q_t`, and receiver `r`, a traversed route may fail
to afford the sought boundary:

```text
not Reach_(H_t,F_t,Q_t,r)(route, sought_face).
```

This statement is scoped. It does not entail

```text
forall future H. not Reach_H(route, sought_face).
```

because the world, body, objective, interfaces, or relation topology may change. The failed route
can nevertheless alter later conduct: it exposes a dead end, barrier, unfamiliar passage,
misplaced landmark, or missing bridge in the current field. Learning is not erasing that route; it
is the failed passage becoming consequential Standing.

Biological navigation supplies comparative realizations of this relation without prescribing an
Eros algorithm. Jumping spiders in detour experiments scan before committing, may lose direct
visual access to the goal, reorient during traversal, and sometimes change initial route. This
supports situated route correction rather than possession of an absolute map:
[Distance assessment of detours by jumping spiders](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7234261/).

Domestic ferrets tested in a cross maze were more likely to take a shortcut they had seen during
training; with an unseen shortcut and hidden goal, shortcut use did not exceed chance. The paper
also reports strong exploration and individual variation. Familiar and unfamiliar passages thus
condition later conduct without licensing the claim that the animal stores a complete route graph:
[Shortcut taking by ferrets](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/7fs2s98h).

The existing flock derivation supplies the same project boundary: a traveler need not possess a
destination coordinate. A local landing changes the shared world and becomes terrain for later
placement. None of these species comparisons installs a search fan, dead-end registry, reward
scalar, or cognitive-map module inside Soma.

## 9. Consequence for probability, loss, and learning

Probability can quotient the recurrence of comparable open boundaries and later fillings from one
receiver's filtration. It does not decide which route the event must take and does not convert an
unseen relation into impossibility.

Loss is the complete open comparison or its lawful receiver projection. It says how actual and
expected/admitted transport failed to commute. A scalar loss may guide an explicit update law, but
the base event deposits the residual without reward, punishment, truth, or automatic correction.

Learning has two different events:

```text
event t:      attempted comparison fails to commute -> OPEN boundary emanates,
later event:  new Current meets that Standing -> RIDE, FOUND, another OPEN, or departure.
```

The first event may make a later search more local because an irrelevant route is now situated
terrain. It cannot know in advance whether that route will remain irrelevant. The second event is
where a bridge may actually be founded, an antecedent refined, an analogy carried, or the former
opening rebased into another question.
