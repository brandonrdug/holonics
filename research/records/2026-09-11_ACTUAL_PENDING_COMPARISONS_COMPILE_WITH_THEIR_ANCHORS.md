`g(θ,h) − Rᵀ b = 0`, with `g=(x(θ),h,x(θ)⊗ℂh,η(θ))`,

and the separate producing-condition equality `h−h_s=0`. Here θ uses the original source
family's generator coordinates, η retains the actual observed return minus the source current,
R is the referenced contemporary member relation, and b is an existential **row witness**.
Complex products are represented in their existing real/imaginary chart. The original source
anchor additionally requires `||a(θ)−a₀|| ≤ radius`. None of those requirements can be replaced
by zero scalar residual alone.
