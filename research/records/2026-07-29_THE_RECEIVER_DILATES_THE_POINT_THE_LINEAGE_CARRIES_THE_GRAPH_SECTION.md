# THE RECEIVER DILATES THE POINT; THE LINEAGE CARRIES THE GRAPH SECTION

**Date:** 2026-07-29  
**Status:** COMPLETED CONSTRUCTION / MEASURED

## Exact question

Can the production receiver engine restrict an arbitrary exact causal incidence
complex into a contemporary receiver-local graph section, let that receiver
traverse and dilate through founded incidence and cross-grain quotient fibers,
and retain unchanged work through lineage without treating a cache, a screen
layout, or a camera as authority over the ecology?

The first closure must also make the resulting network efficiently inspectable.
It need not yet draw the graph. Any later raster, vector, or notation membrane
must consume the same exact section and may not reconstruct another topology.

## Ratified interpretation

The construction is not a conventional shader system. For the contemporary
event

\[
E_t=\Lambda_{F_t}(Q_t\otimes A_t\otimes L_t),
\]

one receiver at grain \(g\) receives a local section

\[
\Gamma_{\rho,t,g}
=
\operatorname{Res}_{\rho,g}\bigl(q_{t+1}(E_t),K_t\bigr).
\]

The future rendering law evaluates this section before a terminal membrane
chooses color, paths, glyphs, or notation. A receiver change is therefore a
carried ecological relation, not a free camera operation.

The existing `ReceiverHolonicComplexStanding` already supplies the decisive
cross-grain object. A `HolonicQuotient` receives a complete lower-grain closed
hull as one coarser point while preserving the entire source fiber. The new
work must expose that relation as receiver navigation instead of rebuilding
it as screen-space level of detail.

## Production construction

### 1. Exact sparse incidence atlas

One generic atlas over `GradedCausalComplex` shall retain:

- every exact caused cell and oriented boundary;
- the exact transposed coboundary relation;
- boundary-closed local-section traversal;
- source occurrence lineage; and
- a grading path back to the complete source complex.

The initial mount may inspect the complete supplied standing once. Receiver
queries must thereafter traverse the retained incidence relation from their
local roots rather than scan the complete ecology.

### 2. Receiver-local graph section

One section shall contain:

- receiver identity and receiver-event chronology;
- a local focus and receiver grain;
- the exact receiver horizon in incidence steps;
- every admitted cell, boundary, coface, witness, and source event;
- the complete lower source fiber of every quotient point;
- actual plural overlap cells;
- the consulted dependency frontier;
- the ordered receiver transition lineage; and
- exact analysis receipts.

The section is always boundary-closed. A visible crossing supplies no
incidence unless the source complex supplies the relevant boundary or overlap.

### 3. Receiver deeds

The first exact receiver deeds are:

- `Found`: receive one caused focus and its local incidence horizon;
- `Dilate`: change that horizon while retaining the same receiver focus;
- `Traverse`: carry the focus over a supplied, incidence-certified path;
- `Refine`: open one quotient point into its retained lower closed hull; and
- `Coarsen`: return that exact source apex to its caused quotient point.

A traversal path is accepted only when every consecutive pair is joined by
actual boundary incidence. Refinement and coarsening are accepted only through
an extant `HolonicQuotient`.

### 4. Lineage-carried retention

The receiver atlas shall classify one successor as founded, retained, dilated,
traversed, refined, coarsened, or source-advanced. It retains exact cell
sections shared by predecessor and successor and emits added and removed
support deltas.

This is observation standing, not a successor cache. It may be discarded and
reconstructed from the source. It never decides the source event and never
feeds a terminal result back into source standing without a later declared
world occurrence.

### 5. Efficient mathematical analysis

Each receiver section shall expose at least:

- dimension and exact \(f\)-vector;
- Euler characteristic;
- boundary-term population;
- connected components and graph-cycle rank where the grade-one boundary is
  genuinely graph-like;
- quotient-point and source-fiber populations;
- exact internal analytical topology populations;
- curved phase-cycle population;
- actual overlap population and maximum overlap order; and
- the open upper frontier through which a later dilation can continue.

The analysis is receiver-relative. It does not claim that one local section is
the whole source ecology.

## Acceptance

The construction is accepted only if:

1. sparse coboundary queries equal direct transposition of the complete source;
2. every returned section is boundary-closed;
3. traversal refuses a screen-like or nonincident jump;
4. refine followed by coarsen returns the exact caused quotient relation;
5. dilating outward and inward preserves the focus and emits exact support
   deltas;
6. a retained event performs no new section traversal while its occurrence
   remains in lineage;
7. a coarse point keeps its complete lower source fiber inspectable;
8. the local analysis agrees with direct calculation over the returned
   section;
9. rest/remount preserves the complete atlas exactly; and
10. a nontrivial existing whole-image receiver ecology can be queried without
    a terminal raster pass.

## Visualization boundary

No layout is authorized in this construction. The future membrane may render:

- each receiver section in its own local chart;
- quotient points that dilate into their exact source fibers;
- transport seams and residuals between receiver sections;
- exact Cartesian or algebraic coordinate foliations where a local coordinate
  algebra supplies them; and
- symbolic labels derived from the same analysis receipt.

It may not impose a global force layout, merge crossings by screen position,
or allow terminal tessellation to become standing.

## Completed production construction

`graph_receiver.rs` now owns the generic receiver law described above.

`ExactIncidenceAtlas` validates one arbitrary `GradedCausalComplex`, derives
its exact sparse coboundary transpose once, and returns boundary-closed local
sections by propagating from receiver roots. A local query records the roots,
fronts, coboundary lookups and terms, closure cells, and returned cells. The
hot receiver transition does not validate or scan the complete source atlas.
Complete validation remains an explicit grading/rest operation.

`HolonicGraphSourceAtlas` mounts either:

- the existing `ReceiverHolonicComplexStanding`, retaining every phase germ,
  transport, cycle, quotient, overlap, occurrence, and grain; or
- arbitrary exact algebraic grains and cross-grain quotients; or
- complete `CausalAlgebraicPresentation` values, retaining their evolution
  occurrences, local coordinate algebras, causal regions, opposed ends, and
  exact incidence together.

The latter bridge is decisive for later mathematical presentation. A
receiver section carries the exact intersection of its support with every
source-supplied causal region, distinguishes partial from complete arrival,
and restricts opposed-end chains without inventing coordinates from a graph
layout.

`ReceiverGraphAtlas` now enacts `Found`, `Dilate`, `Traverse`, `Refine`,
`Coarsen`, and `Retain`. Traversal refuses every consecutive pair lacking
actual boundary incidence. Refinement and coarsening require an extant
`HolonicQuotient`. Every transition retains its predecessor frame and event,
cause, exact added/removed/retained support, and complete receiver lineage.
A retained event performs zero new receiver traversal. Append-only source
radiation updates the sparse incidence atlas and reforms only sections whose
support, quotient fiber, or overlap relation the new material can reach.

Each section derives and self-validates:

- dimension, exact \(f\)-vector, and Euler characteristic;
- oriented boundary-term population;
- grade-zero connected components and graph cycle rank when grade-one
  incidence is genuinely an oriented graph;
- source occurrence, quotient, and complete source-fiber populations;
- internal analytic topology and curved-cycle populations;
- source-authored plural overlap relations;
- open upper-frontier cells; and
- local coordinate algebra, causal-region, and opposed-end populations.

The complete graph atlas and every receiver section rest/remount through RON
without changing a value.
