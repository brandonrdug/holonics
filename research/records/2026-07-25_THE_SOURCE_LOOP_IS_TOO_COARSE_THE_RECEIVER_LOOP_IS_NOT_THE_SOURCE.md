## Present question

The work did not seek a Four Color Theorem application. That theorem supplied
one useful connection among planar faces, triangular duals, and finite
junction states, but it neither scheduled nor governed the construction.

The actual question was what recurrence belongs to one source, what
recurrence is introduced by a receiver's projection, and whether either can
stand as the causal identity of the current.

The standing multi-receiver atlas had already established:

- one actual source occurrence gives the joint receiver tuple;
- projective swing may remain common while metric and crossing faces differ;
- one source change reaches every exact face even where finite grain remains
  dark; and
- tolerance belongs to the joint consequence rather than one screen.

It had not yet recovered the complete planar incidence and closed paths
presented to each receiver.

## 1. Typed topology

For one source event \(X_e\) and receiver \(r\), the construction now retains
three objects:

\[
G_X,\qquad D_r(X),\qquad G_r^\ast.
\]

\(G_X\) contains only declared source vertices and branches. \(D_r(X)\) is
the planarized receiver diagram. A regular visible crossing becomes a
diagram vertex carrying its two source branches, over/under order, and
orientation, but it does not become a source vertex. \(G_r^\ast\) is the
dual of the oriented faces recovered from the diagram.

Outgoing projected rays are cyclically ordered using exact half-plane and
determinant comparisons. No angle is evaluated. The resulting dart
permutation recovers every oriented face boundary. Collinear overlap,
unrelated endpoint coincidence, equal depth, collapsed source vertices, and
multiple crossings at one projected point remain typed discriminants.

## 2. Exact primitive recurrence

For each loop graph the construction computes

\[
Z_G(u)^{-1}=\det(I-uB),
\]

through the equivalent exact Ihara--Bass determinant, where \(B\) is the
non-backtracking dart transition. It also returns the oriented primitive
cycle counts from exact traces of \(B^k\). All coefficients are integers.

This instrument counts recurrence. It does not define identity.

## 3. Bounded result

The exact tetrahedral source \(K_4\) was observed through:

1. a direct receiver;
2. a Cayley-precessed receiver with the source unchanged; and
3. the same precessed receiver after moving only one source height from
   \(1\) to \(1/4\).

The direct receiver has no crossing and a four-face dual. The precessed
receiver adds one apparent \(AC/BD\) crossing and presents a five-face dual
with a different Ihara polynomial and different primitive-loop population.
No source incidence changed.

The later source emanation removes that crossing. Its receiver-dual
polynomial returns exactly to the direct receiver's polynomial, while:

\[
\operatorname{Vol}:16\to4,
\]

and

\[
|AD|^2:3\to\frac{33}{16},\qquad
|BD|^2=|CD|^2:11\to\frac{161}{16}.
\]

## 4. Consequence

The source incidence zeta is lawful but too coarse. It is receiver-invariant
because the receiver does not change source incidence, but it is also blind
to every geometry-preserving incidence.

The raw receiver-dual zeta is lawful testimony but not source identity. It
can change under receiver precession alone and can recur at a later
nonidentical source geometry.

The result therefore rules out both shortcuts:

\[
Z_{G_X}\ne\operatorname{Soul}(X_e),
\qquad
Z_{G_r^\ast}\ne\operatorname{Soul}(X_e).
\]

The required loop carrier must be joint and decorated. At minimum it owes:

- the marked source occurrence and native chronology;
- the participating receiver transition;
- the exact crossing, swing, metric, or other selected geometric weight;
- transport around the loop; and
- the returned holonomy or residual.

A twisted or weighted product is a plausible mathematical species,

\[
\mathcal Z_{\mathcal J,\rho}(u)
=
\prod_{[P]}
\det\!\left(
I-\rho(\operatorname{Hol}_{\mathcal J}P)\,W(P)\,u^{\ell(P)}
\right)^{-1},
\]

but \(\rho\), \(W\), the path population, and covariance through
discriminants remain open derivations. This formula has not been installed
as an Eros law.

## 5. RH jurisdiction

The construction does not assert that a graph zeta is the Riemann zeta
function. It establishes the exact information a candidate cannot discard.
An RH-bearing specialization would additionally owe:

1. a trace or determinant identity for the completed zeta test currents;
2. an exact relation between primitive transported loops and prime Euler

3. compatibility with the Archimedean completion already carried in the RH

4. a positive evaluation or equivalent spectral statement on the complete
   joint carrier.
