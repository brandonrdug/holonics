## VII. What can honestly be called space-time

The existing construction already has several space-time-like constituents:

That is enough for a **curved causal-body representation** and for arbitrary simulation worlds to

It is not yet a derivation of physical general relativity. A literal relativistic space-time world
would additionally owe:

1. a Lorentzian interval or equivalent causal metric at the declared grain;
2. a connection compatible with that structure;
3. a mapping from world matter/current to stress-energy or another declared source;
4. dynamical field equations or a discrete action/constraint system;
5. gauge and boundary conditions; and
6. convergence or invariance evidence showing that the result is not a rendering artifact.

A 3+1 external renderer may show a sequence of curved three-geometries, much as the ADM
formulation foliates a suitable relativistic space-time into spatial hypersurfaces. But the
renderer's sequence is not automatically an ADM solution: lapse, shift, constraints, and Einstein
dynamics would still be owed.

Regge calculus supplies a close discrete bridge. Piecewise-flat cells carry curvature at hinges
through deficit angles. Soma's causal faces, exact transported rotors, and winding have the right
shape for such a construction, but no exact map from Soma cell data to Regge edge lengths,
dihedral angles, action, or Einstein equations has been established. The relation remains an open
bridge, not an imported physics claim.

Fluid analogue gravity supplies another useful boundary. A moving fluid can give perturbations an
effective horizon and metric. That proves a fluid medium can represent causal geometry for waves;
it does not prove that physical space-time is literally a fluid or that every curved Soma render
has gravitational meaning.

The word **fabric** is therefore lawful as a receiver image for the integrated causal body. Its
physical content is whatever intrinsic transport and chosen world dynamics remain invariant when
the display embedding changes.
