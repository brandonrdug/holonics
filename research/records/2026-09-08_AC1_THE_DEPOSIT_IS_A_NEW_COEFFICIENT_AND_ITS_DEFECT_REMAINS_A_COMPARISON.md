# AC1: the deposit is a new coefficient; its defect remains a comparison

[definition] This corrects the numerical-realization assumption behind the
[continuing enclosure failure](2026-09-08_AC1_THE_PAIRED_RETURN_CHANGES_CONTACTS_AND_THE_NEXT_CURRENT.md).
It changes the declared realization of a new contact deposit. It does not claim to make an
uncertain source, material current or unrounded response exact.

## Recovered authority and existing implementation

[established-bounded; source-inspected] The standing [architecture](../../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md)
states: “Finite native arithmetic is exact about its declared representation.” It explicitly
retains the enclosure/quotient boundaries of transcendental reactions and midpoint projections.
The existing HNN owners already implement that distinction:

  dyadic factors after a declared projection, with the pre-projection interval width in its receipt.
- `operative_backward.rs::seal_returned` uses the declared successor projection instead of
  carrying all earlier interval wrapping into each later differential.
- `full_operation.rs` and `operative_segment.rs` retain that numerical realization in the
  native recurrence; this is not a foreign executor or application-authored learner.
- The [September 7 derivation ruling](2026-09-07_AC1_DERIVATION_IS_PART_OF_THE_GENERATIVE_ATTACHMENT.md)
  distinguishes an arithmetic enclosure, compatible causes and an actual retained model current.

[definition] AC1 had instead treated every newly deposited coefficient as an approximation to
one continuing unrounded flow. Its covector enclosure became permanent map uncertainty, then
expanded through every later material/source return. The measured failure is real for that
realization. Requiring it for every finite native model was an overly broad agent inference.
This recovery changes no prohibition on native floats and supplies no unique causal inverse.

## The next receiver distinction

[proved-derived; computational-witness] The current text receiver reads nine bit-pair signs
independently. The [exact witness](../experiments/alpha_passive_junction/bit_marginal_receipt.json)
averages the existing `TextSymbol::inputs` chart for equally weighted `3`, `5`, and `6`.
Every marginal sign is strict, yet the decoded symbol is `7`, absent from that ensemble.
The equally weighted ensembles `{0,3}` and `{1,2}` also have identical bit-current means but
disjoint joint supports. This proves a limitation of that receiver statistic, not that the
whole native history lost the observations or that an HNN may never emit novel symbols.

[definition] A joint packet chart can retain the tensor-product structure of a bit packet:
`|word> = tensor_j |bit_j>`. Its coordinates are a codec chart, not semantic identity or native
topology. Source occurrences and phase/transport remain separate. A complete packet receiver
must distinguish the joint ensembles above; marginal certainty alone is insufficient. The
current material output dimension is tied to the root field's node count. Separating the
material receiver codomain from that root carrier is a concrete implementation obligation
before composing a broader joint-packet chart.
