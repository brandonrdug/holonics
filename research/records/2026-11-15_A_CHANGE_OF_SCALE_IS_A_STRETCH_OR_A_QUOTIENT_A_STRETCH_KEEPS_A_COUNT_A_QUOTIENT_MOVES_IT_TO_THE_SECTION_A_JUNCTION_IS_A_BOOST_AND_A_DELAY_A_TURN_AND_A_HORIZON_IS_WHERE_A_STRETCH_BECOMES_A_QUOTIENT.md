## 3. Two operations

`agent-inferred`: the lens names two operations in one breath, and they do opposite things to a
count.

### 3.1 A stretch keeps a count

A stretch is a change of chart. On a conjugate pair it is the Swing's boost `diag(λ, λ⁻¹)`:
extent rises by `λ`, its conjugate falls by `λ`, and the area is kept.

`tree`, with one line of algebra. The counts the tree holds are areas on such a pair:

- a mode's count `E/(ħω)`, kept by a slow change of `ω`;
- the store's floor, nats at most `2πER/(ħc)`, kept by `(R, E) ↦ (λR, E/λ)`;
- the rate's floor over a duration, state changes at most `2Et/(πħ)`, kept by
  `(t, E) ↦ (λt, E/λ)`;
- a strand's code `S`, kept when its places go to `n/λ` and its nats a place to `λ` times.

A uniform stretch of a locale takes every length and every period to `λ` times itself. Where the
stress has no trace it takes every energy to `1/λ` and is a symmetry. So under a stretch of a
locale with no trace both floors and every mode's count stand where they stood. This is the law
the lens asks for: what a region can hold and what can happen in it are counted by action in
units of `ħ`, and extent enters an action only paired with its conjugate. No volume appears.

`from training` and `tree`: the trace is a mass. A rest energy does not fall under the stretch.
Along `R ↦ λR` for a body of mass `m` the store's floor rises in proportion to `R` once `R` is
well above `ħ/(mc)`, and below that length the energy that holds the body there, of order `ħc/R`,
keeps the floor of order one turn. At `R = ħ/(mc)` with the rest energy the floor is `2π` nats
(receipt, `COMPTON`). A mass marks a place on the axis of scale. A ruler is a gap, and nothing
else is one.

Two limits (`from training`; the tree's rows state both in their scopes):

- **Slow only.** A stretch at a rate near twice a mode's own squeezes that mode's own plane, the
  parametron's boost, and a mode at rest gains `sinh² r` counts for a squeeze `r` (Parker 1968 for
  an expanding space; the algebra is a pumped ring's). Turns keep every count, slow boosts keep
  it, and a fast boost changes it by a hyperbolic law.
- **A count is a view.** Two receivers whose clocks differ by a boost that grows without bound
  across a section disagree on the count of quanta (§8). What every receiver keeps is the
  pairing.

### 3.2 A quotient moves a count to the section

A quotient is a restriction of the tower: an interior eliminated at a boundary.

`Λ(s) = A + sC_B − B(D + sC_I)⁻¹B*`. Its static part is `Λ(0)`, the capacity the boundary
inherits is `Λ′(0) = E*CE`, and the interior's thermal volume enters the free energy as

`agent-inferred`, one line on those rows: at every `s` the interior's part

## 6. A junction between levels is a boost, and a delay is a turn

The tree holds a junction's reflection and, in one chart, a radix as a step `b` of a level's
clock and impedance. This section is algebra on those rows with one standard theorem; the receipt
checks each line.

`proved-derived`. On the pair (forward wave, backward wave), each at unit power, a junction from
`Z` to `bZ` acts as

`M = [[cosh η, sinh η], [sinh η, cosh η]]`, `η = ½ ln b`.

Its reflection is `tanh η = (b − 1)/(b + 1)` and its pass is `sech² η = 4b/(b + 1)²`. A delay
`φ` between two junctions is `diag(e^(iφ), e^(−iφ))`. Each has determinant one and keeps
`|M₁₁|² − |M₁₂|²`, the flux, and the pass of a word is `1/|M₁₁|²`. So a chain of levels in this
chart is a word of the Swing's two kinds: a step of scale is a boost of rapidity half the
logarithm of the step, and a delay is a turn (`from training`: the transfer matrices of layered
media; `tree`: `motion.iwasawa-sl2` in its other chart).

Four consequences for `k` equal steps across a whole ratio `ρ` (receipt, `CHAIN` and `DELAYS`):

1. **No delay.** Rapidities add, and the chain is one step of the whole ratio. It passes
   `sech²(½ ln ρ)`, about `4/ρ`. A level between two others that adds no delay buys nothing.
2. **A quarter turn.** Two equal steps a quarter turn apart pass everything:
   `B(η)·diag(i, −i)·B(η) = diag(i, −i)`. Pairs cancel, so an even count passes all at that
   pitch and an odd count leaves one step.
3. **Unordered delays.** The mean of `ln(pass)` over a delay spread evenly on the turn is
   additive, since the mean of `ln|1 − re^(iθ)|²` over the turn is zero for `r < 1` (Jensen's
   formula). The chain then passes `sech^(2k)(ln ρ/(2k))` in that mean, which stands above
   `exp(−(ln ρ)²/(4k))` and tends to it.
4. **Powers summed.** With every re-reflection counted and no phase kept, `(1 − T)/T` adds and
   the chain passes `1/(1 + k sinh²(ln ρ/(2k)))`.

Across nine decades one step passes `4 × 10^(−9)`. Thirty steps of two pass 0.030 in the mean
over delays and 0.21 with powers summed. Half passes at about 155 steps and about 107.

`agent-inferred`: the levels between two distant levels are a measuring stick only through
their delays. Ordered, they pass everything at their pitch. Unordered, they lose
`(ln ρ)²/(4k)` nats of pass: the square of the logarithmic distance over the count of levels.
With no delay they are not there. Scope: the one chart of `wave.wound-ladder-radix`, itself
`agent-inferred` with nothing built; lossless junctions; equal steps; one pitch.

1. **Depth is not skipped.** The depth of a thing is the running time of its shortest

   it faster than the process runs, and a random one does so with a weight that falls

2. **The rider is faster than its ground.** A level exists where one thing moves on another
