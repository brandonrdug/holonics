# The states a field trains on set the temperature it holds; a draw's states are no state of its own, and its own are its news's landmarks and its release's path

**Date.** November 15, 04:45Z. **Issues.** #404. **Grade.** A derivation and a design (`agent-inferred` throughout), with its guess
fixed before any build; nothing here is built.

## 1. What the loud lap measured

The loud face (the record of November 15 on it, §4 and §5) let the deposit set the readout's temperature from the code. It parked β
near 0.86, flatter than one, and the held one-place read lost 0.0023 nats a place while the whole masks kept theirs. The temperature
the deposit finds is the calibration of the states it trains on, and those states are drawn: each stretch's one state is keyed
(`passage::state_of`): half the time the writer's state, with `k` drawn by octaves and its order keyed within an octave; half the time a
mask whose density `τ = u²` and whose open places are keyed draws (`passage::shape`), weighed back to the order-averaged joint code
(`weighed_domain`).

## 2. Brandon's frame (02:54Z), on the objects

There is no true random and no outside view; cultivation never comes from a draw. On the objects: a keyed draw is a navigator the
field does not hold, and the states it picks are states no receiver of the field chose. The joint code they estimate is the average
over every order of resolution (`tower.field-joint-code`), a God's-eye average over orders the field never takes. What the field
meets when it writes is one order, its own.

## 3. The field's own states (`agent-inferred`)

Two forms, both already in the tree under other names.

- **The release's path.** The writer resolves places in an order (`tower.field-write-by-the-training-clock`: octaves of distance from the
  context, keyed within an octave). Read in the field's own order instead, by its news at each state, the release's states are the
  states the field meets when it writes. Their code is a valid description length (any order's chain is a code, `tower.field-joint-code`),
  and it is the length the field pays when it writes. Training on it lowers the cost of the field's own writing: self-induction
  (Brandon 03:00Z), the path read back through the deposit. Sampled at octaves of `k` (the counts 1, 2, 4, … of resolved places), the
  path keeps no scale privileged (Fable's records #823 and #825).
- **The news's landmarks.** The level already reads a stretch's news (`c_t`, the opening's code) and its sites, the places where no
  larger news stands within a section of their claim (`ρ_t ≥ σ`, `field/scale.rs`). Those are the ceilings the context breaks
  (Brandon 03:00Z; on lap 13 they broke 0.79 to 0.90 of an exchangeable count). A state that opens the landmarks and writes the rest
  trains the field where its own news lands, with no parameter but the flat code `σ`.

Both remove the draw. The first trains generation where generation goes; the second trains reading where reading is surprised.

## 5. The build

1. A declaration flag naming the states (the line's states when off, so a build reproduces the line with it off), carried in the state.
2. The release's path: for each stretch, the field's read of the stretch with its run open orders the run's places by their news
   (cheapest first, as the writer releases); the states at `k = 0, 1, 3, 7, …` resolved are its training regions, each weighed as one
   chain's link (no importance weight). One extra read a stretch, on the card.
3. Gate 3 with the flag off; one lap from lap 16's state against lap 17; the reads (cavity and loudness) and writes from its mean.

Recorded failures this design could repeat: recitation (training on the field's own path could lock onto its own output; the held read
and the writes' copy lengths are read beside, and a miss stops it); a guess on a settled premise (§4 reads the logs first); a law
patched after a miss (the loud face stays off, and this is a different object, the states, named as such).

### 5.1 What the build found (November 15)

- **At a crossing the order does not reach the state.** The writer releases by octaves of distance from its prompt (`octave_of(t + 1 − q)`
  in `release.rs`, the order within an octave keyed), so the release's state with `k = 2^o − 1` places resolved is the one whose first `o`
  octaves are whole and every farther place open, whatever order it keeps within an octave. Step 2's extra read, which ordered the run's
  places by their news, would change no state at a crossing, so the build makes no read and keeps no order: the own state is
  `holonics_engine::passage::own_state_of`. G199's words ("its order the field's news") stand as fixed; at the crossings they are carried by
  the release's octave order alone.
- **The crossing is the passage's clock.** `o` is the stretch's count in the pass modulo the run's crossings `⌊log₂ a⌋ + 1`, so every crossing
  is met in turn and no key is drawn for the state. The cut's lengths are still drawn by key (`lengths_of`): G199 is a test of the states,
  and the cut is the line's.
- **Every stretch's state is its own.** The line's mask states (half its states, `τ = u²` over the whole stretch or past the context, with
  their weighed domain) are not the release's states, and with the word set none is drawn. The held read's places with context on both
  sides (one place open, and the whole masks) are then states the field no longer trains: G199's first part reads what that costs.
- **The word is carried in the state.** The pass line takes the word `own` after its bounds, only where set
  (`StorePass::own`), so a state of the line reads and writes byte for byte as before and a build reproduces the line with it absent.
  `athena train complete-language own-states STATE STATE_SHA OUT` writes a state with it set, its place in the pass kept, every other line
  byte for byte. The held read builds its own pass without the word, so the read is the line's.

### 5.2 Brandon's lens of 04:37Z: a form is fixed by its listener

Brandon, answering the line that the line's states are "regions whose open places a keyed draw picks": there are many ways to write the
same thing; local optimizations are dialects; a form is elaborated or recombined as the listener needs, and a more efficient form exists
for a given listener, which is how programming languages and the finite cases of a proof work. On the objects: the order a stretch's places
are resolved in is a form of the same passage (every order's chain is a code of it, `tower.field-joint-code`), and the receiver that reads
the field fixes which form is efficient (a comparison is an agreement at a receiver, November 14). The keyed draw trains every form at once;
the own states train the one form the writer speaks. The held read is a different receiver, one place open with both sides known. So G199
reads this directly: its first part asks whether the writer's dialect is still read by the held receiver, its second whether the writer
is more fluent in it. A held read that rises while the writes hold longer is a mismatch of form and receiver, not a fault of the field,
and it would make each form's training belong to its receiver (`agent-inferred`).
