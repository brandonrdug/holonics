# Producing receiver continuation contract

[definition; agent-inferred, October 16, revised October 17] The producing receiver is the
arrived ratio's own ReceivingPhases. A successful native refine already binds those phases to
its PendingRatio. The ratio transfers unchanged to Arrived on comparison and survives a
restricting aeon close. The admitted future family is a separate operand: it may narrow or
become empty without invalidating this already produced accounting operand. No duplicate
receiver, second saved-bit charge or archive of former families is retained.

Passage version 3 marks F1: material learned under the absolute-lift phase read is not continued
under the passage-frame read. Version 2 and unversioned passage text remain refused with
ContinuingState; there is no inferred migration. The reader checks the arrived ratio's saved
phases against the field's observation partition, not against the later future family. Saved
phase structural checks still validate ring, field first epoch, nonzero aperture, overflow,
grain/tolerance consistency, finite rank bounds and rank scope. Existing address, source
capacity, mode view, producing commit and target checks remain. The future family retains its
own checks, without imposing a new current-family restriction on refine.

This is native producer consistency, not historical authentication. Historical rank is not
recomputed from contemporary material, and no snapshot-forgery protection is claimed.

No ledger endpoint is reset. Close keeps its existing reread/exchange and continuous endpoint;
no staged or pending handle is discarded beyond existing locus separator decisions. Host and
CUDA preserve the ratio and its phases through the same continuation owner. No model cuts,
source occurrences or released material are added to retention.

The continuation repair changes no deposition or first-law owner. The helical pair interaction
keeps its faces and placement, carried opening, tube and tower-thread operands. Refs #73 #76 #62.

## Acceptance controls

The resident_cold_restore integration target checks the post-close save with an empty future
family; pending across close before compare; staged survivor deposit; a narrower future family;
and repeated cold mounts preserving material, carry, current, address, family, handles and the
ledger balance. The staged control keeps sealed arrival counters and the continuous opening
while adding exactly one deposition. Version-2 and unversioned passage text remain refused.
The passage unit controls retain a native producer outside the future family, a wrong field
observation partition, an invalid ring and an invalid field epoch. There is no one-sided witness
control because there is no second copy. These controls run with the whole library and
integration suite.

Shared source and all-target typechecking do not establish CUDA runtime parity; its full suite
is a separate receipt at the delivery head.
