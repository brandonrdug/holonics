## Finding

The theory-set is not too large to state. The record is too duplicated and too chronological to
serve as routine context. A succession of application records caused the latest example to occupy
the whole frame. Local experimental exclusions were then copied into general vocabulary until they
looked like ontological prohibitions.

Vocabulary proliferation compounded the record problem. Terms coined for one derivation were made
mandatory in unrelated discussions, so an ordinary learning or I/O question accumulated several
interdisciplinary translations before it could be answered. The relational spine therefore needs
one small operational vocabulary; richer terms remain available only when they clarify the
selected relation.

The recurrent error was one inversion:

```text
this face is not the whole construction
                    became
this face may not lawfully participate.
```

That inversion affected source identities, coordinates, labels, targets, tokenizers, pretrained
models, parsers, mappings, loss, data, and existing technology. The valid guard is narrower:

```text
a local face does not identify the whole construction;
when that face is genuine source or world structure, it may participate with its origin declared.
```

## Compact operational vocabulary

- **Current:** material acting or presented now.
- **Standing:** prior relation still able to affect current.
- **Face / boundary:** what one declared receiver and grain can use or observe.
- **Identity / constraint:** locally declared continuity or afforded boundary.
- **Lineage:** consequential continuity through changing constituents, not a copied ancestry.
- **Learning:** an earlier relation actually participates in changed later conduct.
- **Completion / compression:** a lower construction becomes higher consequential relation while
  inactive lower interiors cease participating.
- **RIDE / FOUND / OPEN:** existing support conducts, new support is founded, or the boundary remains
  unresolved at that receiver and grain.

`receiver-relative` means only that the claim names the receiver, frame, and grain at which it is
valid. Hexis, field, chi, illicium, chemistry, biology, lightning, manifold, and transformer terms
remain optional analytical lenses.

## Repeated failure pattern

1. A present example was treated as the whole machine objective.
2. A clean control chosen for that example was restated as a laboratory-wide purity rule.
3. Lawful source structure was removed as `cheating` or `contamination`.
4. The now-impoverished fixture was asked to emit the very correspondence or action needed for its
   first learning event.
5. Its OPEN result was attributed to a universal missing mechanism.
6. More terminology, proof obligations, records, tests, or adapters accumulated around that false
   diagnosis.

This occurred with raw octets, transducers, response deeds, correctness gates, passive data, and
Euclidean roles. The repair is not another application-specific mechanism. It is to preserve
jurisdiction: experimental controls grade only their experiment; inherited structure remains
lawful; a supplied relation is distinguished from an acquired one by attribution rather than exile.
