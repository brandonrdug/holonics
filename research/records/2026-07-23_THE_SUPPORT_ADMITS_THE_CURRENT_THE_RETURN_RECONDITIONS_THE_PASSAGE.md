

## 4. Split, junction, circulation, and opening

The current law sharpens four previously separated relations.

### Split

A split occurs when one actual occurrence participates in several actual outgoing cofaces and
`C_e` gives more than one of those cofaces nonzero or otherwise material conduct. The branches are
co-present parts of one event solve against one predecessor, not serial false instants.

Support plurality does not force a split. It says that several continuations are admitted.
Conductance says which of them actually carry current at this event.

In the triangular chart, the triangle is therefore both a supported conduit and a parallel-path
comparison. It has no private scalar state. Contemporary context selects its participating
section, `C_e` conducts across its oriented sides, and an actual returned residual may change its
later conduct or replace its neighborhood through a hinge-preserving rewrite.

### Junction

A junction exists only over an actual shared interface. The support atlas supplies that gluing;
the current boundary supplies its oriented balance. Equal coordinates, labels, or visual
crossings do neither.

### Circulation

A nonzero current

```text

```

not mean silence. If

```text

```

that cut. A metric can later choose a harmonic representative; topology alone does not privilege
one representative.

### Opening

nontrivial homology class. The two notions can meet when a closed comparison has no admitted
filler, but they are not synonyms. In either case, the absence of a present filler is terrain: it
constrains later navigation without asserting that no future ecology can close it.
