## 1. The lens

The wording is this record's (`agent-inferred`); the reasons are his.

- A value is a word of bits: a chain of two-state places joined by carries, as a string is a chain of symbols, and the same thing can
  be done with a chain of booleans on a graph. Its length is a property of the information it holds. A width written into the engine
  as a number is precision set by the engineering, which is the arbitrary precision the precision law refuses. The declarations show
  the habit in their own type: every width of `width.rs` is a number under 64 held as a 32-bit constant.
- The limits an engineer raises against this (a shift is what a product costs, a device dispatches fixed words, a length a value costs
  a pointer) are form factors of a realization. They point at something physical, since dispatch and addresses cost, and they are no
  laws. Naming them as walls repeats the mistake.
- The mathematics that owns the question is number theory and cryptanalysis, the structure the repository reads in the zeta function,
  in Hodge classes, in the fluid equations and in the hardness of unique games. Precision that grows from inside the machine is
  natural growth, and its other name is tolerance.
- Refinement does not run on without end, as a child's "why" at every answer would. Two reasons: a conservation law never closes at
  an instant, so there is always a time differential, which is coarse graining; and patterns cancel where they meet (a saddle, a bond,
  a fusion; the reverse at a junction that splits), a conserved exchange. That cancellation is what the fundamental theorem of
  calculus and a series expansion run on.
- Transcendental constants are relations about causality, and their digits depend on the radix. There is no perfect circle, so most
  places need no deep precision. His hypothesis: a brain is efficient because it carries no excess precision; patterns intersect and
  hold tension about each other, and that tension is the cancellation, as in a knot.

## 4. The law

Nothing in §4.1 to §4.3 is new mathematics. It is the tree's own, stated once at the engine's consumer; the two statements the tree
lacked are in Lean (§11).

### 4.1 A word has two ends, and each has its own law

The top of a word grows by its carry. A sum of `n` terms below `2^w` is below `2^(w + ⌈log₂ n⌉)` and a product needs `w_a + w_b`
(`radix.width-need`, Lean `Mathematics/PlaceComplex`), so a top place is counted from the operation, or it is a carry that leaves the
word and founds the next place (number law N2). A top that is a bound chosen so a product fits 128 bits, with a refusal past it, is a
carrier's habit: the engine has 83 refusal sites, and the 13 carrier rows are those bounds.

The bottom of a word is a tolerance: the place below which nothing its receiver reads can tell two values apart. It is the kernel of
the receiver, which is what retention already is (the future-sufficient quotient). A bottom is therefore never a property of the
quantity alone, and a width has no meaning until its receiver is named. That is the content of "a size belongs to its part".

### 4.2 The bottom, read three ways

1. **From a declared tolerance, as a certificate at every place.** A face `p(a) = e^(z_a)/Σ_b e^(z_b)` whose every exponent is
   known to within `B` has every symbol's code within `2B` (Lean `face_tail_moves_code`). A receiver that asks a code tolerance `ε`
   therefore needs its exponents on a grain `L_R = ⌈2/ε⌉`, and a locus read through an operand of fan-in `X` needs the lattice
   `L = ⌈log₂(2 L_R X)⌉` (`remainder_below_every_tolerance`); through a chain of maps each locus takes its own share by its
   sensitivity (`hnn.word-sensitivity`). This is the October lattice rule, and it gives a width a part, from the graph.
2. **From the mean, to second order.** The mean code moves by the square of a perturbation (the certificate's
   `codeLength_add_le`; the grain lemma's `D < (ln 2/2)/L²`). A receiver that reads a mean over `N` places confirms about half of
   `log₂ N` places: 6.9 at the held passage's 43,470 places, 14.2 at `10⁹` readings, and a grain of `2^(−16)` is the coarsest so confirmed
   only at `1.2 · 10¹⁰` readings ([the arithmetic](receipts/2026-11-21_widths/grain.txt)).
3. **From the store's own jitter.** No store is at rest: a deposit at rate `2^(−r)` holds a standing spread, and a lap's end stands
   above its mean by that spread's heat (0.017 nats a place at `2^(−12)`, THE_ENGINE §11). A place under the spread carries no
   reading: rounding an entry to a grain `g` adds a variance `g²/12`, which is priced as the walk's own variance is. With the code's
   rise taken to second order, rounding every entry of a block at that block's own rms spread costs a twelfth of the block's share of
   the walk's heat where the walk is even over the entries, and two places finer costs a sixteenth of that (`agent-inferred`; §7
   reads it, with a quotient for the unevenness). The grain of a store's read part is then located by the machine: it is a counted
   number of places under the spread that each lap's end and mean already print.

The three agree in kind and differ in receiver. The first is a bound at every symbol of every place, the second and third are the
mean's. A design names which receiver it serves.

### 4.3 A store's word is a read part and a carried part

`Holonics/HNN/LatticeDeposit` states it: the entry is `value + r`, the value on the lattice and the remainder `r` under half a unit,
`−u/2 ≤ r < u/2` (`rem_bounds`); a deposit adds to `r`, the value moves only when `r` carries, and
`value + r + Σ released = value₀ + r₀ + Σ Δ` (`lattice_deposit_accounting`). The forward reads the value. The deposit integrates in
the remainder. The remainder's depth grows with the locus's own count of deposits, by the Elias gamma length of that count, so the
word's bottom is natural growth on the store's own clock and no number set from outside.

The engine today holds one word for both. Its `theta` is 32 bits on `2^(−24)` and the forward multiplies all of it; where the
remainder line is on, a second word carries 16 places more. The reads of §7 guess that 14 to 16 of the 24 places below the point
carry nothing the held code reads.

### 4.4 Why this decides timing and circulation

- **A register is a tower of clocks.** Place `k` turns once for every `2^k` grains of flux through the word, and a carry is its
  section crossing. A release that drops its remainder silences every flux slower than half a grain a step, however long it runs
  (`plain_rounding_silences`); carried, the released sum stays within a grain of the true sum and a constant rate is kept at any
  size (`release_tracks`, `release_keeps_constant_rate`). So a grain chosen by hand decides which slow fluxes exist in the machine
  when the remainder is dropped, and when they arrive when it is carried. The engine's moment step shows the first case patched in
  place: `toward` moves a moment "at least one grain when that release rounds to zero", a forced step standing where a carried
  remainder belongs, after a measured stall (its own text: 980 weights by pass 3, October 23).
- **The deposit's own step.** `((m << 24) >> rate) / root` releases to zero where `|m/root| < 2^(rate − 24)`. At the line's rate
  `2^(−12)` that silences about a thousandth of the entries a section; at a cooled rate of `2^(−20)` about a fifth
  (computed from the printed mean `|m|/√v` of 0.19; §7 reads it). The cooling law the engine guide asks for meets the material's
  grain at about `2^(−18)`, which is why the remainder line had to be added beside `set-rate`. With the remainder carried by law, a
  grain sets a delay and never a floor.
- **What circulates between carries.** A forward that reads the read part alone sees a constitution that changes only at carries.
  The carries are events on each store's own clock, their rate by block is a reading of where learning moves, and the walk under the
  tolerance never enters a product. The lap mean is taken today to shed that walk after the fact (lap rule 5).
- **The card.** A kernel that streams ends no sooner than its octets over the bus's rate, and a multiprocessor's resident threads
  share one register file: 1,536 threads at 42 registers or fewer, 1,024 at 64, 512 at 128 (`holarchy.register-file-resident-threads`).
  The tiled maps were read on November 19 at 124 to 128 registers a thread, filling 0.30 of the thread positions. A product of two
  words of `ℓ` limbs costs `ℓ²` limb products. Each of these is a capacity that a narrower read part buys; none is a promised rate
  (that row's own read: three blocks a multiprocessor ran at 0.996 of the control's time), so each is taken as a realization with
  its time read in one hold.

### 4.5 Precision by intersection, and by cancellation

Two residues on coprime rings of `m` and `n` places pin a state on the ring of `m n`, and a sum is formed channel by channel with no
carry between the channels (Lean `channels_add`; the helical code's coprime frames meet once a residue pair, modulo their product, by
this law). Resolution multiplies
across channels that meet while each stays as narrow as its own ring. This is the lens's "patterns intersect": depth is bought by the
count of narrow readers that agree, and a carry chain is the one reader that buys it by length. The October 28 record gives the
other half: where two fibres meet at a contact, a cancellation of `k` leading places consumes `k` places of relative depth and
leaves the absolute fibre where it was, and a receiver reads the image `R H` of the joint fibre, so directions in its kernel cancel
there and need no places at all. A width fixed for each scalar apart cannot see either.

### 4.6 Why refinement halts

A refinement ends for the two reasons the lens gives, and each is a statement the tree holds. The receiver's count of readings is
finite, so two media in one grain cell cannot be told apart by it (the grain lemma): the "why" ends at the admitted future, the time
differential of a conservation law that closes over a span. And a tail that cancels is released: a series whose terms shrink, a tick
that contracts, a word read from its top down stops at the first place where twice the tail's bound is under the tolerance
(`face_tail_within`). A constant such as π has no width of its own: it is read at the width its readers' cuts decide
(`radix.face-width-from-readers`, with Ziv's test for the few values that straddle a cut).

### 4.7 The "constraints" are a realization's

A length a scalar would cost a pointer each, and no law asks for one: a width belongs to a part (a block, a locus, a row), which is
where the lattice rule sets it and where the engine already reads its two counted carriers. A device that dispatches fixed words
realizes a narrow read part by a narrow pass with a short list of the values that did not decide (Ziv's test is that list for a
series; a carry is that list for a store). Whether a product runs on integer units, float units or bit planes is the realization's
choice of an encoding that keeps every bit, read from the vendor's documentation for the device and timed in one hold. None of it
bounds what the law may ask, and no limit is argued from a register's kind.

## 9. The engine changes, in order

The engine is the lead's: whether a part is built, its integers, its parity and its arm. Each item says what kind of change it is,
since a reading leaves the state's bytes, a realization keeps every bit with its parity, and a change of law runs as an arm beside
its control.

1. **E1, the reads** (readings): §7's seven, on saved states. They size everything below.
2. **E2, one owner for widths** (realization): restore `crates/holonics/src/number` at `d5ed4adc1` into `crates/engine/src/number`,
   declare `width.rs` through it, and write each literal as its expression (`REMAINDER_BITS = STEP_BITS − FACE_BITS`,
   `CONTACT_BITS = COVECTOR_BITS + FACE_BITS`). Literal falls from 8 to 0.
3. **E3, one declaration for host and kernel** (realization): the kernel's twins are written by the card crate's build script from
   the host's declaration; the two dead defines go; `CONTACT_BACK` takes its host name.
4. **E4, a release returns its remainder** (law, as an arm): `release` gives the part and the remainder, and `toward`'s forced grain
   is replaced by a remainder carried in the moment's own word.
5. **E5, the lattice rule on the constitution** (law, as an arm; then a realization): port `receiver_grain`, `finest_grain`,
   `lattice_exponent` and the lattice deposit; the forward takes each block's read part at the lattice the rule or the read gives and
   the deposit integrates under it. Once the arm holds, the card's operand narrows to the read part, with its parity and its time in
   one hold.
6. **E6, frames and series at their readers' cut** (realization where every face is kept, else an arm): one guard law for both
   series, Ziv's test for the values at a cut, frames at 28 or at what E1 gives.
7. **E7, a tick released by its residual** (law, as an arm): the record of November 20, §8, with `face_tail_within` as its bound.
8. **E8, tops that grow** (law): a carrier's refusal becomes a founded place where the carry is the law's, or a counted bound.
9. **E9, the face in its code chart, and lanes of unequal width** (realization, then a founding): §5's row on shares; a lane's width
   read from its travel (the record of November 14).

E2 to E4 are steps 1 to 3 of the build order of November 8 (the host number owner, the kernel's twins generated from one
declaration, the remainders returned at the release sites: it counted 194 on the host and 169 on the card), and the gate check of
§10 is that order's step 7, its lint. The order is taken up as it stood; E5 adds the lattice rule it did not name.

Resemblances named: E5 is a coarse forward over a fine accumulator, which the classical practice of training at a low width knows,
and it is the October lattice deposit; E4 is error feedback (`release.transported-residue`). Neither stands in for learning: each
changes where a word is read, and the deposit's law is untouched.

## 10. The rule that would have caught it, and what now runs

The guide already holds the rule in words: a width is never a dial, a width picked by hand is arbitrary precision, no unjustified
literal enters a law, a guard that is not run does not hold, and a rule nothing runs does not hold. What was missing is the last of
these applied to the first three. Two changes, and no new prose rule beyond a clause:

- **Gate 1 reads the widths.** `tools/width_census.py` refuses a declared width with no row, a row with no need, a twin that differs
  from its host, and any rise in the counts of picked, carrier, literal and dead widths or of a crate's literal shifts. The counts
  may only fall. A new width enters with its class and its need, or the gate fails. `tools/test_width_census.py` plants ten
  violations and a clean tree and runs in gate 1 beside it.
- **A retirement carries its plan's remaining steps.** An owner that a plan's build order still names is retired with those steps
  moved to an issue with a done condition in the same change (the clause added to "Ownership and retirement"). Reason: on November 15
  the number owner left the tree as unconsumed while the build order of November 8 still named it as its first step, the lattice
  rule left with it, and nothing showed that the order had lost its base.

The campaign has its issue, #1088, with nine boxes; the first is this change.
