plural event correctly crosses the body once rather than becoming several false instants. Passing

than restored information.

The actual missing relation was between co-present live currents.

In the direct machine, every `CurrentEvent` is already one physical incidence at the contemporary
cut. It names one live current, borrows that event's ordered relation population, and carries its
one action. A second incidence ledger would detach the occurrence from the current which is
actually living and would recreate the old cut apparatus. What was absent was the source-supplied
causal hand between those incidences.

The minimum native event is therefore:

```text

                   + borrowed DirectedCurrentRelation population.
```

No pairwise closure is inferred. No ordinal, buffer order, or hardware order becomes causality.
The endpoints are live `CurrentLineage` capabilities. Repeating an actual directed row preserves
multiplicity; omitting a row creates no contact.
