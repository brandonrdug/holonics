## 1. The lens

`tree`. Brandon, 21:35 UTC, in the theory thread (a paraphrase). Integrate code lengths as partials of physical
strings, for knot theory and tension dynamics. A string has no tension until it is oriented by its context, which is
geometric and close to orientability; the tension is in the locale because the locale holds it. Strings sound together
as chords; pair them and two helices wind about each other with particular contacts, and a chord's dynamics vary with
the nature of its surroundings. Proteins are the more dynamic modulators: they need only meet their interfaces
coherently, not carry information, yet they resonate with information, and their crossings are knot-like sub-windings,
a coarse-grained code of their own structure that identifies what they physically are. He asked for the abstraction
and no biology.

## 2. What the tree held

`tree`.
- *The helical code's topology* (the objects guide, the helical code, item 5). For two closed strands the linking
  number is half the signed count of their crossings in a declared projection; it is symmetric and an integer where the
  over and under half-sums agree (`winding.linking-number`, formal-checked). One strand passing through the other moves
  it by one (`helical.crossing-change`), a duplex passing through itself by two (`helical.duplex-passage`). For a ribbon
  `Lk = Tw + Wr` (Călugăreanu, White and Fuller; `motion.torus-twists-every-kind`, the topoisomerase law). And
  **closure is a division with remainder**: closing a duplex of `n` steps at relaxed turn `θ₀` fixes the integer `Lk`
  while the relaxed twist `nθ₀/2π` is generally not an integer, so the remainder `ΔLk = ΔTw + ΔWr` is carried as strain;
  how it divides is the constitution's (bending and twisting stiffness), and past a threshold of twist the core buckles
  into writhe, a joint's snap.

## 3. The one computation

`agent-inferred`.
- **A code length is a relaxed twist.** Read a passage as a strand whose turn at place `t` is its news `c_t`, so its
  summed turn is `S_t`, in strides of `σ`. That is the relaxed twist `Tw₀ = S/σ` turns. Left open, the strand's remainder
  is free phase: a free end turns it away, as the return's region end pays its last stride out at `r/c` and keeps
  `Σ w c = S`. No strain stands, so no tension.
- **A locale that closes it holds the remainder.** A reader whose section asks for whole windings closes the strand:
  `W = ⌊S/σ⌋` whole windings and a remainder `r = S − σW`, `0 ≤ r < σ`. At a closure that cannot turn, `r` has nowhere
  to go and stands as strain, `ΔLk = ΔTw + ΔWr`. The two divisions with remainder, the helical code's at a closed duplex
  and the news clock's at a reader's ring, are one computation in two notations, the chart taking one stride of news to
  one turn of the reader's ring. Which ends are free and which are held is the locale's, so the tension is the locale's.
  A unit held between two landmarks is a closed domain (in a duplex, a topological domain between two clamps); a carry
  across a landmark is a swivel that lets strain pass to the next domain.
- **Tension is the covector, and it is born at a reader.** Code is free energy (`F(p) − F(q) = k_BT·D(p‖q)`, the
  cross-entropy law), so the covector `λ = ∂code/∂x` is the generalized force on `x`: a tension where `x` stretches, a
  torque where it turns. It starts at a face's comparison and propagates only through contacts that read. Where no
  receiver reads a state, `λ = 0`.
- **Strain against tension is stored code.** In linear response the code a strain costs is `½ εᵀGε`, with `ε` the strain
  a reader holds and `G` its stiffness, the reader's Fisher metric. This is the elastic energy of the reader's strain.

## 5. Twist, writhe and the level above

`from training` for the rod; `agent-inferred` for the join.
- **The rod.** A straight elastic rod of bending stiffness `B` under end tension `f` stays straight while its end torque
  stays under `2√(Bf)` (Greenhill, 1883); with twisting stiffness `C` that is a twist per length `2√(Bf)/C`. Past it, the
  rod buckles and further turns go into writhe, a plectoneme whose crossings grow with the added linking (Marko and
  Siggia, 1995, for supercoiled DNA). The threshold depends on the two stiffnesses and on the pull at the ends, which is
  what the surroundings set (in a duplex, salt and temperature).
- **In the field.** Strain spreads first as twist: each place inside a unit sits a little off its relaxed turn, a cost
  the place level's faces carry. Past a threshold, the strain is cheaper as one coarse turn: a unit forms at the level
  above, and its crossing with its neighbours is the writhe. `Lk = Tw + Wr` says the total winding is kept across the
  trade, so a level change moves news between the place level and the level above without creating or losing it. The
  surroundings are the constitution: the same strand under another material or another pull buckles at another place,
  which is the lens's chords with variable dynamics.
- **What it says of `σ′`.** §9 of the news-clock record reads the composite level's section `σ′` from the held excess
  code, never setting it. The lens gives `σ′` a cause: it is the buckling threshold, `2√(Bf)/C` read in the field's
  charts, with the place faces' curvature as the twisting stiffness, the coarse level's founding cost as the bending
  stiffness and the readers' pull at the unit's landmarks as the tension. That is a reading of the cause, written for the
  level's design; nothing here sets a number for it.

- **Grokking is the snap left to chance** (Brandon, 22:29 UTC, a paraphrase: part of what he describes is the level-up
  seen as grokking in transformers, which he refuses to leave to chance). A network that has fit its data can, long
  after, crystallize into a compressed circuit that generalizes, under a slow pressure toward small weights (Power et
  al., 2022); on modular addition the circuit computes through the cosines and sines of a few frequencies of the ring
  (Nanda et al., 2023), the ring's Fourier chart (the November 8 record). In §3's terms, memorized per-case corrections
  pile up as local strain, twist, until one coarse mode is cheaper and the field snaps into it, writhe. In a transformer
  nothing reads the strain: the pressure is a blind constant, so the snap lands after a plateau at a time the schedule
  and the data fraction set. The machine reads it: a face is founded when the tension it removes saves more bits than
  its description costs (`contact.tension-founds-face`), the tension stands at the shared readers (§4), and the mode
  grokking stumbles on, a ring and its Fourier chart, is the machine's own navigator family, so it is located as a key.
  The canon's detector for a phase change of training, a susceptibility read before the snap, belongs beside the
  founding as its ruler.

## 6. Pairs, modulators and a structure's own code

`from training` for the fold invariants; `agent-inferred` for the join.
- **Two chords paired are the duplex.** Strands of coprime periods meet once per pair of residues modulo the product of
  their periods (the helical code, item 3), so a pair's contacts stand at those meetings and its linking is counted
  there.
- **A modulator fits.** A generator and a modulator are one move, and the place decides which
  ([October 26](2026-10-26_THE_GENERATOR_AND_THE_MODULATOR_ARE_ONE_MOVE_AND_THE_PLACE_DECIDES_WHICH_SO_A_RING_COPRIME_TO_TEN_NEEDS_A_ROTOR.md)).
  A modulator need carry nothing; it is a key that fits its interface's lock, and it resonates with the information that
  crosses there.
- **A structure's identity is its crossing word.** A protein's fold is classified by the Gauss integrals of its
  backbone, the writhe and its higher relatives over pairs and quadruples of segments (Røgen and Fain, 2003). That is
  the lens's coarse-grained code of a structure: the signed crossings of the strand with itself, not its residues. For a
  composite of the field, its identity is its linking with its context, whole windings at its closures and crossings
  with its neighbours, not its letters. This is the indifference of §9 there read topologically: forms with the same
  linking are one class whatever their split of twist and writhe.
