## 2. The ideal

A passage is one relatively complete region read at every scale of the scale square at once. Bytes compose into packets,
packets into larger packets, and each packet is what its interior must hand its exterior: the **retention quotient of its
interior** (the retention law, read across scale; a Markov blanket in Beck and Ramstead's sense, the boundary through which
interior and exterior meet). Contacts at each level read every other site of that level by content and by place, so meaning
is the friction of packets against packets (Brandon's lens) and spelling is the friction of bytes inside a packet. The levels
are fields joined by restriction and prolongation, the tower's gluing: a level's restriction to the one above must glue with
what the level above prolongs back. The answer is refined at every level together, never coarse first. Width at each level
is a set of lanes, each with its own normalization and clock, and a level grows a lane when its code stalls. When a level
has spent its scale, the tower grows a level.

## 3. The decisions

**D1. The tower of scales.** Levels `ℓ = 0, 1, …`; level 0 is today's field on the bytes, level `ℓ + 1` a field on packets
of `P` sites of level `ℓ`. The first build has two levels with `P = 6`.
- *Why 6:* it is `2 · 3`, the first period the solenoid's two coprime towers share, so a packet's place within it is a
  residue on both towers at once (`ℤ/6 ≅ ℤ/2 × ℤ/3`); and it is the terrain's clock (a word and its space measure 5.4 bytes,
  the space indicator's spectrum peaks at 4.72). No list of words, no boundary rule and no merge table enters: the period is
  the rings' own (no stores, no catered machinery).
- *Why fixed periods first, and the landmark tower after:* the ideal packet boundary is a landmark, a face where the
  navigator's paths converge (after a word's last letter the next state no longer depends on which word it was). Under the
  training masks most places are open, where no landmark is defined, and at every density a fixed packet exists. The fixed
  tower is the landmark tower in the limit where every sixth place is a landmark; landmark packets follow once the fixed
  tower's first reading lands.
- *The law, with `r̂` the chart (`ratio_to_power`) and `j` a packet:*

```text
down     K₀/2 ticks of level 0 (today's tick: contact, current, pair contact)
restrict s_j = Σ_(k<P) A_k r̂(x_(Pj+k)) + b_A                    each place of a packet its own map: the packet's interior chart
level 1  x¹_j ← s_j, then K₁ ticks of level 1's own constitution  its own rings, on the packet strand's reach
prolong  x_(Pj+k) += B_k r̂(x¹_j) + b_B                          each place its own map back from its packet
up       K₀/2 ticks of level 0, then the face at the bytes
```

- *The covector* returns through the prolongation into level 1, through level 1's ticks, and through the restriction into
  the down ticks: one comparison, the joint code at the bytes, trains every level. No level has an objective of its own.
- *Founding as growth in scale.* `B` and `b_B` open at zero, so a tower founded on a trained level 0 reads every face of
  that level exactly (the growth law's invariant), and `A` is a keyed draw on `±√(3/(P·D₀))`. The tower is grown from
  today's trained state, never founded from nothing.
- *Sizes.* Level 1 meets `(n/P)²` site pairs against level 0's `n²`, a thirty-sixth, and the card's time follows the pairs
  (§16.29): level 1 takes `D₁ = 128`, `H₁ = 8` heads, `K₁ = 4` ticks, which together cost under a quarter of level 0's contact
  time. A question 300 bytes back is 50 packets back at level 1, three octaves nearer under the participation prior.

**D2. Width is lanes.** A level's width is a sum of lanes (Brandon's branch registers), each lane normalized on its own
coordinates (`ratio_to_power` per lane), its charts reading every lane. A growth adds one fresh lane to the level whose
withheld code stalls: its opening rows drawn, every read from it founded at zero, so every face is kept and nothing has to
part from a copy. Growth by copies retires when lanes land.

**D3. Growth in scale.** When the top level's withheld code stalls after its lanes have grown once, a level is founded above
it (D1's founding). The tower's height is the trained body's, as its width is.

**D4. The writer refines every level together.** The writer's reads are the tower's reads, so each draw at the bytes is made
with the packets' current state, and each refinement pass reopens bytes whose packets are then read again. No level is
written first.

**D5. Readings are typed densities.** A receipt states nats (the comparison's natural unit; bits beside them for history)
per place, per packet and per crossing of a packet's boundary, each with its level, region and tick; the code read at each
level's grain across levels gives the passage's information dimension (how the code scales with resolution, the fractal
reading Brandon asked for); and a per-tick reading of each level's expansion (how a perturbation grows across the
constitution's ticks, the discrete Lyapunov reading).

**D6. The plateau is the learner's.** The copy's onset waits on about 8,000 steps whatever the distance; the plateau read
(§16.37) decides which founding or learner term shortens it, and that term enters every level.

## 4. Acceptance, fixed before the build

The tower grown on a trained level 0 (run38's last state) and read on the card against the same state continued alone, at
equal card time:
1. the held answers' joint code at least 0.05 bits a byte lower than the single level's after the same wall time;
2. the question's information at least as high;
3. the written answers (three refinement passes) shown whole, with their share of training words beside them.
On the far copy lab, a tower founded fresh forms the copy within 100 laps (the single level formed none).

## 5. The card

Restriction and prolongation are affine maps over gathered blocks: the card's `field_affine` kernels over a packet's `P`
site reads, and their reverses through `field_outer` and `field_transposed`; level 1 is the field's own kernels on the packet
strand. A level's parity is the field's parity read through the tower (every read, face, covector and learner equal), and no
tower run precedes it (GPU first).

## 6. Failures this could repeat, checked

- **Reach** (the field record's §1): every level reads every site of its level; no section bounds a contact.
- **The codec welding digits** (the same §1): the bytes stay the sites of level 0 and every copy at the bytes remains;
  packets add a level, they replace nothing.
- **A clocked stream** (the old moment tower): no level scans; each is a field on its own strand.
- **Catered machinery:** the period is the rings' own and no boundary rule or word list enters; what a packet carries is
  learned through the one comparison.
- **A design in the programming language:** a packet is the scale square's restriction and its place a residue on both
  towers, not a window chosen for its size.
- **Most certain first:** no level and no place is written first; the writer's draws stay keyed.
