## 3. The failure group

`from training` for the theorems, `agent-inferred` for the sorting.

**One object refutes.** A statement over an unending family is refuted by one reading and completed only by a
cause (`refuted_by_one_reading`, `no_finite_set_of_readings_completes`). The two neighbours of the Hodge conjecture
each fell to one object with one invariant.

- *Integer coefficients.* Atiyah and Hirzebruch 1962: torsion classes that no cycle carries, because the classes of
  cycles are killed by operations that these classes survive. Kollár 1992: on a very general hypersurface of
  suitable degree in projective four-space every curve has degree divisible by a prime `p`, so the generator of the
  degree-four classes is carried by no curve while a multiple of it is a plane section. The invariant is a
  divisibility, a count.
- *No polarization.* Voisin 2002: a complex torus of dimension four, not projective, with classes of type `(2,2)`
  that no analytic object carries at any multiple. The conjecture is false once the rational positive form is
  dropped.

Both invariants are of the kind §4 reads: the first dies under rational coefficients, the second lives where no
rational positive form ties the lattice to the turn.

**The finite object, variety by variety.** For a smooth projective variety `X` and a codimension `p`, write the
failure group

    Z^(2p)(X) = Hdg^(2p)(X, ℤ) / (classes of cycles),

the integer classes the turn keeps, modulo the carried ones. It is finitely generated. The rational conjecture says
the carried classes have full rank, which is to say that `Z^(2p)(X)` is finite:

    Hodge  ⇔  for every X and p, every class of Z^(2p)(X) has positive grain.

Kollár and Atiyah and Hirzebruch show that it is not zero in general. It is studied as an invariant in its own
right: in degree four and in the degree of curves it does not change under a birational map (Soulé and Voisin
2005), and for suitable threefolds it is an unramified cohomology group (Colliot-Thélène and Voisin 2012). So the
word "failure" names information: the group tells varieties apart.

On a finite complex the same sentence is a theorem with nothing left over. An integer boundary map has a cokernel
that is a free part and a finite part read off its invariants (`Landmarks/SmithCokernel.cokernelEquiv`). A class
in the finite part is carried at its grain; a class with a component in the free part refuses every multiple
(`theRationalObstructionRefusesEveryMultiple`). The lens's ecologies are complexes of this kind, with carriers
that come in whole units, and they show both parts at once. The conjecture says of the varieties, with their
carriers restricted to the ones the turn keeps, that the free part is empty.

**The carriers are the least-volume ones.** By Wirtinger's inequality a complex subvariety has the least volume in
its class, and a closed current of type `(p,p)` with integer multiplicities is a holomorphic chain (King 1971;
Harvey and Shiffman 1974). So the conjecture asks whether every rational class the turn keeps is generated by
least-action carriers, and its content is a condensation: from a smooth form spread over the variety to a current
concentrated on a set.

**The cause, where it is written.** In codimension one the passage from class to carrier is the exponential
sequence `0 → ℤ → 𝒪 → 𝒪^× → 0`, a carry: a class of type `(1,1)` lifts to a line bundle and a section's zeros
are the divisor, at multiple one, so `Z²(X) = 0` (`hodge.lefschetz-one-one-passage`). The construction to make is
its successor in higher codimension, and the grain of §4 says what form its remainder takes: it may be reached
at a multiple, and the conjecture allows that.

## 4. A class of grain k closes after k turns

`agent-inferred`, with its finite part in Lean (`Landmarks/GrainPeriod`).

Call the *grain* of a class its order in the quotient by the carried classes. The multiples of the class that are
carried are the multiples of its grain (`nsmul_mem_iff_grain_dvd`), a class reachable only in the multiple `m`
has a grain above one that divides `m` (`grain_of_reachableOnlyInMultiple`), and no reading with rational values
that is blind to the carriers sees a class of positive grain (`rational_reading_blind`). The last statement is
why the operations of Atiyah and Hirzebruch, which take values in torsion groups, can say nothing once the
coefficients are rational.

The same number is a period. A turn `h` of a group returns a state after `m` turns just when the order of `h`
divides `m` (`turns_close_iff`); a turn that is not the identity rests nowhere (`no_rest`); every state moves on a
cycle whose length is the order (`period_of_turn`). So a class of grain `k` has no carrier at rest and is carried
by a cycle of `k` states, and the mean over that cycle is the rational carrier: dividing by `k` is averaging over
the period.

The cases of order two are one object read five ways:

- the Möbius band, whose edge is twice its core (`Objects/Orientation.two_face_mobius_band`);
- the odd ring of sign-reversing contacts, which breaks a contact under every configuration and has a key on its
  double cover and on no odd cover (`cover_of_odd_ring_has_key_iff`, on `LoopPacking.odd_ring_breaks`);
- the flip of two digits, the map of the diagonal argument, which rests nowhere and has period two (`flip_no_rest`,
  `flip_period`; `Foundation/FiniteKey.two_digits_are_a_gap`);
- the parametron's two half-turn sheets, a response at half the pump's rate;
- the place that flips each pass when it hears its own release
  ([November 13](2026-11-13_THE_FIELD_RINGS_UNDER_ITS_OWN_RELEASE_A_PLACE_HEARING_ITSELF_FLIPS_EACH_PASS_THE_RUNAWAY_LEAVES_EVIDENCE_AT_THE_KNOWN_PLACES_AND_RECURSION_HELPS_ONLY_WHERE_THE_DEPOSIT_RUNS_THROUGH_IT.md)).

On the lens: a boom and a bust is a carrier, at grain `k`, of a class that has none at grain one. Nothing in it is
a success or a failure. It is an integer grain and a class of order `k`, and the alternation is how the class is
kept. What would be a refusal is a class in the free part, and the Hodge conjecture says the varieties have none.

A rhyme, marked as one: the lens says causality needs a polarization, and Voisin's theorem says the Hodge relation
is false without one. The lens's polarization is a difference that drives a flow. The classical one is a rational

## 7. The horizon, the unseen and the periodic chart

### 7.1 What a diagonal gives and what it does not

`tree` and `from training`. The diagonal's engine is in the tree (`diagonal_fixed_point`): a reading of `A` that
reached every assignment `A → Y` would give every map of `Y` a fixed point, and the flip has none. Wolpert's
theorems on inference devices (2008) are this engine with a world that answers a prediction with its opposite, and
that hypothesis is the whole content: it holds for two predictors that read each other and it is no property of a
domain in general. The proposal's corollary, that an embedded model must keep slow modes and local closure, does not
follow from a diagonal. What follows from the count is the retention law, which already stands: a receiver smaller
than its domain keeps a quotient, and the quotient suffices for the futures constant on its fibres.

That no learner is better than another over every problem is Wolpert's theorem of 1996 (with Macready 1997 for
search): an advantage is always relative to an ecology. This is the lens's sentence that no intelligence holds
every class, as a theorem, and its positive half is the lens's too: what generalizes is a prior on how composition
unfolds.

### 7.2 The unseen has a price and no name

`from training`. A receiver cannot give a probability to a class it has no cell for, which is the lens's point about
what arose later. It can price the event that some class outside its cells arrives. Good 1953, with Turing: after
`n` draws the expected mass of the classes never seen equals the expected count of the classes seen once among
`n + 1` draws, divided by `n + 1`,

    E[M_n] = E[N₁(n+1)] / (n + 1),        E[M_n] = Σ_x p_x (1 − p_x)^n,

so the share of states seen once reads the mass of the unseen, from the receiver's own counts. The lens's "no
probability beforehand" and this are one statement in two halves: no cell, and a mass.

The proposal that a nonvanishing first cohomology proves novelty without end does not hold as stated. On a finite
complex the invariants that the whole has and no proper part reads number its cycle rank
(`cell.first-betti-torus-sphere`), a finite count, and a tower of obstructions ends within the dimension. Novelty
without end needs the complex to grow, which is a premise about the world and is the lens's own.

**8.4 A terminal product does not tell crossing patterns apart.** Codex's hand result on the earlier record's
ruler, checked here by hand: with three pairs opened in the order `A B C` and closed in the orders that give 0, 1,
2 and 3 crossings, the assignment `A = i`, `B = j`, `C = k` in the quaternion group gives products `1, −1, 1, −1`.
So one terminal product reads the parity of the crossings, and a word with two crossings reads as closed. A
terminal register that tells every crossing pattern of `n` pairs needs at least `⌈log₂ n!⌉` bits, since with all
openers first the closing orders are `n!` patterns. For a fork ruler this says to read prefix states, or two
assignments, and never one product.
