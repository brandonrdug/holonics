## 3. Where the cost lies, as far as it is read

[measured]
- **It is spread evenly over the pass.** Each 32-section window of the founded pass reads 1.52 to
  1.63 bits a byte, against the twin's 1.524 for the whole pass. The window at section 32 reads 1.568,
  against the twin's 1.526 at the same section. Every held-out region pays.
- **The one place the clock helps is where it was built to help.** The answer's first unit, held
  out, codes 0.461 bits lower.
- **The ranking under the top clock is flat.**
  - The sixteen largest drops on the whole text lie within 6.6% of one another.
  - Below the first, the two reads (the eighth and the whole text) share one class among their next
    fifteen: on the eighth they are letters and short pieces (`"er"`, `" s"`, `"e"`), and on the
    whole text they are digits (`"7"`, `"5"`, `"00"`).
  - On the whole text, the first clock's margin over the second is 50.1 parts a million.

[agent-inferred]
- **The noise floor.** For a clock whose face carries nothing about the covector, `E[drop]` over
  the energy is the face's rank over the cells:
  - 64 / 1,342,464 = 47.7 parts a million on the whole text;
  - 381 on the eighth, where the margin is 106.7.

  The blank line's lead is therefore about one noise floor on the whole text and below it on the
  eighth.
- **A common part.** Each of the sixteen largest drops is at least 1,713 parts a million, about 36
  noise floors, while their classes share nothing. Most of each drop is then a part of the covector
  that any piecewise-constant face can carry, whatever class it holds (the spread line reads how far
  down the ranking it reaches). Its likeliest source
  is the covector's mean along each stream, or a drift slower than a segment. The pool reads that
  part with the frozen passage's hindsight. A learned join can only meet it as it comes.
- **Three causes are open:**
  1. The join's own normalized steps fit each section's share of that common part. The certificate
     admits them, because it rereads the section the step was computed on. The next section then
     pays.
  2. The coupling: the encoding `E` is shared with every level's input and moves under
     `G_(E(u)) += Oᵀ δx`. Or the other families co-adapt to `O`.
  3. The opened moments themselves: `first` is the mean section covector, so the first steps move
     `O` along the passage's whole-pass direction at full rate.

  The ring's founding met the same law. Its founded readout read 257 of 495 held-out units at the
  join and 65 after one pass with its moments opened (`hnn.tower-founded-moments`). A founded
  family that does not keep its founding's value through its first pass is now measured twice.
