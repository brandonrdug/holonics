## 3. The physics, one propagation

- **A grid and a parcel.** A flow is written on a grid fixed in the recorder's frame, or on the parcels that move with it. On
  the parcels the law has no advection term: what is conserved is labelled by the mass passed, never by the place. For a stream
  the mass is its news, `S_t = Σ c`, and a level's epochs are parcels of news. A frame is a grid cell, and a recorder's sample is
  a finer one.
- **What a step can carry.** A clock that steps once in `h` carries what turns under about two radians a step (the Courant
  bound; a strobe's Nyquist bound is `π`). Faster cycles are not lost: they return as a beat at the difference of the two rates
  (`pair.crossing-sum-and-difference`), which the reader meets as news.
- **A source has its own cycle.** A reed, the folds, a bow, a pen's stroke and a saccade each close a cycle of their own. The
  count of the recorder's ticks in one such cycle is the source's period, a count of one cycle against another, which is all a
  receiver reads of time.
- **A join exchanges counts one for one.** Where three members with `ω₃ = ω₁ + ω₂` exchange through a lossless join, the counts
  `N = P/ω` are exchanged one for one and the powers go in the ratio of the rates (Manley and Rowe 1956; in Lean,
  `HNN/OwnCycleCells`). A slow member receives the share `ω_slow/ω_fast` of the power: small, by the ratio of the rates, with a
  whole count. The count is the second kept quantity, of one sign, that the cascade's law asks for.
- **A cascade's direction.** With the count kept a chain of joins builds the coarse grain. Without it the total runs to the
  fine grain. In two dimensions a fluid's energy climbs to the large eddies for this reason, and in three it falls to the small.

## 4. The law (weight 0.8, `agent-inferred`)

**A reader holds a stream as nested cycles it has located. Each level's unit is a cycle of the level under it that closes (a
pulse of the folds, a stroke, a word), cut where that level's own news stands. Its clock is the count of its own units; a
duration is a count of one level's cycles against another's; and no frame stands anywhere between a source and a reader. Depth
is the count of nested cycles the reader carries, reach is the product of their strides, and a level is read by its count in its
own unit. A deposit compounds only where a closed unit is kept as a part for the join above it: where no count crosses the join,
the deposit runs to the fine grain.**

It is the same statement for every specimen, and it joins five things the tree stated apart: the clock of news, the calendar, a
level as a difference tone, the build's depth and the cascade's second invariant.

## 8. Vision is the same loop

An image is a field on a surface, read through an aperture that the reader moves. A row-by-row raster is vision's frame: an
order of reading set by the recorder. An eye holds no such order. Its receptors answer change, its gaze jumps three or four times
a second to where the news stands, and a contour's information is concentrated where its direction turns fastest (Attneave 1954),
which is the landmark law on a surface: the places no larger news stands near. A written digit is also a limb's trace. A pen
moves under a few slow controls, slows where it turns (the two-thirds power law of drawing, Lacquaniti, Terzuolo and Viviani
1983), and its strokes are its own cycles; characters held as stroke programs are learned from one example (Lake, Salakhutdinov
and Tenenbaum 2015), which is generalization as a short build from one's own parts. So the digits enter as the voice does:

- a pen with a body of its own (a mass with friction, a few controls, ink where it is down) is a limb beside the tube, and its
  babble is a slice of acts and traces;
- the gaze is a limb too: what she reads of an image is a patch at her gaze's site, finer at its centre
  (`receiver.port-rod-cone-taper`), and where it goes next is her act;
- the order of reading is hers. October's stroke chart was the right kind of chart, with its tracing done outside her.

Before any build, the premise is a host read on the digits: where a count ruler's news stands along a stroke, by part (ends,
corners, junctions, the stretches between).

**The law** (0.7, `agent-inferred`). The second level is cut by the first level's own law one level up, with every quantity in
the level's own unit. Its places are the first level's units, and its clock counts them. A unit's news is the share of it the
join left unsaid,

    n_j = R_j / Z_j,

with `R_j` the level's face's code of unit `j`'s places read from unit `j−1`'s row carried across the join (the square's read leg)
and `Z_j` the same places through the same reader from the opening's row at rest: what the level says of the unit from nothing.
The clock is `S²_j = Σ_(i≤j) n_i`, and the section is one unit: a unit the join told nothing reads `n = 1`, as a flat symbol
carries `ln A` one level down, so the section is the unit itself and no number is handed in. A site is a unit whose `n` stands
above every unit within one unit of `S²` (`code.site-isolation-landmark` on these three); a unit the past misleads, `n > 1`, is
always one. Read through a common scale (the same places coded twice over, or at another grain of nats) `n` stays where it was
(`unsaid_share_scale_free`).

**Why the sum missed.** A unit's summed place codes carry its length and its letters' rarity, both quantities of the first level,
and their mean a place is the news of the level under that. Neither says whether the unit follows from the one before, which is the
only news a second level has: what the join carried cancels, as what the receiver predicts cancels at every section. The charts
are the dip of the transitional binding by which an infant cuts words from a stream of syllables (Saffran, Aslin and Newport 1996)
and the successor peaks of Harris (1955), and in this record's law the binding `1 − n` is the share a count carries across the
join: a unit compounds where it is high.
