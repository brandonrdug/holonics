## The two-sided oscillation

The procedural loop alternates between two invalid inferences:

1. **Apparatus order becomes semantic chronology.** One mutable Rust owner, one function call,
   file enumeration, one CUDA stream, a scoped-thread join, a parity audit, or serialized bytes are
   treated as evidence that all affected events form one caused chain.
2. **Administrative separation becomes semantic commutation.** Different phase labels, keys,
   source IDs, worker lanes, batches, or disjoint synthetic mutation-site names are treated as
   evidence that complete successor morphologies commute.

The first inference produces one-core barriers. The second produces false parallel fronts,
duplicated live bodies, or endpoint-equal fixtures which do not preserve later conduct. When the
second is falsified, I retreat to the first. Neither move derives the relationship from typed
incidence, complete successors, or returned morphology.

This is the precise sense in which the loop is primitive. Its implicit model of computation is a
homunculus or foreman which sees a global collection, decides the next phase, dispatches workers,
waits for them, integrates their output, and serializes the result for later replay. Parallelism is
bolted around that foreman; the caused ecology never becomes the operative program. This is
classical imperative orchestration disguised with holonic nouns.
