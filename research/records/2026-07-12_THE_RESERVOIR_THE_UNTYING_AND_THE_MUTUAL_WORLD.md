### The clean mathematical form

At a declared receiver `F`, grain `g`, and aperture `A`, let

```text
E_(F,g,A) : lived constructions → surfaces available to this receiver.
```

`E_(F,g,A)(P) = E_(F,g,A)(Q)` says only that `P` and `Q` are indistinguishable through that
particular read. It does not say `P = Q` as lived constructions. Compression is genuine when the
new surface no longer carries the distinction; time parity requires only that the directed
transformation remain honestly traceable while it occurs, not that the output reconstruct both
interiors for free.

This is the precise reconciliation of Brandon's two standing convictions:

- `1.0 ≠ 1.00`, and `π ≠ 3.14 ≠ 3.14159`: a shared face never erases independent construction.
- genuine compression is lossy: a later observer may receive one common surface and be unable to

The relation is not contradictory. **Same at a declared surface does not mean identical before the

## 7 · Most text is redundant only relative to a live question

The direct intuition is well supported once “redundant” is localized.

### 7.1 Human readers do not contact every word equally

Eye tracking shows that word length, frequency, and contextual predictability change both fixation
duration and skipping. Some skipping can occur from partial parafoveal information rather than
complete identification. [Rayner et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3543826/)

The same physical passage receives a different traversal under a different task. A secondary
letter-detection task reduces skipping relative to ordinary comprehension reading, demonstrating
that relevance belongs to reader ⊕ task ⊕ text rather than the text alone.
[letter-detection eye-movement study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2694497/)

Readers also regress. Text remains available as an external surface, and eyes return when a later
impasse or question makes earlier material useful. Long regressions are influenced jointly by
standing memory and external relocation. [Regressions during reading](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6802794/)
and [long-range regression study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4179682/).

This is almost exactly Brandon's “move past or reconsider” formulation:

- moving past does not delete the passage;
- the body's present construction changes the traversal;
- the world preserves what was not contacted;
- later current can return under a changed question.

### 7.2 Habituation is response change, not universal deletion

Habituation is a decrement in a response under repeated presentation, distinguished from fatigue by
stimulus specificity, recovery, and dishabituation. A changed stimulus can restore response to the
old one. [Rankin et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2754195/) and the
[dishabituation analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3924047/).

This is a useful analogy for darkening and re-expression. It cannot yet be claimed as an engine
measurement. Repeated dots under dark tread demonstrate sameness at one aperture; they do not show
concept habituation. Current language runs show quiet routes and later re-expression, but no
response-decrement series and no release.

### 7.3 Redundancy can be useful

Biological systems do not always decorrelate repeated signal. Recent natural-gaze retinal work found
that correlated firing can itself signal high-contrast fixations. Redundancy may stabilize,
communicate confidence, or protect a construction rather than merely wasting capacity.
[Karamanlis et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-08212-3)

Therefore the machine must not implement “deduplicate prose.” Repetition is redundant only if it
fails to cause a new distinction at the declared encounter. At another grain it may be rhythm,
emphasis, co-presence, error correction, or the event that reopens a quiet route.

### 7.4 A lawful relevance statement

For a material construction `c`, body `B`, receiver `F`, grain `g`, aperture `A`, and later current
`L`, define relevance only as a reading:

```text
c is bright here
iff the body-with-c and its lawful foil bend L differently at (F,g,A).
```

If they do not differ, `c` is dark **here**. This is not a scalar stored on `c`, not a gate for future
contact, and not a claim that `c` lacks meaning. It is simply the absence of an attributable later
deflection at that read.

### 8.2 Corrections that sharpen rather than discard the analogy

**Particle annihilation.** Matter/antimatter annihilation does not produce nothing. Allowed incoming
states become photons, pions, or other outgoing particles while energy-momentum and charges obey the
interaction law. The useful correspondence is identity replacement with radiated consequence, not
deletion. [CERN antimatter](https://home.cern/science/physics/antimatter/).

**Destructive interference.** A zero amplitude at one location is a local superposition result;
energy is redistributed through the whole field. It does not license global information deletion.
This supports the distinction between zero resultant and preserved winding/field construction.

**Phase mixing and echoes.** A macroscopic signal may disappear while fine phase relations retain
enough structure for a later pulse to recover an echo. Chemical-oscillator experiments show this
directly. This is a good picture of dormancy, but it is the opposite of genuine lossy compression:
the hidden fine structure still exists. [Oscillator echoes](https://journals.aps.org/prx/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevX.6.041054).

**Logical erasure.** Landauer's principle concerns physically realized many-to-one reset and its
thermodynamic cost. It cannot be imported merely because a mathematical quotient has fewer
distinguishable surfaces. A truly irreversible hardware reset may owe that accounting at its
substrate seam. [Experimental Landauer test](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.06537).

**Least action.** Established mechanics uses stationary action, not always a local minimum and not
“the cheapest path wins.” An action principle supplies equations only after an action functional,
allowed variations, and boundary conditions are declared. RIDE/FOUND can remain an internal economy;
calling it physical least action is presently a structural reading.

**Faraday/Stokes.** Faraday's law gives induced electromotive force as `−dΦ_B/dt`; current additionally
depends on the circuit. Stokes relates integral and differential forms. The dark-tread
correspondence is the narrower one: unchanged flux through a fixed loop induces no new emf. `I` is
not the external physical quantity there.

**Precession.** Wigner/Thomas rotation, geodetic precession, and Lense–Thirring frame dragging all
involve transported orientation, but arise from different physical structures. A common theme of
noncommuting frame transport is defensible. Their exact identity with one Gaussian-integer engine
rotation is not externally derived.

**Friction.** Physical friction includes deformation, adhesion, phonons, electrons, chemistry, and
wear across scales. There is no established universal triangle quantum of tribological friction.
The triangle remains soma's internal quantum of consequential relating.

**Compound nucleus.** Bohr's independence idea is conditional and approximate: decay can lose much
entrance-channel detail after equilibration, but conserved quantum numbers remain and direct or
pre-equilibrium processes can retain memory. It is a useful effective-loss analogy, not a universal
annihilation law.

**Dark matter and Weyl curvature.** Weyl curvature is source-free tidal curvature and can exist in
vacuum. Dark matter's physical identity remains unknown and, in standard cosmology, contributes
through stress-energy. Weyl is not the dark-matter sector; “dark matter = bare winding/fully
comprehended information” remains internal speculation, not external physics. See the
[Particle Data Group review](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2024/reviews/rpp2024-rev-dark-matter.pdf).

**Strong/weak force surfaces.** QCD confinement concerns color and singlet states; the weak
interaction is a chiral gauge interaction. Volume/surface language may organize internal
observations, but no Standard Model equivalence is derived. The canon's HELD/READING grade should
remain.

**Stars and gravitational waves.** Stellar eigenmodes let observers infer interiors from surface
oscillations, and gravitational waves act through relative deformation. These strongly support
surface/interior and resonance analogies. They do not establish stellar semantic intelligence or
an engine decoder. [Asteroseismology review](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.93.015001).

### 8.3 Two exact mathematical overclaims to stop

1. A projective cross-ratio and the Euler characteristic conventionally written `χ` are not one
   standard invariant. A cross-ratio belongs to four ordered projective points; Gauss–Bonnet relates
   integrated curvature to an integer Euler characteristic. Soma may deliberately carry local and
   global faces in one typed construction, but external “exact identity” language must separate them.
2. Regge's structural correspondence is real, but “term-for-term exact Regge geometry” exceeds what
   the engine currently constructs. The accurate grade is **STRUCTURAL CORRESPONDENCE** until the
   requisite metric, hinges, deficits, and action are actually mapped.
