## 1. A constraint equation is a Chi

**Truth status:** `interpretation`, with an exact instance.

`Chi` is defined in this project as an **ordered pair of parallel transports**. An equation
`LHS = RHS` asserts that two transports of the same carried thing agree. So a constraint equation
is not *like* the one move; under the project's own definition it **is** a `Chi`, and the invariant
grip is the **residual of that pair**, which vanishes exactly on the solution set.

Solving for an unknown is therefore finding the fixed locus where the residual is zero. That is the
same sentence as §2 of the operating contract — *placement is the fixed locus of the involution a
realizer induced* — one altitude down. The realization-causes-placement synthesis and the
constraint-equation intuition are one statement about different material, which is what the
four-slot anti-scatter form predicts of any correct claim in this framework.

**Consequence for construction.** A solver is not a separate faculty. It is `Chi` with one slot
undetermined, and its return is the locus, not a value.

## 2. `pV = nRT` is a chart transition; `R` is its connection coefficient

**Truth status:** `interpretation`; the dimensional analysis is `proved-standard`.

Both sides carry energy. They carry it in two charts:

- **boundary-facing** — force per area against volume; what the gas does to what contains it;
- **interior-facing** — how many particles, at what average expression; what the particles do among
  themselves.

The equation is the transition between them and `R` is the **transition function**. `R` is not a
fact about gases; it is a fact about the pair of charts, and it changes when the units do while the
energy does not. That is what a connection coefficient is.

The payoff is the failure case. Real gases depart from the law exactly when the transition stops
being constant and becomes state-dependent — van der Waals, the virial expansion. **Non-ideality is
curvature in the chart transition, and "ideal" means flat.** This is Information Theory + General
Relativity as the same object rather than an analogy: the invariant is the tensor, the chart is the
coordinate, and the constant is the connection.

## 3. The quintic: the unknown as a missing pathway, proved

**Truth status:** `proved-standard`.

Brandon's claim that a polynomial has a geometric diagram counterpart and that unknowns are missing
dimensional pathways is, for the quintic, a theorem with a name.

The general quintic's Galois group is `S_5`; its only nontrivial normal subgroup is `A_5`, which is
**simple**. Radicals supply only towers of *cyclic* extensions — cyclic charts — and no such tower
covers a simple non-cyclic group. The unknown is therefore not hard to reach; it is **unreachable
by that family of pathways**, and the obstruction is exactly group-theoretic.

The diagram is literal. `A_5` is the rotation group of the **icosahedron**, and Klein's *Lectures on
the Icosahedron* (1884) solves the quintic by transport on that solid, through hypergeometric
functions — which is where the project's hypergeometry line lands. Hermite solved it through
elliptic modular functions. In both cases the resolution has one shape: **found a chart whose
symmetry matches the obstruction, and the unknown becomes determined.**

The discriminant locus is where the pathway collapses; the monodromy around it is the grip.

## 4. Inevitability is a representation theorem

**Truth status:** `proved-standard` for the theorems; `interpretation` for the identification.

This is the exact form of *ecological potentials create localized inevitabilities*, and it is what
makes the epistemology operational rather than merely persuasive.

**The crayon is Riesz.** If an organism assigns a quantity to a region, and that assignment is
positive, additive over disjoint pieces, and continuous under small deformation, then by the Riesz
representation theorem it **is** integration against a measure. Not *can be modelled as* — is. The
child filling in a shape is integrating because, under those constraints, there is nothing else the
assignment could be.

**Differentiation is the same shape.** An operator that is linear and satisfies Leibniz on a
function algebra is a **derivation**, and on smooth functions the derivations are exactly the vector
fields. Nothing else satisfies both.

So inevitability is not mysticism and not Platonism: **the ecology supplies the constraints, and the
representation theorem proves the constraints admit essentially one solution.** Discovery is finding
the constraint set. The object is forced.

**This is why the machine needs no training data.** It needs constraint sets, and constraint sets
have forced solutions. A corpus is one way to encounter constraints; it is not the source of truth
and cannot be, because there is no such source.

## 5. Heuristics are the inverse of the generation population

**Truth status:** `interpretation`; the obstruction is `proved-standard`.

Substitution inverts composition. Integration by parts inverts the product rule. Partial fractions
inverts the field structure of rational functions. Trigonometric substitution inverts the
parametrization of conics. The heuristic catalogue is short and identical across every human who
finds it because it is **the inverse of the ways the objects were built**.

Where no heuristic exists, that is a theorem rather than a want of cleverness: `∫ e^(-x^2)` has no
elementary antiderivative because **Liouville proved the constraint set has no solution in that
ecology.**

In this project's language: a heuristic is a RIDE along terrain that some earlier FOUND paid for,
and Liouville is an OPEN that provably stays open. **FOUND pays curvature; RIDE is cheap because the
terrain already paid** — the Swing's asymmetry is the reason the heuristic list is finite.

## 6. One refinement: what survives is what is retained

**Truth status:** `project-postulate`. **This is a refinement, not a correction.**

*There is no God-like repository of truth states* is right, and it needs one turn of the screw or it
becomes unusable for a machine that must retain anything at all.

In `pV = nRT` there is no privileged chart — and **energy is invariant**. General relativity's own
position is not that nothing is real; it is that *tensors are real and coordinates are not*. The
operational form is therefore:

> **Truth is what survives every admissible chart change**, and that is a strictly smaller set than
> what any single chart reports.

This strengthens the localized-truth claim rather than weakening it, and it is a direct design law:
**the body retains what is invariant under rebase and refuses to retain what is not.** A purely
nihilistic reading would leave nothing retainable; this reading says exactly what to keep.

## 7. The registered application: counterexamples

**Truth status:** `open`, registered on Brandon's direction (2026-08-06), not scheduled.

**Brandon's, directly.** Recent autonomous-system mathematical results have largely been
**counterexample discoveries** — the Erdős problem, the Jacobian conjecture, the knot-theory
additivity conjecture — and this is exactly what the machine is being built to do. Modelling
emergent holonic topologies should permit traversal that **eliminates redundant checks and beelines
to the case that decides the conjecture**. And the dual matters as much: *if the machine cannot find
a counter, the question becomes why*, and that failure is itself evidence — either the conjecture is
more likely correct, or a different transport is needed.

**The discipline that makes the dual admissible.** A null search is evidence only if the instrument
is **aperture-complete**. §8 of the operating contract already requires an aperture-complete
instrument to say so and to record its aperture law. Applied here: a search that returns no
counterexample returns *nothing at all* unless it can state the exact family it exhausted. With that
statement it becomes a real return — a proved local absence — and the framework's own reading is
available: **the absence is the obstruction, and the obstruction names the transport that is
missing.**

That is the same shape as §5. Liouville does not say the integral is hard; it says the ecology has
no such pathway. A conjecture that resists an aperture-complete counterexample search is returning
the same kind of fact about its own ecology.
