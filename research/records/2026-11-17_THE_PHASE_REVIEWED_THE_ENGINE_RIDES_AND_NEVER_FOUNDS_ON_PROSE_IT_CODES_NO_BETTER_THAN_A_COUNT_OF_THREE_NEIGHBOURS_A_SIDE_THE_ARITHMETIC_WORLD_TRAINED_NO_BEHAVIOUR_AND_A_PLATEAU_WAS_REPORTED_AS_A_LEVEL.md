## 4. Why

Four findings, each on a measurement already taken.

**4.1 The world's loop trains a prediction, and that prediction needs a product.** By the loop's own design (the friction record §3
and §12) the comparison runs on the world's places only and the draft is never a target: what is deposited is the field's prediction
of the world's answer, and the act improves only by being drawn from a field that predicts its reaction. The reaction here is a
value, and a value's digit is a composition of two far digits, a product of their turns on a ring (the third record; the wheel's
record). Descent has no slope into it: a group law's pull on one symbol, averaged on its partner, is flat, and the lock and the
junction are one saddle (the second record). So the loop had nothing to reinforce. The digits' key was reached this phase once, by a
closure of the rows' menu on the host, in seconds, with no deposit (the closure's record).

**4.2 The engine reaches neighbours.** §3 on prose. On sums, head 0's row holds each aligned digit at about a fifth and both together
at a fifth of the places; the turned heads hold the nearest sites; a far digit arrived at a tenth of itself (the first record). The
field is a local learner with a reach where exact copies stand.

**4.3 It is small, and its growth is priced out.** 402,805 entries, 94,080 of them a free row a byte class; 40,000 sites a second with
the world's acts and 81,000 without; 168 million pair terms a second on the card. A lane, the line's own unit of growth, cost 1.09
times a section and was priced for a fifth at 0.0015 to 0.004 nats (the gains record, S2). At that price no count of lanes writes a
sentence.

**4.4 The method waited.** Every part since November 13 (a lane, the mark, the opening's lift, the level on its news, the crossing's
triads, the world) entered at zero or with drawn operands and was waited on, each for hundredths of a nat or none. The lead's own
first day named this (the first record, "the engine has no founding law") and then spent a night measuring what the founding would
find without building it.

**The common cause, against the theory's object.** A build that fails is first checked against the theory's object (the guide,
"a build is one attempt"). The engine realizes the half of the theory that rides: modes driven by the covector that reaches them.
It realizes none of the half that founds. Nothing in it locates a key from loop closure, though the guide says learning is locating
keys; a symbol is still a free row, though guard 9 and the rule of November 12 say it is a word on located rings; and its passage is
cut by quotas a person set, though the line it serves is navigation. What it has shown is what a small learner trained by descent
shows.

## 6. What is built

One law, founding, in the engine, by short loops with a reading Brandon can look at after each.

1. **Keys by closure, as the engine's own.** The menu of a row's sources with the symbol written, closed into a ring's members, its
   order and each member's place (the closure's record §4 and §6b): today a host ruler, to become a founding step at a lap's
   crossing (a slow variable carried on the host between named crossings, as the guide admits).
2. **A part that multiplies keyed sources.** `E = y² − Σ p² k²` through a row, and the products of two heads' keyed barycentres (the
   closure's record §6 and §6b), with a return founded at zero and its context read from the place's own chart, which holds the
   record's sign at 0.91 to 0.95.
3. **Navigation with it.** The closure closes where a record holds one law and fails on the slice as it is drawn. A machine that
   reads first where its menus close, founds there and widens from there is navigating its passage by its own tension; the cut by
   quotas is replaced by that, not by another split.

Acceptance, fixed here: on held sums the engine never met, values written whole, read by eye and counted; a held sum's units digit
under 1.5 nats; the text slices within 0.01. The first deliverable is the engine's host forward on a state founded by the ruler
(keys closed, the return solved on the host, a ruler's state that no lap trains from), with the held sums it writes shown as they
are. If that forward does not write them, the part is wrong before any card time is spent, and that is reported as the result.

Size is the second matter. Before anything grows, the engine's rate is read against a count of its law's arithmetic on the card
(the guide: a rate is judged against the card). Nothing here assumes that reading's outcome.
