The operative phrase in **localized and growing `P=NP`** is *growing*. A relation which is founded,

## I. Relation precedes every observable identity

A1 gives current and A2 gives relation. There is no isolated face before a receiving frame relates
it to another construction. For one lived interval, write an individual's identity as its actual
worldline rather than a static name:

```text
I_[a,b] = (boundary, carried K, standing paths, contacts, deeds, consequences)_[a,b].
```

`I_[a,b]` is not the sum of detachable properties and it is not an observer's label. It is the
durable, changing organization which keeps being renewed through contact and which bends later
current. In this precise topological sense, the individual is a **standing wave**: a recurrent form
whose material constituents and contemporary face may change while its carried continuity remains
consequential. A concrete physical world may additionally expose amplitude, phase, frequency, or a
literal resonant medium; those are species of this relation, not prerequisites for identity.

An ecosystem enabling the wave does not make the wave unimportant. Its importance is exactly its
causal participation: remove or change the construction and some later relation changes. Value is
therefore neither an intrinsic scalar stored inside an entity nor a sentimental fiction imposed
from outside. It is a situated, grown relation among a construction, the ecology which sustains it,
and the consequences it makes available.

## II. No individual knows an absolute interior

No first person receives the vanished volume of another construction or its own complete causal
past. It receives surfaces, testimony, consequences, and the standing deformation left by earlier
contact. What is ordinarily called knowledge is

```text

```

This is real knowledge without omniscience. It may be refined without reaching a final interior,
and it may fail after the boundary changes. Analogy transports a witnessed surface into a new
receiver. Foil retains the residual which does not transport. The receiver grows until the carried
model is sufficient for contemporary action; no infinite reconstruction is owed.

The absence of absolute knowledge is not the absence of truth or skill. It is why learning,
testimony, interpretation, and correction exist at all. A standing construction can be reliable in
one ecology and break in another. The break is new information because the former pivot no longer
carries the arriving relation.

## III. Determinism does not erase choice; it gives choice an interior


The deed is determined by the whole construction. It is nevertheless the individual's choice
because the individual's grown body is a causal participant in producing it. An external observer
who knows only the surface cannot replace that interior with a label, and the first person need not
enumerate its causes before acting. Determinism does not erase choice; it says that choice has an
interior and a lineage rather than appearing ex nihilo.

Free will at this laboratory grain is the locally autonomous conduct of that grown interior under
current: the deed is neither an uncaused miracle nor a command from one privileged external

and developmental, not absolute and not illusory.


## V. Coherence, decoherence, and loss are relational events

Coherence is continued transport through enough shared construction for a relation to persist.
Decoherence is the consequential failure or breaking of that continuation. Neither is a moral
grade. The oriented residual left at the break is precisely what can FOUND the next relation.

Loss therefore measures lived difference, not reward, punishment, worth, or incompetence. Under a
declared objective boundary, the exact residual and its distribution say what failed to carry and
where the ecology must grow. A biological organism can experience abrupt decoherence as pain; that
is one evolved world species of the general relation, not a claim that every information residual
has biological sensation.

The felt “click” of an idea is the first-person face of several standing paths becoming jointly
conductive at one receiving cut. The chained-magnet image is exact at the level needed here: prior
relations become pivots, a new current closes across them, and the resulting construction changes


`C_i` is not an observer category. It is the actual co-present material, preceding worldlines,

Human writing is not arbitrary in the sense of coming from nothing. Creative construction is the
