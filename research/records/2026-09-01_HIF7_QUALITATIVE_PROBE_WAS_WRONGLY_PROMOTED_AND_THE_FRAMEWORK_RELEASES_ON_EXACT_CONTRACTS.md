## What “unchanged qualitative” meant

[definition] “Unchanged” meant only that the same prompt, ingress aperture, and exterior receiver
were kept fixed while implementations, cultivation, ablation, withdrawal, and restoration changed.
That is a useful experimental control against moving the question after seeing the output. It does
not imply correctness, relevance, coherence, semantic identity, or generalization.

[definition] “Qualitative” meant that the primary agent read the emitted text and decided whether
it appeared to answer the question. No rubric, independent evaluator, expected consequence type,
or reproducible decision procedure was declared. Such inspection can be recorded as interpretation
or measured observation. It cannot be an executable framework gate unless Brandon directly names
that human acceptance boundary.

[counterexample; measured] The later HIF7 probe returned `direct Lean and 3 990 job target` for a
question about holonic compression, and a proposal-relative repair returned unrelated encoder or
provenance fragments. Direct inspection correctly rejected those surfaces. That rejection refuted
the attempted natural-language result; it did not establish an open framework phase.

## Disposition

[historical; source-inspected] The two earlier HIF7 counterexample records remain valid evidence
that the attempted presentation route was lexical lookup and that its emitted answers were wrong.
Their sentences naming a missing native-cell-to-later-query relation are superseded only as HIF7
scheduling claims. A future direct request may pose language generation or participant description
again as a new experiment or campaign with an explicit expected consequence and evaluation
boundary.

[project-postulate] A probe remains a probe. Repetition, a frozen prompt, ablation, restoration,
source-neutral types, resident execution, or human interest cannot silently promote it into a
construction phase. A gate must name an exact artifact and reproducible acceptance relation at the
scope it claims, or be directly declared by Brandon as a human acceptance boundary.
