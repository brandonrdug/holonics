## What runs

`generator_modulator.py` is self-contained. It uses only Python's standard library, integers and
`fractions.Fraction`; square roots are `math.isqrt`, and roots of unity are held as exponents of
`ω` or, for the ring of four, as Gaussian integers, never as floats. It reads no data, private or
public. The printed shares (for example `0.377163`) are readings written from exact counts.

Parts and what each checks:
- **A.** Each move `x ↦ λx + b` against its pivot; every numeral below `10^5` as a word of digit
  dilations about `−d/9`; the repdigit `…ddd` as `−d/9` modulo `10^k`, `k ≤ 12`.
- **B.** The product formula `|a|_∞ ∏_p |a|_p = 1` for `a = 2, …, 2000`; `ord_p(10)` equal to the
  period of `1/p` for every prime below 110.
- **C.** Long-division strokes for every prime below `10^5` (2 and 5 left out), with the Artin
  product over primes below 1000 as a reading.
- **D.** The affine group generated by the digit maps on `ℤ/m` for coprime `m < 120`; the rings one
  plane carries (coprime, `m ≤ 300`; phase chart); the least digit grain per ring; the explicit
  one-plane realizations (ring 9, ring 11, ring 10, the contracting suffix coordinate, the nilpotent
  plane) on every numeral below `10^5`.
- **E.** The rotor read for rings 3, 7, 9, 11, 13, 17, 37, 41, 73, 101 and 1001 and the cascade
  chains for 4, 8, 20, 40, 100 and 1000, on every numeral below 3000; the plane count against the
  order for every prime below 200.
- **F.** `F X = Z F`, `F Z = X⁻¹ F`, `Z X = ω X Z` for `n = 2, …, 40`; the ring of four's dual turn
  as the cyclic shift, exact over 4; Gauss sums `τ² = (−1/q) q` for odd primes below 60.
- **G.** The braid relation, the cat map, `TL` of order six, `TLT` the quarter turn, the commutator
  of two boosts, the cat map's Lucas fixed points (`k ≤ 20`, and point by point for `k ≤ 5`), and the
  stroke count of the `(p, q)` torus curve for `p, q ≤ 40`.

The last line is `all 1000350 checks hold`.

## Development notes

- The ring of four's dual turn first failed as "the shift by one". With `F_jk = i^(jk)`, a shift by
  `s` multiplies the dual by `i^(+ks)`; the check was corrected to that sign, which the record uses.
- A float square root in the sieve was replaced by `math.isqrt`.
- `ord_p(10)` over the 9,590 primes below `10^5` is computed from the factorization of `p − 1`,
  and the plain walk of the powers cross-checks it on every one of them.
- Part D was rewritten after an overstatement sent to design: a one-plane bound of `10 ≡ 0, ±1`
  holds in the phase chart only. In the coordinate chart one contracting coordinate holds the
  whole suffix and the nilpotent plane holds units and tens, and part D now checks both.
