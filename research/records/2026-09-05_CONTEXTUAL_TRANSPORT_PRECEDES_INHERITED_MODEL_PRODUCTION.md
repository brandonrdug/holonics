## The distinction that was lost

[definition] Changed transport does not by itself determine contextual transport. A received
utterance, the holon it describes, a physical object that might answer that description, and a
receiver's interpretation are not the same occurrence. `The cube is blue` may establish a
descriptor of a hypothetical object within a particular situation. Receiving those words does
not establish a property of every cube or identify a later referent by equal wording. Even an
actual object's color requires its observation conditions.

[definition] Context is the relevant situated organization of charts, channels, relations,
contemporary conditions and possible continuations. A token window/cache is one apparatus
presentation; a durable parameter change is one possible retained change. Neither supplies the
contextual relation by itself. Conditions must participate in the conducting relation, not be
appended afterward as labels or consulted by an authored relevance switch.

[interpretation] Brandon's prism example illustrates conditional generality: material structure,
illumination, spectral/phase behavior, region and observer delimit the asserted consequence.
The point is the retained constitutive and receiver conditions, not an unqualified color
predicate. This audit does not certify a microscopic optical mechanism from the example's prose.
Generality concerns lawful transport across an admitted family while retaining its hypotheses;
it does not discard those hypotheses.

[definition] The relevance hypothesis concerns which continuations the situated relation admits.
Quotation, explanation, an example and a request to produce code have different orientations,
even when they share code-bearing text. This does not request a programming-language detector,
relevance score, scene-ID lookup or per-format emitter inside Athena. The hypothesis must not be
advertised as an unrestricted theorem before its statement, domain and derivation are supplied.
