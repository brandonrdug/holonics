**Two anti-vacuity controls, and the first form of them was wrong.** The station initially reported

## 6. Falsifiers

- **The ordering claim.** Edit `CausalDiagram::layers()` to return its fronts in a different valid
  topological order. If any station's return changes, a driver was depending on an order the diagram
  did not force.
- **The separation.** Add a caused symbol on which `ReceiverId(17)` collapses two candidates the full
  family separates. The minimal-subfamily figure is then 2 or more and the reading above is refuted
  as stated.
- **The departure.** Point the child at a rest whose `program` names a population the rest does not
  carry. It must refuse rather than reach for the source; the refusal already exists by
  construction of `NativeRestCarrier`, and a return would refute the structural claim.
- **The condensation.** Declare a finer receiver family and re-run. If the 40/24 split does not move,
  the family was not finer, and the station's own no-op and total controls bound that.
