## 1. The input is a contemporary categorical ecology

Let the declared process sites be

\[
P=\{p_1,\ldots,p_n\}.
\]

Each site \(p_i\) has a finite set \(V_i\) of inherited lineage variants.
The additional section \(\bot_i\) means that the site is absent in that
local configuration. It is not an absolute nothing.

The intervention complex is the categorical product

\[
\mathcal X
=
\prod_{i=1}^{n}
\left(\{\bot_i\}\sqcup V_i\right).
\]

An application may lawfully declare:

- site and variant identities;
- inherited lineage identities;
- exact constraint identities;
- which site receives each constraint; and
- the contemporary reference section \(x_\star\in\mathcal X\).

It may not declare:

- which sites form the learned composite;
- which effects are internal or external;
- which variants are behaviorally homologous;
- the interaction polynomial;
- a learned successor; or
- a holon certificate.

Production owns every query \(x\in\mathcal X\). The world membrane returns a
complete exact-valued section

\[
F(x)\in\mathbb Q^m
\]

at the declared constraints. The returned section is source testimony. The
grammar derived from the collection of returns is self-emanated material.
Both remain separately inspectable.

## 2. Traversal is geometric before it is statistical

The product is traversed by a reflected mixed-radix Gray path. Consecutive
ordinary traversal configurations differ at exactly one site, including
sites which admit more than one lineage variant.

This matters for the ontology:

- the input is not a growing flat list of unrelated scalar features;
- one elementary move crosses one categorical face;
- the changed coordinate is explicit;
- higher-order effects are recovered from commuting families of these cuts;
  and
- no random sampler or floating distance selects the next point.

The declared reference section is rotated to the beginning of the path.
Production requests it twice before admitting the intervention fiber.
Equality of the two complete exact returns is the first recurrence witness.

If those two returns differ, the engine does not average them and does not
rename the second return as noise. `RecurrenceObstruction` retains:

- both occurrence identities;
- both complete returned sections;
- the exact differing constraints; and
- the still-compatible structural refinements
  `AddHiddenReceiverFiber`, `RefineBoundary`, `UnfoldTemporalState`,
  `AdmitStateDependentInteraction`, and `AdmitPopulationChange`.

The present law cannot decide among those refinements from one failed
return. It therefore stops without a grammar certificate.

`OrganizationalGrammarFiber` contains one such cell for every point of

## 4. Exact mixed differences derive the grammar

For one exact constraint value

\[
f:\mathcal X\to\mathbb Q,
\]

introduce one-hot indicators \(z_{i,v}\) for the selection of variant
\(v\in V_i\). Because at most one variant inhabits one site at a time, the
complete returned relation has a unique multilinear expansion on the
declared product:

\[
f(x)
=
c_\varnothing
+
\sum_{\varnothing\neq S}
c_S
\prod_{(i,v)\in S} z_{i,v}(x),
\]

where \(S\) contains at most one variant from each site.

Every coefficient is an exact Möbius difference:

\[
c_S
=
\sum_{T\subseteq S}
(-1)^{|S|-|T|}
f(x_T).
\]

Here \(x_T\) selects exactly the variants in \(T\) and leaves every other
site absent. The calculation uses arbitrary-precision rational addition and
subtraction only.

Consequences:

1. A one-site term is a first mixed difference.
2. A two-site term is the residual which cannot be assigned to either
   one-site face alone.
3. A \(k\)-site term is an exact irreducible interaction on that categorical
   face relative to all of its proper subfaces.
4. A zero coefficient removes that term without a tolerance.
5. Re-evaluating the emitted terms at every configuration must reproduce
   every returned value exactly.

The certificate is refused if any polynomial residual is nonzero.

This is a deterministic form of “fitting,” but its object is not a vector of
best-fit parameters. It is the exact incidence of differences over the face
lattice of the receiver's finite intervention complex.

## 5. Interaction remains hypergraphic

If a nonzero term \(c_S\) affects a constraint received at site \(q\), every
site occurring in \(S\) has a caused dependency toward \(q\). The complete
support \(S\) remains attached to every projected edge.

The edge projection is used only for reachability. It does not replace the
hyperedge. Thus an effect supported on

\[
\{p_a,p_b,p_c\}
\]

is not silently rewritten as three independent pair interactions.

This is the connection to the earlier bitwise and machine-language
trajectory. The Boolean cube is the one-variant-per-site special case.
Bitwise cuts become geometric discrete derivatives; mixed derivatives are
the higher faces. The categorical construction adds lineage substitution
without flattening it into another unrelated bit.

## 6. A holon is promoted from recurrent reciprocal support

Let \(G_F\) be the directed site graph induced by the nonzero interaction
supports. Production finds its strongly connected components.

A component \(H\subseteq P\) is promoted only when:

1. \(|H|\geq2\);
2. the induced dependency is mutually reachable;
3. at least one exact interaction support touches two or more sites of
   \(H\);
4. every received constraint in \(H\) has the repeated reference return;
5. every cross-boundary dependency remains an explicit port; and
6. the complete multilinear grammar replays the entire declared region with
   zero residual.

This is receiver-relative closure, not isolation. An inbound dependency from
outside \(H\) becomes an inbound port. An effect from \(H\) into an exterior
site becomes an outbound port.

An exterior site's reference standing is retained:

- `PresentAtReference` means it participates in the contemporary recurrent
  section; and
- `DormantAtReference` means it is a potential exterior contact which is
  absent at that section.

The latter is the precise result currently available for a transient
scaffold. The finite static law can identify a dormant scaffold port. It
does not yet reconstruct the earlier temporal episode in which that
scaffold may have helped form the composite.

## 7. Constraint provenance is plural

One constraint need not have one absolute origin. Its receipt separately
retains:

- a nonzero all-absent constant as `SourceSupplied`;
- any nonzero returned-difference term as `InteractionMaintained`;
- a term touching at least two sites of the promoted component as
  `CompositeMaintained`; and
- a term touching a site outside the component as
  `ExteriorConditioned`.

The same constraint may carry several of these kinds. This is necessary:
inheritance, local maintenance, composite maintenance, and exterior
conditioning are composable causal contributions, not mutually exclusive
semantic labels.

Every emitted term and closure receipt cites the source testimony events
from which it was derived. The self-emanated grammar does not overwrite its
lineage.

## 8. “Cancellation” becomes exact conserved support

For one promoted component and one common support \(S\), collect the
coefficients affecting its internal constraints:

\[
\{c_{S,j}:j\in H\}.
\]

If at least two constraints participate and

\[
\sum_{j\in H}c_{S,j}=0,
\]

production emits a `ConservedInteractionReceipt` containing every oriented
contribution and the exact zero residual.

This is one rigorous meaning of the earlier intuition that repeated ratios
or resonant patterns “cancel out.” The terms do not disappear. Their
oriented internal contributions close while the common support and each
receiver remain visible.

It is also the finite organizational analogue of diffusion current: a
nonzero transfer can move between receivers while its internal total
departure is exactly zero.

## 9. Lineage repair is predicted and then graded

Several variants declared at one site are inherited candidate replacements,
not certified homologues.

For variants \(v,w\in V_i\), production compares

\[
F(x[i\mapsto v])
\quad\text{and}\quad
F(x[i\mapsto w])
\]

over every returned context of the other sites.

One deterministically selected replacement configuration is withheld. If
all other paired contexts agree, production emits
`LineagePrediction`:

- the exact withheld query;
- its exact predicted complete constraint section;
- the source configuration from which that section is transported;
- every event supporting the comparison; and
- the number of supporting contexts.

Only then is the held-out return requested.

If the return agrees, the now-complete finite context family may emit
`LineageRepairCertificate`. The certificate also requires that the lineage
variant actually affects at least one constraint relative to absence; two
inert decoys are not called a repair merely because both do nothing.

If the held-out return differs:

- the returned cell still completes the exact grammar fiber;
- the residual and differing constraints become
  `LineagePredictionObstruction`;
- no repair certificate is emitted; and
- the complete nonhomologous interaction grammar remains available.

This is the direct computational relation to homologous recombination. The
machine does not equate two sequences by name or local resemblance alone.
It tests whether a lineage substitution preserves the receiver-relative
organizational relation through the declared surrounding contexts.

It does not yet model strand invasion, base pairing, Holliday junctions,
branch migration, mismatch repair, or physical DNA chemistry.
