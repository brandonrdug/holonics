## 1. What was built

- **`Scale::units`** (`crates/engine/src/field/units.rs`): `J` circles hold each founded unit's end and its proper prefixes at its key, the
  product of its symbols' drawn turns (a gene, its seed in the state), the marks charged by the delta charge and never stepped by a
  deposit. The level's cut (`level_cut_units`, `strand_level_cut_units`), on the host for both realizations and on the host's threads
  inside the card's batch, takes the units; with none it is the news's, bit for bit.
- **`found-units`** founds at a crossing from a sample read through the state (`Constitution::strand_epochs`): a closed epoch `u` of 2 to 8
  symbols, met `n̂` times by a candidate field's leaky count, is founded where `(n̂ − 1)(c − log₂(U + 2)) > log₂|P| + 1` (her code `c`
  over its places, `U` the units held, `|P|` the pool; the formal thread's charge of 22:55 UTC, prequential: the first meeting spells
  the run by its pieces). Its receipt names the runs founded, beside the state.
- **`read-epochs`** reads each row's anchors in one pass that keeps no tick.
- Gates: the host tests `a_recurring_run_is_founded_and_then_cut_whole` and `a_run_her_code_holds_is_not_founded`; gate 1; gate 3 of
  e5h1, the line with no units reproduced on 24 of 24 sections and its lap's read ([`gate3_e5h1.txt`](receipts/2026-11-27_founded_units/gate3_e5h1.txt)).

The first founding, on W134's end from 2,000 rows of 300 octets of the line's training slices, founded 1,111 units, the lengths 2 to 5 at

## 6. Same-cut is built in; the capacity is read against the type cut's ends

A cut that is a function of the last two or three octets cuts a word met again alike by its construction, wherever the word is preceded
alike (a store at a quarter of the capacity it needs still reads 0.78). The read of a store's capacity is the share of the type cut's own
ends it keeps (`kept`) and of its ends that stand in the type cut's (`in`). Under the count join, `ν̂(t) = Σ_k n_k ν̄_k / Σ_k n_k`, the
last octet's mean weighs most everywhere: her mean news after the last octet alone keeps 0.958 of the peak ends of depths 1 and 2 and
0.947 of those of depths 1 to 8, and reads 0.951 alike at 3.06 places an epoch
([`store_agreement.txt`](receipts/2026-11-27_founded_units/store_agreement.txt),
[`epoch_cut_depth1_peak.txt`](receipts/2026-11-27_founded_units/epoch_cut_depth1_peak.txt)). So the levels' agreement at depths 1 and 2
against 8 is the join's (the formal thread's reading, G-Z72); a deeper run read as its increment over its parent at its credibility is
its own arm, after this one.
