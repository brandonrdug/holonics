# Units founded on the level cut a recurring word less alike than her news, and the rise of her mean news after a run cuts it alike in 97 of 100 meetings

November 27 and 28. Refs #404. The review of November 27 (its build step 1: units founded where the line trains, October's lap law
on the card) and the formal thread's laws of November 27 22:55 UTC to November 28 01:08 UTC. Receipts:
[`receipts/2026-11-27_founded_units/`](receipts/2026-11-27_founded_units/). Counts only: the founded runs and the passages are private.

Computational object: the helical pair interaction, a run's key as the product of its symbols' turns on drawn circles. The reads touch
the helix (the keys), faces and placement (her code at each place, the level's cut) and the tube (the level's epochs); the pair
contacts, cell holonomy and the tower thread stay as the line has them.

## 1. What was built

- **`Scale::units`** (`crates/engine/src/field/units.rs`): `J` circles hold each founded unit's end and its proper prefixes at its key, the
  product of its symbols' drawn turns (a gene, its seed in the state), the marks charged by the delta charge and never stepped by a
  deposit. The level's cut (`level_cut_units`, `strand_level_cut_units`), on the host for both realizations and on the host's threads
  inside the card's batch, takes the units; with none it is the news's, bit for bit.
- **`found-units`** founds at a crossing from a sample read through the state (`Constitution::strand_epochs`): a closed epoch `u` of 2 to 8
  symbols, met `n̂` times by a candidate field's leaky count, is founded where `(n̂ − 1)(c − log₂(U + 2)) > log₂|P| + 1` (her code `c`
  over its places, `U` the units held, `|P|` the pool; the formal thread's charge of 22:55 UTC, prequential: the first meeting spells
  the run by its pieces). Its receipt names the runs founded, beside the state.
- **`read-epochs`** reads each row's anchors in one pass that keeps no tick.
- Gates: the host tests `a_recurring_run_is_founded_and_then_cut_whole` and `a_run_her_code_holds_is_not_founded`; gate 1; gate 3 of
  e5h1, the line with no units reproduced on 24 of 24 sections and its lap's read ([`gate3_e5h1.txt`](receipts/2026-11-27_founded_units/gate3_e5h1.txt)).

The first founding, on W134's end from 2,000 rows of 300 octets of the line's training slices, founded 1,111 units, the lengths 2 to 5 at
a cap of `J/18` = 227 each ([`found_u1.log`](receipts/2026-11-27_founded_units/found_u1.log)).

## 3. The rise of her mean news after a run

The formal thread's law of 00:58 UTC: a span's ends are where its readings agree, a property of its type read as a mean over its meetings.
`ν̄(s)` is her code at the place after a run `s` of 1 to 8 octets, averaged over its meetings in the sample through W134's end (1,448,560
runs, 4,744,000 meetings); at each place the runs ending there join by their counts, and an end stands after `t` where it rises
(`tools/rise_cut_ruler.py`, a ruler with no units; [`rise_cut.txt`](receipts/2026-11-27_founded_units/rise_cut.txt)).

|---|---|---|---|
| rise, `ν̂(t) > ν̂(t − 1)` | 2.07 | 0.974 | 0.973 |

So the stability the review asks of step 1 (a span cut alike at least 90% of the time) is held by a cut read from the mean of her news
over a run's meetings, and not by founded units read through her own epochs. What the level holds next is that mean as a store of its
own, charged with her news after each run; its capacity is the open part, since a sample holds 1.4 million distinct runs and a field of
4,096 circles a depth holds the frequent short ones.

## 4. Two host reads at three threads ran the PC out of memory

At 23:37 UTC on November 27 two `read-strand-cut` reads of the sixteen 3,700-place rows, three threads each (a pass that keeps every
tick: 10.9 GB at two threads), stood beside gate 3 and a lap's start, and the kernel's out-of-memory killer took an instance on the PC.
Such reads now run one at a time on two threads, after a lap's other reads, and `read-epochs` reads the cut in a pass that keeps no tick.
