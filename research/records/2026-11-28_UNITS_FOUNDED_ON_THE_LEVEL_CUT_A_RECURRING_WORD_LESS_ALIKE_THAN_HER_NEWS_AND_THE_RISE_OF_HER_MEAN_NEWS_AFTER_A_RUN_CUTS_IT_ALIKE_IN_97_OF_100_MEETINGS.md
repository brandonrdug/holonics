  over its places, `U` the units held, `|P|` the pool; the formal thread's charge of 22:55 UTC, prequential: the first meeting spells

- **`read-epochs`** reads each row's anchors in one pass that keeps no tick.
- Gates: the host tests `a_recurring_run_is_founded_and_then_cut_whole` and `a_run_her_code_holds_is_not_founded`; gate 1; gate 3 of
  e5h1, the line with no units reproduced on 24 of 24 sections and its lap's read ([`gate3_e5h1.txt`](receipts/2026-11-27_founded_units/gate3_e5h1.txt)).
