## Shared engine ownership

[definition] `exact_linear::BilinearOperator` retains a bilinear target in its ordered tensor
chart. `BilinearProductCore` retains left/right linear forms and their product image;
`BilinearRealization` shares that immutable core and retains its full affine receiver family.
Application uses `D((A x) .* (B z))`. Receiver composition preserves the product core, and port
exchange transports the tensor indexing. A fixed opposite port yields a linear section whose
preimage may be singleton, plural or absent. No inverse is assumed.

[definition] The public `BilinearSupportSearch` streams a caller-declared coefficient grammar
and product-count aperture. Its pending support is explicit and its iterator can be retained
or cloned as immutable exterior search state. Enumeration order is a coordinate order, not
native semantic preference. A stopped search does not claim exhaustion or a global optimum.
The original generator-factorization example now calls this shared owner instead of privately
solving each target row. Existing `exact_linear::contextual` factorization also calls the new
shared `ExactRatMatrix::factor_receiver` owner.

[established-bounded; implemented-exact] That receiver owner returns either the complete
`LinearMapFamily` satisfying `D S=T`, or a source-null vector that the requested target reads
nontrivially. It verifies the returned factorization or separator. Duplicate products retain
free decoder-row directions. The existing mathematical
`Mathematics/GeneratorFactorization.lean` coefficient certificate and receiver/history laws
supply the formal interpretation; no new runtime proof-assistant binding is introduced.

## The native consumer

[definition] `resident_section::ResidentBilinearMap` is the pure native consumer of a returned
realization. Its exact coefficient codec uses integer numerators and a common denominator;
non-dyadic rationals are preserved. Signed-word overflow, empty native ports, foreign surfaces
and incompatible shapes return their declared representation obstruction.

[definition] The two added packet kernels reuse existing checked wide integer multiplication,
addition, positive-denominator validation, `fibre_normalize` and checked word conversion. The
native passage contracts both source ports, forms the rational elementwise product and applies
the receiver, retaining all intermediate sections on the GPU. The branches share no mutable
state; the Hadamard occurrence depends on both, and the final receiver depends on the product.
There is no BF16 conversion or intermediate host numerical replay.

[definition] `ResidentBilinearReturn` retains the source linear images, product and output.
A second map's `read_product` checks the shared immutable core and the resident surface before
applying its receiver to that same product occurrence. It performs one new contraction rather
than replaying both source contractions and the product. The exact scalar-field/core relation
is invariant; the application receiver has changed. This pure operation does not mutate Athena
material or claim completion of Eros's developing-family transaction.

## The complete inspected application

[definition] `hephaestus_operator_cycle.rs` requests complex multiplication from the public
support search, binds a polynomial receiver to the returned core, composes a middle-coefficient
receiver, reads an actual source preimage, and exhibits the distinction lost by keeping only the
complex face. It restores that distinction through the existing contextual lift. It then mounts
the same returned factors natively, computes the complex result, and applies the polynomial
receiver to the retained resident product. Terminal outputs are compared with the exact owner;
section readout count is checked before either terminal read.

[established-bounded; implemented-exact] The input `(2,3)` and `(4,5)` means two linear
polynomials or two complex coordinates, depending on the receiver. Complex multiplication
returns `(-7,22)`; polynomial multiplication returns `(8,22,15)`; the composed middle receiver
returns `22`. These are different faces of the retained product construction. Keeping the
complex face alone cannot reconstruct all polynomial coefficients; the returned annihilator
exposes the lost distinction. The actual execution receipt is retained with verification below.

## The fixed-port family return and the remaining Eros joint

[established-bounded; implemented-exact] `BilinearOperator::left_family_preimage` composes the
fixed-port section with an actual affine source family `x=a+Bθ`, then solves
`F_h B θ = observed − F_h a` using the existing exact preimage owner. Shared parameter columns
remain correlated. For `x=(1+θ,1−θ)`, the dot-product receiver with right port `(1,1)` leaves
θ free when the observed face is 2 and rejects face 3. With right port `(1,2)` and observed
face 4 it returns θ=−1. This is a conditional source-family observation, not an average of
sources or a new native material deposit.

[open; source-inspected] The native producing comparison retains coefficient rows over its
source family. With source `x(θ)`, variable condition h and material m, the complete relation
contains `x(θ) ⊗ h`. Treating these lifted mixed coordinates as independent admits false
combinations. At fixed h and m the source observation has an affine preimage as above; varying
both ports requires their joint consistency relation. Equal marginal populations do not supply
it: diagonal and off-diagonal pairings of `{e₁,e₂}` have equal marginals and distinct products.
The general consuming condition/material transaction remains unbound. Existing point-source
preimage/contact/formation and one-successor publication are reused when that joint relation
is supplied; no universal point gate is added to unrelated generation.
