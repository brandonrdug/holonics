## 0. The one-sentence finding

**A stored floating species is a quotient, so the delta a cultivation deposits is never the delta
its derivation proposed — it is the one the seal admitted — and withdrawing the proposed one fails
on a measured population.** Subtracting the derived delta returned **42 of 64** coordinates;
withdrawing `decode(round(g + δ)) − g` returns **64 of 64** and the predecessor's conduct exactly.

## 2. The obstruction the station returned

The first honest ablation — withdraw the delta from the **cultivated rest** rather than reseal the
predecessor from the source — returned **42 of 64** coordinates and the predecessor's conduct
**false**.

The mechanism is `TABLET_THE_OPERATIONS` verbatim: **an inverse exists only for a rebase.** Rounding
into `bfloat16` is a *quotient*, and a quotient has no inverse; it has a fibre. Writing the seal as
`ρ` and the derived delta as `δ`:

```text
committed word   w = ρ(g + δ)
withdrawal       ρ(ρ⁻¹(w) − δ) = ρ(g + ε)      where ε is the residual ρ deleted
returns g        exactly when |ε| < ulp(g)/2
```

When `g` and `g + δ` sit in different binades, `ulp(g + δ) > ulp(g)` and the deleted residual is too
coarse to re-round back. **22 of 64 coordinates crossed a binade.** That is not noise and it is not a
tolerance to be widened; it is the compression's remainder appearing exactly where the doctrine says
it must.

## 3. The repair, and why it is not bookkeeping

**What was deposited is what the seal admitted.**

```text
admitted   = decode(ρ(g + δ)) − g
collapsed  = δ − admitted            the seal's own remainder, retained and reported
```

The ablation withdraws `admitted`, and then `decode(w) − admitted = g` exactly, so re-rounding
returns the predecessor word at **every** coordinate. Measured: **64 of 64 returned, 0 kept by the
grain, the predecessor's conduct returns `true`.**

The collapsed population is **64 entries**, widest `38957348000494475/85484682541080957` — roughly
`0.456`, which is the same order as the deltas themselves. The seal is not a fine adjustment to the
derivation; it is a coarse re-presentation of it, and reporting the derived delta as the deposited
one would have been the absolute-volume violation at the level of a training step.

`CLAUDE.md` §0k: *a compression is a codec pivot carrying a declared decoder*, and the first chart's
face returns only by running an algorithm. Here the algorithm is `decode ∘ ρ`, the remainder is
exhibited, and the identity of what was deposited follows from the decoder rather than from the
proposal.

**Both were green on the first pass and neither carried evidence.**

| *subject-disjoint material unchanged* | true **by construction of the commit** — 2496 coordinates were never written | reported as grading the **seal's locality**, not the cultivation |

This is `CLAUDE.md` §8's third rule firing on its own author: *a check whose material cannot vary the
property under test is the same defect as a check that cannot fail; it just wears a passing result.*
The question that catches it is stated there — **which declared input, if I varied it across two
members of the same returned class, would move them apart?** For the ablation the answer was *"the
one I set equal for both"*: the source bytes.

## 7. The grade, and its boundary

Cultivation passes on the definition `CLAUDE.md` §0n gives for hexis — *rested conditional transport
that survives rest/remount, changes later conduct, and whose targeted removal removes the
attributable consequence*:

1. **Survives rest and remount.** Three arms sealed as native rests; each conducted by a **fresh
   process** handed only its own rest, with no source path in its reach.
2. **Changes later conduct.** Held-out conduct moves, and is separated from both the predecessor's
   and the matched sibling's by a declared receiver family with the face named.
3. **Targeted removal removes it.** Withdrawing the deposited delta from the cultivated body returns
   64 of 64 coordinates and the predecessor's conduct exactly.

**What this does not claim.** One development pair does not establish transfer. The held-out
separation says the deposited delta's **coordinate incidence** is load-bearing on material it was
never derived from; it does not say the delta generalizes, and no receiver here was asked whether
anything moved **toward** anything. The aperture is one site's contact half, one rebase population,
64 of 2560 coordinates, and three caused symbols.

## 8. Falsifiers

- **The seal claim.** Find a stored species and a `(g, δ)` pair, both in-range, where
  `ρ(decode(ρ(g+δ)) − (decode(ρ(g+δ)) − g)) ≠ ρ(g)`. The repair asserts this cannot happen because
  the subtraction is exact and `g` is already a stored word; a counterexample refutes it.
- **The separation.** Withdraw `ReceiverId(2·k+1)` at coordinate 149 and find that no other face in
  the family separates ARM L from ARM R. That would make the separation a single-receiver artifact
  rather than a property of the conduct.
- **The vacuity finding.** Exhibit a variation of the source bytes that moves the resealed-predecessor
  ablation's verdict. If none exists, the first form of that control was vacuous, which is the claim.
- **The tolerance reading.** Declare a finer stored species — `binary32` — and re-take the
  two-grain table. If ARM L's stored-species column rises to 64 of 64 there, the 19 is a fact about
  `bfloat16` and not about the cultivation, which is exactly what the reading says it is.
