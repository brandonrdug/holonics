## The correction

[definition] A bare scalar in this framework is a receiver face. Reversing a scalar face means
solving a lifting problem against its declared source receiver and returning the entire inverse
image. Context may reduce that fibre; context may not license a guessed representative.

[definition] The word `divisor` is now split into four typed meanings:

1. `NatFactorWitness d n`: a lift through `k |-> d*k` in one natural-number chart;
2. `PartitionReceipt`: an occurrence-to-piece map with paired oriented cut boundaries;
3. `WeilDivisorLedger`: a finite signed population over declared codimension-one prime loci; and
4. `LatticeIndexReceipt`: an integer-linear transport whose returned objects are kernel, image,
   cokernel, index, torsion witness, saturation, and complete lift fibres.

[definition] None of those objects is an untyped operation which divides an arbitrary holon.

## Returned formal owners

### Transport lifting

[proved-derived; formal-checked]
`ElementaryHolonics/Foundation/TransportLift.lean` defines
`TransportLift transport target = {source // transport source = target}` and proves:

- a lift exists exactly when the target lies in the transport range;
- a range obstruction makes the lift fibre empty;
- singleton fibres are exactly unique-source fibres;
- two additive lifts differ by a kernel direction;
- every inhabited additive fibre is equivalent to the kernel; and
- a trivial additive kernel makes each inhabited target fibre unique.

[definition] This is the matrix/tensor replacement for scalar division. A nonsurjective map returns
an obstruction; a noninjective map returns a fibre; an isomorphism is the special case in which all
relevant fibres are inhabited singletons.

(firstPotential, secondPotential)

### Exact partition and paired boundary

[proved-derived; formal-checked]
`ElementaryHolonics/Foundation/ExactPartition.lean` proves that an occurrence population is
equivalent to the dependent sum of the fibres of its piece-address map. Paired cut sides retain an
involutive mate and an integer hand; the mate reverses the hand and each glued pair sums to zero.

[proved-derived; formal-checked] Equal piece measure implies
`total = pieceCount * unitMeasure` only after equality of every addressed piece measure is supplied.
The partition object itself assumes no equal area, angle, mass, or capacity.

[proved-derived; formal-checked] The same file isolates arithmetic factorization. A
`NatFactorWitness d n` is equivalent to `d | n`; if `d > 0`, two witnesses have the same cofactor.
This proves a property of the scalar multiplication receiver only.

### Signed divisor and Cartier atlas

[proved-derived; formal-checked]
`ElementaryHolonics/Geometry/DivisorAtlas.lean` defines a `WeilDivisorLedger` as a finitely supported
integer population over a declared prime-locus type. Effectivity is coordinatewise nonnegativity.
A `PrincipalDivisorSystem` proves

```text
divisor(f*g) = divisor(f) + divisor(g)
divisor(f^-1) = -divisor(f)
divisor(f/g) = divisor(f) - divisor(g).
```

[proved-derived; formal-checked] A `CartierAtlas` carries a chart cover, local equations, admitted
unit transitions, local change, and the overlap cocycle. Reverse transitions compose to one. A
`CartierDivisorAtlas` additionally requires admitted units to have zero principal ledger, and the
local divisor ledgers then agree on every occupied overlap.

[definition] Here the quotient of local equations is literally a chart-transition witness. The
geometric content lies in the declared codimension-one loci and admitted units, not in arithmetic
division syntax.

### Lattice transport

[proved-derived; formal-checked]
`ElementaryHolonics/Foundation/LatticeTransport.lean` returns an integer-linear map's kernel,
image, cokernel, exact finite index, complete lift fibre, and cokernel torsion submodule. A target
has a lift exactly when its cokernel class vanishes. A target is saturated by the image exactly when
its cokernel class has an exhibited nonzero-integer torsion witness. If the image is saturated, every
such represented torsion class vanishes.

### Place ledger bridge

## Cross-problem placement

[definition] The common exact route is now:

```text
situated source
  -> typed transport / partition / chart occurrence
  -> receiver face
  -> complete lift or reconstruction fibre
  -> kernel, boundary, signed-locus, valuation, or cokernel return
  -> compatible gluing across charts, places, and scales
  -> official problem receiver.
```

[open] For RH the missing passage is still the complete prime/zero/archimedean divisor identity and
positive Weil receiver. For BSD it is the actual elliptic divisor-class/local-height/Selmer--`Sha`
and analytic ledger. For Hodge it is an official variety joining Cartier/Weil divisors and higher
cycles to the rational cycle-class map. For Yang--Mills it is bundle transition, reflection-positive
scale transport, and continuum reconstruction. For knots and three-manifolds it is a geometric
connected-sum/Reidemeister quotient rather than arithmetic factorization. For fluids it is the
typed partition/lift/boundary/lattice composition; divisor vocabulary becomes valid only after an
actual zero, pole, or codimension-one defect locus is founded.
