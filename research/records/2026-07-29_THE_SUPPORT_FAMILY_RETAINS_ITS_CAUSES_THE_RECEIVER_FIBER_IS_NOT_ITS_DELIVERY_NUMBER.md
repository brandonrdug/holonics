## The two rejected representations

The prior synchronized GRID construction had already rejected:

1. a Cartesian enumeration in which independently selectable facet pairs
   multiplied into explicit support sections; and
2. a connected-component closure in which reusable local facets were copied
   into one monolithic `LiveConstituent`.

The remaining `Vec<LiveSupportSection>` still described every active support
face as a completed flat boundary set. Even when no Cartesian product was
enumerated, recurrent joins had to repeat the complete boundary union.
The representation retained the result of the join but not its causal
factorization.

The present audit found a separate delivery-order leak in
`SynchronizedOccurrenceChart`. It interned a newly seen
`SynchronizedSectionFace` into a sequential integer and then converted that
integer into a `Cog`. Consequently:

- the same physical receiver face could receive a different durable
  interface identity when unrelated material was delivered first; and
- an arbitrary plural receiver face was being collapsed into a bounded
  scalar solely to fit the existing carrier.

This was administration masquerading as causation. Keeping receiver-arrival
stage in the physical face is necessary; making first delivery allocate the
face's identity is not.

## Production factor family

`soma-membrane::support_family` is now the production owner of exact support
factorization.

One `LiveSupportExpression` is either:

```text
leaf(direct boundaries)
```

or:

```text
join(prior causal expressions, directly emitted seam boundaries).
```

`LiveSupportFamily` canonicalizes those expressions into one topologically
ordered, structurally deduplicated DAG:

```text
F = (nodes, active roots)
node = (cause-node addresses, directly emitted boundaries).
```

The roots are the independently addressable active support factors.
Non-root nodes remain exact causal lineage. A join does not flatten its
causes into a copied boundary union in standing. The complete boundary set is
a rebuildable execution quotient obtained by one topological traversal.

Canonicalization sorts and deduplicates the structural expressions, orders
them by causal depth and exact expression order, assigns addresses only after
that ordering, and rejects noncanonical wire images. Factor insertion order
therefore cannot become standing identity.

The family implements:

- construction from historical explicit support sections as degenerate
  leaves;
- exact structural join;
- boundary rebase and complete remap;
- departure of a factor whose caused boundary is absent;
- founding of uncovered selected boundaries as new leaves;
- active-factor boundary restriction;
- exact node, cause-edge, direct-boundary, and resolved-boundary censuses;
- canonical native persistence and remount; and
- bounded explicit expansion only as an inspection compatibility surface.

Production code does not call explicit expansion for closure or persistence.

## Closure, radiation, and lineage

`LiveConstituent` now owns `LiveSupportFamily`, not
`Vec<LiveSupportSection>`. Historical membrane declarations enter as leaf
families immediately.

When regional cofaces meet:

1. unparticipating roots carry forward structurally;

3. only the new seam boundary is stored directly on the joined expression;
4. equal structural expressions deduplicate canonically;

6. returned OPEN, RIDE, and FOUND transitions are carried back to those

Shared event incidence may still assemble one holistic constituent. It no
longer erases the distinction between its support factors or requires a
recurrent factor to repeat all prior boundary words. A whole connected
standing ecology is not copied merely because one factor recurs.
