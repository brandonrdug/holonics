## The distinction the implementation must preserve

[definition] In `y=rho_F(G_Theta(s,c))`, G_Theta is the generating mathematical operation.
Its retained realization can include parameters, morphology, incidence, recursively composed
constituents and the conditions required for its conduct. A model's parameter tensors are one
representation of retained material; the architecture and execution conventions realize the
function. Holonic formation develops the corresponding functions, their composition and their
applicability from situated differences. A generator need not be emitted as a source-code string
or represented by a closed symbolic formula to execute.

[definition] A code-generating model has code among its requested output faces. An exporter
instead gives another executable representation of an already-retained mathematical operation.
The former does not require the latter as its generating mechanism. A Rust syntax printer can
be a useful exterior receiver without supplying the model's formation, contextual organization
or prediction. Hephaestus includes tool/code uses of the shared construction, not an internal
source-code compiler in place of that construction.

[definition] Fractal/recursive generation and the game-world analogy concern reuse of generating
relations and receiver-relevant expansion. They do not prescribe a global character loop,
enumeration of every interior state, or a separate task-specific function authored for each
example. Equal output at one receiver still does not identify its different generating causes.
