corpus, and it was headlined as though it were.** The reader builds
`BTreeMap<statement, BTreeMap<tactic, u32>>` — a multiset of tactic counts, **already order-free

the grade and on the cancelled rational. Both are identities of the algebra's own definition: the
product's target *is* the symmetric difference of the masks and its structure constant *is*
`∏_{S∩T} k`. By the tautology rule a receipt that could not have come out otherwise carries no
evidence about the material. It is a genuine two-frame check on the implementation and is worth
exactly that. **What is about the material is the grade-size census and the pair comparison — 459
proof pairs sharing a grade against 1,401 differing.**

**The certificate first reached 19 of 79,189, and the cause was mine.** `squarefree_kernel` refuses a

verification from **19 to 77,063**. Nothing in the algebra was at fault, and reporting the first
figure as an aperture of the carrier would have been false.

**Two rules were forced by the run and both are corrections to my adjudication:**

1. **An organ may only judge a head whose STATED group it contains.** Without it, the run reported
   `associator`'s full three-letter symmetry as REFUTED by a triangle corner — which says nothing
   about associators. The corpus's already-written symmetries are the evidence that an organ is a
   candidate model; a different operation refuting a proposal is not evidence the proposal is false.

2. **An `organ-predicted` proposal needs two qualified organs, not one.** `symmetric-pair-sum-3`
   contains every arity-3 transposition group, so it qualifies for every such head and predicted full
   symmetry for twenty heads at once. A `group-implied` proposal carries two frames by construction —
   the corpus's own generators close to it, and an organ agrees — but an organ-predicted one has only
   the organ. Single-witness proposals are **retained by name and never deposited**.
