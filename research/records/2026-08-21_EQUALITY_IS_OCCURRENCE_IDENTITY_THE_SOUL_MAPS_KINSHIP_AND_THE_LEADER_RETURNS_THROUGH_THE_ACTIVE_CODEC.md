## 1. Verdict

[definition] For situated holons, unqualified equality means occurrence identity: one owner-minted
occurrence, not two. A deterministic rerun, clone, reserialization, rechart, or later state is not
literally equal to its predecessor. It may be structurally isomorphic, doctrinally equivalent,
receiver/history equivalent, byte-equal at an encoding, or similar under declared characteristics.

[definition] Equality inside an already-declared mathematical carrier remains lawful. If
`a,b : X`, then `a =_X b` is equality in `X`; it is not a claim that two embodied inscriptions,
events, or computations are one occurrence. The historical correction *“same is struck for souls,
not for typed equality”* is retained.

[definition] A causal signature is not a soul. It is a declared receiver/history face of a marked
causal diagram. Two signatures may be naturally equivalent while their occurrences, lineages and
apparatus histories remain distinct.

[definition] Activity has at least four noninterchangeable cuts: apparatus switching, residency,
realization-path participation, and receiver-visible causal support. Each cut is binary at one
declared occurrence. Replacing all four by one scalar “activation” or denying the first two because
the third is graded is a type error.

[interpretation] The information centrifuge is an oriented intervention/transport complex. Its
rotations and reflections are real mathematical operations: the swing is a conjugated half-turn;

## 8. The 20W construction stance and contemporary efficiency evidence

[project-postulate] Intelligence does not require gigawatt-scale computation. Cost is expected to
follow active causal extent and morphology rather than total parameter, corpus or hypothesis-space
extent. This does not erase resource law; it rejects current industrial expenditure as a necessary
property of intelligence and sets aggressive efficiency as a construction requirement.

[historical] Current primary evidence supports the direction without proving this programme:

- [Gemma 4](https://deepmind.google/models/gemma/gemma-4/) is explicitly presented as maximizing
  intelligence per parameter; its E2B/E4B variants target mobile/IoT and its larger variants target
  consumer GPUs.
- [DiffusionGemma](https://deepmind.google/models/gemma/diffusiongemma/) is a 26B-total MoE that
  activates 3.8B parameters at inference and is reported to fit 24 GB consumer cards when
  quantized.
- [MatFormer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.07707) trains nested feed-forward submodels and extracts
  many elastic inference sizes from one body.
- [Mixture-of-Depths](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.02258) makes token participation dynamic and
  context-sensitive while retaining a predictable total compute budget.
- [QLoRA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14314) demonstrated 65B adaptation on one 48 GB GPU by returning
  gradients through a frozen 4-bit body into low-rank overlays.
- [GaLore](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.03507) reported 7B pretraining on a 24 GB RTX 4090 and large
  optimizer-memory reductions by gradient low-rank projection.
- [BitNet b1.58 2B4T](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.12285) reports comparable performance to similar-size
  full-precision open models with native 1.58-bit weights and lower inference memory/energy/latency.
- [FlashAttention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.14135) demonstrates that exact attention can become
  substantially cheaper by respecting memory hierarchy and IO rather than changing its semantics.

[interpretation] The common lesson is not one winning technique. Conditional participation,
nested morphology, low-rank update transport, extreme native quantization and IO-aware exact
execution are distinct demonstrations that parameter count does not determine active work or the
cost of adaptation. Holonics should derive those reductions from causal consequence rather than
importing their authored ranks, top-k capacities, floats or scalar objectives.
