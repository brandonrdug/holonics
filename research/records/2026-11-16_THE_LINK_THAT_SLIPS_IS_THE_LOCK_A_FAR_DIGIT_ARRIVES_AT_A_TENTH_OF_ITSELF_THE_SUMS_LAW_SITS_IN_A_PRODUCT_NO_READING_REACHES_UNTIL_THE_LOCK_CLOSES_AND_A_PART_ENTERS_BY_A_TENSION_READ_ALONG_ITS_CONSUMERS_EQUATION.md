## 5. On the objects

- **The pair contact slips.** A contact addresses by locking, and a lock is a threshold: within its capture range a receiver follows one
  source and rejects the rest; outside it, it slips and reads their mean. The engine's contact is the mean. Its Gibbs face is the lock's
  warm phase, read at one temperature for the whole passage, and its ticks are a running mean on a site that only fills (the record of
  November 13), so a row blurs across the ticks where a lock would close. The parametron's lock, the object the theory counts capacity in,
  has no realization in the engine.
- **Why the deposit has not closed it.** A lock's covector is each source's use to the row. At a column both operands hold, one digit
  alone is of no use to any reading (§4.1), so those places press on no lock. The copy columns press, and they are the lesser part of the
  digits, on a temperature every open place of the passage shares. From lap 72 to lap 76 head 0's share on an aligned digit went from 0.21
  to 0.17 (different records, the same receiver).
- **The staircase, in its measured order.** The lock closing on the column opens the copy at once (a linear read, §4.4). With the lock
  closed the junction's products are locatable and five of them carry the sum (§4.4). The carry is the next step after those: the
  readings above hold the carry's state fixed within each kind of place, and what brings it along the strand is the carry circle.
- **Friction, read at the contact.** The world answers the line's acts and the covector it returns says which sum was meant
  (the table's 0.95). The line has no contact that brings the operands to the place where that covector lands. The feedback is there; the
  grip is not.

## 6. The method, as rules with their reasons

Written into CLAUDE.md's Method the same day.

1. **A premise is a presence at the consumer, read before the build.** A law's consumer equation is traced link by link on the machine's
   own states by a host ruler, each link read as the share of the operand present at the next place, and the law enters at the first link
   that slips. A ratio of shares is not a presence. Reason: the junction was built, realized on the card, held to the line and trained for
   two laps on a ratio of 7 to 8, while ten minutes of host reading show its operands arriving at a tenth of themselves.
2. **A part enters by its tension read.** Before a part is ordered, three numbers are read at its locus on a held half: what the present
   faces remove of the covector there, what the law's table removes (the ceiling), and what the part's own operands, located from the
   tension, remove. A part whose founding is a saddle (its operands' covectors vanish while its return is zero) enters with located
   operands or not at all. Where nothing locates, the part waits and the reading is the result. Reason: the founding law of October 24
   was restated four times in November and never run; every part entered at zero and was waited for.
3. **The locator is held to a planted law first.** A ruler that reports "nothing located" has recovered a planted product on the same
   shapes in the same run. Reason: a null from an unproved instrument reads the instrument.
4. **A ruler's read is costed against the card it reads beside.** Host readings beside a lap state their threads, and the lap's time
   beside them is read. Reason: lap 76 took 3,154 s against lap 75's 2,942 s while up to eighteen host threads read beside it.
5. **A clock stamp is read from the clock.** Reason: tool calls run faster than they feel, and the guess file's first stamp was a
   minute off.
