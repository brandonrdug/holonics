## One transport schema, not one constitutive law

For a caused path

\[

\]

the elementary transport is the ordered composite

\[

\]

Order is part of the object. Replacing this word by a set of contacts, a sum of scores, or an
unordered cache destroys noncommuting chronology. A closed path retains holonomy

\[
\operatorname{Hol}(\gamma)=U_\gamma,
\]

relative to its receiver chart. The equality or obstruction of that return is then inspectable;
it is not inferred from a drawing of a loop.

For a smooth local connection (A), the corresponding notation is

\[
U_\gamma=\mathcal P\exp\left(\int_\gamma A\right).
\]

The path-ordering symbol matters. The Dyson expansion integrates over ordered simplices:

\[
U(t)=I+
\sum_{n\ge 1}
\int_{0<t_n<\cdots<t_1<t}
A(t_1)\cdots A(t_n)\,dt_n\cdots dt_1.
\]

When the generator is homogeneous and commutes with itself, each ordered (n)-simplex has volume
(t^n/n!), and the expression collapses to

\[
U(t)=\sum_{n\ge0}\frac{t^nA^n}{n!}=e^{tA}.
\]

This gives a precise meeting point between the residue record's combinatorial history quotient
and continuous transport: the exponential is the receiver face formed when ordered attachment
histories become indistinguishable to a commuting homogeneous receiver. It does **not** say that
every ecology is governed by one scalar exponential. Noncommuting, inhomogeneous, branched, and
obstructed histories retain more structure than (e^{tA}).

The matrix exponential is a one-parameter group,

\[
e^{sA}e^{tA}=e^{(s+t)A},

while (e^Ae^B=e^{A+B}) requires the relevant commutation. This is the exact reason a lawful
compression can reuse a recurrent local generator without erasing ordered holonomy

\[

## Traffic separates three rates

The elementary conservation law for a one-dimensional traffic body is

\[
\partial_t\rho+\partial_xQ(\rho)=0.
\]

Three quantities which are often verbally collapsed are already different:

\[
v_{\mathrm{carrier}}=\frac{Q(\rho)}{\rho},
\qquad
v_{\mathrm{phase/front}}=Q'(\rho),
\qquad
s_{\mathrm{shock}}=
\frac{Q(\rho_R)-Q(\rho_L)}{\rho_R-\rho_L}.
\]

Vehicles may move forward while a congestion front moves backward. A broad road network is latent
conductance, not automatically expensive active traffic. Congestion occurs where too many currents
must cross the same capacity-limited boundary. Classical kinematic-wave and dynamic-traffic
assignment models provide the domain-specific equations; holonics contributes receiver, lineage,
and transported-local assembly rather than replacing those models
([Richards](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/abs/10.1287/opre.4.1.42),
[Daganzo](https://its.berkeley.edu/node/2283),
[FHWA dynamic traffic assignment](https://ops-dr.fhwa.dot.gov/publications/fhwahop13015/sec2.htm)).

In language, the direct analogues are kept explicitly typed:

- **carrier rate:** newly caused passage occurrences crossing a conditioning membrane per causal
  wave;
- **phase rate:** ordered relation passages carried inside the contemporary thought population per
  causal wave; and
- **front rate:** demanded entity regions returned by that population per causal wave.

These are exact discrete receiver quotients. They are not physical velocities, probabilities, or

## Biological cycles retain boundary potential

Photosynthetic energy transport is not one scalar diffusion which stops when direct illumination
stops. Excitation, electron transport, transmembrane ion current, membrane potential, pH gradient,
redox state, pigment state, ATP formation, storage, and growth are distinct current species and
receiver faces. The proton-motive force partitions into electrical and chemical components; ion
transport changes that partition and therefore changes later energy conversion
([Nature Plants review](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41477-021-00947-5)).

This is the biological version of retained boundary current:

\[
x=[j_{\mathrm{arriving}}\mid b_{\mathrm{stored}}\mid m_{\mathrm{material}}],
\qquad
y=T(\lambda)x,
\qquad
\lambda'=F(\lambda,b',m').
\]

The later passage sees the morphology caused by the earlier return. Biological rest is therefore
plural and receiver-relative. A pigment can have no present optical excitation while membrane,
redox, or chemical storage remains away from its earlier state.

## Lightning, oceans, optics, and cosmology share the distinction, not one equation

A lightning leader founds and changes a conductive channel; a return stroke then travels through
that caused channel. Leader growth, electrical current, optical radiation, heating, acoustic
shock, and later channel response have distinct fronts. The same event can therefore have several
receiver arrival orders without becoming several unrelated events.

Ocean circulation similarly combines material advection, pressure and density waves, rotating
frames, thermohaline exchange, wind forcing, boundaries, and slower morphology change. A current
map is a receiver quotient of that coupled body, not its source law. NOAA explicitly distinguishes
surface forcing and deep density-driven circulation
([NOAA ocean currents](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/ocean-fact/currents/)); regional circulation
still requires its measured doctrine rather than analogy alone
([NOAA Pacific circulation](https://www.pmel.noaa.gov/pubs/outstand/kess2580/dynamics.shtml)).

Optical radiative transfer separates free streaming, absorption, out-scattering, and in-scattering
along a ray. Its integral operator is another path-conditioned current, not permission to call
language electromagnetic radiation
([PBRT equation of transfer](https://www.pbr-book.org/4ed/Light_Transport_II_Volume_Rendering/The_Equation_of_Transfer)).

Cosmological redshift supplies an expanding receiver relation between emitted and observed
wavelength. The scale factor changes the received phase and energy without implying that the
source inscription changed after emission
([NASA Hubble cosmological redshift](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/hubble/science/science-behind-the-discoveries/hubble-cosmological-redshift/)).

These applications justify a shared accounting schema—typed current, characteristic, boundary,
storage, receiver, return, and lineage. They do not justify exchanging their constitutive laws.
