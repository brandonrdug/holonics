## 1. The scalar correction

[proved-derived; formal-checked] A `DifferenceReceiver X A S` carries a chart `X -> A` into an
additive group and a reading `A -> S`.  Its returned objects are ordered:

```text
(source,target)
  -> chart(target) - chart(source)
  -> read(chart(target) - chart(source)).
```

Reversing the pair negates the difference before reading.  An injective reading reconstructs the
complete chart difference; an even reading such as a norm identifies the two orientations and
therefore retains a nontrivial reconstruction fibre.

[proved-derived; formal-checked] A real constitutive coefficient is formalized as

```text
(numerator(target)-numerator(source))
/
(denominator(target)-denominator(source)).
```

Independently translating the zero point of either section leaves the ratio unchanged.  When the
denominator difference is nonzero, multiplication by that difference reconstructs the numerator
difference exactly.

[proved-derived; formal-checked] The binary normalized exponential face has now been eliminated to
one difference without eliminating its source fibre:

```text
exp(y) / (exp(x)+exp(y)) = exp(y-x) / (1+exp(y-x)).
```

Consequently a common potential shift is an exact null direction.  The theorem supports the
softmax/sigmoid intuition at the two-channel receiver while retaining the full pre-normalized pair
as its reconstruction population.
