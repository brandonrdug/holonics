## Observation

A generated visual probe was asked to express zeta-inspired receiver geometry
without an absolute perspective. It produced useful mesh density, local
chart-like regions, loops, gaps, and branching traces, but it still treated the
whole rectangular image as a shared world. “No absolute frame” was interpreted
as “flat orthographic composition,” not as the stronger claim that no common
coordinate embedding exists before a declared interaction.

The stochastic visual prior also translated:

- branching causal alternatives into tree foliage;
- orientation or transport into generic graph arrows;
- cycles and closed fibers into circular nodes;
- unresolved exterior into decorative black background; and
- line crossings into incidence without a transport witness.

These are not merely style errors. They expose a missing elementary holonic
representation in the conceptual/rendering layer.

## Provisional diagnosis

The primitive is not a point, edge, surface, or higher-dimensional object. It
is a receiver-local causal section with its own boundary, coordinates,
lineage, and permitted transport. A display rectangle may still exist, but it
is only the final receiver aperture. It must not become a world boundary or a
privileged embedding.

Provisional notation is:

```text
H_i = (E_i, boundary(H_i), local_chart_i, lineage_i, receiver_i)
T_ij : boundary(H_i) ⇀ boundary(H_j)
```

There is no assumed global map `H -> R^2`. A visible crossing, shared seam, or
coordinate comparison is meaningful only when a declared interaction supplies
the relevant transport. Otherwise the material remains a plural fiber,
obstruction, or unresolved exterior.

The reference causal diagrams are still projections, but they use the
projection as an observation membrane for relations among null geodesics,
horizons, light cones, hypersurfaces, and returned matter. They do not require
the paper itself to be the source geometry. This is closer to the desired
receiver atlas than a flat network drawn over an object.

## Boundary of the observation

The current laboratory already has partial machinery for receiver restriction,
lineage, obstruction fibers, causal fronts, and exact incidence. This note does
not claim that those production mechanisms are absent or require replacement.
It identifies a missing conceptual contract for visual interpretation: local
charts, boundaries, transports, and returns must be the visible units rather
than decorations applied after a global embedding.

“Zeta geometry” is also not yet a sufficient source declaration. Integer zeta
weights, prime-factor incidence, complex phase transport, and zero-related
obstructions would generate different receiver ecologies. An image model
cannot choose among them without silently substituting visual analogy for a
caused mathematical relation.

**Carry-forward sentence:** The next discussion should ask how a receiver atlas
can display several locally framed causal sections and their partial boundary
transports while keeping the outer raster only as a receipt, never as the
world that contains them.
