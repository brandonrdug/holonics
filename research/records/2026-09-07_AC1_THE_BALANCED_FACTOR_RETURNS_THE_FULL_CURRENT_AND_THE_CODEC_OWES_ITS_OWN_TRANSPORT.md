## A checked factorization of the same field

[definition] For `k` pairs of complex ports, let

```text
B(c,z_0,...,z_(k-1)) = (c-z_0,z_0, ..., c-z_(k-1),z_(k-1)).
```

Its image consists of complete fields whose pair sums agree. The map is injective. Its Gram
matrix `G=B*B` has `G00=k`, `G0i=Gi0=-1`, and `Gii=2` for the other diagonal entries, with other
entries zero. Three copies carry the source outgoing bank, source held bank and target bank.
This is a numerical chart with a complete decoder, not a semantic identifier or a context window.

[proved-derived] If a Hermitian `C` has range in `im B`, then its symmetry gives `C=0` on
`(im B)^perp`. For a right-hand side in `im B`, the solution of `(I+C)v=r` also lies in `im B`.
Writing `v=B w` therefore gives the equivalent positive definite system

```text
(B*B + B*C*B) w = B*r.
```

The reduced residual lifts to an element of `im B`; if its pullback by `B*` is zero, that residual
is zero. This proves equality with the full solution. When the Hermitian coefficients are real,
the same factorization acts on the real and imaginary right-hand sides separately. Both complex
components are retained; a real input is not permission to delete the other component of the
operator.
