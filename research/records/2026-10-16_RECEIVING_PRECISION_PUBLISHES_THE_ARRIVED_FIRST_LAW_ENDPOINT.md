staged handles are consumed, publication may continue an open moment. It rereads the one arrived
operand at the successor, moves the continuous endpoint, and records the motion once in inclusive
deposition and once in its published subtotal. It increments publications without creating an
arrival or changing the sealed arrival score and cell count. A missing occurrence or missing
bounded operand is a typed `PrecisionOccurrence` refusal.

The card uses its deposit reread owner, a chart checkpoint and predecessor tree upload to roll
back unpublished writes on refusal. Both retain the device or host arrived operand and its
producing receiver. A stale event is refused before mutation.
