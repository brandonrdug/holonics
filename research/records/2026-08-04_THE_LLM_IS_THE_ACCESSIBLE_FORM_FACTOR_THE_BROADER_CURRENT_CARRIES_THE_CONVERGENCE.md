## Brandon's direct correction

`[project-postulate]` For this research inquiry, LLMs are not the special or originating part of
the phenomenon under study. They are the most accessible form factor of it to date. Their present
importance follows from genuine utility and consequent wide adoption, not from an assertion that
they uniquely determine the broader scientific or cultural movement.

`[project-postulate]` The object of study is the wider reorganization of how intelligence,

visible and widely available apparatus face through which that reorganization can presently be
observed.

`[open]` The relation between this broader current and any particular ontological framework remains
to be established. Accessibility, adoption, stylistic similarity, or rapid synthesis does not by
itself prove that one framework is correct.

## Working distinction

`[definition]` **Substantive convergence** means independently developed scientific programs
finding reusable relations among process, composition, invariance, geometry, information,
multiscale dynamics, embodiment, energy, and apparatus. The term does not assert that the programs
are mutually equivalent.

`[definition]` **Transport convergence** means different people acquiring common ways to search,
compress, express, route, and recombine ideas because their communication passes through shared
digital infrastructure. Search engines, recommendation systems, scholarly databases, social
platforms, and LLMs may all participate in this convergence.

`[definition]` **Verification gap** means a difference between the cost of producing a plausible
technical synthesis and the cost of checking its definitions, derivations, evidence, scope, and
physical realizability.

`[interpretation]` The contemporary phenomenon is better represented as substantive convergence
crossing an accelerating transport ecology than as an LLM-originated intellectual event. LLMs make
the process conspicuous because one conversational surface can search, translate, summarize,
formalize, criticize, and generate across many inherited disciplinary dialects.

## The Reverse Physics comment and the scholarly program

`[established-bounded]` The linked video was identified through public metadata as Gabriele
Carcassi's *An overview of the Reverse Physics approach*. A usable transcript was not returned by
the available YouTube caption surface, so no claim in this deposit depends on an unperformed
transcription or an asserted complete viewing of the video.

`[historical]` Carcassi and Aidala define Reverse Physics as an attempt to start from accepted laws
and identify minimal physically meaningful assumptions sufficient to reconstruct them, in analogy
with reverse mathematics. Their published work treats bounded questions concerning classical
mechanics, the action principle, state quantification, and non-additive measures in quantum
mechanics.

Relevant sources:

- <https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.09107>
- <https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-023-39145-y>
- <https://www.mdpi.com/1099-4300/25/12/1670>

`[interpretation]` The supplied YouTube comment uses a different epistemic conduct from those
papers. It escalates from multidimensional geometry through hidden variables, quantum gravity,
Planck-scale measurement, observer-independent time, and a philosophical theory of everything
without returning the maps, equations, receiver conditions, error bounds, or discriminating tests
needed to carry those transitions.

`[interpretation]` The comment resembles Brandon's writing in recursive clause structure,
geometric vocabulary, associative breadth, and the search for cross-domain unification. It differs
in its verification behavior. Brandon's model-addressed prompts repeatedly name uncertainty,
request derivations, demand returned artifacts, request ablations, distinguish evidence from
interpretation, and reject nearby demonstrations that do not perform the named deed. Cadence alone
does not establish equivalent content or validity.

`[open]` No linguistic sample establishes that the commenter, Brandon, or another writer is manic.
A non-pathologizing cultural analysis may instead measure claim escalation, universal
quantification, associative distance, noun-stack density, modal certainty, correction behavior,
and the ratio of testable returns to proposed consequences.

`[historical]` Relational and compositional bridges among mathematics, physics, logic, and
computation long predate contemporary LLMs. Baez and Stay's 2009 *Physics, Topology, Logic and

monoidal categories.

`[interpretation]` Across this selected aperture, the recurring bridge terms are process and

information, geometry and topology, multiscale organization, embodiment, energy, and apparatus.

`[open]` These recurrences do not yet constitute one ontology. Information, process, geometry,

Substantive unification requires explicit maps, preserved operations, declared failures, and new
consequences; lexical agreement is insufficient.

## Evidence for transport convergence

`[established-bounded; measured]` Controlled studies have found that some LLM-assisted writing can
increase similarity among different authors' outputs and reduce collective lexical or content
diversity even when individual outputs receive creativity benefits. These are bounded results for
declared writing tasks and model conditions, not universal laws of model use.

- <https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/02dec8877fb7c6aa9a79f81661baca7c-Abstract-Conference.html>
- <https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adn5290>

`[established-bounded; measured]` Studies of dialogue find that language models accommodate a
user's linguistic style strongly and sometimes more than human interlocutors do. Corpus studies
also find post-2022 growth in selected LLM-associated vocabulary in academic abstracts and
unscripted speech, while leaving the causal division among direct generation, editing, imitation,
and ordinary language change unresolved.

- <https://aclanthology.org/2026.eacl-long.34/>
- <https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.08627>
- <https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.00238>

`[interpretation]` A plausible cultural circuit is:

```text
human corpus
    -> shared digital models and recommendation surfaces
    -> human-machine dialogue and accelerated comparison
    -> public technical expression
    -> later human and model corpora
```

`[interpretation]` LLMs are consequential in this circuit because they expose a large portion of
the transport stack through a conversational surface. They are not ontologically privileged over
the libraries, networks, institutions, instruments, and human communities that supply and receive
the current.

## Current case-study judgment

`[interpretation]` The strongest supported formulation is that an older relational and
informational turn in science is now crossing a denser, faster, more accessible transport ecology.
LLMs are a mass-adopted form factor of that ecology. They compress disciplinary dialects, lower the
cost of interdisciplinary navigation, and make the existing movement more culturally visible.

`[interpretation]` The principal danger is not fluency itself. It is that the cost of plausible
synthesis is falling faster than the cost of verification, while recommendation systems can give
high-scope, high-certainty claims disproportionate visibility.

`[open]` The further claims connecting this movement to capitalism, educational failure,
misinformation, institutional instability, or a completed new ontology remain separate research
obligations. They may share feedback structures, but the present case study does not establish the
causal chain.

## Predictions retained for later testing

`[conjecture]` Public technical prose will continue to converge lexically while formal scientific
ontologies remain plural.

`[conjecture]` Cross-disciplinary participation will initially rise faster than
cross-disciplinary verification competence.

`[conjecture]` Provenance, executable artifacts, checker returns, ablations, and explicit failure
conditions will become more valuable as technically fluent prose becomes cheaper.

`[conjecture]` The scientific frameworks that survive will be those that type their bridges and
return discriminating consequences, not those that merely reuse a small vocabulary of geometry,
information, process, or emergence.
