## 1. What is built (`231a8939`)

The factor families' certified step, in `hnn::constitution` (module header, "The factor families'
certified step"). The declared `η_x = ½` and the `Steps` declaration are retired; the field's
description codes step rule 2 ("the certified step of every locus") and no `η_x`.
- **The unit step.** A family `x` carries its statistic `h_x′ = h_x + e` (`e`, the feature energy its
  window adds) as its metric, so its unit step is `D = G_x/h_x′` and its alignment is
  `a = |G_x|²/h_x′`, checked.
- **The covector scale** `c` is the largest entry of the covector one return carries at the family's
  output (`FactorStep::covector`), read by the producer as the linear loci's is.
- **The curvature** is `C = B·½·κ²·b(η)`. The moves `b` of the family's output along the whole ray
  are read at the ray's end by the Schur test, `S(X) = ‖X‖₁‖X‖_∞`:
  - a square carrier `w x xᵀ` moves by `4w² S(D) S(x + ηD) e` (the passive factor, `w = 1`; a
    channel's `c`, `b`, `F` at `w = 2, h, h`);
  - the slices move by `8(S(dU) S(V + η dV) + S(U + η dU) S(dV)) e`;
  - the standing moves, in its lock chart, by `4|D|²_∞ Σ_r S(U_r) S(V_r) e_r` over the rings `r` its
    contrast reaches;
  - a resonator gain moves by `|D|² e ((|g| + η|D|)/|g|)²`;
  - a pair port moves by Cauchy–Schwarz over its `3k` terms.
- **The gains** `κ²`:
  - an element output's is the contrast port's;
  - an injection's is the source port's;
  - a channel's and a loaded resonator's (the case `G = 2Y`) are read through their solve's
    difference power `P = G/(2h) + ½(G/h)²‖C‖ + ⅛G²‖K‖`, with `G_a ≤ Y_a` at `β_a ≥ 0`.
- **The step** is the largest dyadic with `ηC ≤ a` and `ηc ≤ 1`, in one loop with the linear loci:
  `B` counts every family stepping, and every step is halved until each certificate holds at its
  ray's end. The comparison reads its readings at 64 significant bits (`a` at its floor, every
  factor of `C` and `c` at its ceiling), which the exact certificate implies; the step itself is
  exact.
- **Refusals.** A declared boost refuses every step (`ActiveContact`), as a pumped resonator does
  (`UncertifiedGain`). A channel family through a contact with `β_a < 0` is refused
  (`UncertifiedConductance`).
- **Lean.** `Holon/Deposition` §8: `factor_unit_step_alignment`, `square_ray_identity`,
  `square_ray_move`, `square_ray_deriv`, `square_ray_deriv_bound`, `contracting_resolvent`,
  `transit_difference_power`.

No part of the step reads a codec, an alphabet, a chart or a terrain: one `Constitution::deposited`
runs on the moiré, the copy, text and the standing cut. The computational object is the helical
pair interaction:
- the pair contacts' storage and the rings' elements carry the factor families (the pair);
- the station readout carries the gains (faces and placement);
- the words' ticks and stations carry the refinement's span (the tube).

The helix, the cell holonomy and the tower thread stay attached through the field's complex.
