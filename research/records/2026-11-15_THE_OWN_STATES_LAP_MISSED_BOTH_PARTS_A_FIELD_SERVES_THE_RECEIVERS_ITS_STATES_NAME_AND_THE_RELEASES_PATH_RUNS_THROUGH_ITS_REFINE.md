## 3. Why: the built object was not the whole of the theory's object

The record's states were "the release's path", and the build took the path's states at the crossings of the sweeps' octaves.
The release has a second part, its refine (`write-store` runs `REFINE = 2`): each pass revisits every written place, one place
a read, with every other place standing, and draws it again. A refine state is one place open with both sides known, which is
the held read's state (the `P` rows). So the release's own path runs through the held reader's states. The build trained none
of them, and none of the line's mask states, which open places among known neighbours on both sides. One lap was enough for
the field to stop serving that receiver: the held read rose a quarter of a nat a place, and the writes, whose refine reads
those states, lost their words.

This is Brandon's lens of 04:37Z read in a measurement: a form is fixed by the listener that receives it. The keyed draw trains
the field on many forms at once: every order of resolution, both-sided masks at every density, and the writer's own crossings.
Its states are the union of the receivers the field faces: the held reader, the writer's sweeps and its refine. Training on the
sweeps' form alone served the sweeps and left the other two listeners. The record's own clause for a miss holds as written: the
order-averaged code the draw estimates carries what the field's own path, cut at the sweeps, does not. The draw stays.

Brandon's lens of 02:54Z still stands on its own ground: the keyed draw is no randomness in the field. It is the passage's
own key read at the cut, deterministic happenstance, and what it buys is coverage of the forms a receiver may ask for. A state
the field trains on is chosen for the receivers it will meet, and this lap read that cost directly (`agent-inferred`).

## 4. What follows

- The line stands as it was. The word `own` stays in the tree as a law a state can carry. It is now a measured form, and no
  line takes it.
- A state is named by its receiver. A design that changes the states a field trains on names every receiver the field will
  meet (the held reader, the writer's sweeps, its refine, any port) and keeps a state for each. A design that drops one is read
  against that receiver in its first lap (the atlas row `tower.field-states-name-their-receivers`).
- The release's refine is the writer's both-sided receiver. Whether more refine, or a refine whose order is the field's own
  news, lifts the writes is a question of the release, read on the line's own state with no training change. Its guess is fixed
  before it runs.

Recorded failures this lap could have repeated, read back: a law patched after a miss (none; the line is untouched and the word
is left off); a guess on a settled premise (the premise was read from lap 15 to 16 and corrected in the design record before the
run); bits read as progress (the writes were read whole, with the ruler beside them, and decided the second part).
