## 3. What lowers the role

[measured]
- The pass lowers it, not the founding. One more pass from `lapD8/STATE` with nothing founded takes
  the role from 53 to 37 while lowering the held-out code by 1,790.9 bits. The founded lap recovers 6
  of the 16 (43) and lowers the held-out code by 4,277.9 bits.
- So the held-out code and the held-out role disagree: in both arms the code improves while the role
  worsens.

[agent-inferred] The role read takes the largest mass, a margin. At `lapD8` the name's mean code was
2.876 bits; the twin's pass moved it 0.377 bits up, and 16 answers crossed the margin. Two causes fit
what is measured, and they predict differently on the training text's own named answers:
- **The readout learns the training names.** A further pass over the same text lets the readout's
  own face predict each seen problem's name from its context. On a held-out problem that face puts
  mass on seen names, beside the copy's held name. The role on the seen answers would then rise or
  hold over the pass while the held-out role falls.
- **The copy loses its margin.** The copy's steps over the pass lower its own raise at the answer's
  opening. The role on the seen answers would then fall with the held-out.

## 4. The next loop: read the role on seen problems, its decision fixed now

The read, on the PC, outside the machine: the cells of the training text from `copyD8`, `lapT8`,
`lapD8`, `lap3T8` and `lap3D8`, and the observer's `role` read on the training text's named answers
(heard and read text both the training text), beside the held-out `role` read. The observer now also
prints how many classes carry more than the name, in bands, and how many answers each file keeps
and gains beside the file before.

The decision, fixed before the read:
1. If the seen role rises or holds from `lapD8` to `lap3T8` while the held-out role falls, the
   readout is learning the seen names. The next loop's subject is then that the pass deposits
   differences the admitted future cannot distinguish ([retention
   contract](../../docs/ELEMENTARY_OBJECTS.md#the-retention-contract)), and it is derived there.
2. If the seen role falls with the held-out, the copy is losing its margin. The next loop reads the
   copy's own weights before and after the pass.

In either case `lapD8/STATE` stays the state the role and John are read from, and no lap is taken
until the read decides.

## 5. The recorded failures this loop could repeat

- **Bits read as progress.** The certificate is paid and the held-out code fell, and the role fell
  with them; the lap is reported as failing its behaviour items.
- **Seen material graded as unseen.** The next read takes the seen role only as a diagnosis beside
  the held-out role, never as a pass of item 3.
- **A refusal answered with a larger limit.** No further lap is taken to recover the role; the next
  loop reads the cause.
