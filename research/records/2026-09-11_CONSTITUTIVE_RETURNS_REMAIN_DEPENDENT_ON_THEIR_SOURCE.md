## The lift is a family of consequences

[definition] Let the original bounded source be indexed by θ, with jointly produced operands
`x(θ)` and `η(θ)=v−c(θ)`. At the contemporary relation R and actual condition h₀, apply the
existing point operation separately **under the same conditional assignment**:

```
Fθ = { h | (Φ(x(θ),h),η(θ)) ∈ R }
hθ = contact(h₀,Fθ)
Rθ = span(R ∪ {(Φ(x(θ),hθ),η(θ))})
```

[definition] For a nonempty affine Fθ, `contact` is the existing unit-admittance projection;
an empty Fθ retains h₀ and its obstruction before formation extends R. The returned object is
the dependent family `θ ↦ (hθ,Rθ)`, with the original anchor constraint. It is not the single
relation `span(⋃θ Rθ)`. A conditional substitution does not establish which source was actual,
and the source family's coefficient directions are never presented as independent observations.

[definition] This is the literal dependent lift of the admitted point constitutive law. It
does not assert an identity with the adjoint of a minimum-norm family receiver, nor does it
identify an actual source by inverting an emitted symbol. Those are different receiver uses.
The old compatibility compiler still fixes the recorded producing condition hₛ. This return
instead computes a **new** condition from contemporary h₀; it never rewrites hₛ.

## Why an affine condition family is insufficient

[proved-derived; formal-checked] The new `Mathematics/DependentConstitutiveReturn.lean`, imported
by `Framework.Computation`, checks shared-parameter projection/reconstruction and the two-dimensional
one-row contact `h′=h+((b−u·h)/(u·u))u`. For nonzero `u·u`, its normal equation is satisfied and
tangent readings are unchanged. At `u=(1,a)`, `h=(1,0)`, `b=2`, the two source assignments
`a=1` and `a=2` return `(3/2,1/2)` and `(6/5,2/5)`. The midpoint source does not return the
midpoint condition, so even this elementary dependent contact graph is not affine.

[proved-derived; formal-checked] Its material component directly reuses
`HolonicConstitutiveFibre.addPairedCurrentSubmodule`, retaining the original relation and the
new pair under the same θ. The point specialization is the existing sum-with-one-occurrence
formation theorem. No union across the source assignments appears in that dependent definition.

[definition] These exact rational quotients explain the required richer representation. The
source/condition/material dependence must remain an executable generator or joint constraint;
neither averaging its conditions nor replacing it by its affine span preserves the operation.
The separate equal-marginal sign example is a mathematical separator, not evidence that its
finite two-point sets are native affine wave families.
