## The actual pattern in the implementation

[established-bounded; source-inspected] The pre-repair normal owner repeated several collapsed
extents across Rust and CUDA: 17 magnitude limbs, 18 wire words including sign, 36 words for a
complex moment, and 544 carrier bits. These are related representation facts. Other repeated
numbers have different origins: three source ports; two real/imaginary components; six report
balls; five report bounds; four accumulated scalar moments. Equal numeral faces do not make
those roles the same object. Hand-written expanded offsets obscured the distinctions.

[definition] The repair derives the integer carrier from the arithmetic it must hold:

```text
history product bits = bits of one wide current component + bits of the other,
moment product bits  = 2 * (history bits + reference carry),
required bits        = moment product bits + doubled-remainder carry,
magnitude limbs      = ceil(required bits / bits per limb),
moment wire          = magnitude limbs followed by sign,
complex moment wire  = real moment followed by imaginary moment.
```

`ExactInteger` exposes its limb/bit geometry. History and moment aliases derive their capacities
from it; the operative reader/writer follows those types. `MomentWire` carries the corresponding
Rust serialization chart. `NormalLayout` derives matrix, statistic, ball/report and workspace
extents from their actual parts and supplies named ball offsets. CUDA follows the same named
source/report/statistic roles. Neither an integer base switch nor a global constant registry
would accomplish this encapsulation.

[definition] The text codec now derives its bit-pair count from an octet plus its part-boundary
bit, and derives the EndPart coordinate from that bit position. The codeword remains an exterior
chart. These changes introduce no special meaning for 9, 18, 256 or 512 inside native semantics.

[proved-derived] The half in the current quadratic objective has another origin:
`d(||r||²)[h]=2 Re<r,h>`, so the factor `2^-1` returns the current covector under the declared
unit metric. This is exact rational arithmetic; it is not a decimal approximation or the same
causal construction as two quadrature ports. A geometric two-to-one partition similarly owes
its actual incidence and receiving chart. Its cardinal ratio alone does not identify a shape.

[definition] Unit priors/admittances are declared constitutive choices in the existing experiment,
not universal numbers discovered from all physical material. Grain 72 is a chosen binary
realization aperture; its scale is generated as a power of two and its residual remains retained.
Recent-producer capacity serves a current and preceding cut, with an exact decoder for older
cuts; it is a placement choice, not semantic context length. Fixture integers are supplied
experimental inputs. These distinctions must remain explicit rather than pretending every
literal follows from one elementary physical axiom.

[project-postulate] Exact arithmetic is necessary but does not alone retain causal meaning.
The owner also carries source, comparison, units/chart, generator and remainder. A reciprocal,
power, modulus, partition count and wire tag may have equal evaluated faces while belonging to
different relations. Future edits should follow these relations through typed owners instead
of copying their currently evaluated integers into new consumers.
