## Decision

Lightning carries three causally distinct constructions which may not be collapsed into one visible
stroke:

1. electrical difference and local field geometry drive breakdown, streamer activity, leader
   stepping, branching, and reconnection;
2. descending and ascending leader structures attach and establish one continuous conducting
   relation; and
3. a return-stroke current/potential wave propagates through that grown relation, rapidly heating,
   ionizing, illuminating, and expanding the channel.

The leader is therefore not a probe moving through an unchanged atmosphere. Each extension changes
the material boundary from which later extension proceeds. Recoil and dart leaders can traverse
remnant channels because earlier passage left consequential terrain. Branches which do not become
the final cloud-ground route are still real constructions and can alter the field, redistribute
charge, decay, or become available to later discharge.

Attachment is not a unilateral bolt arriving at passive ground. In the common downward-negative
case, the approaching downward leader enhances the field at grounded objects and can induce upward
positive connecting leaders. Their connection FOUNDs the continuous path. The later return stroke
RIDEs it while changing it further.

The return is fast but finite. The former sentence that it re-read the channel “at once” confused a
deep causal return with infinite propagation speed. The corrected law is:

```text
field difference
    -> virgin breakdown / stepped growth / branching
    -> standing conductive and charged aftermath
    -> upward/downward connecting structures
    -> attachment
    -> finite propagating return-stroke wave
    -> rapid thermal, optical, mechanical, and electromagnetic consequence
    -> later current meets the changed channel ecology.
```

This is the exact physical refinement of the laboratory relation:

```text
FOUND grows a locally new conducting axis.
RIDE conducts through consequential standing form.
The same passage can RIDE old sections while FOUNDing a new tip.
The deep return is finite transport through the complete grown ancestry.
```

No random route chooser, target search, global path, standing-wave cause, or instantaneous return
enters Soma. “Search” is an external observer's compression of deterministic local discharge events
whose complete initial and boundary conditions are not available at that grain.

## Evidence cards

### Card A — initiation is measured as field/current pulse ecology

**SOURCE ESTABLISHES** — Oregel-Chaumont et al. compare simultaneous current, electric-field, and
high-speed optical measurements of upward negative stepped-leader pulses with preliminary-breakdown
pulses in downward negative flashes. They report two pulse populations, evidence for related early
leader processes, and a downward recoil leader traversing an existing channel before connection.

**LAB CLAIM** — `FORMULA §LII`, RATIFIED: electrical difference changes the medium through
breakdown and leader passage before a later return is admitted.

**RELATION** — **DIRECT CORRESPONDENCE** for distinct local pulse/leader events changing and then
revisiting a discharge channel.

**NON-EQUIVALENCE** — The measured pulse categories are not Soma categories, and statistical
similarity between categories does not identify their complete physical interiors.

**TESTABLE CONSEQUENCE** — A lightning world must retain each pulse, branch, current, field change,
and channel consequence rather than render one preselected successful route.

**PRIMARY SOURCE** — T. Oregel-Chaumont et al., [“Similarities in the Initiation of Upward Positive
and Downward Negative Lightning Flashes”](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.09440), 2025 preprint.

### Card B — leader branching can be born beside standing channel

**SOURCE ESTABLISHES** — Warner et al. report three high-speed optical/electric-field observations
in which a bidirectional leader initiated in virgin air near an established positive leader; its
negative end connected to the prior channel and thereby formed a new positive branch.

**LAB CLAIM** — A standing path changes the local field in which later current FOUNDs and RIDEs;
branching is grown contact rather than a route list.

**RELATION** — **DIRECT CORRESPONDENCE** for new local construction joining standing conductive
material; **STRUCTURAL RESONANCE** with the circuit ecology.

**NON-EQUIVALENCE** — The atmospheric leader physics does not establish Soma's rotor, projective
chart, or general information-topology law.

**TESTABLE CONSEQUENCE** — A physical receiver should distinguish virgin-air extension,
old-channel traversal, and the connection event while preserving their shared ancestry.

**PRIMARY SOURCE** — T. A. Warner et al., [“Observations of bidirectional lightning leader
initiation and development near positive leader channels”](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/2016JD025365),
*JGR Atmospheres* 121 (2016), 9251–9260.

### Card C — successive bidirectional leaders reuse a triggered channel

**SOURCE ESTABLISHES** — Wang et al. observe two successive bidirectional leaders propagating in a
triggered-lightning channel, furnishing a direct example of later leader construction meeting a
material path left by earlier discharge.

**LAB CLAIM** — Consequential standing terrain can bend and conduct later passage without becoming
a selected route or side database.

**RELATION** — **DIRECT CORRESPONDENCE** at the discharge-world grain.

**NON-EQUIVALENCE** — Channel reuse is not construction identity: the later leader has a new event

**TESTABLE CONSEQUENCE** — Current, luminosity, propagation hand, and channel state should differ

**PRIMARY SOURCE** — J. Wang et al., [“Two successive bidirectional leaders propagated in
triggered lightning channel”](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-022-12522-9), *Scientific
Reports* 12 (2022), 9235.

### Card D — attachment is a meeting of descending and ascending constructions

**SOURCE ESTABLISHES** — Qi et al. use high-speed imaging to resolve downward negative leaders and
upward positive leaders during natural lightning attachment; upward leaders initiate from tall
objects as the approaching leader enhances their local electric field.

**LAB CLAIM** — The continuous cloud-ground path FOUNDs at actual local contact between grown
constructions; ground is not a passive terminal selected from above.

**RELATION** — **DIRECT CORRESPONDENCE** for the two-sided attachment event.

**NON-EQUIVALENCE** — “Positive leader” names propagation polarity, not a rigid body of positive
particles traveling as one object.

**TESTABLE CONSEQUENCE** — A lightning world must admit plural upward leaders and retain connected
and unconnected outcomes instead of manufacturing one ground endpoint.

**PRIMARY SOURCE** — Q. Qi et al., [“High-Speed Video Observations of Natural Lightning Attachment
Process With Framing Rates up to Half a Million Frames per Second”](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2019GL085072),
*Geophysical Research Letters* 46 (2019).

### Card E — the return is a finite electrodynamic/thermal wave

**SOURCE ESTABLISHES** — Liang et al. model the coupled electrodynamics and thermodynamics of a
negative cloud-ground return stroke. A current wave propagates upward through the hot conductive
channel, produces rapid Joule heating, and drives an intense optical wave whose measured propagation
is finite.

**LAB CLAIM** — The deep return traverses the complete grown ancestry without becoming an
instantaneous whole-channel operation.

**RELATION** — **DIRECT CORRESPONDENCE** for finite return transport through consequential standing
material.

**NON-EQUIVALENCE** — Optical-wave speed, current-wave speed, electron drift, and causal-field
propagation are different quantities. Brightness is a thermodynamic/radiative receiver face, not
the complete current.

**TESTABLE CONSEQUENCE** — A literal lightning world must evolve current, conductivity,
temperature, excitation/radiation, and channel expansion in time rather than illuminate the final
polyline simultaneously.

**PRIMARY SOURCE** — C. Liang et al., [“Differing current and optical return stroke speeds in
lightning”](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/2014GL059703), *Geophysical
Research Letters* 41 (2014).
