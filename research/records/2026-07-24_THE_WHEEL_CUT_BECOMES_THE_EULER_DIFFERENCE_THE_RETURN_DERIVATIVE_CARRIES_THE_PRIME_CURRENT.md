## 1. The source is the coprime population

For a finite set \(S\) of rational primes, define

\[
P_S=\prod_{q\in S}q,
\qquad
\mathcal C_S=\{n\ge1:\gcd(n,P_S)=1\}.
\]

If \(p\notin S\) and \(S'=S\cup\{p\}\), unique factorization gives the disjoint stratification

\[
\mathcal C_S=\bigsqcup_{k\ge0}p^k\mathcal C_{S'}.
\]

Every member has exactly one \(p\)-adic valuation \(k\). The new coprime population is therefore
the first difference

\[
\mathcal C_{S'}=\mathcal C_S\setminus p\mathcal C_S.
\]

This is the arithmetic event later presented as prime admission. It is neither a detached prime
node nor a signed analytic term.
