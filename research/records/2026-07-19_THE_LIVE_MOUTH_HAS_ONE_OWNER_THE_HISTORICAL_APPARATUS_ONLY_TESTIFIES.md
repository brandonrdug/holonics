## Complete finding

The direct path is already causally singular.

- `current_world.rs` supplies borrowed native relation populations and actions.
- `LiveCurrentMachine::receive_with` validates and mounts every current against one immutable
  standing-before surface.
- `HostLiveCurrentExecutor` and `CudaLiveCurrentExecutor` enact only the substrate face of that
  same call.
- `LiveCurrentMachine` integrates current-local contributions, prepares one successor before
  visibility, commits continuing and ending lineages, replaces standing, and returns radiation.
- `LiveCurrentRestImage` remounts this body directly without receipts or replay.

There is no call from that path into `ActiveCut`, `LiveEcology`, `LiveSession`, `LiveHolon`, an
emission journal, a transition receipt, a frontier, or the historical world-organ stack.

The defect was public and executable ambiguity around that path.

1. `soma-membrane` exported both `LiveCurrentMachine` and the complete historical ecology at the
   same public root. `LiveEcology` could therefore look like a peer production owner even though
   its module correctly called itself historical.
2. `life` exported `current_world` beside root-level `live_session`, `frontier`, and `world_organ`.
   The `live_session` module still described itself as direct ownership of one living Eros ecology.
3. Invoking the `life` binary with no command fell through to the old diet/card gather. The
   historical machine was therefore the implicit executable, even though the direct general Eros
   launch does not yet exist.
4. `SparseStandingSurface::prepare_successor` publicly accepted the historical `FoldedCut`, leaking
   the compatibility successor path back through an otherwise direct standing type.

The historical ecology really is an alternate execution construction: it conducts an `ActiveCut`,
retains an `EnactedPopulation`, prepares a selected cut's folded successor, and installs that
successor through its own contact/Holon lifecycle. That is valid evidence for the worlds which
measured it. It is not the production implementation of the current live-weave law.
