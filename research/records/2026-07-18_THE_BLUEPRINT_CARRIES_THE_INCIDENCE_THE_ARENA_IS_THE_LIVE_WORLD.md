## Result

The recurrent language world no longer stores every complete path as an independently owned copy
of its ancestry. It now stands as one active, append-only holonic incidence arena. A root stores one
native span reference. An extension stores one parent, one arriving span reference, the enacted
joint, its accumulated extent and digest, and its exposed terminal face:

```text
H_i = (parent_i, arriving_i, joint_i, terminal_i, extent_i, digest_i)

Path(i) = Path(parent_i) ++ (arriving_i, joint_i).
```

The source span itself stands once in the construction journal. A complete expression is
materialized by traversing its parent lineage only when it must cross an output, compiler, or
observer boundary. This is the requested blueprint face: receiving an idea means receiving the
incidences needed to traverse and reconstruct its lived topology, not receiving a frozen duplicate
of every prefix.

## The arena is the active ecology; disk is only its interval face

`PathArena` is not SQL, a semantic database, or a filesystem lookup convention. While a world is
mounted, its nodes and incidence apertures are ordinary live memory. The derived `IncidenceIndex`
maps actual terminal material coordinates, native chart ports, and completed wells to standing path
identities. An arriving current consults those apertures, performs the unchanged exact contact and
Swing laws, and appends every enacted root and extension.

The index is deliberately absent from the durable spelling. It is rebuilt exactly from terminal
faces whenever the body wakes. It may locate possible meetings but cannot decide RIDE/FOUND, merge
lineages, invent a category, select a path, or enter Soma. Therefore it is an aperture into the
causal fabric rather than a second authority over it.

The durable JSON world is presently one receiver/checkpoint gauge. SLEEP here means persistence
across a world interval: reopening reconstructs the active index and the next current changes the
live world through ordinary contact. The physical file is not the ecology while the ecology acts.

## The observer instrument

The strict `soma-holonic-incidence-observer-plan-v1` listener reads ordered world checkpoints and
emits `soma-holonic-incidence-observer-v2`. It does not need to materialize the whole expression
population. It measures:

- root, extension, depth, role, and edge-growth distributions;
- widths of exact material-coordinate, chart, and completed-well incidence ports;
- exact joint species and ordered joint-species motifs (the recurrent structural “songs”);
- branch parents, fan-out, plural arriving joint species, and plural child faces;
- exact visible-byte faces reached by one or plural causal lineages;
- adjacent motif-transition support/total quotients with symbolic `-log2(support/total)`;
- longitudinal birth, persistence, and dormancy of motif families;
- materialized-versus-structurally-shared storage extent.

These are receiver-indexed statistics over the enacted causal body. They neither become a learned
parameter nor feed back as a selector. A motif is an observed ordered joint family, not an authored
morpheme or semantic class. Its relevance becomes causal only when later context, deed, and
consequence distinguish or reuse it.

## Scientific consequence

This is the first practical form of the variable holonic relational structure Brandon described.
It is variable because the relation population is grown by the actual world history, not declared
as a universal schema. It is holonic because every exposed path is simultaneously a complete face
to its receiver and a sparse incidence into shared prior construction. It is relational because a
path is not recoverable from a value alone: its parent, arriving current, joint, terminal, source
journal, and receiving edge together carry its identity.

The observer toolkit now has the correct foundation for information taxonomy. Structural motifs
show where transformation shapes recur. Port widths show where many causal paths share a local
cross-section. Plural faces expose many-to-one compression. Branches expose one-to-many
re-expression. Conditional information lengths measure how surprising a later joint shape is at
one exact path-population boundary. Later deeds and consequences can then determine which of those
structural families are useful, contradictory, dormant, or reactivated without installing a
semantic classifier.
