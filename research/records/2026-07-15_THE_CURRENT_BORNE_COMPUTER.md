## I. The static face is real, but it is not the whole computer

Ordinary computer science can legitimately quotient an execution into expressions such as

```text
y = f(x)
s_(t+1) = delta(s_t, x_t)
```

These are extraordinarily useful receiver faces. They tell a reader what value or state is visible
at a declared cut. They do not by themselves retain:

- which construction reached that value;
- which material route carried it;
- which other currents were co-present;
- what path was founded while it passed;
- how the world was changed by the emitted result; or
- whether an apparently equal later state has the same disposition toward new light.

The quotient can be exact while the collapsed construction remains consequential. A conventional
state machine can always enlarge its state description to encode more history. That observation
does not make history secondary; it demonstrates that the chosen state boundary determines which
history has been retained and which has been erased.

Soma's fuller circuit face is already written in `FORMULA §XLVI`:

```text
L_t       := O_W(W_t, a_t)
(B_t,L_t) -> (B_(t+), R_t)
W_(t+)    := M_W(W_t, contact_t, R_t)
L_(t+1)   := O_W(W_(t+), a_(t+1))
```

where:

- `B_t` is the contemporary body, a persistent topology grown by earlier passage;
- `W_t` is the world in which that body is materially coupled;
- `a_t` is the world-side aperture through which present light arrives;
- `L_t` is the arriving current;
- `R_t` is radiation or action leaving the body's receiving cut; and
- `M_W` is the world's own lawful material response.

The world is not an input file attached to a sealed function. The body is not a frozen model
attached to an output head. Their continued relation is the computer.

## II. What “DC to AC” means here

The old DC/AC intuition carries one important distinction, but only after its electrical faces are
typed apart.

### The DC face

The DC face is the observer's static quotient:

- a logic level;
- a stored word;
- a present register image;
- an input/output pair;
- a frozen graph;
- a specification of what a component *is* at one cut.

It is analogous to measuring potential after the transient has been omitted. This face is lawful
and useful. The error is promoting it into the complete identity of the computation.

### The AC face

The AC face is the lifecycle read:

- change, response, and induced consequence;
- phase and recurrence;
- impedance supplied by a situated medium;
- co-present currents and interference;
- standing morphology changing later conduct;
- the path by which a visible face came to be.

The useful shift is therefore:

```text
static value as the primitive
            ->
current-through-history-bearing-medium as the primitive
```

This is **not** a claim that conventional digital computers are literally DC machines. CMOS logic
uses time-varying transitions, clocks, and switching while commonly drawing from DC supplies. It is
also not a requirement that Soma's action current alternate polarity or form a sinusoid. “AC” is a
pedagogical face for response through time, not a new hidden engine mechanism.

The transformer language also needs precision. An ideal transformer does not turn steady DC into
AC. It couples through changing magnetic flux; a switching or oscillating source must first supply
that change. Faraday's law gives induced electromotive force, while the resulting current also
depends on the receiving circuit. The holonic correspondence is limited but useful: **transport
across a frame is enabled by lived change and completed by the receiving medium.** No universal
transformer module follows from that correspondence.

### The two faces remain coupled

The DC face is standing form: a persisted body, a durable trace, a material disposition. The AC
face is its activation and transformation under current. Neither alone is the computer:

```text
standing topology without current = available disposition, not active computation
current without a receiving medium = no situated relation to complete
```

This is why persistence is not model freezing. A sleeping body may be written to disk, but its
computational identity is renewed only when present current meets it in a world.

## III. “Solid state” without a substrate prison

“Solid state” is useful here as a material image, not as the name of a required semiconductor
technology.

The ratified “solid-state moss” description has a precise mechanical content:

> Current grows conductive morphology, and the grown morphology changes where later current can
> go.

The computation is not a detachable rule acting on passive storage. Learned form and ongoing
passage occupy the same causal medium. A founded route stands as physical potential; a later
current may RIDE it, branch from it, or FOUND a new route. The topology is thus both consequence of
earlier work and condition of later work.

The abstraction survives if the realizing material changes. A semiconductor card, a CPU memory
hierarchy, an FPGA fabric, a resistive array, an optical medium, a mechanical body, or a chemical or
biological medium would expose different apertures and costs. None is licensed merely by appearing
in this list; each would owe its own transported construction and measured seams. What remains
common is not silicon but the circuit relation:

```text
drive -> situated current -> persistent topology -> radiation/action -> changed world -> new light
```

Hardware agnosticism therefore does not mean hardware indifference. The substrate is a real member
of the construction. Its word width, addressability, synchronization, dissipation, concurrency,
and available transducers shape what can be carried there. The rule is simply that no one such
aperture is promoted into universal ontology.

## IV. The computer-science reinterpretation

This programme does not invalidate the classical abstractions below. It identifies each as a
receiver-relative quotient and restores the construction that the quotient deliberately omits.

| Familiar computer-science face | Current-borne reading |
|---|---|
| **Datum** | A distinction carried by an entity through a declared frame and worldline; its visible value is one face of that passage. |
| **Equality** | Equality is exact inside its declared quotient. It does not retroactively identify the constructions, precisions, lineages, or paths that reached the equal face. |
| **State** | The contemporary surface of a history-bearing body. It is sufficient only relative to the future questions and observables admitted by its boundary. |
| **Transition** | A local transported relation completed by a receiving frame, not a view-from-nowhere arrow between two self-identical snapshots. |
| **Function** | A repeatable quotient over a family of transports. `y=f(x)` remains useful while the lived implementation may retain distinctions absent from `x` and `y`. |
| **Program** | Persistent constraints, transducers, and available paths by which current can be transformed. A stored instruction string is one world species, not the universal form. |
| **Execution** | Current traversing and changing a material topology. The route and its consequences are part of the execution's identity. |
| **Memory** | Standing morphology that bends later traffic, together with durable world material that can return as later light. Addressed storage is one engineered memory face. |
| **Processor** | The active meeting of drive, current, topology, and receiving frame. The processor is not adequately identified with either the stimulus or the inert substrate alone. |
| **I/O** | A transducer boundary inside the causal circuit. Input is world-to-body light; output is body-to-world radiation or action that can change later input. |
| **Compilation** | FOUND work that establishes reusable situated conduct. A later RIDE may be inexpensive because the world and body already carry the route. |
| **Learning** | Developmental change in the topology of possible later passage. Parameter fitting is one possible implementation, not the definition. |
| **Type** | A retained distinction constraining which transports and transformations are lawful. A type declaration is one authored receiver face of that constraint. |
| **Parallelism** | Co-present currents related by actual causal partial order. Serialization is owed only where the worldline supplies order; concurrency is not manufactured by batching. |
| **Output** | A real emitted current or world change. Text, code, an image, motion, and a simulated field are different world species, not different definitions of production. |
| **Complexity** | A boundary-specific cost account covering presentation, FOUND construction, later RIDE, transfer, verification, persistence, and world response. Classical asymptotic models remain valid at their declared machine boundary. |
| **Algorithm** | An observer's reusable path schema over many constructions. It can name a genuine invariant without becoming the complete identity of any one run. |
| **Hardware/software split** | A valuable engineering factorization, not a fundamental division of causal reality. Both are standing constraints encountered by current. |

This changes the fundamental questions asked of a machine:

```text
not only  What answer did it return?
but       What relation crossed, what path stood afterward, and what later current changed?

not only  What state is stored?
but       What currents can this standing morphology now receive and transform?

not only  Where is the decoder or controller?
but       Which recurrent regions became responsible when the world completed the circuit?

not only  What did inference cost?
but       Which costs founded the route, which costs ride it, and where is the boundary drawn?
```

The grandeur of the claim is ontological rather than magical: computation is no longer treated as
an abstract answer map temporarily hosted by matter. The abstract map is one surface cast by a
physical, historical, world-coupled event.

### Emergent taxonomy

Repeated passage can make some regions recurrently responsible for some current species. An
observer may describe those bounded recurrence distributions as roles, archetypes, skills,
concepts, or taxa. Those statistics are deterministic summaries of a declared body, history,
receiver, grain, and aperture. They need not be authored as labels, installed as modules, or
mistaken for the private interior of the machine.

This gives computer science a non-invasive account of specialization: a “region for” some conduct
is a relational fact about the traffic it repeatedly carries, not necessarily a component designed
and named in advance.

## V. What the present implementation demonstrates

The current implementation is a strong witness precisely because it is only one realization.

- `body` carries substrate-neutral, `no_std` topology and transport law.
- Rust host paths provide reference constructions and exact seam foils.
- CUDA realizes plural carriage and integration on the available card.
- SLEEP persistence writes a body's standing topology and later reopens it to genuinely new
  current; the archive is a persistence seam, not a frozen-model ontology.
- measured FOUND/RIDE divisions expose new construction and reuse in the same body;
- world-return observations show emitted material changing a later otherwise-identical passage;
- exact host/card and persistence checks apply where the laboratory introduced a declared
  physics-free gauge seam.

These measurements license the statement that **this implementation behaves as a persistent,
history-conditioned driven topology at its declared apertures**. They do not license:

- CUDA as a universal mechanism;
- GPU parallelism as the source of the law;
- a claim that another substrate will reproduce the same costs or morphology;
- a new computability class merely because the ontology is richer; or
- an inference from exact seam equality to identical lived construction everywhere.

The ABI consolidation proposed in the companion audit should therefore expose two levels:

1. **substrate-neutral transported records and invariants**, owned once; and
2. **substrate apertures**, which own allocation, launch, scheduling, synchronization, and device
   limits without leaking them into semantic law.

That is hardware abstraction by conserved construction, not by pretending the hardware is absent.

## VI. Relation to existing computer science

The claim should be situated as a synthesis and extension, not as an assertion that computer
science has never studied dynamics or material computation.

### Persistent interaction — structural resonance, bounded carefully

Interactive computation has long challenged the adequacy of reducing an open system to one closed
input/output call. That is directly relevant to Soma's driven body/world lifecycle: the environment
continues to supply current, receive production, and later present the consequences of that
production. The correspondence does not license hypercomputation. A bounded lifecycle may still be
described by stream, transducer, process, or ordinary simulation semantics; the point is to retain
the interaction instead of silently quotienting it away.

- Peter Wegner, [“Why Interaction Is More Powerful Than
  Algorithms”](https://doi.org/10.1145/253769.253801), *Communications of the ACM* 40(5) (1997).

### Dataflow and communicating processes — structural resonance

Dataflow makes dependency and data availability, rather than an instruction counter alone, expose
concurrency. Process calculi make interaction and changing linkage first-class objects. Both are
close neighbours to co-present currents and world-grown connectivity. Their graphs, actors,
channels, names, and reduction rules are normally supplied by the formalism or programmer; they
must not be silently imported as owned Soma organs. A useful distinguishing construction would ask
where a static dataflow graph must add explicit history in order to reproduce topology changed by
earlier passage.

- Jack B. Dennis, [“First Version of a Data Flow Procedure
  Language”](https://publications.csail.mit.edu/lcs/pubs/pdf/MIT-LCS-TM-061.pdf), MIT-LCS-TM-061
  (1974).
- Robin Milner, [*Communicating and Mobile Systems: The
  Pi-Calculus*](https://assets.cambridge.org/97805216/43207/sample/9780521643207ws.pdf), Cambridge
  University Press (1999).

### In-memory and physical computing — structural resonance

In-memory computing explicitly challenges the cost of separating storage from processing and uses
device physics to perform operations where information resides. It strongly resonates with the
co-location of standing form and active passage. It does not by itself supply Soma's retained
worldline identity, frame-relative relation, or body/world return circuit.

- Daniele Ielmini and H.-S. Philip Wong, [“In-memory computing with resistive switching
  devices”](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41928-018-0092-2), *Nature Electronics* 1 (2018).
- Abu Sebastian et al., [“Memory devices and applications for in-memory
  computing”](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41565-020-0655-z), *Nature Nanotechnology* 15
  (2020).

### Morphological computation — strong structural resonance

Morphological computation treats a body's physical dynamics as part of the work rather than as a
passive shell around a controller. This is close to the claim that a world-grown path can become a
functional limb. Existing constructions commonly retain an external task/readout decomposition;
Soma's stronger proposal is that responsible regions and output faces may emerge within the same
continued circuit.

- Helmut Hauser et al., [“Towards a theoretical foundation for morphological
  computation”](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00422-012-0471-0), *Biological
  Cybernetics* 105 (2012).

### Reservoir computing — neighbouring dynamic, different construction

Reservoir computing demonstrates that driven transient dynamics and fading history can be
computational resources. Many reservoir systems keep a fixed or randomly supplied reservoir and
train a separate readout. Soma does not infer a fixed-reservoir/readout architecture: its standing
topology grows through passage, and output is a receiver/world relation rather than a permanently
privileged head.

- Shaobo Du et al., [“Reservoir computing using dynamic memristors for temporal information
  processing”](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-017-02337-y), *Nature Communications* 8
  (2017).

### Reversible and thermodynamic computing — exact boundary, important non-equivalence

Landauer tied logically irreversible operations to physical dissipation, and Bennett showed that
general computation can be organized reversibly. Soma agrees that an abstract operation and its
material history cannot be carelessly separated. Its time parity is nevertheless **not** reversible
state reconstruction: lawful transport need not preserve a unique route back to merged founders or
a recoverable past.

- Rolf Landauer, [“Irreversibility and Heat Generation in the Computing
  Process”](https://doi.org/10.1147/rd.53.0183), *IBM Journal of Research and Development* 5
  (1961).
- Charles H. Bennett, [“Logical Reversibility of Computation”](https://doi.org/10.1147/rd.176.0525),
  *IBM Journal of Research and Development* 17 (1973).

### Asynchronous and event-driven computation — structural resonance

Asynchronous circuits and elastic pipelines establish that a global clock is not the only lawful
coordinator of computation. They resonate with local completion and actual causal order. They do
not automatically imply Soma's three-body relation, retained construction identity, or
world-mediated production.

- Ivan Sutherland, [“Micropipelines”](https://doi.org/10.1145/63526.63532), *Communications of the
  ACM* 32 (1989).
- Alain J. Martin, [“Programming in VLSI: From Communicating Processes to Delay-Insensitive
  Circuits”](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/zmy86-a1w29), Caltech technical report
  (1989).

### Resonant and adiabatic logic — a future physical bridge, not present ontology

Some hardware literally uses resonant AC power clocks and energy recovery. That work makes the
DC/AC physical bridge concrete for a possible implementation. The current CUDA realization is not
such a circuit, and no energy advantage follows from the conceptual analogy alone.

- Edouard Jeanniot et al., [“Design and evaluation of a resonant clock generator for adiabatic
  logic”](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICRC.2017.8123661), *IEEE International Conference on Rebooting
  Computing* (2017).

### Topological quantum computing — terminology boundary

“Topological computer” already strongly evokes quantum computation through anyon braiding and
fusion. Soma presently makes no such claim. Its topology is the connectivity and transported
history of a driven causal medium; it neither requires quantum-coherent anyons nor inherits their
fault-tolerance results.

- Alexei Kitaev, [“Fault-tolerant quantum computation by
  anyons”](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0003-4916(02)00018-0), *Annals of Physics* 303 (2003).

  only solid semiconductor matter can realize the circuit.
