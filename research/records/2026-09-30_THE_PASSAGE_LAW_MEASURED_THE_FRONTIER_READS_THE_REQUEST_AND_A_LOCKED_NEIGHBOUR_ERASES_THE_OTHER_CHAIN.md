## 1. The law

**The request and its section are one span, read over its transported mass** (Lean
`HNN/IndexedOpen` §4, 13 theorems, standard axioms only). A crossing's datum is carried to the
reading frame by the source navigator's transport, a rotation–dilation of modulus `ρ ∈ (0, 1]` a
tick, and weighs `ρ^a / Σ_k ρ^(a_k)`, `a` its age at the span's end: the weights sum to one, are the
same in every frame, and are set by the span, not by where the request ends. At `ρ = 1` every datum
weighs `ν̂(n + v)`, the one population (candidate (a)); the retired reading weighed a section datum
`n/v` times a request cell (`separate_populations_ratio`), 40 at the open section.

**Chosen [agent-inferred]: (b), the transport's dissipation, its modulus a constitution locus the
executed comparison learns, one at the founding.** At a unitary transport every crossing's term in
the bank's phase-carried face has the same modulus at every lag (`lossless_term_modulus`), so no
reading marks the span's frontier where a continuation's seed lies; the development reads measured
it (a teacher-forced fit under (a) reads 386 of 1,024 held-out decisions, the retired law's 869, and
releases no whole section; with the decay the rule is read, teacher-forced up to 1,024 of 1,024). A
modulus `ρ` weighs the term at lag `r` by `ρ^r` (`dissipative_term_modulus`), a passive element
(`ρ² ≤ 1`), moved only by the certified move under every commit guard.

**Modality.** A text cell, an image pixel at its scan tick, an acoustic sample at its sample tick
and a motor screw at its step are crossings of a ring's clock, carried by one transport and weighed
by one law. Nothing reads a window, a suffix depth, a position table or a lag.

**Scope, owed.** Ages are read from the phases, exact within one turn; a longer passage is refused
below modulus one (the leaky count is owed); the card and the bank's face path refuse a modulus
below one.
