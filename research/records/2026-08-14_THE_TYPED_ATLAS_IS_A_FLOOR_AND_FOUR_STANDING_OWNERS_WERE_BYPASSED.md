## 2. Four owners that stand and were bypassed

### `derivation_atlas` — used only after this record's occasion

*The admitted path is an edge* returned a printed `Vec<Edge>`. `found_circuit` already returns a
`GradedCausalComplex` with vertices, recruitment and reach 1-cells, spanning forest, routes and cycle
agreement, and `invariant_movement`/`route_movement` already read one circuit against another. **That
is the finger-trap defect inside a movement declared complete** — a new organ beside an existing
owner.

Repaired the same day, and the return is what the list could not show
(`crates/holonic-engine/examples/the_proved_edge_moves_the_circuit.rs`):

```text
  before   vertices 42   recruitment 1-cells 63   reach 1-cells 10   routes 10
  after    vertices 44   recruitment 1-cells 64   reach 1-cells 11   routes 11

  invariants still  false
  fields moved      {Cells, BoundaryRank, FillingRank}
  founded statement (∑ n : Old, proportionalFlow … n m) ≤ capacity m
  founded route     -> receiver_load_under_capacity, depth 1
```

**A kernel-admitted proof is a founded statement and a founded route, and it moves three integer
invariants of the complex.** Both reach orientations agree, which is the symmetry a pure recruitment
chain is expected to show.

### `statement_composition` — the 60 mathematical obstructions cause nothing

`compose_candidates`, `adjudicate`, `admit`, `StatementObstruction`, `CompositionSpecies` exist so
that **an obstruction causes a composition**. The typed arm returned 60 mathematical obstructions and
they were counted and dropped. That is the return edge left open at a finer grain than the one this
session closed: the world refused sixty times and nothing was founded from the refusals.

### `conditioned_derivation` and `holonic_training` — the templates are authored, not founded

The emission's five shapes could be founded from the corpus's own proof bodies. `tactic_species` is
*already* read from the material and used only to filter an authored closer table of five, of which
this corpus can contribute three. The machine has organs for founding morphology from occurrence and
for predicting before mutating; the emission uses neither.

### The GPU

Every measurement in this plan ran on one CPU path. `CLAUDE.md` is explicit that the GPU owns the
deed, and the kernel adjudication — 213 paths at ~9 s each — is the one part that is genuinely
serial-per-path and was the whole wall time.

## 3. What the plan did buy, stated without inflation

Structural refusals 147 → 0 on the declared corpus; the emission's arguments built in the recruited
declaration's own frame; `v` verified against the kernel seven of seven; `D` verified against Lean
seventeen of seventeen; a reader whose arrival graph nearly doubled and whose four measured defects
fell by two orders of magnitude. **Those are floors, and floors are what later current threads.**

What it did not buy: **the typed arm admits zero paths the inherited arm did not.** The machine
proves exactly what it proved before.
