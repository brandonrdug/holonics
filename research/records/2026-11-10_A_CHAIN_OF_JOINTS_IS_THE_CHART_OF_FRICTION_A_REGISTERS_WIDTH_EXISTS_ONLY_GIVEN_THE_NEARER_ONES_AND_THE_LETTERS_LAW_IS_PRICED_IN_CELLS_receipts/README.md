# Receipts: host count rulers of November 10 (evening)

Rulers on the host, never machinery. Every script reads the python-codes passages (and, for
`cap_a3.py`, the held locked-visit dumps) under `${HOLONICS_ROOT}.local/`, which stay private; only
aggregate codes are written here. None used the card.

- `guess_capacity.txt`: guesses G1 to G12, each with the UTC time it was fixed, before its read.
- `capacity_reads.txt`: the reads and the scores.
- `yard.py`: the loader (answer letters and the void, a pad past the ends) and the Witten-Bell
  tables. The other scripts `exec` its definitions and read `train.npz`, which is `load('TRAIN.txt')`
  saved as arrays `x` and `seg`.
- `cap_a.py`, `cap_a2.py`: two letters each side, cut to the `k` most frequent contexts.
  `cap_a3.py <state>`: a held locked visit against that cut table (it reads `xr_held.npz` and
  `gi_held.npy`, written by `xr_a.py` and `xr_b.py` of the chord record's `ruler/`).
- `cap_b.py`: scattered written letters, every subset of the eight neighbours its own table.
- `cap_c.py`, `both3.py`: the left tables' cells and the tables by ring.
- `cap_d.py K PASSES [hash]`: the far pair through `K` classes beside the near pair.
- `cap_e.py D`, `cap_e_right.py`, `cap_e.log.txt`: registers by distance.
- `add1.py K tag EPOCHS`: the additive reader (a sum of separate rows, no joint cell).
