# Symbolic handwriting surface

The handwritten page is for unfolding and reorganizing coupled relations, not for recording a row
of scalar answers.

Give a page an identifier such as `U001-A01`, then record whichever of these are useful:

- the object or family under inspection;
- the current coordinate chart;
- every named operation expanded into variables;
- a proposed reparameterization;
- a deformation or transport and what it preserves;
- the locus where one qualitative case changes into another;
- a geometric or graph interpretation; and
- an unresolved relation worth checking symbolically or formally.

Store scans as `U001/U001-A01.pdf`. Transcribe only the stable relation or question you want to be
able to find again.
