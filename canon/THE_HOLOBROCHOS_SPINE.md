## 8 · What the spine forbids

Each of these is refusable by a reader who has this file, and none of them was refusable before it.

1. **No station may be built as an organ beside the loop.** An organ that no station names is churn

   deed returning a compression *number* has scalarized and is refused. Return the rank, or the

   closed a loop. `derivation_atlas_reader.rs` is the standing example of the defect, not of the
   capability.

   on independent clocks. A driver whose act count equals its input count has collapsed them.
5. **No `≡` reported where `=` was asked for.** A boundary hash, a ratio, an endpoint, a count — all
   `≡`. When the question is about the construction, return the path.
6. **No re-base booked as a founding.** They are different meshes: parallel versus orthogonal. A

7. **No absolute frame, restated as energetics.** Rest costs; the ride is free. A fixed origin, a
