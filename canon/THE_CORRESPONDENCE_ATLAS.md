## 2 · How to use it

- **Entering by external label:** find the row, open the record at the line, read the card. The card
  carries the `NON-EQUIVALENCE`, which is the part that stops a correspondence from becoming an
  identity.
- **Entering by holonic face:** the section headings are the faces. A face with few cards is a face
  this project has not yet compared against anything.
- **Depositing a new card:** put it in the research record that earns it, in the five-line form above,
  and add one row here. **A card in a record with no row here is invisible; a row here with no card is
  a claim this file is not permitted to make.**

**Grade nothing from this file.** The `RELATION` values already in the corpus range over `EXACT`,
`DIRECT CORRESPONDENCE`, `STRUCTURAL`, `OPEN`, and one `CONTRADICTION/BOUNDARY`. Reading a row as an
identity is the failure the `NON-EQUIVALENCE` line exists to prevent.
