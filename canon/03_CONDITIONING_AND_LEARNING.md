# Conditioning and arbitrary learning

**Status.** Mounting/exposure/training/reference and the training receipt are `definition`; the
extensible substrate is `project-postulate`; unrestricted arbitrary learning and general
interaction/filler induction remain `open`.

## Intelligence is intermediate morphology

Intelligence is the contemporary causal organization by which a body receives, transforms,
retains, and emits differences. Its operative law is not an external learner acting on passive
data; it is the conditioned morphology itself: incidence, phase distributions, permitted paths,
local constitutive response, resource gradients, recurrent identities, codecs, obstruction, and
response spectra.

This definition is domain-agnostic without flattening domains. A particle ecology, organism,
mathematical ecology, conversation, market, optical field, and program can share the event grammar
while retaining different local constitutive laws.

## Four events that must not be conflated

1. **Mounting** supplies an inherited organ that remains part of the contemporary machine.
2. **Exposure** lets material cross and leave candidate differences or aggregate testimony.
3. **Training** is a returned passage that changes reusable morphology and later conduct.
4. **Reference** consults separately retained testimony without claiming it became native conduct.

Caching a corpus, index, retrieval result, proof, or conversation beside a body is not training.

## Training contract

For body `H_m`, developmental passage `d`, return `r`, and later probe `p`, a valid training event
must establish:

\[
H_m \xrightarrow{d/r} H_{m'},\qquad
\operatorname{conduct}(H_m,p)\ne\operatorname{conduct}(H_{m'},p),
\]

with `m'` persisting after `d` and its source corpus are detached and the body is rested/remounted.
The receipt retains predecessor identity, caused support, local `Δm`, return lineage, alternatives,
and the behavioral ablation.

Training is not required to change every probe. It must change the declared later conduct for
which the returned difference was consequential.

## Arbitrary learning

The machine is arbitrary with respect to medium, codec, and local law because the substrate
requires only:

- typed occurrence and port identity;
- caused local incidence;
- an exact or explicitly bounded carrier;
- a local constitutive response capable of staging a difference;
- a return that can alter reusable morphology;
- receiver-relative grading; and
- rest/remount that preserves the acquired organization without source replay.

Text, images, sound, proofs, sensor fields, geometry, instructions, chemical measurements, and
behavioral events therefore enter through the same causal contract without first being collapsed
into one semantic vector space. Co-presence through shared occurrence, region, chronology,
geometry, calibration, or consequence supplies a common indexing span. Gluing requires a declared
interaction and compatible restrictions under the chosen descent law; every chart, alternative,
and lineage remains present until then.

“Arbitrary” does not mean a prewritten universal response law. It is a `project-postulate`
substrate contract: any ecology supplied with the typed ports and local constitutive capability
above may condition its intermediate morphology without changing the substrate ontology. A
general law which induces unknown interactions or fillers across transported variation remains
`open`; each domain owes the actual interaction that makes its return consequential.

## Generalization, prediction, and generation

- **Generalization:** new current conducts through morphology changed by prior returned passages.
- **Prediction:** an open continuation section is forced by the current local law before its own
  return.
- **Generation:** the contemporary body emanates a new caused passage which was not mounted as a
  stored answer.
- **Recognition:** a current navigates carried incidence and closes against a receiver; it is not
  merely a label lookup.

Each claim owes a held-out return and an ablation. A diagnostic topology, candidate count, or
source quotation is supporting testimony, not the deed.

## Codecs and reflection

A codec is an inherited or conditioned organ relating two presentation charts. UTF-8, a human
language, a proof syntax, image encoding, bytecode, an ISA, and a sensor transduction are codecs.
No codec is universal internal language.

Reflection occurs when a current reifies its operative codec and continuation. A returned codec
revision is genuine only when the lineaged revision governs the same resumed continuation and
changes later conduct. A version number by itself proves nothing.

## Failure modes

Do not rebuild:

- a learner/data/law trichotomy;
- a global loss or acceptance-count gate;
- a semantic model per modality plus an invented bridge;
- corpus scans presented as thought;
- retrieval presented as native memory;
- generated text assembled from source quotations and called learning;
- source-retaining rest;
- CPU-authored phases that decide which organ “thinks” next; or
- device selection based on terms such as language, proof, or graphics.
