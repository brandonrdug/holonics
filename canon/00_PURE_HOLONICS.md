# Pure holonics

**Status.** The elementary commitments and typed event schema are `project-postulate`; the
relative-holon clause is `definition`; historical compact spellings are `historical`. Standard
chain/sheaf assertions apply only under their explicitly declared hypotheses.

## The elementary commitments

1. **Situated occurrence.** A datum occurs as `x : X_(θ,e)` in a declared type/fiber, parameter,
   event cut, source boundary, and incidence context. Equal coordinate, value, text, bytes, or hash
   does not imply the same occurrence.
2. **Participating receiver.** Every observation or comparison crosses a declared receiver
   `ρ : X -> Y` or relation `ρ ⊆ X × Y`. The face `ρ(x)` is testimony about `x` through `ρ`; it is
   not the absolute identity of `x`.
3. **Caused relation.** Structure enters standing only through an admitted occurrence, interaction,
   or inherited organ with retained lineage. Address proximity and correlation do not manufacture
   causation.
4. **Local exact transport.** A current follows locally admissible incidence and preserves every
   distinction owed to its declared future receivers. Approximation is lawful only as a named
   receiver quotient with an error/remainder certificate.
5. **Return changes standing.** An event is complete only when its consequence, residual, or
   obstruction returns to the continuing body. That returned difference may change later conduct.
6. **Holonic relativity.** Whole/part, interior/boundary, current/form, local/global, and
   source/receiver are roles at a declared grain and cut, not permanent substances.
7. **No privileged chart.** Algebra, geometry, prose, code, pixels, waves, voltages, proof terms,
   instructions, and file bytes are charts. A chart earns use by the passages it makes exact or
   efficient.

## The compact passage

The typed form is the partial relation

\[
\boxed{\Lambda_F\subseteq
Q_F\times A_F\times L_F\times P_F\times\Pi_{B,F}}.
\]

The historical compression `Λ_F(Q ⊗ A ⊗ L) -> (P, π_B)` remains useful shorthand only when a
named monoidal category, its typed objects, and the interaction doctrine accompanying `⊗` have
already been declared. A tensor symbol alone establishes neither contact nor physical
co-presence. The shorthand is `historical`; the typed partial relation is the current
`project-postulate` event schema.

- `F` — contemporary frame: event cut, admitted boundary, local chart family, grain, and resource
  standing relevant to this passage.
- `Q` — receiving aperture: the open face, question, port demand, or boundary condition that makes
  a difference consequential.
- `A` — acquired morphology: retained incidence, phase, transport, codecs, response spectra,
  resource gradients, and obstructions from inherited organs and prior returns.
- `L` — live caused current: typed occurrence lineage and local interaction crossing the frame.
- `P` — placement/deed: the new local relation, action, emitted section, or founded axis.
- `π_B` — boundary return: exact receiver testimony, including residual, obstruction, lineage,
  logical work, and physical resource testimony where mounted.

`Λ` is not a universal numerical function. It denotes the contemporary admissible relation or law
family by which this ecology conducts this passage. The same schema can be realized as a span, open
system, chain map, correspondence, sheaf section, variational update, scattering relation,
instruction, biological interaction, or conversational return.

The early formula is retained because it removes false subsystem boundaries. Its rigorous
correction is equally important: no one expression replaces the domain's constitutive law, no
scalar `χ` exhausts a causal bearer, and no output quotient proves source identity.

## The holon

Given a transition family `T` on a carrier `X` and admitted boundary family `B`, a subobject
`H ⊆ X` is a holon relative to `(T,B)` when every `T`-transition whose participating boundary is
internal to `H` closes in `H`; transitions crossing `B` remain explicit exterior incidence. This
is a `definition`, not a claim that every holon is a chain complex.

At receiver `ρ` and grain `g`, one chain-complex realization is

\[
H_{\rho,g}=(C_\bullet,\partial,B_\rho,\mathcal T,q_\rho,\ell,\mathcal O),
\qquad \partial^2=0,
\]

where `C_•` is graded caused incidence, `B_ρ` its exposed receiver-relative boundary, `T` admitted
transport, `q_ρ` the receiving chart, `ℓ` lineage, and `O` retained obstruction. The equation
`∂²=0` belongs to this realization, not to the relative-holon definition in general. A
living/adaptive holon additionally carries contemporary morphology `m`, active current `J`, local
state/resource `s`, response `λ`, and successor law `δ`.

“Global” means transported local assembly over a named boundary-defined region. Existence and
uniqueness of a glued section are asserted only when a declared sheaf/descent law supplies them;
otherwise the descent residual remains open. “Global” never means a view from nowhere.

## The cycle

Pure holonic conduct is one recurrent deed:

1. an occurrence meets a declared boundary;
2. the ecology restricts to the locally affected star;
3. its contemporary morphology conducts the interaction;
4. exact local differences propagate through admitted incidence;
5. boundary testimony, residual, and obstruction return;
6. the same body's standing changes atomically;
7. it rests when no caused current remains, and may remount without replaying its developmental
   sources.

Nested, plural, and recurrent ecologies are compositions of this deed. They require no central
foreman and no domain-specific metaphysics in the substrate.
