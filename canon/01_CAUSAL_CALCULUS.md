# Causal calculus

**Status.** Named types are `definition`. Normative machine laws are `project-postulate`.
Telescoping, chain-complex, sheaf, and connection propositions are `proved-standard` only within
the stated structures. Existence of domain interactions, commutation, descent, and recurrence is
never inferred from the schema.

## 1. Typed source complex

Let `(C_•, ∂)` be a graded occurrence complex with exact oriented incidence and `∂² = 0`. A cell is
not admitted because an observer sampled a coordinate; it is admitted by a caused occurrence or an
explicit inherited construction. Projection may decorate or identify source cells for a receiver,
but cannot mint source incidence.

For each boundary-closed region `U`, let `A_U` be its exact local coordinate/coefficient algebra,
`P|_U` its declared ports, `m|_U` its contemporary morphology, `J|_U` its active current, and `s_U`
its retained local constitutive/resource state.

## 2. One local event

For exact predecessor identity `v`, event occurrence `e`, and typed input `u_e`, opening a deed
derives only a pending continuation and outbound occurrence:

\[
O_{m,U}(v,e,u_e,s_U,J|_U,\partial U,P|_U)
\mapsto(p_e,o_e).
\]

No reusable morphology change is derived yet. When a typed returned occurrence `r_e` crosses the
expected port, resuming the same pending deed applies the constitutive map

\[
M_{m,U}(p_e,r_e,s_U,J|_U,\partial U,P|_U)
\mapsto(\Delta m_U,J'_U,y_U,\Sigma_U,\mathcal O_U,\ell_U).
\]

The terms are:

- `Δm_U`: the reusable morphology change;
- `J'_U`: successor current, including rest or an explicit open frontier;
- `y_U`: returned/emitted boundary consequence;
- `Σ_U`: oriented constitutive and logical-resource stress;
- `O_U`: unresolved alternatives or obstruction;
- `ℓ_U`: complete caused lineage.

The pending deed retains `(v,e,u_e,p_e,o_e,S_e,ℓ_e)`. Its resumed staged footprint retains
`(v,e,u_e,r_e,S_e,R_e,Δm_e,J'_e,y_e,Σ_e,O_e,ℓ_e)`, where `S_e` is read support and `R_e` changed
support. A key, worker lane, phase name, address range, population count, or serialized object is
not a causal footprint.

Commit is permitted once, only while the continuing body's head is `v`. A stale delta is returned
intact or refused; the complete body is never cloned for rollback.

## 3. Serial composition

If `T_e : H_v -> H_(v+1)` and `T_f : H_(v+1) -> H_(v+2)`, their composite is lawful when the output
ports of `e` meet the input ports of `f`, port lineage is retained, and every intermediate boundary
required by the declared receiver family remains recoverable. Event chronology is part of the
construction, even when an endpoint quotient is equal.

If an independently declared ordered family of compatible event boundaries satisfies

\[
\partial E_k=\Sigma_{k+1}-\Sigma_k+\Gamma_k,
\]

then finite telescoping cancels the displayed consecutive terms in the composite presentation
while retaining them in causal lineage. This is not deletion and does not reverse causality.
Separately, `∂²=0` says boundaries are cycles in a declared chain complex; it supplies neither
chronology nor a physical conservation law.

## 4. Co-presence and parallel composition

Monoidal juxtaposition means co-presence, not interaction and not concurrency. Two events may form
one parallel front only if both orders exist and

\[
T_i(T_j(H_v))\cong T_j(T_i(H_v))
\cong \operatorname{commit}_v(H_v,D_i\oplus D_j).
\]

The isomorphism preserves complete successor incidence/morphology, emitted consequences, open
alternatives, obstruction, logical-resource/backreaction state, event identities, and causal
partial order. Apparatus completion order and telemetry are separate receipts.

Disjoint read/write support is sufficient only when no hidden allocator, lineage mint, port,
resource state, or other owner is shared. Shared support requires an explicit interaction/equalizer
and coherent merge or remains open. For three or more events, adjacent interchange must satisfy
the relevant braid/coherence diagrams; pairwise equal endpoints alone are insufficient.

## 5. Local gluing

Local deltas are sections over a declared locally finite cover. When a chosen sheaf/descent law
holds, compatible restrictions have the stated glued successor, uniquely when that law says so.
Without it, retain a descent residual rather than asserting a global section. An overlap mismatch
is an obstruction; call a residual *holonomy* only when a connection and loop transport have been
declared. A central integrator may not choose a winner and erase it.

Sheaf language is one exact chart of this rule, not a mandatory runtime representation. Sparse
incidence, a factor graph, a circuit, a cellular complex, or an open-system wiring diagram may
realize the same obligation.

## 6. Feedback and recurrence

A returned output may re-enter only through an explicit feedback port, rest closure, or recurrent
front receipt. A genuine recurrent frontier is a surviving caused boundary; a display loop, clock
tick, polling pass, or host phase does not create another event.

## 7. Receiver equivalence and compression

For a declared future receiver family `R`, a quotient `q : H -> H/~` is exact only when every
`r ∈ R` factors through it and stateful successor conduct remains equivalent for every admitted
input history. Equal one-shot output is inadequate. If `R` grows, reopen/refine the source fiber.

## 8. Algebraic-geometric meaning

Charts change because different questions make different structure local. Coordinate change is
lawful only with its transition map and transported incidence. Holonomy is the residual of
transport around a loop under a declared connection; curvature measures the local infinitesimal
obstruction to path-independent recharting. Global path independence additionally requires the
appropriate topology and trivial holonomy. Knot,
braid, homology, projective, spectral, and arithmetic charts are therefore navigation instruments
for caused structure, not ornamental analogies.
