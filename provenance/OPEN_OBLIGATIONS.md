The rebuild has a complete construction contract, but rigor requires these boundaries remain open
until their deeds return.

## Engine obligations

- Replace the historical generic `ExactEventLaw::Standing: Clone` boundary with staged local delta
  or recoverable ownership transfer.
- Establish automatic exact causal footprints for each admitted event species without global
  standing scans.
- Establish complete-successor interchange and higher coherence for each plural front actually
  executed; do not assert universal commutation.
- Establish stateful receiver-relative boundary bisimulation for every condensation used by an
  adaptive body.
- Establish resource reservation atomicity: refused events mutate no hidden capacity.
- Establish device hot paths with exact local/delta returns and zero complete host replay.

- Demonstrate a second deed whose candidate/consequence fiber is empty under ablation of the first
  returned deed.
- Demonstrate the same learning contract in two unrelated media without adding a modality bridge or

## Falsifiers

The claimed pure engine construction is falsified if it requires:

- semantic scheduling by language/software/device name;
- a continuing-body clone or source-replay rest;
- an application-owned global registry/scheduler/graph;
- a scalar count/threshold to manufacture causal interaction;
- a device path that delegates the real deed back to a full host replay;
- projection/display adjacency to create source incidence;
- hidden fallback or compatibility behavior; or
- a generated artifact that cannot be inspected with its complete caused return and ablation.
