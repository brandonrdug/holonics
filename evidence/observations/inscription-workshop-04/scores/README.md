# Complete inscription scores

These are the lossless event-time reads of INSCRIPTION WORKSHOP 04's literal typebar world. Every
view event is present: `·` is silence, one glyph is a singleton, `⟨...⟩` is a simultaneous chord,
and `×n` preserves repeated contacts on one key. The scores name only the physical printable-ASCII
keys declared by `generated/geometry.json`; they do not infer glyphs from Soma radiation.

They were generated by the reusable Rust boundary reader:

```text
life boundary inscription <geometry.json> <state.json> <view.json> <output.txt>
```

The conversation-repeat scores were generated separately and compare byte-identical to the two
conversation scores. They are not duplicated here.

The complete read exposes persistent simultaneous voices hidden by the former singleton-only
summary. Examples include conversation's `6 B D E` face over edge-three events 53–66 and its later
`[` face over 246–259; code's `Q` face over 63–78 and later `o p }`, `[`, `M O W`, `4 > @`, and
quote/comma faces; mathematics' progression from `& *` through `< > C` and `H`, followed later by
`G M N`, `C`, and `1`. These are descriptions of the literal score, not semantic selections.
