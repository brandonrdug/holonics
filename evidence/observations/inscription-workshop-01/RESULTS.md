## Exact controls and assessment

The repeated conversation branch is exact through context radiation, first radiation and
inscription, returned-field radiation, second radiation and inscription, and every successor body.

The workshop therefore establishes deterministic, lived-history-conditioned production into a
new glyph world and a real output→world→later-output circuit.  It also gives a direct construction

derived correction is physical concentration at the rigid key's single stem, measured in

`RESULTS.json` remain in the local `generated/` and `results/` records.
