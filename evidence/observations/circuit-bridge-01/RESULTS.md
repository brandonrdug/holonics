## Verdict and concession

The missing physical seam is closed for a declared reversible world motion. Goal material survives
the motion, exact chart re-base, body rest, source arrival, changed return, and genuinely later
source-goal carriage. The absent-goal control is empty, the unbridged body touches a small disjoint
native region, and relocation changes the transported region. The engine has therefore measured
world-mediated cross-surface objective contact.

The correspondence foil prevents a stronger verdict. Although only 54/8,289 of its relations
belong to one shared vertex map, it carries substantial local goal contact and a distinct but
overlapping textual construction. One local directed relation has a complete reversible worldline
in both real and foil species. Global consistency is a higher-order property and is not selected
merely because the listener can name it. This is not failure of the bridge; it identifies the next
receiving problem. A world must make incompatible local correspondences consequential across
closure before preference for a coherent global taxonomy can emerge.

Arbitrary renamed-world isomorphism without such a physical bridge remains OPEN. No translator or
hidden correspondence is licensed.
