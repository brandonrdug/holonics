## Question and stopping condition

This cell asked:

> Can one exact local triangulation law remain consequential after its source lineage departs,
> act on later quadrilaterals in changed scale and chart, and expose the information lost by a
> cross-ratio-plus-endpoint quotient?

The bounded law was the Ptolemy flip for a decorated ideal quadrilateral,

```text
e f = a c + b d,
f   = (a c + b d) / e,
```

but the carrier was not a named `Ptolemy` switch. It was a finite, generic, ordered program:

```text
0 input a
1 input b
2 input c
3 input d
4 input e
5 multiply 0 2
6 multiply 1 3
7 add      5 6
8 divide   7 4
```

The matched quotient retained only the source cross-ratio `chi=(a*c)/(b*d)=10/21` and source
endpoint `f=31`. The run stopped after exact source return, source-lineage departure, exact
rest/remount, four later closed paths, the `e=0` seam, the quotient foil, a no-Standing control,
and exact returned-result comparisons had all been read.

The physical boundary was fixed before conduct: host only, 30 seconds per event, 120 seconds for
the cell, at most 256 regional arcs in any event, no CUDA, and no floating-point causal data.

## Complete later paths

Every later current recruited the resting complete carrier, and the returned constituent exposed
both that exact program and the exact current. A generic rational transducer then evaluated the
recovered nodes. It did not dispatch on the name “Ptolemy.”


The cross-ratio remained `10/21`, while the alternate diagonal changed from `31` to `62`. This is
the direct witness that the projective quotient does not retain metric scale.


remained exact.

## Foils

The quotient control received the same uniform-scale-two current. It recovered its exact
`chi=10/21` and old endpoint `31`, and recovered the new current, but it exposed no program and
formed no output. The scaled receiver is `62`, so copying the old endpoint would have been false.

The no-Standing control recovered the same scaled current but no program. Its requested program
capability remained an open contact and no output formed.

## What this establishes

This cell establishes:

- a source-declared finite algorithm can cross as exact causal incidence and remain after its
  source lineage ends;
- changed scale, changed chart, their composition, and an exact rational receiver can recruit
  that same resting relation;
- the complete node path, not only the endpoint, can be recovered and returned;
- a projective quotient can be exact for one observation while discarding a direction needed by
  a later receiver; and
- a discriminant is an OPEN continuation boundary, not an instruction to erase the valid prefix.

The cell also isolates what it does **not** establish. Soma carried and recruited the program, but
the generic exact world transducer interpreted the recovered nodes and returned the result. The
engine did not itself enact multiply, add, or divide from the standing topology. Installing a
Ptolemy interpreter inside Soma would be the wrong repair; the open engine question is how a live
carried relation can directly participate in a later transition without a domain-side replay of
its operation.
