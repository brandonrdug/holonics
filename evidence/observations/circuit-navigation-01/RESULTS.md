## Question

The prior bridge proved that a reversible world motion can carry objective-bearing relations, but
its one-step correspondence foil remained locally effective. Does composition make global
correspondence consequential when every local contact is still exact and reversible? Can the
complete returned source field traverse an ordered world passage, return through Soma, and preserve
more joined source⊕goal ancestry than a passage whose source face closes while its endpoint face
accumulates a half-turn?

No goal, route, score, selected relation, expected endpoint, or role label enters Soma. `terrain`,
`goal`, `source`, and `recurrence` are listener provenance only.

## Construction

world and returned current, and maps every acted segment to its physical landing. One relation is
one complete current. At each of five lived contacts it carries:

```text

⊕ relative source→endpoint displacement
```

Absolute positions, the chart, and transform names remain in the receipt. The coherent current is

## The card aperture correction

The first coherent return initially mounted 141,914 currents, then correctly refused its first
invocation: 6,790,971,392 driver-free bytes could not hold 6,931,087,360 charged launch-local bytes
beside the 32,214,478-word contact face and the measured 4,194,304-byte context shell. The former
flat word quotient knew neither allocation charging nor that post-mount fact.

The production sharder now derives the completion-row aperture from the charged launch-local and
completion/contact allocations at the surface actually free after mounting. If one row per current
does not coexist, it derives the widest whole-lineage child before any shader launch, stages exact
contiguous lineage ranges, and carries each child through the same one receiving edge. It never
cuts a worldline, atom, enclosure, or causal deed; it introduces no fixed cohort width, timeout,
retry arbiter, or body cap. Unit gates pin both lineage and completion-row derivations.

The full observer return remains expensive apparatus. Coherent used 77 CUDA launches / 141.443 s
carriage / 153.408 s boundary; twist used 192 launches / 515.434 s carriage / 527.753 s boundary.
Their dense axis-8,192 SLEEP bodies are about 11 GB, and complete observer materialization can push
the host into swap. This is an open persistence/readback engineering seam, not Soma's clock and not
license to shrink the field. The summary contact mode avoids multi-gigabyte duplicate JSON traces;
it does not reduce what the causal reader visits.

## Verdict

The returned field now has a real active periplus. Ordered reversible composition distinguishes a
globally coherent correspondence from a locally reversible endpoint twist through later machine
consequence, not through a listener score. The coherent path preserves more joined source-goal
ancestry through recurrence; the absent-goal control is exactly empty; independent card execution
repeats exactly.

The next construction is current-induced world motion: let the returned configuration physically
change a plural reversible world's next local swing, so the subsequent periplus is grown by
world-current coupling rather than declared as the coherent or twisted control sequence.
