source material only when a declared receiver face is OPEN, and later conduct nonidentical currents
with less or no source participation?

## Cultivation law

For current face `Phi_c` and Standing population `H_t`:

```text
OPEN(c,H_t) <=> Phi(y_c(H_t)) != Phi(y_c(J)),   J={0,...,8191}.
```

The source repairs the first orientation mismatch by returning the greatest exact unseen
contribution with the target orientation. If orientation is already exact, it repairs the first
signed-order mismatch by returning the unseen contribution with the greatest positive exact
pairwise difference. Factor ordinal breaks exact ties. The source may return a plural batch, and
that batch crosses one co-present event. It is not serialized into false machine instants.

Persistent Soma changes only when returned factors become Standing. Context activations enter a
transient branch remounted from that Standing; the branch is read and released. Thus inference does
not turn every prior context into active ancestry. Source-absent conduct receives only the
remountable body, factor capabilities, and the present activation current. The upstream activation
current still originates in OLMo and is attributed accordingly.

## The causal learning contrast

The main online passage alone says that some later currents needed no source return. The necessary
counterfactual is stricter: would the original six factors already have closed them?

Two later zero-retrieval currents answer no:

| Later current | Original six | Standing available then | Source return |
|---|---|---|---:|
| prose · relevant after | OPEN | closed under 19 factors | 0 |
| code comment · irrelevant before | OPEN | closed under 20 factors | 0 |

Earlier returned relation therefore participated in changed later conduct. This is bounded
learning under the declared receiver face, not merely repeated source inference.

The converse also occurred. `prose.absent` and `code_comment.absent` would close under the original
six factors, but intervening cultivated Standing changed their signed order and reopened the face;
each needed one further factor. Growth is thus contextual deformation, not monotonic accumulation
of universally helpful parts.

## Interpretation and boundary

This is the first exact source-coupled cultivation passage in which:

1. pretrained numerical material became cellular Standing;
2. an OPEN contextual face caused a bounded, source-attributed return;
3. the returned constituents outlived their source lineages;
4. two genuinely later nonidentical currents used prior growth without another source return; and
5. a frozen change of objective order exposed the boundary of that reuse.

It does **not** establish a portable skill, learned router, alternate neural architecture, language
competence, general update law, or computational compression. The final conduct aperture used only
21 factors, but source selection scanned 122,685 candidates and every current still supplied the
full upstream OLMo activation field. No latency, memory-traffic, energy, or downstream behavioral
advantage was measured.

The result supports the source-ecology hypothesis in its narrow form: pretrained structure can be
circulated into Eros and can change later conduct. It also rejects the stronger shortcut that a
small locally adequate population automatically becomes a context-independent ability. The next
distinct question would require a materially grounded downstream consequence and a recruitment
relation whose own cost does not depend on scanning the complete reservoir. This result schedules
nothing.
