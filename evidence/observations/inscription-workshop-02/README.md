# INSCRIPTION WORKSHOP 02 · THE KEYCAP MEETS THE WORLD AT ONE JOINT

**GRADE: DERIVED JOINT COUPLING BUILT / CUDA-MEASURED · EXACT REPEAT · HISTORY-SPECIFIC
TEMPORAL GLYPH PRODUCTION · RETURN-CONDITIONED SECOND PASSAGE**

WORKSHOP 01 coupled every completed visual contact over a rigid 12×12 keycap directly to its
typebar. That transported the whole machine output, but it made the world mechanically absurd:
one key could be actuated by 144 independent retinal contacts and 124 of 129 tape positions became
85–90-glyph chords.

The correction is a joint, not a filter. Each keycap remains completely visible and every radiation
row remains present, but the rigid key has one central stem through which motion reaches its
typebar. A native CUT landing at that exact world joint stamps the key's own glyph. No magnitude,
threshold, ranking, preferred glyph, or after-the-fact winner decides the passage. Co-present joint
contacts still overprint at one tape position.

The three substantial histories and exact conversation repeat are inherited unchanged from
WORKSHOP 01. They receive the same complete keyboard field and 129-event gaze. The produced field
then returns through the eye and the identical keyboard passes again. This asks whether a physically
concentrated output coupling turns the already-measured plural construction into legible temporal
glyph configurations without deleting the rest of the machine's radiation.

It does.  The first passages contain 540–742 joint actuations rather than 17,632–29,658 whole-key
actuations, with local singleton runs `ZY`, `nn`, and `uu`.  After each produced field returns
through the eye, the second passages form `UUUvv`, `@Z@`, and `KZK`.  Exact repeat holds through
both passages, every radiation wire, the returned field, and every successor body.  The full read
is in `RESULTS.md`.
