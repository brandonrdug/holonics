## The chosen world physics

Each exact source `SegmentDeed` starts one directed state `(p,q) = (source, landing)`. At an
occupied port `q`, its complete `RegionalForm`

```text
(same, other, this_way, that_way)
```

is grounded componentwise at the circuit world's declared dyadic axis. This is a **lossy world
transducer**, not an inverse reading of `RegionalForm`; the complete packed form remains in the
current and receipt. The four ring coefficients form the ordered elementary shears

```text
M_q = X(same) · Y(other) · X(this_way) · Y(−that_way)
```

over `Z / axis`. Every elementary shear has determinant one, hence so does `M_q`. The local move is

```text
F(p,q) = (q, q + M_q(q − p))
F⁻¹(q,r) = (q − M_q⁻¹(r − q), q)
```

with all positions and tangents carried in the dyadic ring. A relation stops only by exiting onto
an absent or locally unborn port, or closes only by returning exactly to its initial directed
state. There is no step extent, visited table, timeout, route, score, selected current, or random
choice. On a fully occupied finite snapshot, the move is a permutation and the orbit returns to
its own initial state. A branch-only pole remains an exit because branches cannot forge a local
field.

The emitted light carries source-edge configuration ⊕ source form ⊕ branch form ⊕ landing form ⊕
initial relative tangent, followed by each encountered standing form ⊕ changed tangent. Absolute
positions, grounded coefficients, matrices, and exact inverse witnesses remain in the receipt.
