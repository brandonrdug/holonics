## The model that generates and learns

[definition] Athena uses one continuing HNN object `M=(K,Θ,|H⟩)`: caused contact incidence and
chart transport K, constitutive/learned material Θ, and a resident joint section with boundary
and interior currents. It generates by evolving that section. Text coordinates, image patches
and acoustic samples are receiving charts of the resulting field; they do not schedule its
refinement. The drawing's toroidal carriers illustrate circulating support and shared contacts,
not a prescribed number of neurons, heads, layers or torus objects in memory.

[definition] A usable model invocation prepares a partial field and its boundary constraints,
transports it through the constituted contacts, composes local reactions and interior evolution,
then reads the requested joint boundary. At a fixed operator cut, one actual constituent is

```text
A=I+D D*,       A v=2(u+D b),
w=v−u,         b_next=D* v−b.
```

D is the very material to be developed, u the incident section, b the retained interior.
The rest of the network composes this with its actual phase/contact transports, normalized
participation and local learned reactions. A pure fixed scattering map is linear and norm
preserving; its existence alone does not supply learned nonlinear organization. Nonlinear
material/participation and changing incidence enter through their own existing laws.

[definition] The subsequent [continuing-plate clarification](RECEIVER_HOLARCHY.md#a-displayed-body-is-a-cut-its-plates-can-be-continuing-bodies)
applies to this same assembly. A receiving interface can have its own field, storage, current,
material and motion, coupled into the joint HNN state. A pictured boundary is one cut of that
interaction, not an inert terminal surface. Its state-dependent transport/participation and
receiving differential are part of the forward/adjoint composition. Context is carried by
those live couplings and retained interior responses; a literal event stack is not introduced.

[definition] The generative invocation and its training comparison use the same operator.
For output covectors `g_w,g_b`, define `λ=A^(-1)(g_w+D g_b)` in this unit-admittance chart.
Its complete local reverse calculation is

```text
g_u=2λ−g_w,          g_in=2D*λ−g_b,
G_D=λ(b−b_next)* + v(g_b−D*λ)*.
```

The derivative also propagates into source, interior current, normalized participation and
any material-dependent transport. An output comparison which changes only a separate readout
matrix would omit these terms. The supplied material metric and update law turn the covector
into an admissible coefficient change; the finite recomputation retains the mixed terms.
