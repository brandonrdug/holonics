## 1. The line

The construction serves one line ([THE_MACHINE](../THE_MACHINE.md#the-line-the-rebuild-serves)):
compression is intelligence is navigation. Holonic Compression couples a fractal navigator's
resonating modes with terrain, landmark discovery locates the faces where navigator paths converge,
and the HNN executes both at scale.
- Every campaign names the navigators, terrain, kernel, cokernel and landmarks it touches.
- RH, Hodge, complex Euler and Navier–Stokes, and BSD are targets of the same objects. Their Lean
  is worked alongside every step, never behind the HNN and never as a separate category.
- Athena is the HNN's first intended product. Text, code, image, sound and motion are boundary
  charts of one machine: a design is checked against every chart, and whatever is particular to a
  medium lives only in its boundary codec.

## 3. The campaign under way: Athena alpha on the store line

**The terrain.** Since November 12 the line reads a store of whole documents
([the passage record](../../research/records/2026-11-12_THE_PASSAGE_WIDENS_TO_FIVE_REGISTERS_OF_OPEN_TEXT_THE_CONSTITUTIONS_SIZE_AND_THE_ENGINES_RATE_BIND_BEFORE_THE_DATA_A_WIDTH_BELONGS_TO_ITS_PART_AND_A_REGION_DECLARES_THE_PERIODS_OF_ITS_OWN_HELIX.md)),
and since November 16 the store has seven slices: code, web, science, books and wikidata (open text, a
prefix of whole documents with held documents a slice), the arithmetic world (keyed records
`EXPR = VALUE`) and word problems. A section mixes the slices, each stretch of a document one region.
GSM8K's test split is the held yardstick and is never trained. The code passage of November 5 was the
line before it.

**The engine.** The line trains through `holonics_engine::field` in `crates/engine`, its card
realization `holonics_engine_cuda::hnn::field` in `crates/engine-cuda` and the commands of
`crates/athena` (the binary `athena`). [THE_ENGINE](../THE_ENGINE.md) is its guide: the pipeline
phase by phase, the declaration word by word, the saved state line by line and the commands with
the lines they print. Its [§11](../THE_ENGINE.md#11-the-theorys-laws-against-the-engine) sets the
theory's laws against what the engine holds, and a design on how the machine learns reads it first.

**The learner** (`Learner`; the trainer is `train-store`,
[`crates/athena/src/store.rs`](../../crates/athena/src/store.rs)).
- A stretch takes one state: a keyed draw of open places, the release's own state, or the
  frontier's (the context known and the next place alone open), the last a share of the pass that
  the state carries. Where the pass says so, a stretch at the frontier is read by two streams,
  every place compared at its own frontier in one forward
  ([THE_ENGINE §5](../THE_ENGINE.md#5-the-learners-state) gives the pass line's words).
- The comparison is the joint code at a state's open places, with the opening compared through its
  own lift, and its covector returns through the ticks by the operands that produced each carrier.
- A section's step is the covector's first moment over the root of its second, deposited at the
  rate the state carries. It deposits at its size with no reread of its own section.
- The pass's place over the store, its bounds and its share at the frontier are law and are carried
  in the learner's state, never in a run's switch.

**The loop.** A lap is a count of sections on the RTX 4080 SUPER, saved as states and as the mean
of its states. Each lap's law is fixed before launch, the guesses are written down as numbers
before the read and scored, a miss included, and the lap ends in its receipt.
- A build enters the line by reproducing the line's own lap, its reads included (gate 3,
  `tools/gate_engine.sh`).
- A change to the line runs beside a control from the same saved state, both read at their lap
  means, and leaves the line when its guess misses (CLAUDE.md, Method).
- A report opens with what the line wrote from its fixed panel of held prompts, with the code in
  nats and the loudness beside it.
- An iteration between saved states stays within one hour on the card (Brandon, November 6); a run
  that would pass it is a design change, not a longer run.

**Where it stands.** [CONSTRUCTION_STATE](../../CONSTRUCTION_STATE.md) holds the position: each
line that trains, its state, its readings by region and what is owed, in order. This plan restates
none of its readings.

**The order within step 8.**

## 4. Rules of the rebuild

- **Build forward from the elementary objects.** Each new owner states which object and law it
  implements ([operator contract](../ELEMENTARY_OBJECTS.md#operator-contract)) and which part of the
  line it serves.
- **Port when a step needs it.** A law, a kernel, a part or a whole configuration is ported from
  history (`13f8c734`, the October machine at `d5ed4adc1` and `d6bbd1644`, any commit) when a step
  needs it: read, rewritten against the current objects, and tested by the law it implements. A
  whole configuration may be restored as an attempt to be read beside the one that stands; nothing
  is kept for compatibility ([CLAUDE.md, a past configuration is an instruction](../../CLAUDE.md#past-configurations)).
  - The census at `13f8c734` locates history's owners
    ([`RUST_R0.tsv`](https://github.com/brandonrdug/holonics/blob/13f8c734/docs/plans/census/RUST_R0.tsv),
    [`LEAN_R0.tsv`](https://github.com/brandonrdug/holonics/blob/13f8c734/docs/plans/census/LEAN_R0.tsv)),
    and so does the manifest on `archive/leftovers-2026-09-24`.
  - Each port is recorded in its step's issue as history path, new owner, law and test.
- **What a port answers.** The retention audit and the lessons found these mechanisms failing a
  law: the per-occurrence tape and frozen-cut replay (retention); `returns` journals and archives of
  completed updates (retention); slot, session and episode wires (the aeon's clock); byte and nibble
  codecs run as the architecture (guard 9); key/value tables, context indexes and copiers with no
  dynamics of their own (stores and caches); foreign forward graphs served whole (equation
  extraction); Lean inside the HNN (languages are applications); floats inside a law (precision). A
  port of a configuration that held one of them states how its mechanism passes that law's test, and
  then proceeds. The list is the laws' test, never a list of configurations barred by name (the
  earlier heading was "Do not port", retired on November 18).
- **Precision is the bit widths of graphs** (CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md, the governing laws). No float
  is stored or consumed inside a law or the machine. Each term has a declared width, a carrier widens
  at a declared crossing, and a term is released only at a declared tolerance with its remainder
  carried.
- **One library for the laws, one backend for the card.** `holonics-engine` builds without CUDA, and
  `holonics-engine-cuda` depends on it alone and realizes its operations. Brandon's `codex/apple-silicon`
  becomes a second realization of the same port (`Realization`), only after the host reference exists,
  with parity for each law.
- **Learning runs on the card** (CLAUDE.md, "GPU first"). A part enters a learner run with its card
  realization and its parity against the host; the host realization is the reference that checks the
  card's bits.
- **Lean first for new mathematics.** A new law lands with its Lean statement, or names its
  obligation in #410.
- **Tests are written for each law.**
  - A test proves its law on the smallest fixture that exhibits it and runs well under a second in a
    debug build; no test builds a campaign's field.
  - Measurements (code curves, timings, laps) are examples run once in release and reported with their
    command and result as receipts, never as gates.
  - A check that an optimization changes no value runs once on the real case as a receipt; the suite
    keeps only its small-fixture law.
  - Cost is a property of the representation: a local, block-sparse law is implemented and certified
    block by block, never by assembling a dense global object.
- **A development read decides; the held read confirms.** A hypothesis is measured first on
  development material, the held read runs once a law, and seen material is never graded as unseen.
  Campaigns on disjoint owners run together, each with its own receipt, and each new law lands at
  its consuming owner, retiring any duplicate in the same change.

## 5. Protocol

- **Pinned families.** Before a validation, its choosing and validation families are pinned by a
  declared keyed split, with the consumer, the charges, the baselines, the acceptance and a
  deadline.
- **The evaluation partition.** The September dataset's evaluation families stay closed. F5, Athena's
  first product outcome, alone reads them, once, after every product gate has passed on development
  material.
- **Budgets.** Each run is projected before it launches (CLAUDE.md, the waiting standard): its time
  from a development read on the card, a deadline fixed at the projection's upper end, at most 12 GB
  of the card's 16 GiB, and the host part's threads declared.
