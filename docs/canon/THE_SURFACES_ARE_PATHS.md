## 4. The front is the unit, and it is Brandon's own law

> *an **arc** is one conducting channel, so what travels it is ordered; a **junction** is where current
> distributes, so what leaves it is co-present; **chronology is not seriality.***

`hardware_cover` states that a device is that law in silicon rather than an analogy for it: warp lanes
are co-present exactly while they share a path, and the hardware serialises them when they diverge.
**The many channels exist to carry combinatorial binary path distributions.**

Hence the covering discipline: a front is covered **by extent, not by count** — *"a branch tip
carrying a thousand continuations and one carrying two are not one unit each."* And placement carries
no authority: `ChartId` *"names where work was placed, and carries no semantic authority whatever"*,
while `Chart::grain` is *"the smallest cell that does not leave lanes idle"* — a conductance grain read
off the device rather than chosen.
