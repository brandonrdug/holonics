# Causal algebraic geometry

**Status.** Ordinary scheme, sheaf, chain, deformation, connection, and arithmetic facts are
`proved-standard` under the hypotheses stated here. Their use as causal-machine carriers is a
`project-postulate`; each concrete organ owes a separately graded construction.

This chapter states how the causal calculus can be carried by ordinary algebraic geometry. It does
not claim that every ecology is literally a scheme; it gives an exact chart when polynomial,
algebraic, infinitesimal, singular, or fiberwise structure is the relevant receiver.

## 1. Local algebraic charts

Choose a region site/topology and affine-open basis where the algebraic receiver actually admits
one. On that basis choose charts `X_U=Spec(A_U)` and explicitly equip
`U ↦ A_U` with contravariant presheaf restrictions `A_U -> A_V` for admitted inclusions
`V ⊆ U`, satisfying identity and composition. An arbitrary family of algebras does not acquire
these maps merely from causal-region inclusion, and an arbitrary ring diagram is not automatically
a scheme atlas. Invoke existence or uniqueness of gluing only after the chosen affine-open and
sheaf/descent hypotheses have been established.

An occurrence is not merely a point of `X_U`. It is a situated section/point together with event,
source, incidence, and lineage. Equal residue-field coordinates can therefore remain distinct
occurrences.

Receiver change is base change or another declared morphism:

\[
X_U\times_{\operatorname{Spec}R}\operatorname{Spec}S.
\]

Its comparison square and transported lineage are part of the receipt. A scalar evaluation is one
fiber/point receiver and cannot reconstruct the whole source scheme.

## 2. Incidence and algebra are coupled, not collapsed

The causal body carries both:

\[
(C_\bullet,\partial)
\quad\text{and}\quad
\mathcal O_X:U\mapsto A_U.
\]

`C_•` records caused oriented occurrence incidence; `O_X` records local algebraic functions. A
polynomial equality does not manufacture a causal edge, and incidence equality does not imply
isomorphic coordinate algebras. A chain morphism must preserve `∂`; an algebra morphism must
preserve its stated ring/module structure; a coupled event owes both when both are used.

## 3. Ports, contact, and gluing

An organ must name the category, variance, port object, and universal construction it uses. A port
may therefore be an exposed subscheme, module map, ideal/divisor datum, correspondence leg, or
typed boundary section—but these are not interchangeable. Juxtaposition and actual contact use the
named product, pullback, pushout, tensor product, correspondence composition, or open-system wiring
only after its existence and descent hypotheses have been stated.

Local successor sections glue only when their restrictions agree on overlaps and the declared
descent law supplies a gluing. A failed cocycle/coboundary condition or a nonzero Čech cohomology
class in the relevant descent problem can witness an obstruction; a cocycle by itself may instead
be valid gluing data. Pairwise agreement is insufficient when higher coherence is owed.

## 4. Events as algebraic correspondences

A situated event may be represented by a span/correspondence

\[
X_{\mathrm{before}}\xleftarrow{s}E_e\xrightarrow{t}X_{\mathrm{after}},
\]

together with the actual event occurrence, typed boundary ports, local chain map, constitutive
section, and lineage. Composition is fiber product of correspondences when defined. The event
object `E_e` retains the interior witness that a bare endpoint relation would erase.

The compact solve becomes a family of admitted correspondences selected by `(F,Q,A,L)`. It is not
implemented as one global function over serialized values.

## 5. Morphology change as deformation

Infinitesimal reusable change can be represented by a square-zero extension

\[
0\longrightarrow M\longrightarrow A'\longrightarrow A\longrightarrow0,
\qquad M^2=0.
\]

The split case is `A'=A⊕M`. Derivations satisfy
`Der_R(A,M) ≅ Hom_A(Ω_(A/R),M)`; derivations classify infinitesimal maps/splittings in the stated
setting, while general deformation and obstruction claims require the appropriate extension and
cotangent-complex data. Finite morphology change may instead replace local algebras,
modules, incidence, or gluing data through an explicit successor morphism. The representation is
selected by the domain's actual return.

Training is not “updating parameters” by definition. It is a caused change `m -> m'` whose new
algebraic/incident conduct persists and changes a later receiver. An infinitesimal model owes its
tangent and integration data; a discrete model instead owes an exact successor morphism. Both owe
obstruction and remount identity.

## 6. Singularities and founding

A singular interaction locus can be studied by localization, normalization, resolution, blow-up,
or stratification. For `X=Spec(A)` and a closed subscheme defined by ideal `I`, the blow-up is the
relative Proj of the Rees algebra

\[
\operatorname{Bl}_I X=\operatorname{Proj}\bigoplus_{n\ge0}I^n
\]

Under the standard quasi-coherent/finite-type hypotheses, its exceptional fiber is described by
`Proj(gr_I A)`, the projectivized normal cone. Only under the relevant regularity hypotheses does
this become the familiar projectivized normal bundle carrying separated normal directions. Thus a
blow-up may supply one precise chart for a “new axis”; it does not always replace a locus with new
directional data and is not the universal semantics of FOUND. The domain must prove that this
construction is the event's required receiver.

The Rees algebra records a filtration and its associated graded degeneration. It is useful for
changing grain while preserving a relation between the filtered body and its boundary face.
Again, a filtration index is not time or causality unless the event lineage supplies that order.

## 7. Phase, connection, and holonomy

On a bundle/module with connection `∇`, transport compares fibers along paths; curvature `∇²`
measures infinitesimal failure of flatness, and holonomy records finite loop return. Algebraic
connections, monodromy, étale/fundamental-group actions, and differential equations provide exact
charts for phase pathways. They become causal testimony only when paths are actual admitted event
lineages or declared receiver probes.

The Gauss `₂F₁` equation has regular singularities at `0`, `1`, and `∞`, with monodromy transporting
its local solution fibers. Confluent and broader generalized hypergeometric systems can have
irregular singularities. Their utility follows from the declared differential/path geometry, not
from a universal label for every computation.

## 8. Prime and arithmetic fibers

For integral base, reduction `A -> A⊗F_p`, localization, completion, valuation, Hensel lifting, and
Chinese remaindering expose receiver fibers at primes. Shared factors/residue phases can found
cells in an arithmetic incidence complex when caused by declared probes. A prime is not a global
semantic category; it is an arithmetic receiver axis with exact algebraic conduct.

## 9. Exact computational carriers

Use the smallest exact carrier that preserves the theorem/receiver:

- integers, rationals, finite fields, residue rings;
- polynomial rings and finitely presented quotients;
- algebraic numbers and certified root isolations;
- modules, chain complexes, sparse matrices, and exact linear solves;
- formal power series in a declared adic topology; a degree truncation is exact only for an
  `n`-jet receiver or with a remainder/factorization certificate;
- convergent analytic objects with explicit domain and remainder certificates; and
- interval/ball arithmetic as certified bounded testimony, not exact equality.

Gröbner bases, resultants, Sturm chains, SAT/SMT, proof assistants, and numerical solvers are
apparatus/algorithmic charts. None is the internal ontology or mandatory path for all ideals.

## 10. Adaptation recipe

To express a new ecology algebraic-geometrically:

1. name situated occurrences and causal incidence independently of coordinates;
2. choose local algebras/charts only where they expose the needed relations;
3. declare overlaps, restrictions, ports, and receiver family;
4. specify the local constitutive correspondence and exact predecessor;
5. stage the algebra/incidence/morphology delta with obstruction;
6. prove serial composition and any admitted interchange/base-change squares;
7. return receiver sections/residuals and physical resource testimony;
8. test stateful boundary equivalence before quotienting interiors; and
9. grade the actual later conduct after returned deformation and source-detached remount.
