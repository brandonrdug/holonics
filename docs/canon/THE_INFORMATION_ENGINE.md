## 1. One recurrence, several mathematical questions

[definition] The continuing HNN operation is

`occurrence -> local current -> reaction -> emission + successor ecology -> next occurrence`.

The next operation uses the returned body. A user message, self-emission or application result
can supply its next ordinary occurrence. Their source and contact maps differ; they do not
require separate world-verdict and inference engines.

[definition] An unknown can be a source family, coefficient, generator, factorization, operator
word, material relation or executable representation of a desired face. Solving its compatibility
equations is mathematical inference. Applying an already constructed generator is productive
use; incorporating a new observed constraint is formation. Their actual dependencies can
interleave inside one construction. State mutation by itself explains none of these computations.

[project-postulate] A mathematical result returns with the next construction it enables. A
native failure returns with the source, representation or consuming map it calls into question.
The implementation should enact that connection while research continues, rather than requiring
another human intuition to restart every useful cycle.

## 2. Information keeps its source and future receivers

[definition] A receiver face is the result of a declared map, with its comparands, reference,

[proved-derived; formal-checked] The standing future-quotient construction identifies sources

[definition] Rebase, exact future-relative compression and approximate reconstruction have

## 3. Growth and condensation are questions about the generator

[counterexample] An infinite output family can have a finite executable generator: Fibonacci
words, a Cantor construction or a correctly specified digit-producing programme supplies one.
Unbounded unfolding or fractal scale therefore does not imply absence of compact representation.
Selecting an arbitrary member still owes its branch information, and producing its requested
output still owes decoder work and numeric precision.

[definition] A finite orbit, finite-dimensional operator algebra, future-stable quotient,
analytic recurrence with a controlled tail, and resource-bounded finite expansion are distinct
ways a construction can close. A finite reflection group supplies one special closure; neither
crystallographic terminology nor finite group order is a universal condensation criterion.

[project-postulate] Resource pressure prompts a change in representation, factorization or
placement that preserves the requested consequence, or a precise obstruction. More wrappers,
a larger unexplained aperture or a cached copy of an ever-growing prefix is not by itself
generative compression. Assess the actual remaining work and retained distinctions.

[definition] The project uses friction for consequential boundary coupling at its declared
grain. Dissipative friction, wave transmission, reflection, thermal relaxation and geodesic
motion have their particular constitutive and boundary laws. A null reflected face can coexist
with transmitted current. A conserved measurement leaves a fibre of possible motions; it does
not select their dynamics on its own.

[proved-derived; formal-checked] The existing code/boundary owner composes

The lifted complex information owner retains a real code mismatch and an oriented phase
comparison. These are available mathematical relations, not an assertion that every bit equals

[definition] Thermodynamic entropy, Shannon information and physical energy can be related

discuss reversibility. Parity is defined by its involution or grading; chronology retains the
actual order; reconstruction and physical reversal keep their own domain and stability questions.

[project-postulate] The GPU owns admitted hot native conduct. CPU work includes exterior

[definition] A proof establishes its stated relation. A source inspection establishes the
inspected implementation boundary. A numerical or native experiment establishes its measured

Record those scopes separately while preserving the programme's breadth and constructive value.
