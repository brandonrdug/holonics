# Holonics beacon

[definition] This page makes useful ideas and their actual sources easy to find. Brandon's
September 13 clarification uses “publication” in this sense. It does not order a manuscript
or submission campaign. The framework's [current position](../CONSTRUCTION_STATE.md) and
[roadmap](plans/THE_ROADMAP.md) retain their own roles.

- **Situated difference and causal relevance.** Values are receiver faces of a fuller
  relation. The [philosophical account](canon/THE_REALITY_OF_DIFFERENCE.md),
  [formal framework](FORMAL_FRAMEWORK.md) and
  [relevance construction](canon/THE_RELEVANCE_HYPOTHESIS.md) connect the intuition to maps,
  invariance and separating future observations.
- **Compression that keeps its continuation.** Generator transport, joint receivers and
  retained fibres determine what may be represented compactly. The
  [source/native synthesis](MATHEMATICS_AND_NATIVE_CONDUCT.md) and
  [four-torus current experiment](../research/experiments/four_torus_current/) supply
  concrete mathematical and executable instances.
- **Geometry, fields and matter.** The [mass/current guide](MASS_ENERGY_AND_CAUSAL_TRANSPORT.md)
  connects Maxwell, gravity, quantum transport, compactification and constitutive mechanics
  through their actual equations. Its source domains and proof/measurement scopes are explicit.
- **Generation, solving and navigation.** [Hephaestus Automata](HEPHAESTUS_AUTOMATA.md) and
  [HNN composition](HNN_COMPOSITION.md) describe uses of the common architecture.
  The [oriented-navigation correction](../research/records/2026-09-13_ORIENTED_CONFIGURATION_NAVIGATION_RETAINS_ITS_SOURCE_AND_THE_BEACON_STAYS_OPEN.md)
  returns from finite cube search to changing joints, Swing transport, constraints and
  source-conditioned variation.
- **What is ours and where it meets other work.** The
  [contribution and prior-art map](../research/records/2026-09-13_PUBLICATION_POSITIONING_DISTINGUISHES_SOURCE_CONSTRUCTION_AND_PRIORITY.md)
  identifies project artifacts, established sources and useful comparisons. Its candidate
  list helps readers find the strongest ideas; it is not a prerequisite packaging process.

[definition] [Research records](../research/records/) preserve derivations and their
corrections. [Formal source](../formal/) and [implementation owners](ARCHITECTURE_MAP.md)
make the constructions inspectable. [Existing manuscripts](../research/papers/source/README.md)
are optional reading surfaces. A known scope or an unfinished extension accompanies its
source without making the useful result wait for a grander claim.
