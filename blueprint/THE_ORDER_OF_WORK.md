**The defect.** `body/live_machine.hpp:150-166` admits **seven of the eight** transition invariants

invariant that cannot be made false is not being checked.

**Why first.** It is small, it is in the live spine, and every later movement's receipts are graded
through this crossing. Grading later work on a constant would propagate the defect.
