## 0. Verdict

[definition] The world return has three seams and none of them is a codec. A **mouth** founds
carriers from a population and a reference relation. A **face** is what a world returns about one
emission: admitted or reflected, the carriers it carried, and a diagnostic as octets. An
**emission** is the machine's own emission section, rendered on the application's side of the
ABI. The junction counts emissions incident and admitted; the deposit rides the crossed route
between its two ends; termination is the world-tube terminal, matched or open. Everything that
names Lean, Rust, prose, arithmetic, PNG, PCM, JSON, a kernel, a compiler, a tactic, or a tower
is an **application**: it reads its own files as octets, hands data across the ABI, and returns
faces. The interior never references it.

[definition] The 2026-09-02 WRD campaign proved the laws and measured real faces, and bound the
mouth, the emitter, the face adapter, and the driver to one language. This plan keeps the laws,
keeps the measured faces as data, removes the binding, and removes the class it belonged to.

## 2. The jurisdiction, restated for this plan

[definition] `canon/THE_RECOVERED_LAW.md` §1: worlds may contain compilers and provers; observers
may use parsers. Brandon's ruling of 2026-09-02 fixes where the line falls for construction: the
world and the observer are **applications on the far side of the circulation ABI**. Inside the ABI
there is no language, format, modality, field, kernel, or tower name, no renderer shaped by any of
them, and no hand-wired call to any of them. An application's files are octets. What an
application hands in is data (§3.1). What it hands back is faces (§3.2). What it receives is the
emission section (§3.3). The interior derives the returned interaction; an application never
types it.

## 3. The three seams, stated without a codec

### 3.1 The mouth

[definition] `found_lattice(population, references, receiver) -> (NativeTransportScaffold,
LatticeWitness)` in `native_intelligence` (or `holonic_engine::native_spool`), the body of the
retracted `found_lean_lattice` with its language removed:

- **population**: an ordered family of carrier identities, each an octet string the application
  chose; the interior never reads inside it;
- **references**: pairs `(referenced, referring)` over that family; the referring member is the
  generator; participating members are states; each pair is an occurrence in the given order; a
  cell current is the exact departure/arrival count; one thread per generator from each premise
  into it; observation is an index the application supplies per pair, opaque to the interior;
- **witness**: the identity/state, pair/occurrence, and generator maps beside the body, cold, the
  only place an identity is read back.

[definition] Two grades of mouth, and the witness records which one founded a body. The mouth
of record takes octets and recovers the incidence itself: `exposure_codec::recover` and the
recovered incidence of `material_incidence` give the units and their co-occurrence at every
scale, and that population and relation enter `found_lattice`. A declared mouth takes a
population and reference pairs an application already read out of its own files; it is
admissible as data, and the witness marks the relation as the application's testimony, because
the application's parser chose it. The 2026-08-17 supersession is the reason for the order: the
codec is recovered, not declared.

[definition] The standing mouths are instances of the same seam and are not changed: the
dialogue spool (messages and causal-depth parity), the raster and acoustic source incidence
(components and frames), the foreign excitation lift (tower sections, modality kept as cold
testimony and absent from the hot excitation), and the modality-erasing
`ExteriorOccurrenceTransducer` of `membrane_transport.rs`, which turns any exterior object into
an `ExteriorOccurrenceFibre` with its complete source fibre retained. `found_lattice` composes
that transducer for its population rather than reading an application's objects itself. What is
missing and is scheduled (§6) is the wire from recovered octet incidence to `found_lattice`:
today no owner founds a native scaffold from recovered octet faces (measured 2026-09-02 by
`grep -rn "ExposureRecovery\|MaterialAtlas\|FaceQuotient" soma/life/src/native_intelligence
crates/holonic-engine/src/holonic_intelligence crates/holonic-engine/src/native_spool`, no
matches). `ExteriorModality` stays cold testimony; it names four modalities and is not a wall.

### 3.2 The face

[definition] `WorldFace { emission: usize, admitted: bool, carried: BTreeSet<NativeStateId>,
diagnostic: Vec<u8> }`, exactly one per emission the interior emitted, indexed by the emitted
grain. An application's own variants of one emission collapse to that one face; the incident
population is therefore what the interior emitted and never what the application multiplied.
The application maps its own carriers back to states through the witness it holds; the interior
never sees the carriers' names. The diagnostic is octets the interior stores and never reads to
select anything; re-entry (§5) is founded from `carried` alone. For a sensory world the face is
founded by the interior's own membrane: an emission's projection went out, a distinct caused
occurrence came back through `ExteriorOccurrenceTransducer`, and `MembraneConsequence::{Returned,
Insufficient}` is `admitted` or not. For a symbolic world the application returns `admitted`.
Both are the same face. The circulation ABI's `Return` carries `faces: Vec<WorldFace>` and no
longer carries a `ReturnedScaffoldInteraction`; the interior derives the interaction (§4) and
returns the staged candidate as before. No backwards compatibility: the old `Return` is removed.

### 3.3 The emission

[definition] The emission that crosses out is the machine's own `NativeEmissionSection` (entering
occurrence, receiver, ordered word, plural futures, emitted future) or the variable-grain
emission, rendered by `ExteriorEmissionCodec` (binary or UTF-8 JSON) at the ABI. The application
reads it however its world requires. Whatever an application does with it (writes a file, calls a
kernel, renders pixels, plays samples, compiles, tests, shows a person) is testimony about the
application, never a capability of Athena, and is graded only by the faces it returns.

[definition] The generation mechanism of record stands: integration by reflection with
termination by impedance matching (`THE_EROS_INSTANTIATION.md` §4b). This plan adds no emitter.
The deposit conducts later current one way rather than another (§4); the mouth grain is what the
application's data founded; the return edge is the face; the termination law is §5.

### 3.4 The application

[definition] An application is a process on the far side of the ABI. It owns its files, its
parser, its kernel, its compiler, its renderer, its microphone, its screen, and its person. It
reads its files as octets, hands `(population, references)` through `Found`, receives emissions,
puts them to its world, and hands back faces. It may keep a witness from its identities to the
states the interior returned. It lives outside `soma/life`, `holonic-engine`,
`holonic-structure`, and `mount`, in an `applications/` workspace member per world. The Lean
reader and kernel caller, the Rust/prose/arithmetic atlases, and the engine's development reader
become such applications or are deleted; none keeps a path into the interior.

## 4. The junction and the deposit, unchanged

[definition] From one conducted boundary and the faces of its emissions: `R` emissions incident,
`M` admitted; `CountedCrossing::meet(R, M)`; matched returns current `(1, 0)` and storage `1`;
partial returns `(T, Γ)` and storage `1 / service rounds`; the contact support is the union of
the carried states of admitted faces, joined with the route's two ends by the existing deposit
law; nothing admitted is a terminus with no deposit. The result enters `stage_return` and
`commit` unchanged. This is `route_return::derive_world_return` with its face type replaced by
§3.2, its witness parameter removed (the faces already carry states), and its diagnostic reading
removed. `R` and `M` are measured crossings of the interior's own emissions, the junction law of
`holonic-structure` and the Lean owner; they are not an authored threshold, and §3.2 is what
keeps an application from inflating them.

## 5. Termination, one law

[definition] The junction's arms are the world-tube terminal's arms: a match is
`DynamicallyCondensedRest`; a partial reflection or a terminus is `OpenLaterFront`, which the
standing owner already refuses to close by a turn limit or a host timeout. `route_return` reads
its termination through `NativeOpenWorldTubeTerminal` rather than carrying a second enum.
Re-entry after reflection is at the smallest occurrence whose generator is a carrier that
reflected, when the lattice has one; otherwise the front stays open.
