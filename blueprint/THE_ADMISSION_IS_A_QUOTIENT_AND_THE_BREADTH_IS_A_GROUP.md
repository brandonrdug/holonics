## 3. Movement two — repetition against breadth, on real holonomies

**Composition of standing owners.** Take the holonomies of a **family** of walks on real material,
close them with `StructureGroup::close`, and compare against closing the holonomies of **one** walk
repeated.

The theorem being exhibited, `proved-standard`:

> Repeating a loop `n` times returns `hol(γ)^n`, so **repetition explores at most the cyclic subgroup
> generated by that one element and saturates at its order. Breadth generates the group, and the gap
> is exactly the non-commutativity.**

**Outcome expectation.** `⟨hol(γ)⟩` cyclic and saturating; the family strictly larger **iff** the
connection is non-abelian.

**Falsifier, already half-built.** On an abelian connection the two must **coincide** —
`structure_group.rs`'s `curvature_commutator` carries exactly that control, and its doc names the
defect it prevents. A run reporting breadth as a gain on an abelian connection has measured itself.

**And the bound to respect**, `H.0473`'s own: bracket generation gives **reachability**, not a cost, a
geodesic, or a schedule. This movement explains why more traversal patterns exist and are reachable.
**It does not explain why they are faster**, and no report may claim it does.
