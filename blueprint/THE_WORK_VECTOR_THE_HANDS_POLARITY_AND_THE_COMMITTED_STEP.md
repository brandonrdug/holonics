## 0. Why this plan exists, and the two returns that ordered it

**An external adjudication refuted five claims that were about to be deposited as doctrine**, and the
fifth is the one that matters here:

> Promoting a timeout into a cost law and using its fitted clock projection to select the aperture was
> the breach. The honest return was `Open: exceeded this apparatus-time aperture; work unknown`, not
> *"3.96 hours, therefore refused."*

`CLAUDE.md` states the law it broke: **a cost is measured in work, and a clock may measure but may
never select.** The driver
`crates/holonic-engine/examples/the_head_reads_the_aim_and_the_census_is_four_classes.rs` authored its
sweep ceiling, fitted an exponent from elapsed `f64` seconds, and printed refusal prose for an extent
it simply never called. Measured 2026-08-17 by `grep -n "REFUSED\|Err(\|elapsed"
crates/holonic-engine/examples/the_head_reads_the_aim_and_the_census_is_four_classes.rs`: the word
appears only inside a `println!`, and the only typed returns in the sweep are the successful
`pullback_inertia_bound` calls — **there is no typed refusal and no work threshold anywhere in it.**
**The aperture is still an authored number**, which is the very defect the fit claimed to remove.

And the fit was weaker than reported: averaging adjacent logarithmic slopes over doubling extents
telescopes to `(log₂ t₆₄ − log₂ t₈)/3`, so the `k = 16` and `k = 32` measurements **do not affect the
fitted exponent at all.** It is a two-endpoint extrapolation, and it did not isolate elimination
anyway, because `pullback_inertia_bound` performs matrix products, rational row reduction and two
inertia computations in the same interval.

**A concept-entry sweep separately returned the finger-trap verdict on what was going to be built.**
`crates/holonic-engine/src/leader_quadrature.rs::integrate_by_leaders` already composes two link
species with different determination laws — `ExtensionKind::{Found, Ride}`, where `Found` re-reads the
material per grain and `Ride` is licensed only by a run of exact agreement and then extrapolates one
jet across the whole reach with no landing check, its error legible at the next tip, where the
residual revokes the licence and refounds the axis. **So the two-species chain is a lift, not a build**,
and this plan carries only the narrow part that is genuinely absent.

## 0a. WHAT THIS ENABLES FOR EROS

**Eros can already change its own terrain and read what a later current does with it.** Measured
2026-08-17: a deposit moved a later arrival's band from PASS to STOP, with a withdrawal control
returning the complex bit-identical to base. What it cannot do is **price a move before taking it**, or
**commit to a multi-step move and be lawfully refounded when the commitment fails.** Those two are the
whole difference between navigating and wandering, and this plan is both of them.

### The work vector is the predictive question, made answerable

Brandon: *"'Is this solvable, and at what cost' is the predictive question asked on microscopic local
scales."* `canon/TABLET_THE_CHART.md` §3.7 already records that the machine cannot ask it:

> **Owed:** the machine currently has no organ that returns *"is this solvable, and at what cost"* as a
> typed answer… the dominating quantity is intermediate entry bit-length and nothing counts it.
> **Until a work vector exists, no cost question in this repository has a lawful answer.**

Four things become possible the moment it does, and none of them is available now:

1. **Cost-aware routing with no governor.** `ExactOrdering` is four-state and its `Open` **retains both
   carriers** rather than tie-breaking, so a route may be admitted on dominated work while an
   incomparable pair stays plural. Eros gets to be cheap without acquiring a chooser, which is the
   thing every scalar cost model gives up.
2. **Apertures derived rather than authored.** `canon/THE_AUTHORED_LEVEL.md` requires that an aperture
   state what would derive its level from the material. A measured width plus a declared metric derives
   one. Every pin that exists because a run *had to stop somewhere* becomes a computation.
3. **The FOUND/RIDE asymmetry becomes measurable rather than asserted.** *"FOUND pays curvature; RIDE
   is cheap because the terrain already paid"* is a claim about two costs, and **neither cost has ever
   been measured.** With a work vector, `work(FOUND)` and `work(RIDE)` can be taken on one material and
   compared. **That converts Localized P=NP from a slogan into an experiment** — §3.7's own statement is
   that *on standing terrain, finding costs what verifying costs*, which is an equality nobody has been
   able to test.
4. **A refusal becomes typed.** `Open: work exceeds the declared metric` is a return. Prose about
   elapsed time is not.

### The committed step is the navigation organ

A body that navigates must be able to **commit** — to take several steps on the strength of a run of
agreement rather than re-reading the material at every grain — and to be **refounded** when the
commitment lands wrong. That is exactly what `leader_quadrature` does and it is trapped inside one
integrator. Expressing it as a `Relating` link puts the whole chain machinery on it: composition,
cocycle defect, holonomy, retained remainder, rebase detection. Measured 2026-08-17 by
`grep -rn "impl Relating for" --include='*.rs' crates soma` → **3**, and they are `Crossing`,
`PhasedLink`, and one test-only `Step`. Neither `Found` nor `Ride` is among them.

It also supplies the repair carrier for a **convicted** owner: `lean_development.rs` records that
`name_elaboration` reads the same global recruitment map at every depth so its jet never moves —
*dead reckoning*, which `soma/body/src/manifold.rs`'s ratified W9 living-boundary law rules out — and
`blueprint/THE_ROADMAP.md` carries it open under the accumulation cut.

### And what it does not enable

It does not make Eros faster. Pricing a route is not taking a cheaper one, and a machine that can
refuse a deed on work has not thereby done the deed. The gain is that **its refusals and its
commitments become returns instead of accidents**, which is the precondition for either being studied.

## STATION ONE — the work vector (the organ, and it is a lift)

**What.** The exact organs count their own work and return it beside the result. Sol's specification,
adopted verbatim as the field list: per phase — rational additions, multiplications, divisions and
normalisations; entries written; **cumulative and peak numerator and denominator bit-length**;
allocation or resident extent; dependency span.

**Why it is a lift and not a build.** `cuda_aperture::CarrierWork` already exists with exactly the
missing field:

```rust
pub struct CarrierWork {
    pub cpu_evaluations: BigUint,
    pub device_evaluations: BigUint,
    pub transfer_bytes: BigUint,
    /// Widest intermediate this carrier had to represent exactly.
    pub intermediate_bits: BigUint,
}
```

and `CarrierWork::order_against` already returns the four-state `ExactOrdering` with `Open` retaining
both carriers. **The type is built, the ordering is built, and the field the chart tablet says nothing
counts is declared.** What is absent is that it is populated **only from a device aperture receipt**:
`CarrierWork::of_candidate` reads a `CudaApertureReceipt` and nothing reads an elimination.

Measured 2026-08-17: `grep -rn "\.bits()" --include='*.rs' crates/holonic-engine/src/inertia.rs
crates/holonic-engine/src/exact_linear.rs` → **0**, against **17** across all library `src`. The two
modules whose intermediate bit-length dominates every exact deed never take it.

**Placement.** `inertia`'s elimination and `exact_linear`'s multiply and inverse. `InertiaSchedule`
already carries the pivot steps and is the right place to hang the count — and it is already recorded
that *the pivot count is provably not the dominating quantity*, so this fills the gap the schedule
itself names.

**Outcome.** A `Work` return beside every inertia and exact-matrix deed, and one driver that takes it
across a declared extent sweep and admits or defers **from the vector under a declared receiver
metric**. Elapsed time appears in the receipt as a measurement carrying its frame and never as a
selector. `the_head_reads_the_aim_and_the_census_is_four_classes` is rewritten against it, and its
refusal becomes typed.

**Expectation.** The peak bit-length grows near `k · b` with `b` the input entry width, so the total
work grows near `k⁴b`. **That is a prediction and it may be refuted** — Sol notes the timing did not
isolate elimination, so the measured operation counts may show the row reduction or the matrix products
dominating instead, which would be a better finding than the confirmation.

**Falsifier, and there are two.** If the counted work vector orders two extents the same way the clock
did, the clock was not lying and the repair is bookkeeping — say so rather than presenting the
agreement as evidence. And the anti-vacuity arm: **the metric must be able to admit.** A declared
metric that defers every extent has refused rather than measured, so the sweep must exhibit at least
one extent admitted and one deferred under one metric.

**Refused.** No wall-clock quantity may enter the admission. No authored ceiling: the sweep's extents
are declared by the caller and reported as a declaration, and the *stopping* is decided by the work
vector against the metric.

## STATION TWO — the hand's polarity (an audit, not a repair)

**What.** `holonic_structure::Hand` is shared, and its own documentation says `Ortho` is *"cohere-null
with the cross maximal — the pure orthogonal turn, the founding hand."* `traversible_chain::Crossing::meet`
assigns `Hand::Ortho` to the junction where **nothing returns at all** — `Γ = 0`, the matched junction.
Those read as opposite conditions, and a concept sweep this session took them for the same object and
built a false identification on it.

**Why this is an audit and not a repair.** There is a reading under which they agree, and it is not
obviously wrong: `arrow.rs` types `aim` as *what STANDS* and `cross` as *what FLOWS*, and at a junction
`Γ` is what came back and `τ` is what crossed — so `Γ = 0` is *stored face null, transported face
maximal*, which is `Ortho` by `arrow.rs`'s own definition. **Deciding this in a document would be
exactly the error that produced it.** The station is to measure, not to conclude.

**Outcome.** Every `Hand`-producing site enumerated, with the quantity whose sign it reads named, and
a verdict: either the shared quantity is exhibited and `Hand`'s documentation carries it, or the sites
read different faces and the type splits.

**Expectation.** The three implementors are `Crossing`, `PhasedLink` and a test-only `Step`. I expect
the stored-face reading to hold for `Crossing` and to be *undocumented*, which is a documentation
repair rather than a type split.

**Falsifier.** If any implementor's `Ortho` is neither *stored-face null* nor *cross maximal*, the type
is overloaded and must split — and a split is the honest outcome, not a failure of the station.

## STATION FOUR — `Found` and `Ride` as `Relating` links (the lift)

**What.** Express the two determination laws as a `Relating` implementor so `Chain::compose`,
`defect_against`, `holonomy` and `remainder` can see them. A `Found` link carries its per-grain
residual; a `Ride` link carries its **committed span** and the run of agreement that licensed it.

**Why the composition is the point.** Today the two species live inside one bespoke integrator and
nothing else can compose them. As links, the cocycle defect answers a question nothing can ask now:
**what separates going through from going straight, when some of the steps were committed
ballistically?** That is a determination-law mismatch rather than a transport mismatch, and it is
invisible to every existing chain.

**Outcome.** A link enum with both species, its `Composes` remainder being the residual a ride
committed past, and a driver composing a mixed chain whose holonomy is read. `derivation_integral.rs`
is the precedent — it already runs `integrate_by_leaders` on both routes of a compared pair.

**Expectation.** A chain of pure `Found` links has an identically zero committed remainder — **and that
receipt is a definition, not evidence**, exactly as the unphased chain's identity holonomy was. The
evidence is a mixed chain whose remainder is non-zero and whose refounding events are named.

**Falsifier.** If a mixed chain's holonomy equals a pure-`Found` chain's on the same material, the ride
committed nothing the material did not already determine, and the two species are one species on that
material — which is a real return about the material and must be reported as one.

**Bound.** This station does **not** wire a prediction into a routing decision.
`crates/holonic-engine/src/approach_front.rs` holds the predicted-crossing signal and refuses to route
on it in writing — *"it reports; it never routes. If it ever reaches a `min`, `sort` or `argmax` that
discards a member it has become a governor."* Whether a predicted crossing may determine a transport
without becoming a governor is a **policy question for Brandon**, not a construction, and this plan
does not answer it.

## What is refused throughout

- **No clock in an admission.** Elapsed time is a measurement carrying its frame; it never selects.
- **No authored ceiling presented as an aperture.** A declared extent is declared out loud; a *stop* is
  derived from the work vector against a declared metric.
- **No identification between the junction law and a pair of directions.** Sol refuted it: impedance
  matching requires conjugate effort/flow variables, incident and reflected travelling modes at a port,
  an admittance on each side and phase-dependent interference, and a dot product supplies none of them.
- **No `T + Γ² = 1` cited as evidence**, here or anywhere. `traversible_chain` records that it is an
  algebraic identity for its convention, and identifying it with Lagrange's identity would be joining
  two tautologies.
- **No scalar cost.** The work vector is a vector and its ordering is four-state.

## What this plan does not claim

It does not claim that pricing a route makes Eros faster, or that a measured `k⁴b` law says anything
about any other organ's cost. It does not claim the FOUND/RIDE asymmetry holds — it makes the question
askable, and the answer may be that the two costs do not meet on any material this body can build.

It does not revisit the five refuted claims. Focus is not impedance matching; evolution's relation to
`P` versus `NP` is that no uniform decision problem has been exhibited; normalisation preserves relative
shape and destroys only an independently calibrated absolute coordinate; and *"you forget what nothing
retains"* is a definition until an intervention separates a named retained population from redundant
standing elsewhere.
