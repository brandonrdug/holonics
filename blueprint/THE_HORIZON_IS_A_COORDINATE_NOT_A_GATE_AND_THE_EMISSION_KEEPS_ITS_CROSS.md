## 2. The defect, mechanically

### 2.1 The deciding filter selects for verbatim continuation by construction

`soma/life/src/causal_language.rs:1638`:

```rust
by_token.retain(|_, (horizon, _)| *horizon == greatest_horizon);
```

`greatest_horizon` is the deepest **productive** matched context (`:1605`, the accessor whose absence
of a caller silenced whole sources until 2026-08-17). A deeper matched context is a longer matching
corpus span. So keeping only the branches at the greatest horizon keeps, at every step, exactly the
continuations attested by the longest verbatim run — and discards the shorter-horizon population,
which is the divergent half.

The accounting was repaired 2026-08-17: `Continuation::withheld_by_horizon` (`:1969`) now carries the
set-aside population out, and `:1631` states it — *"THE DIVISION, TAKEN RATHER THAN THE QUOTIENT
KEPT. Everything at a shorter matched horizon is set aside and carried out; nothing is dropped
here."* **The behaviour did not change.** The kept half is what branches (`:1499`), so the filter
still decides, and what it decides for is the longest inherited span.

That is the mechanism behind an emitted answer that reads as two corpus spans joined at a branch
point. It is not a limit of the machine; it is a gate on one path, and it is the wrong shape of
object to be deciding — a comparison of two horizons is a magnitude face, and it is being asked to do
what a division should do.

## 4. The four stations

### Station one — the horizon becomes a coordinate the ratio carries

Retire the greatest-horizon `retain` as the thing that decides what branches. In its place, read the
whole continuation population — every branch at every productive horizon — as a `RatioFamily` under a
**declared receiver**, with each member's `SymbolicSurprisal` formed from the material's own
`SuffixBranchSupport::recurrence_multiplicity` (`soma/life/src/suffix_ecology.rs`, the support carried
beside `matched_length`) as `p_i = m_i / Σm`. Every ratio is returned. None is crowned. The horizon
travels as a coordinate on the member rather than as a gate ahead of it.

Frequency here is `Π`, the lived construction — counted and reported, never permitted to decide.
`SymbolicSurprisal::of_probability` (`crates/holonic-engine/src/surprisal.rs:161`) refuses `p = 0`
rather than smoothing it, so an unsupported branch FOUNDs instead of taking a constant.

**Outcome.** A continuation population returned as an exact ratio family with `cocycle_holds` true,
the horizon carried per member, and the section modulus of the same population read beside it.

**Expectation.** The cocycle holds exactly over every triple, over `ℚ`, with no float and no root
taken outside `ℚ`. The emitted family under the ratio reading is **strictly larger** than under the
horizon gate, and the added members are exactly the shorter-horizon population that
`withheld_by_horizon` has been carrying unread.

**Falsifier, and it is the one that matters.** Run both readings on the same material and measure
`novel_contiguous_surface` on each. **If the horizon gate's outputs are not more often contiguous
spans of their sources than the ratio reading's, the interpretation in this document's truth-status
line is refuted** and the filter was not the mechanism producing verbatim output. That result is a
real return about the emission and must be reported as one rather than repaired around.

**Second falsifier, against vacuity.** If the section modulus's `second_moment` vanishes against a
nonzero `extreme_fibre`, the population has collapsed onto a single fibre and the ratio family is
carrying nothing — the reading says so rather than presenting a plurality that is not there.

**Outcome.** Contacts on real material returned as whole arrows, with the ortho population named and
