
## 1. Vocabulary firewall

The following definitions are binding in every Phoenix plan, source file, artifact, commit, and
report.

### Lift

**Lift** is the recovery of typed transport functions from inherited material into a native holonic
atlas, with explicit cross-chart maps and a returned defect.

- A tensor inventory, architecture configuration, spectrum, heatmap, static visualization, or
  serialized copy is not a lift.
- A **potential lift** returns transport available in the inherited map but not yet shown active.
- An **active lift** stimulates that transport with caused material and returns local current,
  successor, residual, and lineage testimony.

### Dissection

**Dissection** is intervention-backed attribution of transport functions and their receiver-relative
taxonomy.

- Module names, layer/head labels, attention coefficients, activation magnitudes, singular vectors,
  static correlations, and clustering are diagnostics.
- A taxon stands only when a matched remove/replace/permute/rebase/isolate intervention moves the
  declared consequence while the predecessor remains fixed.
- Source and native taxa are separate populations related only through explicit chart maps.

### Compression and condensation

**Compression** is receiver-relative factorization with a complete decoder or reopening rule.
**Familywise transport condensation** compares source and candidate histories through explicit
cross-chart maps. It is receiver-exact only where every declared future consequence factors.

- Byte reduction, dtype conversion, quantization, pruning, sparsity, low rank, and serialization are
  possible apparatus consequences; none is compression by itself.
- Every approximate or bounded condensation carries the cross-chart defect, collapsed population,
  reconstruction fibre, and shortest separating histories.
- **A population the declared material never excited is `unexcited`, never `condensed`.** The two
  are separate classes of the coverage ledger and they are reported as two numbers whose sum is the
  only quotable one; an unexcited population's bytes stay inside the reconstruction fibre and inside
  the artifact-plus-decoder total. A collapse whose classes are separated only by which material the
  operator chose is a statement about that choice and not about the map — the authored-partition
  defect `CLAUDE.md` convicts, wearing a passing result.
- Exceptional compression means receiver-minimal native morphology: every retained axis has a
  separating consequence and removing any retained relation moves the declared family. No numeric
  compression-factor threshold is authored in advance. The words *exceptional*, *hyper-efficient*,
  and *substantial* are lawful only beside a strict measured reduction in the complete before/after
  vector: artifact plus decoder bytes, semantic work, peak residency, transfer, and separately
  reported physical telemetry. Receiver-minimality without that reduction is condensation, not
  exceptional compression.

### Cultivation

**Cultivation** is a returned passage which changes reusable morphology, survives source-detached
rest/remount, changes held-out later conduct, and loses that conduct under attributable ablation.

- Loss traces, counters, gradients, generated atlases, and changed bytes are testimony only.
- A gradient requires a receiver differential, retained forward lineage, and a declared metric.
- Non-smooth seams return plural adjoints, generalized derivatives, obstruction, or refusal.

### Executable and inference-ready

**Executable** means a declared runtime enacts the native transport. **Inference-ready** means a
fresh process mounts only the emitted model artifacts, accepts declared material, performs the hot
deed on its admitted apparatus, and returns the requested exterior artifact/trajectory.

- A file which parses or loads is not executable evidence.
- A safetensors or GGUF file is a container, never the model's constitutive law.
- One successful output is not inference readiness.
- The foreign weights, developmental corpus, driver fixtures, and a CPU semantic implementation
  must be unavailable during the source-detached grade.

## 2. Architecture-neutral source admission

Every foreign source enters as material through one generic contract:

```text
ForeignModelOccurrence {
    source lineage and legal/provenance declaration,
    container species and decoder version,
    codeword/tensor/graph population,
    typed input and output ports,
    architecture/layout metadata as exterior lineage,
    tokenizer/processor/codec materials,
    quantization and omitted-information fibres,
    declared apparatus boundary,
    unread and refused populations
}
```

The source manifest distinguishes four value species:

1. exact values stored by the source;
2. exact values of a quantized operator as encoded;
3. bounded or interval-valued reconstructions;
4. unknown or unread material.

A source manifest may enumerate tensors or codeword regions which the standing mouth cannot yet
decode. Enumeration is not admission. Every source population is entered in a coverage ledger as
exactly one of:

```text
lifted exactly
lifted boundedly
potential-only
stimulated actively
unexcited
unread/refused.
```

Whole-model language is lawful only when the complete body declared by the source manifest is
accounted for. A bounded population is reported as a partial lift and cannot inherit the source
model's name without that qualifier.

Dequantizing a GGUF `Q2`, `Q3`, `IQ2`, or another packed code does not reconstruct unavailable
full-precision weights. Phoenix may lift the exact quantized operator and its scale/group law while
retaining the full-precision preimage as an unresolved fibre. A format with unknown packing,
scale/zero-point domain, tensor boundary, or omitted region refuses before any transport claim.

The initial source family includes safetensors and later GGUF. Audio, image, video, multimodal, SSM,
MoE, and diffusion metadata remain port/layout lineage. No `LanguageModel`, `VisionModel`,
`DiffusionModel`, `Transformer`, `Mamba`, or modality-specific semantic owner is introduced.

## 4. Construction stations

The stations are ordered by dependency. A later station cannot repair, reinterpret, or waive a
missing earlier return.

### The source is admitted as material

Produce the foreign source manifest. Stream rather than load where source size requires it. Decode
stored values exactly at their own codeword grain. Return every unread or excluded region and the
complete source-coverage ledger. A manifest entry which the mouth cannot read remains manifested
but unread; it is never promoted by association with adjacent decoded material.

**Falsifiers:** malformed dtype/packing is accepted; a path enters identity; quantized values are
reported as original full precision; a source name selects an internal law; peak residency is
unreported.

### Potential transport is lifted

Recover local transport candidates from source matrices, graphs, state equations, convolutional
kernels, normalization/rebase maps, routing/gating maps, codec projections, and diffusion
chronologies. Architecture metadata locates material and never supplies the taxon.

**Falsifier:** the return is an inventory, statistic, or source-label table without predecessor,
successor, constitutive law, receiver, boundary, and unresolved fibre.

### Active pathways are stimulated

Present caused material and enact the relevant source mathematics, streamed by circuit/section when
the complete source cannot be resident. Return the actual active path, not an embedding Gram probe,
one matrix product, or one terminal output. Language-like sources require contextual normalization,
relative chronology, contact, value transport, residual standing, and plural continuation. A
source exposing joint multi-token prediction additionally returns future offsets, branch ancestry,
joint compatibility/interactions, commit/rebase relations, and cross-offset receiver consequences.
Independently repeated one-step alternatives are not MTP. Diffusion-like sources require the
complete admitted corruption/refinement trajectory, seed/current lineage, standing at each step,
and boundary commit; several final samples are not a trajectory. SSM-like sources require state
and observation passages.

The strongest lawful resident surface owns the hot deed. CPU apparatus may mount, persist, and
audit; it may not replay source semantics after device refusal.

**Falsifiers:** active and potential atlases are identical by construction; no caused current
crosses; the device refuses and CPU reproduces the answer; the path omits a load-bearing
normalization/chronology/return; one output substitutes for the trajectory.

### Transport functions are dissected by intervention

Build source and native taxonomies separately. Use matched removal, replacement, permutation,
rebase, isolation, scale, and port-order interventions. Feed complete observations and successor
histories to the receiver-exact quotient. Every admitted taxon exhibits at least one separating
intervention and at least two members where plurality is claimed.

Taxonomy remains approximate/open outside the receiver and intervention family. A name such as
attention, head, layer, expert, token, denoiser, or SSM is lineage until the intervention family
returns a stable conduct class.

**Falsifiers:** a taxon is predicted by source name; removing its alleged owner changes nothing;
the same output is its only shared receiver; the null/shuffle orbit is trivial; one class contains
everything or every item is a singleton without the anti-vacuity arm firing.

### The native ecology is founded

Rotate recovered transports into the existing incidence, atlas/gluing, diffusion, sheaf, phase,
chain, compression, and rest owners. Derive local axes, state dimension, lattice height, branches,
cycles, phase bands, and higher cells from material and receiver consequence.

Composition is attempted through those owners first. A new contextual carrier, cross-chart mapper,
trainer, gradient engine, taxonomy governor, or semantic runtime is unlawful unless the attempt
returns a concrete absent type, port, constitutive relation, or consequence. Exterior application
and realization code may orchestrate addressed forms but owns no Phoenix ontology.

Choose morphology by what the material returns:

```text

finite sufficient state         -> state-space realization

self-similar growing family      -> generator + scale action
sparse local transport          -> incidence/atlas realization
unresolved plurality            -> retained reconstruction fibre
```

No caller-selected latent width, LoRA rank, layer count, horizon, expert count, token limit, or
semantic capacity enters the native law.

**Falsifiers:** tensor shape becomes topology; a driver allocates the semantic dimension; a finite
stack samples a growing generator and calls itself complete; a quotient drops the reopening fibre;
the native ecology cannot recombine lifted parts into a transport the source did not enact.

### Transport families condense

### The lifted body is cultivated

**Falsifiers:** safetensors is presented without an executable law; a standard runtime is claimed

Phoenix success requires a held-out source-independent native composition, caused by at least two

## 5. Apparatus and work law

- The GPU owns active map stimulation, native hot transport, condensation fronts, and cultivation
  where an exact device path is admitted.
- CPU apparatus owns process boundaries, mmap/file I/O, narrow exterior codecs, durable rest, and
  independent audits. There is no semantic fallback.
- Exact semantic receipts are separate from timing, memory, power, and thermal telemetry.
- Every station predicts/counts exact work before expensive execution and returns typed refusal.
- **An exact resident carrier has a finite width and a chained source tower will exhaust it.**
  Measured 2026-08-18 on the first instance by
  `grep -n "CARRIER_OCTAVES\|fn needed_octaves" -A 8 crates/holonic-engine/src/embedding_fiber.rs`:
  the resident exact carrier is `128` octaves wide and each chained multiply-accumulate over width
  `d` costs `2*entry + ceil(log2 d) + 1`, so two chained `2560`-wide products fit and the third
  refuses. Every source deeper than two chained products therefore owes a **declared grain with a
  retained exact residual** at each boundary — `value = grain + residual` holding exactly, which is
  a quotient carrying its complete reconstruction fibre and not a rounding. The grain is read off a
  receiver that stopped separating; it is never authored, and no grain, ladder, or floor may appear
  in source or driver code before that receiver has failed to separate. **The retained residual per
  boundary is a measurement of the transport, not an accounting cost**: a boundary whose residual
  population grows is dissipative and one whose residual shrinks is contractive, and that reading is
  unavailable to any float implementation of the same tower. It is also a condensation axis
  orthogonal to morphology, and it is graded by the same receiver-minimality condition.
- Resource pressure changes partition, factorization, placement, residency, or receiver aperture.
  It never authorizes increasing a magic bound and replaying the same morphology.
- A quantized deployment export may use exterior floating/scaled arithmetic. No float enters the
  native production-semantic dependency cone or determines any native bit, index, topology,
  identity, rank, or committed delta.
- Exterior float/scaled arithmetic is permitted only as a codec/apparatus face. It may not choose a
  route, rank, topology, identity, native coefficient, committed delta, or semantic refusal. If an
  exported runtime's float arithmetic is itself the constitutive law, the result is an external
  deployment realization, not an exact native Phoenix construction.
- With the GPU path hidden or unavailable, an admitted native semantic deed returns a typed
  apparatus refusal. A CPU-produced answer is invalid evidence.

## 6. Modality and architecture portability

Every artifact schema above carries generic typed ports and material lineage from its first version.
Modality support is not added later by an enum branch.

### Recognition and encoder maps

Image, audio, video, sensor, and text recognizers enter as maps from one exterior codec to local
state/section transport. The lift returns the encoder's quotient and reconstruction fibre, not a
claim that the output tokens are meanings.

### Diffusion and generative maps

A diffusion source additionally carries:

```text

denoising/refinement law
trajectory standing
boundary/sample receiver
random/seed lineage where present
```

The lift is graded over the trajectory and its plural canvas/field, never one final sample.
The exterior label *diffusion* does not identify this process with the causal-calculus diffusion
owner. That identification stands only when caused incidence, capacity, constitutive response,
boundary, chronology, return, and reconstruction testimony commute through the cross-chart maps.
Otherwise the source schedule remains a distinct refinement transport with an explicit defect.

### Audio and voice

An audio source carries sample chronology, phase, channel/speaker incidence, tokenizer/codec
decoder, acoustic boundary receiver, and calibration. Microsoft VibeVoice is a proposed future
apparatus instance: its [official source](https://github.com/microsoft/VibeVoice) describes distinct
language/context transport, continuous acoustic and semantic tokenizers, and an acoustic diffusion
head. No local artifact has yet been admitted, so its exact ports and operators remain open. This
blueprint schedules no VibeVoice construction.

### Mixed attention and state-space maps

Hybrid SSM/attention, MTP, MoE, local/global attention, recurrent, convolutional, and diffusion
blocks are all source layout species. Each enters the same potential/active/dissection/condensation
contract. No first instance may hard-code assumptions which make a later mixed architecture
unrepresentable.

An SSM-like source manifest separately types its state carrier, input incidence, transition or
generator law, observation law, direct passage where present, boundary/initial standing, and the
continuous/discrete chart relation including any step-size parameter. In conventional notation the
source may present these as `x' = Ax + Bu`, `y = Cx + Du` or a discretized counterpart; those symbols
are exterior charts over the typed passages, not a new SSM ontology. Hybrid blocks join through
their actual ports and chronology rather than being forced into either an attention or SSM cabinet.
