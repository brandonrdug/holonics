## Governing distinction

[definition] A traditional machine-learning chart consists of a fixed carrier `Theta`, a family
of labelled state transports `U_g : Theta -> Theta`, and declared observations or losses on
`Theta`. A holonic realization consists of a situated carrier `H`, addressed transports
`T_g : H -> H`, receiver families, retained reconstruction fibres, and returned morphology. A map
`q : H -> Theta` is not an identification by its type or dimensions. It becomes a lawful dynamic
receiver quotient only when every admitted generator commutes:

`q (T_g h) = U_g (q h)`.

[proved-derived; formal-checked] Once the generator square commutes, the existing lineage theorem
extends it to every ordered, possibly noncommuting successor word. Every declared receiver/history
then factors through `q`. One separating receiver after one admitted history reopens the proposed
fibre and refutes the quotient.

[definition] The following grades must remain distinct:

1. **observation factorization:** present receiver output factors through `q`;
2. **forward simulation:** every native transition projects to the classical transition;
3. **trace preservation:** admitted finite labelled executions project with order retained;
4. **operational bisimulation:** for total deterministic generator functions, the dynamic quotient
   relation follows transitions in both directions while retaining a possibly nontrivial fibre;
5. **state isomorphism:** a reconstruction section returns the same admitted native occurrence;
6. **native equality:** complete addressed causal signature, including morphology return and every
   admitted separator, agrees.

Trace equality is not causal identity. Surjectivity gives representatives, not a decoder. A bare
occurrence bijection, equal output, equal scalar loss, equal count, equal digest, or equal terminal
state proves none of the stronger grades.

## Strict holonic lift

[definition] Higher dimensionality is not inferred merely from a larger storage count. It is
behaviorally established when a classical quotient has a nontrivial reconstruction fibre and an
admitted transport moves a difference in that fibre into a receiver-visible direction. In the
linear chart this is the exact kernel criterion:

- `ker(q)` invariant under `T` permits a descended quotient transport;
- one `v in ker(q)` with `q(T v) != 0` obstructs every proposed `U` satisfying `q T = U q`.

[definition] Holonic transport exceeds a fixed classical chart in at least three precise ways:

1. **future separation:** two native occurrences share the current classical face but separate
   after an admitted successor history;
2. **dependent carrier:** topology, incidence, available ports, or constitutive fibres vary with
   the situated state, so one fixed product-space chart requires padding or another exterior
   quotient with a retained fibre;
3. **returned morphology:** a world return changes the later transport family itself, while a
   classical inference chart with fixed parameters only observes a state path.

These are exact transport distinctions, not claims that every holonic realization is automatically
more capable than every traditional model.
