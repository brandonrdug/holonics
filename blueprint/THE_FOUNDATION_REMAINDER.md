## The test the goal imposes

The stated objective is that **the machine learn to produce mathematical proofs, with the learning
as the intermediary mechanism.** That imposes one test on any architecture proposed for it:

> Is the learning intermediating **the mathematics**, or intermediating **a corpus about
> mathematics**?

Today it is the second, and the reason is structural rather than incidental. A mathematical deed
commits `admitted_tally += n` into `body/rest_record.hpp`. **There is nothing for its return to
change.** A tally conditions no later conduct; it cannot be reached, composed, or ablated. Meanwhile

theorem re-enters as material a later proof reaches and splices — but that body contains no
mathematics. `body/swing.hpp` reaches 9 of 649 headers and none of the 180 organ headers.

So the two capabilities the goal needs are in two machines, and every further construction on
either one widens the gap. That is why **one standing** is first below and not merely important.
