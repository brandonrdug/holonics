## 0. Direct ruling: an interpretation is work, not a place to stop

[project-postulate] A holonic interpretation is an active theorem-finding program. Once a proposed
correspondence names its source, target, maps, preserved structure, limits, first derivation target,
and a falsifier able to fire, repeating that it is an interpretation is not progress. The work
continues until it returns a scoped theorem, a counterexample, or a concrete obstruction which
narrows the next proof obligation.

[project-postulate] Epistemic grading remains exact, but it never grants or withholds permission to
reason. A theorem does not require an external field to ask for it first. The derivation may import
standard mathematics, strengthen hypotheses, change the proposed map, or kill the correspondence
with a witness. It may not hide a desired conclusion as a structure field, rename a hypothesis as a
law, or promote one proved face into an unproved global identity.

[definition] Every interpretation entering this worktrack carries one development card:

```text
SOURCE              the typed source object and hypotheses
TARGET              the typed target object
MAPS                every proposed comparison/transport map
DIAGRAM             the equation, naturality square, boundary law, or invariant to be proved
LIMITS              the declared aperture in which the maps are meaningful
FIRST THEOREM       the smallest statement whose proof advances the correspondence
FALSIFIER           a model, perturbation, or counterexample able to refute it
RETURN              proved theorem / counterexample / exact residual obstruction
```

[definition] After the card is deposited, subsequent work reports `FIRST THEOREM`, `FALSIFIER`, or
`RETURN`. It does not keep restating the non-equivalence boundary unless that boundary changes.

## 1. The exact formal target

[definition] The target is one theorem family composing the already owned objects

```text
addressed occurrence
  + additive incidence and boundary
  + ordered transport word
  + swing and orientation action
  + declared connection and route comparison
  + receiver/history quotient and complete reconstruction fibre.
```

[definition] Its elementary carrier is an addressed span

```text
X <-source- W -target-> Y,
```

where `W` is the population of carrying occurrences. One occurrence `w : W` induces the oriented
formal boundary

```text
delta(w) = [target w] - [source w].
```

For joined occurrences `w : X -> Y` and `v : Y -> Z`, the pullback witness owns the joining
equality and therefore licenses the middle cancellation

```text
([y] - [x]) + ([z] - [y]) = [z] - [x].
```

The cancellation must be derived from the joining equality; equal endpoint values without a joined
occurrence do not license it.

[definition] A transport generator acts on situated states, and `transportWord` retains its ordered
word. An admitted affine swing is one generator instance

```text
S_b(a) = b + b - a,
```

with `S_b^2 = id`. A word of swings carries the derived orientation unit `(-1)^length`; this generic
orientation is not definitionally the planar crossing `Hand`, which enters later as an instance.

[definition] A connection is a declared assignment of transport to addressed elementary passages
which respects identity and serial composition. Parallel transport is its ordered word. Holonomy is
the return of a closed addressed loop. A commutator may be identified with a holonomy face only
after the relevant generators are invertible transports in that connection and the commutator word
is an addressed loop.

[definition] A receiver/history quotient `q` is dynamically exact when every admitted generator
descends:

```text
q (T_i x) = U_i (q x).
```

The generator equations must extend to every ordered word. Every quotient class retains its full
inverse-image reconstruction fibre, and a richer receiver/history returns a shortest separator
when it reopens that fibre.

## 5. Instance ladder after the core composition

[definition] These instances proceed in order because each consumes the composed passage rather

### I0 — alternating geometry and the cross product

[definition] Derive the 2D alternating span as the boundary hand of an addressed triangle. Then

### I1 — material polygons, winding, and gyroparallelograms

[definition] Bind material-polygon edge telescoping, turn ledgers, planar crossing hands, and the

### I2 — the Navier--Stokes torus

[definition] Instantiate the carrier on the genuine spatial three-torus. Connect closed physical
arcs to integer character windings, addressed Fourier triads to closed frequency polygons, and the
dyadic interact/filter square to the generic route-comparison defect. The field remains primary;
Fourier and Hodge quantities remain receiver faces.

### I3 — torus paths, links, and knots

[definition] Construct an oriented closed path on `T^2`, its integer winding pair, and the `(p,q)`
torus-knot embedding under the required coprimality hypotheses. Connect the traversible-chain
half-twist reading only by proving that its constructed boundary path has the returned slope or by
returning the mismatch. Crossing tables do not substitute for isotopy.

### I4 — complex two-torus monodromy and the completed family

[definition] Construct the period lattice from the columns of `SixSpherePeriods.periodMatrix`, its
discreteness under the proved determinant hypotheses, and the quotient
`C^2 / periodMatrix(z) Lambda`. Prove that the primal/dual variance adapter carries the existing
monodromies to quotient-chart transport. Then construct the local family, exceptional fillings,
collar gluings, and the topology receipts in the order required by the source construction.

[definition] A finite `(Z/4)^4` phase section is an addressed receiver probe of this geometric
fibre. It cannot discharge the quotient, filling, or total-space theorems.

### I5 — curved arcs, gyrotransport, and Einstein receivers

[definition] Type position radius, curvature radius, arc length, phase, differential, and refinement
index separately. Construct oriented arcs, connection transport, curvature return, and polygonal
Gauss--Bonnet as instances of HC4. Then derive or falsify the proposed local arc/differential
coupling inside a Lorentzian realization with dimensions, covariance, Bianchi, metric
compatibility, and stress-energy transport explicit.

### I6 — the dimension obstruction

[definition] Replace arithmetic coincidences at eleven by a rank theorem or counterexample. Define
the independent deformation/transport modes, quotient fixed-board, gauge, closure, and
reparameterization directions, declare the successor receiver family, and compute the surviving
rank. Eleven is returned only if a twelfth direction produces a proved obstruction under those
hypotheses.

### I7 — Reidemeister lineage, unknotting, prime factors, and null receivers

[definition] Begin with mathlib's actual `Rack` and `Quandle` owners rather than a crossing-count
analogy. A pivot acts by the equivalence `Rack.act'`; action/inverse-action cancellation is the local
R-II coloring law; `Shelf.self_distrib` is the R-III coloring law; `Quandle.fix` closes the R-I
coloring face; and `Rack.ad_conj` transports the complete pivot action by conjugation. Bind these
local laws to the swing only through the proved invertible action and conjugation squares.

[definition] Then construct the smallest oriented tangle-diagram owner carrying boundary ports,
component lineage, crossing hand, over/under branch, local disc, and unchanged exterior context. A
Reidemeister occurrence is an addressed passage on this owner. Serial move composition retains the
pullback population of move sites and its joining diagram. An inserted cancelling signed pair in a
bare crossing table is not R-II until the local-disc, adjacency, strand, and orientation hypotheses
are returned.

[definition] Generate `ReidemeisterEq` as the equivalence closure of elementary move passages and
form its quotient only after the concrete step relation exists. The unknot fibre is the complete
population of diagrams in the orbit of a declared unknot diagram. Prove that a receiver invariant
on every generator descends through every ordered move word. Receiver equality characterizes that
unknot fibre only after a separate completeness theorem; constant, winding-net, finite-phase, and
pairwise-linking receivers are required collapsed-fibre controls.

[definition] Separate representation-preserving Reidemeister moves from topology-changing crossing
changes. Unknotting distance is a minimum over crossing-change passages separated by arbitrary
Reidemeister histories; it is not the length of a Reidemeister word. Define connected sum only on
knot classes. A prime knot is a nonunit irreducible: every factorization contains the unit unknot.
The prime-factor multiplicity vector is an object coordinate, not an additive unknotting-cost law.

[definition] Define writhe and framing deltas on the diagram owner. R-I changes writhe by one signed
unit; R-II and R-III preserve it, so writhe descends to regular/framed isotopy but not ordinary
Reidemeister equivalence. An Euler-character receiver is an explicit compression: the full-turn
phase `exp(2*pi*i*w)` collapses every integer writhe to one, while an `n`th-root character retains
only writhe modulo `n`. A richer Laurent-polynomial or state-sum receiver must retain the reopened
fibre rather than erase it.

[definition] For a move-invariant feature `Phi`, unknot `U`, and declared receiver form `B`, set
`deltaPhi(D) = Phi(D) - Phi(U)` and `E_U(D) = B(deltaPhi(D), deltaPhi(D))`. Under positive
semidefiniteness, zero energy places the defect in the radical. Under definiteness it gives feature
equality. Only receiver completeness identifies feature equality with the unknot orbit. A
Lorentzian null vector is not automatically receiver-invisible: an indefinite null cone is
generally not the radical and may carry nontrivial transport.

[definition] The engine compression consequence is word-local. Adjacent inverse moves annihilate
only when both contextual compositions return the declared identities. Independent moves exchange
only through an exact interchange/comparison cell. R-III is an exchange law, not a deletion.
Dynamic condensation then consumes the existing generator law so all future move words factor,
while the complete reconstruction fibre retains every diagram collapsed by the receiver.
