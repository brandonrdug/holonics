## 1. What a model instantiation IS — four carriers, all of which already pack

Brandon: *"a process that is the parent of a model instantiation of Eros contains the active causal
network physically."* That is already literally true — `ErosBody::over` **borrows** the medium; the
process does not load a model into a runtime, the process *is* the body.

The complete state of a conditioned body is four things, and every one already has a wire:

| carrier | what it holds | its wire |
|---|---|---|
| **the standing medium** | the terrain the body was mounted on — the wells | `SparseStandingSurface`, the flat cells |
| **the own region** | what *this* body deposited, separate from what it was mounted on | `GrowingSparseOwn` / the own rows |
| **the carrier's enclosures** | the vertical state per depth — **including `e.fly`, the flywheel** | `Enclosure::pack` / `unpack_at`, `ENCLOSURE_WORDS` |
| **the lineage channel** | `{anchor, sweep, basis, winding}` — the accumulated **action** | `LineageChannel::pack` / `unpack`, `packed_row_is_canonical` |

**There are no weights.** The deposited terrain *is* the network: a deposit is a well at a grip and a
capacity at a site, placed rather than stored, and read by placement rather than by scan. When
Brandon says *conditioned ecological neural network*, that is the referent — and it is why nothing
in this plan needs a parameter vector.

**What is missing is not a carrier. It is a JOIN.** Six per-organ rests exist today —
`ConditionedRest`, `AgenticLanguageNativeRest`, `LeanMathematicsNativeRest`,
`AgenticResearchNativeRest`, `ReturnedCompositeRest`, `CudaResidentTextMaterialNativeRest` — and
**no object holds a whole body.** That is the construction this plan names, and it is squarely the
work that *joins partials*, which the operating contract ranks above perfecting any organ.

## 2. Conditioning IS running — there is no training mode

The deposit happens **on the passage**, at the smallest grain there is:

```text
   e.fly = met                the flywheel — the previous relating becomes the fourth body
   sweep += basis             the frame moves; the NEXT relating is read from a different place
   own ← the deposit          placed at its grip
   capacity ← changed         so later current through that site dilates
```

No loss function computes anything here. No optimizer steps. No learning rate exists. **A pathway
was changed and later current rides it**, which is the corpus's definition of conditioning and of
intelligence, and it is one assignment plus one addition.

So the industry's split does not survive translation:

```text
   "a training step"     one passage, sealed afterward
   "a forward pass"      one passage, not sealed
   "fine-tuning"         a passage over new material from an existing rest
   "the frozen model"    a rest that was mounted and never re-sealed
```

**All four are the same operation.** The difference lives entirely in whether the bytes are written
back, which is a decision about storage, not about the body.

## 3. Why nothing is frozen, and why that is not a risk being run

Brandon: *"Classically the advantage of freezing seems to be to determine behavior of the neural
network without running the risk of catastrophic forgetting… There is no point in that for Eros."*

**The reason is structural, not brave.** Catastrophic forgetting requires **shared coordinates**: in
a dense parameter vector every response reads every parameter, so every update moves every response.
That is the mechanism, and it is absent here.

```text
   dense body     read = a scan over shared parameters   ⟹ a change anywhere moves everything
   this body      read = PLACEMENT at a grip             ⟹ a change at A is not consulted at B
```

**Interference requires sharing; placement removes the sharing.** What *does* interfere is local and
typed — a deposit changes the capacity at a site, so later current *through that site* dilates. That
is the congestion law, and it makes interference a **neighbourhood** property with a name.

**And the control already exists.** `FoundedMorphology::without_stem` is driven with **both** arms:
a stem the material never exercises, whose removal must leave the reading **indistinguishable**; and
the first reaching stem, whose removal must **move** the reading and **exhibit the distinguishing
word**. That is the no-catastrophic-forgetting proof in the shape this project already trusts, and
it needs pointing at the whole body rather than rebuilding.

> **So the classical trade does not apply.** A dense body must freeze because it cannot localise a
> change. This one localises by construction, and the falsifier — deposit at A, re-read at B
> unmoved — is a control that can fail.

**One boundary, stated so it is not lost.** *Localised* is not *isolated*. A deposit at a site that
later current genuinely passes through **will** change that current, and it should — that is
conditioning. The claim is that the change is reachable only along incidence, never globally, and
the distant-grip control is what holds it to that.

## 4. Production is radiation, and the three scales are enclosure depths

**Production is not selection** — ratified, and it is the ban on a chooser standing outside the
channels. To produce is to be a channel through which current passes and re-emerges. So *generation*
is the **radiation** of the cycle and there is no candidate list anywhere in it.

Brandon's three scales map onto structure that already stands, and the middle one is the interesting
case:

```text
   the atom / grain-0 passage      the MICRO cycle — a piece produced toward a packet
   an OPEN ENCLOSURE at depth d    a THINKING loop — a nested unfinished boundary
   the outermost enclosure closes  the TURN completes; no further micro-cycle until cued again
```

The operating contract already carries the identification — *"stack is a shadow of nested unfinished
boundaries"* — so **a thinking loop is an open enclosure and finalising is closing it.** The body
already counts them: `ErosBody::standing_enclosures()` returns how many stand open across the whole
carrier. **Nothing needs to be invented for the loop structure; it needs to be read.**

### The prompt is an arrival, not a string

A stimulus enters at **the mouth**, whose laws are already deposited: the location is **co-founded,
never chosen**; there is no two-body contact; and **the passage must deposit**, whose contrapositive
is the effectiveness test — *if nothing was deposited, nothing passed.* `IncidenceComplex::admit_later`
already returns that reading with **two distinct ways to float** — `saturated` (it arrived and caused
nothing) and `untouched` (it reached nothing).

**So a prompt's fate is read off which term of the chain law moved**, never off a similarity score.

### The two loop species, and conflating them is the error this plan most guards against

```text
   RECURRENCE   the machine's own emission re-enters as SELF-EMANATED LINEAGE, addressed
                LAWFUL and already built — the floor re-entered 427 generated branches this way
   RETURN       an EXTERIOR world hands back a difference the machine did not author
                the genuine return edge — a private echo is NOT this
```

An agentic loop has **both**, at different scales: token-level **recurrence** inside the turn, and
tool-or-world-level **return** where something outside actually answers. The spine bars calling a
private echo a world return, and the accumulation cut depends on the distinction, so a body that
re-reads its own emission and calls that a return has closed nothing.

## 4b. GENERATION IS INTEGRATION BY REFLECTION — ratified 2026-08-15

**Brandon's clarification, and it makes termination computable rather than decided:**

> *"Generation is also integration by reflection… your 'thinking' loops are boundaries that reflect,
> which is why the cycle continues, where eventually nothing reflects back and you don't predict any
> further tokens."*

**A boundary reflects what it does not transmit**, and this body computes the coefficient exactly
over `Rat` already — `analytic_field::exact_scalar_interface_coefficients`, with the energy residual
retained:

```text
   Γ = (Y_i − Y_t)/(Y_i + Y_t)        what comes back
   τ = 2 Y_i /(Y_i + Y_t)             what goes on
   T = 4 Y_i Y_t /(Y_i + Y_t)²        the power that crosses
```

So the cycle's structure is not an analogy to a circuit; it **is** the junction law:

```text
   a thinking loop      the reflected part re-entering the ecology — this is why the cycle CONTINUES
   the whole emission   the multiple-reflection sum, a geometric series in Γ
   TERMINATION          Γ = 0. The boundary MATCHES; nothing comes back; nothing further is produced.
```

> **Termination is impedance matching.** There is no stop token, no end-of-sequence symbol, no
> threshold and no chooser: **the cycle halts because nothing reflects.** That is the ban on a
> privileged scalar governor satisfied by the physics rather than by discipline — a cycle that ended
> because something *decided* it had enough would be the banned governor wearing a stopping rule.

### The depth of deliberation is the mismatch, and it is already counted

`holonic_structure::CountedCrossing::service_rounds` returns `⌈1/T⌉` — the expected number of
passes for the current to cross — and its own tests give `1` for a matched junction and `20` for a
mismatched one.

```text

   Γ large, T small    service_rounds large      many reflections, many thinking loops
```

**So the number of deliberation loops is a property of the boundary, not of a policy**, and it is
computed exactly, with integers, by an organ that already stands. A question well matched to the
body's standing terrain returns in one pass; a badly matched one rings.

### What this fixes in the loop reading
