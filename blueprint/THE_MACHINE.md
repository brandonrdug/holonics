This is one carrier with organs on it, not a cabinet. Following one run:

```text

        ▼

  ConditionedCircuit             the production as a GradedCausalComplex, with
        │                        provenance: CausalCellId -> the passages that
        │                        founded it, total under every aperture
        │
        ├─► derivation_atlas         invariants: what was derived, by how many routes
        ├─► derivation_curvature     deficits per site, named by passage; write-back
        │                            into local_star's own event law

        ├─► derivation_skein         moves; supported vs OPEN with the obstruction kept
        └─► derivation_codec_intake  foreign codecs entering by the native seam
```

**The control that makes the first arrow real.** Unconditioned, an identifier is an atom:
`exactCarrier` and `exact_chart_carry` share no structure, no bridge is licensed, and **nothing is
emitted, for every query**. Conditioned, they share a stem and the passage emits. And deleting a
licensing stem removes exactly the passages it licensed and the circuit cells they founded — with
the two controls that make that mean something: *the absent passages are exactly those the deleted
stem licensed*, and *at least one removal reopens*, so suppression is measured rather than assumed.

That fourth ablation shape — delete a founded fiber, re-ask, later production structurally absent —
was reported by a laboratory survey on 2026-08-08 as implemented **nowhere in either repository**.

## 4 · The gaps, each verified, located, and with what closes it

### 4.1 · The temper is tested and never run

`crates/holonic-engine/src/temper.rs`, 8 tests, 8 mutations killed, **zero consumers and zero
drivers**. Every leg in this campaign was required to ship a runnable driver on real material and
this one did not.

It is also not connected to the machinery it was built to ride. `graph_receiver.rs` carries
`ReceiverGraphDeed::{Dilate, Refine, Coarsen}` with `Coarsen` retaining the entire source fiber;
`Twist::may_condense` decides which structure may take that deed; **nothing joins them.** Closing it
is one driver that reads a real production's circuit, tempers the family, and takes `Coarsen` on
exactly what closed.

### 4.2 · `Θ = C/r` is not ported, and the ad-hoc form is in the tree

`soma/body/src/channel.rs` carries the real form: `WorldlineTurn { c: ComposedTurn, r: Cog }`, where
**reach is supplied at the read and structurally cannot be carried** — `LineageChannel` has no reach
member, and the doc names the failure it prevents: *"a carried reach counter is the von-Neumann
hideout."*

**Corrected 2026-08-08, and the correction shrinks this gap.** A mathematics inventory across both
repositories established that `Θ` and `π` are **one object measured by two rulers**, related by
`Θ = 2·(C/d) = C/r`. There is no contradiction to reconcile and no factor-of-two defect anywhere:
every Einstein-form and Gauss–Bonnet statement in the older strata that writes `2·(C/d)` **is already
writing `Θ`**. What is owed is a notation normalization, not a port — and one real hazard, which is
that a **third** `C/d` shares the glyph: the nest depth, and the strike's mirror-versus-founding
discriminant. Those must be kept typed apart from the turn.

`crates/holonic-engine/src/cuda_aperture.rs:1024` carries `CarrierDilation { arc, chord }`, which I
wrote today. It holds the pair and never divides, which is right, but it measures against the chord
rather than from the pole:

> *"`C/d` measures the turn against the diameter — a chord through the whole body, a both-ends,
> view-from-outside ruler. `C/r` measures from the pole — the frame's own reach, one hand… The
> diameter is the view from both sides; the radius is the frame's reach; **the machine measures from
> the pole.**"*

**And the sweep Soma itself declared owed was never done.** The load-bearing strata still read
`C/d`. `C/d = 2⁻¹Θ`, so they differ by a factor of two, and `d` is explicitly declared *not
fundamental* — it cancels.
